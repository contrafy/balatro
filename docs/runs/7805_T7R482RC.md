## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no scoring joker for the ante-1 Big Blind (supply + money). The only shop had $6: The Order $8 (out
of reach) and a Buffoon pack offering Astronomer (no score) or Walkie Talkie (perishable rental, +10 chips +4 mult per
scored 10 or 4). The run then lost the Big by 6 chips.

Decision I would change:
- Buffoon pick: Walkie Talkie over Astronomer. With zero scorers at ante 1 the only thing that matters is clearing 450
  and 600; Astronomer adds nothing to a hand at ante 1 (planets are only $3 anyway, and I had $2). Walkie Talkie fires
  on every scored 10/4 (8 of 52 cards); the final club flush contained TC: (296 chips-side +10, mult 4 -> 8) = ~672 and
  the blind clears. Its rent (-$3/round) is exactly the "scorer you need to survive ante 1-2" exception in PLAYBOOK 2a.
- Big Blind hand 2: Pair KK (60) + draw-for-flush vs Two Pair KK88 (112) + needing 250 from the last hand. I picked the
  flush line (~30% est.); the flush came but at 296/302. Either line was a coin-flip; not the real cause.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF a Buffoon pack at ante 1 offers only a non-scoring joker (Astronomer, economy, utility) and a stickered joker that
  ADDS chips/mult to some hands (Walkie Talkie, Odd Todd, Scholar, Fibonacci...), and you own 0 scorers, THEN take the
  scorer even as a perishable rental: 5 rounds of rent (-$15) is cheaper than losing the 450/600 blinds jokerless.
- IF jokerless at the ante-1 Big Blind THEN count on ~3 hands averaging 150: a single L1 flush/straight is 260-310, so
  you need a second hand of ~150 (trips or a high two pair) as well; bank a made two pair only when the remaining draw
  still supports a 5-card hand.
- IF you hold 4 to a flush with 0 discards and 2 hands left THEN play the other cards as a hand that uses NO suited cards
  (pair + off-suit fillers) to see 5 new cards; here it hit the flush (but low ranks: low clubs score ~296).

Advisor/CLI defects:
- Pack `advise` ranked Astronomer{PERISHABLE} 75 over Walkie Talkie{PERISHABLE,RENTAL} 45 while owning 0 scorers at
  ante 1 with $2. A LEVELS joker with no money to buy planets scores nothing; with 0 scorers at ante 1-2 any joker that
  adds chips/mult (even rare-condition, even rental) should outrank pure utility/levels jokers.
- Pack `advise` described Walkie Talkie as "PLUS(rare condition)": 10s and 4s are 2 of 13 ranks (~1 scored card in 3
  five-card hands), not rare.
Bridge issues: none; every action settled first try (12 steps). Note: `shopadvise` immediately after `cash` printed
"shopadvise only works in the shop" once; a 2 s sleep fixed it.
