  (Constellation, capped at x2.1 by ante 6) plus a good set of hand levels (Pair L3, Two Pair L5, Full House L4,
  Straight L2, Straight Flush L2) and modest flat jokers (Blue Joker, Walkie Talkie, Hiker, Raised Fist, Mad Joker).
  Per playbook 7a "Ante 6-8: Two or more xMult sources plus main-hand level 4+ is the minimum" - we had only 1 xMult
  and hit that wall exactly on schedule at ante 6. The single decision I would change: at ante 6 shop 1, buy The Idol

  sell$2) to make room, rather than following shopadvise's LEAVE. Blue Joker's raw chip contribution was small

  likely have cleared the 30000 Big Blind. Money was NOT the binding constraint late (ended with $8, plenty of
  interest-earning shops throughout) - the binding constraint was xMult scarcity, exactly as playbook 7a predicts.
  Estimate calibration: `est` ran persistently 20-45% LOW versus actual scores from ante 4 onward (e.g. Trips-7
