# Run FL4ZGVC1 (port 7809, claude-sonnet-5-5) - Black Deck Gold Stake - LOSS at ante 1 blind The Head (249/600)

Ante 1: Small 300 (Rare tag), Big 450 (Foil tag), Boss The Head 600 (hearts debuffed). Start $4, no jokers.
- Small 300: discarded twice; Two Pair T5 100, then kept 4 spades+4H and drew a 9-high Straight 260 = cleared. Shop $5: Buffoon pack ($4) -> Wily Joker (+100 chips trips) over rental Hanging Chad; $1 left. Skipped rental Greedy $1.
- Big 450: dealt 8-8-8 on the first hand, Trips + Wily est 462 (exact) cleared in one hand. Shop $7: bought perishable Clever Joker ($4, +80 chips Two Pair), $3 left (Vampire/Celestial pack not affordable).
- Boss The Head 600: 3 of the first 8 cards were hearts (debuffed), no jokers triggered. Discarded twice (kept pair of 6s, then 4D5D6D7S), drew nothing; 2 discards used, then 3 hands scored 5 (cycle play), 220 (Straight 3-7), 24 (Pair 2s) = 249. Dead.

Post-mortem:
- Binding constraint: a gated-chip build at ante 1 (both jokers chips-only: Wily +100 chips Trips, Clever +80 chips Two Pair) with 2 mult base means any non-trips/non-two-pair hand scores 25-260. Boss 600 needs ~2 hands of 300 or one trips; both jokers need hands I did not get (trips 1 in ~20 hands on 5-card draws). Luck was part (The Head debuffed 3 of 8 starting cards), but my build had no unconditional scorer.
- Decisions I would change: (1) At the Big shop ($7) I bought a perishable Clever Joker for $4; the alternative Jumbo/Celestial packs were worse, but I should have considered Vampire/Greedy... honestly none was a good scorer; the real mistake was the first shop: Buffoon pack with $5 left $1, and I never got +mult. (2) On the boss I burned my 2 discards chasing a straight with 4 outs and then used a play (High card) as a cycle, which uses a hand: with Black Deck (3 hands) a cycle play costs 1/3 of the round. In hindsight playing Straight 220 on hand 1 and then two draws would have had the same total. The decisive failure was no hand ever reaching ~300 more than once. (3) Should have kept pair of 6s + KS and discarded differently? Not clear; the discard after the first draw broke the Two Pair draw (Clever) which was the joker I owned.
- Rules (if-then):
  * IF both your jokers are chips-only and gated (Wily Trips, Clever Two Pair), THEN your base mult is x2: Two Pair = (20+80+30)x2 = 260, Trips = 462. Only trips clears 450+; plan to discard toward trips/two pair, not straights, when the gated joker matches (I abandoned Two Pair T5 draw for a straight at the boss).
  * IF the boss is The Head (hearts debuffed) and you hold 3+ hearts in the opening hand, THEN discard debuffed hearts first and keep a non-heart pair; a debuffed pair scores only the non-debuffed card.
  * IF a play uses a hand just to cycle cards (no discards left), THEN on a 3-hand round it costs 1/3 of your damage; only do it with >= 2 hands left and a >=60% out to a hand that scores >= need/2.
- Advisor/CLI defects: `hands` est for the junk-cycle play was 24 vs actual 5 (debuffed QH in the scoring slot: High Card scored only the debuffed card idx 1). `advise` Small shop ranked Buffoon pack above a Vampire xMult without noting that a gated-chip joker from the pack is worth little with x2 base mult; ante-1 Gold shops mostly lack real +mult.
- Bridge issues: none.
