## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING + MONEY. The build never had more than one real xMult (Constellation, grew only via planets: x1.6) plus a free polychrome Fortune Teller x1.5; money was $0-$6 from ante 2 on because every shop bought something just affordable. Flat build (Green +17 mult, Sly +50, Stuntman +250 chips, Dusk final-hand retrigger) made ~25k on a two pair at ante 6 versus 90k/120k needed. No growth like Throwback/Obelisk was ever offered; no shop in antes 4-6 had a joker I could afford with $0-6 anyway.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop 2 / ante 2: reaching ante 2 at $1 and ante 3 at $0-1 meant I could never buy Astronomer ($8, free planets feed Constellation), Jumbo Buffoon ($6), Director's Cut, or any $5+ joker in antes 3-5 shops. Skipping the Dusk buy ($5) at ante 2 would have kept interest-free flexibility; I followed the SIM (+0.12) and it was probably right for score, but money stayed at $0-$4 for 4 antes. Each blind pays only ~$5 at Gold, so there is no catching up.
2. Ante 4: skipped the Small (Polychrome tag) correctly (poor, 9000 vs 6k hands) and the poly Fortune Teller x1.5 + Psychic clear came from it. Should have kept the free slot for it deliberately (I had it).
3. Ante 5: skipping BOTH Small and Big and fighting only the boss with Mr. Bones as the safety net was the best line available (cleared ante 5 with 26.6k of 50k); it cost nothing since $3 could not buy anything.
4. Stuntman: hand size 6 was a real cost (fewer 5-card draws) but +250 chips doubled my score; take it at any ante when your chip total is tiny relative to need.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the Buffoon pack offers {ETERNAL,RENTAL} Lusty/any weak joker AND something clean exists, THEN take the clean joker even if the SIM scores the eternal rental higher (SIM ignores that rent is -$3/round and unsellable).
- IF you own Constellation, THEN every planet (even off-hand ones like Mars/Jupiter from High Priestess, Fool-copied Uranus) is +0.1x for $3: buy planets in the shop with your last $3. The Fool copies the LAST planet used, so use your main-hand planet before buying a Fool.
- IF a Gold blind is more than 2x your best 3-hand total AND the Small pays no money you can use, THEN skip it for the tag (Polychrome tag = free x1.5 joker in the next shop after Big; Rare tag = free rare joker such as Stuntman).
- IF you own Mr. Bones THEN skip Small and Big at ante 5 and fight only the boss: 25% of the boss need survives (12.5k of 50k).
- IF The House is the boss THEN discard the 5 visible-uncertain cards first (only first hand is face-down; discarding refreshes them face-up), then play normally.
- IF est (with Dusk/Constellation) is within 1-2% of a final need, THEN play it: in this run actual scores ran 3-6% ABOVE est consistently (24729->26073, 10382->11000, 8568->8874) - est does not count Constellation growth/mult card fully.
- Dusk (retrigger scoring cards on the FINAL hand) was a real +60-70% on the last hand (2232 vs ~1300; 6240 vs 3244): hold your best two pair for the last hand and spend hands 1-2 on digs/mid hands.
- The Pillar: debuffed (previously played) cards still count as pair members; discard them and draw; the final Dusk hand cleared 6400 with 6240 (+junk dig hand) despite 3 debuffed cards.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- pack advise ranked ETERNAL+RENTAL Lusty Joker first (SIM p_next 0.60) although the rule text says -$69 unsellable; the ADVICE line picked Green Joker anyway (fine) but SIM on stickered rentals is misleading.
- shopadvise ante 3: "Constellation{ETERNAL} XMULT(growth)" scored 110 but the SIM said +0.00 and the ADVICE flipped to a Celestial pack; sim does not model Constellation growth from future planets.

- `s`/shopadvise "Boss Tag" skip advice absent at ante 5 (do not skip: shop worth more) although with $3 and Mr. Bones the shop was worth nothing.
- Perishable counter for Fortune Teller ticked on boss round only; skip does not tick (as documented), fine.
