## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING. No joker-phase xMult until Ramen at the ante-4 boss shop; Blackboard (conditional, perishable rental)
was the real engine from ante 4 and both perishables plus Mr. Bones were bridges expiring at once. Every ante-3/4 shop showed
flat jokers only (6 rerolls at ante 3-4: Delayed Grat, Greedy, Wily, Astronomer, Shoot the Moon, Gluttonous, Mr. Bones/Ramen on the last).
Without Blackboard the build made ~3.5k per hand at ante 5 (needs 16.7k); with it ~10-16k. Money never recovered after ante 4:
two/three rentals + Small $0 + a Bones save paying nothing left $1-2 at ante 6.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 Big shop: Photograph{ETERNAL} $5 as a "scorer" (card-phase x2 on base mult, ~+2 mult on L1 pairs). An eternal slot all run.
   Keeping $5 for interest + the ante-3 rerolls would have been worth more.
2. Ante 3 first shop ($38 after Investment): filled all slots with flat perishables (Odd Todd, Even Steven) + Wily, which then
   expired together at the Wall. Better: Abstract + one bridge, keep $15-20 for Buffoon packs/rerolls across ante 3.
3. Ante 5: skipping Small+Big kept Mr. Bones + Blackboard for the Hook (survived), but it handed ante 6 a $2 wallet and a perishing
   Blackboard. Given 25000/37500 vs ~4k flat hands there was no better line; the real mistake was earlier (no xMult by ante 3).
4. Process: I sent two state-changing commands in one parallel tool batch twice (skip+blind at ante 4, skip+order at ante 6). The
   first one turned a planned Small skip into playing it. Never batch b.sh calls, even "obvious" sequences.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Blackboard, THEN (a) discard red cards that are not part of the play first, (b) play red cards as fillers next to a
  made Pair/Two Pair/Trips so only black cards stay held (AA + KH 8H fillers holding 4 blacks: 12972; Trips 999 + TH 5H: 21600),
  and (c) a Flush of ANY suit works if the 3 held cards are black (diamond Flush 16605 holding AC 7C 6C).
- IF The Hook and Blackboard, THEN play 4-5 cards: the Hook discards 2 of your held cards before scoring, so fewer held cards need
  to be black. The Hook's discards also tick Ramen (-0.01 per card): Ramen went 1.96 -> 1.90 over 3 Hook plays.
- IF Mr. Bones saves a blind, THEN expect ~$0 from that cash-out (no blind reward): do not plan the next shop around it.
- IF the Wall (x2) is the ante-4 boss and you have 0 xMult, THEN every reroll in that ante's shops is for xMult/Mr. Bones; Mr. Bones
  at $1 turns 36000 into a 9000 survival floor (here it was not needed, but it carried the Hook one ante later).
- IF a D6 tag is pending, THEN arrive with $5+: rerolls cost $0,$1,$2... (I arrived with $2 and got 2 views).
