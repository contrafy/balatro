## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no xMult at all by ante 4. The whole build was flat +mult/+chips plus Abstract (+18) and Chad/Scary single-card tricks; per-hand ceiling ~4-7k at ante 4 vs 18000 boss (needs ~6k x 3). The three perishables (Steven, Chad, Scary) all expired between the ante-4 Big and boss, and the Droll rental ate $3/round so I never had more than ~$18 for rerolls to hunt an xMult. Flint halved base chips/mult, which hurt the Flush/Two Pair base far more than single-card plays.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 Big: with 2 hands left I played a Two Pair + two singles at est exactness (13522 vs 13500) - fine, but I never used my 2 discards before them; at the boss I spent them well. The real miss: ante-4 Big shop ($18) bought Mega Celestial + Celestial (3 planet levels ~ +25% on hands that are not even the best hand) instead of rerolling the 2 joker slots hunting a multiplier / a Strength-type rescue. Planets could not close an 18000 gap.
2. Ante 4 Flint last two hands: at 11904 needed with 2 hands I chose Pair JJ (4945) to keep a 4-heart flush draw (47% to hit, flush est ~10k). It missed. The safer alternative (2P KJ 6900 first) needed a 5004 final that no draw could supply either, so the flush line was right; the real error was the first discard (kept QC QD 8D, discarding 4 hearts 9H 6H 5H 3H) - the solver's 0.70 line broke a 4-card heart flush whose Flush with Droll+faces est ~10k.
3. Ante 2-3 skips (Small x3, Big x1): good value (tags: Foil->Scary, Buffoon->Chad, Coupon->Abstract) but I fought ante 4 with nothing permanent. Coupon shop packs: took Lovers; Empress/Death would not have mattered either.

Reusable rules (if-then):

- IF The Flint is the boss THEN base chips/mult are halved: Flush/Two Pair/FH lose ~45% while flat per-card joker chips/mult are unchanged; est already shows it, do not plan around the unhalved hand.
- IF the advisor/solver says to discard a 4-card flush draw whose flush est is ~2x every made hand THEN compare the flush est yourself (Droll/Abstract builds make a flush worth 2x a Two Pair).
