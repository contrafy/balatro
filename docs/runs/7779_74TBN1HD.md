# LOSS at ante 5 boss (The Arm, 22000 needed, scored 19752 - short by 2248)

Post-mortem: Binding constraint was The Arm's level-degradation mechanic compounding with bad luck on Kings (my only reliable Baron trigger) during the boss round itself - I only found a King held for the FINAL hand, and by then Pair had already degraded from L5 to L3 from the first two hands. The decision I would change: entering The Arm at Pair L5 with no unleveled backup hand type strong enough to substitute (Trips L2 was my only alternative and scored similarly on paper but wasn't actually tested), I played Pair all three hands and let it degrade L5->L4->L3, rather than switching to Trips (or even Two Pair, which still gets Duo's x2 via the "contains Pair" rule) for hand 2 to preserve Pair's level for a stronger final hand. In hindsight, playing Trips on hand 2 (unleveled, so no degradation risk to my main engine) and saving full-strength Pair L5 for the hand where I actually held a King might have closed the 2248-point gap. I did correctly prioritize discards to preserve Kings-held-in-hand for Baron and used the "keep 1 discard for the final hand" rule, but did not manage the type-rotation half of The Arm's counter-strategy from playbook section 6.

Playbook suggestions:
1. **High priority**: The `est`/`hands`/`advise` calculator appears to overestimate scores whenever Baron ("x1.5 mult per King held in hand") is owned and 1+ Kings are held unplayed - overestimates of 23-39% were observed on otherwise-normal Pair plays. Investigate the estimator's handling of held-card xMult jokers; until fixed, treat `est` as an OPTIMISTIC upper bound (not a reliable pass/fail signal) whenever Baron or similar held-card jokers are in play, and budget an extra ~30% safety margin before trusting est >= need.
2. **High priority**: Under bosses that force a minimum card count (The Psychic = exactly 5, possibly others), the estimator does not seem to re-check played-card-count-conditional jokers (Half Joker: "+20 mult if <=3 cards played") against the REAL total cards played (including mandatory fillers) - it appeared to still credit the bonus, producing 59-75% overestimates across 3 consecutive hands. Playbook section 4 point 7 should warn: under The Psychic (or any forced-hand-size boss), assume small-hand-size jokers like Half Joker will NOT fire once fillers push the total above their threshold.
3. Section 6's The Arm entry should add explicit tactical guidance beyond "sell Luchador or play only unleveled types": if you have a strong leveled main hand AND a viable secondary (even unleveled), rotate types round-robin (main, secondary, main) rather than playing the leveled type in all 3 hands - this preserves at least one full-strength play of your main hand for whichever slot has the best card luck (e.g. a held King for Baron), rather than guaranteeing your main hand is already degraded by the time good luck arrives.
4. Consider adding to section 4: when the estimator itself shows est < need on the LAST legal hand of a boss round with no discards left, this is a hard loss signal worth flagging loudly in the log/output (not just quietly playing the only legal action) so the agent explicitly registers "this hand cannot mathematically win" before committing.
