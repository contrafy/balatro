## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. Every multiplier I owned was perishable: Mystic Summit (+15 mult, antes 1-2) and Ramen x2
(antes 2-4). The only permanent scorers were holo Droll (+10, +20 on Flush), Hanging Chad, Lusty (free Negative) and a
perishable foil Certificate. When Ramen perished after the ante-4 Big, the round fell from ~14.7k to ~9.4k against an
18000 boss with The Plant debuffing faces. Money never exceeded $14, so no shop could fund a joker hunt: 0 r/g xMult
was offered in a clean (sticker-free) form in 9 shop views + 4 Buffoon packs; the xMult-shaped offers were The
Order{E,R} (Straight), Driver's License{E,R} (16 enhanced cards) and Joker Stencil $8 at ante 1 when I had $6.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 shop 1: holo Droll $7 took me from $8 to $1 and pulled the run into a Flush engine (Jupiter, The World,
   chasing flushes every round). It was the only permanent scorer seen, but a Flush main on Black Deck needs 1-2
   discards every round, and 0 discards were left for pair-building. Keeping $8 for the next shop's Buffoon packs
   plus interest was probably equal and kept the pair-family plan.
2. Ante 4 shop 1: Jumbo Buffoon: Certificate (foil +50 chips, perishable) over Golden Joker{ETERNAL} (+$4/round).
   Certificate made the Big survivable, but $4/round x 13 rounds is the money that buys xMults at ante 5-6. At ratio
   0.12 neither saved the run; with a free slot the econ joker was the longer play.
3. Ante 4 shop 2 (last before the boss): $13 on Spectral (Deja Vu), a reroll and Arcana (The World) added ~0 score.
   Nothing better was offered; the run was lost at ante 3-4 by having no permanent multiplier.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF a tag will create a joker for you at Gold (Rare/Uncommon/Negative/Holo/Poly), THEN value it at ~half: the created
  joker rolls Gold stickers too (Double+Negative gave Mime{ETERNAL,RENTAL} and Lusty; Rare gave Driver's License{E,R}).
- IF your only xMult is perishable with 1 round left AND the next Small pays $0, THEN skip the Small so the xMult plays
  the Big (here Ramen x1.82 made Big 13500 clear with 15.3k; without it the round was ~9.4k).
- IF Ramen (x2, -0.01 per discarded card) is your multiplier AND a made hand clears this hand's share, THEN play it and
  keep the discards; burning 10 cards for Mystic Summit costs Ramen -0.10 for the rest of its life.
- IF Diet Cola is owned AND you are about to skip for a tag, THEN sell it first: the Double Tag copies the skip tag.
- IF you own Hanging Chad and a red-seal card, THEN `move hand` the red-seal card first: Chad's 2 retriggers plus the
  seal's 1 hit the same card (Flush S 8091).
- IF the shop SCORE CHECK stays < 0.3 for two antes with no permanent xMult, THEN the run is lost unless a Buffoon
  pack hits; prefer economy jokers (Golden Joker) over a perishable flat scorer when a slot is free, because only money
  buys the xMult.

  Two Pair 960 vs 1000; ante 2 Big: Trips 1404 vs 1500). The solver meant "clears with the later hands", but the text
  claims a one-shot clear.

  and after a pack pick); running `s` first fixes it.
