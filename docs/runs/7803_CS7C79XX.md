## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING. Zero permanent xMult in 6 antes (shop views: ~16 incl. 6 rerolls + 1 Jumbo Buffoon; the only
multiplier ever offered was the perishable Card Sharp at ante 6). The run lived on Two Pair levels: Orbital tag (+3) and
Space Joker (eternal, 1/4 level per play) took Two Pair from L1 to L13 by ante 5 (~24k per hand with Mystic Summit). That
carried ante 5 but every round needed 2-3 MADE Two Pairs out of 3 hands + 2 discards; the Big at ante 6 needed two, I
found one. A single-type engine with no flat multiplier dies to one bad deal.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 6: I skipped the Small for Handy (+$30) and then walked into the Big with $46 and no shop in between. Either play
   the Small (its shop could have spent the money before the Big) or, having skipped, also skip the Big for Economy
   (+$40 -> $86) and fight only the Tooth. Taking the money and then fighting the Big with it idle was the worst mix.
2. Ante 4-5 shops: left with $19, $17, $15 three times while EMERGENCY (0.26-0.37). Interest is right at ante 3, but at
   ante 4-5 with 0 xMult the playbook's "ante 3-4 with $25+ reroll" rule should have been applied earlier: 2 more rerolls
   in the ante-4 Big shop (Card Sharp-type finds) would have mattered more than $3 interest.
3. Ante 1 Popcorn rental: fine as a bridge, but I kept it one round too long (sold at +4 mult after paying $9 rent).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF a skip tag pays a lot NOW (Handy = $1 per hand played this run, ~$30 by ante 6; Orbital = +3 levels to your main
  hand; Economy = up to +$40), THEN it beats a $0 Small Blind even at ante 5+; but do not skip into a blind you must
  fight with that money unspent: plan which shop will spend it.
- IF you own Mystic Summit and Card Sharp, THEN spend both discards (dig with 4 cards each while holding your made hand)
  BEFORE the first play: the registering hand also gets +15 mult (16240 -> 22600 here), and every later hand too.
- IF your round needs N made hands of one type (Card Sharp x3 needs the type twice), THEN count the dig: with 3 hands
  and 0 discards left, one dig play (4 junk, keep a pair + 2 singles) gives ~50-70% for the second pair, not 90%.
- IF you own Space Joker on a Two Pair main, THEN every Two Pair play is +0.25 levels: prefer playing Two Pair over
  other types (Trips/Straight) whenever both clear; it reached L13 by ante 5 here.
- IF The Serpent is the boss and you own Mystic Summit, THEN discard 1 card at a time twice: hand grows 8 -> 12 and
  Summit turns on for all three hands (three made Two Pairs came out of the 12-card hand).
