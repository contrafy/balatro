## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING, not luck or misplay in the boss round itself. By ante 2 boss the main hand (Full House/Two Pair) was still Level 1 - no planet/Celestial pack was ever bought (shopadvise never surfaced one as top pick; Uranus was skipped at ante-1 shop 2 to preserve the $4 floor, and Uranus/Mars appeared again at ante-1 shop 3 but were also skipped for the same reason). The Hook (ante-1 boss, 600) and both ante-2 blinds were cleared by one-shot lucky trips/full-house draws (900, 1168, 1408), which masked that the engine itself (flat L1 hands + one partial-firing joker) could not reliably produce 1600+ in three hands once The House's forced-blind draws chewed through the deck. The Hook boss round used a full house that hid this weakness.

One decision I would change: at the very first ante-1 shop, buy Uranus ($3, Two Pair's planet) instead of leaving with $9 unspent-on-scaling — the $4-floor rule was applied too conservatively when the only alternative purchase (a joker) wasn't even on offer that visit. Getting the main hand to L2 by ante 1 would have meaningfully raised the est on every subsequent forced/weak hand (especially the two face-down-recovery hands this loss came down to).

Bridge/CLI issues: none. All commands returned expected state; `est` matched actual score exactly on every non-face-down hand this run (FLUSH 292/292, STRAIGHT 324/324, all earlier wins matched or were beaten). No ACTION FAILED or retry needed except the one deliberate reroll.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Section 5 (ante 1-2 shop rule) says "keep $4 after any pack/planet/voucher" as a hard floor — but this run never once crossed that floor into buying the main-hand planet across 3 shop visits despite Uranus/Mars being offered twice. Consider explicitly noting that a main-hand planet at $3 leaving exactly $1 is still worth it at ante 1 if no joker is competing for that money that same visit (the $4 floor exists to afford a *joker*, not to hoard past the point where no joker is on offer).
2. The House boss note (section 6) should explicitly warn that with Juggler (+1 hand size) the FIRST face-down hand can be up to 9 cards, and burning 2 discards just to reveal it (as advise correctly directed) leaves very few discards for the rest of the boss round if the boss also demands 1600+ chips at L1 hand levels - i.e. The House is much more dangerous with a hand-size joker and no levels banked beforehand.
