## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: MONEY at ante 1-2, then SCALING. The run never had more than $7 in a shop. Small Blinds pay $0, the only
scorers available were a rental Joker (+4 mult, -$3/round) and a perishable Odd Todd, and no shop before ante 3 offered an
affordable permanent +mult/xMult joker (Castle $6 at $5, Mystic Summit $5 at $2). With 2 flat jokers the best hands were
~1000-1800 against 1600/hand needed at the ante-3 Big. The solver's p 0.00 at the first ante-3 Big hand was right.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 Big shop ($5): I left with $5 to keep interest. Interest on $5 is $1; the ante-3 shop then had $7, still one
   pack. Greedy Joker{RENTAL} $1 (sim p_next 0.24 vs 0.00) would have added +3 mult per Diamond for the House and ante 3. It
   was a second rental, but with $5 and nothing else to buy, more score now beat $1 of interest. A run with $5 at ante 2
   cannot save its way to an xMult anyway.
2. Ante 3 shop ($7): Jumbo Celestial ($6) gave Pair L2 (+~35% on Pair only). An Arcana ($4, Justice/Empress/Hermit) plus $3
   kept might have done as much. Either way it was not enough. The real miss was earlier.
3. Ante 1 Big: the Economy tag (doubles money, max $40) was worthless at $1. Fine. But the ante-1 Small Boss Tag skip would
   also have been worthless. No change there.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF The House deals the whole hand face-down, THEN `sort rank` before discarding: newly drawn face-up cards are inserted in
  rank order, so each face-down card's position between visible cards gives its rank range. Then `sort suit` (order S, H, C,
  D) gives its suit range. Here: face-down cards before KH were spades/AH; one between 7H and 2D was a club/diamond.
- IF you hold $0-7 at ante 2 with only flat jokers and a $1 rental scorer is on offer, THEN take it when the sim gain is
  large (0.24 vs 0.00). Interest on $5 is $1/round; a missing scorer is the run.
- IF a perishable scorer has exactly 2 rounds left at the start of an ante, THEN skipping the Small keeps it alive through
  the boss (rounds tick only on played blinds).
- IF the Small Blind tag is Coupon AND you have under $4, THEN skip it: the Small shop buys nothing, and the Coupon shop after
  the Big gives free jokers/packs. It only helps if you can clear the Big alone, so check est x 3 >= Big first.
- IF at ante 3 your best typical hand is under a third of the Big Blind, THEN the run is already lost to economy. The fix is
  at ante 1-2: buy a permanent scorer in the first shops before packs, and keep at most one rental.
