## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling, caused by money. The wallet was $0-5 at every shop from ante 2 on (Red Stake $0 Smalls, rent,
every shop spent to $0 on a perishable bridge), so I only ever bought the cheapest scorer on offer: Crafty, Even Steven{P,R},
Raised Fist{P}, Misprint{P}, Half Joker{E,R}, Abstract{P}. No joker-phase xMult was ever offered (6 shop views + 1 reroll +
1 Buffoon pack in the whole run; the only xMult-shaped offers were Baseball Card in the ante-1 pack and Photograph at ante 3).
Final build: Pair L4 / Trips L4 + Half Joker +20 + Abstract +15 + Misprint ~11 + Raised Fist; ~3-4k per pair, ~6-7k per trips
or Crafty flush, a ~12-15k round vs the 18000 boss.

Decisions I would change:
1. Skipping 5 of 9 non-boss blinds (ante 2 Small+Big, ante 3 Big, ante 4 Small+Big). Each skip had a local reason ($0 wallet,
   Orbital levels, Juggle, perish ticks), but together they left me 6 shop views by ante 4. Gold needs joker views to find an
   xMult; a $0 shop shows the joker that the NEXT round's money can buy. The ante-2 Big skip in particular: +$5 Big reward
   would have made the ante-2 boss shop $10+ instead of $3.
2. Ante 2 first shop ($10, EMERGENCY): reroll + Raised Fist{PERISH} to $0 started the $0 spiral. Mercury ($3, Pair main)
   plus keeping $7 might have compounded better; with no interest the whole run was bridge-to-bridge.
3. Ante 4 Club: Raised Fist was worth 0 on the last hand because the lowest held card was a debuffed club (5C). I had
   discarded 4C earlier but kept drawing clubs; with 0 discards there was nothing to do, but it cost ~1.3k (still a loss).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Raised Fist AND the boss debuffs a suit (Club/Goad/Head/Window), THEN treat the low cards of that suit as junk:
  discard them first (and play them as fillers when Half Joker is not involved), because a debuffed lowest held card makes
  Raised Fist score 0.
- IF your wallet is $0-5 at ante 2-4 with 0 xMult, THEN do not skip the Big Blind for a level/utility tag: the Big's $5+ is
  what turns the next shop into a buying shop; skip only the $0 Small.
- IF every shop ends at $0 because each buy is a perishable bridge, THEN by ante 4 all bridges expire together; prefer one
  permanent +mult scorer (even a $1 eternal rental like Half Joker at ante 3) over a second perishable bridge.
- IF Orbital tags offer +3 levels on Pair/Trips at ante 2, THEN taking them is strong (Pair L4 + Trips L4 carried antes 2-3),
  but take at most one skip in that ante when broke.
