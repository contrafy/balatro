## Post-mortem

Best result this run: stake 1 (White), lost at ante 4 boss blind (The Plant, need 10000, scored 1959).
Jokers at death: Even Steven, Cloud 9, Smiley Face (polychrome, face-dependent), Lusty Joker, Photograph (xMult, face-dependent), Card Sharp (xMult, repeat-type-dependent). Main hand: Two Pair, leveled to L3.
Money curve: mostly spent down to $0 in antes 1-2 building the joker board, recovered to the $25-30 target by antes 3-4, but ended at $21 (unused - a lost-run signal per playbook section 7a, though moot since the blind itself was unwinnable).

Binding constraint: BOSS (The Plant, faces debuffed) combined with build composition. Two of our six jokers (Smiley Face, Photograph) and a large fraction of the dealt cards each hand were face cards, all of which scored zero. Our main hand (Two Pair) needs two independent pairs; the boss round dealt us mostly single face cards plus one pair of 6s, and three straight discards failed to surface a second non-face pair. By the final hand we had zero discards and only a pair of debuffed Kings to offer.

The one decision I would change: on the FIRST hand of The Plant boss round, instead of registering Card Sharp with the JJ/TT Two Pair (all it bought was x3 on a *future* Two Pair that never arrived), I would have discarded far more aggressively toward *any* pair of non-face ranks from the very first look at the hand, spending all 3 discards hunting a second scoring pair rather than 1 discard on registration + 2 discards that still didn't find a second pair. In hindsight the registration play cost us a discard cycle we couldn't afford when two-pair material was this scarce. More fundamentally, this build's two xMult jokers being face-card-conditional (Photograph, and Smiley Face as a flat-mult face joker) is a real vulnerability the playbook doesn't flag: Photograph is listed as "reliable" xMult in section 7a, but it is only reliable when face cards aren't debuffed - The Plant (and The Mark/Wheel-style face-down bosses) neutralize it entirely for that one boss round.

Bridge issues (exact text): `cash` returned `ACTION FAILED: Round eval blocking events still pending, retry shortly None` three times total this run (end of ante1 boss, end of ante3 big blind, end of ante3 boss); each time a `sleep 2` + retry succeeded immediately, consistent with the playbook's retry guidance. No other CLI errors encountered.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Section 4/PACK_OPENING: explicitly warn that packs bought/opened during the SHOP phase have NO active hand to target - any card-targeting tarot (Justice, Strength, Death, etc.) picked there is silently wasted (consumed with zero effect, not even added to Cons). Advise picking only non-targeting cards (Fool, Hermit, planets, spectrals that don't need a target) from shop-phase Arcana/Spectral packs.
2. Section 7a: flag Photograph (and any "x2/xN on first/every scoring FACE card" xMult) as face-dependent, not unconditionally reliable - it goes to zero for the entire boss round under The Plant (and similar face-debuff/face-down bosses). Recommend pairing it with at least one xMult source that is NOT rank/suit-conditional (e.g. Hologram, Constellation, Baron) before ante 4-5, especially once The Plant/Mark/Wheel is the upcoming boss shown in the shop.
3. Card Sharp registration guidance (section 7a) should note: only spend the registration hand on a hand type you are confident you can repeat with the cards remaining in hand - if the registered hand used your only current pairing material (as here, JJ+TT was our only source of pairs), the "repeat" hand may never materialize before hands run out; consider registering with the CHEAPEST possible version of a type you can rebuild from a fresh draw, not with the biggest hand available on turn 1.
