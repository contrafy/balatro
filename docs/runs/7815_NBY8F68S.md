## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: The Psychic at ante 1 with a one-trick joker. Half Joker (+20 mult only on <=3 cards) scored 700 on a 2-card pair in the Big, but it is worth exactly 0 against The Psychic (5 cards forced), and the only other scorer was Business Card (economy). The 5-card, no-joker line makes ~270 flush + ~120 two pair + ~150 trips = ~550, short of 600 by 51. The advisor's BOSS PREP printed this at the Big shop ("Half Joker never fires") but offered no fix at $6.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop 1 ($5): Half Joker was the only mult but the boss was already known to be The Psychic at the first blind select. Alternatives at $5: Wily (+100 chips on trips, weak) or Buffoon pack. With a known Psychic boss, Half Joker is a trap; a flat joker that works on 5 cards (Jolly/Zany/Mad/Clever, or any +mult) would have scored every hand. I took it because the advisor said NEEDED NOW and it made the Big a one-shot; the Big then paid $5 and I spent the $6 shop on a Buffoon pack that held only Business Card and Golden Joker{RENTAL}.
2. Ante 1 shop 2 ($6): the Buffoon pack (value +0.24) found nothing; Seed Money $10 and The Magician $3 were unaffordable/irrelevant. Alternative would have been Standard pack, no better. Probably unlucky.
3. Psychic hand 1: kept QH 8H 4H (3-flush draw) from AD QH 8H 6D 4H 4C 3C 3D which held Two Pair 4433; it got 6 hearts by luck (flush 272). Hand 2 had AA KK 77 + Q: no 5-card line over 124. The loss was already decided by the lack of a second scorer, not the discards.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the ante-1 boss is The Psychic (visible at first blind select), THEN Half Joker (+20 mult on <=3 cards) is dead against it: buy it only if you will also own a 5-card-capable +mult/chips scorer before the boss, otherwise prefer a Jolly/Zany/Mad/Clever-type joker or keep money for the 2nd shop's scorer.
- IF the Psychic is the boss and you own no 5-card scorer, THEN each hand is ~150-270 (Flush/FH/Straight/Trips) and the total is ~550: not enough for 600. Plan a second scorer or a flat mult joker in the Big-blind shop; the Big pays $5 (+$1/hand left), so the shop budget is ~$6.
- IF a Buffoon pack is the only buy at $6 and the shop has no scorer, THEN compare with Wily/Seed Money before burning it: the pack gave only Business Card/Golden{RENTAL}.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- shopadvise at the first shop printed NEEDED NOW for Half Joker{ETERNAL} while the blind-select screen already named The Psychic: the BOSS PREP warning only appeared in the NEXT shop, after the eternal was bought. Half Joker should be penalised/flagged in shop 1 when the ante-1 boss is The Psychic.
