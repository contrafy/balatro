## Post-mortem

**Binding constraint: boss debuff (The Plant) collided with money/scaling shortfall.** We never found a single
xMult joker across 4 antes of shop visits and 3+ rerolls (Crazy Joker's Straight-conditional bonus never
mattered once we committed to Pair; The Order, at ante 3, was the one XMULT offered and it didn't fit). Our
entire multiplier stack was flat/conditional (Half Joker +20, Abstract Joker +18, Misprint 0-23 random) rather
than multiplicative, so The Plant's blanket face-debuff (which neutralized Scary Face and Sock and Buskin
entirely, half our joker roster) left us with only Half Joker + Abstract + Misprint on ordinary rank pairs/trips
— not enough chips/mult to clear a 10000 target from a deck already picked over (25 cards drawn/discarded by the
final hand).

**The one decision I would change:** at the ante-3/4 shops, when no xMult appeared after rerolling, I should
have kept rerolling more aggressively before the money ran dry (we stopped at $2, one reroll short of another
look), and/or bought a hand-level pack earlier (we only reached Pair L3; L4-L5 would have added real headroom
even without xMult) instead of filling the 6th joker slot with Abstract Joker at ante 3. Abstract Joker's value
is proportional to joker count, so buying it before the roster was even full undervalued the alternative of
saving that $4 toward one more reroll pass or a Celestial pack level.

**Bridge issues:** none — no `ACTION FAILED`, no `Connection refused`, no `blind_on_deck` errors this run. All
commands behaved as documented.

**Calibration misses (est vs actual > 20%):**
1. Ante 1 Boss (The Psychic): PAIR J with 3 fillers, est 660 vs actual 60 (91% miss). Cause: Half Joker's
   "<=3 cards played" checks the TOTAL cards played (including non-scoring fillers), not the scoring-card count;
   The Psychic forces exactly 5 cards played, which always exceeds 3, so Half Joker can never fire under The
   Psychic regardless of hand shape.
2. Ante 2 Small Blind: PAIR K with 3 fillers, est 1980 vs actual 180 (91% miss). Same root cause outside any
   boss constraint — `advise`'s filler suggestions for Half Joker hands do not account for the joker's own
   <=3-total-cards condition, so its listed fillers silently break the very bonus the est assumed.
3. Ante 2 Small Blind (corrected): HIGH 1 alone (no fillers), est 945 vs actual 945 — exact match, confirming
   the fix (omit fillers entirely whenever Half Joker's bonus is load-bearing and the hand is <=3 cards without
   them).
4. Ante 3 Big Blind: FLUSH est 3195 vs actual 2556 (20% under, borderline) — attributable to Misprint's random
   roll landing below the 11-placeholder `hands`/`advise` uses for its estimate, not a bug.

**Playbook suggestions:**
1. Add an explicit rule under section 4/7: "If you hold a card-count-conditional joker (Half Joker, or similar
   '<=N cards played' effects), NEVER take `advise`'s listed fillers — they count toward the same total the
   joker checks, and will silently zero out the bonus the est already assumed. Play the minimal scoring set
   alone whenever such a joker's bonus is load-bearing, and re-run `hands` to see the est without the assumed
   filler play if unsure."
2. Note explicitly that The Psychic (forced 5 cards) makes any "<=3 cards played" joker (Half Joker and similar)
   worthless for that boss round — plan around it (sell it beforehand, or accept the loss of that bonus) rather
   than assuming `est` already reflects the interaction.
3. Section 6 (bosses) should add The Plant explicitly to the list of bosses where `advise`'s "keep face singles"
   guidance must be overridden: when ALL face cards are debuffed for the round, kept face singles are dead
   weight (zero chips, zero Scary-Face/Sock-and-Buskin bonus) and should be discarded immediately in favor of
   rebuilding around non-face ranks.
