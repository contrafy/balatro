## Post-mortem
- Binding constraint: misplay at The Mouth (one hand type per round). The first hand played locks the type for the whole round; I locked Full House, a type I could not repeat, with no discards left and 2 hands to go, and relied on a single hand clearing 2000 with a conservative est of 2143 while Misprint (0-23 mult) can roll 0 (it did: 94 x 6 x 1.9 = 1071, i.e. half the estimate's neighbourhood). Strategically the build (Burnt Joker + Misprint + Delayed Gratification + Ramen, 3 hand types at L1-L2, $5) was also far too weak for ante 3 (3200/4800/6400).
- Decisions I would change:
  1. The Mouth: with 2 discards available I spent BOTH before the first play (one to level Pair via Burnt, one to chase a Full House). I should have kept the Pair type as the locked type (pairs appear in almost every 8-card hand: three Pair L2 hands ~870-1300 each = clears) or at least kept the last discard until after the first hand to retain flexibility.
  2. Never lock a Mouth type that needs a rare draw. A one-hand clear that depends on Misprint's roll is a coin flip; the fallback after a roll of 0 was nothing.
  3. Burnt Joker eternal as first pick was OK (it levelled Pair), but Delayed Gratification (Judgement result) conflicts with it and with discards.
- Reusable rules:
  * If the boss is The Mouth, pick the locked type by repeatability: Pair (or your leveled pair-family type) over Full House/Flush/Straight; play 3 hands of it. Only lock a rare type when its est with Misprint at 0 mult still clears the whole requirement.
  * If a random-mult joker (Misprint) is in the build, judge a one-hand clear by est at mult 0, not the advisor's conservative 6.
  * If The Mouth locked a type and you have 0 discards, you cannot recover: keep at least one discard until the first hand is played.
  * Judgement from the shop ($3, buyuse) gives a sticker-free joker: take it when you own fewer than 4 jokers (advisor scored it 12, below LEAVE).
  * Ramen (x2, -0.01/discarded card) is worth $1 + rent at ante 2 even though shopadvise says AVOID: Flush went 1560 est -> 3744 actual.
