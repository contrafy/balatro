# Run BLFU9X3V (port 7780) - LOSS at ante 1 Big Blind (stake 3, Green Stake)

Ante 1: Small Blind 300 tag=Charm, Big 450 tag=Juggle, Boss The Head 600. Start $4.
- Small Blind: no jokers yet. Two Pair (8s+4s) L1 est 88 -> actual 88, then a lucky J-T-9-8-7 Straight est 296 -> actual
  296 finished it in 2 hands (scored 384/300). Cashed out, $5 in shop.
- Shop (ante 1): $5. Bought Even Steven $4 (unconditional +4 mult per even card, floor joker per playbook). Left $1,
  nothing else affordable; shopadvise said LEAVE. endshop.
- Big Blind (450, jokers: Even Steven 1/6): sequenced pair-family hands per advise throughout, zero discards left after
  hand 3 of 3 discards used (round used all 3 discards to keep redrawing around Two Pair):
  - Hand 1: Two Pair AAKK, est 124 -> actual 124.
  - Hand 2: Two Pair Q7 (kept the 55 pair alive for the final forced hand per FINAL-HAND AWARENESS), est 108 -> actual 108.
  - Hand 3 (forced, 0 discards, 0 hands after): only pairs available were JJ and 55, no straight/flush/full house
    possible in the dealt 8 cards. Two Pair J5, est 100 -> actual 100. Total 332/450. Blind lost, run over.

Post-mortem: binding constraint was BOTH luck and pacing. With only one floor joker (Even Steven, +4 mult/even card)
and hands leveled at L1, three Two Pair hands (88+124+108+100 across two blinds) is close to the ceiling of an
unleveled pair engine at stake 3 -- the ante-1 Big Blind (450) needed ~150/hand average and the deck only produced
100-124. The single joker bought was a flat-mult joker but a fairly weak one (+4 per even card, only helps if several
scoring cards are even) compared to Jolly (+8 flat) or Sly (+50 chips) which the playbook flags as stronger pair-shop
floor jokers. Shopadvise's own ranking (buy 2, Even Steven) was followed exactly, and no stronger joker was offered at
ante 1 (Business Card is a utility joker, not a scorer). The decision I would change: none of the taken actions
deviated from advise/shopadvise, and I did not have a viable alternative purchase (only $5 total, and the only other
shop item was a $4 utility joker + $10 voucher + packs at $4/$6, none of which the advisor ranked above Even Steven).
This looks like an unlucky opening deal (no Even Steven synergy hands) more than a misplay -- Green Stake's steeper
ante-1 Big Blind (450 vs 300 at White Stake) combined with a single +4-per-even-card joker simply wasn't enough.
No `est` vs actual deviation exceeded 20% in this run -- every play matched its estimate exactly (88/88, 296/296,
124/124, 108/108, 100/100).

Bridge issues: none. Every command succeeded on the first try; no retries needed.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Consider ranking Even Steven below Jolly/Sly/Odd Todd/Scholar when multiple floor jokers are offered in the same
   ante-1 shop, since its payout depends on card-rank luck (needs even-ranked cards in the scoring hand) rather than
   firing unconditionally on the hand type -- this run only had it offered as the single joker in the shop, so it
   could not be tested against an alternative, but future shopadvise data should watch for lower realized/est ratios
   on Even Steven/Odd Todd specifically vs Jolly/Sly.
2. This run reinforces the existing STAKE 3 ANTE 1-2 lesson: a single floor joker plus zero levels is a coin-flip
   against the Green Stake ante-1 Big Blind (450) -- consider whether shopadvise should weight rerolling harder at
   ante 1 when $5 total only affords ONE joker and no reroll, since with $5 you cannot both buy Even Steven AND
   reroll to see if a stronger floor joker (Jolly/Sly) is available underneath.
