Post-mortem: binding constraint was SCALING - three ante-1/2/3 shops produced zero xMult and zero Glass/Steel-worthy
  targets beyond two Bonus-chip enhancements (flat, not multiplicative); the only "joker package" was Raised Fist +
  Smiley Face + Hit the Road, none of which is a real xMult source, going into an ante-3 Big Blind that needed 3900.
  The decision I would change: at the ante-3 shop with $10, I bought two $4 Arcana packs back-to-back without a single
  Justice/Empress/xMult joker appearing; per playbook 7a ("if no reliable xMult has appeared by ante 3, take a

CLI/playbook problems found:
  1. `advise`'s filler selection for the forced last hand did not account for Raised Fist's "+2x lowest held card
     rank" mult: it played the highest-rank card (Ace) as a filler and left the lowest card (2H) unplayed, which is
     backwards for that joker (fillers should be the LOWEST-rank cards, keeping high cards held). This cost roughly
     half the possible score (480 vs 960 achievable) on the one hand where it mattered most. Playbook/CLI should special
     case "held-card-rank" jokers (Raised Fist and similar) in filler selection: play your lowest cards as fillers, not
     your highest, when such a joker is active.
  2. Confirmed again (per ante-1 note): `advise`'s pre-play est line for a Raised-Fist-affected hand under-predicts vs
     the `play` command's own est line, which is close to actual (within ~10%). Trust the `play` line's est over
     `advise`'s pacing-stage est when Raised Fist is on the board.
Playbook suggestions:
  1. Add a filler rule for "lowest-held-card" jokers (Raised Fist): fillers must be your LOWEST-ranked spare cards,
     not simply "junk cards that don't change the type" - the current rule (section 4 step 5) is silent on card RANK
     for fillers, which is fine for most jokers but actively wrong for Raised Fist.
  2. Reinforce the ante-3 rule from section 5/7a with a harder floor: two flat-enhancement Arcana packs in the same
     ante-3 shop with no xMult/rescue-tarot check in between is a trap; `shopadvise` should demote a second
     enhancement-only Arcana pack once a rescue tarot is affordable and none is held.
