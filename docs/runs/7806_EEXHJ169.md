## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SUPPLY + MONEY at ante 1. Three shops (ante-1 Small, ante-1 Big, ante-1 boss) offered exactly one joker that scores
(foil Seltzer $8, at $5), one Eternal Red Card and no Buffoon pick with a scorer. Income was $4 (Big) + $5 (boss) with the Small
paying $0, so I entered ante 2 with zero scoring jokers and Trips L2 as the only upgrade. A jokerless deck makes 100-300 per hand;
1000 is out of reach (solver p_clear 0.05-0.07 from the first deal).

Decisions I would change / examine:
- Shop 2 (ante 1 Big, $4): Celestial pack -> Venus. In hindsight it was necessary: the Pillar was cleared 612/600 and the Trips
  L2 hand scored 290 vs 126 at L1. Holding the $4 instead would have given $10 at the ante-2 shop (Seltzer $8 affordable) but
  very likely lost the Pillar first. No better line visible.
- Buffoon pick: To Do List never paid (its hand was High Card, and every High Card play would have cost a needed hand). Hit the Road
  {RENTAL} at $0 money would have charged $3 per round with no Jack discards planned. Neither scored; the pack was the gamble.
- Ante-2 shop: no reroll ($5 -> $0 cannot buy what it reveals). Correct by the rules; the run was already lost on supply.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF you are jokerless after the ante-1 Big shop and the boss projects under 1.0x, THEN a $4 Celestial pack for any pair-family
  level (Venus/Mercury/Uranus/Earth) is worth more than saving for the next shop: Trips L2 turned a 126 hand into 290 and the boss
  was cleared by 12 points.
- IF the Pillar is the boss, THEN play its debuffed cards as fillers on your first hand: it clears them out of the hand at no cost
  and draws live cards (QD TC dumped with Trips 444, the next draw made a Straight).
- IF a jokerless Big Blind is down to one hand with a 4-card gutshot vs a 4-card open-ended draw over fewer cards, THEN compute
  outs x draws: 4 outs over 4 draws (45%) and 8 outs over 2 draws (47%) are a tie; take the line that also keeps flush backdoors.
