## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING, not misplay or bad luck. The run never found a single xMult joker across 17 rounds and ~15 shop visits (matches playbook 7a's "1 in 45 shops" base rate, just unlucky this run). All damage was done by flat +mult/+chips jokers (Green, Crazy [Straight-only], Abstract, Blue, Hanging Chad) plus hand levels (Straight L5, Two Pair L3, Pair L3). That combination cleanly cleared every blind through ante 5 with 1.3x-3x margins, but ante 6's Big Blind (30000, more than double ante 5's Big Blind of 16500) outpaced flat scaling: even two decent Two Pair hands (7616+7104=14720) plus the best final hand (6592) totaled only 21312, a 29% shortfall.

Decision I would change: at ante 6's first shop, when shopadvise flagged 0 xMult jokers and recommended a reroll, I should have rerolled more aggressively earlier (ante 4-5, when I had bigger cash cushions of $15-20) rather than only starting the xMult hunt at ante 6 with just $11-17 in hand. The single reroll at ante 6 left only $6, not enough to keep hunting. Playbook already says "if you own no xMult joker... and have $15+ after the reroll, REROLL until one appears" - I had $16 and $15 available after Hermit/Temperance windfalls in ante 3 and ante 5 but spent it on Celestial/Arcana packs instead of rerolling, since shopadvise did not flag the xMult danger until ante 6. In hindsight, once ante 4 hit "0 xMult" with $15+ cash, I should have rerolled proactively even without an explicit REROLL advice line, per playbook 7a's "zero xMult at ante 5 loses" warning.

Also worth noting: two single-suit jokers (Lusty Joker/Hearts, Gluttonous Joker/Clubs) appeared multiple times across ante 1, 5, and 6 shops and were correctly skipped by shopadvise/my own judgment each time - the run never had a suit-based deck, so these were always dead weight, consistent with playbook guidance.
