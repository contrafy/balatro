## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING, exposed by The Needle. Final engine: Odd Todd (holo) + Half Joker + Supernova{E} + Hanging
Chad{E} + polychrome Swashbuckler{E} (x1.5) + The Duo{E} (x2), Pair L3. A good odd/Ace pair made 37-53k, i.e. ~3x the
ante-5 boss per round, but The Needle needs 60000 in ONE hand: only a Mult-Ace trips (AAA, ~63k) reached it. The Pair
level never got above L3 (3 Celestial packs offered no Mercury; Venus x3 instead), and the 2nd xMult (Duo) came at ante 5.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 6 Small: I played it and skipped the $23 Garbage tag. Playing it bought one extra shop (Empress) but the Needle
   was the real gate. Skipping the Small (+$23) and arriving at the Big shop with ~$40 would have funded 5-6 rerolls for
   Mercury x2 / a 2nd Death / Mr. Bones / Luchador before the Needle. [INFERENCE] With Big 90k cleared anyway at 98k, the
   skip would have cost little.
2. Ante 5 shop: Gift Card ($6) with polychrome Swashbuckler is a growth engine (+$1 sell value per joker per round =
   ~+5 Swashbuckler mult per round, x1.5 x2). SIM ignores the growth (-0.14). Over 6 rounds it would have been ~+30 mult
   (+40%) by the Needle, enough for Pair AA ~70k. I would buy it, selling Banner, once Duo/Swashbuckler are in place.
3. The Needle dig: I kept Mult AC + 2 fodder and discarded 5 for a second Ace (p~0.35). Correct line, it missed; the
   real mistake was not having a single hand >= 60k BEFORE the boss shop ran dry (Mercury not bought in ante 4 shops).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the boss is The Needle, THEN at the first shop of that ante compute your best realistic ONE hand (`hands` on a good
  draw) vs the need; if below, everything goes to a single-hand fix: main-hand planets, a 2nd xMult, Mult/Glass on your
  best rank, Death x2, Mr. Bones/Luchador. A 3-hand-round SCORE CHECK of 0.9-1.0 is meaningless vs a 1-hand boss.
- IF a Small/Big Blind offers a Garbage tag ($1 per unused discard, often $20+ at ante 5-6) AND the ante has a 1-hand
  boss you cannot yet beat, THEN skip that Small to fund rerolls in the next shop.
- IF you own Swashbuckler (esp. polychrome), THEN Gift Card is a growth +mult (~+5 per round for 5 other jokers): buy it
  over a flat chips joker from ante 4 on.
- IF you own Hanging Chad + Odd Todd/Fibonacci, THEN Aces are your best rank: Empress (Mult) on two Aces turned Pair AA
  from 40k into 51k; lead with the Mult Ace (Chad retriggers it twice = +12 mult).
- IF Riff-raff comes perishable/rental from an ante-1 Buffoon pack, THEN take it, select Big AND Boss with it (4 free
  commons: here Misprint, holo Odd Todd, Banner, Half Joker), and sell it before the next round-end rent.
- Death direction: move the copy SOURCE to the right end first (`move hand 1 8`), then `use N i 8`.
