## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING through a single hand-gated xMult. The Trio (x3 on Trips/FH/Quads) was the only multiplier; every
other source was flat (+12 straight-only, Fibonacci +8 per A/2/3/5/8, Gros Michel +15). Without the x3 the best hand was
~1450, so each round past ante 2 was "hit trips/FH or lose": the Big Blind hit on the last draw cycle, The Arm missed after
16 drawn cards. Money was $0 at every shop from ante 2 on (two skipped Small blinds, every dollar spent on a joker), so there
was never a reroll budget or rescue tarot.

Decisions I would change:
- Ante 3 Big shop ($5): Gros Michel was fine, but the real hole was having no way to raise the NON-trips floor. A Strength/Death
  in the consumable slot ($3) would have converted a 9 or 3 on the last hand of The Arm (Strength on an 8 -> T no; Death 8->9
  copy: yes). With $5, Death ($3, if offered) beats +15 mult when the build is a draw-dependent x3. It was not offered here,
  so the real fix is earlier: keep $3-5 instead of going to $0 three shops in a row.
- Ante 2 Big shop: $8 Fibonacci left $0. Fine as a permanent scorer, but its +8s only multiply on A/2/3/5/8 hands; Mad/Even
  Steven were cheaper and I passed them. Neutral.
- Under The Arm I could not find a line that reached 6400 without the FH; the dig itself was correct (~75%), it missed.

What worked:
- Open-ended straight draw (keep 3-4-5-6, 8 outs) over advise's inside draw: hit immediately, 800 vs 450.
- D6 tag skip at ante 2 (wallet $4): free reroll + $1 reroll found Fibonacci for $9 total spend instead of $15.
- Meteor tag skip at ante 3 ($0 wallet): 2 free levels + saved a Trio perish tick.
- Cycling a junk 4-card play while holding AA33 (Big 4800): drew the third Ace, FH 10428.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your only xMult is hand-gated (The Trio/Duo/Family), THEN treat every blind as a draw: count outs before the round, keep
  BOTH discards for the dig, and buy a rescue tarot (Death/Strength) before any flat +mult joker - the tarot adds outs, the
  +mult adds nothing when the gate misses.
- IF you hold two pair and need a Full House, THEN keep only the two pairs and cycle the other 4 cards (discard, then 4-card
  junk plays at a L1 type): each cycle is ~35-43% with 4 outs; 3 cycles ~75%.
- IF the boss is The Arm, THEN L1 hands are immune (cannot drop below L1) and a leveled hand loses a level BEFORE scoring
  [INFERENCE from est vs requirement, not measured]: plan the boss around L1 Full House/Two Pair, not the L2 Trips.
- IF Popcorn is offered as Perishable, THEN ignore the sticker: it decays to 0 in 5 rounds anyway.
- IF the Small Blind tag is D6 and the next shop needs rerolls for a first scorer, THEN skip: rerolls start at $0.
