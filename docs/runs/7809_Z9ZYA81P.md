## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: money at ante 1-2, then The Wall (x2 = 4000) as the ante-2 boss. I left ante 1 with $0-3 because a
foil Cartomancer rental cost $3 every round ($9 over 3 rounds), and Supernova{ETERNAL} took the last $5. At the first
ante-2 shop Wily $4 and Dusk $5 were on offer with $3 in hand. My scorers at The Wall were Supernova (+2..+4 mult),
foil Cartomancer (+50 chips) and a free Baron (x1.5 per held King). That made ~850-1300 per hand against 1333 needed
per hand. The deciding draw: the final hand held 3 Kings (x3.375) but had no pair outside them.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop 1: Buffoon pack with $6 and 0 jokers was fine, but taking a RENTAL with $2 left meant every later round
   ended near $0. Superposition was useless, so the realistic alternative was skipping the pack and keeping $6 for the
   next shop's $4-5 scorer. The tarots Cartomancer gave (Empress, Strength, High Priestess, World) did win the ante-1
   Big and ante-2 Big blinds, so this was close. Taking it and then SELLING it after the ante-1 boss (when Supernova
   was bought) would have saved $6 of rent and bought Wily or Dusk at ante 2.
2. Ante 2 Big shop ($5, Wall next): Arcana $4 -> Judgement -> Riff-raff was a gamble that produced Square Joker + Splash
   (about +10%). Planet/Arcana did not matter much. The real problem was arriving with $5.
3. Wall, first discard: I broke 88 to keep AA + KH. Keeping AA 88 + KH and discarding 3 kept the Full House draw
   (8 outs instead of 2). The solver rated both ~0.49. With Baron in play, the Kings in the deck were the real outs
   either way.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you buy your first permanent scorer at ante 1 AND own a flat/utility RENTAL bridge, THEN sell the rental at the next
  shop or blind select. $3/round at ante 1-2 is a whole joker by the ante-2 shop.
- IF The Wall is the ante-2 boss (4000), THEN treat ante 2 as an EMERGENCY ante: you need ~1350 per hand. That means
  2+ real scorers or a multiplier. Count dollars for it from the ante-1 Big Blind shop onward, with no packs/planets
  that do not score.
- IF you own Baron, THEN at the Wall-type boss keep every King you draw HELD and build pairs from the other ranks. Strength
  on Queens (Q -> K) and Death copying a King are the best tarot uses.
- IF Splash is owned, THEN pad every Pair/Trips with the highest-chip non-held cards (+20-30 chips per hand).
