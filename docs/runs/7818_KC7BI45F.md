## Post-mortem

### Binding constraint
- Scoring power too low for the ante 4 boss pace. Best hand types were Pair/Three of a Kind at about 3-6k. No xMult was ever bought. Gold target at ante 4 was 9000/13500/18000, and the Big (13500) was reached only through Mr. Bones' 25% save.
- Money was $0 to $6 from ante 3 onward. Astronomer rent ($3/round) plus skipping Small 3200 for a tag left almost no cash for the ante 4 xMult/scorer buys.
- Jokers: Half Joker and Crazy Joker are conditional (Half Joker never fires on the forced 5-card Psychic). Blue Joker is chips-only. Zero xMult by ante 4 is the main gap (advisor GOLD CHECK: target 2 xMult by end of ante 5).

### Decisions I would change
1. Ante 3 Small skip (3200) for a Voucher Tag left $0. Taking the Small would have kept about $3-5 and one more scorer/planet. Rule: do not skip the Small at ante 3 with money under $5.
2. Ante 4 shop 1 ($6): I declined Wasteful/Arcana and the Vampire swap. Arcana ($4) and Vampire were not SIM-rated, but money stayed at $6 and bought nothing scoring. With the Psychic at 18000 and Big at 13500, buy the cheapest scorer or xMult before endshop.
3. Psychic boss: buy a Luchador, Chicot, or Director's Cut before ante 4 Big (advisor BOSS PREP listed them). I had $3 at the Big and $0 at the boss and bought none.
4. Mr. Bones saved the Big at 25% of 13500 (3375 threshold) and self-destructed. It was already spent by the Psychic, so the boss had no save. Keep it for a boss you can reach 25% of, and do not expect it to cover two blinds.

### Concrete reusable rules (if-then)
- IF the boss forces 5-card plays (The Psychic) and your build is Half Joker / Crazy Joker (conditional, not flat), THEN buy/hold an anti-Psychic (Luchador, Chicot, Director's Cut) before the boss, or treat the boss as lost.
- IF money < $5 at ante 3 blind select, THEN do not skip the Small for a tag (the tag saves $0 today and costs score tomorrow).
- IF the free Celestial pack offers 5 planets and your main hand has a L2+ trips/straight, THEN take the level of that hand; a planet for a hand you never play is wasted.
- IF your typical hand is under 1/3 of the next boss (ante 4: 18000), THEN the next shop must buy an xMult or the cheapest scorer, regardless of the money floor.
- IF Mr. Bones is in play and the Big is projected unreachable, THEN spend the last hands to pass 25% of the blind (it self-destructs after saving). It saved the Big here at 11906 vs 3375 needed.

- SIM "clears next 3 blinds" showed 0.00-0.23 even after the Big success, so the boss-prep ratio is not calibrated against the Psychic 18000 target.
