Post-mortem: binding constraint was LUCK, not misplay - the Small Blind at ante 4 (need 5000, a 66% jump from ante 3's 4000) drained all 3 discards and both consumables (Strength used ante 3, both Chariot copies used ante 3/4) chasing an unleveled Two Pair/pair-family hand, then hand 2 drew a genuinely pairless, straight-less, flush-less 8-card spread with zero discards left to fix it, cascading into a forced High Card and then a forced weak Pair on the final hand. The jokers (Wily +100 chips if Trips, Jolly +8 mult if Pair, Walkie Talkie +10chips+4mult per 10/4, Photograph x2 first face, Egg econ, dead Joker Stencil) and levels (Full House L2, Three of a Kind L3) were reasonable for ante 3 but had no answer for a hand with literally no scoring structure and no discards/consumables in reserve. The one decision I would change: at ante 3-4 I spent both Arcana-pack picks and the Fool copies on card enhancements (Chariot/Steel) applied to single physical cards, which have low probability of being drawn together with their pair-partners in any given hand; a Strength or Justice held UNUSED as a true rescue tarot (bought directly from a shop tarot slot, not consumed inside a pack) going into ante 4 would have let me rank up or Glass-ify a dead card on the pairless hand 2, likely turning it into a real pair and avoiding the discard/consumable drought that doomed hand 3. I would prioritize keeping at least one rescue tarot in reserve at all times from ante 3 on, even at the cost of a pack pick that "does nothing yet."
Bridge issues: none. All commands executed as expected; `est` matched actual score within a few percent on every hand played (no >20% deviation observed). Playbook suggestions:
1. Clarify that Strength/Death/Justice/Chariot picked FROM A PACK cannot be banked unused - they must be targeted with `sel` and applied immediately at pick time, unlike a shop `buy` of the same tarot which does bank into a consumable slot. The current playbook wording ("bank it from your first Arcana pack instead of spending every pick immediately") is misleading and should be corrected to say the bank must come from a direct shop buy.
2. Add an explicit ante-4+ rule: never let both consumable slots go to 0 with no rescue tarot in reserve, even if it means skipping a scaling pick (Chariot/Justice) that shift a shop's item into "apply now" territory. A dead hand with 0 discards and 0 consumables is unrecoverable by definition.
3. Add a note that Chariot/Justice/Empress enhancements applied to a single physical non-paired filler card are low-value compared to keeping one Strength/Death/Justice unused as a rescue tool for a genuinely dead hand - the enhancement only helps if that exact card is drawn into a scoring combination, whereas a held rescue tarot can act on whatever 8 cards you are actually dealt.
