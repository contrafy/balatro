## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING, specifically a single xMult source (Photograph, bought ante 4 shop 2, one hand before
  the loss) arriving too late and no card-enhancement (Glass/Steel) ever landing on more than one card (one Steel
  KD from ante 3). The Full House fallback (L2) never leveled further and was too weak to save the final hand.
  The Tooth's per-card money tax combined with an already-low money curve (never above $9 all game) meant zero
  buying power going into ante 4's boss; by the final hand there were 0 discards and only a King-Nine two-pair
  in hand, no route to 4024 more points.
Decision I would change: at the ante 4 shop after clearing Big Blind, spend the last $6 on Photograph was correct
  (only xMult available), but the earlier ante 3-4 shops should have prioritized economy (skipped the second
  Temperance/Chariot detour) to reach $15+ money for a reroll hunting a second xMult or a stronger fallback-hand
  planet before entering The Tooth; entering a -$1-per-card boss at $1 cash left zero margin for error.
Bridge issues: none - every command behaved as documented. `advise`'s `est` consistently UNDER-estimated actual
  scores by 29-57% throughout the run (Hiker's permanent per-card chip growth is not reflected in `est`'s
  calculation), and once (ante 2 boss, The Club) it also produced a WRONG est direction: it estimated 2070 for a
  Flush made entirely of debuffed Club-suit cards (as if the debuffed cards' chip values still counted), which
  should have been closer to base-only (~1170); the actual score (2590) exceeded even that, so the debuff may not
  zero out card chips the way playbook section 4.7 describes, or Hiker's permanent bonus survives debuff. Negative
  money is allowed by the bridge/game (went to $-14) without any command failing.
Playbook suggestions:
  1. Section 3/4.7 "debuffed card scores nothing" should be re-verified against Hiker-style permanent-chip jokers;
     this run's actual scores under The Club (debuffed clubs) and generally (Hiker present) ran 30-57% and once
     ~5x above the naive base-only calculation, suggesting `advise`'s `est` needs a documented correction factor
     when a permanent per-card-chip joker (Hiker) is in play, rather than trusting `est` at face value near a need
     threshold.
  2. Add an explicit rule for -$-per-card bosses (The Tooth, The Ox): check money BEFORE selecting that blind;
     if money is under ~$10, consider selling a non-engine joker or consumable first, since the boss taxes every
     played card even into negative balance with no safety floor mentioned in section 6.
  3. This run never found a second xMult and only converted one enhancement card (Chariot->Steel) despite buying
     three Arcana/Celestial packs across antes 2-4; the single-xMult-source lesson from section 7a held (Photograph
     arrived ante 4, one blind before the loss) - reinforce "reroll for xMult once money >= $15" more aggressively
     even at ante 3-4 when only flat-mult/level scaling has been found so far.
