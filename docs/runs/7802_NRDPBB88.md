## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: a single hand-gated multiplier. From ante 4 every blind was cleared by exactly one Trips/Full House
hand (Blueprint copying The Trio = x9, ~50-110k); any other hand scored 5-11k (1/9 of it). At ante 6 the Small (60000)
was unreachable without a Trips/FH, so the round was one draw sequence (4 tries at 6 outs, ~85% cumulative by my count)
and it missed. Ramen (x2 always-on) came at ante 5 but the flat base (Fist, Abstract, holo Sly) was ~45 mult, L1 FH.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 6 shop ($33): Venus (Celestial) + Palette ($14). Palette did help the dig, but $15+ of rerolls hunting an
   always-on xMult (Cavendish, Baron-free, Hologram...) would have raised the non-Trips hands, which were the failure case.
   SCORE CHECK said 0.90 this ante / 0.46 next: that was the signal the build was short.
2. Ante 5 Small shop ($17) and ante 4 ($13): kept money for interest instead of rerolling/Buffoon packs; with a
   one-gate engine, a second always-on multiplier was worth more than $3 interest.
3. FH level: I never bought Earth (FH was my most played type from ante 4). Trips/FH were L1-L2 at ante 6.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your only multiplier is hand-gated (The Trio/Family/Order, incl. a Blueprint/Brainstorm copy of it), THEN every
  non-gated hand scores ~1/3 (1/9 with a copy): from ante 5 buy an ALWAYS-ON xMult before planets/vouchers, and count
  P(make the gated type this round) as your P(clear) when the gated hand is the only one reaching need.
- IF you own Blueprint and a hand-gated xMult, THEN re-aim Blueprint per hand with `order` (free, between plays): left of
  The Trio when you hold Trips/FH, left of a pair joker (Sly) otherwise. It was worth +50-60% on pair hands (6032->8932).
- IF digging for a Full House with 0-1 discards left, THEN keep ALL made pairs (3 pairs = 6 outs) and discard/play only
  the unpaired cards: 6 outs x 4 draws (47-61%) beats 4 outs x 5 draws (41-54%).
- IF the Big Blind's tag is Buffoon at ante 3-4 and your typical hand >= boss/2, THEN skipping the Big for the Mega
  Buffoon is good: here it found Blueprint (+x3) and kept the deck clean for The Pillar.
