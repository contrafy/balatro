## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. Zero xMult was ever offered in 13 shop views (6 free Chaos rerolls + 4 paid rerolls
included) and every real scorer I owned was a perishable or decaying bridge (Sock and Buskin, Popcorn, Ice Cream, Half
Joker, Abstract, Swashbuckler). The only permanent scorers were suit jokers (Wrathful, Gluttonous). The run was a chain
of bridges that each carried exactly one ante; at ante 5 the bridges and the blind curve crossed (24.4k vs 25k).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1-2 joker picks: Mega Buffoon (Wrathful/Drunkard/Fortune Teller{E}/Trading Card) was fine, but Drunkard and Chaos
   the Clown took 2 of 6 slots for 2 antes and scored nothing. Chaos's free rerolls (6 views) found no xMult; a
   permanent +mult (e.g. keeping Gluttonous AND buying Lusty $5 at ante 3) would have been worth more by ante 5.
2. Ante 4 shop 2 ($18): I passed the $4 Arcana and kept $16 for an ante-5 xMult that never showed. The ante-5 Small
   was lost by 584: one Hierophant (+30 chips on a pair card) or a 2nd Mercury earlier would have cleared it. When the
   next ante's Small is projected at ~1.0x, MONEY DOES NOT SCORE applies one shop earlier than I applied it.
3. Ante 2 Small skip (broke, Investment tag) paid $25 and was right; but at ante 3 I spent $11 of it on two paid rerolls
   that found only suit jokers. Rerolls at ante 3 with <$30 rarely find an xMult (matches GOLD_ANALYSIS rule 7).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF every scorer you own is perishable/decaying (Popcorn, Ice Cream, Half Joker, Abstract{P}, Swashbuckler{P}), THEN
  count your ante-N+1 score WITHOUT them in every shop (shopadvise does: 4975 vs 25000 here) and spend each shop on the
  first PERMANENT +mult/xMult, even a weak one, before another bridge.
- IF Half Joker is your main mult, THEN 1-card High Cards score ~70% of a Pair: use them to bank the last chunk of a
  blind and keep pairs for big hands (ante 4 Manacle cleared with High KS 6360 + High TS 6095).
- IF the projected round for the next blind is <= 1.05x (shopadvise BOSS WARNING / "round reaches ~X"), THEN buy any
  +score item in the shop (planet for the main hand, Hierophant on a main-rank card) instead of saving for an xMult that
  may not appear; a $3-4 item was the 584-point difference here.
- IF you are broke (< $5) at the ante-2 Small with an Investment tag on it, THEN skip it: +$25 after the boss funded the
  whole ante-3 shop (worked here).
