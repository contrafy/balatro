Post-mortem:
- Binding constraint: luck + ante-1 power. 600 boss with a Two-Pair-only joker and L1 hands needs a Full House or a Photograph-face two pair; I never saw one in 3 hands/2 discards.
- Decisions I would change: (1) First discard of the boss: I had AH TH 8H 3H + 9C debuffed in hand = four hearts to a flush (est ~300 plus face singles for Photograph) and discarded them to chase a second
  pair with a 3-pair (two pairs are only ~20% from a single pair + 5 draws). With the junk-cycle I later made the exact same flush gamble but with only 1 draw instead of 2 discards + draws. With 2 discards a 4-heart flush is ~85%; I
  should have kept the 4 hearts at the first discard (flush with Photograph-face ~500-600 would nearly clear the boss alone with one more hand). (2) Spent a HAND (not a discard) on the junk-cycle: it zeroed my last chance; instead playing straight/pair for 200 would not have won either, so the real mistake was step 1.
  (3) Played Two Pair 360 first with 2 hands left and no discards: fine, but then the remaining 240 needed another made hand from 8 random cards.
- Reusable rules (if-then):
  1. Ante-1 boss, you own a Two-Pair-gated joker but the hand has 4 cards of one suit and 2 discards left: KEEP THE 4 SUITED CARDS (flush ~85% over two discards, est 300+) over a single pair (two pair ~20% per draw).
     Flush L1 is worth ~300 with no jokers and x2 with Photograph if a face is among the flush cards.
  2. Perishable+Mad/Photograph combos at ante 1 are fine to buy (they cleared Small and Big in 1 and 2 hands), but a Two-Pair-only joker needs the discards spent on Two Pair, not on flush; plan the keep decision by outs: count outs for each target before discarding.
  3. A debuffed-suit boss (Club) removes ~25% of dealt cards: pairs/two pair are less likely; flushes in the OTHER suits are relatively better; prefer flush/straight chases of non-debuffed suits.
  4. Do not spend a play to cycle cards when the remaining plays cannot add up: with 1 hand left and need 229, only a single-card draw chance exists (78%): fine, but it should have been done with discards earlier.
