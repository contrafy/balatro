Post-mortem: binding constraint was BOTH (a) the Loyalty Card x4 estimate repeatedly failing to materialize in actual score under The Hook (two hands in a row estimated a big x4 hit and delivered ~1/5 of that), and (b) never finding a rescue tarot (Death/Strength/Justice) in any ante-3/4 shop despite `shopadvise` flagging it every single shop - the shop simply never offered one in its card row. Decision I would change: at ante 3, when Blueprint appeared for $10 with $39 cash and jokers full, I followed shopadvise's LEAVE instead of proposing a sell-then-buy (Blueprint could have copied Loyalty Card or Ancient Joker's xMult, doubling scaling well before The Hook); shopadvise had no sell-then-buy suggestion there and I did not override it, but in hindsight paying to unlock Blueprint earlier (selling Odd Todd, the weakest rank-gated joker) may have prevented the Hook loss.
Bridge issues: `advise`/`hands` printed "Loyalty Card fires on THIS hand (x4)" for two consecutive hands under The Hook boss (ante 4), and both times the actual score came in far below the est that assumed the x4 fired (est 8256 vs actual 1548, and a similar est/actual gap on the first Loyalty Card note that hand). This looks like either (1) The Hook's forced random discards desync the hand-played counter Loyalty Card uses to time its "every N hands" trigger, making the CLI's note wrong, or (2) the est calculation applies the x4 optimistically without confirming it actually fired that specific hand. No other ACTION FAILED / retry errors seen this run; `sel`/`pick`/`sort` all behaved as documented.
Playbook suggestions:
1. Add a note to section 6 (The Hook) or 7a: Loyalty Card (and possibly other "every N hands" xMult jokers) should not be trusted against `est`/notes under The Hook - the boss's random forced discards appear to desync the hand-count trigger, so treat the "fires on THIS hand" note as unconfirmed and do not rely on it to clear a blind.
2. Add an explicit rule to section 5: `shopadvise`'s ORDER advice should also fire when a joker slot is filled by buying (not just after a sell), since Odd Todd sat to the right of two xMult jokers for a full ante (ante 3 buy through ante 4 boss) without a warning; the run manually reordered with `order` only right before the final hand of the boss, too late to matter.
3. Consider: when `shopadvise` says LEAVE but jokers are full and cash is well above the interest floor (this run left $39-48 idle at ante 3-4 three separate times), it may be worth a rule that explicitly considers a sell-then-buy for a genuine xMult utility joker like Blueprint even when the ADVICE line does not propose it, since idle cash at ante 4+ with only conditional/growth xMult (Loyalty Card, Ancient Joker - both "unreliable" per shopadvise's own XMULT(conditional, unreliable) tag) is exactly the "under-scaled" state the tool itself warned about every shop this ante.
