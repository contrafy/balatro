## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no permanent scorer. In 8 shops I saw exactly two scoring jokers I could afford: Ramen{PERISHABLE}
(Buffoon pack, x2 for 5 rounds) and Gluttonous{ETERNAL} (+3 per club). Money was $0-7 the whole run (Red-stake $0
Small Blinds, $4 start), so the engine was "club flush with Gluttonous x Ramen" and it died with Ramen at ante 3. The
Flint halves the base hand (Flush L1 = 17x2), so a build whose mult comes from the hand level and card chips scores
~116 per flush there; only flat +mult (Shoot the Moon +13 per held Queen) and The Order x3 produced real numbers.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3: skipping the Big Blind (4800). It saved $3 rent and gave $3 (Economy), but the boss was The Flint, where only
   flat +mult jokers count, and the post-Big shop (~$12 with Golden) was my only chance to buy one. Lost the boss by 1375.
   With The Flint upcoming, the shop before it is worth more than the skip.
2. Ante 3 Jumbo Arcana: Judgement gave Golden Joker (economy, not a scorer). The Moon (3 cards -> clubs) would have
   fed Gluttonous and the flush every round; with a suit-gated +mult joker, deck-shaping toward that suit is a real
   scorer while a random joker is ~50% non-scoring.
3. Ante 2 Big skip for the Uncommon tag: the tag joker arrived as Flash Card{RENTAL} (junk). Neutral at $0 money,
   but it cost $4 and one shop.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the boss is The Flint AND your mult comes mostly from hand levels/card chips (no flat +mult joker), THEN do not skip
  the Big Blind before it: its shop is where you buy the flat +mult that The Flint does not halve.
- IF your only +mult joker is suit-gated (Gluttonous/Greedy/Lusty/Wrathful), THEN in Arcana packs take the suit tarot
  (Moon/Star/Sun/World) over Judgement: those jokers add mult per scoring card during the card phase, so a flush of
  that suit gets +15 mult and Glass/Steel on those cards multiply it.
- IF The Pillar is the boss AND you are not on pace on the second-to-last hand, THEN still play fillers to dig: losing
  the blind ends the run, a few debuffed cards at the boss do not (Pair TT + 2 fillers found the 5th club here).
- IF you hold 4 cards of a suit and one dig hand plus a discard remain, THEN dig with a play first (banks points, does not
  decay Ramen) and keep the discard for the miss.
