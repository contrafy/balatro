Post-mortem: binding constraint = misplay/pacing, not a single bad decision. Only 2 jokers by ante 2 (Loyalty
  Card, a conditional xMult that fires 1-in-6 hands, and Scholar, an Ace-only floor joker) meant no flat
  scaling on non-Ace hands; the ante-1 shops offered no unconditional Pair-triggered joker (Jolly/Sly/Joker+4)
  and money was too tight ($4-5 per shop at stake-2's $0 Small Blind) to reroll for one without going to $0.
  The decision I would change: after the Full House (248/800, 2 hands 1 discard left, needing 552 more), the
  advise tool chose to keep the Pair Q and chase the straight through the K/J/T/9 draw for est 316 rather than
  playing the safer made Pair immediately and preserving more card variety for the final hand — the straight
  itself scored fine (316 actual, matched est), but it emptied the deck of exactly the ranks (9, 4) that would
  have completed a follow-up straight or paired the final 8-card hand, leaving an unsalvageable all-distinct-rank
  final hand with no consumable to rescue it. With only 2 jokers and zero rescue tarots by ante 2 (none held,
  none affordable in the shop), a single bad final draw is a run-ending event; this matches the playbook's
  "most losses are ante 1-2 with zero-to-few scoring jokers" data point.
Bridge issues: none. All `play`/`disc`/`advise`/`shopadvise` calls behaved as documented; est matched actual
  score within a few points on every hand this run (best: Straight est316/act316; Quads est616/act616).
Playbook suggestions:
  1. Section 4/7b: note that keeping a "floor" made Pair while chasing a straight can strand the deck of the
     exact completing ranks for a LATER hand in the same round when discards are already exhausted — `advise`
     should weigh remaining-deck rank availability, not just the current hand's est, when 0 discards will remain.
  2. Section 7b (RESCUE TAROT) already recommends holding one from ante 3; this run lost at ante 2 before that
     threshold applied, but the same failure mode (final all-distinct-rank hand, no pair possible) can happen
     as early as ante 2 - consider extending the rescue-tarot-by-shop guidance down to ante 2 when a shop has one
     for $3 and the player holds fewer than 2 scoring jokers.
  3. Loyalty Card (x4 every 6 hands, bought at ante 1 shop when it was the only affordable joker) is explicitly
     flagged elsewhere in the playbook as an unreliable sole-xMult; confirmed again here as a weak floor joker
     for a zero-joker ante-1 shop - it does not score on a plain Pair, so `advise`'s WARNING about "no joker
     scores on a plain Pair" persisted the whole run. Consider whether shopadvise should rank a Buffoon pack
     gamble above Loyalty Card in a zero-scoring-joker shop, since Loyalty Card provides no floor benefit at all.
