## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling, caused by money. I never owned a reliable xMult: the only multiplier was Constellation
(x1.3 after 3 planets). Scorers were Odd Todd (chips only), Misprint{E} (+0..23 random), Supernova and, from the ante-4
skip, Hanging Chad. Money was $0-15 every shop (Small pays $0, no economy joker, every dollar went to packs), so every
ante-3/4 shop opened at $3-8 and the only xMult-shaped buy that showed up (holo Bull $9) was unaffordable.
Real scores: ~4-5k per good odd-card Two Pair at ante 3, ~5.6k at ante 4 vs 6000 per hand needed at the Wheel.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 shop ($15): rerolled ($5) and bought Constellation{E} ($6), leaving $4. Constellation reached only x1.3 because
   I could afford just 3 planets before ante 4; with $0-8 per shop a growth xMult that needs planets never grows.
   Keeping $15 (interest $3) and buying the next Buffoon/+mult would probably have scaled more [INFERENCE].
2. Ante 2 first shop: Mega Buffoon $8 at $7. Selling Odd Todd ($2) for 2 picks of 4 jokers was likely better than a
   chips-only joker that only fires on odd cards.
3. The Diet Cola Double Tag + Negative tag skip at ante 3: both Negative tags landed on junk (Mail-In Rebate{ETERNAL,RENTAL},
   Castle{RENTAL}), i.e. zero value. The skip itself was fine (shop would have had $3), but I counted on the tags.

What worked: ante 4 at ratio 0.15 and $5, skipping Small (Coupon) and Big (Top-up: 2 commons into 2 free slots)
scored 12619 at the boss vs the shop's 7410 projected round (Hanging Chad + odd cards: first odd card retriggered
twice adds ~80 chips with Odd Todd). Still 30% short.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF a Negative/Foil/Holo/Polychrome tag is pending, THEN expect it to land on whatever joker the next shop rolls first
  (here an eternal rental Mail-In Rebate and a rental Castle): never skip a blind or buy a Diet Cola for it when broke.
- IF your shops open at <= $8 at ante 2-3, THEN do not buy Constellation as your xMult plan: it needs ~1 planet per
  shop to reach x2 by ante 5; buy +mult jokers / Buffoon packs instead and hold interest.
- IF Misprint is your main +mult, THEN plan every hand at roll ~3 (est uses 6): its rolls of 0-5 cost 2 blinds' worth
  of margin here (Small 296 instead of ~740, Wheel 5605 vs est 7607).
- IF ante 4 SCORE CHECK < 0.2 with $5 and 2 free slots, THEN skip Small+Big for a Top-up/Buffoon tag: two free jokers
  before the only fight beat two blinds you would lose (12619 vs 7410 projected), but it does not save a run with no xMult.
- IF Hanging Chad + Odd Todd are owned, THEN put the highest odd card first in the scoring order (AS first: (11+31)x3).
