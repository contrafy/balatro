## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: MONEY -> SCALING. I never held more than $8 after ante 1. The Trio rental (-$3 x 8 rounds = $24) plus
the $0 Small Blinds kept every shop from ante 3 on at $0-5, so I could not buy a single real xMult, Celestial pack or
rescue tarot. The build (Blue + Odd Todd chips, Lusty, Shoot the Moon, Joker, Trio x3 conditional) peaked at ~17k per hand
on a Full House with one Queen held, about 1/3 of what the ante-5 boss needed per hand. 0 reliable xMults all run.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 Odd Todd{ETERNAL} as the second joker: a chips-only joker on mult 2 was a weak buy and the Eternal blocked the
   slot all run. Two chips jokers (Blue + Todd) and no mult until ante 3 meant every early hand relied on Straights/Flushes.
2. The Trio{RENTAL} at ante 2: it won 5 rounds (FH/Trips x3 were my only big hands) but cost $3 every round. Net it was
   probably right (no other mult at all), but the alternative was never available, and its rent turned every later shop
   into a $0-5 shop. I should have planned an exit: sell it once a permanent +mult/xMult replaced it — that never happened.
3. Skipping Big at ante 5 (for Orbital Pair) avoided a 37500 check but left the Goad (spades debuffed) as the only blind;
   neutral at best. The real loss was earlier: zero money at ante 4-5.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF you own Shoot the Moon (+13 mult per Queen HELD), THEN never discard or play a Queen; keep every Queen in hand and
  play around it. The rule-based ADVICE discarded the held Queen twice (ante 4 Big: `disc 1 2 3 4 5` including QH; ante 5
  Small: `disc 1 2 3 4 5` including QC); the SOLVER lines kept it and were 0.95-0.99 vs 0.73. Follow the solver there.
- IF The Mark (faces face-down) and you own Shoot the Moon, THEN hold the face-down cards: E[Queens] = 1 per 3 face-downs
  and a Pair 66 scored 2800 vs est 200 (two Queens hidden). The actual score of the first hand tells you how many Queens
  you hold (mult jump / 13); use it to plan the next hands.
- IF your only mult at ante 2 is a rental conditional xMult (The Trio), THEN Trips/Full House become your main hand:
  keep pairs+trips through discards and dig with High Card fillers when the solver says so (Flint: dig line 0.26 vs 0.03,
  then Trips 999 + FH 333JJ cleared 18000).
- IF money is $0-3 at ante 4+ and rent eats the blind reward, THEN a Small Blind skip for a tag that pays NOW (Orbital,
  Top-up) is worth more than the shop; the "take the shop" advice assumes you can buy something there.
