## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING, made worse by one misplay. The engine was Stuntman (+250 chips) x The Duo x2 x Seeing Double x2
(clubs) on Pair L7, with only flat/temporary mult (Half Joker{PERISH}, Abstract{PERISH}, Misprint{ETERNAL}, Supernova).
A club pair scored ~55-95k, a non-club pair half that. 9 shops at ante 4-7 with ~$60 of rerolls never showed a pair-usable
xMult (seen: Seeing Double, The Order, Hit the Road, Driver's License, Hologram, Glass Joker rental). Money was never the
constraint: four Hermits and three Temperances put me at $40-76 from ante 4 on, and most of it went into rerolls that found nothing.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 7 shop: I sold Supernova (+16 mult, +1 per Pair) for Hologram at x1 with no way to feed it. Supernova adds about 16/39
   = +40% to every Pair. That covers the 6k I lost the Big by (the AA and 88 hands alone would have made ~+40k). A growth xMult
   at x1 is worth zero until it is fed.
2. Misprint{ETERNAL} as the permanent replacement for Abstract: its 0-23 roll decided the run on the last hand (needed >=7,
   rolled ~2). A steady +mult (Walkie Talkie, Supernova kept) would have been better than a high-variance one.
3. Ante 5-6 rerolls (about $60 total, 11 rerolls) found nothing. Seed Money ($10 at $43-76 held) or more Celestial packs
   (Pair levels) would have paid more. Interest caps at $5, so idle money above $25 is worth nothing.
4. Hierophant (+30 chips on AS AD) and Empress/Magician on random cards were near-zero value. Hermit/Temperance were the right
   picks, but money was never short.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Seeing Double, THEN Lovers (Wild) on a card you pair often is a permanent x2: a Wild card counts as a Club, so any
  pair containing it fires (JD wild: est 24790, actual 49580). Moon/Cryptid on a club card also raise how often it fires.
- IF a forced/needed hand is one Strength away from a club pair (Seeing Double) or a face pair, THEN use the held Strength
  before the first play of the boss. Strength JH->QH turned Pair 33 (36k) into Pair QC QH (76k) at The Wall.
- IF you are about to sell a working +mult joker for a growth joker (Hologram, Constellation, Glass Joker) at x1, THEN do it
  only when the same shop can feed it (Standard pack for Hologram, Celestial for Constellation). Otherwise sell the
  perishable or keep the slot.
- IF a perishable scorer has <= 2 rounds left at ante 6+ AND the Small Blind pays $0, THEN skipping the Small keeps it alive
  through the Big and Boss (here Abstract covered The Head 120k).
- IF Half Joker is owned, THEN play pairs/trips with at most 1 filler (3 cards); Trips beats Pair only through card chips.
- IF Misprint must roll above its mean to clear a final hand, THEN treat the hand as ~50/50 and spend discards to find a
  non-random upgrade (mult/lucky/glass card in the pair) first.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- `hands`/`advise` est ignores Wild cards for Seeing Double (JD wild + JS: est 24790, actual 49580).
- `hands` picks the wrong pair partners for Seeing Double: with 4S 4H 4C in hand it listed "PAIR 4: 4S 4H est 24344" instead
  of 4S 4C (~48k with the club). It should prefer the club + other-suit combination.
- SOLVER/advise never consider a held Strength/Death that turns a card into a club pair (The Wall); the rule pick wanted to
  discard toward a hearts flush worth 7.7k.
- shopadvise at ante 7 ranked Hologram (XMULT growth) 80 and told me to sell Half Joker for it with no feeding source in the
  shop. An unfed growth xMult should be valued at its current x1.
- shopadvise SIM printed p_next 0.00 for every option at ante 7 (baseline included), so it could not rank anything.
- Ante 5 shopadvise said LEAVE with $31 while Abstract{PERISH} (+18 mult) could replace holo Hallucination (+10, its tarot
  effect dead with full consumable slots). The Wall was cleared with that swap.
- `s` at ante 5+ says "do not skip, the shop is worth more than a tag". It ignores that a $0 Small skip saves a perish tick
  on perishable scorers.
