## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING (zero xMult) + money $0 the whole run. Every joker was flat +mult/+chips, so per-hand score topped out near 9-10k at Pair L5 (~140 chips x ~68 mult). Ante 5 Big needs 12.5k/hand; ante 5 boss 16.7k/hand; ante 6 needs 40k/hand. A flat Pair build ends exactly here. I never had a shop with $6+ after ante 1: Small pays $0, rentals (-$3/round) and rerolls drained it, and I bought $1-4 items each time.
Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 shop: spent $5 on a reroll that showed nothing buyable; the $5 would have bought Mystic Summit one ante earlier or a Celestial. Rerolling to under the price of any joker is wasted.
2. Ante 4: two blind skips (Double Tag + Buffoon) were right (no shop money anyway, 2 Mega packs gave Half Joker + Space Joker), but I took Space Joker/Half Joker (flat) when no xMult existed in either pack. Fine given the offers.
3. Ante 5 Arcana pack ($4, my last money): Judgement gave Reserved Parking (junk). The Wheel of Fortune on a joker, or skipping and saving $4 toward the Jumbo Celestial ($6) for Pair L6/L7, was no better. The real mistake was earlier: no Pair planets (Mercury never offered; two Celestial packs gave Uranus and I picked it as Two Pair filler) while Pair was the main hand.
Reusable rules:
- IF you have $0-5 at ante 1-4 and the Small Blind pays $0, THEN skip the Small for any tag with value (Orbital(Pair)=+3 levels on your main hand, Double Tag + a pack tag on the Big) - I did this three times and it cost nothing.
- IF Mystic Summit is owned THEN spend both discards every round (5 cards each, keeping pair cards) so every played hand has +15 mult; est already models it.
- IF Half Joker is owned THEN play a 2-card Pair with no fillers: +20 mult; four +mult jokers + Half + Summit made 2-card pairs score 8-10k at Pair L5.
- IF The Mouth is the boss THEN play only pairs (any rank) - a 2-card pair is the same type each hand and 3 of them cleared 18000.
- IF The House is the boss (first draw face down) THEN discard 5 face-down cards immediately (new draws are face up) and use the 2nd discard on the remaining face-down cards: the rest of the round is fully visible.
- IF Double Tag is offered with a pack tag (Buffoon/Charm/Meteor) on the next blind THEN skip both blinds: it yields two Mega packs.
- IF no xMult by ante 4 and all jokers are flat THEN expect to die at ante 5 Big (37500): prefer rerolling for an xMult at ante 3-4 over buying a third flat +mult joker.
