## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: scaling. Whole run was flat +chips and +mult jokers (Blue +100 chips, Square foil, Scary +30/face, Fist, Moon, Onyx) plus Ramen x2 decaying; zero hand levels and no second xMult. Hands swung 3k-27k depending on whether the condition jokers fired (Queens held, clubs scored, Fist held low card). At 50000 boss/3 per hand no flat build works; the House then scrambled the condition (cards face down, no Queens visible).

Decisions I would change:
- Ante 5 shop: with $19, EMERGENCY, only 6 slots full of flat jokers: I rerolled twice ($10) and found nothing. A Celestial/Arcana pack for Death (copy Queens to feed Shoot the Moon) or the Wasteful-type ideas were never explored. Moon + Death on Queens = +13 mult/queen held is the only real multiplier available to this build.
- Ante 2 Justice: I typed target index 5 instead of 7 (cards were re-laid out after my last look). Re-read the hand line and count positions before `pick N i`.
- Did not buy any Pair/Flush planet all run (money $0-11): hands stayed L1 through ante 5.
- Ante 5 Small: I discarded 5 cards from a Trips/pair draw chasing a club flush at ~2.5%; it happened to pay off through K K Q Q draws, but the EV line was the same.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF you own Shoot the Moon (+13 mult per Queen HELD), THEN never play Queens; play low/other cards and keep Qs in hand. Each held Queen was worth +4k at ante 5 (17k Pair K with two Queens held vs 5k with none). Use Death/Strength on spare cards to make more Queens.
- IF you own Raised Fist (+2x lowest held rank), THEN play the 2-6 cards as fillers and hold K Q J T: Pair 3s with KC JC TC held scored 4278 at ante 2 (vs ~1200 without).
- IF you own Blue Joker (+2 chips per deck card) and one mult source, THEN single cards score: a lone High Card AS scored 7779 at ante 4 and cleared The Fish; check `hands` for HIGH 1 before building a two-card pair.
- IF Onyx Agate (+7 mult per club scored) is owned, THEN a 5-club Flush is 27k at ante 5 (35 mult): discard toward 4+ clubs whenever 3 are in the first 8 cards.
- IF a perishable (Fist) provides most of your mult, THEN skipping blinds does NOT tick it: I skipped Small at ante 3 and 4 for Buffoon/Ethereal tags and kept it alive through the ante-4 boss. Plan its replacement in the shop before its last round.
- IF the boss is The House and you rely on held-card jokers (Moon/Fist), THEN you have no visible cards on hand 1: discard 5, then the other 3 only if you do not need them held; expect hand 1 ~ blind.

Advisor/CLI defects:

- `hands` est for a Pair with held cards is correct (Fist/Moon/steel modeled; actual = est every time), but `hands` does not list HIGH CARD with a bigger est than the pair when held-card jokers make one card enough; `advise` surfaced `HIGH 1` only when needed.
