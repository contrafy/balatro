## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no +mult / xMult. Two chips-only jokers (Odd Todd +31/odd card, Banner +30/discard) on base mult 2-4 produced ~250-750 per hand; the 1500 Big needed ~500 x 3 clean hands and the draws gave 240, 15, 744.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop #2 ($7): I did not buy a Celestial or anything; $1 short of the holo Flash Card ($8) with no way to raise $1. A cheaper ante-1 shop #1 choice (Odd Todd $4 left $2) was what put me there: had I bought the $4 Buffoon pack first (a mult joker is the playbook priority; "FIRST SCORERS ADD MULT") I'd have been at $3 after shop 1 and still $1-2 short. The root issue is chips-only scorers; I accepted Odd Todd{E} anyway because SIM said +0.25.
2. Ante 2 Big: at est 240 vs need 1500 on hand 1 I followed the solver's "play Two Pair, keep discards for Banner" (p 0.51); the realised hands (240, 15 dig, 744) were all below pace. Banner's +30/discard is worth keeping discards for, but each discard dropped it 30 chips; consider discarding first toward a 5-card scoring hand (FH/flush with odd cards ~750-1000) while Banner is at +60.
3. Skipping the ante-2 Small (Boss Tag) lost a shop visit that would have been the only way to find a mult joker with $4-6; with 2 chips-only scorers and TIGHT 0.99x projected, playing it for a second shop view may have been better.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF both owned scorers are chips-only (Odd Todd, Banner, Sly, Scary) at ante 2, THEN a 1500 Big needs a 5-card hand every time: dig for FH/flush/straight with all odd cards; a Pair/Two Pair (200-370) is a wasted hand.
- IF a holo-edition +mult joker is $1 over budget at ante 1 and you own only an Eternal joker, THEN you cannot raise money (no sell): buy the first scorer only if it leaves enough to afford the next $8 xMult-class buy, or take the Buffoon pack first.
- Odd Todd: Aces count as odd (+31 each): FH AAAQQ 744, Trips AAA 468; play Aces/3/5/7/9 as scoring cards.
- The Psychic at ante 1 (5 cards forced): keep both discards until a made 5-card hand; a 4-card draw (solver 0.97) beat holding Two Pair (0.75) once only 1 hand remained.

Advisor/CLI defects: none material. SIM labelled buy-nothing/Banner/Celestial all "noise" in the ante-2 emergency shop; no advisor option could have fixed a shop with no mult joker. SOLVER p at the ante-2 Big (0.51 -> 0.24 -> 0.14) was well calibrated (lost).
