One decision I would change: after using both Strength tarots (original + Fool copy) for boss/big-blind rescues in ante 3-4, I did not prioritize re-buying a THIRD rescue tarot (Death/Strength/Justice) despite `shopadvise` explicitly flagging "no rescue tarot held" at the ante 5 shop - money was $-7 so it was not actually possible, but the deeper issue is I let Two Pair's level stall at L2 from ante 2 onward: every Celestial pack from ante 3-5 offered no Uranus, and I never bought Uranus directly from a shop's card row when available (none ever appeared for direct purchase either) nor pivoted the main hand to Full House (which reached L3 and consistently scored higher, e.g. 7810 vs 4640 on the same round) despite the playbook's explicit instruction to "re-anchor to Full House and level THAT if Full House keeps out-scoring your nominal main hand." That re-anchor should have happened by ante 4.
Bridge issues: money going negative under The Tooth boss with no floor, and remaining negative through subsequent blinds/shops with no cash injection to recover from it (Red Stake's removal of Small Blind rewards compounds this - there was no way to climb back to positive money after ante 4's Tooth boss). No crashes or ACTION FAILED errors encountered this run.
Playbook suggestions:
1. Add explicit guidance: "if your nominal main hand stalls below the ante-number level for 2+ antes while a secondary pair-family hand (Full House) is consistently leveled higher and out-scoring it, re-anchor immediately - do not wait for a crisis." This run's Full House L3 out-scored Two Pair L2 by roughly 60-70% every time both were available, but Two Pair kept getting played per the `advise` leveling-priority logic.
2. Warn specifically about The Tooth (ante boss, -$1/card): a run entering that boss below ~$10 can go permanently negative with no recovery path under Red Stake (no Small Blind reward); playbook should recommend banking at least $15-20 before the ante where The Tooth is the boss, or minimizing cards played even more aggressively than "no fillers" - possibly skip non-essential discards that draw extra cards if it's already clear the round will finish in 2 hands.
3. Rescue tarots (Strength/Death/Justice) got used twice successfully to save otherwise-losing final hands (turning a Pair into a Full House both times) - this is high-value and worked exactly as documented; the playbook's existing rule to always hold one from ante 3 on is correct and should be emphasized even harder, e.g. "buy a SECOND rescue tarot when consumable slots allow" rather than relying on The Fool to duplicate one opportunistically.
