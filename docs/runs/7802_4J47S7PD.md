## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING (0 xMult all run) made concrete by THE WALL at ante 4 (36000 = 12000 per hand) while my best repeatable hand was a face Two Pair at 9500-12000 under a rented Seltzer. Over 10 shops the only xMult-shaped offers were Loyalty Card{PERISH,RENTAL} (fires every 6th hand) and nothing else; the build was a flat +mult pile (holo Scary Face +10/+30 per face, Joker +4, Abstract +18, Lusty, Even Steven) on L1 Pair/Two Pair. Money was the secondary constraint: two rentals (Abstract, Seltzer) charged $6 per round, the Small Blinds paid $0, so the two ante-4 shops had $4 and $-1 and could not have bought an xMult had one appeared.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante-2 Buffoon pick: Joker{ETERNAL} (+4) over Chaos the Clown. The +4 never decided a blind (every ante-2/3 blind was cleared by 1000+), while a free reroll in each of the next 8 shops would have doubled my joker views (16 extra jokers ~ 1.5 expected xMult sightings). Rule: with 3 scorers already and ante 2 cleared comfortably, take the shop-economy joker over a 4th flat +mult.
2. Ante-3 Mega Buffoon: Abstract{PERISH,RENTAL} + Even Steven{PERISH} gave +22 mult for exactly 5 rounds and cost $15 of rent; both died on the Wall round. Correct for surviving the Mouth, but I should have planned the ante-4 shops around their death: I had $15 entering ante 4 and spent it on Seltzer + 2 Arcana (Chariot/Justice on non-face cards) instead of holding for a permanent multiplier. In hindsight the Steel 7S and Glass AH added ~+1-4 mult and ~0 respectively (see rule below).
3. Ante-4 first shop: Seltzer was right for the Wall math (+75%), but the second rental pushed rent to $6/round, which emptied both later shops. One rental max is the rule for a reason.
4. The Wall itself: with 0 discards after the second discard I chased a Broadway straight on the solver's advice (I believed a glass Ace would make it 69k; the real figure is 23.6k because Glass multiplies only the base mult, see below), then had nothing. Keeping the T pair and playing Pair T + fillers twice would have given two ~2500 hands and the same loss; the Wall was lost in the shops, not at the table.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your mult comes from +mult jokers (Joker, Abstract, Scary holo, suit jokers) THEN a Glass card (x2) or a Steel card held (x1.5) multiplies ONLY the base hand mult plus per-card mults, because enhancements and held-card effects resolve BEFORE the joker row. Verified exactly: Two Pair JJ99 with Steel 7S held = 216 x ((2+6) x 1.5 + 32) = 9504; Pair A with Glass AH retriggered = 54 x ((2 x2 +3) x2 +3 + 32) = 2646. On such a build Justice/Chariot are worth +1..+8 mult, not x2/x1.5; do not buy Arcana packs for them. They only pay on builds whose mult is already in the base (leveled hands) or on xMult-joker builds.
- IF The Wall (x2 blind) is the ante-4 boss and you own 0 xMult jokers THEN the ante is lost unless the two pre-boss shops produce an xMult joker; spend ante-3 money on joker views (rerolls/Buffoon packs), not on perishable +mult. A flat +mult face engine tops out at ~12000 per hand at ante 4 (3 hands = 36000 only with three perfect face Two Pairs).
- IF you hold a Buffoon/Mega Buffoon tag at ante 3 with a free slot and the Small pays $0 THEN skipping is right only if the pack cannot leave you with two rentals; at Gold 30% of pack jokers are rentals and a second rental ($6/round) empties every later shop.
- IF The Mouth is the boss and you hold a face Two Pair that scores >= 80% of the blind THEN lock Two Pair with it: P(two pairs among 8 cards) is ~44% per look, so one more Two Pair in 2 hands + 1 discard is ~80%; a Pair lock needs a suited face pair every hand.
- IF a boss debuffs a suit (The Club) and your hand has only one scoring card per pair THEN compute the High Card floor: with a +mult pile a single face High Card scores ~800-2800 (face +10 chips +30 Scary x mult); two face High Cards plus a weak pair was a guaranteed 2019/2000 before any draw.
- IF you are at $0-4 with rent due THEN the Gold card (Devil) on a junk card you hold is worth ~$1.2/round; prefer it over Lovers/World when no multiplier tarot is offered.
- IF a Seltzer rental is on offer in the shop before a x2/x3 boss THEN it is the strongest one-ante bridge available ($1 + $3/round for +60-75% per hand for 10 hands); buy it only as your single rental.
