## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: money -> scaling. I never owned a permanent +mult joker or any xMult. Mult came from Popcorn
(decaying, perishable, gone at ante 3) and Walkie Talkie (+4 per scoring 10/4); chips from Blue Joker{RENTAL} and a
free negative Wily{E}. Money never exceeded $13: Small Blinds pay $0, Blue cost $3 every round (8 rounds = $24 paid,
plus lost interest), so at the three decisive shops I held $2-3 (Hanging Chad $4, Death $3, Celestial/Arcana $4 all
unaffordable). Offers were also poor: 7 of 12 jokers seen were stickered rentals (Supernova{R}, Greedy{P,R}, Square{R},
Swashbuckler{E,R}, Mad{R} x1, Blue{R}), and the only permanent +mult offered before ante 3 was none.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 first shop ($5): I bought Blue{RENTAL} $1 AND the Buffoon pack. Popcorn (+20 mult) alone would have carried
   antes 1-2 (Pair (10+20)x22 ~ 660 vs 300-2000 needs) and the $24 of rent would have been $8-10 of buying power at
   each ante-3 shop (Hanging Chad, Death, a reroll). Blue is chips-only: on base mult 2-4 it never scales.
2. Ante 3 first shop ($13): one reroll then Walkie $4 left me $4. Ride the Bus/Walkie were both weak; nothing better
   was reachable. Probably the run was already lost here (ratio 0.10).
3. Ante 2 first shop ($7, EMERGENCY 0.22): left with $7. Nothing scoring was offered; a Jumbo Spectral ($6, Wraith/
   Ectoplasm chance) might have been the better gamble than holding money that rent ate anyway.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the ante-1 shop offers a $1 chips-only rental AND a Buffoon pack you can afford alone, THEN buy only the pack
  (or only a +mult joker): a chips-only rental's $3/round with $0 Small Blinds leaves you at $2-3 in every ante-3
  shop, exactly when the $4-6 scorers appear. SIM rated Blue +0.47 over a 3-blind horizon that ignores ante-3 money.

  an unwinnable fight): its shop could not buy anything anyway.

  rich type (FH 444AA 4064 cleared the last 1458).

  cards, never at a 10/4: the solver proposed Stone on 4C.
