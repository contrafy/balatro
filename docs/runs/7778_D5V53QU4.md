Post-mortem: binding constraint was LUCK combined with a structural gap, not money or joker quality. The Water boss
(no discards, 3 fixed hands from whatever is dealt) needs every one of the 3 dealt hands to be strong; we drew a
Two Pair on hand 1 (28985) but hands 2 and 3 each dealt only a single Pair with zero setup material (no second pair,
no straight/flush draw), scoring 7106 and 7920 -- nowhere near the ~23000/hand pace needed to hit 70000. With no
discards to fix a bad deal, three hands with combined scoring potential far below the boss's need is unrecoverable no
matter how good the joker stack is. The one decision I would change: at ante 7 with the Big Blind cleared comfortably
(61902/52500, 1 hand banked), I would have banked LESS margin and instead used that spare hand's discards budget more
aggressively to level a second/third scoring path (e.g., commit a shop buy toward Justice/Death restock so a rescue
tarot was in hand entering The Water -- it was NOUSE/absent because the only held tarot, Devil, doesn't help scoring).
Structurally: going into a no-discard boss with only ONE leveled scoring hand type (Two Pair) is fragile -- Full House
L3 needs Trips+Pair material that a Two Pair-only deal cannot provide, so when the deal is a lone pair, no rescue
tarot targeting a SECOND pair (Strength: rank up 2 cards) was on hand to manufacture one. Holding a Strength tarot
specifically (not just Justice/Death) for exactly this scenario would have let a lone pair become a second pair.
