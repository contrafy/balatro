## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling (misplay of money). The whole engine was two suit-gated +3 mult jokers (Greedy diamonds,
Lusty hearts) on Flush L2; every blind from ante 1 Head on was won only by a red Flush (~1.8k). The Goad needed 2000 and
the red flush tops out at (88+x)*21 ~ 1.85-2.0k, so the boss came down to the 5th heart's rank. I died holding $16.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 shop 1 ($8, EMERGENCY 0.17, TIGHT 1.10x): I kept the $8 for "the next shop's scorer". The Celestial pack ($4) for
   Flush (4 of my plays were Flush, it was the de-facto main hand) would have made Flush L3 65x8: the final flush becomes
   (105)*(8+15) = 2415, a clear. The SIM showed only +0.08, but in EMERGENCY with a one-type engine a level of that type is
   the cheapest multiplier on offer. The playbook already says: TIGHT = floor OFF, spend on main-hand score now.
2. Ante 2 shop 2 ($8 after Lusty): I kept $8 to double it with the Economy tag on the Big. The doubled $16 was never spent
   because skipping the Big also skipped the only shop before the boss. Money that cannot reach a shop before the next
   blind does not help that blind: Arcana ($4) or Uranus would at least have been a shot; better, play the Big (Greedy +
   Lusty red flushes were ~1.8k vs 1500) and use the Big shop's $11+.
3. The Goad hand 1: the solver kept AS(debuffed) AD KH for "two pair" (0.83). Two pair tops ~500 here; only a red flush
   could reach 2000. Keeping the red cards of one suit (KH plus the dig) from the first discard would have given one
   more draw cycle toward the flush.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your only scorers are suit +mult jokers (Greedy/Lusty/Wrathful/Gluttonous) and every win came from one Flush, THEN that
  Flush IS your main hand until you own a real scorer: buy its planet/Celestial pack in EMERGENCY even when the SIM gain is
  small; one level (+15 chips, +2 mult) is +25-30% on a 2k flush.
- IF a skip tag pays money (Economy/Investment/Garbage) but the next blind is the boss, THEN the money arrives after the
  boss is decided: only skip when the current build already clears the boss; otherwise play the Big and spend its shop.
- IF the boss needs one specific-suit Flush to clear and you are digging with plays, THEN compute the threshold card value
  first: (base chips + held card chips + x) * mult >= need; score the dig hands as high as possible (a 3-card dig with a
  diamond scored 40 vs 15 for the 4-card dig led by a club Q) because the last ~30 points decide it.
- IF you hold $10+ at a boss you might lose, THEN you already misplayed the shop before it: no money survives a loss.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- SOLVER at The Goad: p 0.83 then 0.88 "calibrated" on lines that needed 3 more hearts and a 5th heart of rank 4+; the real
  chance was ~0.5-0.6 and the run lost by 31. Same overconfidence as the playbook warns about for antes 1-2.
- Solver discard at The Goad hand 1 kept a debuffed AS for a two-pair draw (exp 2045) although no pair-family hand could
  reach 2000 with this build; it should drop hand types whose max score is below the need.
- shopadvise ante 2 shop 1 (EMERGENCY 0.17, main hand "undecided" with 3 Flush plays): rated the Celestial pack a "hand
  level gamble" at score 15 and SIM +0.08; with a one-type suit-flush engine it was the run-deciding buy.
- Blind select `s` says "never skip the Big" even when the Big tag is Economy and money is the bottleneck; it gives no
  estimate of what the tag is worth vs the Big's shop visit (I overrode it, and the override was wrong for a different
  reason: no shop before the boss).
- Dig advice (`advise` PLAY HIGH ...) picks fillers to maximize draws only; it ignores that the dig hand's score counts
  toward a tight total.
Bridge issues: none (37 steps, no rejects).
