## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING, specifically the absence of any joker-phase xMult (x2+) by the ante-5 boss. The build was a pure +mult stack (Supernova +27, Abstract +18, STM +13/Queen, Raised Fist +4..22) on Pair L3 with foil +100 chips: ~8-12k per plain hand, which is exactly the "0 xMult tops out ~10k/hand at ante 5" number in GOLD_ANALYSIS. Every blind from ante 2 on was carried by the Loyalty Card x4 landing on one hand per 6 played; I steered the cycle (3-hand rounds, one Big skipped) so the x4 hit every boss through ante 4, but the ante-5 cycle put it on the Big's last hand and the boss had to be cleared with three plain hands (~17000 each needed, ~10000 available). The Eye made it worse (three distinct types: Pair 8000 + FH 3900 + Two Pair 2900) but even Director's Cut would have left a 50000 boss at ~33000. Offer luck: 11 shop views (5 rerolls) and 2 Buffoon packs showed zero joker-phase xMults (The Idol and Photograph-style x2 fire during card scoring and multiply only the base mult, so they are not xMults for a joker +mult build). Money was secondary: the Investment skip gave $30 at ante 5, but $30 bought +mult, not multiplication.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante-5 first shop ($30): I spent $5 STM + $11 rerolls + $5 Mystic + $4 Celestial. The two rerolls were the right idea but $11 for 4 jokers was poor value; with the Eye upcoming, Director's Cut ($10) + one reroll + Celestial would have kept the boss random (still 50000, still lost without an xMult). Honestly nothing in that shop fixed the real problem; the fix had to come earlier.
2. Ante-4 shop before the Hook ($0) and ante-3 shops ($0-1): the run lived shop-to-shop on $0-6 until the Investment tag. The Even Steven rental at ante 2 ($1 + $9 rent) and Greedy ($5) were necessary bridges, but the $5 reroll at the ante-2 first shop (nothing) was the only wasted money before ante 5. With $5 more at the ante-4 Hook shop I could have bought Raised Fist AND a Celestial.
3. Ante-5 Buffoon pack: Luchador{RENTAL} vs foil Riff-raff. I took Riff-raff for the +50 chips and the Common lottery (Mad Joker, Business Card: both junk). Luchador would have let me play three Pairs at the Eye (~10-12k each = 31-36k): also a loss. Neither pick mattered; correct call was whichever had the higher ceiling (Riff-raff's Cavendish chance).
4. Not buying Director's Cut at the ante-5 first shop was defensible only because the boss was unreachable either way; in a run with ~17k hands it would have been mandatory.

What worked (reusable):
- Loyalty Card cycle steering: counting hands so the x4 lands on the boss's last hand. Three-hand rounds on purpose (play the lowest pair first, keep a made pair for the last hand), and skipping the ante-4 Big (Investment tag) so the Hook got the x4. It cleared Club, House, Psychic and Hook with 2-3x margins.
- The House: discard 5 face-downs, keep the 3 lowest (rank-sorted idx 6-8), hold a scoring single (8D with Greedy/Even Steven = 504 alone) for the x4 hand.
- Selling both perishables (Greedy 1 round left, Even Steven 2 rounds + rent) in the ante-3 Big shop to afford Abstract Joker ($4, permanent +3/joker): it scored more than both within one round and stopped the rent.
- Raised Fist management: play 5 cards (pair + 3 lowest fillers), hold the 3 highest; with Shoot the Moon, hold Queens (Q=10 counts as a high card for RF too). Est was exact on every hand (calibration: 8036, 9300, 8580, 39680 all exact).
- Supernova on Pair bought at ante 3 for $5 reached +27 by ante 5 and was the single biggest scorer.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Loyalty Card, THEN count hands every round: the x4 fires on the 6th hand after the last fire. Before each blind select, compute which hand of which blind gets it; make rounds take exactly 3 hands (cheap pair first, made pair kept for hand 3) and skip a Small/Big only when that moves the x4 onto the boss's final hand. Never let the x4 fall on a Small Blind's hand 1 if the boss is 3+ hands away without it.
- IF a blind is already locked by made hands in your 8 cards (sum of ests >= need), THEN play the lowest pair first with fillers that do not break the other pairs; the advisor's filler choice sometimes breaks a held pair (it proposed 3S as a filler with 33 held, and 5C/6S/4H with 55/44 held).
- IF Supernova is offered at ante 2-3 and Pair is your main hand, THEN buy it even Eternal and even if the advisor says LEAVE: +1 mult per Pair played is +20-27 by ante 5, permanent, no sticker risk.
- IF two perishables are within 2 rounds of dying and a permanent +mult joker costs their sell value plus your cash, THEN sell them now in the shop and buy it; they are worth more as $3 than as 1-2 rounds of scoring.
- IF your only xMult is Loyalty's x4, THEN by the ante-5 first shop you need a joker-phase x2+ (Cavendish, The Duo, Ramen, Acrobat, Card Sharp, Hologram, Constellation, Campfire, Stuntman): the ante-5 boss sits outside the x4 cycle whenever Small and Big take 3 hands each (they do at 25000/37500 with ~10k hands). Photograph, The Idol, Glass cards, Baron and Steel cards do NOT count: they multiply during card scoring, before Supernova/Abstract/STM add their mult (Photograph x2 on a 4-base Pair = +4 mult).
- IF Shoot the Moon is owned, THEN never play a Queen; spend discards on non-Queen singles and hold every Queen drawn (+13 each on every hand, including the x4 hand: one Queen on a x4 hand was worth +8300).
- IF The Eye is the ante-5 boss and your three best distinct hand types sum to less than the requirement, THEN Director's Cut does not save you either (it keeps the requirement); only an xMult does. Buy the xMult first, DC second.
- IF Mystic Summit is owned, THEN use both discards on hand 1 (discard 1-2 junk cards at a time if your pairs are already made); every hand after that gets +15.
- IF you are at ante 1 Small with no joker and a Rare/Uncommon tag, THEN still play the Small: here the shop after it bought the foil Loyalty Card that carried the whole run; both 300 and 450 fell to one-shot straight/flush draws (4 outs/open-ended, ~33-57%) which were the right digs with no jokers.
