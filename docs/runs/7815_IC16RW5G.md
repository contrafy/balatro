## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no xMult (0 seen all run) and a build whose score is (face-pair-dependent) additive: a face Two Pair/Full House scored 8-20k, a non-face pair or high card 1-2k. Ratio vs The Wheel was 0.80x "TIGHT" in the last shop and I left with $21 unspent: I was spending to fix it only through +mult/chips scorers that never came.
The Wheel hand: the opening deal had no pair (face-down card hides one more), I burned both discards digging (each also cost Banner -30 chips x ~50 mult = -1500 each), drew no face pairs in 3 draws (pair draw p was 0.7-0.84 per solver and missed three times in a row; also the last three draws kept K Q J but produced zero pairs).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 shop #2: sold Golden Joker ($4/round) for Swashbuckler (+10 mult, perishable). A perishable +10 mult on a ~50 mult hand is +20%; I would have rather kept the economy or taken nothing and rerolled harder with $30 for an xMult before the Wheel (I rerolled once, then stopped with $21).
2. Banner is a trap for dig-heavy bosses: it rewards never discarding (+60 chips x ~50 mult = +3000/hand) but forces 3 plays, so a bad deal (Wheel) cannot be dug out of without losing ~1500 per discard. A holo Banner was still correct to buy at ante 3 (SIM P 1.00) but I should have planned to swap it by ante 5.
3. Skipped the ante-3 Small for a Rare tag with $1 on hand: it returned DNA{ETERNAL} (useless). A Rare tag is a coin flip; fine, but note the shop slots were also full so it was never going to pay off.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your hands score 8-20k only when they contain 2-4 FACE cards (Smiley+Scary, Pair/Two Pair of face cards) and 1-2k otherwise, THEN the round is only as good as the face pairs you are dealt; at ante 4 boss expect 1 in 3 deals to be a dud. Buy a card-independent multiplier or a guaranteed fixed score source before the boss.
- IF the boss is The Wheel (face-down 1/7 cards) and the opening hand has no pair, THEN the face-down card is likely a rank near your held faces; discard the low junk but expect to need all 3 hands to be big; bank the first pair only after one dig.
- IF Banner (eternal) is owned, THEN every discard costs 30 chips x mult: with ~50 mult that is ~1500 per discard. Count it in the dig decision.
- Shoot the Moon (+13 mult per Queen HELD) was the best early joker on this run (Full House 1394 at ante 1, Pair 1200 at ante 2): take it over Ride the Bus at ante 1 when you own 0 scorers, and keep 1-2 Queens in hand when you play.
- IF the Negative tag produces a Merry Andy/Negative junk joker, skip it; hand size -1 is worse than any slot.
