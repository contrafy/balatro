# Run U12749QX (port 7810, claude-sonnet-5-5) - Black Deck Gold Stake - LOSS at ante 1 blind Small Blind (216/300)
Ante 1 (300/450/Club 600, start $4, Small tag Voucher): jokerless. Opening hand AC QH TH 9S 9H 8D 2S 2C (two pair 99/22 est 84). Discarded 4 (kept 99 22) chasing Full House: drew 3H 3C 5C QD (three pairs, no FH). Second discard (QD,5C, kept three pairs) drew QC 8H: miss. Hands: two pair 99/33 88, two pair 88/22 80, then final hand was a lone Pair of 7s (48) -> 216/300. Lost.

## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: LUCK/ZERO JOKERS at the ante-1 Small Blind: 300 chips with no jokers and 3 hands needs ~100/hand every hand, but Two Pair L1 is ~85, so you need a Full House/Flush/Straight (>=250) in one of three hands or a lucky run of two pairs.
Decisions I would change:
1. Opening hand already held Two Pair (84). I burned BOTH discards chasing a Full House with 33% then 28% odds and fell from "two pair guaranteed ~85 x3 = 255" to a two-pair-pair-pair outcome anyway; the better line was to discard 2 non-pair cards (AC, QH, TH are no help) only once, keep 4-of-flush/straight draws. Honestly nothing reaches 300 with two-pair-only (3 x 88 = 264); the real choice was between a 33% FH shot and spending hands on two-pair hands whose total (264) was already short, so the FH chase was right in EV, and the variance simply lost.
2. Final hand: after the second two-pair play I kept 7H 6D JC as straight material and drew 8C 7S 7H 6D 5D (8-7-6-5 needs a 4 or 9; one-gutter-both-ends drawn too late). With 2 hands left and 212 needed there was no line that reached; the loss was decided by the opening discards missing.
3. Could have kept 8H 9S 9H TH QH... no flush/straight available (hearts QH TH 9H only 3).
Reusable rules:
- IF ante-1 Small Blind (300) with no jokers and the opening hand is Two Pair only: three Two Pair plays total ~264 < 300, so you MUST convert a discard into a >=250 hand (FH/flush/straight) or you lose; chase the draw with the most outs while keeping the made two pair, and play the made two pair only AFTER the discards are used.
- IF you draw into three pairs (99 33 22) with a discard left, discard the 2 junk cards (6 outs for FH, ~28%) only if you still have a free hand to afford a miss; otherwise play the best two pair first to bank score.
- The first Gold shop only appears AFTER the Small Blind, so a jokerless Small Blind at ante 1 is a coin flip; there is no way to prepare for it other than the draw luck. Do not read it as a strategy failure.
Advisor/CLI defects: advise said "PLAY two pair, keep discards for later hands" on the opening two pair (est 84) even though 3 two-pair plays cannot reach 300; advice did not model that a jokerless Small Blind needs a one-shot >=250 hand. `hands` did not list any draw odds. Bridge issues: none.
