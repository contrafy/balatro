## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no xMult at all. I saw ~25 shop jokers and 4 Buffoon/pack jokers from ante 3-5 and not one multiplier, so the engine was a flat stack (Abstract +3/joker, Misprint, Odd Todd, Blue Joker, Crazy) that topped out at ~11-13k per hand at ante 5 against a 25k/37.5k/100k curve. Money ($2-29) was never enough to hunt: Small Blind pays $0 and I spent every dollar of the early shops on flat scorers that kept me alive (Space Joker eternal, Greedy, Crazy, Odd Todd, Abstract, Misprint).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop: took an eternal Space Joker (free) that occupied a slot all run. It leveled Flush/Straight/Trips a few times, but a 6-slot build with 2 eternals (Space, Blue) at ante 5 left no room to add an xMult without selling Odd Todd/Crazy.
2. Ante 3: skipped nothing, but I never built toward a skip economy: the Small Blind tags (Juggle, Standard, Polychrome, Holo) were all passed. Polychrome/Holo tags at ante 5 would have given a free x1.5/+10 mult joker; with 0 xMult I should have tested skipping the ante 5 Small (Polychrome) to turn the next-shop joker into an xMult edition, rather than fighting a 25k Small that already ate the round.
3. Ante 4 shop: kept $17-25 in two shops (2 rerolls total) instead of buying Celestial/Standard packs; but the true miss was ante 3-4 when I had a free slot (after Greedy perished) and bought Wily (Trips-only) rather than keeping the slot and $ for a reroll hunt.
4. Ante 5: 4 rerolls + Hermit all whiffed on xMult; the planet gamble at the end (Jupiter, Flush L4 80x10) was the best score upgrade of the run. A Celestial pack at ante 3-4 when the Flush/Straight engine appeared would have levelled the real main hand (Straight with Crazy) earlier.
