## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING. One xMult all run (Ramen x2 from an ante-1 Buffoon, decayed to x1.62). Everything else was flat:
Pair L11 (free planets via Astronomer + Burnt Joker), holo +10, Abstract +18, Bootstraps +4..+26, Gros +15, Sly +50 chips.
That build caps at ~22-26k per hand; ante 6 Big needs 30k. ~19 rerolls over antes 4-6 and 6 packs at ante 5-6 showed no
reliable xMult except Campfire{ETERNAL} (passed) and Photograph (x2 on base mult only). Money was never the problem from
ante 4 ($22 Handy, $39 Economy, Hermit, Temperance): there was nothing to buy.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2: Earth over Uranus/Mercury in the Jumbo Celestial (SIM +0.13). FH was rarely played afterwards; a Pair level then
   would have been worth the same and stayed useful.
2. Ante 4 Flower Pot $6 (EMERGENCY rule): it never fired on a pair build and was sold next shop for $3. Conditional xMults that
   need 4 suits do not belong in a Pair build even in emergency; that $6 would have been Ice Cream a shop earlier.
3. Ante 6 shop: spending $45 on 7 rerolls + 2 Arcana packs drained Bootstraps from +22 to +4 mult (~-25% score). Correct as a
   variance gamble only because the Big was ~0% otherwise; the real error was earlier: I should have skipped the ante-6 Small
   (Foil tag) only if the shop after it could not reach the Big - it could not, so the result would have been the same.
4. Passed Campfire{ETERNAL} $9 at ante 5: with Astronomer, every free shop planet can be bought for $0 and sold for $1, which
   feeds Campfire +0.25 per sale; 3-4 free planets per ante would have been x1.75-2 at each boss. Worth trying next time.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF a Handy tag is offered and you have played 20+ hands this run, THEN skipping that blind pays $1 x hands played now
  (here +$22 at ante 4) and saves a rent/perish tick; check raw run.hands_played_total.
- IF an Economy tag is offered with $30-40 in hand, THEN the skip is worth +$30-40 immediately (doubles money, cap $40).
- IF you own Burnt Joker and Pair is main, THEN the first discard of every round must be a pair (+junk): it is +1 Pair level
  per round; keep one other pair to play. The solver ignores this level-up (it called a 62958/60000 round UNWINNABLE).
- IF you own Astronomer, THEN every $0 shop planet you do not need can be bought and sold for $1 (Bootstraps/interest steps,
  Campfire feed), and every Celestial pack is free: open all of them.
- IF you own Bootstraps, THEN every $5 spent costs 2 mult: rerolls cost ~3% of your score each; spend only when the next
  blind is unreachable without a new multiplier.
- IF The Mouth is the boss and you hold Burglar (6 hands, 0 discards), THEN lock Pair with the first hand; 6 pair hands at
  ante 4 cleared 18000 with 3 hands to spare.
- Holding three pairs: play each as a bare 2-card Pair (no fillers) so the other pairs stay in hand; this guaranteed three
  rounds here (Big a5, Head, Small a6).
