Post-mortem: binding constraint was SCALING, not misplay. Main hand levels (Pair L3, Two Pair L2, Full House
  L2) plus one CONDITIONAL xMult (The Trio, fires only on Trips/hands-containing-Trips, not on our actual
  Pair/TwoPair plays) were not enough by ante 6 - flat-build ceiling per playbook 7a. shopadvise flagged
  "under-scaled" from ante 4 onward but the shop never offered a second xMult joker or Justice/Chariot-style
  enhancement pack often enough, and joker slots hit 7/7 (Scary Face negative gave the 7th) by ante 3, closing
  off flexibility to grab a better xMult later without selling something first.
  Decision I would change: in the ante 4 shop, after filling to 7/7 with Rocket (econ), I should have kept
  hunting rerolls harder for a second xMult before committing the last slot to an economy joker - Rocket never
  fired meaningfully (money was already adequate) and a flat build with only one conditional xMult could not
  keep pace with ante 6's blind curve. Also: the final Big Blind hand having 0 discards left for the deciding
  play was a direct consequence of using all 3 discards to chase Two Pair/Flush earlier in that same blind;
  `advise`'s own pacing logic walked through each discard as correct in isolation, but the aggregate effect
  left no cushion for a bad final deal. Worth flagging: advise should perhaps weight keeping 1 discard in
  reserve more heavily once cumulative score is trending well short of need with only 1 hand margin left.
  Est vs actual deviations >20%: Ante5 Big Blind FullHouse est13284/actual23966 (+80%, Lucky card mult);
  Ante5 Boss FullHouse est20418/actual36942 (+81%, Lucky+Trio synergy). Both in our favor (est is a floor per
  design), no case of actual falling short of est by >20% until the final unwinnable hand (est was accurate
  there: 12789 est vs 12789 actual contribution).
  Bridge issues: `cash` returned "ACTION FAILED: Round eval blocking events still pending, retry shortly None"
  twice (after ante 4 boss and ante 5 boss wins); both resolved on a single retry after `sleep 2`, consistent
  with the playbook's documented retry guidance. No other CLI errors encountered.
  Playbook suggestions:
  1. Consider adding explicit guidance to hold back the last discard once cumulative-score-vs-need pacing is
     tight (e.g. within 1.5x need) so a bad final deal has a fallback, rather than always spending all
     discards on chasing an improved made hand.
  2. The "take the first xMult from ante 4 on" rule is right, but this run shows a single CONDITIONAL xMult
     (fires on a hand type you rarely play) is not enough scaling security - suggest flagging in shopadvise
     when the only owned xMult's trigger condition does not overlap with the player's actual main hand type,
     so the run knows it still needs a second, more-applicable xMult sooner.
  3. Confirms playbook 4.8 (last-hand consumable rescue) works well in practice: Empress+Strength turned a
     3528-est dead hand into a 12789-est Full House, a >3x improvement, even though it was ultimately not
     enough this time - worth keeping as a strong, reliable pattern.
