## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: chips, then money. The engine was Half Joker (+20 mult on <=3 cards) + Misprint (+0..23) + Card Sharp (x3 on hands 2-3 of
every round, repeating High Card/Pair) + Constellation (x1.3 after 3 planets) + polychrome Jolly from ante 6. Mult was fine (33-42 x 1.95 x 3)
but the card chips of a 1-3 card hand are 5-30; Blue Joker{PERISHABLE} (+2 chips per deck card ~ +90-100) was 75% of my chips from ante 3 to
the ante-5 boss and the only reason antes 3-5 were cleared; when it perished after the Water the pair hand fell from ~26k to ~16k at x3. I kept
Blue alive for the ante-4 and ante-5 bosses by skipping 4 of 6 Small/Big blinds (no round end = no perish tick), which also skipped 4 shop
views and 4 blind payouts: the wallet never passed $16 and two eternal rentals (foil Hiker, poly Jolly) plus Half Joker's rent took it to
-$9 at ante 6, so Pluto ($3, a level on my repeated High Card) sat unbought in the last shop.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 first shop ($12, Blue 3 rounds left): Card Sharp was right, but the plan "skip Big to stretch Blue" then cost the shop views that
   had to find Blue's permanent replacement. Blue's real replacement never came; I should have taken the Mega Celestial ($8, 2 planets:
   Constellation x1.4 + probable Pair/High Card levels) at the ante-4 Small shop instead of the Hiker eternal rental ($1 + $3 x 9 rounds =
   $28 for +50 chips) and kept the ante-4 Big for its $4 + shop.
2. Ante 5 Arcana ($4) and ante 6 Spectral ($4) were both whiffs (Chariot; Sigil/Hex). With $4-7 the certain +0.1x Constellation (Celestial)
   was worth more each time than a 13-20% jackpot; two Celestials would have been x1.5 and likely a Pluto/Mercury level: the a6 Small was
   cleared on a 62% Misprint roll and the Big missed by 26k.
3. Ante 1 Buffoon pick: Golden Joker{PERISH} over Zany{PERISH} was right for money but the real lesson is that this seed never offered a
   permanent +chips joker in 11 shop views; a Buffoon pack at ante 2 Small shop (instead of the $5 reroll that showed DNA/Odd Todd{E,R})
   would have been a better view.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Card Sharp on Black Deck (3 hands) AND Half Joker, THEN every round is "High Card (1 card) at 1x, then two High Cards at x3":
  a guaranteed 2-hand clear whenever 7 x (single-card hand) >= need. Register with the LOWEST useful card, keep the stone/foil/high cards for
  the x3 hands. Pairs only when a pair + stone (3 cards) beats the single card.
- IF Card Sharp is your xMult, THEN a Stone card is the best third card of a 3-card pair play: it always scores (+50 chips) without changing
  the type (KK+stone 25931 vs KK 19745 at the Water; 99+stone cleared the a6 Small). Take Stone cards from every Standard pack.
- IF a Perishable carrier (Blue Joker, Jolly) has N rounds left, THEN skipping Small/Big does not tick it: count its remaining rounds in
  ROUND ENDS, not blinds, and skip so that its last round is the boss (Blue cleared the a4 Plant and the a5 Water this way). But every
  skip is a lost shop view: do it only when the carrier is >50% of your chips or mult.
- IF The House is the boss, THEN the first 8 face-down cards are still rank-sorted: play positions 1-3 as a 3-card hand (High Card of the
  top rank at worst, a pair at best: 5521 here) instead of discarding; all later draws are face-up and both discards stay.
- IF The Fish is the boss, THEN discards BEFORE the first play draw face-up (`press_play` sets `prepped`): spend both discards first, then
  play; keep visible pairs for hands 2-3 (Trips 666 + AA + 99 cleared 2424/2000).
- IF your mult is ~35+ from jokers but your scoring cards give <30 chips (1-3 card hands), THEN every shop's first priority is +chips (Blue,
  Stuntman, foil editions, Stone/Bonus cards, hand levels), not more mult: Misprint/Jolly/Half stacked mult while chips stayed at 30.
- IF Misprint is in the build, THEN plan the last hand of a blind on roll >= 9 (62%) at best; bank bigger hands earlier so the forced final
  hand needs a below-average roll.
- IF two eternal rentals are owned, THEN the wallet is frozen (rent $6-9/round vs $5-7 income): from then on only $0-1 items and skip tags can
  add anything; take them only when the alternative is losing the next blind, and prefer a planet/Celestial with the same money.
