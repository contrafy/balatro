# Run 4P4XLMWP (port 7777) - LOSS at ante 1 Big Blind

Stake 1 (White), Black Deck.

Ante 1 Small Blind (need 300): discarded twice per `advise` chasing a diamond flush off a starting two-pair
  hand (5s/4s), completed FLUSH D est 276 (scored 276), then PAIR A est 64 to close it out. Scored 340/300. WIN.
  Shop: $8. No joker/planet in direct shop slots (Hanged Man tarot $3, Venus planet $3, Reroll Surplus voucher $10).
  Bought both Buffoon Packs ($4 each) per shopadvise -> picked Golden Joker (+$4/round economy) then Brainstorm
  (copies leftmost joker = a second +$4/round). Ended shop at $0, 2/6 joker slots filled, no scoring (+mult/+chips)
  joker acquired.
Ante 1 Big Blind (need 450): hand kept reshuffling into two-pair (Q6, then 5/4) and a lucky trips of 7s on the
  last hand, but no scoring joker meant every played hand landed at 20x2/30x3 base value only. Est progression:
  104 (TWOPAIR Q6+filler), 76 (TWOPAIR 54+filler), 153 (TRIPS 7). Ran out of discards (used all 3 in round 1's
  carryover pattern... actually discards reset per round: used all 3 chasing pair setups) with only made hands
  available. Final score 333/450. LOSS. Run over per hard rule (losing any blind ends the run).

Post-mortem: binding constraint = SCALING / SHOP LUCK. The shop after the Small Blind offered zero scoring
  (+mult/+chips) jokers or hand-level planets for the hand actually being played (pair/two-pair) — only a Tarot,
  a Venus planet (wrong hand, Three of a Kind, never played), a reroll voucher, and two Buffoon packs. Both
  Buffoon packs also produced only economy/utility jokers (Golden Joker, Brainstorm) with zero scoring jokers
  on offer across two pack openings (4 cards total). Per playbook 5's ante 1-2 guidance ("buy the FIRST
  affordable joker that adds mult or chips... without one the Big Blind is usually lost"), this run never got
  that joker and the Big Blind was lost almost exactly as the playbook predicts (est ~76-153 per hand vs needing
  ~270-346).
  Decision I would change: none of the individual plays deviate from `advise`/`shopadvise`, and both pack picks
  were the best economy options in a pack devoid of scoring jokers. The one lever available would have been to
  `reroll` the shop instead of buying the second Buffoon Pack blind, but shopadvise's floor logic said BUY and
  a reroll would have cost $5 (more than the $4 pack) with an already-fragile no-joker economy — not clearly better.
  This looks like a case where the playbook's ante 1-2 shop guidance has no fallback when NO scoring joker
  appears in either direct shop slots or two Buffoon packs; a possible addition: if two Buffoon packs both
  fail to produce a scoring joker, prefer rerolling the shop (even below the normal reroll-avoidance threshold)
  over ending the shop with zero scoring jokers, since the playbook itself documents this exact failure mode.
  `est` accuracy: matched actual scored values exactly on every play this run (276 est/276 actual for the
  Small Blind flush; 104/104, 76/76, 153/153 for the three Big Blind hands) — no case of `est` differing from
  actual by more than 20%.
Bridge issues: none. Every `advise`, `shopadvise`, `hands`, `disc`, `play`, `pack`, `pick`, `cash`, `endshop`,
  `blind` command returned cleanly with no `ACTION FAILED` or connection errors.
Playbook suggestions:

     reroll (even under the normal $10 floor) over ending the shop with 0 scoring jokers, since this run
     confirms the documented failure mode (Batch 1 in section 7) exactly.
  2. Section 7a's "xMult joker offered in 1 of 46 shop visits" data point could be paired with a similar stat
     for "scoring (+mult/+chips) joker offered in ante-1 Buffoon/shop visits" — this run saw 0 scoring jokers
     across 1 direct shop + 2 packs (4 cards), suggesting ante-1 shops can also whiff entirely on scoring jokers,
     not just xMult ones.
  3. Consider whether Golden Joker + Brainstorm (economy-only stack) is ever worth prioritizing over holding
     cash for a reroll when the alternative is 0 scoring jokers by the Big Blind — this run's outcome argues
     for reroll-then-buy over buy-buy-hope in that specific state (fewer than 2 jokers, no scoring joker seen yet,
     $8 in hand).
