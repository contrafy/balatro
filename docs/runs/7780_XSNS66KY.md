## Post-mortem

**Result: LOSS at ante 7 boss (The Club), stake 1 (White). Best result this run: ante 7, boss blind, 57222/70000.**

**Binding constraint: scaling, compounded by bad luck on the final hand's draw.** The build (Smeared Joker + Droll/Greedy/Crafty/Abstract/Tribe flat-and-x2 stack on a Diamond/Heart merged Flush, Full House as boss insurance) cleared every blind from ante 1 through ante 6 with large margins (often 1.5-3x the requirement) and cleared the first two blinds of ante 7 comfortably too. It never found a second reliable xMult joker in 7 antes of shopping (The Tribe, bought ante 3, remained our only one) - consistent with playbook 7a's finding that xMult jokers are rare (about 1 in 46 shop visits in the cited data). By ante 7 the score requirement (70000) had outgrown what flat +chips/+mult stacked on a single x2 xMult could reliably deliver once card luck ran cold: the final hand of the boss blind drew nothing above a 9, and both of our "insurance" enhanced cards (a Wild King and a Steel Three) had been swept into The Club's Clubs-debuff (via the Smeared Joker's suit-merge, which we confirmed extends real-suit boss debuffs to the merged partner suit AND to Wild cards) and contributed nothing.

**The one decision I would change:** in the ante-7 pre-boss shop, I spent the last $6 (down to $0) on a Celestial Pack for a fifth Full House level (already L4, overkill as backup insurance) instead of pursuing Lusty Joker ($5, +3 mult per Heart card). Lusty Joker would have stacked additively with Greedy Joker on literally every card of our main Flush (since Smeared makes every Heart also register as Diamond), roughly doubling our suit-conditional mult bonus from +15 to +30 per typical 5-card hand - a substantial, reliable, unconditional-for-our-build upgrade that would very plausibly have covered the ~12800-point shortfall on the final hand. I noted this synergy at the time but deprioritized it because joker slots were full and no single existing joker looked "weakest" to sell; in hindsight, selling Crafty Joker (a flat +80 chips, additive and easily out-scaled by a mult-side upgrade at this point in the run) for Lusty Joker would have been the correct trade, mirroring the earlier successful override where we sold Jolly for Crafty and Mail-In Rebate for Abstract.

**Bridge issues:** none. Every `play`, `disc`, `sel`/`use`, `buy`, `pick`, and `sell` command behaved as documented across all 7 antes; no `ACTION FAILED`, no `Connection refused`, no unexpected aborts except the one informative Wild+Smeared-suit rejection noted below (which is a game-logic finding, not a bridge bug).

**Cases where `est` differed from actual score by more than 20%** (all traced to the same root cause: the CLI's own pre-play `est`/`hands`/`advise` tooling does not model Smeared Joker's suit-merge, so it systematically undercounts any hand that relies on merged-suit Greedy/Droll/Tribe/Crafty bonuses or on multiple Steel-held cards stacking; once a hand no longer needs the merge to validate, or the merge/Steel effects are otherwise unusual, `est` tracks `actual` closely):
- Ante 2 Small Blind: est 1328 vs actual 1992 (+50%)
- Ante 2 Big Blind: est 1552 vs actual 2328 (+50%)
- Ante 2 Boss (Flint): est 1330 vs actual 2520 (+89%, largest gap - Greedy's Smeared-inflated diamond count went entirely unmodeled)
- Ante 3 Small Blind: est 4048 vs actual 7176 (+77%)
- Ante 4 Small Blind: est 3588 vs actual 5772 (+61%)
- Ante 5 several hands: est 3564 vs actual 5022 (+41%), est 8134 vs actual 27984-cumulative context (individual hand +65%)
- Ante 6 Small Blind: est 15548 vs actual 20111 (+29%)
- Ante 6 Big Blind (double-Steel-held hand): est 22879 vs actual (contributed to) 50116 cumulative - the double-Steel stacking (x1.5 * x1.5 = x2.25) was NOT reflected in `est` at all until after the fact
- Ante 7 final hand: est 13962 vs actual 18258 (+31%)
Every mismatch favored the player (actual > est), so this is a systematically CONSERVATIVE estimator for Smeared-merge builds, never a dangerous overestimate - but it means `hands`/`advise` could not be trusted at all for hand selection in this build; every flush had to be found and verified manually by reading raw suit letters and mentally applying the Smeared merge.

**Playbook suggestions:**
1. **Document the Smeared Joker interaction matrix explicitly** (section 3 or a new subsection): (a) Smeared's suit-merge is bidirectional and applies to EVERY suit-conditional effect we observed - Greedy/Lusty-style bonuses, Droll/Crafty/Tribe-style Flush bonuses, AND boss suit-debuffs (a "Clubs debuffed" boss also debuffs the Smeared-merged Spades); (b) Wild-suit cards (The Lovers, The World, etc.) also get swept into a suit-debuff boss's effect, since a Wild card counts as every suit including the debuffed one; (c) a Wild card does NOT bridge the TWO merged-suit groups together for flush validity (tested once: Wild King (Diamond-natural) + real Clubs was rejected as a Flush, read instead as the fallback Pair) - Wild only helps within whichever merged group it's naturally part of, or possibly only when the other 4 cards already share one real suit.
2. **Add "stack multiple Steel-held cards" as a named scaling path** alongside the existing xMult guidance: buying every Chariot tarot offered and applying Steel to spare/filler cards (never cards you plan to score) multiplies their x1.5 bonuses together (confirmed x2.25 for two stacked Steel cards) every single hand they're not played, with zero opportunity cost once you have >5 useful cards in a merged suit pool. This was more reliable and more impactful in this run than hunting for xMult jokers.
3. **Flag `hands`/`advise`/pre-play `est` as unreliable for Smeared Joker builds**: every estimate undercounted actual score by 20-90% in this run whenever a merged-suit bonus or a held-Steel-card bonus was in play. An agent running a Smeared build should verify merged-suit flush candidates manually (read raw per-card suit letters, mentally apply Hearts=Diamonds / Spades=Clubs) rather than trusting `hands`'s candidate list, which will often show only a much-worse HIGH CARD or PAIR reading. Section 4 step 0's "follow `advise` unless you have a concrete reason" should explicitly list "the candidate hand doesn't account for a Smeared-merged flush" as a standing concrete reason to override.
4. **Section 5's late-game money guidance ("never leave money unspent at ante 6+") should be sharpened**: spending the last dollars on a marginal-value backup-hand level-up (a 5th Full House level, already overkill insurance) instead of a suit-bonus joker that stacks with an already-owned suit-bonus joker (Greedy+Lusty here) was the single highest-leverage mistake of the run. When a joker directly doubles the effect of one you already own AND your build's only weakness is raw mult scaling at the boss ante, prioritize it over further leveling an already-strong backup hand.
