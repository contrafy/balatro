## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no xMult (only card-phase Photograph) and no chips; the whole build was +mult (Summit 15, Jolly 8, Abstract 12) over tiny chip totals, so each hand was ~1.2-2.8k at ante 3 vs The Arm 6400 (needs ~2.1k x 3, i.e. all three hands made a 2-pair-or-better). The Arm punishes repeating types (each Pair/Trips play drops the level permanently and scores at the lowered level) and Two Pair L1 was the only immune hand I could repeat; it capped ~2k.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 shop ($12): bought two Celestial packs for Mercury/Venus (levels that The Arm then deleted) instead of rerolling for a Buffoon/xMult or buying a chips joker. Before The Arm, planets for a type you will repeat are wasted; the correct buys were chips jokers / Wee-type growth or rerolls.
2. Ante 1: passing Scholar/Faceless was fine. The Family (x4 on Quads, $8, perishable) was in the post-Big shop and I drew Quads twice (Club, Wall); a conditional xMult would have been a decent gamble with $5... but unaffordable ($5 vs $8).
3. Ante 3 Arm: I could not make 3 distinct 2k+ types. Hand 1-2 both Two Pair (fine, L1 immune) but hand 3 had no big hand. Digging with fillers each hand would not change that.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Mystic Summit, THEN burn both discards on the first hand (it also digs), then every hand gets +15 mult; a 1-card High Card scores 450 at ante 3 (Photograph/Abstract stack). The CLI advise says this correctly.
- IF the boss is The Arm AND your main hand is leveled (Pair L3), THEN do not play it: Two Pair L1 (immune to the drop) is the repeatable hand; plan chips/flat for Two Pair, and do not buy a Pair planet in the shop before The Arm.
- IF D6 tag is owned, THEN the post-skip shop's first reroll is $0 then $1,$2: do 2-3 of them before spending.
- IF money is $0-3 and a $3 +mult scorer is available (Jolly), THEN buy it; Handy Tag (+$1 per hand played this run, $10 at ante 3) skipped Small paid $10 and saved rent: skip the $0 Small whenever a pay-now tag is there.
