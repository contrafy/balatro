## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no multiplier at all (0 xMult, 0 growth) and zero money. Whole engine was flat +mult x tiny chips: Half Joker (+20 on <=3 cards) + Splash + Walkie Talkie + Smiley + Summit + Zany gave ~2000/hand = a hard cap of ~6-7k per round, so ante 3 Big/Boss were cleared by luck on Trips and ante 4 Big (13500) was unreachable. Money: the $0 Small pays nothing, a single Half Joker rental cost $3/round, and I was at $1-$3 in every shop from ante 2 on, so I could never reroll or buy a Buffoon pack (the only way to find an xMult). I never saw an xMult in 6 shops (12 joker slots + 2 packs).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop 1: took Splash from the Buffoon pack and bought Zany{ETERNAL} (Trips-only) at ante 1. Zany permanently blocks a slot of 6 and fired only 4 times in 14 hands; Splash and Half Joker (<=3 cards) are anti-synergistic (Splash wants fillers, Half wants no fillers) so Splash only mattered for scoring the 3 played cards' chips plus Walkie/Smiley triggers.
2. Ante 2: bought Half Joker rental ($1 + $3/round) AND Walkie Talkie, ending every shop at $1-$4. Interest was zero and Rerolls were never possible. A cheaper plan would have been selling Half Joker once Smiley/Walkie carried (I never did).
3. Ante 3: skipped Small for Top-up (Droll Joker, junk) while at $2: the tag only helped to save rent. Fine, but the real fix needed was money: one more shop visit with $10+ was worth more than one rent charge.
4. Ante 4: skipped Small for D6; the shop only comes after the Big, so D6 (free reroll) could not be used on the Big's need of 13500 anyway. Had I played the Small (9000 est reachable ~50%) I would have had a shop with cash... no cash either (Small pays $0). The run was already dead at the ante-3 -> 4 jump (6400 -> 13500 with 0 xMult).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you have 0 xMult at ante 3 and money < $8 in every shop, THEN the run caps near ante 4: at ante 1-2 prefer jokers that scale with the number of cards/faces (Smiley, Walkie Talkie, Even Steven) AND keep $10+ for a Buffoon pack at ante 2-3 instead of buying a 2nd/3rd flat joker.
- IF you own Half Joker (+20 on <=3 cards) AND Splash, THEN 3-card hands of faces/10s/4s with Smiley Face and Walkie Talkie score ~2000+ (High Card QS JH TS = 2250, beating Pair 88+4 = 1640); play 3-card High Cards of face/10/4 cards. Use `sel` to compare: est differs a lot from `hands` candidates.
- IF Mystic Summit (+15 mult at 0 discards) is owned, THEN burn both discards on hand 1 of every blind: hands 2-3 get +15 (~+35% each).
- IF the next shop-visit follows only a Big you cannot beat, THEN a D6/reroll tag taken on the Small is wasted (the shop appears only after Big).
- IF an Eternal/Perishable/Rental joker is in the first Buffoon pack with a clean alternative (Splash), THEN the clean one is fine but neither scores; at ante 1 prefer Pair/+mult pack picks.
