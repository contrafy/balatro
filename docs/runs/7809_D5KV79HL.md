## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. I never owned a permanent xMult. The only multiplier was The Trio{PERISHABLE} (x3 on Trips/FH), bought at ante 4,
and it carried ante 4 Big through ante 6 Small (FHs of 22-29k). When it perished, the same Full Houses made 6.8-7.4k, i.e. a max round of
~40k against 90k. Across ante 4-6 I saw ~12 shop/reroll screens and 4 Buffoon/Mega Buffoon packs with zero reliable xMult on offer.
The run lived on flat +mult (holo Swashbuckler, Mad) + Blue Joker chips + Burglar's 6 hands, which tops out ~6-7k per hand.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2: holo Swashbuckler{ETERNAL} for $7 (to $2). It was a good scorer at ante 2-4, but buying it left me broke for 2 shops and
   locked a slot forever. Fine in hindsight, but it started the "always $0-10" money pattern that kept rerolls rare.
2. Ante 4: Blue Joker{ETERNAL,RENTAL}: -$3 every round for the rest of the run (~$24 paid) for +~90 chips that DECAY inside each round
   (2 per card left in deck; Burglar digging drains the deck fast, so the last hands got ~40). A non-rental Sly (+50) would have been close.
3. Ante 5: skipping the Small (projected 0.77x) for a Buffoon tag gave an empty Mega Buffoon. The shop it cost was one more xMult roll.
   With no xMult, every skip throws away a shop roll, which is the only thing that can save the run.
4. Ante 5-6: about $45 went into rerolls/packs/Earths after Temperance x2, Hermit x2 and Immolate (+$80 total). That was right in
   direction (EMERGENCY), but the money arrived late; at ante 3-4 I had $9-20 and spent it on bridges (Arrowhead rental, perishable Joker).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the boss is The Serpent AND you have spare hands (Burglar), THEN play 1-card High Card hands while digging: the Serpent always
  draws 3, so each 1-card play GROWS the hand (6 -> 8 -> 10 -> 12 -> 14 cards here). Do NOT play 5-card filler digs under the Serpent
  (they shrink the hand by 2 per play). The last hand from 14 cards was a Full House that cleared 50000.
- IF you own Burglar (6 hands, 0 discards), THEN dig with 1-4 card junk plays that keep your pairs; 6 hands is enough to wait for
  Trips/Full House when you own a Trips-gated xMult (The Trio). Discard-dependent jokers (Banner, Wasteful voucher) are dead with it.
- IF your only xMult is perishable, THEN its last round is your deadline: every shop before it must hunt the permanent replacement
  (reroll, Buffoon packs), and do NOT skip a blind that would give a shop. Skipping only saves the perish tick when the next blind
  is the one that needs the xMult.
- IF The Goad (or another suit boss) debuffs cards in hand, THEN a held The Sun/Star/Moon turns 3 debuffed cards into a live suit;
  here it turned JD/9S/8D into a heart Flush (3275) and saved the boss with Blue Joker decaying.
- IF you own Blue Joker, THEN its chips fall 2 per card drawn: plan the big hand early in the round and expect the last hands ~30-40%
  below the first.

  draws 3, so that line shrinks the hand to 4 cards. Later calls did suggest 1-card plays, so Serpent handling looks inconsistent.
