# Run 6CH7LDMU (port 7809, claude-sonnet-5-5) - Black Deck Gold Stake - LOSS at ante 2 blind The House (730/2000)

Ante 1: Small 300 (Juggle tag), Big 450 (Coupon), Boss The Club 600. Start $4, no jokers.
- Small 300: dealt 4 clubs, two discards -> club Flush est 304 (cleared 300 by 4). Shop $6: Buffoon pack $4 -> Card Sharp (x3 repeat) over perishable Bloodstone; $2 left.
- Big 450: Straight K-9 316, then Straight again with Card Sharp x3 = 660. Shop $7: bought Crafty Joker (rental $1, +80 chips Flush); $6 left.
- Boss Club 600: clubs debuffed; Straight 240 (2 of 5 cards debuffed), cycled a junk High Card, then repeat Straight 720 with Card Sharp. Cleared on the last hand.
Ante 2: Small 1000 (Top-up tag), Big 1500 (Boss tag), Boss The House 2000 (first hand face down).
- Shop $8: Saturn $3 -> Straight L2. Skipped Small 1000 for the Top-up tag: got Photograph + Devious Joker free (4 jokers).
- Big 1500: Broadway Straight est 2954, one hand. Shop $8: Buffoon pack offered only perishable+rental Even Steven/Half Joker (skipped); Celestial pack gave Neptune/Earth/Pluto (took Earth, wasted). $0 left.
- Boss House 2000: whole first hand face down. Discarded 5 then 4 (all discards used), got a club Flush 596, then a cycle hand (HIGH 30, no discards left) drew 4 hearts but no 5th, last hand Two Pair 104 -> 730/2000. Dead.

Post-mortem:
- Binding constraint: scaling/consistency. The build was Straight-or-Flush only (Devious +100 Straight, Crafty +80 Flush, Card Sharp x3 on repeat). When the dealt cards offered neither, hands scored ~50-100. A 2000 boss needs two straights/flushes, and The House hid the first draw, which burned 2 discards (8 cards) just to see the hand.
- Decisions I would change: (1) Bought the Earth planet from the Celestial pack at $0 (a Pluto/Earth/Neptune pack had no Saturn/Jupiter); the $4 would have bought The Star/Supernova only after ante 3, but entering the boss with $0 and no consumable left no rescue. A better alternative was skipping the Celestial pack and keeping $4 for a tarot. (2) On the House, the first discard of 5 face-down cards was forced information-gathering; I should have played the face-down first hand as a free cycle (play 5 hidden cards: draws 5 face-up cards and keeps both discards) since discards are worth more than the hands' score on a boss I cannot beat in one hand anyway. (3) Last-hand cycling with 4 hearts: with no discards, I could only wait for 1 heart; ~35% and it missed.
- Rules (if-then):
  * IF the boss is The House (first hand face down), THEN play the 5 hidden cards (or any 5) as the first hand to cycle (Card Sharp registers the type) and keep both discards for the face-up draws; do not spend a discard on hidden cards.
  * IF your jokers only pay on Flush/Straight (Crafty, Devious), THEN keep discards for 4-card flush/straight draws and treat any hand type besides these as ~50 points: three hands need two of them.
  * IF you reach a boss with $0, 2+ jokers and no consumable THEN you have no rescue on a forced last hand; keep at least one $3 tarot slot or planet for the boss.
  * IF Card Sharp is owned, THEN play the biggest hand type first only if the second hand of the same type is reachable; otherwise first play a cheap hand of the type you can repeat (here both flush draws were broken by playing the first one).
- Advisor/CLI defects: pack `advise` in Celestial pack picked via shopadvise ranked the Celestial pack above Buffoon at 76 but it showed no planet for my hands (Saturn/Jupiter absent); the advisor could not know. `advise` at House first hand printed "no hand found" without noting that discards redraw face-up cards. Small-blind skip advice (Top-up) worked well: gave Photograph + Devious.
- Bridge issues: none.
