## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: never saw a single usable scorer. Three shops (ante 1 after Small, ante 1 after Big, ante 2) offered: Odd Todd{E,R}, Swashbuckler{P,R}, Runner{E,R}, Riff-raff{E,R}, Hallucination{P}, Shortcut{P}, 8 Ball{P}, Matador{E} $7 - every joker was either a hard-rule-barred sticker combo or non-scoring utility, and two rerolls at $5-6 revealed nothing (Emperor, Pluto, Matador, Riff-raff). With 0 jokers the 1000-chip ante-2 Small is ~3 hands of FH L1 (needs 3 x 333) = impossible.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 Big/Manacle: I won with luck (every draw hit). Fine. No real alternative.
2. Ante 1 shop after Small: Odd Todd{ETERNAL,RENTAL} $1 (+31 chips per odd card) was the only scoring joker I saw all run; I obeyed the hard rule. In hindsight with 0 jokers and the run decided by the next 2 shops it was probably the only way to survive ante 2 Small, but -$3/round for 22 rounds is unsellable death at ante 3-4 as well. Hard rule kept; not a mistake by the rules.
3. Ante 1 shop after Big ($6): I rerolled to $1 and found Emperor/Pluto. The alternative (Arcana pack for a Hermit/Strength, or keeping $6+$5 boss reward for the ante-2 shop = $11-12 which would have bought Matador $7 eternal) did not exist before the Manacle; but at ante 2 I reached the shop with $6 only because of the reroll... the Matador{E} $7 (xMult-ish on boss only) wouldn't have helped anyway.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you reach ante 2 with 0 jokers and no scorer is offered, THEN the run is ~lost (p 0.04 vs the 1000 Small); the only lever is the ante-1 shops, so at ante 1 spend the Small-blind money on a Buffoon pack immediately and keep the reroll for after the pack.
- IF the only jokers on offer are Eternal+Rental / Perishable utility and you own 0 scorers at ante 1, THEN the hard-rule pass costs the run; consider taking a $1 non-eternal rental scorer (none existed here).
- A 300/450/600 ante-1 with no jokers is clearable by flush/straight draws (solver p 0.92-0.96 matched: 3/3 clears), but ante 2 Small 1000 is not.

Advisor/CLI defects: none material. shopadvise ante-2 ADVICE=REROLL to $1 was reasonable but found nothing (variance). Pack advise correctly labelled both ante-1 pack jokers barred/AVOID.
