Best result: stake 1, ante 4, Big Blind (7500), lost by 2004 (scored 5496).
Jokers at death: Odd Todd (+31 chips/odd card), Gluttonous Joker (+3 mult/Club), Lusty Joker (+3 mult/Heart), Half Joker (+20 mult if <=3 cards played). Zero xMult the entire run.
Money curve: $4 (start) -> $0 (ante1 shop1, bought 2 jokers) -> $1 (ante1 shop2) -> $0 (ante2 shop2 Half Joker) -> $0 (ante2 shop3 Earth pack) -> $0 (ante3 shop2 Hone voucher) -> $1 (ante3 shop3 Chariot) -> $0 (ante3 shop3 Jupiter pack) -> $2 (ante4 shop1 Justice pack) -> $1 (ante4 shop2 Earth pack) -> $1 at death. Money was essentially always at the floor; never accumulated past $14 (peak, right after ante2 boss).
Binding constraint: scaling. Zero xMult jokers ever appeared for purchase in this run's 10 shop visits (only flat +mult/+chips and one UTIL joker, Half Joker). Full House reached L4 (115x10) and Pair (our de facto main hand via Half Joker's <=3-card bonus) stayed at L1, but with no multiplicative jokers the score ceiling per hand was ~2000-6000, while the Big Blind needed 7500 at ante 4 (needs roughly double each ante per playbook section 3). The single Glass card (Jack of Hearts) and Steel card (King of Clubs) never both appeared together in a played hand before the loss.
The one decision I would change: at ante 3 shop2 spent the last $10 on the Hone voucher (doubles enhancement odds in shop) instead of saving toward a second rescue tarot or reroll-hunting for an xMult joker; with 0 xMult jokers offered in 10 shops, money should have been hoarded harder from ante 3 on per the "reroll until xMult appears" rule, even though shopadvise never flagged an xMult as available to buy (none were ever offered).
Bridge issues: none. Every command executed cleanly; no ACTION FAILED / retry messages, no connection issues. `advise` and `shopadvise` both worked every call.
est vs actual: one big divergence -- Ante 3 Big Blind PAIR AA est 2585, actual scored 3335 (about +29%, likely the Steel King of Clubs x1.5 hold-bonus or a joker interaction not fully modeled in the est formula for that specific hand). All other plays this run matched est within a few percent (e.g. final PAIR AA matched est 2350 exactly).
Playbook suggestions:
1. Section 7a already says "if no reliable xMult has appeared by ante 3, take a conditional one... zero xMult at ante 5 loses" -- this run confirms it happens even earlier (lost ante 4) when literally zero xMult-shaped jokers (clean or conditional) are offered in 10 consecutive shops; add a note that below-ante-4 money should be hoarded more aggressively (skip vouchers/Hone-type QoL buys) if no xMult has appeared, since flat/level scaling alone caps out fast without any x-mult.
2. Confirm/tighten the RESCUE TAROT rule: after using Death to save a blind (ante 4 Small here), the very next shop should prioritize re-buying/re-banking a card-targeting tarot even at ante 3-4 money-tightness, since running out of hands one blind later (ante 4 Big) with zero consumables and zero discards was the direct cause of the loss.
3. `est` for Pair/Trips hands with a Steel-enhanced card in the scoring set can run meaningfully low (+29% seen here); worth a modeling note or a wider safety margin recommendation on `est` when a held-card enhancement (Steel/Glass) is in the played hand.
