## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING, then variance. The build was Two Pair L5 + flat mult (Mad +10, Jolly +8, negative Misprint 0-23) + flat
chips (Blue ~+90, Odd Todd +31/odd) behind ONE end-phase xMult (Ramen, decayed to x1.84). Bloodstone, bought as "the second xMult"
(advisor SIM +0.45), is a card-phase effect: it fires per scoring Heart inside the card loop (card.lua:3639, same `individual`
block as Odd Todd), so its x1.5 multiplies only the base hand mult (6 at 2P L5) before Mad/Jolly/Misprint are added: ~+3 mult,
about +8%. Typical Two Pair at ante 5 = 15-18k (max 23.7k with Misprint 23), i.e. 37500 needed three made Two Pairs in three hands
with zero misses. The round dealt no pair, both discards missed, and the final hand needed Misprint >= 5 (p 0.79): it rolled 0.
Money was never the issue after The Hermit ($30 at ante 3); the issue was that ~32 shop jokers in 16 views held exactly one
real xMult (Ramen, from a pack) plus Madness (AVOID), The Tribe (Flush-only) and Bloodstone (card-phase).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 shop 2 ($36): Bloodstone $7 + Odd Todd $4 + Jumbo Celestial $6 + Jumbo Arcana $6. The Celestial/Fool (two Uranus) were
   right. Bloodstone was a +8% joker sold as an xMult; the $7 was one more reroll toward a real multiplier. With $36 at ante 3 the
   line that wins at Gold is 3-4 rerolls ($5+6+7+8) for an end-phase xMult, keeping $10, not two flat jokers.
2. Ante 5 shop ($19): the $5 reroll showed nothing and the $4 Celestial added +11%; both were fine but neither could reach the +20%

   fire on FH, est ~17k): both lines were ~p 0.6 for the made hand; with hindsight keeping AAA kept Trips 7.5k as a floor, which
   plus the 23k and 3.7k hands would have been 34k... still short. No single play changes the outcome; the build did.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF a joker's text is "per scoring card of suit/rank" (Bloodstone, Ancient Joker, Photograph, Lusty/Greedy/Wrathful/Gluttonous,
  Smiley, Scary, Odd Todd, Even Steven) THEN it resolves in the card phase BEFORE +mult jokers: an x1.5 there is only
  +(0.5 x base hand mult) = +3 at Two Pair L5. Never count such a joker as your second xMult; it does not change the ante-5 math.
- IF your end-phase xMult count is 1 at the end of ante 4 THEN ante 5's Big (37500) needs three made Two Pair L5 hands in a row:
  expect ~50% death. Treat ante 3-4 shops with $25+ as reroll shops (3-4 rerolls) until an end-phase xMult appears; flat +chips
  jokers (Odd Todd, Blue) are not the buy at that point.
- IF The Fool is in an Arcana pack THEN read `raw` run.last_tarot_planet: if it is your main-hand planet, The Fool IS a free level
  (worked: Uranus -> Two Pair L4). The pack advisor ranked Strength over it.
- IF The Arm is the boss THEN play the leveled main hand exactly once (it scores at L-1 and keeps the loss) and finish with L1 types;
  Full House fires every Pair/Two Pair joker (Mad, Jolly) and scored 13209 here. Do not follow a second main-hand ADVICE under The Arm.
- IF Riff-raff is owned with empty slots THEN every blind select fills two slots for free; sell it the moment the row is full
  (it produced Mad, Chaos, Blue, Ride the Bus, 8 Ball over 3 blinds: 3 keepers for $6).
- IF Chaos the Clown is owned THEN reroll FIRST in every shop (free; packs and vouchers survive the reroll), then run shopadvise.
- IF Gros Michel is your ante-1 scorer THEN plan for its 1/6-per-round death: it died on round 3 here; Cavendish (x3, common)
  joins the pool after, so keep $6 and a slot ready for it from ante 2 on.
- IF at ante 5 blind select the Small's tag is Garbage with 12+ unused discards AND your cash minus rent cannot buy a joker THEN
  skip the Small (+$16 here, rent saved); `s` says "do not skip" regardless.
- IF a forced final hand depends on Misprint THEN P(clear) = (24 - needed roll)/24; with Misprint as a core mult source, give the
  last hand a 25% margin (play the dig first, keep the best made hand for last) or expect a 1-in-5 death.
