## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: zero +mult jokers. In 5 shop views at antes 1-2 the only jokers were Ice Cream, Runner{E}, Drunkard{E,R},
To Do List{E}/{R}, Crazy Joker{P,R}, Bootstraps{E,R}, Gift Card and Vampire{R}: not one clean non-rental +mult joker. The
single scorer was Ice Cream (chips-only, decaying 100 -> 55), so every hand ran at base mult 2-4 and ante 2 Big (1500)
needed a made Straight/Flush on most hands. Ante-2 projection was 0.64x of the Big already in the shop before it.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 first shop ($6): Ice Cream ($5) over the Buffoon Pack ($4). SIM favoured Ice Cream (0.41 vs 0.24) because it
   clears ante 1 for sure, but ante 1 was cleared by made hands anyway (360, 453, 934) and Ice Cream decays; the Buffoon
   pack was the only chance at a permanent +mult joker in the whole run (no clean one appeared in 4 more views). The
   first-buy rule (permanent any-hand +mult first; chips-only and decaying jokers are not tier 1) should have won.
2. Ante 2 first shop: after the $5 reroll I left with $9. Celestial/Arcana packs or a second reroll would not have
   changed much; the loss was decided by the absence of +mult supply plus decision 1.
3. Saturn (Straight L2) was the SIM's best but only raises a hand I make ~1 in 4 rounds. With $5 the Arcana pack
   (Empress = +4 mult cards on pairs, Strength) might have been equal; low confidence.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the ante-1 first shop offers only a chips-only/decaying joker (Ice Cream, Popcorn-type) and a Buffoon pack, THEN buy
  the Buffoon pack: made hands clear ante 1 with no jokers ~most of the time, but ante 2 Big (1500) needs mult.
- IF you reach the ante-2 Big with 0 +mult jokers and the shop has none, THEN expect to need a made Straight/Flush/FH;
  spend discards on the one hand type that scores 4x mult and level it (Saturn/Jupiter) only if you will draw to it.
- IF the shop's only "xMult" is Vampire and you own no enhanced cards, THEN it is a x1 rental: do not buy it.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- shopadvise ADVICE BUY Crazy Joker{PERISHABLE,RENTAL} at ante 2 ($1, +12 mult if Straight): that is a flat rental at
  ante 1-2 (hard rule ban); Bootstraps{E,R} in the next shop was correctly BARRED, so the bar misses perishable rentals.
- shopadvise ADVICE BUY Vampire{RENTAL} (rule 65, "XMULT growth") while printing "no enhanced cards in your deck yet, so
  it starts and stays x1"; SIM had it +0.01 over nothing. A x1 growth joker with no feed should not outrank the SIM pick.
- Ante 1 SIM ranked Ice Cream (chips-only, decaying) over a Buffoon pack by 0.17; it only looks 3 blinds ahead and the
  ante-1 blinds were cleared by made hands; the decaying chips joker was worthless by the ante-2 Big.
- No bridge errors. Note: a shell function wrapper for b.sh does not persist between bash tool calls; use the full command.
