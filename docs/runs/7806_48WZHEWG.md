## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING through conditions, plus money. Every multiplier was conditional: Blackboard (x3 only when held cards are all black), Ancient Joker (x1.5 per Hearts/Diamonds card via Smeared), Acrobat (x3 on the final hand), Photograph (needs a face). No hand was ever leveled (zero planets bought). A "normal" hand at ante 4 was 1-7k; only a hand meeting 2-3 conditions at once reached 15-38k. The Mark (faces face-down) at 18000 needed one such hand. I did not draw it: 4 reds and 4 blacks at the final hand, so there was no red flush. Money: Smiley rental cost ~$21 of rent over 7 rounds. Shops had $0-12, so no Celestial packs and no rescue tarot.

Decisions I would change:
1. The Mark, first discard: I kept 3 "black holder" cards and discarded only 2 (then 1 more). In 10 cycled cards I drew 2 reds out of a deck that was 55% red. Discarding 5 per cycle (keep only the reds and face-downs) sees 2-3x more cards. Black holders are easy to come by (half the deck). Draw volume was the real constraint.
2. The Mark: `sort suit` should have been the FIRST action. The face-down suits were then readable from their positions (see rules). I sorted only after both discards were gone.
3. Ante 4 shop: Smeared Joker{ETERNAL} $7 took my last money and the 6th slot. It was worth +40% on the Big (15706 vs 11211), but a Celestial for the main hand plus $3 for a rescue tarot (Death/Strength) might have been the steadier buy for an 18000 boss.
4. Ante 2: taking Smiley{RENTAL} was right for scoring, but I kept it to the end. Rent made every shop $0-6.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the boss is The Mark (or Wheel/Fish/House with face-down cards in hand), THEN run `sort suit` before any discard. Suit sort order is Spades, Hearts, Clubs, Diamonds, high rank first within a suit, and face-down cards still sort by their real suit. A face-down card's position between known cards tells you its suit. Example: FD between 8S and AC = Hearts; FD between AC and 8D = Clubs. Confirmed by the final reveal.
- IF you own Blackboard, THEN choose plays so the 3 HELD cards are black. Use red cards as fillers. Spend discards on red non-scoring cards. Under The Goad (spades debuffed), hold the spades. It made x3 on ~9 of 13 hands here at ante 1-3 (2P AAJJ 124 -> 372, 2P KK22 1584, spade Flush 1728).
- IF you own Smeared Joker + Ancient Joker (or another suit joker), THEN the partner suit also triggers. Ancient=Hearts makes Diamonds x1.5 too: Straight J-7 with TD 9H 8H scored 15706 vs est 11211. Do your own math. The advisor/solver undervalue these lines.
- IF you own Acrobat and your best made hand is already in hand, THEN spend earlier hands as throwaways on other cards and play the best hand last (x3).
- IF you chase a specific card mix (N of a suit + M held of another color) with 2 discards + 2 throwaway hands, THEN cycle 4-5 cards per action. Never cycle 1-2 cards to "protect" cards that are half the deck.
- IF a skip tag pays now (Handy: $1 per hand played this run, $8 here) and the Small's shop would have <$5, THEN skip (no rent tick, nothing buyable anyway).
- IF Smiley Face and Photograph are both owned, THEN put Smiley LEFT of Photograph: (1+5)x2 beats 1x2+5 on the same face (HC KD 255 -> 330).
