Post-mortem: binding constraint = SCALING. We had 6 joker slots full of weak synergy (Abstract Joker +3mult/joker, Credit Card debt-utility, To the Moon econ, Supernova conditional +mult, Stone Joker +25chips/stone, Vampire xMult but only reached x1.2) and NO reliable xMult (Duo/Trio/Card Sharp/Hologram/Constellation/Cavendish/Baron never appeared in any shop this run). Full House was only leveled to L2 by ante 4 - too slow. The Wall's doubled target (20000 vs standard 10000) was simply out of reach for a build with zero strong xMult.
Decision I would change: at ante 3-4, with 6/6 joker slots full of low-impact jokers (especially Credit Card, which contributed nothing to scoring) and no xMult in sight, I should have sold Credit Card or Abstract Joker to free a slot and rerolled harder/earlier for an xMult, even below the $15 floor, once ante 4 arrived with zero xMult owned - the playbook's ante-4 rule ("secure xMult by ante 4... sell a weak flat joker to keep a slot free") was not acted on aggressively enough; I followed shopadvise's per-visit LEAVE/BUY calls but never proactively sold a dead joker (Credit Card) to force room for an incoming xMult candidate.
Bridge issues (exact text): `hands`/`advise` repeatedly proposed invalid combinations that used a Stone Card as if it had a real rank, e.g. "FULL HOUSE 55588: idx 5 6 8 2 3" and "STRAIGHT 7-high: idx 3 5 8 6 7" and "TWO PAIR 95: idx 2 3 5 8" and "PAIR 5: idx 6 8" - all four aborted with `ABORTED: you asked for [TYPE] but these cards form [lesser type]. Re-run hands and pick indices again.` This happened every single time a Stone Card sat in a same-rank display group (ranks display, e.g. "5x2", silently included the rankless Stone Card as if it shared the rank). No hands/discards were consumed by these aborts (state's hands/disc counters unchanged after abort), so it was always safe to test-then-fallback, but it cost extra round-trips and could confuse an agent that trusts advise blindly.
Also: est was significantly LOW (never high) whenever Stone Joker was owned, by 20-83% (e.g. est 825 vs actual 1512, est 800 vs actual 1809, est 1425 vs actual 2168, est 1773 vs actual 2488, est 2631 vs actual 3346) - the tool's own printed note ("Stone Joker +25 per Stone card in deck not counted") explains part of this but the magnitude of some gaps (>50%) suggests Vampire's growing x_mult and/or other joker interactions are also under-modeled once mid-game xMult/enhancement jokers are owned. Always treat est as a lower bound once Stone Joker or Vampire are on the team, not a reliable go/no-go number.
Also: on The House boss (first hand face-down), advise/hands returned "no hand found (all face-down?)" and est for a blind HIGH-card play was wildly low (est 85 vs actual scored 600) - face-down cards evidently still score real chip/rank values once played, the estimator just can't preview them.
Playbook suggestions:
 1. Fix `hands`/`advise` to exclude Stone/rankless enhanced cards from same-rank groupings entirely (they should only ever appear as neutral fillers in candidate hands, never as a "partner" for a pair/trips/straight) - this cost several wasted round-trips this run and would actively mislead an agent that plays without testing first.
 2. Make `shopadvise`'s reroll/sell-for-xMult recommendation more assertive at ante 4+ when 0 xMult is owned and joker slots are full: explicitly recommend selling the weakest non-scaling joker (e.g. a pure utility joker like Credit Card) even when the shop currently offers no xMult, rather than only recommending sell-then-buy at the moment an xMult appears.
 3. Note in section 6 (bosses) that boss target values shown at blind-select/shop already include the boss's own multiplier (e.g. The Wall's "x2 chips" is baked into the printed need, e.g. 20000 instead of the base-ante 10000) - this run initially had to infer that from the numbers; making it explicit would help agents gut-check feasibility earlier (we could have recognized at blind-select that a Full-House-L2-only build could not plausibly clear 20000 and adjusted strategy, e.g. gone all-in on Credit-Card debt to buy any xMult available beforehand).
