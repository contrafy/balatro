## Post-mortem

**Binding constraint:** no xMult and no second scaling scorer by ante 2. Pair-family chips (Sly +50, Ice Cream +100) gave ~340-380 per hand, so Big 1500 needed ~500 per hand. Ante 1 money stayed at $2-$7 after each buy, so no Buffoon or xMult was affordable in the one shop before the Big.

**Decisions I would change:**
1. Do NOT skip the Small at ante 2 when the build has no xMult and no shop before the Big. The Economy Tag (+$8) bought nothing, because no shop came before the Big. The skip cost the only chance to buy a scorer or xMult before 1500. If you skip, you lose a shop, not just a blind.
2. Do not buy a decaying bridge (Ice Cream) as the second scorer at ante 1 when a permanent +mult scorer is affordable later. Its value (+100 decaying 5 per hand) was spent by the Pillar boss.
3. On a boss where two plays clear with margin, prefer the bigger-margin play even if the advisor names the pair. I did this (flush 620 over pair 338); it was correct, but it does not change the outcome here.

**Concrete reusable rules (if-then):**
- If the next blind after a skipped blind is a Big (or Boss) and there is no shop between them, do NOT skip. A skip removes the only money-to-scorer conversion before the big blind.
- If your ante 2 build has 0 xMult and 0 mult scorers, then the Big 1500 needs ~500 per hand; a Pair build with chips-only jokers (Sly, Ice Cream) will not clear it, so keep $8+ for a Buffoon pack or xMult in the shop before the Big.
- If the shop offers no xMult, no Buffoon, and no +mult scorer, then take the cheapest scorer you can keep and bank the money for interest; do not skip the next blind for a tag that only pays cash.
- If two plays both clear a boss, pick the one with the larger est-to-need margin.
