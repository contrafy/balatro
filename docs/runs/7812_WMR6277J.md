## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no multiplier at all and no money. Every joker was flat (Abstract, Business Card, Juggler, 8 Ball holo, Superposition foil): max hand ~1.5-3.3k at ante 3, so the ante 4 Small (9000) was three times out of reach. Rentals (Juggler + 8 Ball = -$6/round) kept cash at $0-5 for the whole run so no shop ever had a real purchase.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop 2: Gros Michel had already been destroyed at the Big's round end, and I still bought Abstract Joker (+3 mult per joker) as a "second" scorer. A perishable flat +3x jokers with 1 other joker was almost zero. With $5 a reroll or the Mega Arcana was no better, but I should have READ the joker list after cashing out before deciding (the advisor's SIM thought Gros Michel was alive? it printed 0/6 in `s` jokers line).
2. Ante 2 shop 1: took Juggler{RENTAL} for Abstract's mult count. Net +3 mult for -$3 per round: bad when the wallet is $1. Same for holo 8 Ball{RENTAL} (+13 mult total, correct to take, but with two rentals I never again held more than $5 and could not buy Director's Cut/Emperor/Priestess).
3. Ante 3 Small skip was right (Foil tag gave a free foil joker) but the Big 4800 then took all 3 hands; no money was gained anywhere.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF a Buffoon-pack pick is Gros Michel, THEN assume it is dead by the next shop (1/6 per round end and it died after the first round); do not count it as a scorer when buying joker #2.
- IF the wallet is <= $5 at ante 2-3, THEN do not hold two rentals (-$6/round): cash stays at $0-5 and no xMult can ever be bought; sell the one that is not a +10 mult edition.
- IF Abstract Joker is the main scorer (+3 mult per joker incl. Abstract/rentals), THEN each cheap flat joker adds +3 mult: with 5 jokers a 1-card High Card made 1700 at ante 3 (est accurate). It perished after 5 rounds with no replacement (ante 4 Small 9000 unreachable): buy a permanent xMult before it dies.
- IF The Fish and 2 hands remain with face-down cards in hand, THEN discard the face-down cards (draws are face up); it turned 4 unknowns into JJJ (3080).
- IF Flush/Straight are available in a 9-card hand (Juggler), THEN they beat Two Pair by ~1.5x with flat mult builds (Flush 2054 vs Two Pair 1392 at ante 3): `advise` found them.
