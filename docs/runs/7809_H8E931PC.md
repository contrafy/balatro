## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING (zero xMult the whole run), fed by MONEY. The engine was additive only: Half Joker holo
(+30 mult), Pair L10 (Burnt Joker + Orbital tag + Mercury), Stuntman +250 chips, Blue Joker, Swashbuckler. That tops
out at ~25-26k per hand at ante 6, i.e. 3 x 25k = 76k < 90k Big. The only xMult offers all run were perishable
Blackboard ($6) / Driver's License ($7) at ante 2 with $5, and an Eternal+Rental Flower Pot. Money never exceeded $17:
two rentals (Burnt Joker ~$27 of rent over 9 rounds, Stuntman), $0 Small Blinds and three Small skips.

Decisions I would change:
1. Burnt Joker held 9 rounds cost ~$28 for 5 Pair levels (~$5.6 per level; 4 rounds produced no level because my
   first discard had no pair or I needed the pair). Good at ante 2-3 (cheap +50%/level at L1-L4), poor from ante 4.
   Sell it in the first ante-4 shop: that $9-12 is the price of one xMult.
2. Ante 5 shop: $5 Blue Joker (+30%) was right, but the reroll before it ($5) found it by luck; with $12 I could not
   afford both an xMult and the floor. Earlier money discipline (no ante-3 Buffoon pack that gave Red Card) matters.
3. Ante 6 blind select: with a max hand ~26k and Big 90000 I was dead entering the ante; nothing to change there,
   the fix had to come in antes 3-5 (an xMult).
Good decisions worth copying: selling Odd Todd for holo Half Joker at ante 1 ($8, carried antes 1-5); skipping the
ante-3 Big for an Orbital(Pair) tag (+3 main-hand levels, cleared The Wall 16112/12800); The Fish with 3-card plays
of face-down cards (Half Joker stays on, a match becomes a pair).

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF the Big Blind tag is Orbital for your main hand (Pair) at ante 2-4, THEN skip the Big Blind for it: +3 levels
  beats the $4-5 Big payout and one shop, and saves rent/perish ticks.
- IF you own Half Joker (<=3 cards), THEN under The Mark play at most 3 face-downs (sorted by suit, one from each suit
  group): 4 face-downs guarantee a pair but switch Half Joker off (~240 instead of ~2000).
- IF you own Burnt Joker, THEN make the round's FIRST discard a Pair (low pair + junk), never a 3-of-a-kind (levels
  Trips) or a no-pair discard (levels High Card); sell it once your main hand is >= ante+4 levels or by ante 4.
- IF The Psychic is the boss and your mult comes from Half Joker, THEN plan 5-card plays without it: use a held Justice
  on a pair card you will play (Glass x2 on the base mult: KK 5250 -> 6750) and buy any flat +mult before it.
- IF your best Pair at ante 5 is < boss/3 of ante 6 (40k) and you hold 0 xMult, THEN every dollar from now goes to an
  xMult or rerolls for one; flat jokers (Blue, Swashbuckler, Stuntman) only buy one more ante.
- IF the post-Small shop would have <$4 to spend at ante 4-6, THEN skipping Small for a Rare/Coupon/Holo tag is worth
  it (Rare gave Stuntman ~x1.9; Coupon gave a free Buffoon + Jumbo Arcana + Justice).
