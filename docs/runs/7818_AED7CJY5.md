## Post-mortem
**Binding constraint:** no scorer engine after ante 2. Both perishable scorers (Crafty +80 chips flush-gated, Gros Michel +15 mult) died inside ante 2, leaving Abstract (+3 mult per joker, i.e. only +6 with 2 jokers) as the only scorer. Ante 3 Small needed 3200 against a projected ~1000-1200 round. Zero xMult was acquired by ante 3 (SCORE CHECK ratio 0.06 at ante 3 shop).

**Decisions I would change:**
1. Ante 1: the two perishable scorers (Crafty, Gros Michel) were bought as the only ante-1 scorers. Both were gone by ante 3. Prefer a permanent scorer even at lower EV (Hanging Chad was ETERNAL and passed over for Crafty).
2. Ante 2 shop at $9: Half Joker ($5, SIM +0.15 P(clear next 3)) was skipped under the $8 floor. Money was $8 at ante 3 start anyway, so the floor cost a scorer for nothing. Corrected rule below.
3. Ante 2 Celestial pack bought as a Jupiter gamble; Jupiter wasn't in the pack, Venus (off-hand) taken. Net effect ~0. Treat Celestial packs as a gamble only when the main-hand planet is worth more than the $6.
4. At the ante-2 Big/Boss, I broke made pairs for flush draws twice (Arm boss: broke 22 pair and 9s pair). Both worked for the boss, but the margin was thin; the flush line only pays with a flush joker, which I did not have at ante 3.

**Concrete if-then rules:**
- If at ante 1 the only scorers are perishable, then buy one eternal or permanent scorer before the Boss, even at $2 EV loss.
- If the ante-2 shop has a scorer with SIM delta >= +0.10 and buying it leaves money >= $4, then buy it and accept the floor dip (floor is for xMult/planets, not conditional scorers).
- If the shopadvise SCORE CHECK ratio < 0.2 at ante 2 or 3 and no xMult is in the shop, then sell dead weight and take the best scorer in the shop regardless of floor.
- If the main hand is Flush at Gold and no flush joker is alive, then the next ante's projected flush is ~1000; plan for a pair/trips build instead of waiting on Jupiter.
- If a blind target is >= 2.5x the projected round damage with 2 jokers, then the run is effectively over; log it and play out.
