## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: boss mechanic (The Club) combined with bad luck on suit distribution. Our whole engine is rank-based (pairs/two pair/full house across arbitrary suits), so a suit-debuff boss doesn't target us specifically - but this particular deal repeatedly paired ranks where one card of the pair happened to be a club, roughly halving 3 of our 4 hands' effective scoring (Quads, Two Pair Q/2, Two Pair K/3 were all >=50% debuffed). We had no non-club-clean big hand available in 3 draws.

One decision I would change: spending our reserved Death copy (from The Fool) on ante-5-shop deck building instead of holding it as the "rescue consumable for a boss" the playbook explicitly recommends. Playbook section 6 says "keep a rescue consumable (Death/Strength) for face-down bosses" but The Club is a suit-debuff boss, not face-down - in hindsight a rescue tarot would have been just as valuable here: Death could have converted one of the last hand's debuffed club cards into a copy of a same-rank non-club card, un-debuffing that scoring slot right before the final hand. The playbook's "keep a rescue consumable" guidance should be broadened from "face-down bosses" to "any boss that can invalidate cards in your hand (face-down OR suit-debuff)".

Bridge issues / CLI problems (exact text):
- Buying a Tarot when both consumable slots are full failed cleanly: `ACTION FAILED: buy_from_shop returned false: no space or card not purchasable None`. This is NOT the retryable "ACTION FAILED ... retry" pattern (no "retry" in the message) - it is a legitimate no-space rejection. shopadvise kept recommending the same unbuyable Temperance/Judgement across 2 shop turns even after slots were confirmed full; had to override with a concrete reason each time.
- Death tarot applies by HAND POSITION, not by the order cards were passed to `sel`. Running `sel 3 1` then picking Death made idx1 (leftmost, lower index) become the copy and idx3 (rightmost, higher index) the source - i.e. "first selected becomes copy of second" (playbook section 7a) actually means "lower-indexed selected card becomes a copy of the higher-indexed one," regardless of which index is typed first in `sel i j`. This inverted my intent once (turned a King into a Ten instead of a Ten into a King) - net-neutral this run but worth fixing in the playbook wording.
- Two separate est-vs-actual misses over 20%, both from partially-debuffed hands: Full House (blind FULLHOUSE call under The House, ante 2) est 640 vs actual 360 (44% miss, hand was worse than declared type assumed); Quads under The Club (ante 5) est 6612 vs actual 4332 (34% miss, 2 of 4 scoring cards debuffed). A third miss (est 340 vs actual 1620, ante 4 Mark boss, blind PAIR play) undershot instead of overshot: the pre-play est for face-down/type-skipped plays doesn't reliably predict the real outcome in either direction.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Broaden "keep a rescue consumable" (section 6) to cover suit-debuff bosses (Club/Goad/Window/Head/Plant), not just face-down ones - a targeted Death/Strength can un-debuff a specific scoring card right before a make-or-break final hand.
2. Clarify the Death tarot mechanic: it applies by ascending hand-position index of the two selected cards (lower index becomes the copy, higher index is the source), not by the click/argument order passed to `sel i j`.
3. Note that `est` for hands with debuffed cards can miss actual score by 30-45% (worse than the general "log if >20% off" threshold) when more than one scoring card is debuffed - treat est as a floor, not a reliable predictor, once 2+ debuffed cards are in the played hand.
