When Popcorn/Mad died (ante 5 Big end), base mult was 3-4 and a Two Pair fell from 19600 to ~6900. Money was $0-8 for the whole run (Small pays $0, Credit Card debt, 3 skips), so rerolls (cost $5-8 after the free D6/Chaos ones) found only junk.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 shop (after Small skip): spent $21 -> $0 on 4 rerolls + a $3 Flush planet looking for xMult; all junk. Without that I would have held $20 for the Duo ($8)/Grabber and the ante-5 Mega Buffoon Pack ($8, seen, unaffordable at $2).

3. I never kept Chaos' free reroll useful: Chaos itself occupied a joker slot all run (6/6 full at ante 4); selling it for a scorer slot was never possible because I never saw a scorer.
