Post-mortem
- Binding constraint: SCALING + MONEY. Zero xMult all run and ~$12 income per ante (Small pays $0 at Gold; three
  shops in a row had $1-3 to spend). Peak hand was 6930 (Trips L2 at full Banner); the ante-4 boss needs 6000 per hand
  with zero slack and ante 5 needs 8300-16700 per hand. The Half Joker engine (2-3 card hands) has a hard ceiling:
  Pair ~3465, Trips ~6900, and nothing in 11 shop visits offered a real multiplier (only Hologram $7 at ante 1 when I
  had $7 and no scorer, Bloodstone, The Order{perish+rental}, Baseball Card with no uncommons).
- Gold Stake sticker reality (this run): 9 jokers seen in shops/packs; 4 Eternal, 3 Rental, 3 Perishable (some both).
  The only clean ones were Hiker-rental-free alternatives... i.e. Banner, Hologram, Square, Wily, Mail-In Rebate,
  Bloodstone, Baseball, Burnt{perish}. Eternal on a cheap scorer is fine; Rental is not (-$3/round is my whole income).
- Decisions I would change:
  1. Ante 1 shop 2 ($7): Hologram $7 (growth xMult) instead of the Buffoon pack that gave Half Joker. With Familiar and
     two Arcana/Standard packs it would have been x1.75+ by ante 4, and I would not be locked into 3-card hands.
  2. Ante 4 shop 1 ($7): Bloodstone was wrong for me, but Burnt Joker{perishable} at the next shop (sell Banner $2 -> $9,
     buy $8) would have given +5 Pair levels (L7 = 100x13) by ante 6; I passed because Banner felt essential. With a
     dying build, trading a +60-chip flat joker for permanent levels is right.
  3. Skipping Small at ante 4 for Meteor was correct (Mercury+Earth), but the Big Blind then had to be won before any
     shop; I was lucky to be dealt five Jacks.
- The House boss: the bridge shows face-down cards as NoneN but the hand is still rank-sorted, so a hidden card's rank is
  bracketed by its neighbours (hidden between 7S and 6S = a 7 or a 6 = guaranteed pair with either). Used it for two
  guaranteed pairs; it does not rescue an under-scaled build.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- If a Gold Stake shop joker has RENTAL, then treat its price as $1 + $3 x rounds left in the run; never buy a rental
  scorer at ante 1-3 (income is ~$12/ante). PERISHABLE scorers are 5-round bridges only; ETERNAL cheap scorers are fine
  as the first 2-3 of 6 slots.
- If you own Half Joker, then your main hands are Pair and Trips only: buy Mercury/Venus, Wily/Sly-type +chips, and
  xMult; never buy Two Pair/Straight/Flush jokers or planets (Earth/Uranus/Saturn are dead).
- If a growth xMult (Hologram, Constellation) is offered at ante 1-2 for all your money and you already have one
  scorer, then buy it over a second flat scorer: Gold Stake ante 4 (9000/13500/18000) cannot be cleared by flat jokers.
- If the boss is The Arm and your hand types are L1, then play them freely: The Arm cannot lower a level-1 hand.
- If Banner is your chip source, then plan every round with zero discards: each discard costs ~1000 (pair) to ~1400
  (trips) per remaining hand; discard only when the made hands cannot reach the requirement.
- If the hand is face down (The House/The Fish), then read the rank-sorted positions: a hidden card between two cards of
  the same rank (or between rank r and r-1) is known; play it as a free third card with a visible pair.
- If ante 4 Gold Stake is next and you have 0 xMult and < $10, then expect to lose the ante-4 boss: prefer any
  permanent level source (Burnt Joker, planets) over packs; spend down to $0 on levels for the main hand.
