## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING, specifically lack of a second reliable xMult and running out of hands/discards before the Big Blind requirement was met. By ante 5 Big Blind we had: Joker Stencil (decayed to x2, will hit x1 once 6th slot fills), Hologram (x1.5, slow grower), Constellation (x1.3, slow grower - only fed by Celestial packs/planets bought, and we could only afford ~1 per shop), Supernova (flat mult scaling with repeat plays, still modest), Luchador (boss insurance, never used). None of these are strong enough xMult by ante 5; the run's real engine was HAND LEVELS (Pair L3, Two Pair L3, Full House L2, Straight L2, Three of a Kind L2) plus a handful of card enhancements (2x Glass King, Bonus Jacks, Gold/Red Seal 7, Glass/Gold Seal 9). That was enough through ante 4 but the Big Blind at ante 5 (16500, a ~2.2x jump from ante 4's boss 10000) outpaced it.

Money was managed reasonably (sold Egg for $26 to buy Constellation at ante 4, spent essentially everything every shop with no hoarding), so money was not the binding constraint - it was that no strong permanent xMult (x2+ reliable, not decaying) ever appeared in the shop across 13 shop visits, matching playbook 7a's observation that xMult jokers are rare. Joker Stencil in particular was a trap: it looked great at x6 with 0 other jokers in ante 1, but its value mechanically fights against the "fill all 6 slots by ante 4" strategy, decaying to x1 exactly when the run needs the most scaling (a full 6-joker board). In hindsight it should have been sold once 2-3 slots were taken and a real scoring joker was available, rather than kept as one of the 6 permanent slots.

One decision I would change: at Shop11 (ante 4, $20) I filled the 5th joker slot with Luchador (boss insurance) instead of a second scaling joker; Luchador was never used (no boss blind forced a disable decision this run - The Head/Goad were suit debuffs we played through fine). With one more scoring/xMult slot instead of Luchador, the Big Blind at ante 5 likely clears. Concretely: I would keep Luchador as a shop-slot option to buy reactively right before a genuinely dangerous boss (The Arm, The Ox, Amber Acorn, etc.), not proactively fill a joker slot with it four antes early when Two Pair/Full House scaling was still short of target.

No `est` vs actual score discrepancy exceeded 20% anywhere this run - the CLI's estimates were consistently accurate (all recorded plays matched or nearly matched `est`, including debuffed-card notes correctly zeroing scoring for face-down/debuffed cards).

Bridge issues: none. Every command behaved as documented. One near-miss: `pick 3` on a Hierophant card once aborted with `ABORTED: The Hierophant targets 2 hand card(s) but 1 are highlighted` after only `sel 3` - the CLI correctly caught the mismatch and did not consume the tarot; re-running `sel 3 4` then `pick 3` succeeded. This is expected/documented CLI safety behavior, not a bug, but worth noting that Hierophant's target count is not always 1 (varies, possibly scales with something) so always check the abort message rather than assuming a single target.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Add an explicit note that Joker Stencil is a fast-decaying xMult that actively conflicts with the "fill 6 slots by ante 4" rule - recommend selling it once 3+ scoring jokers are owned, rather than treating it as a permanent slot.
2. Section 7a should state more concretely how many *simultaneous* xMult sources are needed by ante 5 Big Blind specifically (this run had effectively ~4 weak/slow xMult sources - Stencil/Hologram/Constellation/Supernova combined - and still fell short by ~18%); a single-number heuristic like "reliable xMult total multiplier should be >= Nx by ante 5 Big Blind" would help gauge shortfall earlier (e.g. at the ante 5 shop, before entering the Big Blind) rather than discovering the shortfall mid-round with no discards left.
3. Luchador guidance should be sharper: buy it reactively right before a blind whose boss is confirmed dangerous (visible at blind select/shop as "Upcoming boss"), not as a proactive slot-filler; this run's Luchador was a wasted slot for its entire life.
