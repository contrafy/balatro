## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING. The only permanent xMult was Hologram (holo, x1.75 -> x2.0), fed by 3 Standard packs and
Certificate. Ramen (perishable x2) carried ante 4-5 and died after The Plant. That left ~19k per hand going into ante 6
(SCORE CHECK 0.25 = EMERGENCY) with 18 shop views + 8 rerolls that never offered a reliable xMult (only perishables:
Ramen, The Trio; conditional: Bloodstone, The Tribe). The Trio + Orbital (Trips L5) made a Trips hand ~59-72k, but the
Big needed 90k and the 9-card opening hand had no pair; two 5-card discards found one pair and a final 444.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 shop ($9): I passed the Jumbo Standard ($6, Hologram +0.25) to keep the money floor. Hologram was the only
   growth xMult; with a feed plan, every Standard/Certificate card was +14-25% permanent. Feed it on every shop where
   rent/floor allows from ante 2.
2. Ante 6: skipping the Small for Orbital removed the shop between it and the Big and a Certificate card. The Big then
   had to be won by a Trips; with 3 hands and 2 discards a specific hand type is ~35-60%. Playing the Small (0.96x
   projection) was also bad. The real error came earlier: ante 5 money ($41 + $36) went to levels, Arcana and Celestial
   packs while xMult hunting needed rerolls; at ante 5 with 1 xMult and a perishable one dying, rerolls/Buffoon packs
   should come first.
3. Ante 5 Plant shop: sold Scary Face for Hanging Chad. Fine for The Plant, but it cost face-pair chips for ante 6.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF a perishable xMult has exactly 2 rounds left and the boss is 2 blinds away, THEN skip only ONE of Small/Big (the
  one whose tag pays now); it survives to the boss either way and you keep a shop view (ante 5 here: Big cleared 62k vs
  37.5k, then a $41 shop before The Plant).
- IF you own Hologram, THEN every card-adding source is a growth xMult buy: Standard pack $4 = +0.25, Certificate
  ($1 rental) = +0.25 per round + a sealed card; take them before Celestial packs.
- IF Temperance is in the shop and your jokers' sell values total $10+, THEN buy-use it ($3 -> $13-16 net); it appeared
  3 times here and paid +$42.
- IF The Mark shows 4 face-down cards, THEN `sort suit` reveals their suits; any 3 face-downs make a face pair 7/9,
  and the 4th face-down is a safe face High Card for the rest.
- IF you own The Trio and an Orbital tag names Three of a Kind, THEN the +3 levels make Trips your main hand, but a
  blind needing one specific type still fails ~40%: do not skip the shop before a blind you can only clear with it.
