## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: MONEY -> SCALING. I bought three rentals in ante 1-2 (Popcorn, Raised Fist, Jolly) and held two at once for 5 rounds: ~$21 of rent against Big/Boss payouts of $4-5. Money sat at $0-3 from ante 2 shop 2 to the end, so I never bought a single planet (Two Pair L1 at ante 4), never saw a Buffoon pack after ante 2, and owned zero xMult all run. The two eternals from packs (Lusty +3/heart, Clever +80 on Two Pair) were the only permanent scorers. Every blind through ante 3 was cleared (several one-hand clears), but with L1 hands and flat jokers the ceiling was ~2700/hand vs 3000/hand needed at the ante 4 Small.

Decisions I would change:
- Ante 1 Big shop ($7): Popcorn{RENTAL} $1. Popcorn helped the Window, but rent + decay meant I sold it 2 rounds later for $1 after paying $6. Keeping $7 for the ante 2 shop (Jumbo Celestial / Buffoon) would have been better: the Window was a 600 blind that Pair K + Lusty plus a Flush could likely clear.
- Ante 2 Big shop ($2): Jolly{RENTAL} as the second rental. It added +8 mult but locked me at $0-3 for the rest of the run. With Fist still alive for 3 rounds, I should have left with $2 and started building interest/planet money.
- Ante 3: with ~$2 and no planet, the run was already lost at ante 4; the error was in ante 1-2 economy, not ante 3 play.

What worked (reusable):
- Raised Fist play: fillers = lowest cards, and discarding low cards before playing a made Two Pair raised Fist from +4 to +16-24 mult (Pair 3: advise est 400 vs 592; ante 3 Big: Two Pair 2346 -> drew Full House 5712).
- Clever Joker (+80 chips) turns Two Pair into the main hand at ante 2-3 (est 3000-4500 with one +mult joker and Fist).
- The Mouth with a Two Pair engine: lock Two Pair even when a Full House scores more on hand 1; Two Pair is re-makeable with 2 discards (cleared 7110/6400), FH is not.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF you already own one rental at ante 1-2, THEN do not buy another non-xMult rental: two rentals cost $6 per round, more than a Big Blind pays, and your money stays under $5 until the run dies.
- IF your main hand is still L1 and you have under $5 at the ante 3 shop with no xMult, THEN the ante 4 Small (9000) is out of reach; prevent it at ante 2 by keeping $4+ for main-hand planets/Celestial packs instead of a third flat joker.
- IF you own Raised Fist, THEN pick the lowest cards as fillers and discard low cards before playing a made hand, so the lowest held card is as high as possible.
- IF The Mouth and your hands are Two Pair with Clever/Mad, THEN lock Two Pair on hand 1 even if a Full House scores more, unless the FH alone clears the blind.
