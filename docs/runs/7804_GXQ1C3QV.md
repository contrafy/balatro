## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SUPPLY + MONEY at ante 1-2. In 7 shop views (3 rerolls) and 1 Buffoon pack, the only scoring jokers offered without
an eternal/rental/perishable trap were Wrathful ($5), Mystic Summit ($5) and Golden Joker (econ). The one scorer I had (Smiley) was
perishable and died exactly at the ante-3 Small. The Rare Tag (Big skip at ante 1) produced Vagabond {ETERNAL,RENTAL}: worthless.
I never had more than $11, so rerolls left $0-6 and could not buy what they revealed.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante-1 Big skip for Rare Tag. It cost ~$5 (the Big payout + a shop) and the tag joker rolled an eternal rental Vagabond. At Gold
   the Rare Tag joker carries the same 30/30/30 sticker odds, so its expected value is far lower than at low stakes. With $0 in hand
   the extra Big shop would have had ~$5 for a $4-5 scorer. Playing the Big would also have ticked Smiley earlier, though, so this is
   close; but the Rare Tag should not be valued as "a free rare joker" at Gold.
2. Ante-2 Big shop ($8): Hanging Chad{ETERNAL} over Wrathful Joker. Chad was right for The Arm (KKQQ 2560 one-shot) and the ante-3
   Small (FH 2712), but both of those leaned on Smiley; once Smiley perished Chad alone adds ~20 chips. Wrathful would also have been
   weak. The real error was not having a permanent +mult before ante 3.
3. Ante-3 first shop ($11): rerolled to $6 instead of buying the Arcana pack ($4) with $7 left. With Chad, any of Empress / Justice /
   Magician / Hierophant on a face card is a retriggered effect (Glass first card x2 three times = x8). Probably not enough either.

What worked:
- Hanging Chad + Smiley Face: the first scoring face triggers Smiley 3 times. Pair K 1100, Two Pair KKQQ 2560, FH 2712 at ante 2-3 with
  only two jokers. Hand ORDER matters: `move hand` the face card to position 1 (FH AAAKK est 1610 -> 2712).
- Big Blind at ante 2 (1500) with 1 joker: High Card K as a 4-card dig keeping A K J T for a Q (P~45%) beat Pair K (360, then 832 from
  one hand, P~25%). It hit: Broadway 1539.

## Reusable rules (if-then)
- IF you own Hanging Chad, THEN before every play put the card whose effect you want tripled (a face with Smiley/Scary, a Glass/Mult
  card, a Photograph face) in the leftmost scoring position with `move hand FROM 1`; `advise` does not suggest the reorder by itself.
- IF the Rare Tag is offered at Gold and you own <2 scorers, THEN do not skip a blind for it: the tag joker can be (and here was) an
  eternal rental utility joker; the skipped blind's payout + shop is worth more.
- IF your only scorer is perishable and dies before the next ante, THEN the shops before that point must buy a permanent +mult
  (Wrathful/Greedy/any flat) over utility/engine pieces; an engine piece with no scorer next to it scores nothing.
- IF one hand cannot reach the remaining requirement but a 4-card dig keeps a 1-out draw to a winning hand (Q for Broadway), THEN
  play the low-scoring dig as a High Card of a face (Smiley/Chad count) and keep the draw, rather than banking a mid pair.

## Advisor/CLI defects
- `advise` FH AAAKK est 1610 and ADVICE play without reordering; moving KC to position 1 (Chad retrigger + Smiley) made it 2712. The
  advisor should consider reordering for Hanging Chad / Photograph (it already models order in est).
- `shopadvise` WARNING "none of your jokers scores on a plain Pair" printed while owning Smiley Face, which it lists as a Pair floor
  joker in the same warning (it fires on face pairs).
- `shopadvise` labels Flash Card as "[UNKNOWN]: unknown effect" (+2 mult per reroll, growth).
- `shopadvise` ranks Hanging Chad{ETERNAL} -15 UTIL even when it doubles Smiley/Photograph output (est itself showed Pair K 360 -> 1100).
- Blind select: no warning that a Rare Tag at Gold can roll a sticker joker (it did: Vagabond{ETERNAL,RENTAL} at $0).
Bridge issues: none; every action settled first try (52 steps).
