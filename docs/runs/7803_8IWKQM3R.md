## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING, and RENT made it worse. Pair L1 all run (I bought no planets), one xMult (Ramen x2) from ante 4, no second one. From ante 4 I carried three rentals (Half Joker, Hanging Chad, Ramen), which cost $9 a round. The blind payouts could not cover that, so every shop from the ante-4 Big on had $2-12 and could not buy the second xMult or a level. The best hand at ante 5 was ~13k (pair KK, glass first), against the 16.7k per hand that the Flint needed even before halving.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 Small shop: Ramen{RENTAL} was correct, since it was the only xMult offered and doubled every hand. Buying Hanging Chad{ETERNAL,RENTAL} one shop earlier was the problem. It was a strong scorer (high card face 2625 cleared The Water), but an eternal rental cannot be sold, so its $3 a round lasted the whole run. With Chad, Half and Ramen all rented, money hit $2 by ante 5. A non-eternal scorer, or the $24 kept for interest, would probably have left $25+ at ante 5 to buy an xMult.
2. I never bought a Pair planet. Glass first under Chad multiplies the BASE mult by x8, so every Mercury level would have been worth roughly x8 its normal mult. That is the strongest level-up I have seen: Pair L3 (mult 4) with a glass first card gives 32 base mult before the jokers.
3. Ante 3: Hermit on $15 (sell Egg -> Hermit -> $30) was good. Spending $5 of it on a reroll for a rental was not.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Hanging Chad and a Glass card, THEN put the glass card FIRST in hand order (leftmost scoring card). It is retriggered 3x = x8 on the mult accumulated up to that point (base hand mult). Then level the main hand: each level's +mult is multiplied by 8. A glass K first in an L1 spade flush made 18704 at ante 4.
- IF you own Hanging Chad + Scary Face/Half Joker, THEN a lone face card played as HIGH CARD (1-3 cards) scores like a pair (2625 at ante 3, 5118 with Ramen). This is a strong The Water/Needle/Psychic-free answer. The `hands` list does not show these lines. Check them with `sel <idx>`.
- IF you already pay rent on 2 jokers, THEN do not buy a third rental unless it is an xMult. NEVER buy an ETERNAL+RENTAL flat/retrigger joker before ante 6. Rent of $9 a round is more than the Small+Big+Boss payouts.
- IF money is under $5 at ante 5+ with rentals ticking, and the Small/Big need more per hand than your typical hand, THEN skipping Small/Big is legitimate: no shop is affordable anyway, rent does not tick, and a Buffoon/joker tag still adds a joker. (Here it got me to the Flint with Sly, but the boss was out of reach either way.)
- IF a shop has Riff-raff, you have 3+ empty slots and you are in EMERGENCY at ante 2, THEN Riff-raff ($6) is ~4 sticker-free Commons in 2 blinds. Sell the junk ones next shop for a real scorer: it gave Egg (later Hermit fuel), Walkie and Even Steven.
