## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. Ante 5 needs ~17k/hand across 3 hands vs my ~10k/hand. I never found a second lasting xMult; the build was flat (Blue +104 chips, 2x Greedy, Abstract) + Obelisk + a 3-round perishable Blackboard, and money was spent to $0-8 for 3 straight antes (Hermit/rerolls at ante 3 found nothing; Wraith emptied $24 for an Invisible Joker that copied a weak Greedy).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3-4: I took 3 eternals (Blue, Greedy, Abstract) + Obelisk as eternal; with 4 eternal slots and no way to sell, the Invisible Joker copy landed on the worst joker (Greedy) and no slot was ever free for a Rare/xMult. Greedy Joker{E} at ante 1 (diamond-only +3) was the weak pick; it also had no real mult at The Window/Goad.
2. Ante 4 Wraith: spending $24 (all my money) for a random rare gave Invisible Joker, whose payoff is a random copy of 5 mostly flat jokers. At $24 I should have kept money for 3 rerolls/Buffoon packs.
3. Obelisk handling: I let Pair, Two Pair, High Card and Flush all reach 4 plays. At the Goad every legal hand then reset Obelisk (x2.0 -> x1). Play ONE sacrificial type (e.g. High Card) clearly above the others from ante 3 and never tie them.
4. Ante 5 Goad: spades debuffed and I had converted 3 hearts to spades with The World (the CLI warned me). The Goad also meant my held-black Blackboard trick worked but played cards had to be red/club.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Blackboard (x3 if all HELD cards are Spades/Clubs) THEN play 5 cards per hand (4 fillers incl. every red card) so the 3 held cards are black; est printed by `hands` already includes the x3 only when the held cards are all black. A single red card played alone (7 held black) also works (High AH 9028 vs 3009).
- IF a boss debuffs spades (Goad), debuffed spades still count as black for Blackboard (bypass debuff): keep them as the held cards and play only red/club cards.
- IF Blue Joker is owned, remember it counts cards remaining in the deck: its +chips drop during the round (each draw/discard), so play the big hand first and discard less.
- IF all hand types you can make are tied at the top play count with Obelisk, THEN the next play resets it; plan one sacrificial type before the boss, never tie 3+ types (OBELISK WARNING in `s` says it).
- IF Wraith is offered at $20+ money, prefer keeping the money unless a slot is free AND an Eternal-free copy source is not the plan; Wraith zeroes $ (cost me 4 shops of flexibility).
