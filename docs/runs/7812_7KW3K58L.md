## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: The Needle (one hand, 3200) with a Pair-family build that tops out ~3100 on a good Two Pair; no growth xMult, 0 real xMult by ante 3 (two Photographs are card-phase). The loss was decided by a 68-chip gap and a breaking decision.

Decisions I would change:
1. Needle, second discard: I held a MADE Two Pair QQ77 (est 3132 vs need 3200) with 1 discard and 1 hand, and broke QQ for a 4-club flush draw (solver 0.75 vs 0.64 rule pick). It whiffed (3 of 4 draws missed). A better line was the rule pick (disc 4 8, keep QS QC 7H 7C 6C 6D KC: 2 draws for any 8/9/T/K/Q/7 pair-up, flush still p0.40, floor Two Pair) or even better: at the FIRST discard, keep Two Pair 9977 + KC QC and draw for a bigger Two Pair/KKQQ. The flush plan in the first discard threw away 99 and 77 for p0.61.
2. Before the Needle shop I should have computed that Two Pair est 3132-3200 was the ceiling and bought +chips (Hierophant bonus +30 chips on two Two-Pair cards would have added +60 chips = ~+2800 est!) with the leftover $13. The advisor listed Hierophant at $3 and I ignored it; with Photographs the base-mult x4 makes every extra chip worth ~46 points, so +30 chips on 2 scoring cards was +2000 total.
3. Ante 3 first shop: 2 rerolls ($11) hit nothing; Standard pack ($4) could have fed Vampire (kept from ante 1 instead of selling it): Vampire + enhanced cards was the only growth xMult available and I sold it for $3 on day 1.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the boss is The Needle (1 hand) AND your best made hand est is within 5% below need AND you hold a last discard, THEN keep the made hand and draw only to improve its ranks/chips; never break a made pair for a flush draw (3 of 4 draws missed here; 'solver 0.75 vs 0.64' margin was 0.11, but the solver counts 4 draws).
- IF you own Photograph (or any card-phase xMult) AND flat +mult jokers, THEN each card chip is worth ~(base mult x Photograph + flat mult) points; Hierophant/Standard-pack bonus cards and +chips jokers are cheap hand-score boosters before a one-hand boss.
- IF Invisible Joker has been owned 2 round-ends THEN selling it duplicates a random OTHER joker for free and pays its sell value; here it hit Photograph (x4 on a face hand). Buy it at ante 2 with 2+ good jokers and free slots.
- IF a Skip Tag / Economy Tag is on the Small and you are broke with <2 shop-worthy items, THEN skip it: +$7..$10 and no perishable tick (used twice; both paid).
- IF you face The Fish, THEN discard first (draws come face up), keep face pairs, play before drawing face-down cards.
