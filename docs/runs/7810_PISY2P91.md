## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: scaling. Whole run had zero xMult and only three flat scorers (Joker +4 perishable, Even Steven, Misprint perishable); ~1500-2000 per hand at ante 3 vs a 6400 boss (needs ~2100 x 3 of ONE type under The Mouth). Never saw an xMult joker in 6 shops/4 packs.

Decisions I would change:
- Ante 1 first shop: passed Gluttonous ($5) for a Buffoon pack that gave junk and left $1. A $5 +3/club joker is weak, but the pack lottery cost me a full shop of progress; roughly neutral.
- The Mouth: I discarded twice chasing a Full House (locking FH would have scored ~2500 each) and missed; with three pairs in hand I should have played Two Pair 88/22 FIRST on hand 1 with discards still available? No: the real error was spending both discards before the lock. If a lock-type hand (Two Pair 1472-1978) is already made at hand 1 and you need a type you can REPEAT 3x, keep a discard to rebuild the second Two Pair; I ended with a deck draw of a single pair after hand 1 and no discard, so hands 2-3 scored 0 (Mouth: any other type = 0 even though 4422 short anyway).
- Credit Card + Judgement + reroll at ante 3 (money 7 -> -8) bought Jupiter and a Square Joker (+4 chips per 4-card hand, dead with Splash). Judgement into the last free slot gave a junk joker. A better use of the debt would have been 2 more rerolls for a scorer.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF you own Splash AND Even Steven (or Scholar/Walkie/Smiley-type per-card jokers) THEN play 5 cards every hand (fillers of the right parity/rank): Two Pair with five even cards scored 1978; Flush 1104; FH L2 1824+.
- IF The Mouth is the boss and your best hands are Two Pair/FH ~2000 against 6400 THEN it needs 3 repeats of the same type; plan the first hand's type by what you can rebuild after drawing (Two Pair), and keep one discard for it.
- IF the shop has Judgement ($3) and your slots are full except one, THEN it is a lottery on a common joker (I got Square Joker); only buy it with 2 free slots.
- IF Credit Card is offered at $1 while in EMERGENCY, it is a strong buy (-$20 debt), but spend the debt on rerolls/packs for scorers, not on tarot lotteries.
- IF a Wheel of Fortune is offered with a 2-pick Mega Arcana, taking it is a 25% edition lottery (it said nothing this time); Temperance (sum of sell values) is a clean +$9 when you hold 6 jokers.
