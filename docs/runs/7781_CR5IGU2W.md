Playbook suggestions:
1. `hands`/`advise` est should account for Splash-style "every played card scores" jokers by picking the chip/suit-optimal filler (or at least warn "filler choice matters, suit/club/heart bonuses not modeled") rather than silently picking an arbitrary filler card - this run repeatedly needed manual override of the suggested filler indices to unlock several-fold more score, including a near-loss at ante 4 Big Blind (7555 vs 7500 needed, saved only by overriding the advised filler).
2. Add an explicit playbook callout: Flower Pot (and similar "all N suits/conditions" xMult) should never be counted as satisfying the "2 xMult by ante 4+" rule on its own once a boss or bad draw can strip a needed suit from the final hand - prefer it only as a 3rd+ xMult source, not a primary one.
3. shopadvise's "sell Splash" recommendation (raised twice this run) is actively harmful for a Splash-centric filler-scoring build: Splash was the single highest-leverage joker in play (every filler-optimization override in this log depended on it) despite being flagged "weakest joker." Recommend shopadvise detect when Splash is present and stop suggesting it as the sell target, or at minimum tag it "load-bearing, verify before selling."
