# Run ZUR6ZGNM (port 7780) - LOSS at stake 3, ante 3, Big Blind (3488/3900)

Post-mortem: binding constraint was scaling - zero xMult jokers ever appeared in 3 antes of shops (only flat/conditional pieces: Misprint random mult, Droll Joker flush-only, Shoot the Moon queen-count), and hand levels were capped at L2 by ante 3. The playbook's own stake-3 data (section 7a) flags exactly this failure mode ("an xMult by ante 3, and never enter ante 4 with fewer than one") but no xMult was ever on offer to buy, and money never exceeded $9 in a single shop so a reroll-hunt for one was never affordable either. Given no xMult appeared, the shortfall (412 out of 3900, ~11%) was close enough that a different ante-1-2 spending choice might have closed it.
One decision I would change: at the ante-2->3 shop with $9, spent $4+$4 on two Celestial Packs (Jupiter for Flush, Uranus for Two Pair) instead of holding back $3 for a rescue tarot (Death/Strength/Justice) from the shop's card row. Neither planet purchase was a guaranteed match for the main hand (no Earth/Mercury offered), and a held Strength or Death could have turned the final 1777-short hand into a clear by ranking up or copying a card into the Queens/7s. Playbook 7b says buy the rescue tarot from ante 3 on when none is held - this run never held one and had no way to react to the shortfall on the last hand.
Bridge issues: none that aborted an action (no "ACTION FAILED" retries needed this run). One unexplained observation: a Blue Seal card (from the Trance spectral) was scored in a Two Pair play (ante 2 boss) and should create a Planet card consumable, but no consumable ever appeared in subsequent `s` output (cons_slots stayed effectively empty/unused for the rest of the run) - possibly the seal fired but was immediately auto-sold/discarded off-screen, or the trigger condition needs the card to be the FIRST scoring card, or it simply did not fire; not confirmed as a bug, just flagged for the next agent to watch for.

1. Section 5/7a could add: when no xMult joker has appeared by ante 3 and money is thin (never reaches the $15 reroll-hunt threshold), treat a shop-bought rescue tarot ($3) as higher priority than a second non-matching Celestial Pack - it directly answers the "forced final hand falls just short" failure mode seen here.
