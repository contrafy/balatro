Ante 1 (Rare/Charm/The Club 600, $4): Small: Straight 240 + 2P 100. Shop $5: Clever{E} $4. Big: 2P 256 + 2P 232. Club: FH 556 + pair + trips. $6 end.
Ante 2 (Coupon/Orbital/The Wheel 2000): Photograph{E} $5 + Uranus (2P L2) in first shop (emergency 0.13). Skipped Small (Coupon). Big: 2P KK88 936 + 2P JJ99 948. Coupon shop: Earth, Standard -> Glass+Goldseal KD. Reroll ($5 of $9) found junk. Wheel: 2P KKJJ 1920 + 2P 414.

## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. Best normal hand was ~3-5k at ante 4 (Big 13500 needs ~4.5k x 3 with zero misses). The build had 0 reliable xMult: Photograph is card-trigger, The Order only fires on Straights, everything else flat (Clever, Devious, Mad, Swashbuckler). Money was $1-6 at every shop from ante 2 on, so no xMult/Buffoon could be afforded; I spent $4+$4 on Arcana packs at ante 3 and $4 Celestial (Venus, a wasted level) at ante 4 while broke.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 shop: bought The Order ($8) + Venus pack ($4) to $1. The Order fires on straights only (~10-25% of hands); Venus levelled Trips, which I never play. A Two Pair planet or keeping $13 for the Coupon shop (free joker next) would have been better. Do not buy a conditional xMult for a hand type you are not building.
2. Ante 4 Big: the solver's straight dig (play junk, keep K-Q-J-T) is a 7-out draw per hand; it missed twice in a row (0.61 then 0.66 each). Two hand slots burned on 210-point junk plays means the final hand had to be Two Pair 5400. Solver "p 0.95/0.80" for the dig was far above reality; the plays only cleared if the straight hit AND hit with a face-first order. Treat a dig p>0.8 for a 7-out draw as overstated.
3. Ante 3: two Arcana packs ($8 total) at ante 3 got Justice + Judgement (Devious, junk). OK value, but left $2 and no rent/interest base.
4. Ante 4: skipping Big (Negative tag) was NOT an option as the Coupon shop was needed, fine; but I should have looked at playing the Small at 9000 for its extra shop? No (it pays $0). Skipping Small was right.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Photograph and a Glass face card, THEN face pairs/Full House with both ranks ~5-10k at ante 3 (10036 for KKK99 at ante 3); the combo carried antes 2-3 but stops scaling at ~5k for two pair at ante 4.
- IF broke ($1-6) at every shop from ante 2, THEN the run is already on borrowed time: stop buying 4-dollar planets/packs for off-types and save for the first r/g xMult.
- IF The Eye is the boss, THEN the solver's 3-distinct-type plans worked here: Trips (630), Pair (420, kept KD 9H 9D), then FH 10036 (it overrode advise's Two Pair 5874 vs 5350 need - the safer bigger hand).
- IF a Coupon Tag is on the Small Blind and cash is <$4, THEN skip it: Small pays $0 and the post-Big shop becomes free (got a free Celestial/Standard/Glass K at ante 2).

Advisor/CLI defects:
- shopadvise ranked "Celestial Pack (Uranus gamble)" first at ante 4 with $5 and an xMult just bought, and the pack gave Venus/Neptune/Pluto: no main-hand planet. At $5 the pack is a 1/3 gamble that took the last $4.
- Solver p for straight digs (7 outs, 4-card draw p~0.6) printed 0.95/0.80 clear; actual two-in-a-row miss. The p seems to double count the second hand's draw.
- advise said PLAY TWOPAIR 5874 (need 5350) on the Eye's last hand "because Two Pair is the hand you are leveling", although a Full House est 10036 was available; leveling is irrelevant on a clear-the-blind hand.
- advise at ante 3 Small (Hand 2 after dig): "play PAIR T est 240" showed pair est 420/240 inconsistently when a debuffed card was in the hand (Club ante 1: est 40 vs needed 44, actual pair T 40 -> fine).
