Post-mortem: binding constraint was VARIANCE/DRAW LUCK, not money or scaling. Full House L6 with Caino's ~2x multiplier could clear 60000-80000 in one hand when it hit (seen repeatedly antes 3-5), comfortably beating even ante 8 targets (100000). But ante 6's two blinds both failed to deal trips+pair together across 3 full hands + 3 discards each - we fell back to Two Pair/Pair (worth only 1/6th to 1/3rd of a Full House) and came up just short (21818/30000) on the Big Blind decider.
The ONE decision I would change: never leveled Two Pair (Uranus never appeared in any Celestial pack all game) despite it being our most common fallback hand (played it far more often than Full House in practice, especially when trips didn't materialize). Every Celestial pack pick went to Earth (Full House) on the assumption "level the hand you actually play," but the DATA shows Two Pair was actually played nearly as often as Full House and stayed at L1 (20x2) the entire run - a single Uranus level (Two Pair L2 ~40x4) would likely have been enough to clear the final Big Blind (est would roughly double: 5500->~11000 already with Caino, going to L2 would push it further). Lesson: track which hand type is ACTUALLY landing most often mid-run, not just which one is "leveled so far," and pick planets accordingly instead of always doubling down on the single highest-level hand.
Also: at $8-12 with joker slots full every ante 5-6 shop, we passed on selling a weak/situational joker (Gluttonous Joker only fired when clubs specifically scored, which was inconsistent) for a stronger option like Hack or Sock and Buskin (retrigger face cards, direct synergy with Photograph+our face-pair engine). Being unwilling to sell-to-swap when slots are full cost us potential upgrades in antes 5-6.

Calibration note (important for playbook/CLI): Caino (Legendary joker, obtained via The Soul spectral card at ante 4) is NOT modeled by the `hands`/`advise` estimator - every single estimate the entire run undershot the actual score by almost exactly 2.0x once Caino was active (est 5474->act 10948; est 33807->act 64176 [1.9x]; est 30816->act 61632 [2.0x]; est 40320->act 80640 [2.0x]; est 5600->act 11200; est 4070->act 8140; est 2418->act 4836; est 3500->act 7000; est 1909->act 3818; est 5500->act 11000 [2.0x, the final losing hand]). This is a >20% deviation on every single hand played after acquiring Caino (roughly 10+ instances). Recommend the CLI's estimator special-case Caino as a flat x2 score multiplier (or discover its exact ability text) so `advise`/`hands` estimates stay accurate once a run has it - in this run it caused us to underestimate how close/far we were from clearing blinds, though thankfully never in a way that caused a bad discard/play decision (we always had the correct relative ranking of candidate hands).

Playbook suggestions:
1. Add explicit guidance: track which hand type is landing most often in actual play (not just intent) and prioritize its Celestial planet over the "official" main hand once ante 3+ data shows a mismatch, since Full House requires trips+pair simultaneously (rarer) while Two Pair only needs two separate pairs (commoner) - both are worth leveling if one is carrying most of the round's hands.
2. When all 6 joker slots are full and money is available (ante 5+), evaluate selling the weakest/most conditional flat-mult joker (e.g. a suit-specific one that rarely fires) for a strictly stronger option rather than defaulting to LEAVE/endshop - "never leave money unspent at ante 6+" should include sell-then-buy, not just direct buys.
3. Note that Legendary jokers obtained via The Soul (Canio, Triboulet, Yorick, Chicot, Perkeo) may not be modeled by the score estimator - flag this explicitly so agents know to expect actual scores to exceed `est`, and don't panic-discard when `est` looks short if the joker roster includes an unmodeled Legendary.
