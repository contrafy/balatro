## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no xMult and money. Build was flat +mult (Smiley, Scholar, Fibonacci, Mad, Crazy) at base levels: great Ace/Full House hands made 5-7k, typical hand 2-3k, vs 18000/3 = 6000 per hand. I was at $1-5 for the whole run (rent, Small pays $0, three skips), so no reroll/pack ever hunted for an xMult; the one Buffoon pack I could afford at ante 4 was junk.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2: skipped Big (Rare tag) to avoid a 1500 blind; fine, but it cost the Big income and the shop. Fibonacci (the key flat scorer) came from the tag, so the Rare tag paid.
2. Ante 4: skipped Small and Big, which took me to the Flint with $1 and no new xMult; Mad Joker rental still ticking. With $1 nothing was buyable anyway. A stronger alternative would have been to sell Crazy Joker (straight-gated, dead) and DNA early (ante 3) for slots + money, keeping $8+ for the ante-4 Buffoon pack.
3. Ante 3 shop ($5): skipped the Jumbo Celestial ($6, unaffordable); I should have sold Crazy/DNA ($2+$4) to afford it plus keep $8 floor, because levels (Earth/Mercury: +25 chips on ~60 flat mult ~ +30-45%) were the only scaling available.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Scholar + Fibonacci, THEN Aces/2/3/5/8 pairs and Full Houses are the hit hands (AA+333 FH 6882 at L1): discard toward Ace pairs/trips, play Aces as the scoring cards; Grim/DNA/Familiar-type Ace adders are real scaling.
- IF a flat +mult build has chips << mult (e.g. 30 chips vs 60 mult), THEN every planet level on the played hand is +25-45%: buy Earth/Mercury/Uranus first, they beat a 3rd flat joker.
- IF The Flint (halves base chips and mult) is the boss, THEN est drops ~25% on hands whose value comes from base chips; flat +mult jokers are not halved, so Two Pair/FH est stays ~5-7k.
- IF the Hook with a 4-flush draw and need ~600: Flush est 603 cleared by 3 chips; est from `hands` is exact when no Misprint-style jokers are owned.
- IF a tag shop is the only shop you can afford (money < $5 after rent), THEN skip the blind for the tag: Rare tag gave Fibonacci/DNA, Top-up gave Crazy, Ethereal gave Grim.
