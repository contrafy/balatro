Post-mortem: Binding constraint was BOSS FIT, not money or scaling - the engine (Pair/Trips/Full House,
  single xMult) had no strong Two-Pair, Flush, or High-Card contingency, and The Eye (no repeat types)
  specifically punishes a build with only one strong scoring shape. By the time Pair was spent (hand 1),
  every remaining hand this round was forced into a type we had not built for (Two Pair L2, Straight L2).
  The one decision to change: at the ante-3/4 shops, when no xMult was in the rotation, spend a couple of
  the reroll dollars on a SECOND planet type (Uranus for Two Pair) instead of exclusively hunting xMult -
  a build with 3+ leveled types survives The Eye; a build with 1-2 does not. Also: only 1 xMult (Photograph)
  was ever found across the whole run despite persistent rerolling within the money floor - bad luck, not
  misplay, but it left no margin once the boss neutralized the main hand type.

     verbatim even when `est` looks right."
  3. Section 5 should note: when no xMult appears after 2-3 rerolls at ante 3-4, consider banking a SECOND
     hand-type's planet (not just the main hand) as insurance against no-repeat-type bosses (The Eye/The
     Mouth), rather than exclusively continuing the xMult hunt.
