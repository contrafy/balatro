## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no real scorer. Ante 1 was cleared by raw hand quality (FH/FH/Straight) on level-1 hands, and at ante 2 the only scorer was a $1 perishable rental whose +13 mult per Queen HELD only fires when a Queen is in hand (4 Queens in 52 cards). Without Queens every hand is worth 40-290, versus 2000 for the boss. Zero flat scorers + zero xMult at ante 2 = dead (score check 0.13 -> 0.22).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 first shop ($5): took Buffoon -> Juggler (utility, perishable). Would have been better to buy a flat +mult joker if offered; none was. The real error was the second shop: I bought a Buffoon pack ($4->$2) over eternal Droll, and the pack offered Vampire/Flash Card (both useless now). Droll+eternal was also useless; nothing good was available. Keeping $6 for ante 2 would have bought more joker views.
2. Ante 2 shops: left with $4-5 twice because nothing was a scorer; two rerolls would have been unaffordable anyway (rent -$3/round on Shoot the Moon plus $0 Small blind payout). The rental drained $3 each round, which blocked every Standard/Buffoon pack I might have bought. A $1 rental at 0 scorers is still right but cost me the shops.
3. Wheel: spent both discards digging for Queens with a straight kept; probably equal. The loss was variance plus a thin build.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your only scorer is "per card held" (Shoot the Moon, Baron-like), THEN keep hand size big and never play Queens as fillers; score swings 40 -> 1000 depending on Queens in hand, so count Queens in the opening 9 cards before committing discards.
- IF The Wheel is the ante-2 boss and your build depends on specific held ranks, THEN facedown cards still count for held-rank jokers (the game evaluates rank), but the CLI cannot show them: rank-sort bounds tell you if a facedown can be that rank.
- IF a boss debuffs a suit (The Window), THEN debuffed cards still form Straights/Flushes; the straight with debuffed K/J still scored 236, so the type is available even when cards are not scoring.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- shopadvise at ante 1 shop 2 ranked eternal Droll Joker (flush +10, ETERNAL) as BUY with SIM +0.49 on a build with zero flush support; SIM seems to credit it as if flush were common with 9 cards.
- Pack advisor SIM prefers Vampire over Flash Card by +0.01 with a build that has no enhanced cards, i.e. effectively zero value; "no option clears" should say skip a free choice or prefer the joker that can actually score.
- `advise` under The Wheel printed "PLAY -> b play 5 6 2 9" with no type; the CLI then plays with the type check skipped. Acceptable, but it should suggest the type from rank bounds (two 4s + a 2 + facedown 2 = Two Pair).
