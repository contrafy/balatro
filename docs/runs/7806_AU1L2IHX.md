## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. At ante 5 the build was 1 conditional xMult x3 (Blackboard rental, needs every held card black), 1 conditional x2 (Seeing Double), Misprint/Swashbuckler flat mult, Pair/Two Pair/High Card all L1, no planets ever bought. A good round (Two Pair + Blackboard) was 26-35k, a bad one (High Card/Pair) 3-8k. The Fish (50000) needed two Two Pairs; I got one. Money was $1-8 at every shop from ante 3 on (rent $3/round + $0 Small), so I could not buy a planet, reroll for a real xMult, or Egg/Cloud. Ante 5 Boss was a coin flip (solver p 0.41 at the first hand) and I knew it.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 5 shop 1 ($14): bought Grabber ($10) leaving $4. It gave the extra hand that nearly won, but it left no money for the following two shops (stuck at $4 and $7) and Ice Cream / Blackboard rent kept draining. A Celestial pack / Pair or Two Pair planets at ante 3-4 (when I had $17-26 and used it on rerolls/Arcana) would have lifted every hand (+Pair/Two Pair levels multiply everything: Two Pair L3 = 60x4 vs 20x2).
2. Ante 5 Big: I burned both discards to recolour held cards, then banked 29700 with Two Pair and needed Pair 44 for the last 7800: fine, but it consumed the whole Big and left the boss with the same deck and no new tools.
3. The Fish hand 1: I used both discards to dig for a Two Pair (got 8844 = 35328), leaving 3 hands with 0 discards; later hands were facedown High Cards worth 3-5k. Should have kept the first dig lighter? Not clear; main problem was no way to make later hands worth more than 5k.
4. Blackboard's x3 made hand TYPE irrelevant (High Card with all-black held = 6-9k), which is why I cleared antes 2-4 one hand at a time, but it capped me at ~7k on a non-Two-Pair hand. Seeing Double (eternal, x2) only fires with 2+ scoring cards of mixed suits incl. a club; it never fired on High Card.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF Blackboard (x3) is owned, THEN count red cards: discard reds first, play red cards as fillers so the HELD cards are all spade/club; a debuffed spade (The Goad) still disables it. A High Card with all-black held scored 6-9k at ante 3-5.
- IF Seeing Double is owned, THEN it needs a scoring Club AND a scoring non-Club: only Pair-or-better hands; Two Pair with Blackboard gave x6 (26-35k at ante 4-5).
- IF The Fish is the boss, THEN the hand auto-sorts by the last sort mode: after `sort rank` every draw is slotted in rank order, so facedown positions give rank bounds vs known cards (top 3 >= J, bottom 2 in 5..7). Use that to choose what to hold, but remember only the first hand can use face-up discard draws.
- IF the ante-5 boss needs 2+ Two Pairs and you own no planets, THEN the ante 3-4 shops should have bought Two Pair/Pair planets (Pluto/Uranus/Mercury) with the $17-26 I spent on a reroll + Arcana packs.
- IF Swashbuckler{E} is bought, THEN the ~$12 of sell value of other jokers (+12 mult) is worth about +50% at ante 4: it is a legitimate eternal scorer; keep the sell-valued jokers.
- Misprint is high variance: min-roll estimates (shown by `sel`) are the safe numbers; est (mean) was 30-60% below actuals about half the time and 40% above the other half.
