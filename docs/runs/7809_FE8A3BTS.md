# Run FE8A3BTS (port 7809, claude-sonnet-5-5) - Black Deck Gold Stake - LOSS at ante 2 blind The Goad (1112/2000)

Ante 1: Small 300 (D6 tag), Big 450 (Investment), Boss The Window 600 (diamonds debuffed). Start $4, no jokers.
- Small 300: dealt 5 clubs, Flush 328 (exact est) cleared in one hand. Shop $6: Buffoon pack ($4) -> Sly Joker (Perishable+Rental) over Red Card (Rental); $2 left.
- Big 450: Pair Q 160 first (cycle + bank), then two discards keeping 8833 drew a Full House 888 33 = 480 (exact). Round-end rental took money to -$1 (cash out brought it to $4). Shop: nothing worth it (Juggler eternal, planets for other hands); kept $4.
- Boss Window 600: no made hand; kept JH TH 3H, drew AH KH -> heart Flush 316, then Two Pair KK88 212, then Pair 9s 138 = 666. Cleared with the last hand.
Ante 2: Small 1000 (Negative tag), Big 1500 (Polychrome), Boss The Goad 2000 (spades debuffed).
- After boss shop $6: bought Shoot the Moon $5 (ETERNAL, +13 mult per Queen held), $1 left. Skipped Small 1000 for the Negative tag (Small pays $0, I owned 2 scorers; advisor said skip).
- Big 1500: discarded to keep JJ99 and dug for a Queen; drew QH, Two Pair w/ Q held est 1620 (exact) cleared in one hand. Shop: Negative tag made rental Bloodstone free (x1.5 per heart 1/2 chance); bought it as a free slot. $4 left.
- Boss Goad 2000: no Queen ever came. 2 discards (kept 4-pair then 6644 two pair): drew Full House 55566 = 672 (est 448, Bloodstone helped); then junk cycle HIGH 24; last hand Full House 33344 = 416 -> 1112/2000. Dead.

Post-mortem:
- Binding constraint: scaling. Ante 2 boss 2000 with L1 hands and flat jokers (~150-450 per hand without a Queen) needs ~700 per hand. My only multiplier, Shoot the Moon, scored 0 extra whenever no Queen was held (4 Queens in 52 cards, ~0.6 held per 8-card hand), so the build was feast-or-famine: 1620 in one hand vs 150-450 otherwise. Money was $1-4 the whole run (Rental -$3/round on top of the $0 Small Blind), so I never bought a planet/pack to level a hand.
- Decisions I would change: (1) Took Bloodstone (rental, free via Negative tag): it did help once (FH 448 est -> 672) but costs $3/round that I could not afford; fine on its own but it would have been better to look at the Wheel of Fortune/ Arcana pack. (2) Skipping the ante-2 Small (1000) for a Negative tag: it gave a free but rental joker, i.e. low value; Small gives a shop visit and a hand-cycle to find Queens, though $1 wallet made the shop moot. (3) On the Goad, the first discard (kept pair of 4s) and second (kept 6644) had a 25-40% chance of a Queen each and missed; the alternative of keeping Flush cards (4 diamonds KD TD 7D 4D) was not obviously better. The real fix is not depending on a conditional +mult.
- Rules (if-then):
  * IF your only multiplier is Shoot the Moon (Queens held), THEN every discard should keep Queens in hand and play pairs/two pair with fillers; count Queens remaining before each discard; with 0 Queens in the first 8 cards expect ~40% to find one per 5-card draw and plan 2 attempts before the hand is played.
  * IF the boss debuffs a suit, THEN cards of that suit held do not count (spade Q under The Goad would not trigger Shoot the Moon): count only non-debuffed Queens.
  * IF you own two Rental jokers at Gold with <$10, THEN every round-end drains $6 and you can't buy anything: sell the rental that does not score (Bloodstone/Sly) as soon as the perishable one dies.
  * IF Full House appears on a first draw with 3+ cards of 2 ranks (e.g. 8833 held), THEN keep both pairs through a discard: 4 outs across 4 draws is ~40% to a Full House that clears a 290 gap.
- Advisor/CLI defects: `hands` est for full houses under-reports Bloodstone (50% x1.5 not counted) so est 448 vs actual 672; `advise` at Big 1500 suggested keeping a flush (est 328 < need 1500) without mentioning that a Queen held would multiply a pair by x7; est for junk HIGH 50 vs actual 24 (debuffed spades counted as chips). The advisor did not warn that keeping the Sly joker (perishing, rental) at $1 leaves wallet negative.
- Bridge issues: none.
