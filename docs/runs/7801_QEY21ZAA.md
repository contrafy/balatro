## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling, then money. The build was holo Banner (+10 mult, +30 chips per unused discard) + Green Joker (+15 by the end) +
Mad (+10 on 2P/FH) + Even Steven + Ice Cream (decaying chips) with The Trio (x3 on Trips/FH) as the only xMult, Two Pair L3 / FH L2. That
makes a round "one Trio hand (FH ~30-35k) + two 2Ps (~9-11k)" = ~55k at FULL Banner, and every discard spent hunting the Trio hand
costs 30 chips and 1 mult on all remaining hands: at the Psychic the FH came on the last hand at Banner 0 (25122 instead of ~34k at
Banner 60) and the round made 41360. Ante 5 needs ~17k per hand; without a second general multiplier the build was ~10k per plain
hand. Money: the Small pays $0, I skipped 4 of the 5 ante 3-5 Small/Big blinds for tags, and the only big wallet ($30 at the ante-4
first shop) went to Overstock ($10, extra views I could never afford to buy from), a reroll and Ice Cream. The a4 boss shop ($13) had
to buy The House survival kit (foil Runner rental, Uranus x2) and rent took the wallet to -$2, so ante 5 opened with $3.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 first shop ($30, Trio just acquired, ratio 0.33): Overstock $10 was a long-run buy in an emergency; 2 rerolls + the
   Celestial + keeping $10 for the next shop's joker would have been 2-3 more joker views WITH money to buy. Overstock paid 0 views I
   could afford (ante 5 shop: $3).
2. Ante 4 boss shop: the survival kit (Runner rental + two Uranus) was needed (House cleared by 1.9k), but the rental took money to
   -$2; selling Ice Cream ($2) for the Fool instead of going to $1 would have kept rent from going negative. Minor.
3. The Psychic hand 2 (KK TT 8 7 6 2, Banner 30, 1 discard): banking 2P KKTT (8970) was right by the arithmetic, but hand 3's second
   5-card discard (Banner 0) made the final FH worth 25k instead of 31k; with 41k still needed neither line reached 50k. The round was
   lost in the shops, not at the table.
4. Ante 3: skipping Small AND Big for Handy ($9) + Rare tag paid off (The Trio), but it also skipped 2 of ante 3's shop views; with 0
   xMult that is the playbook's warned trade. It worked only because the Rare tag hit a Trio.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Banner (and/or Green Joker), THEN a discard costs ~30 chips and 1 mult on EVERY remaining hand of the round: plan rounds
  as "no discards" and count a hunt discard as -15% per later hand. Under The House, play 5 blind adjacent cards from the rank-sorted
  hand as hand 1 (pairs are adjacent; 49% it is a pair) instead of discarding: it keeps both discards and Banner +60 for the two
  informed hands (this cleared 18000 at 19872).
- IF a face-down card sits above the highest visible card in the rank-sorted hand, THEN it is at least that rank (above an Ace = an
  Ace): play it as part of the pair (2P AA33 scored 10101 blind). A face-down between two visible ranks is bounded by them.
- IF The Trio is your only xMult, THEN a round is one Trio hand + two Two Pairs: that is ~55k at ante 5 with full Banner and fails
  whenever the Trio hand needs 2 discards. Treat the Trio as half an xMult: the second multiplier must be bought by the ante-4 boss
  shop, before any voucher.
- IF the SCORE CHECK is EMERGENCY (<0.35) and you hold $25+ at an ante-4 shop, THEN spend it on joker views you can still buy from
  (2 rerolls + Buffoon pack, keeping $8-10), never on a voucher: the voucher's extra views arrive in shops where you hold $3.
- IF a shop offers The Fool right after you used a planet (or a pack planet), THEN it is that planet again for $3 (Two Pair L2 -> L3).
  `buyuse` on The Fool leaves the copy in the consumable slot: `use 1` afterwards.
- IF a foil or holographic joker appears at ante 1-3 (any effect, even Faceless/Banner), THEN its edition alone (+50 chips / +10
  mult) is a scorer: foil Faceless ($6) and holo Banner ($0) carried antes 2-3 (Goad one-shot 2961).
- IF you hold a rental and <$3 before a boss, THEN rent takes the wallet NEGATIVE (-$2 here) and the next shop opens at ~$3: sell the
  rental at blind select if the boss does not need it, or keep $3 for the rent.
- IF ante-5 Small/Big tags both give jokers now (Top-up, Buffoon) and your plain round is under 0.5x the Small, THEN skipping both is
  the better of two bad lines (Top-up gave Even Steven, which the Psychic plan needed), but it is ~20%: the fix is earlier.
- IF you take The Trio, THEN level Full House/Trips (Earth/Venus) over Two Pair: the Trio hand decides every ante-5+ round and a
  level there is +10-15% on 30k, while Two Pair levels add +1k.
