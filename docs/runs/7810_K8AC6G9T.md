## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: scaling + money. After Gros Michel blew up I had +4 mult, Photograph (needs faces), Ramen: ~500-1100 per hand vs 6400 boss. Income was ~$5/round vs $6/round rent, so I never had >$6 after ante 1.
Decisions I would change:
1. Skipping the Big Blind (ante 3) for a Holographic Tag with the BOSS next: the free Holo joker only exists in the shop after the boss, so it did nothing for the Eye. Playing Big 4800 would also likely have failed, but skipping both left zero shop visits between ante 2's $2 wallet and a 6400 boss. The right move was to play the Small (3200 was clearable by ~1400/hand two pairs) and use the Big/Small shops.
2. Taking Gros Michel as a 1-of-4 rental joker: it carried the whole ante 2 (x2 x2 on +19 mult) and its 1/6 destroy roll hit on the very last hand. A single-joker-dependency build with $0 cannot recover.
3. Not buying Square Joker/saving $6 at ante 1 was fine; ante-2 Even Steven ($4, +4 per even card) not bought because rent left no cash - alternatives were all weak.
Reusable rules:
- IF the next blind after the one you skip is the BOSS, THEN the tag from the skipped Big (Holo/Polychrome/Rare/Uncommon/Investment) pays nothing before the boss; only skip the Big when a shop follows (never).
- IF all pack jokers are rentals and you hold <= $6, THEN count rent x rounds before taking two of them: 2 rentals = $6/round vs ~$5 income, wallet goes negative.
- IF The Eye is the boss THEN plan three DIFFERENT types each >= need/3 BEFORE the blind; a face Full House/Flush with Photograph is ~2500, a Pair is ~500. Without xMult-level scaling by ante 3 the Eye (6400) is unclearable.
- IF Photograph is owned THEN every kept card should be a face; hands without a scoring face lose the x2 (Trips 3s 524 vs ~1700 with a face).
