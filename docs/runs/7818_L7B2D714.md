## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling/economy at ante 1-2. One chips-only joker (Clever +80 on Two Pair) and two Jumbo Celestial
packs. Every hand needed a made Two Pair or better; one blank final draw ended the run. Money was $0 after both shops,
so the ante-2 shop had no joker budget and the only joker source (Buffoon packs) was never affordable again.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 Big shop ($6, 1 chips-only scorer): bought Jumbo Celestial ($0 left) on SIM +0.04 against the playbook rule
   "no planets/Celestial packs while you own 0-1 scorers" (shopadvise's own rule line said LEAVE). Keeping $6 makes the
   first ante-2 shop ~$13: enough for a reroll plus a $4-5 +mult joker, or a Buffoon pack. Uranus did help The Club, but
   Clever two pair L1 (~260 x 3) was already close to 600 with 2 discards.
2. Ante 2 first shop ($6, EMERGENCY 0.21): bought a second Jumbo Celestial (whiff: Earth). EMERGENCY says "cheapest
   multiplier"; a planet is the last item on that list and a planet is not a multiplier. A $5 reroll (shop had 0 jokers)
   leaving $1 was not great either; best was probably keep $6, play the Small (unused hands + interest -> ~$9-10 at the
   Small shop) instead of skipping it.
3. Skipping the ante-2 Small at $0: it cost a shop view where I would have had ~$3-4 (or ~$9 under decision 2). The skip
   rule "broke -> skip the $0 Small" only holds when you are broke AND the shops can't buy anything; here being broke was
   self-inflicted by pack buys one shop earlier.
4. Big blind first discard: solver (p 0.63) broke a made diamond flush (320) for AA+K; the made-hand alternative was ~p 0.4.
   Followed the higher p; it got FH 1116 but the last draw blanked. Not a clear mistake.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own exactly 1 scorer and it is chips-only (Clever/Sly/Banner) at ante 1-2, THEN never spend to $0 on Celestial
  packs: keep $4-6 so the next shop can buy a +mult joker or a Buffoon pack; a planet level on L1-L2 hands adds ~+40% to one
  hand type, a +mult joker adds +100-200% to every hand.
- IF shopadvise prints EMERGENCY and the shop holds no joker/xMult, THEN keep the money for the next shop (or a Buffoon pack)
  instead of a Celestial/Jumbo Celestial gamble; the SIM gain of a pack at p ~0.05 is noise.
- IF you are at $0 at ante 2 because of your own pack buys, THEN play the Small anyway (its unused hands + interest fund the
  Big's shop); skipping it only removes a shop.
- IF the final hand needs a two pair or better from 3 kept cards + 5 draws (here ~45%), THEN prefer the line that keeps a
  made trips/FH draw alive when the solver margin is small; one blank draw ends the run at ante 2.
