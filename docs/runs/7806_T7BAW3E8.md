## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING, caused by MONEY. I reached ante 4 with zero xMult (Photograph perished that round), Two Pair L4
and flat jokers (Even Steven, Sly, Mad, holo +10). A good Two Pair made ~5000, so the 13500 Big Blind needed a Two Pair on all
three hands. The deal gave one pair, and both discards missed. I never had more than $14 in any shop. The money went to the
Even Steven rental ($3 x 5 rounds = $15), Photograph ($5, which perished after 5 rounds), a $5 reroll and three packs.

Decisions I would change:
- Ante 2 shop 1: the $5 reroll + Even Steven rental. Its rent cost $15 by ante 4. That money is five $3 planets (Uranus x3 would
  have put Two Pair at L7 with the Orbital Tag) or one Arcana pack plus Death/Justice. At Gold, a rental is worth it only if it
  is the core scorer. Even Steven was an even-ranks-only bonus.
- Ante 3 Big shop ($9 after selling Ride the Bus): Jumbo Buffoon $6 + Emperor $3. The holo Mail-In Rebate (+10 mult) and the
  two tarots did win The Eye. A Celestial pack / Uranus for Two Pair would have scaled further into ante 4. Close call.
- Ante 4 Big: with 1 discard left and no second pair, I used the second discard. Playing junk High Card hands draws the same
  number of cards, so this did not matter. The blind was lost at the deal: 3 hands x ~5000 was the ceiling, so any miss lost it.

What worked:
- Hand order with Photograph: put the per-card +mult cards (Even Steven evens, Mult cards) LEFT of the first face card so x2
  multiplies them. Two Pair QQTT 2420 -> TTQQ 3300; Flush with JD first 2516 -> JD last 3700 est / 6200 actual.
- The Eye: Empress (2 Mult Tens) + Lovers (wild TS) turned 4 diamonds into a Flush containing a Pair. Sly fired on the
  pair inside the flush: 6200 actual in one hand vs 6400 needed.
- Skip for the Top-up Tag at ante 2 gave Sly + Ride the Bus for free. Skip for the Orbital Tag (Two Pair +3 levels) at ante 4.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Photograph (or any card-trigger xMult), THEN before every play `order hand` so the face card that triggers it is the
  LAST scoring card, and +mult cards (Mult enhancement, Even Steven/Odd Todd ranks) are earlier. Check with `sel`.
- IF a Flush or Straight contains a Pair (or Two Pair), THEN Sly/Jolly (and Mad/Clever for Two Pair) fire on it. Count +50 chips /
  +8 mult in your head; `est` leaves them out.
- IF you use The Lovers (Wild) or any enhancement tarot on a card that is already enhanced, THEN the old enhancement is replaced.
  Lovers on a Mult card cost me the +4 mult. Put enhancements on different cards.
- IF a rental joker is not your main scorer, THEN do not buy it before ante 5. Each round of rent is a $3 planet you can't buy.
- IF the Small Blind tag is Orbital for your main hand at ante 3-4, THEN skip: +3 levels beats a $0 Small Blind shop.
- IF you reach ante 4 with no xMult and the main hand under L6, THEN expect each Big Blind hand to need your best hand type. Keep
  one discard for the last hand and plan every hand from the first deal. A Gold ante-4 build should hit 13500/3 = 4500 with a Pair,
  not only with a Two Pair.
- IF you own Mail-In Rebate, THEN read its rank in `raw` (`mail_card.rank`) every round and include those cards in discards ($5 each).

  pick printed "NOT your main hand Full House". It corrected itself one shop later.
- `s` does not show the Mail-In Rebate rank for the round; only `raw` (`mail_card.rank`) has it.
