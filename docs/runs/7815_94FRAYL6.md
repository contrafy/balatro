## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. Whole run was Dusk + Misprint (rental, +0..23 additive mult) + Banner (perishable). Hands scored 700-2000 each on additive mult, fine through ante 2 (all blinds cleared in 1-3 hands) but ante 3 needs 3200/4800/6400 and the best any 3-hand round could do was ~5-6k with luck. No xMult was seen in 6 shops (shop items: Credit Card, Sly, Misprint, Hack, Shoot the Moon, Jupiter, Clever, Pluto, Delayed Gratification, Ride the Bus, Banner; Buffoon pack once -> Dusk/Mail-In Rebate). Money was $1-9 all run because Small pays $0 and rent took $3 per round, so I could never afford a reroll hunt more than once.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 Buffoon pack pick: Dusk (retrigger on final hand) is nearly worthless without good cards; no better choice in that pack, but the pack itself (vs buying Sly $3 as the first scorer) cost me the Pair-gated scorer. With $5 and a Sly in the shop I should have bought Sly and kept a pack for later.
2. Ante 2 shop 1: I rerolled $9 -> $4 instead of buying Jupiter/Clever; the reroll showed only Juggler/Chariot. Should have kept $9 and bought Clever next shop (a perishable chip joker for Two Pair) - income was the problem not item count.
3. Ante 3: skipping Big (4800) for a Holo tag with no shop in between left a 6400 boss with a build capped near ~5k. Neither line clears it; the real mistake was upstream (no xMult by end of ante 2).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Misprint (additive +0..23 mult, `est` assumes 6, true mean 11.5) THEN est is 2-3x too low on most plays: a 1-card High Card with Banner scores ~500-1000; levels and chips (Banner, Stuntman-like +chips) multiply a mult of ~14, so +30 chips = +~430 per hand.
- IF Banner is owned THEN never discard unless the discard completes a better hand: each discard costs 30 chips x ~14 mult per remaining hand (4 hands... ~430 each).
- IF the final hand needs more than max Misprint roll x best hand (check `hands` roll-23 range line) THEN the blind is already lost: decide the previous hand (2 hands left) by the roll-23 ceiling, not the average.
- IF the SCORE CHECK ratio is < 0.2 at ante 2 end and no xMult exists, THEN spend the last shop's money on a Buffoon/Arcana pack rather than leaving with $3-8 (I left with $6 and $3 across two shops).
