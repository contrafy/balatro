## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING. Not one xMult joker was offered in 11 shop views (incl. 7 rerolls) plus 3 Buffoon packs and 2
Judgements; every scorer was flat +mult/+chips (Gros Michel, Mystic Summit, Half Joker, Swashbuckler, Smiley, Ice Cream).
Flat builds peak at ~8k per hand at ante 5 (mult ~60 x chips ~135), a third of the ante-5 Big's per-hand need. The early
game was smooth (every blind of antes 1-3 one- or two-shot), which hid that the ceiling was ~6-8k.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2-3: two Judgements ($3 each) produced Rocket and Astronomer (economy/utility). Fine value, but a Buffoon pack
   would have shown 2 jokers each. More important: with $17-27 at ante 3 I kept the money for interest instead of hunting.
   By the time I rerolled hard (ante 4-5, ~$35 of rerolls) every shop needed an xMult that never came. Rerolling at ante 3
   with $27 when the score check said 0.89-0.99 would have given more looks while it still mattered.
2. Ante 3 free Celestial: I picked Mars thinking it was Trips; Mars is Four of a Kind (Venus = Trips). Wasted level.
3. Ante 4 Mega Buffoon: sold Astronomer for Swashbuckler (+10 mult). Astronomer would have made the two later Celestial
   packs free ($10); marginal either way.
4. Gros Michel (1/6 extinction per round) was my biggest +mult for 4 antes; it died at the end of ante 4. Plan its replacement
   before ante 4, not after.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your build has zero xMult at the end of ante 3 AND all scorers are flat, THEN the ante-5 Small (25000) is out of reach
  (flat cap ~8k/hand): spend ante-3 money on rerolls/Buffoon packs (above the $8 floor) instead of banking for interest.
- IF a perishable/flat build faces the ante-5 Small with projected round < 0.6x, THEN skip it for a tag that pays NOW
  (Orbital = +3 levels of your main hand); it does not save the run alone but it is strictly better than losing the Small.
- IF you hold Strength on a forced pair hand, THEN look for a 2-card upgrade that makes TWO hands at once (QD->K and TH->J
  turned KD+JS+JD into Pair KK + Trips JJJ and cleared The Club with 2 hands).
- Planet names: Mercury=Pair, Venus=Trips, Earth=Full House, Mars=Four of a Kind, Uranus=Two Pair, Pluto=High Card,
  Saturn=Straight, Jupiter=Flush, Neptune=Straight Flush.
- Mystic Summit: always burn both discards before the hands that need it; `advise` already sequences this well.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- Celestial `pick` printed "Mars levels Four of a Kind (NOT your main hand...)" only AFTER sending the pick. A pick that levels
  a hand you have never played should print the warning BEFORE sending (or require confirmation), like `play TYPE` aborts.
- Blind-select advice at ante 3 said "you own 1 scoring joker" with Gros Michel, Ice Cream, Gluttonous and Mystic Summit owned.
- Mega Buffoon pack advice (ante 4, 0 xMult, EMERGENCY) said SKIP although Swashbuckler showed +26% round est; it did not
  consider selling the utility joker (Astronomer) as the swap target.
