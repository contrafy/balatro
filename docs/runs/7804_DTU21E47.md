## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. Zero xMult all run (no r/g xMult in ~9 joker views across antes 1-4), only flat +mult
(Supernova, Walkie, decaying Popcorn) on a Straight main hand that the 13-card Turtle Bean hand made easy at antes 2-3.
When Turtle Bean decayed to 9 cards and The Water removed discards, straights came on 3 of 6 hands and each was only
~4.4-5.4k vs 6k needed per hand-pair.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 last shop (before The Water, projected 0.64x even after Burglar): I passed two $4 Celestial packs on credit to
   keep money for ante 5. A single Saturn (+30 chips +3 mult on ~100x40) is ~+30% per straight = ~+4k over my three
   straights, more than the 2433 I lost by. "Money does not score" applied: the debt would have been repaid by the
   Investment tag ($25) anyway.
2. Ante 3 Big-blind shop ($11, Psychic projected 1.5x): I left with $11 for interest. With 0 xMult at ante 3, one reroll
   ($5, $6 left) was a joker view; the interest earned was $2.
3. Ante 2: picked Saturn (SIM +0.18) and drifted into a Straight main. It worked while hand size was 12-13, but it died
   exactly as the playbook predicts (Straight engines 0/6 to ante 5) once hand size returned to 9 and discards to 0.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF The Water is the boss AND you can buy Burglar, THEN buy it: The Water already zeroes discards, so Burglar is a pure
  +3 hands (6 hands). It took me from p ~0.0 to 15.5k/18k; SCORE CHECK/TIGHT still project 3 hands (11479), ignore that.
- IF your main hand is Straight/Flush and the next boss is The Water, THEN count only ~half your hands as made hands
  (3 of 6 here) and buy levels for it in the last shop, through debt if needed.
- IF you own Credit Card and the next blind projects < 1.0x, THEN spend the full -$20 on levels/scorers in the last shop
  before it; a pending Investment/Skip tag repays the debt.
- IF Turtle Bean is your hand-size source, THEN plan the boss two antes out with 8-9 cards: it decays 1 per round and the
  engine it enables (straights from 13 cards) disappears with it.
- IF a Psychic/Big hand of 12 cards holds two disjoint straights, THEN play the lower one first and keep the other made
  (4240 + 4280 cleared 4800 with 2 hands).
