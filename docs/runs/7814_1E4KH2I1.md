## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: no xMult by ante 4. The ante 3 buys (Jolly +8 perishable, Gros Michel +15 rental) and the ante 4 buy (Ice Cream, decaying) gave a flat-mult and chip-only build. Gros Michel was destroyed during the ante 4 boss, and Jolly perished, so scoring dropped from about 16k per hand to about 5k per hand in one ante. Ante 5 Small 25000 was unreachable from there.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 Shop 1 ($10): I spent $10 on Jumbo Arcana, a Spectral pack and a reroll (Temperance, Cryptid, Jolly). The advisor said EMERGENCY and recommended these. The money should have gone to a permanent xMult or to saving toward one. Net effect: no xMult was ever bought.
2. Ante 4 Shop 1 ($10): I bought Ice Cream (decaying, chip-only) for $5. A decaying chip-only joker is a poor use of $5 when no xMult is offered. Better: keep $10 toward a permanent xMult at ante 4 shop 2 or 3.
3. Ante 4 Small: I overrode the SKIP for the Garbage tag. That was fine economically, but the run never recovered the lost scoring, so the skip would not have changed the result.
4. Gros Michel (rental, 1/6 destroyed chance each round) was a weak anchor. A +15 mult rental was worth less than a permanent scorer because it could vanish mid-boss. Do not treat a destructible joker as the main scorer.

Concrete reusable rules:
- If the shop at ante 3 or 4 offers a permanent xMult and you have $5 or more, BUY it before any planet, pack, reroll or decaying joker.
- If you own no xMult entering ante 4, do not buy decaying or perishable jokers with the money; save toward a permanent xMult or a scorer that survives.
- If a joker can be destroyed or perish (Gros Michel, Jolly), do not count it as your ante-5 scorer. Plan the next boss without it.
- If your best round at the start of ante 5 is under 0.2x the Small blind, the run is likely lost; spend everything on the highest-scoring permanent joker (Greedy, Spectral Wraith) and do not wait for a rescue.
- If the solver says UNWINNABLE on a blind, play out the blind with the highest-expected line; do not spend extra time on it.
