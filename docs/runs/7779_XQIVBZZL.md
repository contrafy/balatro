## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: variance/luck on the final forced hand of the Big Blind, compounded by a scaling gap - 0 xMult
jokers were ever offered across the entire run (~10+ shop visits with reroll hunting), so the build relied purely on
hand levels (Full House L3, Two Pair L5, Straight L3, Flush L2, Pair L3) and flat +mult/+chips jokers (Clever,
Fibonacci, Jolly, Zany, Misprint, Superposition). That is a slower scaling curve than an xMult stack, so by ante 6 the
margin for a single bad-luck hand (no pair on the last draw with 2 discards already spent) was too thin: normally a
Two Pair or Full House would have cleared 9764 in one hand, but High Card alone (990 est) could not.

Decision I would change: on the Big Blind, the second-to-last discard (b disc 1 4 5 6, after playing Full House
QQQTT) chased Two Pair J6 for est 5240 and used the LAST discard immediately after on a hand with no pair rather
than holding it in reserve. With 2 hands and 1 discard left at that point (20236 scored, needed 9764 more), playing
Two Pair J6 for est 5240 left only 4524 needed from the final hand - reasonable on paper - but the final discard
(disc 4 5 6 7 8, keeping A/K/Q/T-Bonus) produced a hand with zero repeated ranks. In hindsight, keeping MORE cards on
that last discard (e.g. keeping a wider spread including a low pair candidate, or discarding fewer cards to preserve
higher odds of retaining/drawing a pair) might have improved the odds of a made hand for the truly final,
un-discardable draw. This is a probabilistic argument, not a certain fix - the CLI's own advise engine made the same
call - but the specific risk (a High-Card-only final hand under a large remaining-need blind) is worth flagging.

Bridge issues: one `ACTION FAILED: Round eval blocking events still pending, retry shortly` on a `cash` call after the
ante 5 Small Blind win; resolved with a single `sleep 2` retry per the ground rules, no further issues. One learning
about consumable-generating tarots (The High Priestess, The Emperor): if held tarot slots are already full of
unused card-targeting tarots (e.g. Hanged Man + Lovers), the newly generated tarot(s) can be silently wasted with no
effect - this happened twice this run (once fully wasted, once losing 1 of 2 promised outputs). Recommend using or
selling held-but-unused tarots before opening any pack/tarot that generates more consumables.

Calibration (est vs actual, see playbook 9): most hands were within the expected +20-45% range (Misprint's
conservative floor plus favorable joker triggers), consistent with the rest of this run's log. No instance this run
underperformed est by more than 20% except the ante-5 Big Blind Two Pair K3 hand (est 7056, actual 5880, -17%,
within tolerance so not separately flagged). One HIGH CARD instance (est 990 actual 1320, +33%) confirms Misprint's
conservative modeling holds even for trivial hands - it simply was not enough margin to matter.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Add an explicit warning to section 4 (LAST HAND CHECK) that the SECOND-to-last discard, not just the final one,
   deserves scrutiny when the remaining need after the next hand is still large: prefer keeping a wider or
   safer card pool (e.g. a backup pair) rather than the theoretical-max chase, specifically when 0 discards will
   remain afterward and the hand type has no guaranteed floor.
2. Add a note to section 5/7a: newly-generated consumables (The Emperor, The High Priestess, The Fool with a
   non-immediate-use source) can be silently wasted if held consumable slots are already full of unused
   card-targeting tarots - use or sell existing held tarots before buying/using a consumable-generator.
3. Consider flagging in section 3 that at ante 6+, with 0 xMult jokers across an entire run, the hand-level-only
   scaling path (this run's approach, per section 7a) is viable to reach ante 6 but has a thinner safety margin on
   forced final hands than a build with even one xMult source - worth reiterating the "reroll for xMult, sell a
   flat joker to make room" guidance more assertively past ante 5 even below the $15-after-reroll cash floor.
