## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. No reliable/growth xMult was affordable all run: Obelisk ($8) appeared at ante 1 when I had
$3, and the only other xMult was The Trio from a Rare tag at ante 5. The build ran on Swashbuckler (+mult = other jokers'
sell values, doubled by Blueprint) and flat chips: ~6-7k per hand at ante 4-5 against 25-50k. Money never exceeded $26:
Blue Joker's rent took $3 every played round, and three perishable bridges (Bull, Clever, Seltzer) cost $16 and then died.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 5 Serpent, hand 2: Trips 222 (solver 0.56) vs FH 222QQ now (solver 0.41). The trips line missed its 6-out draw.
   Both were roughly coin flips; the real mistake was earlier: arriving at a 50k boss with ~7k typical hands.
2. Ante 5 shop: The Trio came at the same time as Blueprint, but I only moved Blueprint next to it at the boss. Once The Trio
   is owned, play Trips/FH lines (x9 with Blueprint) instead of Two Pair (x1) from the first blind. Leveling Two Pair
   with Uranus was the wrong planet once The Trio + Blueprint existed.
3. Ante 1-2: the Blue Joker rental was needed to survive ante 1, but I kept it for 8 rounds (~-$24). Once Wily/Bull/Swash
   carried hands at ante 2, selling it would have funded a Celestial/Buffoon pack.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the boss is The Serpent, THEN discard or play ONE card at a time early: each play/discard draws exactly 3, so a 1-card
  action grows the hand by 2 (8 -> 12 -> 14 here, turning a no-pair deal into 222 + QQ + TT + 99).
- IF you own Blueprint/Brainstorm AND an xMult joker (The Trio/Duo/Family...), THEN put the copier directly left of the
  xMult (x3 -> x9) and switch the main hand to the type that xMult wants; copying a +mult joker is worth far less.
- IF you get a free Blueprint (tag/coupon), THEN check its position immediately: rightmost Blueprint copies nothing.
- IF you own Swashbuckler, THEN every cheap joker you add also adds its sell value as mult (Blueprint $5, The Trio $4); a
  Swashbuckler+Blueprint pair was worth ~+28 mult. It is a usable ante-2 eternal for a build with no mult source.
- IF ante 5 typical hands are under 1/3 of the Small Blind, THEN skipping Small+Big and fighting only the boss beats
  three losing fights (Garbage paid $14; Blueprint/rentals did not tick). It still needs a ~50% draw; it is a last resort.

  4988 -> 6020; Serpent Trips 13824 -> 15828. The Flint last hand showed "est 4988 < need 5050, discard" while the actual

  xMult in the build.
