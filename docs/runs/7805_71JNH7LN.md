## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING, with RANDOMNESS as the trigger. Seven shops/packs offered zero lasting +mult or xMult jokers
(Jolly was $8 Eternal at $5; everything else was rental/perishable/eternal junk or utility). My only mult was Misprint
(+0..23), so every hand was a dice roll: actuals 1560 (est 960), 2024 (644), 2304 (1440), 1651 (1270), then 248 (992).
The final hand needed Misprint >= 5 (79%) and rolled 0. Two rentals (Chad + Misprint, -$6/round) kept money at $0-9, so
no shop could buy a real scorer even if one had appeared.

Decisions I would change:
- Ante 3 Big, hand 1: I chose Straight J-7 over the equal-est club Flush to keep 4C 2C. Irrelevant in hindsight, but a
  flush would have kept the 9H, which did nothing. Neutral.
- Ante 3 Small skip: it saved $6 rent and kept Misprint alive, but it also skipped a shop with a free Chaos reroll. With
  $5 that shop could only have bought a $3-5 joker; a Jolly/Sly there would have turned the last hand into a sure clear.
  I would now PLAY the Small Blind when the build is a coin flip anyway and a free reroll is waiting: the extra look at a
  scorer is worth more than $6 of rent at ante 3.
- Ante 3 shop: Arcana -> Death copying a gold King gave +$0 in practice. The Hanged Man (remove two junk cards) or keeping
  $4 for interest would not have changed the outcome either; the run needed a +mult joker by ante 2.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF Misprint is your only mult source THEN treat every hand as a 0..23 roll: a "clearing" hand at Misprint 6 fails ~1 in 4
  and at Misprint 0 scores only chips x base mult. Never let a run depend on the final hand with Misprint; any flat
  +mult joker in a shop outranks a pack or a tarot.
- IF a foil (+50 chips) rental joker costs $1 at ante 1 with zero scorers THEN buy it: foil Hanging Chad turned ante-1 pairs
  from ~60 into ~200 and carried the Big Blind and The Head. Sell it the moment a real +mult joker appears (rent $3/round).
- IF you hold two rentals THEN you cannot keep interest; every shop opens at $5-9. Plan to sell one as soon as a permanent
  scorer is affordable, and never take a third.
- IF the final hand has 1 discard and the held cards (K 9 7 7 6 5) are all pairable THEN discard the 2 unpairable cards:
  ~85% of 2-card draws improved the hand here (it hit KK77).

Advisor/CLI defects:
- `shopadvise` ante 1 rated foil Hanging Chad (RENTAL, $1) at -10 "UTIL"; the foil edition is +50 chips on every hand
  and was my only scorer for two blinds. Editions on utility jokers should count as scorers when you own none.
- Mega Buffoon `advise` said SKIP the whole pack (Misprint perish+rental, Chaos perish) with zero scorers; Misprint was
  the only mult source available and won 3 blinds. With 0 scorers at ante 2 a perishable +mult bridge should not be SKIP.
- `advise` ante 3 Big hand 1: suggested `disc 6 7` (2 cards) to keep Pair T + gold QS while the pace needed 1600/hand;
  a 4-card flush discard (61% vs 37% to hit) was clearly better and hit.
- Misprint est uses 6; actuals ranged 0..20. The est is fine as a floor but `advise` could print P(clear) for Misprint hands.
Bridge issues: none.
