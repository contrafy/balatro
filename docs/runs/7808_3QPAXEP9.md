## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: zero scoring jokers at ante 1 (Economy tag Small gives $0, $4 start, jokerless 100/hand pace cannot clear 450 without a made Flush/Straight/FH). Luck on draws was also bad (3 straight/FH draws missed at p 0.36-0.7 each), but the decision that removed all margin was the shop.

Decisions I would change:
1. Shop $5 with 0 jokers: I followed SIM (Buffoon pack 0.23) over the advisor/tier rule (buy Green Joker $4, +1 mult per hand and -1 per discard, permanent, no rental). At Gold, 30% of pack/shop jokers are Rental (and some Eternal), so a 2-joker pack has ~50% to have at least one rental; this pack had two, which the HARD RULES barred, leaving me with $1 and nothing. A guaranteed, sticker-free, buyable scorer in the shop (even a weak Green Joker) beats a Buffoon-pack gamble at $4-5 when the pack leaves only $1. Next time: with 0 jokers and a sticker-free scorer in the shop, buy that scorer first, even if weak.
2. I could have taken Odd Todd{PERISHABLE,RENTAL} or Greedy{E,R} (SIM +0.17 each, P(clear next 3 blinds) 0.56 vs 0.01) as a bridge; the hard rule forbids it, so this one is the cost of the rule, not a violation I wanted to make. Data point for the rule: here the rule-compliant branch (jokerless at $1) had P(clear Big) ~0.71 by solver at hand 1 and still lost.
3. Hand choices followed the solver's highest p each time; no override. The Big Blind loss was draw variance given that p.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF ante 1, 0 jokers, and the shop has a sticker-free +mult joker for <= $5 (Green Joker, Jolly, Half...), THEN buy it before a Buffoon pack: a pack at Gold can show only barred rentals/eternals and then the $4 is gone for nothing.
- IF a Buffoon pack at ante 1-2 shows only rental/eternal-rental jokers, THEN skippack is forced by the hard rules and you must not have spent below the price of the shop's backup joker (keep it affordable before opening the pack).
- IF the Small Blind pays $0 (Gold) and you have 0 jokers at ante 1, THEN the Big 450 needs a made Flush/Straight/FH or ~3 Two Pairs: jokerless p(clear) ~0.7 only on a good opening hand.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- shopadvise ranked Green Joker (BUY, 118) above the Buffoon pack (78) while its own SIM line valued the pack higher (0.23 vs 0.20); ordering and SIM disagree and the pack outcome (two barred rentals) was not priced.
- Pack `advise` ADVICE said `pick 2` Greedy Joker{ETERNAL,RENTAL} although it printed BARRED: flat rental at ante 1-2 / eternal rental before ante 7. The ADVICE line contradicts the hard rule; it should say skip.
- Buffoon pack at $5 with Green Joker in the shop: shopadvise did not warn that a pack's sticker odds (30% rental each, 2 cards) leave ~50% to show at least one barred joker.
