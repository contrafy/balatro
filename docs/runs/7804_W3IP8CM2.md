## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. One xMult (Card Sharp, rental) on flat +mult (Mad, Sly, holo Ride the Bus, holo Drunkard) and
Two Pair L3. A Card Sharp round is hand1 + 3 x (hand2 + hand3), so its ceiling is ~7 base Two Pairs: ~5k base -> ~35-37k,
exactly the ante-5 Big (37500). It cleared ante-5 Small with 39.9k and died at the Big by 1926. No second xMult appeared in
13 shop views (6 rerolls, $33 spent on rerolls).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 5 Big shop: pass on Fibonacci $8 (sell holo Drunkard). SIM said +0.05, but the actual hands were AA88 and 6633:
   Fibonacci would have added +32 and +16 mult to the two x3 hands (~+12k), an easy clear. Near a Card Sharp ceiling, a
   +mult joker that fires on common ranks beats a +10 flat utility joker.
2. Ante 3: Golden Joker{ETERNAL} took a slot for the rest of the run. Money was never the binding constraint (died holding
   $14, had $40-44 at antes 4-5 and spent $33 on rerolls that found nothing); a +mult joker in that slot would have cleared.
3. Ante 3: sold the Fibonacci rental to save $3/round. Correct by rule, but the rule cost ~+15-30% score on every A/2/3/5/8
   pair later; with Golden Joker paying $4/round the rent was affordable.
4. Ante 4: skipped the Small for a Skip tag (+$10): one fewer shop view while hunting the 2nd xMult.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your only xMult is Card Sharp, THEN your round cap is ~7x your base main hand (1 + 3 + 3). Before each Big/Boss shop,
  compute 7 x base vs the next need; under 1.15x, buy the +mult joker that fires on your hands NOW (even over the floor),
  do not bank money for a hypothetical xMult.
- IF Card Sharp is registered and you need two x3 hands, THEN keep high-chip FACELESS pairs (Ride the Bus keeps growing)
  and enhanced (Mult) cards for the x3 hands; register with the weakest Two Pair you have.
- IF the Big Blind tag is Buffoon at ante 2 and your hand beats the boss/2, THEN skip the Big: the Mega Buffoon gave 2 holo
  jokers (+20 mult) for a $4 payout loss and no rent tick.
- IF an Arcana pack offers The Fool and your last-used consumable is your main-hand planet, THEN take the Fool: it is a
  free main-hand level (2P L2 -> L3 here).
- IF The Water and Card Sharp: register Two Pair, then dig with a 3-card High Card keeping one pair + singles (solver
  0.70 vs 0.44 for Trips; keeping all three 7s would have turned a hit into a non-x3 Full House).
