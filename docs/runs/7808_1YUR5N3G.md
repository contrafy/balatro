# Run 1YUR5N3G (port 7808, claude-sonnet-5-5) - Black Deck Gold Stake - LOSS at ante 1 blind Big Blind (448/450)
Ante 1: Small=Polychrome Tag, Big=Rare Tag, Boss=The Hook (600). Start $4, no jokers, no shop visited.
- Skipped Small (Polychrome tag = free polychrome shop joker) -> but the tag only pays out in the NEXT SHOP, and a shop
  only appears after beating a blind. So the 450 Big Blind had to be beaten with ZERO jokers and 2 discards.
- Big 450: dealt AA22 two pair; discarded twice (2 discards) hoping for full house; second draw gave AAA222 -> Full House
  AAA22 est 308 = actual 308. Hand 2: only Pair 9 (est 56) with junk fillers. Hand 3: Two Pair 77 44 est 84 vs 86 needed.
  Final 448/450. Lost by 2 chips.

Post-mortem:
- Binding constraint: no scoring joker at ante 1 on Gold (Small blind skipped, so no income/shop before Big 450;
  3 hands, 2 discards, jokerless Pair-family ~50-110/hand). The skip itself was the decision I would change.
- Decision to change: at ante 1 with $4 and no jokers, a skipped Small does NOT lead to a shop. Skipping Small (tag payoff
  in shop) means the Big Blind 450 is played with no joker, exactly the failure mode the playbook warns about (284/450,
  404/450, 436/450). Also the second discard: on the 2nd draw I re-discarded 4 cards (keeping AA22) which worked, but
  hand 2 (Pair 9 + 3 fillers) was the weakest line; could have played TWO PAIR/pairs more carefully: Hand 2 options were
  just Pair 9 (56). Nothing better existed. Real loss was the skip.
- Rules (if-then):
  1. If you are at ante 1 on stake 2+ with no joker, NEVER skip Small for a tag that pays in the shop (Polychrome, Foil,
     Holo, Rare, Uncommon, Buffoon packs open at once only for pack tags): the next shop only comes after the Big
     Blind is won, so you play 450 jokerless. Play the 300 Small instead (it gives the shop visit before Big).
     (Pack tags like Buffoon/Charm open immediately on skip and are fine; shop-edition/joker tags are not.)
  2. Skipping a blind never opens a shop. Shops come only after a blind is beaten.
  3. Jokerless Big (450) with 3 hands/2 discards: expect ~300-450 max; do not rely on it.
Bridge issues: none.
