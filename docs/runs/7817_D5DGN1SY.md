## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: money, caused by stacked rentals, which then made scaling impossible. I held 2 rentals from the
first ante-2 shop, 3 from ante 3 and 4 at the end: -$6, -$9, -$12 per round. Money went $7 -> $5 -> $4 -> $-1 -> $-5,
so every ante-3/4 shop was unbuyable (Swashbuckler $6, Riff-raff $6, Abstract $4, Celestial/Arcana packs) and I never
saw a single xMult or bought one level. Every hand stayed L1. The rentals each scored, and each one won the blind it was
bought for (Needle, Flint), but together they took away every shop, the only route to an xMult.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 first shop: I bought holo Joker{RENTAL} and KEPT Jolly{RENTAL} (SIM 0.49 vs 0.38 for sell-then-buy). The SIM
   counts 3 blinds of rent; the second rental cost $3/round for the rest of the run and set off the chain. Sell Jolly
   (holo Joker +14 is strictly better than Jolly +8) and keep 1 rental, as the playbook says.
2. Ante 3 Big shop: I overrode "SELL Jolly" for the Flint. The Flint cleared 8344/6400 (1.3x), and Jolly was ~20% of mult,
   so it would have cleared without Jolly too. The sale would have saved $3/round. With 3+ rentals, sell the weakest
   one as soon as the next blind projects >= 1.2x without it.
3. Grim (Spectral) pick was fine (advisor said skip): it added a lucky and a gold Ace, used in AA pairs.

What worked: ante 4 rent-broke at $-5 with ~1.08x on the Small: skipping Small (Buffoon tag: Mega Buffoon gave Raised
Fist + Seltzer, +60% est) and Big (Boss Tag rerolled The Club into The Water, where Summit is always on) beat fighting
two blinds. The boss was still lost, because 0 discards with no pair in the 2nd deal left no dig.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you already pay rent on one joker AND a better rental scorer appears at ante 1-3, THEN buy it AND sell the old rental
  in the same shop, even when the SIM shows +0.1 for keeping both. Two rentals before ante 5 lost every later shop here
  (money $7 -> $-5 in 2 antes, 0 xMult views bought).
- IF money is negative at a shop, THEN sell rentals whose blind they were bought for is done (Jolly after the Needle,
  Summit after the Flint), unless the next blind projects < 1.2x without them.
- IF you have spare discards with a made hand at The Needle (1 hand), THEN spend them on cards of Castle's suit (free
  growth): Castle 0 -> +12 chips there.
- IF rent-broke (< $0) at ante 4 with no xMult, THEN skip Small and Big for tags (Buffoon = 2 jokers now, Boss Tag = boss
  reroll): no round end means no rent and no perish tick, and the boss is the only fight.
- IF The Water and Mystic Summit is owned, THEN Summit is on for every hand; est was exact (5895/5895, 7020/7020).

Advisor/CLI defects:
- shopadvise ante 2: SIM ranked "keep both rentals" (0.49) over "sell Jolly, buy holo Joker" (0.38) and the ADVICE was
  sell-then-buy, which was right. The SIM's 3-blind window ignores rent after it; 2 rentals at $7 should carry a
  large penalty in the SIM value, not only in the rule text.
- Pack advise rated Grim 25 / SKIP. 2 random enhanced Aces (here lucky + gold) on a pair engine are worth more than nothing.
- SCORE CHECK at the ante-4 shop (0.12) correctly flagged a lost run, but the shopadvise "SELL FIRST" for rentals did not
  ask whether a rental was needed for the next blind (it was not for the Flint, as it turned out).
Bridge issues: none (58 steps, no rejects).
