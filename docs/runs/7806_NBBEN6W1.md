## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: MONEY -> SCALING. I had two rentals (Raised Fist from ante 1, Crafty from ante 4) at -$3 each and $0-10 all
run, so I could never afford a permanent xMult or a single planet. Every hand type stayed L1 for the whole run. The only multiplier was a
PERISHABLE Blackboard ($6, ante 3). It carried ante 3 to ante 5 Small (5 rounds, 15-25k per round with x3), then perished.
After that the deck made 3-5k per hand against 37500.

Decisions I would change:
- Ante 1 shop: Raised Fist{RENTAL} $1 on top of Misprint. Over about 12 rounds it cost ~$33 in rent, the price of 3-4 planets
  or a permanent xMult. It was a real scorer (+8..+20 mult), but I should have sold it in the ante-2 shop once Mad + Misprint
  carried the Two Pairs (ante 2 Big was 2070/1500 in one hand).
- Ante 2 Big shop ($7): passing Vampire was right. Mercury/Uranus were never bought, though. With $7-10 at ante 2-3 a $3 main-hand
  planet each shop would have taken Two Pair to L3, roughly +60% on every non-x3 hand.
- Ante 4 shop: foil Crafty{RENTAL} was worth it (+50 chips on every hand carried The Eye), but it made two rentals.
  Raised Fist should have been sold then (the advisor said so twice). Fist's mult mattered less once x3 was firing.

What worked (reusable):
- BLACKBOARD PLAY PATTERN: play your red cards as FILLERS and spend discards on red cards. Then the 3 held cards are black, and
  Raised Fist doubles the lowest held one. Hand-only scores of 1-2k became 5-15k. Six of the eight x3 attempts fired.
- The Moon (Arcana) on red cards and Death copying a black card onto a red one both permanently make more of the deck black for Blackboard.
- THE MARK: `sort suit` puts the face-down cards in suit order. Faces of different suits can pair. With 4 face-downs of 4 different suits,
  a pair is guaranteed. Pairing 3 face-downs with a visible pair made Two Pair 78% of the time.
- THE EYE: Straight KQJT9 (x3, red 9D played) beat Straight Flush AKQJT, which would have held the red 9D (no x3): 15036 vs ~8600.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF you own Blackboard THEN pick the 5 played cards so all 3 held cards are Spades/Clubs: red cards are fillers, and discards go
  to red cards first. A made hand whose held cards include a red card scores 1/3 as much. Discard that red card instead of playing.
- IF you own Blackboard and an Arcana pack offers The Moon (or Death/Lovers) THEN take it and target red cards in hand.
- IF you own Blackboard and a hand's best line holds a red card THEN check whether a weaker type with the red card as a filler
  scores more (Straight with the red 9 x3 > Straight Flush x1).
- IF you hold a flat RENTAL joker and own 2 other scorers that clear the current blind in one hand THEN sell the rental in that shop.
  Two rentals = -$6/round, about half of ante-3+ income.
- IF your only xMult is PERISHABLE THEN count its last round (perish_tally=1) as the end of your scoring. Skipping the ante-4 Small
  kept Blackboard alive for the ante-5 Small, but nothing was left for the Big.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- The SOLVER at n=16 returns several unrelated lines with identical p_clear (0.62/0.62/0.62, 0.19/0.19/0.19), and they often throw away
  made pairs or keep red cards under Blackboard. With n=16, ties mean "no information". The ADVICE still follows the top line.
- The Eye, ante 4: the solver preferred `play 9D` (dig 1 card, 56% for a black card to make Straight Flush x3) at 0.65. It never listed
  "Straight KQJT9 with the red 9 played, held all black x3" (est 11814). That line cleared in 2 hands (23892).
- The Mark: ADVICE was `disc 2 3 4 7 8`, which discards all three face-down faces (78% to contain a pair). Later ADVICE was `play 1 3 5 7`
  (4 face-downs: Two Pair only 22% of the time) over 99 + 3 face-downs (Two Pair 78%).
- shopadvise SCORE CHECK used the "75th pct of 40 deals" B of ~1400-3000 while the real Blackboard rounds made 15-25k. It does not
  model Blackboard x3 when held cards are chosen black, so projections were 0.37-0.51x on blinds I then cleared by 1.3x.
- Psychic hand-1 `advise` est on the High Card / Pair lines (e.g. "est 176", "est 990") ignores the fillers that set Raised Fist's held
  card. The `play`/`sel` est was right (432, 5412).
