# Run ZIBZ3PEK (port 7809, claude-sonnet-5-5) - Black Deck Gold Stake - LOSS at ante 3 blind Small Blind (938/3200)
Ante 1: Small 300 (Coupon tag, played it per playbook), Big 450, Boss The Hook 600. Small: discarded to a heart flush (284) + two pair 68 = cleared. Shop $5: bought rental+perishable Misprint ($1) and Buffoon pack ($4, picked perishable Photograph; other card was Eternal Mail-In Rebate) -> $0. Big 450: discarded twice keeping A K Q J, drew Broadway straight, 2511. Hook 600: two pair JJ/TT 2xx.
Ante 2: blinds 1000/1500/2000 (Psychic). Small: trips 4s 714 + full house 1178. Big 1500: two pair 660, two pair 432, full house 1071. Shop money $2-7 only (rental -$3 per round) so nothing bought except Celestial pack (Earth -> Full House L2). Psychic 2000: straight 1495 + Broadway straight 729. Jokers perished at the end of this boss; sold both.
Ante 3: $8, zero jokers, shop offered only Eternal Fortune Teller / perishable Astronomer $8 / Lusty Joker $5 / Ride the Bus $6. Rerolled once ($3 left): nothing affordable. Blinds 3200/4800/6400 (Window). Small 3200: flush 320, pair 60, full house 558 = 938. Dead.

Post-mortem:
- Binding constraint: money + joker turnover. Both jokers I owned were perishable (5 rounds) and one rental (-$3/round), bought at ante 1 for $1/$4 total. They carried ante 1-2 (hands scored 1.5-2.2x `est`, Misprint rolls high) but expired exactly at the ante 2 boss, leaving me with 0 jokers, $8 and a 3200 Small Blind. Gold blinds are much bigger than playbook table: a2 1000/1500/2000, a3 3200/4800/6400.
- Money never rose above $7 in the whole run: Small Blind pays $0, rental -$3/round, and I spent the initial $5 on joker+pack. No interest was ever earned, so no ante-3 purchase power.
- Decision I would change: at ante 2 shop #1 ($7) and shop #2 ($5) I kept cash for "a real scorer" that never appeared; the shops only had Eternal/junk jokers. A better plan: at ante 2 take an Arcana/Celestial pack early (levels) or buy Onyx Agate/Droll as a cheap rental body so I would own ANY joker entering ante 3. Even better: while Photograph+Misprint were alive (est 1.5-2x), I should have prioritised the first affordable NON-perishable scorer over keeping cash. Also I should have sold nothing but kept the pace: the perish timer (5 rounds) means ante-1 perishables die at the ante-2 boss - plan their replacement for the ante-2 SMALL shop at the latest.
- Ante 3 with zero jokers is unwinnable on Gold (Small 3200 vs Full House L2 ~560/hand).
Reusable rules:
- IF a joker shows PERISHABLE(5 rounds left) bought at ante 1, THEN it dies at the ante-2 boss: you MUST own a non-perishable scoring joker (or two) BEFORE the ante-2 boss shop, so spend ante-2 shop money on a permanent scorer rather than saving.
- IF you hold a RENTAL joker, subtract $3 from every round's payout when planning the shop: Small Blind (+$0) -> net -$3, so you cannot save; sell the rental joker the moment a better joker is affordable and always when it perishes (a perished rental still charges -$3 until sold).
- IF a jokerless/near-jokerless Gold shop has $8+ at ante 3, rerolling for a cheap joker burns $5 and may leave you unable to buy (I had $3 left vs $5-6 jokers); with 0 jokers instead buy the best affordable joker even if Perishable/Rental (Astronomer was $8 but Lusty Joker $5/Ride the Bus $6 appeared after reroll, which I could not afford).
- IF hand is A K Q J + any junk and you have Photograph, keep AKQJ and discard 4: Broadway/pairs of faces scored 2511, 1495, 729 on L1 straights with Photograph x2 + Misprint.
- `est` is conservative about Misprint; actuals ran 0.8-2.2x est (mean ~1.5x).
