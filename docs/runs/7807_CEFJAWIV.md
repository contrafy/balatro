## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: MULTIPLICATION. At ante 3 the build was ~2.5k per hand (Sly +50 chips, Popcorn +20 mult decaying, Hiker chips, Hanging Chad); The Wall (x2) needs 12800 = ~4300 per hand. Money was $0-7 all run (Small pays $0, Popcorn rent $3/round), so I could not afford Supernova $8 (holo) or a reroll for an xMult. There was never an xMult or a leveled main hand. A flat +20 Popcorn made every hand type score ~2k regardless of type, so the 3 hands add up linearly: 3 x 2.4k = 7.3k.
Decisions I would change:
- Ante 1 shop $8: bought an Arcana pack at $4 (Empress) instead of rerolling for a second scorer; got lucky with Empress but still had one joker at ante 2.
- Ante 2 -> 3: Popcorn rental decays 4/round, so Popcorn made ante 2 boss and ante 3 Big, then faded (+8 by the Wall). I knew the Wall (12800) was upcoming and spent the shop on Hiker ($5) rather than saving for Supernova $8 holo (Supernova + holo +10 mult was affordable only if I had skipped Hiker AND had $8; I had $7).
- Skipping the Small Blind twice (Coupon, Buffoon) was right: both converted into free jokers/packs and kept Popcorn's mult for the Big.
- A sort rank command I sent for no reason was recorded as training data (junk action): do not use state-changing commands to inspect.
Reusable rules:
- If the upcoming boss is The Wall (x2) and you own no xMult, the boss needs ~2x the usual per-hand score: arrive with a scaling joker or a level-heavy hand, otherwise the ante is lost; plan at the ante's Small blind, not after.
- If a Mult card (Empress) is available and you hold a pair-based hand, put both Empress targets on the same rank pair: Pair of two Mult cards = x(2+8), est 640-740 at ante 2 with just Sly.
- If Popcorn (+20 mult) is offered at $1 and you have no mult source, buy it for ante 2-3 only: it turns every hand type into ~2k at ante 2-3, but it is gone by the third blind after purchase and costs $3/round.
- If Hiker is owned, est UNDERSTATES the score: each scoring card's +5 chips accumulates and Hanging Chad retriggers the first card; real scores were 300-500 above est.
- Debuffed cards (The Pillar) still count for the hand type (Full House QQQJJ with 2 debuffed cards was est 480 exact), so chase made hands with debuffed cards rather than discarding them.
- If a skip tag gives a free shop (Coupon) or Mega Buffoon, skip the Small at $0-3 money: free jokers beat a 1000-3200 blind you would have scored with the same hand.
