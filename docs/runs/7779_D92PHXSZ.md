## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: bad luck on the flush chase compounded by a joker portfolio with zero
"always-on" scoring. Both jokers were conditional (Scary Face needs face cards, Droll Joker
needs a completed flush) and neither fired on any hand actually played in the losing round
(two pairs of non-face ranks, no completed flush). The two-pair-heavy hand we were actually
dealt all round had no support from either joker, so the ante-1 scaling (Flush L3, Droll Joker)
was invisible until we drew a genuine flush again, which never happened.

Decision I would change: after the second discard in ante-2 Small Blind, a Two Pair (K8 or K2,
est 232) was already available with 3 hands still remaining. Spending the 3rd and final discard
to keep chasing the flush (per advise) left zero fallback capacity once the flush missed again -
all three remaining hands were played completely blind with 0 discards against a target that
needed roughly 267/hand average. Banking that Two Pair earlier (or discarding for it explicitly
once the flush draw missed the 2nd time) would have banked ~232+ with 2 hands and 0 discards
left needing "only" ~568 more, versus discarding away the only made hand and ending up needing
755 with hands that couldn't recover. More fundamentally: buying a second flat +mult/+chips
joker (not conditional on face/flush) in the ante-2 shop instead of the second Jupiter level
would have given a scoring floor on every hand type, not just the ones matching Scary
Face/Droll Joker's conditions.

Bridge issues: none. All commands executed as expected; `advise`'s filler choices for HIGH and
TWOPAIR twice picked diamond-suited cards as fillers when the play was flush-adjacent (i.e. the
player still held 3-4 cards of the flush suit outside the play) - this discards flush-suit cards
unnecessarily. Suggest the advisor prefer non-flush-suit cards as fillers when the player holds
an active flush draw and the played hand type is not itself the flush.

Playbook suggestions:
1. When a flush draw (4 of a suit) is active and the recommended play is a non-flush hand type
   (HIGH, PAIR, TWOPAIR, etc.), choose fillers from off-suit cards first so the flush draw stays
   alive across forced plays after discards run out. `advise` currently does not do this.
2. Add an explicit rule: never spend the LAST discard purely chasing a flush/straight if a made
   hand (pair/two pair) already clears at least ~25% of `need` and 2+ hands remain - bank partial
   progress instead of going all-in on a single low-probability draw with no fallback.
3. In ante 1-2, prefer at least one joker that is unconditional (flat +mult/+chips every hand,
   e.g. Joker, Half Joker) over stacking two conditional jokers (face-card-only, flush-only) -
   conditional jokers can both go dark in the same round if the dealt hand doesn't match either
   condition, leaving zero joker contribution exactly when needed.
