## Post-mortem

**Binding constraint:** no xMult source by ante 4. The build was Two Pair/Pair mains (L2 Two Pair, 40x3) scaled only by +mult jokers (Mystic Summit +15 at 0 discards, Red Card +3, Supernova, Scary Face/Even Steven chips and mult). Ante 4 Small Blind 9000 needed ~3000 per hand with 3 hands; the best hand was ~2170 at 0 discards. Money was low for the whole run ($1-$9 most shops), so the Gold $8 floor blocked the Celestial/Arcana/Buffoon buys that could have levelled or scaled the main hand.

**Decisions I would change:**
1. Ante 2-3: I took Uranus-gamble Celestial over the advisor's Saturn, and passed on Celestial at ante 3 ($4, below floor). Two Pair levels were never bought beyond the free ones. Prefer buying the main-hand planet directly when shop has it and the advisor shows it as ADVICE.
2. Ante 3 shop ($11): I bought Scary Face and Rocket (ETERNAL economy) instead of the Jumbo Arcana / other gambles. Rocket is ETERNAL with no scoring value; it cost a joker slot that could have held a scorer. Prefer scorers over economy when money is above $8.
3. Ante 4 shop ($17): after one reroll the best was Even Steven (+4 mult per even card, perishable). Should have rerolled more (two rerolls, $7 left) or bought Wheel of Fortune for a polychrome xMult chance; with no xMult at ante 4, the run was already behind the curve.
4. Ante 4 Small: the first discard sequence burned both discards to 0 for Mystic Summit, but the hands drawn (7s/4s, then 4 high cards) produced only Two Pair; no pair-draw plan could reach 9000. Two Pair at 2170 needed ~4 such hands; the run needed xMult or a 3-of-a-kind/straight build by ante 3.

**Concrete if-then rules:**
- IF ante 3 starts with 0 xMult and money < $8, THEN the next Celestial/Arcana for the main hand is worth the floor break; a Two Pair at L2 (40x3) cannot reach ante 4 Small (9000) on mult alone.
- IF Riff-raff shows nothing (slots full), THEN sell it at the next shop instead of keeping a dead slot; but do NOT sell Banner while a boss disables it only if you have a better scorer.
- IF the boss is The Water (0 discards), THEN Mystic Summit is always active: buy scorers that add flat mult/chips, and count the boss hands as 0-discard hands.
- IF a hand is High Card and the final hand needs a high-card score, THEN use the Pluto (High Card planet) before the last play: it moved High Card from 810 to 1045 (+29%).
- IF the advisor says UNWINNABLE on the final hand, THEN there is no recovery; the run is lost, so spend the remaining effort on logging.
