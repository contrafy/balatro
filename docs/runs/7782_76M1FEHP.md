# Run 76M1FEHP (port 7782) - LOSS at ante 1 Big Blind

Ante 1 (stake 1 White): starting $4, no jokers. Small Blind tag=Holographic, Big Blind tag=Holographic. Boss: The Goad.
- Small Blind (need 300): discarded to a spade flush (advise), played FLUSH est 272 (scored 272), then TWOPAIR est 120
  (scored 120) for filler draw. Total 392/300, cleared. Cashed out to $8.
- Shop 1: no scoring joker offered (8 Ball $5 util, Jupiter $3 planet, Paint Brush $10 voucher). Opened Buffoon Pack
  ($4, per shopadvise) — choice was Constellation vs Egg, neither a scoring +mult/+chips joker. Per playbook 5
  ("if the pack offers no scoring joker, take the one that scores soonest: economy > slow scaling") picked Egg over
  Constellation (Constellation needs planets we don't own yet). shopadvise then said LEAVE at $4 remaining; ended shop
  with 1 joker (Egg, non-scoring) and $4.
- Big Blind (need 450): dealt no pair better than a low pair each draw. Used all 3 discards chasing better pairs
  (advise: keep pair, draw around it), reaching a 9-9/4-4 two pair at best (est 92). Played TWOPAIR est 92 (scored 92),
  then forced (0 discards) to play PAIR 7 est 48 (scored 48), then PAIR A est 64 (scored 64). Final total 204/450 —
  lost. All three ests matched actual scores exactly (Egg has no scoring effect, so no variance).

Post-mortem: binding constraint = no scoring joker by the Big Blind. The single Buffoon pack pick was economy (Egg),
which the playbook explicitly endorses when the pack has no +mult/+chips option, but it left 0 scoring jokers to
survive a Big Blind that needed three real pair hands. The deal itself was also weak (no pair better than 9s across
3 discards), so no joker choice from this pack would have guaranteed survival, but a Jolly/Sly-type joker (had one
been in the pack) would very likely have closed the ~250-chip gap.
The one decision I would change: nothing available in THIS shop — the pack held no scoring joker and shopadvise
correctly said LEAVE (no joker/pack worth the remaining $4). The actual gap was upstream: this shop offered zero
scoring jokers in either the shop row or the booster pack, which the ante-1 curve does not tolerate at Black Deck
stakes (only 3 hands/discards). This matches playbook 7's Batch-1 finding: "shops spent on rerolls/planets instead
of a first joker" — here there was no scoring joker to buy or reroll into (reroll would have cost $5, unaffordable
at $4 with 0 jokers).

Bridge issues: none. Every command returned expected state; `est` matched actual score exactly on all three hands
of the Big Blind (no case >20% off, and no case at all of any deviation, since Egg has no scoring interaction).

Playbook suggestions:
1. Section 5's ante 1-2 reroll rule ("reroll ONLY when the shop has no scoring joker and you keep $4+ after it") should
   also cover the shop ROW at $8 (before the Buffoon Pack purchase): with $8 and zero scoring jokers in J1/J2, a reroll
   ($5, leaving $3) was never on the table because the pack was bought first per shopadvise's ADVICE. Consider having
   shopadvise weigh "reroll the shop row before opening a non-scoring pack" when ante 1 money allows it, since a pack
   with a 50/50 non-scoring outcome (Constellation/Egg) cost the only shot at a real scoring joker that shop.
2. Log confirms playbook 7's known failure mode (weak ante-1 shop -> no scoring joker -> Big Blind loss) still occurs
   even when advise/shopadvise are followed exactly; no rule change needed there, just noting frequency.
