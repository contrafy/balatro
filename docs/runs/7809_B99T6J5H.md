# Run B99T6J5H (port 7809, claude-sonnet-5-5) - Black Deck Gold Stake - LOSS at ante 1 blind Big Blind (328/450)

Ante 1: Small 300 (Rare Tag), Big 450 (Rare Tag), Boss The Club 600. Skipped Small for the Rare Tag (advisor said skip). The Rare Tag only
redeems when the next SHOP opens, which is after the Big Blind, so I went into Big 450 with $4 and ZERO jokers.
Big Blind: hand 1 two pair A/Q est 124 (actual 124), then discard (kept 77+A), two pair A/3 96 (actual 96), last hand two pair K/7 108. Total 328/450.
All three hands were two-pair-level (L1 two pair ~100-125 each); 450 needs ~150/hand with no jokers.

Post-mortem: binding constraint = skipping the Small Blind at ante 1 forced a jokerless 450 Big Blind on Gold stake (3 hands, 2 discards).
The tag's payoff (a free Rare joker) is only available after the Big Blind is beaten, so the skip did not help the very blind it was meant to help.
Decision I would change: PLAY the Small Blind (300, jokerless clearable with a flush/2 two pairs, still gives a shop... actually $0 pay but a
shop visit with $4) OR, if skipping, accept that Big is a coin flip. Expected: P(clear 450 with 0 jokers, 3 hands, 2 discards) is low (~20%).
In this run I could have taken the 300 Small first (it was clearable at ~100-150/hand) to reach the shop, with the Rare Tag from skipping Big instead
(skip Big to go to... no shop before boss either). Better still: play Small (300) to get a shop with $4, redeem nothing, buy a cheap joker.
Reusable rules:
- IF ante 1 and the Small Blind tag is Rare/Uncommon/Buffoon and you have no jokers: the tag only pays at the next shop, which comes AFTER the Big
  Blind. Skipping Small therefore means playing Big 450 with no jokers. Only skip if you can accept ~80% loss odds there; otherwise play Small 300.
- IF jokerless on Big 450: plan hand 1 as the highest-chip made hand, discard 2x to chase flush/full house, never spend hands on 100-pt two pairs.
Advisor defects: `s` at Gold stake blind select says "b skip ... gives a free Rare joker" without noting the joker only arrives at the next shop,
after the jokerless Big Blind (450). That advice cost this run at ante 1. Advisor should not recommend skipping Small at ante 1 with $4/no jokers
when the next blind (Big 450) cannot be cleared jokerless.
