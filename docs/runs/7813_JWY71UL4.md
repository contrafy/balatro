## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling from ante 6. The whole engine was one conditional xMult (Blackboard x3, all held cards black) times a card-phase face stack (Photograph x2 copied by Brainstorm, Scary Face chips, Smiley +5/face, holo +10) on a Flush L6 base. That tops out at ~40k per HAND with a perfect armed hand and ~10k median; ante 6 needs 60k/90k/120k. No growth xMult (Throwback/Obelisk/Hologram/Constellation/Lucky Cat) was ever offered in ~10 rerolls, and with 3 eternals (Photograph, Scary, Brainstorm) only 3 slots were flexible.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3: I sold The Order (x3 on Straight) for Blackboard one hand before I played a Straight with Blackboard armed (5343 would have been ~16k with both). With a free slot after Perishables died I should have kept both.
2. Ante 5: Brainstorm{ETERNAL,RENTAL} $1 was a good score gain (+50% once Photograph was copied) but cost -$6/round with Smiley; at ante 6 I was at $13 with nothing to spend it on anyway.
3. Ante 6: spending $50 of Temperance/rerolls/packs found no xMult. With 0 growth xMult at ante 5 the right target was Throwback/Obelisk from the first ante-4 reroll; I rerolled only 2x per shop because of the $25 interest habit. Interest money that I held ($20-34) never became score.
4. Blind skipping: skipping Small at ante 3-5 (tags Economy/Rare/Orbital) was good; I think skipping Big at ante 5 for Standard (KH) was marginal, the Big shop with $15 would not have changed the boss.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Blackboard, THEN keep 3 BLACK (spade/club) cards in hand as "armor", never play them, and always play 5 cards containing every red card: Blackboard x3 is then on every hand. Discard reds first, then arm. Debuffed clubs under The Club still count as black (24840 one-shot).
- IF you own Blackboard AND the hand contains 5 hearts/diamonds, THEN play the red Flush and hold black armor: a Flush L1 with faces was 14385 at ante 3, 39312 at L6 with Photograph+Brainstorm at ante 5.
- IF you own Photograph and Brainstorm, THEN put Photograph leftmost so Brainstorm copies it (x2 twice on base mult): the advisor measured 27k -> 41k on a Flush L6 (base mult 14). On low base-mult hands copy Scary Face instead.
- IF a Double Tag sits on a Small whose Big has a Buffoon tag, THEN skip both: 2 Mega Buffoon packs at ante 2 gave Scary Face + Photograph + The Order (4 jokers from 8).
- IF Economy tag is offered at a Small and money >= $10, THEN skip it (doubles money, max +$40) and the Small pays $0 anyway.
- IF Orbital Tag names a hand you already play, THEN skip for it (Flush L3 -> L6 = 65x8 -> 110x14).
- IF Temperance is in a shop/pack, THEN +$12-13 with 6 jokers: it converts sell value to rerolls (net +$10).
- IF a Perishable (Popcorn/Raised Fist) bridge is your best +mult, THEN skip the blinds before the boss so it does not tick, and sell it the moment a flat +mult replaces it.
- IF the engine is conditional-xMult based with ~40k best hand at ante 5, THEN the run ends at ante 6: from ante 4 spend EVERYTHING on joker views (rerolls) for Throwback/Obelisk/Hologram, do not hold $25 for interest.
