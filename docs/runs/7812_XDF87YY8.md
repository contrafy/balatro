## Post-mortem

**Binding constraint:** no permanent scoring engine by ante 3. The run had one flat +4 mult Perishable (expired), one ETERNAL non-scorer (Smeared), one x4 Loyalty Card that only fires every 6 hands, and one +3 mult/Club joker bought at the last shop. Planets and the Flush-focused build (Flush L2) never reached the 6400 boss target. The Head debuffs one suit each round, which cut scoring cards per hand to about 3-5 useful cards.

**Decisions I would change:**
1. Ante 2 Smeared Joker ($7, ETERNAL, no mult) was a weak buy; with $7 a +mult or xMult option was not affordable, so prefer saving to the next shop if no scorer is affordable. (Advisor flagged EMERGENCY ratio 0.11.)
2. Ante 3 Arcana pack bought with the Judgement pick gave Diet Cola (non-scorer). Skip Arcana when there is no Flush-tarot need; $4 on a Celestial or a scorer was better.
3. Do not play a hand the advisor solver rates "p>=0.99" without checking arithmetic. In the Big 4800 I spent discards on the advisor's "p>=0.99" play lines that were arithmetically impossible; the Big 4800 was saved only by a late club flush (est 3840).
4. Money: at Ante 3 shops I held $2-$4 most of the time. Money floor advice was OFF yet nothing scored. More income (Economy Tag, selling dead weight earlier) would have funded a scorer.

**If-then rules for weaker models:**
- If the advisor solver says "p>=0.99" for a play but (current hand est + later hands x est) < need, then the solver line is wrong: play the best discard line instead and log the mismatch.
- If a boss debuffs a suit (The Head), then count debuffed cards first and discard them; expect 3-5 scoring cards per hand, so need roughly 2x the normal blind score in boss EV.
- If the shop has no +mult or xMult you can afford and the boss is 2.5x the current round, then buy the Buffoon pack (not a $4-7 non-scorer).
- If a Perishable +mult joker has 2 rounds left, then buy a permanent scorer in the next shop; do not wait.
