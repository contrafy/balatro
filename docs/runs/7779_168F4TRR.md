## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: misplay/sequencing, not raw scaling power. Pair was leveled to L8 (115x9) with Jolly+Sly+Scholar+
Acrobat+Supernova+Photograph all supporting it - the engine was strong enough to clear 39382 on the very last hand of
the very last blind of ante 6. The loss came from spending the last discard one hand too early: on the Boss blind,
the last (3rd) discard was used on hand 2 of 3, leaving hand 3 (the actual final, Acrobat-boosted hand) with zero
flexibility to react to a weak draw (Pair T only, no better option visible). Had 1 discard been held back for the
true final hand, it could have been used to fish for a third Ten, a Jack, a Queen, or any better pair after seeing
the final draw, likely clearing the 618-point gap.

Decision I would change: reserve the LAST discard specifically for the round's final hand whenever the running total
after 2 hands is close to (but under) the blind requirement, rather than spending all discards greedily on the
earliest hand that looks marginally better. `advise`'s per-hand DISCARD suggestions optimize each hand somewhat
locally; on a Boss blind where the gap is this tight, an override to hold the last discard in reserve for the final
hand would have been correct. Also: the Chariot-enhanced Steel Jack (x1.5 mult while held) was allowed to be
discarded away by the time of the final hand - keeping it in hand as a permanent held-card filler across all hands of
a round would have added meaningful mult to every play, including the fatal one.

Bridge issues: no CLI errors this session other than the one `ACTION FAILED: buy_from_shop returned false: no space or
card not purchasable None` noted above (both consumable slots full when trying to buy a Planet directly) - correctly
identified as non-transient and resolved by using an existing consumable first.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Add an explicit rule to section 4/7a: "On a boss blind (or any blind) where scored-so-far + best-remaining-hand-est
   is within ~10% of `need`, hold your LAST discard in reserve for the round's final hand rather than spending it on
   an earlier hand that only marginally improves its own est." This run's advisor spent the last discard on hand 2 of
   3 because hand 2's own est looked improvable, without weighing that hand 3 (final, Acrobat-boosted) would then
   have zero flexibility.
2. When a Steel/Glass/other held-card enhancement has been applied to a specific card (via Chariot/Justice), flag it
   in the `advise`/`hands` output (e.g. "keep idx N: Steel Card, do not discard") so it is not accidentally discarded
   away in a later hand of the same run - it is a scarce, permanent asset once earned.
3. Confirmed and worth promoting from a footnote to a hard rule: Arcana/Standard packs bought and opened while still
   in SHOP phase (not yet re-entered SELECTING_HAND) have no hand to target - card-targeting tarots picked from such a
   pack are silently wasted (confirmed via `raw`: the picked tarot never appears in `consumables`). Only pick The
   Fool, The Hermit, The Emperor, The High Priestess, Wheel of Fortune, or Judgement from a shop-phase pack; skip or
   accept the loss on targeted tarots (Chariot, Justice, Empress, Strength, Death, The Tower, Lovers, Sun/Moon/Star/
   World) unless the pack can somehow be opened mid-round.
