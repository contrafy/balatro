## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. Zero xMult of any kind was offered in 15 shop views (incl. 9 rerolls, ~$50) + 3 Buffoon/Mega
packs; the only xMult-shaped offer was The Trio{ETERNAL,RENTAL} at ante 3 (hard rule, SIM -0.20) and Blueprint{E,R} at
ante 4 (hard rule). The build was purely additive: foil Egg{E} +50 chips, Wily{E} +100 on trips, Banner +60, holo Mr. Bones
+10, Bootstraps +2/$5, Abstract{E} +18. That peaks ~6-7k/hand on Pair/Two Pair and ~10-11k on a Full House at ante 5,
exactly the GOLD_ANALYSIS "0 xMult tops out ~10k/hand at ante 5" cap. Ante 5 Small was cleared by 96 chips, Big by
Mr. Bones, the Serpent was out of reach (est 6k per FH after Bones and Banner were gone).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 5 shop 2: sold Banner (+60 chips = ~35% of my chips on Pair/Two Pair) for Turtle Bean. The 13-card hand made
   Full Houses (2 of 4 hands) but Pair/Two Pair ests fell 5.8k -> 3.8k; a pure wash. Banner was worth far more than its
   "+30 chips" label suggests when the build has ~40 mult and ~150 chips.
2. Ante 3-4: Grabber ($10) + 6 rerolls ($33) at ante 4-5. The rerolls found nothing; with Bootstraps the money itself
   was +2 mult per $5. In hindsight, the ante-3 Trio{E,R} (x3 on Trips/FH, my Wily hand) at SCORE CHECK 0.45 was the only
   xMult the seed offered; the hard rule (and SIM -0.20) said no, and I followed it.
3. Ante 4: kept money for Bootstraps instead of buying the main-hand planets (Earth for FH never appeared at a shop
   price). Levels would not have closed a 2.5x gap either.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Bootstraps (+2 mult per $5), THEN spend only down to the next $5 boundary (e.g. $19 -> $15 costs no mult
  and no interest); buys that cross a boundary cost 2 mult each on top of the price.
- IF you own Banner and play without discarding, THEN treat it as +60 chips (+30-40% on Pair/Two Pair at ~150 base
  chips); never sell it for a hand-size bridge unless the new joker adds more than that to your typical hand.
- IF you own Ceremonial Dagger, THEN `order` it directly left of a cheap/expiring joker (here Bootstraps at $1 = +0
  mult); it skips Eternal jokers, so an Eternal Egg's huge sell value cannot be eaten.
- IF a holo Mr. Bones is offered at ante 2 with a Wall/Psychic boss ahead, THEN buy it ($8): +10 mult while alive and it
  converted an otherwise lost ante-5 Big (28.4k/37.5k) into a pass.
- IF Turtle Bean is bought, THEN expect Full House/Trips most hands at 12-13 cards (3 FHs at the Serpent from 16 cards),
  but each Turtle round loses 1 hand size; it is a 1-ante bridge, not a fix for 0 xMult.
- IF the build has 0 xMult at the first ante-5 shop and SCORE CHECK < 0.3, THEN the run is lost unless that ante's shops
  show an xMult; the money is better spent on the best scoring joker visible (Banner-class flat adds) than on 3+ rerolls.
