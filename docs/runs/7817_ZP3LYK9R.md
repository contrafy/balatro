## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling, specifically the one-shot repeat engine. Every blind from ante 2 on was won with Card Sharp
plus Blueprint copying it (x9 on the second hand of a repeated type). That only pays when you make the same type twice
in 3 hands, and the 2nd/3rd hand is the only one that counts. With Smeared Joker (two-colour flushes) the Flush repeat
was near-certain (antes 4-6, 9 clears, never under 1.5x). Smeared was perishable; from ante 7 a four-suit Flush twice in
3 hands was a coin flip (cleared Small and Big at ~0.5 solver p), and The Hook needed THREE flushes (first flush 20k +
x9 flush 183k < 220k) with 2 random held cards discarded per play. Money was $0-10 the whole run (rent on Blueprint
every round, every shop spent to $0-3), so ante 7 had no reroll budget to find a permanent Smeared/Four Fingers.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4-6: I never planned for Smeared's death. Each of those shops should have weighed a permanent Flush enabler or a
   non-Card-Sharp xMult over flat buys (Swashbuckler{E} $4, Baseball relied on Smeared being uncommon).
2. Ante 2: the $5 reroll to $2 (whiff) cost the Big-shop budget; it was EMERGENCY but a $2 wallet buys nothing.
3. Ante 7 Hook: a Card Sharp engine needs the first hand to ALSO be big when the requirement exceeds one x9 hand. I
   had no way to raise the first hand (it is x1 Card Sharp); a second always-on xMult was the missing piece.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Card Sharp AND a free Blueprint/Brainstorm appears, THEN put it directly left of Card Sharp: x9 on every
  repeated type. It carried antes 2-7 from a $1 rental Blueprint.
- IF your engine is Card Sharp (x9 repeat), THEN register the type with your WORST cards (debuffed/low cards, the
  cheapest Pair) and save the high cards for the repeat: the first hand is only ~10% of the round. Under The Window,
  a debuffed red Flush still registers the type (8775) and still scores level+Crafty chips on the repeat (78975).
- IF you own Smeared Joker, THEN Flush is a 2-colour hand: keep 4 of one colour, discard the other colour; two flushes
  per round are near-certain. Level Flush (Jupiter) and add Crafty. Count Smeared's perish tally: skip Small/Big so its
  last round is the boss (ante 6 here), and buy its permanent replacement before then.
- IF the boss need exceeds your single best x9 hand (e.g. The Hook 220k vs 183k), THEN the round needs three type
  repeats; plan that before entering, and buy hand size / a second always-on xMult instead of levels.
- IF a boss gives one hand (The Needle) and Card Sharp is your engine, THEN re-order Blueprint onto your best flat
  joker (Jolly, Walkie) for that hand; dig with both discards for Walkie ranks / pairs (TT44 two pair hit 4012/3200).
- Serpent: discard ONE card at a time (draws 3, hand grows) to build the second flush.
- Temperance ($3) at ante 7 with ~$16 of joker sell values is a free +$13 for a reroll/pack.
