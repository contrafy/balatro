## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING. Zero xMult and zero hand levels all run: Two Pair L1 at the ante-5 boss, ~13-17k on
the best visible hand against 12.5k/hand needed with 4 hands. In 8 shop views plus 4 Buffoon packs (one Mega x2,
one Jumbo, one normal, one free) the only xMult-shaped offer was Baseball Card{PERISH,RENTAL} with 0 uncommons.
The build was chips-starved: mult reached ~50 (Gros +15, Zany +12, Even Steven), chips stayed ~100-300 and depended on
Blue Joker. The first Blue Joker was perishable and died exactly at the end of ante 4; until the permanent one came
from the ante-5 Mega Buffoon the 75th-pct hand fell from ~7.7k to ~1.9k.

Money: antes 1-3 ran at $0-4 every shop (perishable rental Even Steven -$9 rent, perishable Blue $7, $5 reroll),
so the run never held $15+ until the Investment tag paid $25. 6 skips (a2 S, a3 S+B, a4 S, a5 S+B) were right on
money each time (shops would have had $1-8), but they also meant only 8 shop views in 5 antes: few xMult sightings.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 5 Big skip for the Boss Tag. The Head (hearts debuffed) was a mild boss for me; the forced Boss Tag reroll gave
   The Fish, which turned 3 of my 4 hands into blind face-down plays. A Boss Tag only helps when the current boss is bad.
2. Never bought a level. Chips were the bottleneck, and Uranus/Celestial gives +20 chips per Two Pair level. With $16 at the
   ante-4 Big shop (Coupon) and $24 at ante 5 I spent on Directors Cut, Grabber and packs instead of 2-3 Two Pair levels.
   Directors Cut was needed for The Wall; Grabber ($10) was roughly a wash against ~2 levels.
3. Ante 2 reroll -> perishable Blue Joker $7 at $13. It carried ante 2-4, but it perished one round before the ante-5 shop
   and left me with $1-4 through ante 3. A permanent $4-5 scorer next shop (Mad Joker appeared one shop later) was the
   cheaper bridge. (Baseline SIM was 0.17, so this is close.)

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the current boss is a suit debuff (Head/Club/Goad/Window) or other mild boss AND the Big's tag is a Boss Tag, THEN do not
  skip the Big for it: the reroll is forced and can land The Fish/Wall/Needle/Water.
- IF The Fish is the boss, THEN spend BOTH discards before the first play (discard draws are face up, play draws are face
  down), put the best visible hand first, and keep a visible pair for hand 2. Later hands: `sort rank` groups the face-down
  cards by rank, then play 5 adjacent cards (two such plays scored 8052 and 10388 vs advise est 3570/2618).
- IF your jokers are mult-heavy (+40 mult or more) but chips are under ~150 on the main hand, THEN chips are the multiplier:
  buy +chips jokers (Blue, Stuntman, Square, Clever/Sly), main-hand planets and Stone cards (Tower) before another +mult joker.
- IF a perishable joker carries your chips, THEN count its rounds left against the next ante's boss: buy the permanent
  replacement before it dies, not after (Blue died with 0 rounds of warning in the ante-5 shop: 1.9k per hand).
- IF Seltzer is offered with Even Steven / per-card jokers, THEN sell a flat +mult joker for it, not the per-card joker:
  the retrigger doubles Even Steven.
