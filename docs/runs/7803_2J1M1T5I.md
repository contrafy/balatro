## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING with no money. No +mult joker and no reliable xMult was ever offered in a shop I could afford. Jokers seen in 9 shop views: Photograph, Juggler, Chaos, Clever, Hallucination, Square, Crazy, Crafty x2, 8 Ball, Wily, Mr. Bones, Smeared, Flash x2, The Trio (perishable, $8 when I had $3), Egg, Banner. Packs: Burglar, Mime, Brainstorm, Summit. The run lived on two perishable bridges (Burglar +3 hands, Mystic Summit +15). They carried antes 2-4 Small (rounds of 2-5k), then both perished together at the ante-4 Small and the base engine fell to ~2000 per hand: Photograph (card-phase x2 on a base mult of 3) + Clever +80 + holo +10. Money never got above $20 because each ante had an EMERGENCY spend (Clever + Jumbo Buffoon, Buffoon, Celestial, Bones + Grabber), Small pays $0, and $15 of rent went to Summit and Bones.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 second Buffoon pack: Summit over Brainstorm. Summit was right for antes 2-4 (+432%). But its rent ($12) and perish date lined up with Burglar's, so both bridges ended in the same round. Brainstorm copying Photograph would only have been another card-phase x2, so this was probably still right. The lesson is about timing: two bridges that expire together leave nothing.
2. Ante 4 shop: Grabber $10. With 2-4k hands a 4th hand was +2.5k per round, and that $10 plus interest would have let me buy The Trio{PERISH} $8 at ante 5 (my hands had FH/trips 3 times in 4: FH x3 = ~9k). That still loses 25000 most of the time, but it was the only real multiplier the run saw.
3. Ante 1-2: both early buys were ETERNAL (Photograph, Clever). Neither became a real multiplier: Photograph doubles only base mult + card mult. Two of six slots were locked for the run.

What worked:
- Burglar + Mystic Summit: Burglar sets discards to 0, so Summit's +15 is always on, with 6 hands. Ante-2 rounds went 560 -> 2278 per hand.
- Skipping a Small blind to keep perishables alive: skips do not tick perish counters or charge rent. Burglar's last round landed on The Mark (6 hands, cleared 6374+).
- Mr. Bones as a planned save: when one blind of the ante was unwinnable, I skipped the Big (D6 tag) so Bones would cover the boss at 25%. 4832/18000 = 26.8% was enough.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Mr. Bones and the ante has 2 blinds you cannot clear, THEN skip the Big (or the Small) so that only the boss is fought, and aim to reach 25% of it. Bones saves exactly one blind. Expect $0 blind reward on the saved round, and rent is still charged.
- IF you own Burglar (0 discards), THEN Mystic Summit is a flat +15 on every hand: take it over any non-xMult in a pack.
- IF two perishable bridges expire within 1 round of each other, THEN in the shop before the earlier one dies, buy a permanent scorer before any voucher. Do not spend $10 on Grabber while your hands are under boss/6.
- IF a perishable scorer is about to tick and the Small blind is clearable but its shop has little to buy (<$10), THEN skipping the Small keeps the perishable for the boss (no tick, no rent).
- IF a RENTAL joker shows PERISHED, THEN sell it at once. It still charges -$3 per round.
- IF a hand under The Mouth needs one more Two Pair and you have no discards, THEN play 5 off cards (scores 0) and keep the pair. Do not keep a third copy of the pair rank: trips + pair is a Full House and scores 0 under a Two Pair lock.
- IF The Fish has turned your whole hand face down, THEN play 5 unknown cards per hand (49% pair). Est shows 80-255; actual was 256-570 per hand with Summit.
