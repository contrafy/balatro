## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling with no xMult. The whole build was flat +mult (Droll +10, Onyx +35 on a black flush) times a L2 Flush; one-shot flushes of 7-8k at ante 3-4 were enough until The House (18000 = three 6k hands) where a single missed flush on 3 hands with 0 discards was fatal. I never saw a single xMult joker in 6 shops, 3 Buffoon packs and 3 rerolls.

Decisions I would change:
1. House: I used my 2nd (last) discard to chase a black flush with 4/5 held (solver said p 0.97). It missed and left me with 2 hands to make 18000. Better: use the first discard on the 5 face-downs (right) but keep the second discard for hand 2/3 once the dealt cards are known, and play hand 1 as a cheap cycle (it ended up being the cycle anyway). The 0.97 "flush draw" figure counted face-down cards as possible black cards; real p was lower.
2. Skipping the ante-4 Big for Top-up: the 2 Common jokers were Crazy Joker (+12 mult on Straight, not perishable) and To Do List, both useless, and the Big's shop (~$10 + a Buffoon/Celestial look) was forgone. Final shortfall was only 765; one more Jupiter or Mars-level plus even a Joker +4 would not have cured it, but $6 from the Big and a shop look might have. Top-up is NOT a joker source worth a Big skip when the commons are random.
3. Ante 3 shop with $14: I held it instead of the Mega Celestial pack ($8); two Jupiters would have added ~+1000 to every flush (17235 vs 18000 would have passed with one Jupiter: flush 7752 -> ~8800 on the first flush alone).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF a Buffoon pack offers Smeared Joker AND you own a Flush joker (Droll/Tribe/Crafty), THEN take it even though the advisor marks it BARRED (2nd eternal): flush becomes any-5-of-one-colour and flushes were hit on 9 of 10 hands.
- IF you own Smeared Joker, THEN Onyx Agate (+7 per Club) scores spades too: black flush = +35 mult (7191 vs 2300). Prefer black cards when choosing which flush to play.
- IF a straight/flush engine is held with Smeared, THEN scan `hands` every deal for a Straight Flush (A-2-3-4-5 low straight counts): SF L2 est 14835, 6x a flush.
- IF the boss is The House, THEN discard the 5 face-downs first, and do not spend the last discard until the visible cards show a flush with 4+ black/red; its "p" counts unknown cards.
- IF a Top-up tag is offered at ante 4 with 0 xMult, THEN play the Big instead unless you are rent-broke; the tag rolls Commons.
- IF flush `est` is ~7k at ante 4 and the boss needs 18000 (2.5x), THEN buy every Jupiter/Celestial pack with spare money in earlier shops; each Flush level is worth ~+10% of every hand.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- shopadvise `BUY Jumbo Arcana` at ante 4 EMERGENCY (ratio 0.14): the pack gave Empress/Strength/Lovers/Magician/Chariot, none lifting the round estimate more than ~3%; SIM saturated and the advisor still ranked Arcana 45 over doing nothing.
- Pack advisor marked Smeared Joker [BARRED: a 2nd ETERNAL non-scorer] although SIM showed it as the run-enabling pick (+0.17, then Onyx Agate SIM 0.98): the BARRED rule should exempt jokers that gate a flush engine already owned.
- advise on The House: "SOLVER DISCARD 3 6 7 8 flush draw 4/5, p=0.97" treated the 3 face-down cards as live; real flush p was well below that (drew red cards), and the "round p 0.26" overall was optimistic.
- advise after the Small when `est` shows Straight Flush 14835 was only discovered via `hands` (the earlier discard advice never considered keeping A,2,3,4,5 of one colour for a SF draw).
- `buy Crazy` warns nothing about a non-perishable/not-eternal Top-up result; `s` after Top-up showed new jokers without saying they came from the tag (minor).
