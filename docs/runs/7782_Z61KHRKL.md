# Run Z61KHRKL (port 7782) - stake 2 - LOSS at ante 1 Small Blind

Ante 1: start $4, no jokers. Small Blind tag=Double Tag (not a skip-worthy tag per playbook section 5), ADVICE at
blind select: `b blind`. Played it (never had a shop visit).

Hand-by-hand (Small Blind, need 300, zero jokers throughout):
1. Dealt AS KS QC TH 9S 8C 7C 3C. `advise`: no made hand, DISCARD -> disc 1 2 4 5 (drop AS KS TH 9S) chasing a
   flush/pair draw on clubs. Kept QC 8C 7C 3C.
2. Redraw gave QS QC 8S 8C 7C 5S 3C 3D -> three pair-pairs (Q,8,3). `advise` PLAY TWOPAIR 1 2 3 4 7 (Q8, filler 3C).
   est 112, actual scored 112. Running total 112/300.
3. Redraw KH QH 7S 7C 5S 4H 3S 3D -> Two Pair 7,3 available. `advise` DISCARD 1 2 5 6 (drop KH QH 5S 4H) to keep the
   pair core and draw around it. 2 hands, 1 disc left after.
4. Redraw QD JD 9D 7S 7C 3S 3D 2S -> `advise` PLAY TWOPAIR 4 5 6 7 8 (73, filler 2S). est 80, actual scored 80.
   Running total 192/300, 1 hand 1 disc left.
5. Redraw QD JD 9C 9D 6S 6H 4S 2D -> Two Pair 9,6 (est 100). `advise` DISCARD 7 (drop 4S only) on the final-hand
   floor logic: keep the made Two Pair as floor while drawing one card for upside. Need 108, 1 hand 0 disc after.
6. Redraw swapped 4S for 8H: QD JD 9C 9D 8H 6S 6H 2D -> same Two Pair 9,6, est 100, need 108 (8 short). No discards
   left, no better hand available (only 4 diamonds, not enough for a flush). `advise` PLAY TWOPAIR 3 4 6 7 8
   (forced final hand). Played it: **scored 100, total 292/300 — 8 chips short. GAME_OVER.**

`est` vs actual: every hand matched `est` exactly (112, 80, 100) — no >20% divergence to log this run.

Post-mortem: binding constraint was the classic zero-joker Small Blind wall the playbook already documents (section
5: "most losses are ante 1-2 with zero scoring jokers... $0 Small Blind leaves $4-7 for the first shops"). This
run never even reached a shop — it died on the very first blind of the game, 8 chips short of 300, entirely on raw
Two Pair L1 scores (no joker existed yet to buy). The one decision I would change: at step 3, `advise` chose to
discard KH QH (dropping two face singles) to protect a Two Pair floor rather than gambling on those faces toward
something bigger; in hindsight there was no joker in play to reward chasing anything else, so the discard was
correct — the real lever was earlier: at step 1, keeping AS/KS (11+10=21 raw chips) as filler on hand 2 alongside
the Q8 two pair could have added card-chip value without changing the hand type, since Two Pair scoring only uses
the 4 matched cards' chips plus level base, and unmatched filler cards contribute 0 either way — so that lever
doesn't actually exist under this scoring model. Genuinely the run just came up 8 chips short on pure variance:
three Two Pairs of L1 (100-112 each) sum to 292 against a 300 requirement, and there was no shop and no possible
joker purchase before or during this blind to close an 8-chip gap. This is a variance loss, not a misplay.
Bridge issues: none. Every command returned expected state; no ACTION FAILED, no retries needed.
Playbook suggestions:
1. Section 5's stake-2 Small-Blind-tag skip rule lists tags worth skipping for (Uncommon/Rare/Buffoon/Top-up/Coupon
   etc. with <2 jokers) but Double Tag is not mentioned either way; add explicit guidance for Double Tag (it applies
   the Small Blind's own tag twice, which for a $0-Small-Blind stake-2 game only matters if the tag itself is
   worth doubling — otherwise it is a neutral tag and playing the blind for the shot at a first-shop joker remains
   correct, as this run did).
2. This run is a clean data point for "raw Two Pair L1 without any joker can fall short of the Small Blind (300)
   even playing every hand near-optimally per `advise`" — worth noting alongside the existing stake-2 data in
   section 5 so future agents don't second-guess a near-miss zero-joker loss as a misplay.
