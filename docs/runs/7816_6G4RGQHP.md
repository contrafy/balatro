## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. Whole run was flat +mult/+chips (Half Joker, Sly, Lusty, Misprint, holo Juggler) multiplied only by a PERISHABLE Acrobat (x3 on the final hand). Acrobat died exactly at the ante 5 Small, which needed ~25k against a best round of ~25k. A single permanent xMult or two more Pair levels would have cleared it; the 178 gap equals one low Misprint roll.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 shop 1: I rerolled once ($5) then spent $4 on a Celestial pack (Mercury). With Acrobat having 2 rounds left and 3 jokers, I never held enough money for a second Celestial/pack. In hindsight skipping Big for the Buffoon tag (only junk + holo Juggler) cost the Big's $5 + shop: a post-Big shop with $8 would have bought a second planet or Red Card.
2. Ante 5 hand 2: played Pair KK (3135 actual) when Pair JJ with a Heart (Lusty +3) was an option later; minor. The real loss was 3 hands only (Black Deck): Pair L2 at ~4k/hand vs 25k. Should have put the $4+$8 into planets (Mercury x2) at ante 3-4 instead of holding $6 at ante 3 shop (left with nothing bought).
3. Ante 2 Ectoplasm vs Sigil: Sigil was nearly worthless; negative Half Joker would not matter either. Neutral.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Half Joker (+20 on <=3 cards) and Raised Fist, THEN every 2-card pair clears ante 1-2: discard/play low cards so the lowest HELD card is 9+ (Pair KK + 3 low fillers: est 600).
- IF you own Acrobat (x3 final hand), THEN hold the best pair/trips for the last hand and spend hands 1-2 on the next best pair (or a 1-card throwaway at ante 2-3); Trips 888 x3 cleared Big 4800 alone, Pair AA x3 cleared Manacle.

- IF the final-hand engine's est is within ~10% of the need, THEN Misprint variance (+-3k at 14k) decides it: bank extra margin earlier (planets) rather than relying on rolls.
- IF Acrobat has 1 round left and no xMult replacement is in the shop, THEN the run ends at that round; buy permanent xMult (Hologram/Cavendish) or Pair planets BEFORE it, not after.
