Post-mortem:
Binding constraint: bad luck combined with insufficient xMult depth. The Needle boss allows exactly ONE hand for the whole blind with a 5000 requirement; our best buildable hand (Flush L5, 95x12 base +45 card chips = 140x12=1680, joker adds took it to est 3920/actual 4592) simply could not reach 5000 without an xMult joker (we only ever had Photograph, a conditional x2 that fires on first face card - not reliably present/leveraged in the final spade-heavy hand which had only a King filler, not scoring). By ante 4 the playbook target of "secure xMult by ante 4" was not met: no true xMult joker ever appeared for purchase in any ante-1-4 shop despite 4/6 -> 5/6 slots being filled with useful but non-multiplicative jokers (Walkie Talkie, Even Steven, Wee Joker, Crazy Joker all add flat chips/mult, not xMult). We correctly followed shopadvise's LEAVE calls each time (no xMult was ever offered at an affordable price), so this was bad shop luck, not a misplay - but see suggestion below about being more aggressive with rerolls once ante 3+ passes with zero real xMult.

Decision I would change: at the ante-4 second shop (before Big Blind, $13 in hand), shopadvise said LEAVE because nothing was "worth it," but with 5/6 slots filled and zero xMult by ante 4, I should have reroll-hunted for an xMult joker anyway even below the normal $15 comfort floor, selling a flat joker (e.g. Walkie Talkie or Even Steven, both only situational) to make room - the playbook's "reroll below the floor if you still have none" clause technically permitted this and I under-used it because shopadvise's LEAVE verdict didn't flag the missing-xMult risk explicitly at that point.

Bridge issues: `play STRAIGHT i j k l m` on a straight-flush-forming set of indices ABORTS with "these cards form Straight Flush" - must use `play STRAIGHTFLUSH i j k l m` instead. This cost one wasted command/retry in ante 1 (documented above).

Est vs actual mismatches >20%: (1) Small Blind ante 3, Full House 888KK: est 2688, CLI-reported actual capped display at 2000 (= need) with an explicit "(est 2688 vs actual 2000: logged for calibration)" note - looked like a display clamp to `need` rather than true score, worth investigating in the harness. (2) Big Blind ante 4, Two Pair FACE J4: est 1936, actual 2464 (+27%). (3) Boss The Window ante 2: est 1680, actual 2064 (+23%). All three should be checked against Wee Joker's cumulative permanent growth (chip_mod grows every time a 2 is scored) potentially not being reflected in `est` at hand-selection time versus the moment of scoring.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Add an explicit rule: "If ante 4 has ended with fewer than 1 true xMult joker (Photograph/Blue Joker/Card Sharp/etc. count only if their trigger condition is reliably present in your build), reroll aggressively in every ante-4/5 shop even below the normal money floor, selling a flat +chips/+mult joker to make room, rather than trusting a blanket LEAVE from shopadvise."
2. Flag `play STRAIGHT` on straight-flush hands explicitly in section 1's CLI reference table, noting the required `STRAIGHTFLUSH` keyword, so agents don't waste a retry discovering it mid-run.
3. Investigate/verify the est-vs-actual score calibration for Wee Joker (permanent chip growth) and Photograph (conditional x2) - three instances this run showed actual > est by 16-27%, and one instance showed actual capped at exactly `need` despite a much higher est, suggesting the harness may clamp displayed "scored" to the blind's requirement rather than reporting true score on a decisive final hand.
