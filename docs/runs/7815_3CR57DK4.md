A skip tag that pays after the boss does not help beat the boss. With a $0 wallet and 0.19 SCORE CHECK it was still the best P(reach boss), but the boss was the real gate, and 10104/12800 shows one more shop (even $7) could have mattered. If the wall hits next time with a Trio/Duo engine, play the Small/Big for the interest-free $ and shop views.
