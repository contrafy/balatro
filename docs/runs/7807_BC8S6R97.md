## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. The whole run was flat +mult/+chips (Lusty, Scholar, Red Card holo, Clever, a perishable Fibonacci) plus ONE conditional xMult (Loyalty x4 every 6th hand) on all-L1 hands. Typical hand ~4-9k at ante 4; The House boss needs 18000. No planet was ever bought (Straight/Flush/FH/Two Pair all L1), no growth xMult, money never above $16. Loyalty was effectively a one-hand-in-six lottery: it landed on the first House hand (drawn face-down, then redrawn into AH/TT junk = 3024 instead of ~25k).

Decisions I would change:
1. Loyalty Card timing: after Pillar I knew Loyalty would fire on the third hand after (Big hand 3 / House hand 1). At Big (ante 4) I cleared in 3 hands by stacking my two best hands (9240 + 3968) so the counter fell on House hand 1 - the one hand of the round that is dealt face-down and where I get fewer usable discards. I could have burned one low hand (or played 1-card digs) in Small/Big to move the x4 onto a non-House round. A 6-hand cycle needs scheduling: put the x4 on the BOSS only when the boss hand is not face-down/limited; vs The House, count hands so x4 lands on hand 2-3 AFTER the discards have produced a visible hand (hand 1 can be a cheap dig).
2. Ante 4 shop with $12-16: two rerolls + a Jumbo Arcana found nothing. Money would have been better in the Celestial pack/High Priestess planets or buying the Standard pack skip (+3 Red Card) - none of these would have saved it either; the real miss was ante 3-4 never finding a second xMult. 5 of 6 slots were flat jokers; slot 6 stayed empty because nothing affordable appeared.
3. Taking Scary Face (chips only, perishable) and Fibonacci (perishable) as the bridges meant the engine lost 1-2 scorers every ante; an eternal Scholar/Lusty were the only permanents.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Loyalty Card, THEN count hands (the note says "x4 in N hands (this one counts)": hand N fires, and clearing a blind early carries the counter into the next round) and steer it onto the round you most need, never onto a face-down House first hand.
- IF Sun (Tarot) is in hand and Lusty Joker is owned, THEN convert 3 non-heart cards of your best 4-5 card hand to hearts first: it turned Two Pair 3906 into Flush 6795 (+75%) and the cards stay hearts.
- IF Temperance is in the shop and you own several cheap jokers, THEN buyuse it: it pays the total sell value ($9 here) for $3 and funded a Scholar at ante 2.
- IF Turtle Bean is in the shop at ante 2 with an emergency, THEN it is a real bridge: +5 hand size made Broadway/Flush/FH one-shots on Big 1500 and Flint 2000 (hand size decays 1 per round, sell it before it vanishes).
- IF the Small Blind of ante 4 needs 9000+ and your typical hand is < 4k, THEN consider skipping it for the tag (I played it, got 11.7k via Sun-flush luck, then lost the boss by 12k): the shops/rounds saved nothing, but the fresh Loyalty/hand counter for the boss should be planned first.
