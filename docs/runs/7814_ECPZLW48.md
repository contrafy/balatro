## Post-mortem

**Binding constraint:** Scoring power never reached the ante-2 -> ante-3 jump. The only permanent scorer bought was Blue Joker (chips-only, +104), with no xMult and no mult joker at any point. Ante 2 Big and Boss were cleared only on the last hand, leaving no buffer; ante 3 Small (3200) then needed roughly 4x the best hand the build could make (~750 with Blue Joker).

**Decisions I would change:**
1. Ante 1 Buffoon: Lusty Joker (+3 mult per Heart, rental, perishable 5) was a weak scorer; with its rental cost it paid about $3 per round and perished before ante 2 ended. Prefer a permanent mult or xMult source, even at extra cost, or skip the pack and keep $4 for the ante-2 shop.
2. Ante 2 shop: I left with $6 and could not afford the Mega Buffoon ($8). Earlier saving toward $8 for a Buffoon pack would have given a second scorer.
3. Ante 2 shop: I overrode ADVICE Wrathful (perishable) for Blue Joker (permanent). In hindsight the build still had no xMult, so neither purchase fixed the ante-3 jump. Blue Joker was the better of the two but was not sufficient.
4. Ante 3 shop: at $4 with ratio 0.10 (EMERGENCY) I bought only Hanging Chad ($1) because the money floor had already been spent. Death ($3) was passed due to rent; a Gold run at this ratio is unlikely to clear Small 3200 without a scorer.

**Reusable if-then rules:**
- If ante 1 shop shows a Buffoon pack and only a rental mult joker is available, prefer skipping to a later shop and keeping $4+ so the ante-2 shop can afford a permanent mult joker.
- If at ante 2 the build has 0 permanent mult/xMult scorers and money is under $8, do not spend down to $0 on chips-only jokers; keep $4 for an ante-3 Buffoon or xMult.
- If a made Two Pair or Straight has an est that clears the blind on its own (est >= need), play it at once; do not chase a higher est line with discards.
- If The Window debuffs cards, the Flush est still counts debuffed cards' suit (verified: est 452 scored 452). Debuffed cards score no chips, but still count for flush/straight.
- If the shop SCORE CHECK shows ratio < 0.15 for the next ante and money is under $5, expect a loss; still follow advice, but do not spend the last $4 on rent-bearing rentals.
