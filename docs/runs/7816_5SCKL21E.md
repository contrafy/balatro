## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: a single hand of luck. The build was Flower Pot (x3 only with 4 suits among the SCORING cards) + Odd Todd on a Straight/Two Pair engine at L1; every non-4-suit hand scored 28-370 (Two Pair AA99 = 368). The only clears were 4-suit straights. At ante 2 Big (1500) a made Two Pair was worth 368, so the round needed a straight or Full House and got three junk hands. No scorer other than these two was ever offered (3 shops, 1 Buffoon pack), and I was at $0-$5 the whole time (rent -$3/round, Small pays $0).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 Big, hand 2: with AA 99 22 made I discarded the 4 non-pair cards (6C KD..) to draw for FH with 4 outs (9C/AC for 4 suits). The 4 fresh cards were junk (KD QS TD 8H) and I ended with 3 hands of ~370 max. The solver's best lines were all ~0.5-0.55 (noise); nothing was clearly better, but the order matters: playing Two Pair 368 FIRST and keeping the discards to dig for a 4-suit straight with 2 hands left would have preserved the discard for the last hand (I played hand 1 only after burning both discards).
2. Taking Flower Pot at all: it is the only xMult, but at ante 2 its x3 fires only on straights (and flushes of 4 suits are impossible), i.e. ~25% of rounds' hands. The alternative (The Family, x4 on Quads) was worse and skipping would have left 0 scorers, so I think the pick was right; the build just had no support.
3. Ante 2 shop: $5 -> Odd Todd $4 was right (Pair AA est 188). The Mars ($3) planet could not have helped.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Flower Pot, THEN every hand must score 4 suits: pick straights (5 cards of 4+ suits) or two pairs whose 4 cards cover S/H/D/C; `hands` est shows the x3 only on hands that qualify. Pairs of the same two suits (AS AD + 9S 9H = S D S H) do NOT qualify; look for the 4-suit combination before choosing which pairs to play.
- IF Flower Pot is owned and the boss debuffs a suit (The Goad), THEN debuffed cards still count for the suit requirement (verified: A-2-3-4-5 with 3S debuffed est/actual 624 vs 600 needed).
- IF Odd Todd is owned, THEN odd ranks (A,3,5,7,9) score +31 chips each: build pairs/trips of odd ranks (Pair AA est 188 at L1).
- IF the Small Blind tag is Rare and you have $1 and a rental, THEN skip the Small (pays $0, saves rent) and hope the shop shows a Rare joker, but it only appears after the Big.
