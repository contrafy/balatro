## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no flat scorer at ante 1. The only joker was Flower Pot (x3 when the scoring cards cover all four
suits), so every hand without four suits scored at L1 base (2P ~80, Flush ~256). The ante-1 boss at 600 needs ~200 per hand.

Decisive misplay (advisor-driven): at The Head, hand 2 was AH* KC 8S 8H* 6H* 5C 5D 3H* (* = debuffed). Two Pair 8S 8H 5C 5D
covers Spades/Hearts/Clubs/Diamonds, and Flower Pot counts DEBUFFED cards for its suit check (card.lua: `is_suit(suit, true)`,
bypass_debuff; confirmed in play: 2P JH* JD TS TC est 100, actual 300). That play was ~228, not the 76 `advise` showed. I
followed the solver and discarded 5C 5D for a trips draw. Playing it, then digging with 2 hands for ~370 more, was a much
better line; the run lost by 8.

Decisions I would change:
1. The Head hand 2: play 2P 8855 (4 suits, x3 ~228) instead of discarding the 55. Do the Flower Pot suit count myself and
   not trust `est`/SOLVER, which both left the x3 out whenever a debuffed card was in the hand.
2. Ante-1 Big shop ($6, TIGHT 1.10x vs the boss, 0 flat scorers): I left with $6. A $5 reroll still leaves $1, which buys a
   $1 rental scorer (30% of Gold shop jokers are rentals). With 0 scorers going into the ante-1 boss, a joker view was worth
   more than $1 interest.
3. Ante-1 Small shop: the Buffoon pack was correct by SIM, but it gave only a conditional xMult, so I had no scorer for the
   boss. If a pack gives no flat scorer, the next shop's reroll budget should go to finding one (see 2).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Flower Pot AND a boss debuffs a suit (Head/Club/Goad/Window), THEN debuffed cards still count for Flower Pot's
  four-suit check: build 2P/FH/Straight plays with one card of each suit, including the debuffed one. 2P x3 at L1 is
  ~230-300, which is half an ante-1 boss.
- IF you own Flower Pot, THEN before every discard look for a Two Pair with four different suits among its 4 scoring cards
  (FH: 5 cards). It scores x3 on pair-family hands, not only on flushes. Do not break such a Two Pair for a draw.
- IF you reach the ante-1 boss shop with 0 flat scorers and the shop has none, THEN reroll even when only $1 is left:
  $1 rentals are common at Gold, and a rental scorer is the right buy at 0 scorers (2b ante 1-2).

Advisor/CLI defects:
- `est`/`hands`/`sel`/SOLVER drop Flower Pot's x3 when a debuffed card supplies one of the suits (2P JH*(debuffed) JD TS TC:
  est 100, actual 300; 2P 8S 8H* 5C 5D est 76, real ~228). Game code: Flower Pot uses `is_suit(suit, true)` (bypass_debuff).
  Because of this, the solver advised discarding a winning 4-suit Two Pair at The Head.
- Ante-1 boss shop with 0 scorers and TIGHT 1.10x: ADVICE LEAVE with $6. It did not consider a reroll down to $1 to look for
  a $1 rental scorer.
Bridge issues: none (19 steps, no rejects).
