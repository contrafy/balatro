## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING + MONEY at ante 5. The only xMult ever offered at a price I could pay was Acrobat (x3 on the final
hand only, ante 4). Everything else was flat: Summit +15, holo Fortune Teller +13, Abstract +18, Arrowhead +50/spade, Wily.
That made ~12-14k on the best final hand (x3 ~38k) against 50k with The Eye forbidding a second strong type. Money went
$37 (ante 3) -> $3 (ante 5): Fortune Teller rent -$3 x 6 rounds, packs at ante 4 (Buffoon x2, Arcana, Mega Buffoon = $20) that
returned only Emperor, and the ante-5 Celestial.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 5 shop: selling Seltzer (4 hands left) for Wily. The SIM said +0.06 but the SCORE CHECK dropped 27.6k -> 18.4k: Seltzer
   retriggered Arrowhead's +50 per scoring spade and every card's chips; Wily only fires on Trips/FH. With $13 the better buy was
   nothing (keep $13 -> interest, $16+ next shop for a real xMult) or Wily into... no slot. Keep retrigger jokers until they expire.
2. Ante 4 shop 1: four packs/rerolls ($20) with 0 xMult. Two Buffoon packs had no xMult; per GOLD_ANALYSIS a Buffoon pack costs
   ~$24 per r/g xMult seen, so this was in line, but it left $9-13 for ante 5 where the Eye needed one more multiplier.
3. Ante 5 Eye, hand 1: played the made Straight 4-8 (1920, no Summit) as the type-burner. Fine in hindsight, but it used the
   heart cards (4 hearts) that could have been a Flush L4 draw.
4. Fortune Teller{RENTAL}: +16 mult was worth it at ante 3-4, but its $18 rent over 6 rounds is exactly what left the ante-5
   shops at $3. Sell a flat rental once an xMult (Acrobat) carries the final hand.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Mystic Summit, THEN burn both discards before the first play every round (est roughly doubles: Pair 250 -> 2125 at
  ante 2) and keep made hands while burning; it carried ante 1-3 alone with Ice Cream.
- IF you own Acrobat AND the boss is The Eye, THEN your final x3 hand needs ~(need - 2 small hands)/3 with a type you have not
  used: count it before the first discard. A FH held for the final only helps if the 3 spare slots can also make a strong
  second hand; otherwise dig for the single hand that clears alone (here: spade Flush L4 with Arrowhead, ~59k x3).
- IF you own Arrowhead, THEN spade-heavy hands are your best hands (+50 chips per scoring spade): a 5-spade Flush is +250 chips;
  a High Card 9S (8448) beats High Card AD (2112). Do not sell a retrigger joker (Seltzer, Hanging Chad) while Arrowhead is live.
- IF rent-broke at ante 5 (<$5 after rent, shop after the Big would have ~$4) AND the Big projects under 1.2x, THEN skipping the
  Big for a level tag (Orbital) is better than fighting it for a shop that can buy nothing.
- IF under The Hook, THEN bank made hands immediately: the Hook discards 2 random held cards per play, so "hold the pair for the
  Acrobat final hand" (solver line) can lose a card of the pair.
