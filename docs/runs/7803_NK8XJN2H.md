## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling, caused by an ante 1-2 with no permanent +mult joker. The shops of antes 1-2 offered
no permanent +mult joker until the second reroll of the ante-2 Big shop (Even Steven); the first joker was a pack
Gros Michel (destroyed after 3 rounds), the next were Hanging Chad (no mult) and two perishable flush-gated bridges
(Crafty, Bloodstone). Every ante 2-4 shop ended at $1-2, so no xMult was ever affordable, and no xMult (apart from
perishable Bloodstone) was ever offered. Ante 3-4 blinds were cleared by Bloodstone variance on heart flushes
(17336 and 10147 actual vs 3520 and 2952 base est); once it perished at the ante-4 Big the build was ~4k per hand
against a 6k-per-hand Hook.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 Big shop: after Even Steven + Hermit I rerolled again and bought Crafty{PERISH} to $2. Holding $6-12 into
   ante 3 would have afforded a $6-8 xMult in the next shops (none appeared, but the money would also have bought the
   ante-4 Jumbo Arcana one shop earlier).
2. Ante 4 Small shop: Chaos the Clown $4. Its free rerolls showed 4 jokers over 3 shops, none useful; the $4 plus
   the slot would have been better as part of an Arcana/Justice buy at the first ante-4 shop.
3. Hanging Chad{ETERNAL} as the first ante-2 buy: it adds no mult. It worked with Even Steven (an even first card
   x3) and would have made a glass card x8, but a +mult joker would have scored more at ante 2-3.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Hanging Chad, THEN `move hand` the best card to the FIRST scoring position before every play: an even
  card with Even Steven (+4 x3), a heart with Bloodstone (3 rolls), a Glass card (x2 x3 = x8 on the card-phase mult).
  The advisor's REORDER line does this; on a 5-card hand it doubled est (2052 -> 3340).
- IF you own Bloodstone and play a heart flush, THEN the advisor's est (no Bloodstone) is ~1/3 of the mean; with 7
  rolls (Chad on a heart) P(>= 3 hits) = 77%. Plan the round on hearts, but never count the rolls as certain on a
  one-hand need (an FH 777JJ "est 1253" line scored 404).
- IF you must play exactly 5 cards (Psychic) or pad a dig hand, THEN pick fillers that do not belong to your draw
  (non-hearts when drawing to a heart flush); the solver's line threw 2H away as a filler.
- IF the only multipliers left are perishable bridges at ante 3-4, THEN the first ante-4 shop must buy the permanent
  multiplier (Arcana for Glass, an xMult) before rerolls/utility jokers; the run dies the round the bridge perishes.

  Crafty with 1 round left.
- Pack advise ranked Death 45 over Justice 30 in a Jumbo Arcana while owning Hanging Chad: Glass on the first-scored
  card is x8 with Chad; the ranking does not model retriggers on Glass.
