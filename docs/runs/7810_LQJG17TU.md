## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. Zero xMult for the whole run (flat Walkie/Scary/Onyx/Chad/Summit + Seltzer retriggers topped at ~12k for a 2-pair, ~32k for an L3 Straight). The Needle (25000 in ONE hand) needed a Straight/FH (P ~ 35-60% with 2 discards); I missed the straight draw (62% shot) and a 7260 high card was the best fallback. Rare Tag at ante 4 produced a conditional xMult (The Order, x3 on Straight) that I passed on as {ETERNAL,RENTAL}; in hindsight a Straight-centred build (Saturn L3, Seltzer retriggering 5 scoring cards, Hanging Chad first card) made Straight my best hand by 3x (31.8k vs 12k) so The Order x3 would have made the Needle a ~95k Straight. I passed because the main hand was Two Pair and SIM said +0.12 / 'x1 on your deck'.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 shop: passed The Order (free Rare-tag item, x3 Straight). With Hanging Chad + Seltzer + Straight levels, a 5-card hand where all cards retrigger is the highest-value hand, and Straights came ~1 in 3 rounds. Buying it (rent -$3/round, I had $28) would have turned the Needle into a ~95k Straight (needed 25k): same missing-straight risk but a much safer second hand/ante 6.
2. Ante 5 shop: spent $18 on 3 rerolls (no xMult in 9 views) and then Seltzer; the Seltzer buy was good, but the rerolls consumed the money for Chariot/planets. Rerolls at ante 5 with EMERGENCY and no slot to put an xMult into (6/6 slots, all contributing) were low-yield.
3. Never had a rescue/consumable for the Needle (Death/Strength/Familiar would not clear anyway); no Mr. Bones/Chicot/Luchador seen.
4. One accidental no-op `order jokers 1 2 3 4 5 6` at ante 3 (recorded junk action); do not test commands with state-changing verbs.
