## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING (zero xMult the whole run) plus money. Final build: Zany{E} +12 (trips), Gros Michel +15,
Mystic Summit +15, holo Scary Face +10 / +30 per face, foil Showman +50 chips, Burglar (+3 hands, 0 discards), 2P L5.
That is ~44-56 flat mult on ~150-200 chips = 8-11k per hand; Burglar's 6 hands made a ~50k round, which cleared ante 5
(Goad 52086/50000 on the 6th hand) and nothing beyond. In 12 shop views I saw exactly two xMult-shaped jokers, both
Photograph{ETERNAL} (one also RENTAL) and Bloodstone{ETERNAL}; all are card-phase/suit-gated multipliers that act on base
mult only, so they were worth less than a level on this flat-mult build. Money never exceeded $16: every shop was spent to
$0-7 in EMERGENCY, so no shop ever held $15+ to reroll twice or buy a $8 xMult.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 first shop: Celestial $4 (Earth) and ante 3 Small shop: two Celestials ($8 -> $0). Each level was worth ~10-30%
   on hands I made, but leaving ante 3 with $0 meant ante 4-6 shops ran at $5-13. Holding $8-10 from ante 3 would have
   paid ~$2/round interest and allowed a 2-reroll xMult hunt at ante 4-5. (Hard call: the SCORE CHECK said EMERGENCY.)
2. Ante 6: the Mega Celestial ($8) rolled no pair-family planet except Venus. A $5 reroll + $4 Celestial or keeping $13
   for the Small-Blind shop would not have saved the run either; by ante 6 with 0 xMult the run was over (as the
   GOLD_ANALYSIS table predicts: 0 xMult tops out ~10k/hand at ante 5).
3. Skipping ante-4 Small+Big (Foil + Orbital: Two Pair L2->L5) and ante-5 Small (Holo) was RIGHT: with $0-6 the skipped
   shops could buy nothing, and the tags gave +3 levels, a free foil Showman and a free holo Scary Face. Keep doing this.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Mystic Summit and are offered Burglar, THEN take it: 6 hands per round with Summit always on (+15), and the
  hand budget doubles. Plan dig plays (1-3 junk cards) instead of discards; `advise`'s SOLVER handles digs well.
- IF your build is flat +mult (+40 or more) with no xMult, THEN Photograph/Glass/Steel/Bloodstone are NOT multipliers:
  they multiply the base hand mult (4-8) before the +40, i.e. +5-15%. Levels (chips) and +chips jokers are worth more.
- IF a skip tag gives levels now (Orbital) or a free edition joker (Foil/Holo/Polychrome) AND your money is under $8 (the
  skipped shop can buy nothing), THEN skip, even at ante 4-5: you also dodge a Gros Michel roll / perishable tick.
- IF you own Burglar, THEN read the SCORE CHECK/TIGHT ratios as about 2x too pessimistic (they model 3 hands).
- IF you reach ante 6 with 0 xMult and 6 slots of flat jokers, THEN Small 60000 is ~10-15% even with 6 hands; the run
  needed an xMult by ante 4-5, so from ante 4 on put every spare dollar into Buffoon packs/rerolls, not planets.
