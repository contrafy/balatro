## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling with zero xMult, compounded by poverty. In 9 shop views (plus 1 reroll at ante 1 and 1 at ante 4)
not one xMult joker was offered, and money never sat above $9 (ended ante 2 and 3 at $0), so there was never a reroll
budget either. Six flat jokers (Zany, Droll, Scholar, foil Scary, foil Square, Walkie) peaked at ~4.7-6k per hand at
ante 4 against a 13500 Big (4500/hand needed with 3 hands): the round needed every hand to be a made FH/Flush and the
third deal had no pair.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 shop 3 (after Big): Jumbo Celestial $6 to $0 for Uranus. Two Pair was never actually my best hand (Flush/FH/Trips
   carried every big round); the $6 held would have paid $1 interest per round and made a later $8 xMult reachable.
2. Ante 4 shop: the $5 reroll left $3. With TIGHT 0.70x and no xMult in sight, Celestial ($4) + Mercury was the advisor
   line; neither would have closed a 2665 gap, so the call is close. Better: keep $8 and rely on the Boss Tag skip.
3. Ante 1: Zany{ETERNAL} over a Buffoon pack was fine (it powered Trips/FH all run), but every later shop was spent to $0,
   ruling out the money floor entirely; the TIGHT "floor OFF" rule fired in every shop and kept me broke.

What worked (reusable):
- IF the shop offers Riff-raff early AND you own few jokers with free slots, THEN buy it even when SIM says +0.00: it made
  4 sticker-free commons (Droll, Scholar, Delayed Gratification, Scary) over 2 blinds; then sell it once slots are full.
- IF The Mouth locks Trips/Pair AND you hold Strength + Death, THEN rank-up a card to pair a face (Strength J->Q) and
  Death a junk card into the third copy (move the source card rightmost first: Death turns the LEFT card into the RIGHT):
  that made QQQ 3750 to clear 6400.
- IF the Small Blind tag is a Boss Tag AND the ante's boss is The Wall / Violet Vessel (x2/x3) AND your hands are below
  boss/3, THEN skip the Small: the Small pays $0 at Gold and the reroll halves the boss (36000 -> 18000 here).
- IF you own no xMult at ante 3 and 6 flat jokers, THEN your ceiling at ante 4 is ~5-6k per hand: 13500 Big needs two
  made FH/Flush plus a third decent hand. Save to $8+ from ante 2 so an xMult sighting is affordable; flat stacking past
  4 jokers adds ~10% each.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- `s` at ante 4 Small: "do not skip: the Boss Tag tag is not worth a shop visit" while the boss was The Wall (36000, 9.6x my
  best hand). A Boss Tag against Wall/Violet Vessel should be advised as a skip.
- shopadvise ante 2: SIM rated Riff-raff +0.00 and advised a Juggler{PERISH,RENTAL} (+0.09); it cannot model jokers that
  create jokers. Riff-raff was the run's best purchase.
- advise SOLVER at ante 4 Big final 2 hands: "dig: play 8D 7H 7C 4C, keep AH QH QH 8H p 0.42" beat the made Flush (0.38),
  but any heart flush from those cards scores ~4.7-5.5k, below the ~7260 that line would need. Looks like a solver bug
  (maybe crediting a bigger final hand than the cards allow).
- advise SOLVER under The Mouth ignored held tarots (Strength + Death): it recommended a 5-card discard at p 0.61 when the
  tarots made a sure QQQ.
- shopadvise ante 3: Tower ranked first (30, tie with Death); Death was the boss-winning buy under The Mouth.
- SOLVER p looked overconfident early (ante 3 Small "p 0.99" after a discard with best hand 430 vs 3200); outcomes were OK.
Bridge issues: none; 76 steps, no rejects.
