Post-mortem:
- Binding constraint: scaling + money. Blinds jump 9000/13500/18000 (a4) -> 25000/37500/100000 (a5). My hands were ~5-10k flat; I never had an xMult
  (best was Photograph, perished at ante 3), never leveled a hand (all L1), and money was $0-6 every shop (Small pays $0, Tooth cost -$5).
  Money never allowed a reroll or a planet; every buy was a pack/joker at the edge of zero.
- What worked: Splash (holo, eternal) is a monster with flat-mult builds: every played card scores, so a Pair + 3 odd/spade fillers beats a Two Pair.
  Wrathful holo = +10 mult flat at ante 1. Dusk (final-hand retrigger) added ~+50% on hand 3. Ice Cream is fine when mult is ~30 (chips x mult).
  Strength on 2 low cards to make them odd (Odd Todd) permanently. The jumbo Buffoon packs were the main source of jokers on a $0 economy.
- Decisions I would change: (1) At ante 4 shop 2 ($5) I bought a Standard pack (Purple-seal Ace) instead of keeping money / a planet; neither mattered.
  (2) Ante 3 Emperor/Judgement wasted $3 on random Mime; better to buy the planet (Venus/Jupiter) or save for Dusk-like scoring. (3) Never found
  an xMult; with six eternal/flat slots I could not take Four Fingers/Business Card. (4) On the Small 25000 I used the 2 discards badly: first discard
  (3 cards for a FH, 28%) missed; second kept QQ99 only; with hands ~7k I needed 3 hands >8k each. Playing the 6732 straight first was right, the
  gap was structural.
- Reusable rules:
  * If you own Splash (every card scores) on Gold, ALWAYS play 5 cards, filling with odd-rank / spade cards when you own Odd Todd / Wrathful.
  * If Gold ante 4 shop has $5+ and Dusk/Hack/Sock-and-Buskin-like retrigger exists, prefer it over a flat joker for the boss: final hand x1.5.
  * If you plan to reach ante 5 on Gold (25000/37500/100000 Wall), you need ~8k/hand per hand at ante 4 AND an xMult or two hand levels; save planets money
    from ante 2 instead of buying marginal packs.
  * If the Small Blind pays $0 and rentals/perishables are on, expect $0-6 per shop: plan buys that are one-shot (jumbo Buffoon $6) not ongoing costs.
  * The Tooth costs $1 per card played incl. Splash fillers; 2-card pair finishes are worth it once the main hand cleared 5k+.
