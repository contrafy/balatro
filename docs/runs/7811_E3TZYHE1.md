## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scoring. Joker set was 6 flat-mult or chip jokers (Green Joker, Shoot the Moon, Mad Joker, Photograph, Bull, Gros Michel) with no reliable xMult beyond one Photograph. Hand levels were Pair L3, Full House L2, Two Pair L2. Per-hand score was 6-8k at ante 4-5 against Small 25000 (ante 5). Gold Small 25000 needs about 6k per hand over 4 hands with no misses.

Decisions I would change:
- Ante 3: I bought Bull ($6) and sold Riff-raff. Riff-raff output Photograph and Mad Joker. Keeping Riff-raff for more rolls would have been worth testing. Money-as-chips is weak when there is no multiplier.
- Ante 4: I took Justice (Glass x2) on KC over Hierophant, and Chariot over Hermit. Those were correct for xMult need, but the advisor's xMult tally did not count card-level Glass/Steel. Glass KC did score in the Mouth pair KC KD (est 6536); Steel QC (x1.5 only when held) was not scored in any hand I played. A card-level multiplier only helps when that card is in the scoring cards.
- Ante 4 shop: Grabber ($10) was bought over a reroll for xMult. Grabber helped the Mouth (4 hands) but did not fix ante 5 scoring.
- Ante 5: I did not buy Odd Todd (second rental). That was correct per the rental rule, but no permanent xMult was in the shop.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- If the shop has no xMult joker at ante 4 and you hold 0 xMult, then reroll with $10+ rather than buying a utility voucher (Grabber).
- If a Glass or Steel card is your only xMult source, then keep it in every hand's scoring cards; a Glass card sitting in the hand does not multiply.
- If the Mouth lock is Pair, then discard for a second Pair with the first hand and reserve the last hand for the highest Pair (it needs ~4.4k each).
- If ante 5 Small is 25000 and your hands estimate under 7k, then assume loss unless you own xMult; a Death or Justice tarot alone does not save a no-pair hand.
