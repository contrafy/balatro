## Post-mortem

**Binding constraint:** Economy and scoring engine together. I had one xMult-less scorer (Half Joker +20 mult, which costs $3 per round as a rental) and a chips-only Clever Joker that rarely triggered (Two Pair was almost never the made hand). Pairs topped out at 616-660 per hand at ante 2, and the Window boss needs 2000 in 3 hands. The Clever buy ($4 at $7) meant money was $1-$3 for the entire ante 2 after the Small, so no shop could buy a second scorer or a planet.

**Decisions I would change:**
1. Ante 2 Small (D6 tag): I overrode the advisor's skip. With $3 and no income, a skip (D6 = free reroll) might have found a mult/xMult in the shops, and the skip cost nothing in money. Next time, when broke and no scorer besides a rental, take the skip if the tag grants reroll or money.
2. Ante 1 shop at $4-$5 (Celestial/To the Moon/Lovers/Jumbo): I kept the $4 floor and bought nothing. A main-hand planet (Pair) would have raised every later Pair score. Next time, when the main hand is Pair and a Celestial for Pair is in the shop at $4+, take it.
3. Ante 2 Window: spending two discards on debuffed cards and then a weak pair draw left nothing for the final hand. Against a boss needing ~3x the blind's per-hand score, prefer the made Pair plus one discard, not discard-chasing.

**Concrete reusable rules (if-then):**
- If you are at ante 2 with 0 xMult, a rental, and $3 or less after the Small, then take a skip that grants a reroll or money over playing the blind; the playbook's skip rule is too strict for this state.
- If the main hand is Pair and a Celestial/planet for Pair is in the shop and you can afford it with $4 left, then buy it (it lifts every later Pair).
- If a boss needs more than 3x your per-hand Pair score, then spend discards to draw toward Two Pair/Flush before playing the pair; never burn discards on debuffed cards you can simply play around.
- If a shop offers only a second rental (Lusty, Misprint) and you already own one, then leave; the advisor's rule-advice line still says BUY (see defects).
