## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: no real scorer at the ante-1 boss. My only joker was Ride the Bus, which reset on every face hand
(QQQ, then the J-high straight) and added +0 in the boss round. Every boss hand was L1 base.

Decisions I would change:
1. Buffoon pick: Walkie Talkie{ETERNAL} (advisor 90, +10 chips +4 mult per scoring 10 or 4) over Ride the Bus (73).
   I avoided the Eternal sticker. Counterfactual: the boss Straight 7-J contained TH -> (30+44+10)x(4+4) = 672
   instead of 296, so 150 + 672 = 822 > 600 would have cleared the boss with a hand to spare. Ride the Bus was worth
   +2 mult on the Big Blind and 0 on the boss.
2. Shop 1: Buffoon $4 over Lusty Joker $5 (advisor 133). Lusty (+3 mult per heart) fires under The Club and would
   have added +3 to QQQ and +3 to the straight. That is small, but it was a guaranteed scorer, not a lottery.
3. Boss hand 1: QQQ trips for 150 with fillers (solver p0.87, n=100) over discarding toward FH/quads from QQQ. With no
   joker, 150 of 600 left 450 over 2 L1 hands, and that needs a straight/flush/FH. That outcome follows from point 1.

## Reusable rules (if-then)
- IF a forced ante-1 Buffoon pick is Ride the Bus vs a flat scorer (even Eternal), THEN take the flat scorer. A
  standard deck has 12 faces, so Ride the Bus resets in most rounds where you play trips, straights or face pairs. It
  is worth about +0 to +2 mult at ante 1. An Eternal tag costs nothing while you have 5 empty slots.
- IF the ante-1 boss is The Club/Goad/Window/Head, THEN prefer a joker that does not depend on the debuffed suit, and
  buy the first sure scorer (Lusty for The Club) over a Buffoon pack gamble when the shop has one.
- IF you own no +mult joker going into the ante-1 boss, THEN expect each L1 hand to make 50-300. 600 in 3 hands needs
  two of straight/flush/FH, so the boss is close to a coin flip. A sure +4 mult joker before it is worth more than $1-2
  of saving.
