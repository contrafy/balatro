## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING (no reliable xMult until Ramen at the end of ante 4) on a starved economy. Antes 1-3 were
carried by perishables (Raised Fist, Space Joker, Riff-raff commons) that all died by ante 3; the only permanent mult
source was Shoot the Moon{E} (+13 per held Queen). Every shop ended at $0-10, so the first real xMult (Ramen) and the
second multiplier (Hit the Road x1.5) came only in the ante-4/5 Buffoon packs. Lost the ante-5 boss by 3748 (7%).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 5 first shop: Jumbo Spectral ($6, Trance) gave one Mercury. The same $6 on a Jumbo Celestial (Pair level now)
   plus keeping the blue seal idea for later would have added ~+10% to every Pillar hand: about the 7% I lost by.
2. Ante 3 first shop: two rerolls ($11) bought Mercury + Riff-raff/Scary; fine, but I then entered ante 4 with $10 and
   0 xMult. Skipping the ante-4 Small for nothing was not possible (Polychrome); the real fix was finding a Buffoon pack
   earlier: none was offered in 7 shops at antes 2-4.
3. Pillar, first discard: I followed the solver and broke Pair 99 keeping a DEBUFFED JH; keeping 99 + KH QD and discarding
   the debuffed card would have kept a made pair (the same 99 came back anyway).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Shoot the Moon, THEN never play a Queen: play pairs/junk around them, hold every Queen (4 held = +52 mult;
  a 1-card High Card JH holding QQQQ scored 2385 at ante 4 and closed the Small). Digging with junk plays accumulates Queens.
- IF you buy Riff-raff (rental), THEN sell junk jokers before each blind select so it fills the free slots, and SELL
  Riff-raff mid-round once slots are full: rent is charged at round end, so a mid-round sale pays no rent at all.
- IF you own Hit the Road, THEN spend a discard on clean (non-debuffed) Jacks before the big hands: one Jack = x1.5 for
  the rest of the round (Pillar p 0.00 -> 0.65 per the solver after the discard). Debuffed Jacks do not count.
- IF the ante-5 Small offers a Handy Tag, THEN count `run.hands_played_total` (in `raw`): it pays $1 per hand played
  ($21 here) immediately at the skip.
- IF a Blue Seal goes on a card you always hold (a Shoot the Moon Queen), THEN end every round with a Pair as the LAST
  played hand so the seal makes Mercury, not Earth/Jupiter.
- IF you own Mr. Bones and a blind is clearly lost, THEN play only the fewest cards that reach 25% (Pillar: cards played
  in Small/Big are debuffed at the boss) and use discards, not plays, to dig.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- `est` omits Fortune Teller's +1 mult per tarot used: Straight A-T est 684, actually cleared 848 (171 x 5 = 855).
- Solver/est ignore Hit the Road's growth from discarding Jacks: before the JC discard the Pillar was "UNWINNABLE"
  (p 0.00), after it p 0.65; pack `advise` ranked Hit the Road -5 ("rarely fires") and SIM equal to skipping.
- Pillar solver line `DISCARD 4 5 6 7 8 (keep KH QD JH)` kept a debuffed JH; debuffed keepers should be treated as junk.
- shopadvise printed "REROLLS ARE CHEAP: the next reroll costs $0 (D6 Tag ...)" for Chaos the Clown's free reroll.
- Shoot the Moon's value is underrated in SCORE CHECK early (it treats held Queens as luck); the build reached 4185-14813
  per Pair with 1-3 Queens held.
