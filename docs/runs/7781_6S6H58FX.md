## Post-mortem

Final result: LOSS at ante 6, The Mark boss blind (40000 needed, scored 38293, short by 1707/4.3%).
Jokers at loss: Space Joker, Hiker, Droll Joker, Mystic Summit, Abstract Joker, The Duo (only 1 xMult, acquired late
at ante 5). Levels at loss: Full House L6, Flush L6, Straight L2, Two Pair L5, Pair L4.
Money curve: $4 (ante1 start) -> $9/$5/$12 (ante1-2 shops) -> $7/$10/$6/$2 (ante3) -> $12/$8/$5/$0 (ante4, hit $0
right after filling jokers) -> $7/$13/$2/$6 (ante5, Temperance rescued a $1 low point) -> $6/$2/$6 (ante6). Money was
persistently thin (frequently $0-6 after shop purchases) because nearly every shop dollar went into packs/planets;
by ante 6 the $15 interest floor was routinely unaffordable, so no interest income compounded.

Binding constraint: SCALING, not a single misplay. The run found its first (and only) xMult joker (The Duo, x2 if
Pair) at ante 5 instead of the ante 3-4 target the playbook recommends, and no second xMult or Glass/Steel-on-scoring-
rank stacking ever arrived (the two Steel cards give x1.5 only while HELD/unplayed, so they routinely did NOT
contribute to the hand that mattered, since most winning hands used all 5 relevant cards). Every one of the last
four blinds (ante5 Small, ante5 Big, ante6 Small, ante6 Big) cleared by under 1000 chips (108, 216, 700, 674) - the
margin kept shrinking each blind despite consistently following `advise`, which is the clearest sign the scaling
curve (roughly x1.5-2 per blind from levels+jokers) was falling slightly behind the blind curve (which roughly
doubles per ante). The final boss loss by 1707/40000 (4.3%) is well within the range that one more xMult joker, one
more Glass/Steel card actually landing on a played card, or one better planet-pack pick (2 wasted Celestial packs at
ante 6) would have closed.

Decision I would change: at ante 3-4, when the Arcana pack forced an immediate tarot with no scoring use for THIS
hand (e.g. The Chariot with no held-permanently-unplayed slot planned, or Hierophant on cards that get played
every hand and thus rarely stay "held"), I treated card-targeting tarots primarily as one-off chip/mult stickers.
In hindsight, Justice (Glass, x2 on EVERY score, no held-card timing requirement) would have been categorically
better than Chariot/Hierophant applied to cards that get played (not held) most hands - but Justice never appeared
in any pack this run, which the playbook already warns about (7a: "Justice never appeared... do not rely on a single
multiplication source"). Given that, the real lever I'd pull differently is shop money discipline: this run bought
almost every Arcana/Celestial pack offered from ante 3 on regardless of whether it hit our main hand, which kept
cash too thin to reroll aggressively for xMult before ante 5. I would bank 1-2 more shops of cash at ante 3-4
specifically to reroll for xMult sooner, even at the cost of a wasted-planet pack or two.

Bridge/CLI issues: none. Every `disc`/`play`/`pick`/`sel`/`buy`/`sell`/`reroll`/`endshop`/`blind`/`cash` command
returned cleanly on the first try all run; no `ACTION FAILED ... retry` was ever seen. Two behaviors worth noting
for the next agent (not bugs, just non-obvious mechanics confirmed this run): (1) a tarot picked from a PACK always
forces immediate `sel`+target if it targets cards, even with 0 cards highlighted - it CANNOT be banked as a
held-for-later rescue consumable that way; only a tarot bought directly from the shop's buy-slot (`buy N`, not
`pick N`) lands in a free consumable slot untouched. (2) `advise`'s est is a pre-hand estimate and can undershoot
actual score substantially (by up to ~106%, e.g. a Straight est 720 -> actual 1485) when Space Joker's random
level-up procs mid-hand; conversely `advise` sometimes recommends PLAYing a hand whose est is BELOW the remaining
need with hands still left in the round ("pacing" logic) - this worked correctly every time it fired this run, so
trust it, but it means "est < need" is not itself a signal to discard when advise explicitly says PLAY.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Section 5 (ante 3-5 money target: "reach $25 by ante 4 and stay above it"): this run's money never reached $25
   at any point past ante 3 (peaked at $13 in ante 5), while still following every `shopadvise` recommendation.
   Consider having `shopadvise` more aggressively refuse pack purchases that don't hit the main hand once ante 4+
   with 0-1 xMult, in favor of holding cash for a reroll-to-xMult hunt - the current logic bought 2 planet packs in
   a row at ante 6 that missed the main hand (Full House) entirely.
2. Add an explicit note that Steel/Chariot-enhanced "standby" cards (kept deliberately unplayed for the x1.5 held
   bonus) are frequently swept INTO the scored hand anyway when they complete a Full House/Two Pair/Trips (as
   happened repeatedly this run, e.g. "Full House 222AA" and "Two Pair J2" both consumed a Steel 2D as a scoring
   card, losing the held-bonus for that hand) - so the x1.5 payoff is much rarer in practice for a pair-family
   build than the "permanent x1.5 whenever held" framing implies; Justice (Glass, scores on every play regardless of
   held/played timing) should be called out as strictly better for a pair-family main hand when both are available.
3. Document the "face-down filler swap" end-game trick used this run (substitute a face-down card for a normal
   filler in a forced final hand when discards are exhausted and the safe play falls short): since unscored filler
   cards never contribute chips, swapping one for an unknown face-down card is a strictly-dominant gamble (same
   floor, higher ceiling if the hidden card completes a better hand type) and should be added to section 4.8's
   last-hand checklist alongside the consumable-rescue check.
