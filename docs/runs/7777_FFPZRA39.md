# Run FFPZRA39 (port 7777) - LOSS at ante 1, boss blind (The Manacle)

## Stake 1 (White)

Ante 1: Small Blind (300, tag Uncommon), Big Blind (450, tag Rare), Boss The Manacle (600, -1 hand size).

- Small Blind (need 300): started with pair T / pair 5, chased a club flush across all 3 discards (advise-driven),
  landed 5-club flush est 264, played it (actual 264), then Pair 8 est 52 (actual 52) to close at scored 316. Cleared.
- Shop 1 ($4->$8): shopadvise ADVICE buy Supernova ($5, PLUS: +mult = times this hand played) - only affordable
  scoring joker in shop (other option was a UTIL reroll joker). Bought. 1/6 jokers, $3 left. Second shopadvise said
  LEAVE (nothing worth it at $3). endshop.
- Big Blind (need 450): held a straight 3-7 draw, advise chased it into a flush by discarding around it across all
  3 discards. Landed 6-club flush, played best 5 (KC 9C 8C 5C 4C) est 355 -> actual 426 (+20% over est, first
  Supernova-boosted Flush play). Then Pair T est 90 -> actual 120 (+33% over est) closed the blind, scored 546/450.
  Cleared, cashed out to $8.
- Shop 2 (Neptune/Saturn planets, Celestial Pack, Standard Pack, Reroll Surplus voucher, no jokers offered):
  shopadvise ADVICE = LEAVE at $8 (nothing worth it, no scoring joker in shop). Followed. endshop.
- Boss The Manacle (need 600, hand_size 7, only 1 joker: Supernova): dealt weak hands all round (best made hand
  never above a low pair/two pair). Used all 3 discards chasing a stronger pair/two-pair (advise-driven each time),
  ended up repeatedly playing PAIR (not the higher-chip Two Pair candidates) because Supernova's per-play mult
  stacks on the exact hand type already played twice, so repeat Pair plays out-scored a fresh Two Pair (est 120 vs
  112, then 168 vs 104). Hand 1: Pair J+J est 120 -> actual 150 (+25%). Hand 2: Pair 8 est 130 -> actual 176
  (306-130 delta... see below). Hand 3 (last hand, no discards): Pair 9 est 168 -> actual 196 (502-306).
  Final scored 502/600. LOST — run ends here (single joker, no xMult, no flat +mult beyond Supernova's self-scaling,
  never drew a flush/straight/full house at the boss with only 1 joker slot filled and hand_size reduced to 7).

## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: **jokers/scaling**, not misplay. One PLUS joker (Supernova) by ante 1's boss is simply not
enough raw mult+chips to clear a 600-point boss off pair-only hands (Manacle's -1 hand size also hurt: fewer cards
to build a flush/straight with). The two shops before the boss offered no second scoring joker (one had only a
UTIL reroll joker and a voucher; the other had planets/packs with no joker at all) — this was bad luck, not a
missed buy; `shopadvise` correctly said LEAVE both times since nothing on offer added scoring power at $3-8.

The one decision I would change: at the first shop (had Chaos the Clown $4 [1 free reroll/shop] as the only other
buy besides Supernova, plus Buffoon Pack $4 and Arcana Pack $4), I followed shopadvise's Supernova buy at $8->$3
and had nothing left to also grab Buffoon Pack ($4, guaranteed joker choice). In hindsight, skipping Supernova
($5) for the Buffoon Pack ($4, guaranteed second joker slot pick, likely a flat +mult joker) plus keeping $4
would have left me a shot at TWO jokers before the boss instead of one, at the cost of not having Supernova's
self-scaling play-count mult during those same two blinds (which was already carrying most of the flush/pair
scores here). This is a genuine tradeoff, not a clear misplay, but worth flagging since Supernova is a slow-start
joker (its mult only grows through repeat plays) and the ante-1 boss came before it had scaled up much.

Bridge issues: none. Every `advise`/`shopadvise`/`play`/`disc` call worked as documented, no `ACTION FAILED` or
retry needed the whole run.

Calibration notes (est vs actual, all on the high side — Supernova's growing mult is not fully priced into `est`
until after enough repeat plays accumulate):
- Big Blind Flush: est 355 vs actual 426 (+20%)
- Big Blind Pair T (2nd hand of round, Pair already played once before): est 90 vs actual 120 (+33%)
- Boss Pair J (1st hand): est 120 vs actual 150 (+25%)
- Boss Pair 9 (3rd Pair play this round): est 168 vs actual 196 (+17%, within threshold but trending down as % as
  Supernova's per-play mult growth rate decelerates relative to the base)

Playbook suggestions:
1. `est` under-predicts scores for repeat-play-scaling jokers (Supernova, Card Sharp on repeats, etc.) by roughly
   20-30% once the joker has already fired 1-2 times this round — worth a note in section 3 so agents don't
   over-discard/over-fold a hand that `est` marks marginal when a scaling joker is already active.
2. Consider flagging in section 5 (ante 1-2 shop) that Buffoon Pack (guaranteed joker pick) is a safer floor than
   a single PLUS joker whose value depends on multiple repeat plays landing before the ante-1 boss, when only one
   of the two is affordable and the shop's non-joker slots (voucher/UTIL reroll/planets) add nothing to ante-1
   scoring either way.
