Best result: stake 2, ante 5, boss blind (The Mouth), 16469/22000 (75% of target). This is a deeper run than the prior run on this port (which lost ante 2 boss The Water at 252/1600).

Final jokers (6/6): Scary Face (+30 chips/face), Bootstraps (+2 mult/$5 held), Fibonacci (+8 mult per A/2/3/5/8), Wee Joker (+8 chips per scored "2", permanent, reached +32), Shoot the Moon (+13 mult per Queen held), Constellation (xMult, grew from x1.0 to x1.6 over the run by feeding it every spare planet).
Hand levels at game over: Two Pair L4 (80x5, our main hand), Pair L4 (55x5, fallback), Four of a Kind L3, Flush L2, Straight L2.
Money curve: mostly spent down near $0-3 every shop (fed Constellation and joker upgrades); briefly spiked to $35 (Investment Tag payout ante 2) and $16 (Uncommon Tag ante 4 Big Blind). Never accumulated a cash reserve; entered the ante 5 boss with only $2 and zero spare consumables after using Strength.

One decision I would change: after buying Strength as a rescue tarot at ante 4, I should have re-bought a SECOND rescue tarot (Death or another Strength) at the very next opportunity per playbook 7b ("RESCUE TAROT ... RE-BOUGHT right after you use one"), instead of spending on Mars/Jupiter to feed Constellation. Strength was used mid-round on The Mouth's second hand and there was nothing left for the third/final hand, which came up short by 5787. A second rescue tarot in hand for that final hand could plausibly have turned a losing 16469 into a win, since the Strength rescue alone added roughly 5500+ points of swing on the second hand.

A secondary factor: Constellation was the only real xMult source all game (reached only x1.6 by ante 5 -- growth was slow because celestial packs kept offering Mercury/Mars/Saturn/Jupiter instead of Uranus, so most planets fed Constellation without also leveling the main hand). No second guaranteed xMult (Photograph/Card Sharp/Baron-style) ever appeared in any shop this run. The build never got the "two xMult sources by ante 6" the playbook targets.
