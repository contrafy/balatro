## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING. I never owned a real joker-phase xMult until Throwback at ante 4, and no second one ever
showed (13 shop/pack views after ante 2: Family{R}, Bloodstone{P,R}, Throwback{P} were the only xMult-shaped offers).
The build was holo Lusty (+10 always, +3 per heart) + Photograph + foil Midas, i.e. a heart-flush engine at Flush L1-L3:
~3-6k per normal hand at ante 4, ~11-24k for the rare heart flush with a heart face. Ante 5 boss needed ~17k per hand
on all three hands; the sim correctly called it unwinnable before the second hand. Money was $0-7 through ante 3
(Small pays $0, one rental at a time), so ante-2/3 shops could only afford $1 rentals and free Astronomer items.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 Buffoon pick: Astronomer{ETERNAL} took a slot for the whole run. It produced 3 free Celestial packs + 1 free
   Jupiter (~4 levels, ~$16), but the Arm stripped two of them and none of it was a multiplier. Skipping the pack
   (both cards eternal) would have kept the slot for an xMult that a later Judgement/pack could have filled.
2. Ante 3 first shop ($7, EMERGENCY): an Arcana pack (Empress) instead of a $5 reroll for a joker view. Small value either
   way, but joker views were the scarce resource all run.
3. Ante 5 shop ($31 after Temperance): I spent $11 on two rerolls and kept $15. With the skip-to-boss plan that $15
   bought nothing (I died at the boss). Should have spent down to $0 on rerolls/Judgement (sell Midas, it was perishing)
   hunting a second xMult: money does not score.

What worked (reusable):
- Skip-to-boss with Throwback from ante 4 turned an unwinnable Big 13500 + Arm 18000 into one Arm fight at x1.75 with
  the Juggle Tag's +3 hand size (11 cards made a flush AND a full house in the same round). Perishable jokers do not
  tick on skipped blinds, so a perishable Throwback bought at ante 4 would have lived through the ante-8 boss.
- Hand ORDER on a per-card +mult build: Photograph's x2 multiplies the mult accumulated SO FAR in the card phase,
  which includes Lusty/Gluttonous-style per-card +mult and Mult cards. Moving the heart face to the RIGHT end of the
  flush took the Arm's last hand from ~7k (face first) to 11501 (face last) and won the blind.
- Midas Mask + gold cards held at round end: +$12 at the Hook by playing a 1-card High Card and holding 4 gold faces.

Rules (if-then):
- IF you own Photograph AND a per-card +mult joker (Lusty/Greedy/Wrathful/Gluttonous, Mult cards, Onyx/Arrowhead), THEN
  put the face card you want doubled LAST among the scoring cards (`move hand`) and check with `sel`: x2 then applies to
  all card-phase mult instead of the base only (KH last: 11501 vs ~7000).
- IF Throwback is owned at ante 4+ AND the Small/Big need is > 1.5x your typical round, THEN skip both; pick the Big tag
  that helps the boss (Juggle = +3 hand size for that boss). A perishable Throwback only ticks on bosses.
- IF a Buffoon pack offers only ETERNAL utility jokers (Astronomer, Square, Drunkard) and you hold < 2 xMults, THEN skip
  the pack: a permanent slot is worth more than ~$16 of free planets.
- IF the plan is skip-to-boss, THEN the single shop per ante is the only place money becomes score: spend it to $0 on
  joker views (rerolls, Judgement, Buffoon) instead of holding $15 for interest.
- IF The Hook is the boss AND you hold gold cards, THEN finish with a 1-card play so the Hook's 2 random discards are
  least likely to take the gold cards ($3 each held).
