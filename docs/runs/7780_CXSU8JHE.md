Flat/random scaling plateaus while ante targets grow ~1.5x each level; without xMult the Big Blind (16500) was out of reach once the draw ran cold.
  Decision I would change: at ante 2 I overrode shopadvise's recommendation to buy Seeing Double (conditional xMult, x2 if a Club and another suit score) citing playbook 7a's warning against unreliable xMult as a *sole* source.   Playbook suggestions:
    1. When 0 reliable xMult jokers are owned by ante 3, and a shop offers ANY xMult joker (even a "conditional/unreliable" one like Seeing Double, Zany Joker, or Crafty Joker), buy it as a placeholder and swap it out later when a reliable one appears - do not pass on the only xMult candidate seen, even citing the "never let it be your only xMult" rule; that rule should read as "don't stay married to an unreliable xMult forever," not "refuse to ever hold one."
    2. `advise`/`hands`: when multiple cards of the same rank exist in hand and only one is debuffed by the active boss, the straight/flush/pair selection should prefer the non-debuffed card automatically. Flag this in the CLI wrapper if it can be fixed there.

For jokers with random mult/chips ranges (Misprint and similar), consider printing both the average-based est AND a "worst case" (min roll) figure next to `need`, so a play/discard decision made when a hand's est only narrowly clears `need` can account for downside risk explicitly, rather than a single averaged number.
