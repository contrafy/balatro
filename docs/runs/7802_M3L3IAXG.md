## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: multiplier and money together. In 9 shop views (plus 4 Buffoon packs) the only xMults offered were
The Family twice (Four of a Kind only, $1 rental at ante 1, $8 perishable at ante 4) and a free Bloodstone{E}. Every
scorer I could afford was a perishable/rental bridge (Droll{P,R}, Even Steven{P}, Popcorn{P}, Smiley{R}): they cost
~$25 in rent and replacement buys and all died or decayed by ante 4, so the Plant was fought with 2 permanent flat
scorers (Scholar, Sly{E}) + decayed Popcorn (+8) + a face joker the boss disabled. Round ceiling ~11k vs 18000; the
SCORE CHECK said ~11.4k before the boss and the result was 11234, so the loss was decided in the ante-4 shops.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 Jumbo Buffoon: I took holo Smiley{RENTAL} (SIM +0.16) over Mad Joker (+10 mult on Two Pair, permanent, no
   rent) with The Plant already shown as the boss. At the Plant Smiley was worth only its holo +10 = Mad Joker, and
   it cost $3 per round. Mad Joker was the right pick.
2. Ante 4 Small skip for the Buffoon tag: the Mega Buffoon held only economy jokers (Delayed Grat, Gift Card, Golden,
   To Do List). The skip was still defensible (Small 9000 vs round ~8.5k), but it removed one shop visit in an ante where
   I had no xMult. Golden Joker money never got spent.
3. Ante 4 Big shop: $14 went to a reroll + Celestial (Pluto/Jupiter/Neptune) + Arcana (Fool -> Jupiter). Flush levels were
   unused at the boss. Keeping $9 would not have saved the run either; the real miss was having no permanent mult earlier.
4. Ante 1: Droll{P,R} from the first Buffoon (flush-only rental, 5 rounds) won the ante-1 Big, but rent kept me at $1-5
   through ante 2. Acceptable at 0 scorers; sell it the moment a permanent +mult appears (I did at ante 2).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the upcoming boss is The Plant, THEN never pick a face-scaling joker (Smiley, Scary, Photograph, Sock and Buskin)
  in that ante's shops/packs; a permanent hand-type +mult (Mad/Jolly/Zany) is worth the same at the boss and more later.
- IF you hold Tower (or any Stone source) before The Needle / a one-hand blind, THEN turn a junk card into Stone and add it
  as a filler: +50 chips on a ~100-chip Two Pair is +45% (KKJJ 2852-class hand -> 4140, cleared the 3200 Needle).
- IF a Mega Buffoon tag is the Small skip reward at ante 3-4 with 0 xMult, THEN skip only when the Small itself projects
  under ~1.0x; the pack is ~50% economy jokers.
- IF every option at the boss is p < 0.05 with 2 hands left, THEN play the made Two Pair now (it banks) rather than a dig;
  a lone remaining FH draw cannot reach the need anyway (here max ~10k vs 14.4k).
- IF The Plant debuffs your faces and you own Scholar, THEN Aces carry the round: AAA Trips 8733 was the best non-face hand
  in the ante; keep Aces through discards.
