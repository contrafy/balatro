## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: money, which became scaling. The run never ended a round with $5+ after ante 1, so it earned $0 interest
in 9 rounds. Red Stake's $0 Small Blind plus $10 of Popcorn rent ($1 + 3 rounds x $3) kept every shop at $0-4 at ante 2-3,
so the only xMult was a conditional one (The Trio, x3 on Trips/FH) and the flat side stayed at Green Joker(~6) + Smiley.
Typical non-trips hand at ante 4 was ~1000 vs 3000/hand needed; every blind from ante 3 on depended on drawing trips or a
full house (Flint and ante-3 Big were won exactly that way, ante-4 Small was not).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 Popcorn{RENTAL}: I took it because SIM said baseline p 0.10 for The Club; the Club was then one-shot by a
   heart Flush (1904, would have been ~480 without Popcorn but 2 hands were left). The rental's real cost was ~$10 and
   pushed the run to $-3 at ante 3. Take a decaying rental only when the made hands clearly cannot clear.
2. Ante 3 Small shop: I kept Popcorn (8 mult, decaying) through the Small Blind and paid another $3 rent, ending at
   $-1 with Uranus ($3) and Celestial packs on offer that I could not buy. Selling it first would have left ~$3-4.
3. Ante 4: Supernova + Celestial pack drained $9 to $0 for a Mercury (off-type). With main hand Trips at L1 and a Trio,
   the single Celestial gamble (38% main planet) was not worth going to $0; Supernova alone and $4 kept was similar.
4. Ante 2: Green Joker{ETERNAL} at ante 1 was correct for survival, but its value (grows +1/hand, -1/discard) stayed
   at 5-8 mult all run because every hard blind needed both discards.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own a decaying rental (Popcorn) and its mult has dropped to <=8, THEN sell it at the START of the next shop,
  not after the next blind: one more round costs $3 that is 75% of an ante-3 shop budget.
- IF the Small Blind pays $0 (Gold) AND your money is under $5, THEN interest is gone for the run unless you skip the
  rental/pack spend for one shop; plan to end at least one ante-2/3 round at $5+.
- IF your only xMult is The Trio, THEN Trips/Full House is the main hand: in the hand, keep trips/pairs and use the
  solver's "dig" (junk High Card play, keep the set) when the made hand cannot cover the rest; it won the Flint
  (FH JJJKK 7140 on the last hand) and the ante-3 Big.
- IF the Uncommon Tag is the skip prize at Gold, THEN expect its free joker to be stickered (here Castle{RENTAL}); it is
  not "a free joker" for planning.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- shopadvise ante 4: `ORDER FIRST (free): b order 1 4 2 3 (round est 14760 -> 16200)` with jokers Green, Smiley,
  Supernova, The Trio: it moves The Trio (x3) LEFT of Smiley and Supernova (+mult), which can only lower scores. The
  order est is inverted for this joker set.
- Blind select ante 2: "you own 0 scoring joker(s)" while owning Green Joker (+4) and Popcorn (+16 mult).
- shopadvise ante 3 named Flush as main hand ("BUILD: your main hand is Flush ... shift to Pair") one shop before it
  switched to Three of a Kind; the Flush plays were forced one-shots, the Trio was already owned.
- shopadvise SIM baseline for the ante-2 shops: p_next 0.77 (before Big) then 0.00 (before The Needle 1000, which was
  cleared with a flush); SIM "p_next" values swing too much to rank on at ante 1-2.
- Solver p at ante-4 Small (disc 0.70, 0.71, dig 0.73, FH 0.42) all lost; at The Flint (0.43-0.61) all won: fine as
  coin flips, but the 0.7s were optimistic on a deck needing trips.
