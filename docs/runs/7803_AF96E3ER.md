## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: joker supply and money. In 9 shop views (incl. 3 rerolls) I saw exactly one any-hand +mult joker
(Popcorn, decaying) and zero permanent +mult/xMult I could afford; the only real multiplier (NEGATIVE Joker Stencil,
x6 with 1-2 jokers) cost $13 when I held $7. Final build: Popcorn (+8, decaying) + Mystic Summit (+15) at Pair L1 / Two
Pair L2 = ~2000 per hand at ante 4 against 3000/hand needed. Antes 1-2 never reached 2 permanent scorers: the first
Buffoon pack offered Splash / Shoot the Moon, and every Gold Small Blind paid $0, so each shop had $4-9.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 Small shop ($9): I left to save for the Big shop. The Big shop then showed no scorer either and I spent $11 on
   2 rerolls there anyway. With $9 then and $15 later, a reroll at BOTH shops would have shown 4 more joker cards for the
   same money; at ante 1-2 with one conditional scorer, saving between consecutive shops buys nothing.
2. Ante 3 first shop: Celestial ($4 -> Uranus) instead of holding $7. Holding would not have reached the $13 Stencil
   either (the Small paid $0 and I skipped it), so the Celestial was fine; the real miss was having only $7 entering
   ante 3 (no interest all run).
3. Selling Shoot the Moon for Popcorn won The Water (Flush 1776 at +20) but left me with zero PERMANENT scorers going
   into ante 3. Judgement bailed me out with Summit; without it the run ends at ante 3. Correct for survival, but it
   shows how thin the build was.
4. Ante 3 double skip (Charm Mega Arcana + Boss Tag) was right: it turned The Wall 12800 (my round ~6800, 0.53x) into
   The Arm 6400 (cleared 7000+), Popcorn did not decay, and Judgement gave Summit. Without it the run dies at ante 3.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the Big Blind carries a Boss Tag AND this ante's boss is The Wall (or Violet Vessel) AND your projected round is
  under ~0.8x of it, THEN skip the Big (and the Small too if its tag pays now): a base boss at half the chips is the
  only clearable fight. Here: Wall 12800 at 0.53x -> Arm 6400 cleared.
- IF you own a decaying joker (Popcorn, Ice Cream) as a main scorer, THEN prefer skipping blinds whose shops you cannot
  use (< $5): a skip has no round end, so the decay does not tick.
- IF you hold a Judgement option in an Arcana pack AND own <= 3 jokers, THEN take it over any enhancement tarot: a free
  random joker (here Mystic Summit, +15 mult) is worth more than +30 chips on 2 cards.
- IF Mystic Summit is owned, THEN burn both discards before the first play every round (the advisor does this) and
  count its +15 into every hand's pace.
- IF at ante 1-2 you own only one conditional scorer (Shoot the Moon: +13 per HELD Queen, ~0.6 Queens per deal) THEN
  treat it as half a scorer: it made 450-810 on lucky deals and 52-60 on normal ones.
