## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING with no money. Zero reliable xMult was offered in 9 shop views and 4 Buffoon packs (the only xMult was a
perishable rental Photograph). The score came from flat jokers: Blue Joker{RENTAL} +~90 chips, Banner +60, Ice Cream, Abstract +24, holo +10,
and Shoot the Moon x Mime (+26 mult per held Queen). That flat build plateaued at ~10k per hand (17k with a Queen held), while
ante 5 needs ~17k per hand. Two rentals (Blue + Photograph) at -$6/round kept money at $0-5 from ante 3 on, so no shop could
buy a multiplier even if one had appeared.

Decisions I would change:
1. Photograph{PERISHABLE,RENTAL} at ante 3. Photograph's x2 fires when the face card scores, BEFORE joker +mult is added. On a build
   whose mult is all joker +mult (Abstract, Shoot the Moon, holo), it doubled base mult 2 -> 4: +2 mult, for $1 + $3 rent per round.
   `hands` showed Two Pair face J8 est 7372 = 194 x 38, not x2. I sold it 1 round later; it cost ~$5 in a run that died $1-5 short of every buy.
2. Blue Joker{RENTAL} at ante 2 was right (+104 chips took Pair KK from ~420 to ~2300 and carried antes 2-4), but after it I should
   not have taken a second rental. With one rental income was ~+$3/ante; with two it went negative.
3. The first Serpent discard (1 card, Banner -30 chips) found QH, so it paid. The last-hand dig was the only line with outs.
   Banking with the Banner kept: High A 16.6k vs 18606 needed was a certain loss.

What worked:
- Mime + Shoot the Moon: each held Queen = +26 mult. Every Serpent hand scored 25-42% over est (17408/13872, 13986/11100, 14168/9982).
- Flat-chip build vs The Flint: halving base chips/mult barely matters when 90% of chips and mult come from jokers. Any hand scored ~6k,
  so I played no discards (Banner +60 kept) and 1-2 card hands (Blue Joker counts cards left in the deck at scoring time).
- Ante 5 Small+Big skip (Double then Rare): with $1 and -$6 rent/round, the Small/Big shops would hold ~$0. Playing them only risked
  the run and decayed Ice Cream. The Serpent was the only blind that mattered.

## Reusable rules (if-then)
- IF your mult comes from joker +mult (Abstract, Jolly, holo editions, Shoot the Moon) and not from card-time sources (Smiley, Mult cards,
  levels), THEN Photograph/Glass/Triboulet-type card-time xMults are worth almost nothing: they multiply only the base and card mult
  before the jokers add theirs. Check the `hands` est before paying for one: if est does not double, it is not a multiplier.
- IF you already pay one rental, THEN do not take a second one before ante 6, even an "xMult": 2 rentals (-$6/round) cost more than the
  Big+Boss payouts, and money stays at $0 for the rest of the run.
- IF you own Banner, THEN count each discard as -30 chips on every later hand that round. Discard only when a found card is worth more
  (e.g. a Queen for Shoot the Moon is +13..26 mult); never to "improve" a hand that already paces.
- IF you own Blue Joker, THEN play the fewest cards that score (no fillers): each card drawn costs 2 chips on the next hand.
- IF you own Shoot the Moon, THEN Mime doubles every held Queen (+26 each). Never play a Queen; dig for Queens with 1-card discards
  under The Serpent (it always draws 3).
- IF money is below $5 with rentals eating the payouts, THEN the Small/Big shop is worth nothing: skip Small (and Big, if its tag
  is good) and spend the risk only on the boss. Re-check this whenever money is stuck under $5 at ante 4+.
