## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING (no growing xMult, Pair/TwoPair at L1/L2) plus a Blue Joker that decays as the deck drains. Engine was Raised Fist (+2x lowest held card) + The Duo (x2 Pair) + Blue Joker: ~6-9k per good hand at ante 3-4, 18000 needed 3 hands of 6k with no slack. Hands 1-2 of the Hook made 13240; the last hand had 24 cards left in the deck so Blue Joker gave +48 chips instead of +92 and I only had Pair 3s (2816).
Decisions I would change:
1. Hook round: I played hand 1 with 8-card draws unmanaged, then burned 3 discards chasing pairs. With Blue Joker the FIRST hands of a round are worth the most (deck size shrinks by ~5 per play); so play the strongest hand first and discard small, keeping deck count high. Last hand lost ~45 chips x mult ~45 to deck shrink.
2. Ante 4 Small skip for Coupon Tag: the free shop had no xMult; I would have kept the Small (9000 was clearable) shop visit and its ordinary stock, since I owned only 1 xMult. Coupon is only worth it when you can afford the best item anyway; its value is the free packs/joker slot, not extra money.
3. I never found a second multiplier: stopped after 2 rerolls at $9. Arcana/Celestial packs ($4) at ante 3-4 (planets for Pair, Justice for Glass Kings) were passed for interest that was spent anyway.
Reusable rules:
- IF you own Raised Fist, THEN every play should be 5 cards (pair + 3 LOW fillers) and keep the 3 highest cards held; dump low cards by discarding before playing, because it adds 2x the lowest HELD rank (K held = +26 mult). Est from `sel` is accurate.
- IF you own Blue Joker, THEN play your biggest hand first each round and avoid discarding more than needed; its bonus falls 2 chips per card drawn (92 -> 48 by the third hand).
- IF a Rare Tag is available at ante 2-3 and you hold 2+ scorers and a free slot, THEN skip the Small: it gave The Duo and The Tribe for free (7810_CCLZQ4S8).
- IF the boss is The Hook, THEN held cards beyond what you play are discarded randomly after you play, so do not rely on holding more than 1-2 high cards across hands.
