## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. A flat chips/+mult build (Abstract +18 mult, Half Joker +20, Blue Joker +104 chips, Odd Todd +93 chips with Chad) made ~8-10k from a single card/pair and ~12k from trips, which carried antes 1-4 (every blind one-shot or 2 hands) but cannot reach 37.5k+ at ante 5 (needs ~12.5k on every one of 3 hands, I had 8-10k pair/high-card hands). Never saw an xMult: 1 Top-up, 4 Buffoon packs (Smiley/Walkie, Half/Shoot the Moon, Delayed/Square, 8Ball/Greedy/Summit/Popcorn), 8 rerolls, 3 Uncommon/Rare-type tags. Held 3 eternal flat jokers (Walkie, Half, Blue) which is fine for ante 1-4 but meant the slots were locked when xMult hunting began.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 reroll (to find a joker with $12) rerolled away The Empress $3 and Jupiter; Empress on 2 cards would have turned base mult 2 into 10 for pairs back then. With 0 scorers prefer an Arcana pack/Empress over a blind reroll when only a Chad-class joker may appear. Minor.
2. Ante 4-5: I spent ~$27 on 5 rerolls (no xMult shown, Droll/Cloud 9/Misprint rental/Scary Face/Riff-raff/Gift Card) - money that never scored. Better: buy the Jumbo Celestial/planet packs for Pair/Trips levels each shop from ante 3 (Trips L2->L5 would be +60 chips +6 mult on 3-card hands) and the Venus at ante 5; I left Venus $3 on the table.
3. Level the hand type the build uses. My main play was 1-3 card hands under Half Joker (High Card, Pair, Trips) but I only levelled Pair/Trips by 1 and High Card by 1; each Trips level is +20 chips +2 mult on a 3-card Half Joker hand (~+7%).
4. At ante 5 Big with a dead draw I could not clear 37.5k; skipping the Small blind at ante 5 for the Holographic Tag (free holo joker +10 mult in next shop) was rejected by the 'never skip at ante 5' rule; unknown whether it would have helped.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Abstract Joker and 3-4 other jokers early (Top-up Tag gives 2 free Commons), THEN even a 1-card High Card scores ~1500 at ante 2: skip the ante-2 Small for a Top-up Tag whenever you have 2-3 jokers (+2 jokers = +6 mult via Abstract).
- IF you own Half Joker (+20 mult, <=3 cards), THEN play 1-3 card hands only; a Pair + 1 filler (3 cards) keeps +20 and draws 3 fresh cards without spending a discard (advisor does this).
- IF you own Hanging Chad + Odd Todd/Walkie Talkie/Scary Face, THEN the first scoring card triples every per-card effect: put an odd rank (A,9,7,5,3 or a 4/10 for Walkie) first and a 1-card High Card is ~8k.
- IF the shop offers Blue Joker (+2 chips per deck card = +104 chips at 52 cards) at ante 3-4, THEN buy it even Eternal (it was ~+60% on my hands); it is a real scorer.
- IF Ice Cream is the first buy at ante 1 ($5, +100 chips), THEN it carries the Big Blind and ante-2 Small/Big at ~250-700 per hand but melts 5 chips/hand: sell it for a permanent chips joker when it reaches ~50.
- IF a flat build reaches ante 5 with 0 xMult (round ~33k vs Big 37.5k), THEN it dies at the ante-5 Big or Boss: the xMult hunt must start by ante 3 with every spare dollar on Buffoon packs (Mega Buffoon $8 is the best $/xMult-look) rather than single rerolls.
