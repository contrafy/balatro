## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING, specifically CHIPS. Every joker I bought was +mult (Fibonacci +8/card, Gros Michel +15,
Bootstraps +2-6, Half Joker +20, Jolly +8) and none was an xMult. A Pair of Aces ended up 40 chips x 55 mult = 2200; a Pair
of Kings 38 x 39 = 1482. Against the ante-3 boss (6400 = ~2130 per hand) only 5-card hands (straights/flushes, 55-80 chips)
or Trips (Wily +100 chips) reached pace, and the final Arm hand dealt only pairs. Score check said 0.42-0.44 for the whole
of ante 3 (65% death band) and I never found an xMult (none offered in 6 shop views).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 Big: skipped for a Rare tag with 3 flat scorers and $6. The tag gave Wee Joker{ETERNAL} (+8 chips, grew to 8)
   and cost the Big's ~$5 + one more shop view. In a chips-starved build the extra shop (a chance at an xMult or Sly/Wily)
   was worth more than a random rare.
2. Ante 3 Small shop: bought Half Joker (+20 mult) on top of ~45 mult. With mult already 1.5x chips, a +mult joker adds
   ~30%; I should have kept $13 (Bootstraps +4, interest) or taken the Jumbo Arcana for Hierophant/Justice.
3. Ante 2: Bootstraps{ETERNAL} $7 at $14. It only pays while rich; at Gold I sat at $5-13 so it was +2-4 mult in an
   eternal slot. A permanent-slot buy should be a real scorer.
4. The Arm: I broke TT (Pair 1482) on the last discard for a 99/88 set draw (solver 0.78). It missed. Not clearly wrong,
   but the Arm round showed the deck could not reach 2.1k per hand without luck.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your flat +mult already exceeds the chips of your main hand (Pair: 30-45 chips) THEN the next joker must be +chips
  (Sly, Wily, Clever, Stuntman, Hiker, Scary) or an xMult, never another +mult joker: here Half Joker +20 on 55 mult = +36%,
  Wily +100 chips on 40-chip trips = +250%.
- IF you own Fibonacci + several +mult jokers THEN 5-card hands with A/2/3/5/8 (A-2-3-4-5 straight 1980 at ante 1, flushes
  3081) outscore pairs by 1.5-2x because they carry 35-75 chips; let the solver chase them on Small/Big.
- IF you are at ante 2 with 0 xMult and only flat scorers THEN do not skip the Big for a Rare tag: the shop + $5 is the
  better xMult lottery (Rare tag here = Wee Joker{ETERNAL}).
- IF the boss is The Arm and Pair/Two Pair/Straight are still L1 THEN play them freely (L1 cannot drop); only the leveled
  type (Trips L2 here) loses.
- IF Bootstraps is offered{ETERNAL} at Gold with <$20 THEN skip it: the run never holds $20+ before ante 5.
