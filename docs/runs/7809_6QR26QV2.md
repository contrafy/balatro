## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling/ceiling. Three flat additive eternal jokers (Summit +15, Half +20, Ride the Bus +5) gave ~1100 per hand on a pair and ~1500-1900 on a 5-card hand, never an xMult and never a planet. Ante 3 Small needs 3200 = 3 hands of ~1070, which is exactly the ceiling with zero variance room; the draw to a flush missed (55%) and I lost by 327. I arrived at ante 3 with $12 and no jokers worth buying, spent it on a reroll -> Half Joker, and had $2 and three unsellable eternal slots left.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 Big: with $6 left after Ride the Bus I bought nothing at the ante-2 shops (kept $8 / $6). Both Celestial packs (Mercury/Pluto levels add chips AND mult on top of Summit's flat +15) were passed. At 0 scorers/1 scorer with flat stacks, every level of the main hand adds roughly +8% per hand; I held cash that did not score (and I skipped interest by spending anyway). Spend on score, not on a floor, when SCORE CHECK < 0.5.
2. Ante 2 shop after Small ($11): rerolled once and left with $6, then bought nothing. Should have taken the Arcana/Celestial pack or the 2nd reroll for a real scorer.
3. Three eternals (Summit, Ride the Bus, Half) is allowed by the playbook (core scorers), but none was an xMult: the build had a hard cap of ~1.1-1.9k/hand and nothing to buy that could break it (no sellable jokers). At ante 3 Small I could not even sell for a Celestial/anything. If the 2nd/3rd scorer is also an eternal flat joker, plan an xMult hunt immediately: the cap is below ante 3's 3200.
4. Ante 3 last-hand play: after hand 1 (pair 99 1148) I chose a 3-card junk High Card (615) to chase a 5th diamond (55%). Alternative lines (Pair 55 820, Pair 44 ~756) totalled 2730 max, also short; the chase was right in EV but the whole round needed ~1.07k/hand average which this build cannot give. The real mistake is upstream (no scaling).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Mystic Summit, THEN burn both discards on every hand (keep the made hand) and read `est` at 0 discards (advise prints it); the flat +15 mult is worth ~3x a Pair. It still caps ~1.1-1.9k a hand.
- IF Half Joker + Mystic Summit + Ride the Bus (all additive) are your only scorers at ante 3, THEN 3200 needs 3 hands of 1070: you need an xMult, a planet level or 2 more scorers before ante 3 Small, so at ante 2 spend ALL cash on score (planets for Pair, Buffoon packs) rather than holding $6-8.
- IF a hand needs Half Joker (<=3 cards), THEN pair + 1 filler (3 cards) keeps it and still draws; 4-card Two Pair loses it (est 966 vs Pair 1148).
- IF Ride the Bus is owned, THEN avoid faces; a scoring face K resets it (the final Pair KK reset it from 5 to 0 and was only 1110).
- IF three eternal flat jokers fill half the slots with $2, THEN you have no outs left at a failed last hand: keep at least one sellable joker or a consumable (Strength/Death) for the last-hand check.
