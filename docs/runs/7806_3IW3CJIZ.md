## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling at ante 2 with no permanent mult. The only real scorer was a perishable Shoot the Moon that
needs a Queen held; Spare Trousers starts at +0 and was worth +2..+4 mult when it mattered. Without a Queen in hand the
best L1 hands made 200-580 against 1500. Two shop views at ante 2 (plus one reroll) offered no scoring joker.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop 2: Spare Trousers{ETERNAL} for my last $6. It adds 0 mult on the first Two Pair of the run and only +2
   per Two Pair after; it was effectively a $6 non-scorer that took the ante-2 reroll money. Keeping $6 would have made
   the first ante-2 shop $13-14 = two rerolls with $3-4 left for a Jolly/Sly, or one reroll plus a $6-8 joker.
2. Ante 2 Big, first discard: kept the made Two Pair KK33 (est 184, 12% of the 1500 need) on the "never break a made
   Two Pair on n<300" guard; the solver's 5-card discard (keep AC KH KD) was p 0.90 vs 0.76 for the rule pick. When the
   made hand is a small fraction of the need and the score comes from a held-card joker (Queens for Shoot the Moon),
   more cards drawn is what matters.
3. Ante 2 shop 1 ($7, EMERGENCY 0.11): leaving was defensible but the reroll there ($5 -> $2, plus $2 Shoot the Moon
   sale) was the only joker view before the Small; with $6 saved from decision 1 it would have been clearly right.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own 0-1 permanent +mult jokers at ante 1, THEN do not spend your last money on a growth joker that starts at
  +0 (Spare Trousers, Supernova at 0 plays, Ride the Bus, Green Joker): it scores nothing in the next 3 blinds; keep the
  cash for ante-2 rerolls/+mult jokers.
- IF a made hand's est is under ~20% of the remaining need AND a solver line that breaks it leads by >= 0.10 p (even on
  n=100), THEN take the solver line: the made hand cannot reach the need, so "never break a made Two Pair" protects
  nothing.
- IF your scorer is Shoot the Moon (+13 per Queen HELD), THEN every hand should draw as many cards as possible
  (5-card plays/discards) until a Queen is in hand, and the Queen is never played.
- IF the only scorer is perishable and the ante-2 shops show no +mult joker, THEN treat the Big Blind as EMERGENCY and
  reroll at the Small shop down to the price of a $3-4 joker.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- shopadvise ranked Spare Trousers{ETERNAL} 110 "NEEDED NOW: a scorer for the next blinds" at ante 1 while its own SIM
  showed +0.01 over buying nothing: a joker at +0 mult is not a scorer for the next blinds; NEEDED NOW should require the
  joker to add score in the SIM (or exclude growth jokers at 0 stacks).
- advise kept the rule pick (disc 1 4 5 6, p 0.76 n=400) over the solver's disc 4-8 (p 0.90 n=100) because "breaking
  a made Two Pair needs n >= 300", although the Two Pair was 184 of 1500 (not on pace). The guard should not apply when
  the made hand plus 85% of later hands is far under the need (the PACE line itself said so).
