Small 800 (scored 2112 in one hand, Full House QQQ66 hitting both Zany 3oak-mult and Sly pair-chips). Shop: Celestial Pack -> Uranus (Two Pair L2, 40x3); bought The Empress tarot ($3). Boss The House (need 1600): all 8 dealt cards showed NoneN(facedown) - `advise` had no candidate. Discarding the facedown cards drew normal face-up replacements (the debuff only applies to the originally-dealt cards, not redraws), so we cleared all 8 to visibility over 2 discards. Two Pair L2 hands (384, then 384 again with a filler to maximize redraw) got us to 768/1600 with only 1 hand and 0 discards left. On the final hand (Pair 5-5 est 255, still 577 short - an unwinnable spot already), I used the unused Tower tarot on one of the paired 5s (`sel 5` then `use 1`) WITHOUT checking its effect first: The Tower converts the card to a Stone Card (+50 chips but no rank/suit), which destroyed the pair itself, dropping the best available hand to High Card (est 16). Played it out; final score 834 vs need 1600. GAME_OVER.
(est 16 vs actual 66 on the final High Card play: +50 flat from the Stone Card's own chip bonus, not a real misestimate - ratio looks large only because est was tiny.)

Post-mortem: binding constraint was scaling (Two Pair L2 alone tops out around 384-1188/hand, nowhere near ante-2-boss 1600 over 3 hands) compounded by a self-inflicted misplay. The decision I would change: do NOT use The Tower (or any untested tarot) on a card that is part of the only scoring pair/hand in a must-clear situation - `sel` and check the "Preview of highlighted cards" line for the enhancement name/effect (or ask `s`/`raw` for its description) before `use`, and prefer targeting a filler card that is NOT part of the scoring hand. Even without that mistake the hand (Pair 255) was still 577 short with 0 discards left, so the deeper issue was under-scaled jokers/levels for a boss at 1600 by the end of ante 2 - needed either a second joker on the pair-family stack (we only had Zany + Sly, no xMult) or the Pair/Two Pair level higher before hitting this boss.
Bridge issues: none - all commands behaved as documented, no ACTION FAILED/retry needed, no Connection refused.
Playbook suggestions:
1. Section 7a should warn explicitly: never `use` an unidentified tarot on a card that is part of your only viable scoring hand under time/discard pressure - `sel` first and read the "Preview of highlighted cards" effect name, and if it converts to a Stone/enhancement that could strip rank (Stone Card has no rank/suit), target a non-scoring filler card instead.
