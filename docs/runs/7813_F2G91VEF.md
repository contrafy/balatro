## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling + economy. Three flat jokers (Popcorn decaying +20, Even Steven +4/even card, Odd Todd +31 chips/odd card) and a perishable Chad reached ~4.5k per good hand at ante 4, vs 13.5k (Big) and 36k (The Wall). No xMult was ever offered in 6 shops, 4 Buffoon packs, 1 reroll. Money never exceeded $15 (Gold Small pays $0, rentals cost $3/round, Half Joker->Chad rent kept me at $0-$3 for ante 1-2): the pack/reroll hunt was starved. The ante-1 shop with only $4 forced a single Buffoon pack, whose only scorer was a perishable rental that died at the ante-2 boss, leaving 0 scorers at the ante-3 shop.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 Big: I spent both discards early digging for a Flush (p 0.37) and then had 0 discards for the last two hands (best 3672/2408). Keeping a discard for the last hand might have found a 4.5k straight; with p 0.14 the run was nearly lost anyway, but the sequence "Flush first with discards at 0" threw away the ability to fix hands 2 and 3.
2. Ante 3 shop ($15): I bought a $4 Buffoon for Odd Todd (fine) but left with $11 not rerolling; the SCORE CHECK there was 0.73 and the next shop (ante 4) had no joker anyway. A Mega Celestial for Two Pair would have been dead money too. The real issue is no money.
3. Ante 4 Small shop ($3): could have sold Popcorn (+8 mult left, sell $2) to afford Swashbuckler ($4) - not obviously better.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF Hanging Chad + Odd Todd/Even Steven are owned, THEN `move hand` the highest-value same-parity card to slot 1 before every play: Straight est 3072 -> 4512 and Two Pair 3040 vs 1550 by putting an odd/even card first (Chad retriggers the FIRST SCORING card 2x, so a 3x Odd Todd proc). `advise` prints `b move hand` for it - follow it.
