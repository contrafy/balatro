## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING. The whole run had one xMult, a perishable The Duo found at the end of ante 4. Every other scorer was flat: Summit +15, Jolly holo +18, Mad +10, Fibonacci +8 per A/2/3/5/8, two Steel cards. The 75th-percentile hand at the ante-5 shops was 26-29k against a 50k boss, i.e. ratio 0.66-0.72. The Wheel turned that into a 3% miss. 25 jokers were seen across about 9 shop views and 4 Buffoon packs. The xMult-shaped ones were Campfire (ante 1, $9 with $5), Bloodstone{PERISH} (hearts), Baron (ante 5), The Duo{PERISH} and Campfire{PERISH,RENTAL}.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 5 money: I spent $23 on Arcana (Stone), Spectral (Aura), Jumbo Celestial (Mercury), a reroll and a Celestial (Mars). I still died holding $12, the same pattern as 7805's earlier ante-5 loss by 132. Under TIGHT, the $12 should have gone on the second Celestial pack plus anything with a level, before the boss. 1584 points is about one Pair level on the final hand.
2. Wheel hand 2: I played TT66 with the steel 6H as part of the Two Pair (15552). The steel x1.5 was worth more held. Playing TT + 6D + fillers loses the Two Pair, so this was probably right. But I should have run `sel` on every TT/66 split before committing.
3. Skipping the ante-2 and ante-3 Small blinds (Meteor, Coupon) cost two shop views. Those are about 2 more chances at an xMult during the window where they are cheapest. The Coupon shop paid well (Mad, 2 Uranus, Steel 8D for $0). The Meteor gave only Earth + Saturn (off-type). Skipping for Meteor with no pair planet guaranteed was the weaker skip.
4. Baron pass (ante 5): correct. The SIM agreed and the final hands held no Kings.

What worked (reusable):
- Mystic Summit at Gold is a near-permanent +15. Discard twice before the first play almost every round. With 8-card hands the made pair/straight survives two small discards, and the Big/Boss rounds then usually close in 1-2 hands (+$1-2 per unused hand).
- The A-2-3-4-5 straight with Fibonacci (4 triggers = +32 mult) beat the 6-high straight by 2x. With a Steel 6 held it beat it by 3x (11272 vs ~5300).
- A Coupon Tag skip at ante 3 with 3 scorers: the free shop gave 2 Uranus, a free Mad Joker and a Steel card.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Mystic Summit, THEN treat every hand's est as est + 15 mult once both discards are spent. Discard first even when holding a made hand that clears after the discards (e.g. Pair TT 60 -> 510 at ante 1). Never discard a card of the made hand for that purpose: discard junk 1 card at a time.
- IF Fibonacci is owned and A-2-3-4-5 and 2-3-4-5-6 are both available, THEN play the wheel A-5. It has 4 Fibonacci triggers vs 3, and it keeps a 6 (possibly Steel) held.
- IF the final hand of a Wheel/Fish/Mark round is short and a face-down card is in hand, THEN include it with two visible fillers whose ranks have the most unseen copies. A match turns Pair into Two Pair (+Mad, L4, Fib triggers), roughly x3 here. It missed this time (about 7/30 outs).
- IF ante 5 starts with only a perishable xMult, THEN every shop must buy levels for the main hand or another xMult. Do not leave the ante-5 boss shop with $10+ when TIGHT is printed. Interest is worth $2; a lost boss is the run.

Advisor/CLI defects:

With Summit, TT after the last discard was 30x17 = 510 > 450. Summit's post-discard +15 is not modeled in PACE/rule picks.
