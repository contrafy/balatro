## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. The build never owned a reliable/growth xMult: the only multiplier was Seeing Double (x2,
conditional), on top of suit-flat jokers (Arrowhead +50 chips / Wrathful +3 mult per spade) and a perishable rental
Hanging Chad. That peaked at ~30-60k per hand, enough for ante 5 (Psychic 50k) and, with a lucky Misprint 19+, the ante-6
Needle (62560/60000), but ante 7 needs 73k per hand (Hook 220k). 7 shop rerolls and 3 packs at antes 5-7 showed zero
xMult jokers. 10 skipped blinds (every Small/Big from ante 4 on, plus ante-1 Big and ante-2 Small) kept the run alive
(each skipped blind was one I could not have won at ante 6-7) but without Throwback the skips added no scaling, and they
cost ~10 shop visits: the xMult that might have appeared in those shops never had a chance to.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4-5 skipping: skipping Small+Big at ante 4 (Charm/Economy) and ante 5 (Economy/Handy) gave money and preserved the
   perishable Chad, but I then had $37-48 at ante 6-7 with nothing to buy. Playing the ante-4 Small (9000, ~1.5x my hand)
   would have bought 1-2 more shop views at the point where an xMult still mattered. Skip when the blind is unwinnable or
   the shop is unaffordable; when the blind is easy and you are rich, play it for the shop.
2. Ante 3: Seeing Double over a third reroll was right, but I should have bought Jupiter/Celestials more aggressively for
   the Flush main (stayed Flush L2 all run). Each Flush level is +15 chips +2 mult on a hand that carried every boss.
3. Ante 2: Faceless Joker (from Judgement) sat as dead weight for 2 shops; a Judgement into a full-ish board is a gamble.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF a Coupon/Arcana pack offers The Fool right after you used Judgement, THEN take The Fool: it recreates Judgement = a
  second free joker (did it twice here: Arrowhead and Faceless).
- IF you own Smeared Joker, THEN Seeing Double fires on ANY hand with 2+ black cards (Smeared makes a club also count as a
  spade, and SD's suit loop then finds both "suits"): verified 29538/35492/37680 black flushes scored with x2.
- IF you own suit jokers (Arrowhead/Wrathful) and The Lovers appears, THEN make your best non-suit card Wild: it scores
  as the suit AND triggers Seeing Double (the Needle clear needed the wild AD).
- IF you own Hanging Chad, THEN `move hand` your best suit/face card to the first scoring position before every play: it
  turned est 10500 into 16800 (House) and est 48760 into 62560 (Needle).
- IF a perishable scorer is key for the boss and has N rounds left, THEN count rounds: skipping Small/Big does not tick it,
  so skip enough blinds that it is still alive at the boss (Chad, 1 round left, lasted to the ante-7 boss).
- IF the boss is The Needle, THEN compute your one-hand ceiling (`sel` prints the Misprint range) and use discards to fish
  for the specific card that lifts the ceiling over the need (KS replacing 3S raised max 58.9k -> 64.4k).
- IF at ante 6+ the solver says UNWINNABLE for the next boss even at Misprint max, THEN spend everything in the shop
  before it (I died holding $46): money only matters if the run survives.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- `advise` candidate list underestimates hands with a wild card / Chad retrigger: Pair AA (wild AD first) listed est 2288
  while `sel` of the same cards gave 6936 (actual 12240); it also repeated "move hand 2 1" after the move was made.
- `advise` at The House said "keep the 3 highest cards" (AH KH QC) and would discard the only spade (4S) on a spade-joker
  build; it should keep suit-joker cards.
- Ests ran 25-65% low all run (10500->16800, 8800->13728, 22240->28912, 6936->12240, 48760->62560): the Misprint
  "conservative 6" plus Chad retriggers; one case ran high (36102 -> 29538, Misprint roll 0).
- `s` at blind select kept saying "do not skip: ante 5+ the shop is worth more than a tag" even when the next blind was
  1.5-3x beyond my best round (Small 110k vs ~35k hands) and a perishable key joker would have died first.
- shopadvise "main hand Two Pair (L1)" while Flush carried every boss from ante 2 on (play count vs. score weighting).
- Note: I accidentally issued 3 b.sh commands in one parallel batch once (use 2 / sellc 1 / reroll); they executed in
  order without stale-state problems, the `use 2` was refused (NOT LEGAL, nothing sent).
