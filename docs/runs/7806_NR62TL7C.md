## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: money -> no xMult. I never held more than $12 and was at $0-5 in 8 of 10 shops: Small pays $0,
the Ancient rental cost $3 x 9 rounds = $27 (more than all blind rewards after ante 1), and every $4-5 went to a
flat/conditional scorer. Not one reliable xMult was offered in 9 shops + 4 packs (The Idol{E} $6 at $2, Photograph
perishable). The real engine was Ancient Joker on flushes of its suit (8307, 10197, 9835: x1.5 per card, x1.5^3 on
the Chad-retriggered first card), while the leveled "main hand" Two Pair L2 made 2-4k. Without a second multiplier
the ante-4 boss under The Fish (face-down draws after the first play) needed two such flushes in one round.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop: Buffoon pack to $0 (SIM +0.02) gave Fortune Teller (useless). Keeping $4 would have bought Hanging
   Chad a shop earlier with money left, and started interest.
2. Ante 3 Small shop: I overrode the SIM (Photograph{PERISH} +0.27) for Abstract{E} (+0.13). With Hanging Chad,
   Photograph on a face-first hand is x2 three times (x8 on base mult); it would have been alive for The Fish (4
   rounds: two skipped Smalls). Abstract's +15 was steady but not enough. When the SIM gap is >= 0.10 and the ante
   after next is the risk, follow it.
   Rental study note (docs/analysis/rental_study.md): this run is one more example of the ante-1 rental pattern. The
   holo Ancient{RENTAL} $1 was a conditional, not reliable, xMult; the shop's Buffoon pack was the right stopgap.
3. The Fish: with a club Flush needed twice, I spent both discards before the first play (correct for The Fish), but
   should have accepted that the round needed ~6k per hand and checked earlier (ante-4 Big shop) that only an
   Ancient-suit flush reaches that; the Celestial/Jupiter route or Justice (Glass) on a club would have helped more
   than Emperor.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Ancient Joker, THEN your real main hand is a Flush of its current suit (x7.6 on 5 cards, more with a
  retrigger on the first card): infer the suit from `advise` (the high-card est of that suit is highest), discard
  toward it, and move a suit card leftmost when Hanging Chad is owned (+70%: 1206 vs 988, 2279 vs 1358).
- IF a rental leaves you at <= $5 in three consecutive shops at ante 2-3, THEN money will never reach an xMult:
  replace it with a permanent scorer in the next shop where one is affordable, or skip $0 Smalls to stop the rent.
- IF you hold Death and need a flush, THEN copy a suit card onto a card LEFT of it (`use N left right`) that you would
  discard anyway (AS->AC here): no reorder needed.
- IF The Fish and your round needs two big hands, THEN the second big hand is nearly impossible (face-down draws):
  plan the boss as ONE big hand + bank, or buy the multiplier before it.
- IF The Fish has dealt face-down cards into a rank-sorted hand, THEN their positions bound their ranks (between the
  visible neighbours): play the block that lies among high ranks.
