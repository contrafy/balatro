## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: zero xMult and only flat scorers at ante 3. Even Steven + Ice Cream + Dagger gave ~2000-2800/hand vs The Wall (2x boss = 12800). Ante 1-2 shops offered no +mult/xMult joker in 6 shop looks (only eternal conditional jokers, utility, planets); the single Buffoon pack at ante 1 gave Even Steven, the ante-2 and ante-3 packs gave junk (Trading Card/Oops 6s/Business Card/Driver's License). I never owned a second real scorer and a main hand never got a planet level.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 Big shop ($7): Raised Fist holo (+2x lowest held rank, +10 mult holo) was $8, $1 short. I did not sell Trading-Card-style junk because I had none yet; consider selling a $2 joker to afford a holo +mult scorer, or not reroll at ante 2 shop 1 ($14 -> $9 -> reroll found nothing) so that $14 + $... could buy Raised Fist+The Order.
2. Ante 3: I passed Director's Cut ($10) three times. With The Wall (12800) known to be next and est round ~3100 (0.24x), Director's Cut + a $10 boss reroll was the only out; I spent money on a Buffoon pack ($4), reroll ($5) and Arcana ($4) instead and ended at $2. Rule: when SCORE CHECK < 0.3 and the next boss is The Wall/Violet Vessel, Director's Cut is the only real line: keep $20 (voucher $10 + reroll $10), do not buy packs/rerolls.
3. Skipping the ante-3 Small for the Uncommon tag only produced a $0 Ceremonial Dagger (a poor conditional uncommon). It cost one shop visit and one blind of income at EMERGENCY; the advisor's "do not skip" was probably right.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the next boss is The Wall at ante 3 and best hand x3 < 0.4 of its 12800, THEN the blind is lost unless you buy Director's Cut ($10) and hold $10 more for the reroll: do it at the earliest shop that shows the voucher (it was visible in two shops).
- IF you own Even Steven, THEN a lone even High Card (est ~500) beats an odd Two Pair; play/keep even-rank cards, and Death/Strength onto evens.
- IF Ice Cream{PERISHABLE} is offered at ante 2 with 1 scorer, THEN it is a real bridge (+100 chips made Full House 1836 vs 1776 stand-alone-less ~600/hand) but it lasts only ~3 blinds and shrinks 5 per hand; plan the permanent replacement during those 3 blinds.
- IF an Uncommon/Rare tag is on a blind, THEN the free joker appears in the NEXT shop (after the next played blind); it can be a poor joker (Ceremonial Dagger), so skipping for it is a gamble, not a scorer.
- Ceremonial Dagger: place it directly LEFT of a junk joker with sell value $3 (+6 mult), Ice Cream stays left of Dagger to be safe.
