## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: multiplier / scaling. The whole run was ONE xMult (holo Acrobat x3 on the final hand) on top of flat mult (Spare Trousers +22, Smiley Face +5/face, Raised Fist +~18, holo +10). A perfect face Full House as the final hand topped out at ~24-28k at ante 5 against Big 37500 and a Plant boss of 50000 (faces debuffed: Smiley dead). Money was the second constraint: Small pays $0, Smiley rent $3/round, and I was at $0-12 at every shop from ante 3 on, so I could never afford more than one reroll or one pack; the only xMult-class item seen after ante 2 was unfed Hologram{E} $7 (passed) and Stencil-type nothing. No second xMult / Blueprint / Brainstorm ever appeared in ~6 rerolls and 3 Buffoon packs.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 Mega Buffoon: I took Golden{E} + Smiley{RENTAL} instead of keeping a slot for an xMult. Golden Joker paid only +$4/round (net +$1 with Smiley rent) and its eternal slot cost a joker slot for the entire run; Raised Fist{E} later took another. By ante 5 I had 4 eternals (Trousers, Acrobat, Golden, Raised Fist) and could not make room for anything. Hologram{E} at $7 (ante 3) was the only xMult-shaped offer and I passed on it as "unfed"; with Standard packs (+0.25 per card) and Death/Cryptid it would have been a second growing xMult.
2. Ante 4 shop 2: 2 rerolls ($10) found only eternal junk; Jumbo Arcana ($6) or the Celestial pack would have used the money better. Rerolling at <$20 with a full-ish board whiffed again (confirms playbook).
3. Ante 4 Small: I played it rather than skipping for Investment (+$25 after the boss). It cleared easily (quads 19404), so fine, but the $25 would have bought 2-3 more shop visits' worth of rerolls at ante 5.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF Acrobat is your only xMult, THEN every round is: hands 1-2 are digs (High Card of the best odd/face card + 2-4 filler cards to draw), hand 3 is the made FH/Quads/Two Pair held back; `advise` handles this correctly. At ante 5 this caps ~24-28k per round, so you MUST add a second xMult (or Blueprint/Brainstorm) by ante 5 or you die at the Big (37.5k).
- IF a Buffoon pack shows Golden Joker{E} and you own <2 xMult, THEN skip it for a slot-neutral pick (it pays $4/round but blocks a slot; rent + Small $0 swallow it). Keep at least 2 non-eternal slots at ante 4 for xMult finds.
- IF The Arm is the boss, THEN `est` already applies the level loss; hold the best hand for the final x3 hand (6840 vs 5271 needed worked).
- IF Raised Fist is owned, THEN play 5-card hands so the held 3 are your face cards (lowest held J=10 -> +20 mult); est includes it.
- IF a dig hand is HIGH CARD, THEN the scoring card is the HIGHEST rank among the played cards, not the one you listed first (I lost 435 by including a J as a "filler" next to a 9).
- IF Hermit is in the shop with $12+, THEN buyuse it (+$6 net at $12 -> $18); did that at ante 4.
