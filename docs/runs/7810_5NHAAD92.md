## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no multiplier and no +mult, ever. Two flat chips jokers (Banner +60, Odd Todd +31/odd card) on L1 hands with base mult 2-4 give ~200-600 per hand; the ante-2 Big needed 1500 (500 per hand). Shop offers were thin ($5 income, $0 from Small): ante-1 shop offered only Banner{E} / Buffoon pack / Celestial; ante-2 shop offered Driver's License (dead), Justice, Egg{E,R}, Odd Todd.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 first shop ($5): bought eternal Banner instead of the $4 Buffoon pack. Banner is worth ~+60 chips only if I never discard, which fights the discards I need to dig; with no +mult it barely helps and it is a permanent slot. A Buffoon pack could have given a +mult joker. Rule below.
2. Ante 2 Big: with 1500 needed and best hand ~600 I discarded twice chasing a flush; both missed. The real mistake was earlier (no scaling), but the flush chase also threw away the Banner chips (Banner 0 after discards: pair est 60).
3. Ante 1 Small: I skipped nothing; the Investment tag ($ later) would have given +$ at the boss but it was not taken either way. Neutral.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF it is ante 1 and the only joker you can afford is a chips-only joker (Banner, Odd Todd, Scholar-less) and a Buffoon pack costs $4 and fits in your money, THEN take the Buffoon pack: you need +mult, flat chips on mult 2-4 give ~+100 per hand.
- IF Banner is owned, THEN do not discard unless forced: each discard costs 30 chips on every later hand AND the discarded hands lose the bonus; est shows 164 -> 60 for a Pair after two discards.
- IF an ante-2 blind needs more than 4x your current best hand and you have no mult joker, THEN a reroll is worth $5 only if you keep $4 for a joker; I found Odd Todd on the first reroll and had $2 left, i.e. no money for a Celestial.
- IF Driver's License is the only xMult on offer, THEN ignore it: it needs 16 enhanced cards (verified in description).

Advisor/CLI defects:
- `shopadvise` at ante 1 ($5) ranked Banner{ETERNAL} 110 "NEEDED NOW" and buy; its SIM ratio showed buy 0.71 vs baseline 0.72 (no gain), i.e. the rank contradicts its own SIM. It should have preferred the Buffoon pack when the SIM shows no improvement.
- `advise` solver at the Manacle first hand p 0.48 for a Pair with fillers (est 164): the real outcome needed a miracle flush; p values look inflated at n=100 with 7-card hands.
- `advise` described `High Card 9C est 74 + fillers 4S 4H` as a High Card though 4S+4H with 9C would be Pair; the labelled hand type did not match the played cards.
- `hands` est for Banner counts discards remaining at the time of the est, which is correct, but the PACE line does not warn that discarding lowers later Banner hands.
Bridge issues: none.
