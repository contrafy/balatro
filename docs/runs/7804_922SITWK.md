## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING (flat, levels). The engine was Card Sharp x3 on a repeated Pair, with Half Joker (+20 at <=3
cards), Mystic Summit (+15 at 0 discards), Misprint and a negative Hanging Chad. A 2-card Pair L2 x3 made 7.4-12k, i.e. two
"real" hands per round after a throwaway registration hand. Pair stayed L2 all run: money hovered $0-8 so there was never a
spare $3 for Mercury, and the two Celestial packs landed on Mercury (good) and Venus (never played).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 Big-blind shop ($6 after Card Sharp): bought a Jumbo Celestial ($6 -> $0) and got Venus. The shop had Mercury $3
   directly (SIM +0.11). Pair L3 (+15 chips +1 mult) on the Goad's KK/QQ x3 hands = ~+2.3k each: the boss is cleared by
   ~4k. Take the guaranteed main-hand planet over a pack that might not hold it.
2. Pack pick: I took Venus over the SIM's Jupiter; both were wasted. Neither choice mattered; the pack buy was the error.
3. Ante 4 Small-blind shop ($7): Juggler $4 (+1 hand size) would have improved pair finding on every Card Sharp round
   (SIM +0.02 understates it for a pair-hunting engine). Marginal.

What worked (keep doing):
- Skipping the Big Blind to keep a perishable scorer alive for the boss: ante 3 skip kept Clever (+80 chips) for The Wall
  (cleared 24010/12800); ante 4 skip kept Half Joker (+20 mult, ~45% of the Pair x3 score) for The Goad (missed by 2%;
  without Half the boss was ~5k per hand, hopeless). Skipping the Big also saves one rent and one perish tick.
- Card Sharp + Mystic Summit sequencing: hand 1 = cheapest version of the type (register, keep discards), discards dig
  for the next pairs and the last one arms Summit, hands 2-3 are x3 with +15.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF a perishable scorer has exactly 1 round left AND the next blind is the Big AND the boss needs it, THEN skip the Big:
  the perish tick happens at round end, so the scorer stays alive for the boss (worked twice in this run).
- IF you own Card Sharp, THEN register the round's type with the lowest pair you hold (3-card play if Half Joker), keep
  both discards for digging, and burn the last discard before the first x3 hand when Mystic Summit is owned.
- IF your main-hand planet is in the shop for $3, THEN buy it before any Celestial pack; a $6 Jumbo Celestial without
  that planet is a ~40% gamble.
- IF Misprint is a key mult source on a forced final hand, THEN the clear probability is P(roll >= threshold): compute it
  before the round and buy the level/joker that removes the dependence (lost here on a ~54% roll).
