## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: luck on a jokerless Small Blind (300 need, Pair ~60, Two Pair ~100-120, Flush ~320 est). Starting deal had only Pair TT; two discards found no second pair/trips/flush/straight, then two dig plays (solver 0.94 / 0.76) missed a 4/5 spade flush twice (p 0.69 then 0.74 each, joint ~0.2 to hit at least once... the miss chance was ~0.26 x 0.31 on paper). Overall solver p at the start was 0.96; the loss is the ~1-in-20 tail, but part of it is structural: at 0 jokers a Small Blind needs a Flush/Two Pair-plus hand and there is no slack.

Decisions I would change:
1. Hand 1 discard: I kept Pair TT (rule: never break a made pair at solver margin < 0.10) while the solver had 0.96 for breaking it (keep KC TS TC 4C flush draw) vs 0.93. With 300 needed and Pair worth only 60, a pair is NOT a floor on a jokerless Small: 3 pairs = 180 < 300. The pair-floor rule should not apply when jokerless Pair est x 3 < need.
2. Discard 2: keeping TT again (draw 4) instead of the solver's 'keep AS TS 2S' (0.91 vs 0.91 equal) was neutral.
3. The 3-hand/2-discard budget: both discards and the 3rd hand burned digging; had I played Pair TT as hand 1 (60) I would still need 240 in 2 hands: not feasible either. The real fix is none available at $4 and no joker: it is a coin-flip blind at 0 jokers.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you have 0 jokers at ante 1 Small (need 300) THEN the made Pair (est ~60) is worth nothing as a floor: choose the line with the highest P(Flush/Two Pair/Trips+) from the solver, even if it breaks the pair (the 'keep the made pair at margin < 0.10' rule is for jokered hands).
- IF the first two discards of a jokerless Small produce no 2nd pair/flush, THEN the blind is ~lost; consider that a skip of the Small (Boss Tag here, pays nothing) would not have helped either (Big 450 needs the same luck): the real mitigation is only a Buffoon/Top-up skip tag at ante 1 when offered.
- IF digging for a 4/5 flush with the last 2 hands, THEN expect ~0.74 x 0.26 chain: the first dig (draw 4 for 1 needed spade, p=0.69) failed, which cost the whole blind; there was no better line, only the earlier discard choice could change it.
