## Post-mortem

Best result: stake 1, ante 4, Big Blind, lost needing 7500 with 5645 scored (75%).
Final jokers: Wily Joker, Raised Fist, Fibonacci, Jolly Joker, Shoot the Moon, To Do List (6/6, none xMult).
Money curve: essentially flat at $0-9 every shop all run (ante1 end $9->$0, ante2 end $9->$4->$1, ante3 end $16->$0, ante4 end $4->$4->$0 at loss). Never banked toward interest; every dollar was spent same-shop on packs/jokers/vouchers.
Binding constraint: scaling. Pure flat +mult/+chips stacking (no xMult anywhere by ante 4) plus a main hand that only reached Full House L2 could not keep pace once Big Blind hit 7500 and the actual card draw in the final round produced only Trips->Straight->bare Pair instead of another Full House. The Glass Ace shattering on its one big scoring opportunity (est 3740 -> actual 2420) was the proximate trigger, but the deeper issue was zero xMult and a Full House stuck at L2 for 3 antes straight -- exactly the failure mode the playbook warns about ("zero xMult at ante 5 loses").
Decision I would change: at the ante-3 shop with $16 (after Temperance's payout), I spent it on Judgement (random joker -> mediocre To Do List) and the Seed Money voucher instead of continuing to level Full House (no Earth was offered, but should have rerolled to look) or holding cash toward an xMult purchase. Filling the 6th joker slot via a random-effect tarot (Judgement) instead of a deliberate buy locked in a weak joker with no way to swap it out later without losing a sell-value slot.
Bridge/CLI issues: none. All commands behaved as documented; no ACTION FAILED or connection issues this run. One notable CLI behavior worth flagging: the `play` command itself logs "(est X vs actual Y: logged for calibration)" when the two diverge significantly (seen once, ~35% under), which was useful for catching the Glass-card-shatter variance in real time.
Playbook suggestions:
1. `advise`/`hands` est for hands containing Glass cards should either apply the expected-value discount (0.75x the Glass bonus, since 1/4 shatter) or flag "(includes N Glass card(s), variance possible)" so the agent doesn't treat a close-to-need est as safely-clearing.
2. Section 5 should say more explicitly: if all 6 joker slots are full, do not spend a tarot/pack pick on a random-joker generator (Judgement, Buffoon-pack RNG) purely to fill the last slot -- prefer a targeted buy or hold the slot open for a scaling joker instead of locking in a random weak one.
3. Consider adding a rule: if by ante 3 end you have zero xMult AND main hand is still <= L2, treat the situation as urgent (reroll aggressively even below the $10 floor) rather than continuing to buy incremental flat sources -- this run had $16 at ante 3 shop and could have rerolled for an xMult instead of Judgement+voucher.
