## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: MONEY, then SCALING. I never held more than $13, and that $13 (ante 2, first shop) went to a $5
reroll, a $1 rental and a $6 Jumbo Celestial that held no planet for a hand I play (took Venus, played Trips once all
run). From then on every shop had $1-4, two rentals ate $9 of rent, and I reached ante 3 with Joker (+4) and Supernova
(spread over 6 hand types, +1..+5) as the only scorers: ~800-1700 per hand against 2133 per hand at The Arm. Neither
an xMult nor a +mult joker beyond the $1 Joker was ever affordable.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 shop ($13): after the reroll found Joker{RENTAL} $1 I should have stopped at $7 (interest $1) instead of the $6
   Jumbo Celestial. The next shop had Mad Joker{PERISH} $4 (+10 mult on Two Pair, my most-played pair hand) that I
   could not afford at $2. A Jumbo Celestial with no main hand is a $6 gamble at ~1/3 for a useful planet.
2. Ante 1: Hanging Chad rental bought at $6 left me no ante-2 budget margin. Selling it mid-round before the boss's
   clearing hand avoided the rent, which worked; but the $1 + the chance of a better buy was wasted.
3. Hand-type spread: Supernova grows per type, and I played Flush 6x, Two Pair 4x, Trips/FH/Straight/High/SF once each.
   With Supernova eternal, committing to one type (Flush, since I had Smeared/Jupiter by ante 3) would have added ~+5 mult.
4. Ante 3 double skip: gave Smeared + 2 Worlds + Steel for free but the build still could not reach 2133/hand; the
   Jupiter level was then destroyed by The Arm. Playing the Big (4800, ~25%) instead was not clearly better. The real
   fix was earlier (point 1).

## Reusable rules (if-then)
- IF you hold a rental and your best +mult joker costs $4-5 in the next shops, THEN do not spend below $5 on packs in
  this shop; a rental's -$3 per round turns a $2 balance into an unaffordable shop.
- IF the post-blind shop will have under $3 (rent included) AND the Small/Big tag gives something now (Charm = Mega
  Arcana, Orbital = 3 levels), THEN skip the blind: the shop visit is worth $0, the skip also skips $3 rent per rental.
- IF you own a rental and the current round is already won by the next hand, THEN sell the rental before that hand
  only if you would sell it next shop anyway (rent is charged at round end).
- IF Orbital/levels make High Card L3+, THEN a single Ace/King is a 400-500 point last hand at ante 2: check `sel` on
  the top single card before following a chase discard.
- IF The Arm is the boss, THEN do not buy a planet for the hand you will play there in that ante's shops: the level is
  lost on the first play.
- IF a Mega Arcana offers Judgement with free joker slots, THEN take it: Judgement jokers carry no stake stickers.
