## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. Money (Small pays $0 and Raised Fist rent -$3/round kept me at $0-5 all run, so I never afforded a Buffoon pack at ante 2-4) and no xMult source ever appeared. Final build was Raised Fist{R} + Clever + perishable Arrowhead, Two Pair L4, ~4-5k per hand vs 18k boss.

What worked: Raised Fist is a huge early scorer on Black Deck: mult is +2x the LOWEST card HELD, so play 5 cards (low junk as fillers) and keep K/Q/J/A held; est went 504 (High Card) at ante 1 and one-shot ante 2 (2160, 2646). Madness as sole joker is a free x1.5 for the ante-1 Big (nothing to destroy). Sell Madness before the next Small/Big select once you own another joker. Orbital/Boss tags paid immediately.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2-3 I held Raised Fist{R} while broke; every round-end cost $3, so shops had $2-9 and offered Mr. Bones / Scary Face / Buffoon packs I could not afford. Consider selling a rental once it no longer one-shots and the wallet is < $8 (but it was my only +mult).
2. Ante 4 Big: I skipped 13500 (round est ~10-13k, actual runs beat est by 25-30%) to take Meteor; that gave only Venus/Mercury (no main-hand planet) and skipped a shop. Probably neutral, the run was already short ~1.6x of the Psychic.
3. Ante 3 shop: passed Mr. Bones/Justice for cash; $9 became Arrowhead (perishable, 3 rounds). A permanent scorer would have mattered at ante 4.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Raised Fist, THEN play 5 cards every hand (fillers = your lowest cards) and keep your highest held; est includes it. A High Card AH with 4 low fillers + K/J/T held scored 504 at ante 1.
- IF Madness is your ONLY joker, THEN it is a free +0.5 xMult per Small/Big select; sell it the shop BEFORE you add another joker and then select a Small/Big (it destroys a random non-eternal joker, not at bosses).
- IF a Small Blind tag is Orbital naming your main hand type (here Two Pair), THEN skip it: +3 levels now (and no rent/perish tick).
- IF The House is the boss, THEN discard 5 face-down cards first; the draws come face-up, then discard the other face-downs. Cleared 6400 with FH 3232 + 2P 2464 + Trips 1870.
- IF The Psychic (5 cards) is the boss and typical 2P is ~5k, THEN the boss needs ~3.5 such hands: unwinnable below 18000/3 per hand; hunt scale at ante 3.
