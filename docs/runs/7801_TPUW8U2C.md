## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING, caused by SLOTS. Three eternals (Riff-raff $6 at ante 1, Swashbuckler $4 at ante 3, Egg $4
at ante 3) left 3 flexible slots for the whole run, and Riff-raff was pure dead weight from ante 3 on (it only spawns
into free slots, and a Gold build cannot afford free slots). The only xMult seen in 14 shop views + 8 rerolls at ante 4-6
was Seeing Double (conditional, ~50% on pairs). Chips came from bridges (Stuntman 5 rounds, Ice Cream decaying) and mult
from Swashbuckler+Egg (+3/round) copied by a perishable Brainstorm; every bridge died exactly when the curve tripled
(ante 5 -> 6: 50k -> 120k). Money was fine ($67 peak at ante 4, two Hermits + two Temperances = +$71) but there was
nothing to buy. Peak hand: 34028 (ante 4), 28.4k ceiling at ante 6 vs 40k/hand needed.

Decisions I would change:
1. Egg{ETERNAL} at ante 3 (sold Juggler for it). Egg+Swashbuckler was +3 mult/round (+6 with Brainstorm), ~+30 by ante 6,
   but it cost a permanent slot; a 3rd eternal with Riff-raff already dead meant any later xMult had to replace a scorer.
   Rule: never own more than 2 eternals before an xMult is in place.
2. Riff-raff{ETERNAL} at ante 1: it carried antes 1-2 (Fist, Mad, Juggler, Golden, Chad for free, sticker-free) but is a
   dead eternal slot from the moment slots are full. On Black Deck with 6 slots it is worth it ONLY if you keep selling
   junk to leave 1-2 slots open until ante 3; after that it is a -1 slot joker. Sticker-free commons are the only upside.
3. Ante 4 Big shop ($67): 3 rerolls + 2 Mega Celestials. The levels (Pair L7, 2P L4) were fine, but $16 on Celestials
   while owning 0 xMult was the wrong priority; 2-3 more rerolls had a better chance at the x2-x3 the run never found.
4. Ante 6 double skip was correct given the ceiling (85k < 90k Big), but it exposed the real error: entering ante 6 with a
   build whose best hand was 28k and no Throwback-style growth. The playbook's "2 xMults by ante 5" was never close.
5. Mega Celestial pick indices shift after each pick in a choose-2 pack: I picked Neptune (index 4) when Uranus had moved
   to 3. Re-read the pack line after every pick.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF a Buffoon/shop joker is ETERNAL and you already own 2 eternals, THEN pass unless it is an xMult: the 6th slot is the
  one that must stay free for the x2-x3 that appears in ~8% of shop views.
- IF you own Riff-raff, THEN keep at least one slot free at every blind select until ante 3 (sell $1-3 junk right before
  selecting); once slots fill with real scorers, treat Riff-raff as a dead slot and never buy it eternal after ante 1.
- IF you own Swashbuckler, THEN Egg (+3 sell value/round) and Gift Card are +mult growth engines and Brainstorm/Blueprint
  on Swashbuckler doubles it; order: Swashbuckler leftmost for Brainstorm, copy before the xMults. Verified: Brainstorm as
  a Swashbuckler copy gave +20..+33 mult.
- IF a joker copies the leftmost joker (Brainstorm), THEN copy a +mult joker, not a conditional xMult that would then sit
  leftmost and multiply only the base mult (SD-copy 102 vs Swashbuckler-copy 128 total mult here).
- IF the boss is The Flint and your mult comes from jokers, THEN chips are the bottleneck: a +100 chips Ice Cream beat a
  +16 mult Raised Fist (halved base 70 chips -> 160), and a held Hierophant (+30 chips x2 on the pair you play) is a
  one-hand +37%; buy it from the shop the round before.
- IF Seeing Double is your xMult, THEN a Wild card in the scoring hand always satisfies it (wild fills the missing suit),
  including a one-suit Flush (verified 15950 with wild QC in a spade flush); use The Lovers on a high card.
- IF a perishable scorer has N rounds left and the boss is N+1 rounds away, THEN skip the Small Blind: a skip is not a
  round end, so neither rent nor perish ticks (Stuntman survived The Eye this way; Brainstorm survived to the Tooth).
- IF your 3-hand ceiling (best hand x3 with every conditional firing) is below the Big Blind, THEN skip it for its tag and
  fight the boss with the intact build: a certain loss at the Big is worse than a lottery at the boss.
- IF the game is mathematically lost at a boss, THEN free a slot before selecting it when you own Riff-raff: a free
  common is the only remaining nonzero line (Cavendish x3 would have cleared 120k; got Ride the Bus).
- IF a $0 rental joker is in the shop (Rare/Uncommon tag), THEN buy and immediately sell it: +$1, rent only ticks at
  round end (Invisible Joker here); the extra $1 crossed an interest threshold.
