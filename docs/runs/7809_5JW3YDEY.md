# Run 5JW3YDEY (port 7809, claude-opus-5-5) - Black Deck Gold Stake - LOSS at ante 1 blind The Psychic (171/600)

Ante 1: boss The Psychic (every play exactly 5 cards). Small tag Skip, Big tag Juggle (+3 hand size next round).
- Small 300: 2 discards to a heart flush QJT96 (320) after a Pair 8 dig (52). $5.
- Shop 1 ($5): Half Joker {ETERNAL} $5 (advisor 133 NEEDED NOW) vs Buffoon Pack $4 / Hermit / Mega Arcana. Bought Half Joker -> $0.
- Big 450: discard 4, Pair AA + Half Joker = 704 in one hand. $6.
- Shop 2 ($6): Wily Joker $4 (+100 chips on trips/FH), the only joker. Advisor now printed BOSS PREP: Psychic kills Half Joker. Bought Wily -> $2.
- Psychic 600: hand AKT98 3H2H 2C. Disc A K 9 (kept 22 + 4 hearts) -> drew KS 7S 4D; solver disc 5 keeping TH 2H 2C
  (p 0.62) -> 3322 two pair. Solver dig: Two Pair 3322 + 8D (60), keeping TH 6D 5D -> drew 55; solver dig High KH
  (15) keeping 55 4H for FH/quads -> final draw Q 99 55 44 3, best Two Pair 96. Lost 171/600.

## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: BOSS (The Psychic at ante 1) plus no joker that works on 5-card plays. My only real scorer was
Half Joker, which never fires under The Psychic. Wily (+100 chips) only fires on trips/FH/quads. Without a joker,
5-card hands score 60-320 (Two Pair ~60-100, Flush ~300, FH L1 ~400 or ~600 with Wily). 600 needed two big hands or
one FH+Wily, and three draws with 2 discards did not get there.

Decisions I would change:
1. Shop 1: the Buffoon Pack ($4) over Half Joker {ETERNAL} ($5). The boss was already known to be The Psychic. A pack
   joker like Joker/Jolly/Sly/Greedy works on the Big Blind AND on the 5-card boss hands. Half Joker was the strongest
   Big Blind tool (704 on one pair), but it scores 0 on the boss, which is the blind that ends runs. It is also eternal.
2. Big Blind tag was Juggle (+3 hand size for the next round, i.e. the Psychic). Skipping the Big for 11-card hands
   at the Psychic would have made flushes/full houses far more likely. The cost: the $4-5 Big reward and the shop
   (Wily). With Half Joker already owned this is close. Without a 5-card scorer, Juggle into the Psychic was probably the better line.
3. Psychic first discard: I kept 22 + four hearts (discarded 3). That was fine, but on the next decision I left the
   flush draw for the solver's set-draw line. A 4-flush with 1 discard left (~50% to hit ~280) plus a pair was
   at least as good as a 5-card redraw.

## Reusable rules (if-then)
- IF the ante-1 boss is The Psychic, THEN in the first two shops value jokers that fire on 5-card hands (Joker,
  Jolly/Sly on pairs inside Two Pair/FH, Greedy/Lusty suit jokers, Banner, Mystic Summit). Rank Half Joker as Big
  Blind-only: buy it only if a 5-card scorer is also affordable before the boss.
- IF the ante-1 Big Blind tag is Juggle (+3 hand size) and the boss is The Psychic/The Needle/The Water, AND you
  already beat the Small Blind, AND you own no 5-card scorer, THEN consider skipping the Big Blind. 11 cards make
  Flush/Full House likely for 5-card plays.
- IF you own Wily/Zany (trips jokers) at a 5-card boss, THEN Full House is the target hand: FH contains trips, so
  FH L1 + Wily = (40+chips+100) x4 ~ 600 clears the ante-1 boss alone.

## Advisor/CLI defects
- shopadvise shop 1 ranked Half Joker {ETERNAL} 133 "NEEDED NOW ... a core joker you would keep anyway". It printed
  "Upcoming boss this ante: The Psychic" but had no BOSS PREP warning. The warning ("Psychic counters this build:
  Half Joker never fires") only appeared in shop 2, after the joker was bought. The boss counter check should run
  BEFORE recommending a joker that the known upcoming boss disables.
- The Psychic solver p_clear looked overconfident: p 0.71-0.75 at the start of the round with 0 multiplier jokers
  (needs 600 from 5-card L1 hands), and still p 0.53 with 540 short, 2 hands, 0 discards. It printed p 0.43 for the
  final dig, which needed FH/quads on one 5-card draw. At ante 1, n=100-400 samples with "calibrated" labels
  should not be read as reliable for Psychic rounds.
- Bridge: no rejects, 19 steps. Shell functions do not persist between bash calls (env exports do); I used the full b.sh path.
