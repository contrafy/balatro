## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING, and specifically that every scorer I owned was a bridge. The permanent build at death was
Misprint{E} (+0..23) and The Order (x3 on Straight, from Wraith) with Pair L2 / Straight L3. Everything else was perishable or
decaying (Chad, foil Blueprint rental, Blue Joker, Popcorn, Golden Joker) and the money never exceeded $15, so no shop could
buy a permanent xMult: 9 shop views + 3 rerolls showed one xMult of any kind (The Idol{PERISH,RENTAL}). Ante 2-4 were cleared
mostly by Straight x3 one-shots (15120, 23436, 23001) and the double Misprint; at ante 5 the Straight hand alone was ~17k vs
25000 and a pair was ~2.5k, so a round needed a Straight plus a top Misprint roll.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2: the foil Blueprint rental ($1 + $15 rent over 5 rounds) copied Misprint and carried antes 2-4, but rent kept the
   wallet at $0-4 through 5 shops. The alternative (Jumbo Celestial / holding $7) was worse for survival, so the buy stands;
   the money problem was structural: rent, the $0 Small Blind, and three skipped Smalls meant no shop after ante 1 ever held
   $8+, and the one-shot margins (23436/4800, 9240/6400) cannot be converted into anything (unused hands pay $1).
2. Ante 3-4: I skipped three Small Blinds (Ethereal, Top-up, Meteor). Each skip was individually right (free Rare joker, 2 free
   commons, 2 free levels, and no perish/rent tick), but the three lost shop visits were three lost xMult views at the ante
   where an xMult must be bought (GOLD_ANALYSIS: 1 by end of ante 2, 2 by ante 5). With $1-3 in pocket those shops could not
   have bought one anyway, which is the actual lesson: a $0-3 wallet at ante 3-4 is already a lost run.
3. Ante 4 Buffoon pack: Golden Joker (+$4 x 5 rounds) over keeping Wily. Fine, but $16 arrived too late (ante 5) to matter.
4. Ante 5 shop ($15): 2 rerolls + Arcana for $0. Correct as a lottery; the alternative (Sly $3 + keep $12 for the Big shop)
   had the same ~0 survival.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your only xMult is hand-gated (The Order x3 on Straight) THEN treat every round as "find the straight with the 2 discards,
  keep an open-ended 4-straight (8 outs) over a gutshot (4 outs), and never keep a pair that blocks the draw": here a kept
  6-4-3-2 (one out, 4 draws, 33%) found the Eye's one-shot; the solver kept 3-card draws (14-16%) instead.
- IF a Mega Celestial is offered and you own a hand-gated xMult THEN level THAT hand first (Saturn: Straight L2 -> L3 took the
  Eye one-shot from 15.7k est to 23k actual) - the SIM ranked it +0.21 vs +0.03 for the main pair planet.
- IF the Mark leaves 3 face-down cards and you hold a pair THEN use the face-downs as the pair's fillers: 3 unknown J/Q/K share
  a rank 71% of the time (free Two Pair upgrade) and 9/5/4 fillers add nothing. (It missed here, but the EV is right.)
- IF Misprint is your mult engine THEN the est (Misprint at 6) is ~1.5x low on average and a single hand varies 3x: never plan a
  blind on one Misprint hand; bank a made pair first so the big hand only needs an average roll. Actuals this run: 1728/720,
  528/1144, 352/1232, 15120/6720, 23436/10416, 3843/5673, 23001/15708.
- IF you copy Misprint with Blueprint/Brainstorm THEN two independent rolls halve the variance; order Blueprint LEFT of
  Misprint (it copies the joker to its right). Verified: 9240 on a Pair KK at ante 3.
- IF a perishable joker has exactly 1 round left and a skip tag is on offer THEN skipping moves its last round to the harder
  blind (Blueprint's last round went from the 9000 Small to the 13500 Big); rent and perish tick only at round ends.
- IF you hold $0-4 entering ante 3 with 0 permanent xMult THEN the run is already lost at ante 5 with ~95%: from ante 2 on,
  refuse rentals that are not xMults and refuse any buy that leaves under $4 unless it is the difference between clearing the
  next blind or not (my Blueprint rental was that difference; the Popcorn/Blue Joker bridges were too).
- IF a Buffoon pack offers only an economy joker (Golden) vs a conditional chips joker (Wily) at ante 4 THEN take the money
  only if it arrives before the ante-5 shop where you need $8+; otherwise it is too late.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- SOLVER p is "calibrated" on this run's actual/est ratio, which was inflated by TWO Misprints (actuals 1.5-2.3x est); after
  Blueprint perished it printed p 0.98 for the Eye and p 0.67 for the ante-5 Small (actual outcome ~10%). Calibration should
  drop or re-fit when the joker set changes (perish/sell), and Misprint variance should be simulated, not scaled.
- advise under The Eye / ante-5 Small listed only 3-card straight draws (p 0.14-0.16) and never the 1-out 4-card draw
  (keep 6 4 3 2, 33-36%); it also ranked "keep KK pair" lines when a pair (3.5k) could not contribute to 18000.
- `hands` Misprint range note is inverted/garbled when Blueprint copies Misprint: "roll 0 -> 5863, roll 23 -> ~1478"
  (ante 3 Mouth) and "roll 0 -> 4719, roll 23 -> ~2527" (ante 4 Big).
- shopadvise kept calling the main hand "Flush" (4 plays at ante 1) through ante 4 while Straight (with The Order) and Pair
  were the played hands; Mercury/Saturn picks were flagged "NOT your main hand Flush; wasted".
- shopadvise at ante 2 ranked Jumbo Celestial $6 as BUY with $7 and a rental owned (would go to -$2 at round end); the rent
  due at round end should be part of the floor check.
- Blind-select `s` at ante 3 said "do not skip: typical hand under half this ante's boss" for a Top-up Tag with 2 free slots;
  a tag that gives jokers NOW raises the typical hand before the Big and should be evaluated after adding them.
- The Rare Tag on the Big Blind is only paid by SKIPPING the Big (I misread it as a reward for beating it); `s` could say
  "tag pays only if skipped" on Big/Boss lines.
