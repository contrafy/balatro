## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING, caused by MONEY. I was at $0-5 at every shop from ante 1 to 4. The ante-1 Gros Michel rental ($3/round), $0 Small Blinds and 3-4 dollar purchases left no interest and nothing to buy a $6-8 xMult with. The only xMult was Photograph. It applies at card scoring, before the +mult jokers, so it doubled only base mult 3 (Two Pair L2 3 -> 6, then +10 Mad +16 Popcorn). It added ~20%, not x2. Two Pair stayed at L2 the whole run: one planet was bought (ante 1), and no Celestial pack was ever affordable.

Decisions I would change:
- Ante 1 shop 2: Uranus $3 at $4 money, while paying $3/round rent. Keeping $4 would not have bought a joker either, so this is roughly neutral. The real error was having no money plan around the rental.
- Ante 2-3: I skipped all four Small/Big blinds for tags. That gave 3 jokers (DNA, Photograph via shop pack, Mr. Bones), and the Mega Buffoons were mostly junk. It also cost 4 rounds of income and 2 shop visits. Each Big Blind pays $4+hands even after rent. With 2 more shops I would have seen ~4 more joker offers. I think skipping Big for Buffoon at ante 3 was wrong; skipping Small (pays $0) was right.
- Ante 4: Popcorn rental over Campfire was right for the Big (it cleared). Nothing in reach would have beaten The Head.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF Photograph is your only xMult and your mult comes from +mult jokers, THEN do not count it as x2. It doubles only the hand's base mult (L2 Two Pair: 3 -> 6), so +16 extra mult beats it. Value it as a real x2 only with a high-level main hand or mult-enhanced face cards.
- IF you hold a rental +mult joker at ante 1-2 with <$5, THEN skip the Small Blind (pays $0, saves $3 rent), but PLAY the Big Blind (pays $4 + unused hands): shops are the only joker source and you need the cash.
- IF a Mega/Jumbo Buffoon offers Mr. Bones (non-rental) while you have a free slot and B < this ante's per-hand need, THEN take it: it saved the ante-4 Small at 4944+520/9000 here.
- IF a Buffoon pick has a joker with no stickers that you will not use (Ceremonial Dagger), THEN take it into a free slot and sell it in the next shop for $3. Watch out for Dagger's blind-start effect: it destroys the joker to its right.
