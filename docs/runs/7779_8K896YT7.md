## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. By ante-1 boss we had exactly one joker (Blackboard) whose condition (all held cards black) was never met even once, and one unusable tarot (Justice, bought instead of a hand level or Mr. Bones). With zero effective scoring jokers, no hand level, and The Pillar debuffing most of the previously-played rank ladder, there was no path to 600 with only base-level hands off whatever non-debuffed cards remained.

Decision I would change: in Shop 2 ($8), instead of Justice tarot ($3, which sat unusable all run), buy Mr. Bones ($5, survive-at-25%-of-blind) or spend on the Celestial-tier level for whichever hand type was actually landing (Straight was our best performer at 200-260 twice). Either would have materially changed the boss outcome — Mr. Bones alone would have prevented the loss outright at 324/600 (well above 25% of 600=150), and a Straight level-up would likely have cleared 600 across the two Straight-capable hands we drew.

Also: Blackboard was a weak pick from the ante-1 Buffoon Pack in hindsight — it never fired once across 4 rounds of play. Per playbook 7a it should never be a sole/primary xMult bet, and this run confirms it: no discard strategy realistically holds an all-black 8-card hand while also drawing scoring pairs/straights.

Bridge issues (exact text): none related to connectivity or retries - every action returned `ok:` on first try, no `ACTION FAILED` or connection errors encountered this run.

`PAIR 9: idx 4 5 | L1 10x2 + 18 card chips => est 20` and `ADVICE: PLAY -> b play PAIR 4 5 8 7 6 (est 20, still 316 short; 0 hands left after this)`.

The `play`-time est (60) matched the real outcome; only the `advise`-time est (20) was wrong here, even though both claimed to price in the same debuffs.

Add a rule to section 5 (ante 1-2): when no scoring joker AND no hand-level pack is affordable, prefer Mr. Bones (survive-at-25%-of-blind) over a card-targeting tarot if the tarot cannot be used before the next boss fight (no SELECT_CARD/USE_CONSUMABLE ever became legal outside pack-opening in this run) - a targeted tarot bought outside PACK_OPENING may be dead money.
3. Investigate/flag the `advise` vs `play` est mismatch for hands where ALL scoring cards are debuffed (pair of two identically-debuffed cards): `advise` appears to zero base chips too aggressively in this specific case, while the actual play-time calc (and real game score) do not zero them.
