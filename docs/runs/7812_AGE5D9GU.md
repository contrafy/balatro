Never saw a second xMult in ~8 shop views at ante 3-5 (rerolls 3 total, 2 Buffoon/Celestial packs); Trio was bought at ante 1 as the right call and carried everything through ante 4 (4 one-shots by Trips).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 5: skipping Small AND Big without Throwback just moved all risk onto the 50000 boss with $46 that could not be spent before it. With the Big played (37.5k needs ~Trips+pair, ~50%) I would have had a shop with $35+ (more Celestial/reroll chances for an xMult) and the Handy/Economy tag value is only usable AFTER the boss.
