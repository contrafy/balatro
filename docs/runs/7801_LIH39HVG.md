## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. The build never found a joker xMult in 12 shop views + 7 rerolls (Blackboard was the only one, a 5-round perishable at ante 2). Fibonacci + Odd Todd + Shoot the Moon put ALL the mult in the card phase, so the only multipliers that worked were card-phase ones: Hanging Chad retriggering a Glass card first (x8 on the base mult) and a held Steel card (x1.5 after the card phase). That reached 53k on one hand at ante 5 but ante 6 needs 60k/90k/240k (The Wall) every round; one-hand builds without a joker-row xMult cap around 50k. Money was secondary: the ante-4/5 rerolls ($29 total) found Justice, Chad and Bones but no xMult, and two rentals (Chad + Bones, -$6/round with $0 Smalls) made the ante-6 shops dead ($0).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 Coupon shop: I took Saturn/Mercury/Jupiter for free but had no second shop that ante. Fine. The mistake was ante 3's first shop: one reroll with $26 found nothing and I kept $21 instead of buying Paint Brush ($10, +1 hand size); on a 5-card-hand build (Fib/Odd Todd fire per scoring card) hand size 9 would have raised straight/flush frequency for the whole run.
2. Ante 4 first shop: 2 rerolls ($11) in EMERGENCY. Right call (Justice came and won the Hook), but Droll ($4, +10 on Flush only) was a waste; the $6 should have waited for the Chad/Bones shop.
3. Ante 5: buying Mr. Bones was correct (it allowed skipping the Big and arriving at the Wall), but I never got the value: the Wall at 240k needs 60k for Bones and I had 40.8k. Skipping the ante-6 Small/Big was still right (60k and 90k were ~30% each; the Meteor+Double tag gave 4 free planet levels).
4. The Wall final hand: `move hand` the Steel K to the RIGHT of the held Queens. Held-card effects resolve left to right in hand order: KH steel at position 1 multiplied only the played-card mult (25 -> 37.5) and the three Queens' +39 came after it (13846 instead of 17376). Would not have saved the run, but it is a general rule.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your mult comes from per-card jokers (Fibonacci, Odd Todd, Smiley, Scary, Shoot the Moon, Raised Fist) and no joker-row xMult exists, THEN a Glass card is an xMult: `move hand` it FIRST under Hanging Chad (x2 x2 x2 on the base mult: Straight L3 10 -> 80) or LAST without Chad (x2 on base + all card +mult accumulated before it). Verified: 28080 (Glass last, x Blackboard) at the Hook, 53280 (Glass first + Chad + Steel) at the Mark.
- IF you hold a Steel card AND held-card +mult jokers (Shoot the Moon Queens, Raised Fist), THEN `move hand` the Steel card to the RIGHT of those held cards: held effects resolve left to right, Steel x1.5 only multiplies what came before it (KH steel left of QQQ: 13846; right of them it would be 17376).
- IF Hanging Chad is owned, THEN every hand's first scoring card must be your best per-card trigger: `move hand` a Fib+odd card (A/3/5) or a Glass card to position 1 before playing (5D first: 29920 vs 19712 with 9D first). The CLI `hands`/`advise` do not reorder for you; `sel` after the move shows the real est.
- IF The Hook is the boss AND you own a held-card joker (Blackboard, Shoot the Moon), THEN play 5 cards so only 3 are held, and make the held set's majority what you need: the Hook removes 2 of the 3 BEFORE scoring, so one red card among 3 black is gone 2/3 of the time; 3 black held = guaranteed Blackboard.
- IF a perishable xMult has 1-2 rounds left and the boss is the hard blind, THEN skip the Small AND the Big (no round end = no tick) so its last round is the boss; the skip tags (Skip $15, Rare, Buffoon) are paid on top. Done at ante 3 (Buffoon tag opened a Mega Buffoon immediately at blind select, not in the next shop) and ante 4 (Blackboard carried the Hook).
- IF Mr. Bones is owned at ante 5+ and the Big is projected under 1.0x, THEN skip the Big: Bones backstops the boss, you keep the perishables un-ticked and the rent unpaid, and the Big's shop at $0-8 buys nothing anyway. But Bones at The Wall needs 25% of 240k = 60k: a flat build cannot reach it; use the Boss Tag (I played the ante-5 Small and never got it) to reroll the Wall first.
- IF the Double Tag is held and the next skip tag is Meteor/Buffoon/Charm, THEN the skip yields TWO packs (two Mega Celestials here = 4 levels); the advisor's skip line does not mention the doubling.
- IF money is $0-8 at ante 5+ with two rentals, THEN stop buying: every later shop is dead; sell the perished joker and leave. Rent at $0 Smalls makes a third purchase impossible, so the third-shop plan must already be in hand by the first ante-5 shop.
