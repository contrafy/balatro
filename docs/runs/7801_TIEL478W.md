## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING (0 xMult, main hand L3, no permanent +mult) made worse by VARIANCE: my only mult source was Misprint (+0..23). Every clear until ante 4 was a one-shot on a high roll (22, 17); the two decisive ante-4 hands rolled 4 and 0. A 200-chip Two Pair at mult floor 7-15 is 1400-3000; at the mean it is ~4100; 9000 in 3 hands needs ~3000 EVERY hand, which this build only delivers on luck. Money was never the problem this run ($31 at ante 2 from the Investment skip), but it was all spent on perishables (Scholar $9 + 2 rerolls $11, Zany, Wrathful rent) that were gone or dying by ante 4, and no xMult was offered in 9 shop views (3 rerolls) and 3 Buffoon packs (Zany/Gluttonous; Astronomer/Faceless/Half/Mail-In).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante-4 Jumbo Buffoon: Astronomer over Half Joker{PERISHABLE,RENTAL}. The advisor printed EMERGENCY (one Two Pair ~4128 vs boss/2 9000) and still ranked Half Joker -75 for being a second rental; I followed it. Replaying the actual ante-4 Small with Half Joker (+20 mult on <=3 cards): Pair T alone 130x26 = 3380 (roll 4), High Card A 116x24+ = ~2800 (roll 10), Pair A (Mult Aces, Empress) 132x30 = 3960 (roll 0) = ~10100 > 9000 with the same bad rolls. Half Joker cleared the blind; Astronomer scored nothing. A +20 mult bridge for $0 was worth -$6/round for an ante.
2. Ante-2 first shop ($31): $20 for a 5-round negative Scholar (2 rerolls + $9). Scholar did fire (Flush 1472, Broadway 1212, Needle FH) but a $9 perishable is expensive; with $31 the Telescope voucher ($10, Celestial packs always contain the main-hand planet) + the Jumbo Celestial + $15 kept would have built Two Pair L4-5 by ante 3 and still bought Misprint/Scary at the ante-2 Big shop.
3. Ante-4 Small, second discard: I kept A K Q T T (5 cards) and drew 3. Keeping only T T + K Q (4 cards) draws 4; marginal. The real error was earlier: entering a 9000 blind whose floor-case score (Misprint 0) was ~5000 with no rescue beyond one Empress.
4. Ante-3 Big shop: the $5 reroll (nothing) + Empress $3; the Jumbo Arcana ($6) had a chance at Justice (Glass K/Q = x2 on a face Two Pair card) which is the multiplier this build lacked. With 0 xMult and $11, a 5-tarot pack beats one 2-joker reroll at Gold sticker odds.

What worked:

- The House: discard 5 face-downs, then read the rank-sort leak: the two face-downs sat between 7C and 5S, so they were 5/6/7 and guaranteed a pair with 7C/5S or each other. KK + 7C + both face-downs was Two Pair KK66 (Misprint 22) = 6868/2000 one-shot.

- Skipping the ante-3 Small (Foil Tag, $3 wallet) kept Even Steven and Scholar alive one more round each: Even Steven's +8 was in the 6090 Big one-shot.
- est was exact whenever Misprint rolled the conservative 6+ (calibration lines show the roll: 6868, 6090, 1170, 1624, 3000 all consistent with a 0-23 uniform).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF Misprint is your only mult joker, THEN plan every must-clear blind on the roll-0 floor (chips x (base + flat adds)), not on est: when the floor-case total of 3 hands is under the blind, buy a flat +mult (Half Joker, Jolly, any "+X mult") before anything that scores later (Astronomer, econ, vouchers).
- IF `shopadvise` prints EMERGENCY/SCORE CHECK below boss/3 and a pack offers a +20 mult joker with rental/perishable stickers, THEN take it over a non-scoring utility even as a second rental: -$6/round is survivable, a lost blind is not.
- IF The House is the boss, THEN discard 5 face-downs, then treat each remaining face-down as the rank bracket of its rank-sorted neighbours (a face-down between a 7 and a 5 is 5/6/7) and play your pair plus the bracketed face-downs; `play TYPE` skips the type check with face-downs selected.
- IF the Needle (one hand) is the boss, THEN use both discards around your best pair BEFORE using any rescue tarot: discards cost nothing in a one-hand round and the tarot stays for the next ante.
- IF ante-1 Big carries an Investment Tag and you own a scoring joker, THEN skip it at Gold: the Big pays $4; $25 after the boss buys two jokers and a planet at the ante-2 shop.
- IF you own Scary Face (+30 chips per face), THEN hold K/Q/J singles over an Ace when choosing what to keep for a Two Pair draw: an Ace pair is 22 chips, a face pair is 80.
- IF a Foil/Polychrome/Holo tag is on the Small Blind at Gold, THEN expect the edition to land on a sticker joker (here a perishable rental Wrathful); value the skip for the saved perish/rent tick, not for the edition.
