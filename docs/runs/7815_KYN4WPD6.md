## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling/money at ante 4. I reached ante 4 with $0-3 for three shops running (Gros Michel rent -$3 every round plus $0 Small blinds), one conditional xMult (Card Sharp x3, x9 with Blueprint) and a typical round of ~6-9k vs Big 13.5k / Fish 18k. Card Sharp + Blueprint makes the first hand of a type nearly worthless and demands the same type 2-3 times in 3 hands, which a 2-discard Gold hand cannot guarantee beyond Pair.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1: Gros Michel{RENTAL} was needed, but I kept paying -$3/round for 11 rounds (~$27) while staying at $0-3 and never could buy the $5-6 joker/pack that showed up. Once Blueprint + Even Steven arrived at ante 2-3 I should have sold GM (sell $1, saves $3/round) if the +15 (+0 copied, Blueprint copies Card Sharp) mattered less than cash for a Buffoon pack at ante 4. GM was only +15 mult on a ~100-chip hand.
2. Ante 3 shop ($7): spent $6 on the Jumbo Buffoon (found Card Sharp). Fine, but Card Sharp is a hand-gated "xMult" that does not count as a reliable xMult; I still had 0 reliable xMults at ante 4.
3. Ante 4 Big: with a 13.5k need and only Two Pair x9 working, a Pair-line (350 + 4900 + 4900 max ~10k) could not clear; the right move was to skip Big too ONLY if the boss were beatable, which it was not either. The real fix was earlier: skip both ante-3 shops' small purchases and keep $8-10 for a xMult.
4. I did not consider selling Even Steven/Clever for a rental scorer; none was offered anyway.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Card Sharp + Blueprint directly left of it (x9 on repeat), THEN open the round with a cheap hand of the type you can repeat (Pair), and put your best face/ace pair second; at 3 hands the chain is worth ~2 hands of x9 (Pair AA 4896, 44 4050 at ante 3) but it caps near ~10k with Pairs; only Two Pair/Trips/Flush repeated scales past that, so do not rely on it for ante-4 Bigs (13.5k).
- IF a High Card is played once with Card Sharp owned, THEN High Card is registered: every later single card scores x9 (TH est 2700 vs 192) - a surprising floor of ~2.5k per hand that `hands` shows; use it as a last-hand fallback when the chain breaks.
- IF the Big blind is skipped for a Buffoon tag at ante 2 with 1 scorer, THEN the Mega Buffoon (2 picks) was worth more than the $5 + shop it replaced (got Blueprint + Clever).
- IF you hold 0 reliable xMults at ante 4 with $0-3, THEN skipping the Small does not fix it; the problem was spending $4-6 per shop at ante 2-3 on additive jokers (Even Steven) instead of saving toward a Buffoon pack with an xMult.
