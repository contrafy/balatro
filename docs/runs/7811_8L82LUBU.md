## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no xMult at all (0 in 5 antes, never saw one in ~12 shop jokers + 3 packs viewed after ante 2). Flat +mult (Half Joker 20, Summit 15+10, Gros Michel 15, Misprint ~11, Popcorn 20) on Pair L6 gave ~9k per hand, i.e. 27k per round vs 37.5k Big. The curve (ante 5 Big 37500) needs ~12.5k/hand; a flat build tops out near ante 5 exactly as the playbook says.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3: spent $16 on two Mega Celestial packs (Pair L2->L4, Trips L2->L3) and skipped the Small for $9; it worked (Pair L6 by ante 5), but there was no Buffoon pack at all in ante 3-5 shops, so the xMult hunt was only rerolls ($5-7 each, 6 rerolls, 0 xMult seen). In hindsight, banking $20+ for Buffoon packs is impossible when none appear; the real lever was the ante 2 Mega Buffoon (picked Summit + Gros Michel; Abstract{RENTAL} was the only other scorer).
2. Ante 5 Small shop: sold Joker(+4) and Misprint(+11.5 avg) to buy Seltzer. Seltzer was not modelled by shopadvise (SIM showed no gain); est afterwards showed it roughly neutral (-15 mult lost vs +chips, +Photograph x2 again on faces only). It did not change the outcome.
3. Photograph holo $8 (ante 3) only multiplies base mult (+5% on Pair); that $8 would have been better as a Buffoon pack or one reroll.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF Mystic Summit is owned THEN burn both discards on junk first (keeping your pairs) and play 3-card hands at 0 discards: +15 mult is ~+25% on a Half Joker engine; it cleared Big 4800 (5395) and Goad 6400 (6930) as one-hand clears.
- IF Half Joker is owned THEN play 2-3 card hands only; add a junk filler to a Pair (3 cards) to draw 1 extra card per play (the +20 stays on).
- IF a Star/Moon/Sun/World tarot is held and the hand has a 333QQ-type Full House with 5 cards, THEN recolour 3 cards to make the Flush House (L1 140x14 = 11576 here, ~2x any pair). It saved the ante-4 Manacle boss (18000).
- IF Hermit is held THEN keep it until after the boss cash-out ($12 -> $24, max +$20) and spend it on rerolls in the same shop.
- IF all non-eternal flat jokers are perishable/Gros Michel THEN assume 1 of 3 vanishes each ante (Summit perished, Gros Michel died at ante 5 end-of-round, Misprint sold): plan 2 replacements per ante.
- IF the Big at ante 5 needs > 1.3x the sum of three hands you can make, THEN there is no skip alternative (boss is 50k); the only remedy was an xMult by ante 3-4.
