## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: ZERO SCORING JOKERS at the ante-1 Big Blind (supply + money). The only shop ($6) offered a Flush-gated perishable
Droll, Mercury, and a Buffoon pack that rolled two rentals (Dusk, Mail-In Rebate). With no joker, 450 needs two big hands out of three
(Straight ~316 + Trips ~189, or Full House ~356 + Two Pair ~120); a jokerless dealt hand with no pair makes that a coin flip at best.

Decisions I would change:
1. Big Blind second discard (hand AKQT 8 4 3 3, 1 discard, 3 hands). I re-discarded 8433 for the same gutshot. Alternative was
   `play PAIR 33 + 8 4` (32 points, same 4-card draw, discard kept for later). The pair play banks points AND keeps the discard,
   costing a hand that would otherwise have been a ~12-point dig later anyway. With 3 hands and a 2-big-hands requirement, the
   third hand was a dig in every line, so spending it early on a scoring dig is free. Rule below.
2. First shop: nothing better was available. Droll{PERISHABLE} ($4, +10 mult on Flush) would not have scored any hand I made.
   The Buffoon pack was the right $4 (18% xMult chance, any scorer); it simply rolled rentals.

What worked:
- Picking a rental utility joker from a pack and selling it immediately for $1 (no rent is charged until round end) beats `skippack`.
- Digging with 3 junk cards (High 7-6-2, 12 points) while holding AAKQT kept both the pair and the straight draw alive; it drew the
  third Ace (Trips 189). `advise` wanted Pair AA + fillers (64), which would have thrown the Aces away.

## Reusable rules (if-then)
- IF a Buffoon pack offers only rental (or eternal-rental) utility jokers, THEN pick one and `sell` it at once: +$1, no rent paid
  (rent ticks at round end), instead of `skippack`.
- IF you are jokerless at an ante-1 Big Blind (450) with 0 pairs after the first discard, THEN prefer a scoring dig
  (play the low pair/junk + fillers, keeping the draw) over a second discard when hands > 2: it draws the same cards, banks
  points, and keeps the discard for the final setup.
- IF 0 discards remain and no hand paces the blind, THEN play the junk cards as a dig (High Card of the lowest card + other junk)
  and keep the made pair + draw for the last two hands; do not play the pair with fillers first.
- IF the ante-1 shop leaves you jokerless, THEN expect the 450 Big to need Straight/Full House + Trips: from the first discard plan
  for TWO big hands, not one.

## Advisor/CLI defects
- `advise` with 0 discards, 3 hands, need 450 and AAKQT762: ADVICE `play PAIR 1 2 8 7 6` (Pair A 64 with 7 6 2 fillers)
  spends the Aces on a 64-point hand. A junk dig (play 7 6 2) keeping AAKQT was better (drew trips). The advisor
  has no "dig by playing junk while keeping the made pair" option when pace fails with 0 discards.
- `advise` (AKQT8433, 1 discard): ADVICE discard AKQT8 keeping Pair 3 (est 32); and earlier "keep the 3 highest cards" dropped a T
  that made a Broadway gutshot. Neither considers the straight draw for a jokerless 450 target.
- Pack advisor says SKIP for an all-rental pack; picking and immediately selling is +$1 at no cost.
Bridge issues: none; 14 steps, all settled first try.
