## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling at ante 2 (only Smiley Face +5 per face and a conditional Flower Pot; no any-hand +mult).
The ante-1 shops offered no permanent +mult joker (Photograph{P}, eternal rentals, planets), and the ante-2 shops offered
Flower Pot{E}, Crafty{E}, Square{E}. Four of my seven played big hands were Flushes (dealt by chance), none a 4-suit hand.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 Small shop ($6): I refused Crafty Joker{ETERNAL} $4 (+80 chips if Flush, SIM +0.08 over leaving) because it is
   flush-only and eternal. The Big Blind then played two Flushes: with Crafty, Flush H 280 -> 600 and the final Flush C
   675 -> 1395 = 2011/1500, a clear. At ante 2 in EMERGENCY a dead slot later is cheaper than a dead run now.
2. Ante 2 first shop: the $5 reroll + Flower Pot{E} $6 (x3 needs four suits among SCORING cards) took $15 -> $4 and never
   fired on a played hand. A 4-suit two pair (AS AC TH TD) appeared once and was only 372. Holding $10 for the next shop
   would not have found a scorer either (Crafty/Square), so this one is roughly neutral.
3. Ante 2 Big hand 1: I followed solver p 0.88 to break Two Pair 66 33 (est 76) for AS JC 8H. Probably right, but the p
   was far too high: there was no realistic 1500 path without a 3-face hand or a Flower Pot hit.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF ante 1-2 and SCORE CHECK says EMERGENCY AND the shop's best SIM line is a cheap ($4) scorer with SIM >= +0.05 over
  leaving, THEN buy it even when it is eternal and hand-gated (Crafty/Droll/Crazy): ante-1/2 hands are Flushes and
  Straights often, and a blocked slot only matters if you survive to ante 4.
- IF your only "multiplier" is Flower Pot, THEN do not count it in pace math: it fires only when the SCORING cards cover
  four suits (a 4-suit Two Pair or Full House), which a Flush never does.
- IF the solver reads p >= 0.85 but your best made hand x hands left is under half the need, THEN treat p as unreliable
  and prefer the line with the highest single-hand ceiling.
