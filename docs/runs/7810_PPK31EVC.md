## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: scaling. All run long there was ONE multiplier (Joker Stencil, x3-x5) and a Two Pair L6 engine; no growth or card-trigger xMult ever appeared (0 xMult jokers beyond Stencil in ~10 shops, 4 Buffoon/Arcana/Celestial packs). Ceiling ~14k per hand (up to 19k with Gros Michel/glass) vs 25k/37.5k/50k at ante 5 and 60k+ at ante 6. SCORE CHECK ratio was 0.26-0.33 from ante 4 on (EMERGENCY) and never recovered; Mr. Bones bought exactly one extra ante.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 5 shop: I spent $4+$4 on Celestial/Arcana packs for single +1 levels while sitting at ratio 0.29. Nothing in the shop could have fixed it, but keeping Mad Joker + Stencil + Gros slot-free and buying Mystic Summit (+15 mult at 0 discards) might have added ~40% on hands played with discards spent first; SIM rated it equal and I trusted SIM.
2. Ante 3 Rare tag skip returned only a rental Invisible Joker: skipping a blind for a Rare tag at ante 3 with ratio <0.7 gave no scorer. Treat the Rare tag as a 1-in-3 xMult at best.
3. Took Greedy Joker ($5) at ante 1 instead of a Buffoon pack: it carried antes 1-3 only because my flushes were diamonds; it is a trap beyond that (sold at ante 5).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Joker Stencil THEN every extra joker costs x1 (x5 at 2 jokers, x3 at 4). Swap a weak joker for a +mult joker instead of adding a 5th; keep Stencil rightmost with `order`.
- IF Mad Joker (+10 mult Two Pair) is offered at ante 4 with Two Pair L5+ THEN take it even if ETERNAL: it more than doubled hand score (5.1k -> 13.4k) because Two Pair base mult is only 5-7.
- IF a Jumbo/Mega Buffoon shows Joker Stencil at ante 2 with <=3 jokers THEN pick it (x4-x5 for free) but fill the remaining slots slowly.
- IF Mr. Bones (foil) is in a pack THEN take it: +50 chips and one forced survival at 25% of the blind; it saved the ante-5 boss at 27.7k/50k.
- IF The House first hand is all face-down THEN discard 5, then the 3 remaining unknowns; the cards are never revealed until played.
- IF The Mark is the boss THEN the face-downs are J/Q/K: pair them with a held low pair to make Two Pair, but expect actual << est (FH est 9180, actual 1550: debuffed/unknown counts).
