## Post-mortem (Stake 1 / Black Deck)

Best result: Stake 1, ante 3, Boss Blind "The Water" (need 4000, scored 2973 = 74.3%). Run lasted antes 1-2 fully (6 blinds cleared) plus the ante 3 Small Blind, lost on the ante 3 boss.

Final joker board (3/6): Joker (+4 mult), Wily Joker (+100 chips if Three of a Kind), Photograph (x2 mult on first scoring face card, single-fire). No permanent/reliable xMult joker at all -- Photograph only doubles once per hand when a face card is present, not a real scaling engine. Levels at loss: Two Pair L2 (40x3), Pair L2 (25x3).

Money curve: $4 (ante1 start) -> $2 (after Grabber voucher) -> $9 (end ante1) -> $9 (start ante2) -> $12 -> $5 -> $9 (end ante2) -> $9 (start ante3, spent on Celestial+Uranus+Arcana packs) -> $11 at loss.

One decision I would change: skipping the ante-3 Big Blind for the Holographic Tag cost a full round of income and, more importantly, meant walking into a zero-discard boss (The Water) with only 3 jokers and no xMult scaling. Per playbook 7a ("reliable xMult beats conditional xMult... never let one be your only xMult"), by ante 3 the board should already have a Duo/Card Sharp/Hologram-type joker; instead $11 was spent on a Uranus planet, a Mercury planet, and a tarot that silently failed, none of which raised the score ceiling enough to survive a no-discard boss on unlucky draws. I would have prioritized any xMult joker offered in the ante-2/3 shops over planet levels, and would not have skipped the Big Blind purely for a tag gamble this early without an xMult joker already secured.

Binding constraint: build composition / scaling, compounded by bad luck under The Water specifically. The Water removes all agency (no discards), so a build with only flat-add jokers and no xMult was always going to need lucky draws to clear 4000 across 4 hands; three of the four hands dealt were only Pair/Two Pair (max ~740), and only one lucky Full House (AAA88, 1512) got close. No amount of play-skill could recover once discards were disabled and the hand shapes did not cooperate.

Cases where `est` differed from actual score by more than 20%: none. Every hand played this run scored exactly its `est` value (0% deviation) -- the advisor's model was accurate throughout.

Bridge issues:
- `cash` failed once with `ACTION FAILED: Round eval blocking events still pending, retry shortly` (ante 1 boss cash-out). Resolved with `sleep 2` + retry per playbook guidance.
- Justice tarot (picked from an Arcana Pack, $4 charged) never appeared in the `Cons:` inventory and produced no visible card enhancement (checked raw JSON for a "Glass" enhancement, found none). This differs from the previous run's finding that non-targeted tarots (Hermit, Fool, etc.) auto-resolve from the pack screen -- Justice is a card-targeting tarot and should either sit in Cons as NOUSE (like The World did) or require a target selection during PACK_OPENING; instead it silently vanished with money spent and no effect. Worth flagging to the playbook/bridge maintainers as a possible bug: card-targeting tarots taken from a pack outside of SELECTING_HAND (no hand cards on the table) may have no valid target and get silently discarded.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Add an explicit rule: do not pick a card-targeting Tarot (Justice, The Chariot, The Magician, The Empress, The Hierophant, The Lovers, The Devil, The Tower, Strength, The Hanged Man, Death) from a pack during SHOP/PACK_OPENING unless you plan to use it during the immediately following SELECTING_HAND -- if it can't find a valid target it may silently vanish with no effect and no refund (observed with Justice this run).
2. Reinforce section 7a for ante 3 specifically: before skipping any Small/Big Blind for a tag (even a good one like Holographic), verify you already have at least one xMult joker (Duo/Card Sharp/Hologram/Photograph is NOT sufficient alone, since Photograph only fires once). If you do not, prefer playing the blind for the guaranteed cash + a chance at an xMult joker in that shop instead of gambling on a tag.
3. Under The Water (or any no-discard boss), if entering the round with no xMult joker and hand levels below L3, consider that the round may be unwinnable on bad draws -- this is a signal to prioritize xMult jokers and hand levels far more aggressively in the ante 2-3 shops than "money target $25 by ante 4" alone suggests, since a single bad no-discard boss can end an otherwise healthy run regardless of banked cash.
