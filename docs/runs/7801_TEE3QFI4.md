## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: MONEY -> SCORING. Small Blinds pay $0 at Gold and the ante-1 Buffoon pack offered only an ETERNAL
RENTAL (Hanging Chad) or junk; the rent (-$3 x 5 rounds = -$15 by the Wheel) ate the whole ante-1 income, so the four
shops ran at $1 / $3 / $5 / $2 and bought exactly one joker (Gluttonous $5). The Wheel (2000) was fought with 2 jokers,
no xMult, Pair/Two Pair L2 and a first deal with no pair; it was lost by 193 with the last hand decided by the order of
two face-down cards (4S 4C). Chad did pay for itself in ante 1: the Big was 524/450 only because the heart flush was 396
instead of 308, and the Big-blind Two Pair at ante 2 went 828 -> 1380 by putting the club first.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 Buffoon: Hanging Chad{ETERNAL,RENTAL} vs skip. The rent made every ante-2 shop empty (the post-Big shop had
   Buffoon $4 and Jumbo Buffoon $6 with $2 in hand). With the $9 saved I would have entered the Wheel with a 3rd joker.
   But without Chad the ante-1 Big (416 < 450 on those draws) is probably lost. It was a coin flip; the real cause is a
   Buffoon pack whose both cards were rentals.
2. Wheel hand 2: banking Two Pair 7733 (222, no club) and keeping K Q + the face-down was right (lower bar 439 = any club
   pair); the last-hand ordering (8C first, face-downs before K Q) maximised outs (4 of 30 per card) and still lost.
   Nothing at the table would have found 193 more chips except luck.
3. Ante 1 shop 1 ($5): Buffoon $4 was the advisor's and the playbook's pick; the alternative (keep $5, buy a $5-6 joker
   after the Big) risks a jokerless 450. Keep the rule, but when the pack shows two rentals the pick is the one with the
   shorter rent (Rebate perishes in 5 rounds) ONLY if the Big is already on pace; here it was not (TIGHT 1.02x).

  Smiley, Scary, Even Steven, Odd Todd), THEN before every play `move hand` the qualifying card to the FIRST position:
  the retrigger fires the card joker 3 times (JJ66 828 -> 1380 actual; High Card AC = 380 at ante 2). Also move a
  debuffed card (Pillar) out of the first spot (Pair A 42 -> 86).

  first), THEN order: known trigger card first (8C: wins if a face-down pairs it), then the face-downs, then the other
  known cards (a face-down KC/QC becomes the first scoring card). Count the outs per face-down (here 4/30 each).
- IF an ante-1 Buffoon pack offers only RENTAL jokers, THEN an ETERNAL RENTAL scorer is a -$9/ante tax on a ~$10/ante
  income: take it only when the next blind's projected round is under 1.0x (TIGHT) with 0 scorers; otherwise take the
  perishable rental (rent stops when it perishes) or skip.

  were worth more than a $0 blind and a $-3 shop.
- IF The Pillar is the boss and you hold a made Two Pair with 2 discards, THEN discard the debuffed junk around it; a

  low pair: the only hands that reach 1300+ were straights/Two Pairs with the club first.
