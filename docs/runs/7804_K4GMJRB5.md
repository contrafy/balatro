## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling with no money. Zero xMult was ever affordable (shop xMults seen: Baseball Card{E} $8 at
ante 1 with $6, Bloodstone{E} $7 at ante 4 with $3). The whole run was a flat additive build: Clever +80 chips, Abstract
+18, Swashbuckler +9-10, Smiley +5/face, Walkie +4/10-or-4, Two Pair L2 -> 5-8k per Two Pair at ante 4, against 6000
per hand at The Mouth. The wallet never exceeded $10 (Small pays $0, Abstract rent -$3 x 7 rounds = $21).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 first shop: sold Ride the Bus ($3) for Wily ($4, +100 chips on Trips/FH). Wily never fired (Two Pair main, and
   The Mouth locks Two Pair). Keeping Bus: Swashbuckler +1 (Bus sells $3 vs Wily $2) and Bus +1 on the faceless TT88
   final hand = mult 40 x 176 chips = 7040 = exactly the 7040 needed, and $4 more for the pre-boss shop. Lost by 352.
2. Ante 2 first shop: I overrode the Jumbo Celestial ($6) to save for joker views. The money did buy Walkie + Abstract +
   Swashbuckler, which was correct; but Walkie Talkie{ETERNAL} (+4 mult per 10/4) was weak and permanently ate a slot.
3. Ante 1: Ride the Bus $6 to $0 then Smiley Face from a pack: these two conflict (Smiley wants scoring faces, Bus resets
   on them). Pick one direction.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the build's main hand is Two Pair/Pair AND a Trips/FH-gated chips joker (Wily, Zany...) is the only buy, THEN do not
  sell a working joker for it; at The Mouth/Eye it is dead. Swap only into jokers that fire on your main hand.
- IF you own Swashbuckler, THEN every sell lowers it by that joker's sell value (and Abstract by 3): a "weakest joker"
  swap must beat old_joker_value + its sell value + 3 (Abstract), not just its own effect.
- IF The Mouth is the boss and your Two Pair est is ~1.0x the per-hand need, THEN the last shop must buy anything that
  adds flat mult/chips to Two Pair; a 1-mult difference decided this run.
- IF you own Smiley Face (or other face payoffs), THEN Ride the Bus is a dead joker; sell it only when the replacement fires
  on your main hand.
