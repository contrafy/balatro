Seeing the xMult was never offered: both Buffoon packs at ante 3 were junk (Greedy/Ride the Bus/Business Card/Egg; Erosion/Egg rentals), the Rare tag gave DNA and the Uncommon tag gave Ceremonial Dagger (both useless), and I was broke ($0-$7) most of ante 3-5 because of two $1 rentals (-$6/round).

1. Ante 2 shop: buying Scary Face as a second rental (-$6/round) kept me at $0-$7 for ante 3-5; the 4-hand extra and Droll happened only because the Skip Tag paid $20. A pair-family engine with a flat stack never scaled; I never saw an xMult either way but money to reroll for one would have been the only out.
