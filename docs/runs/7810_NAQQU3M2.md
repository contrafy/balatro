## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: MONEY and SCALING. Never had more than $9. Two rentals ate $3/round each, Small pays $0 at Gold, and interest never triggered. Whole run was flat +mult jokers (Popcorn, Joker, Gros Michel, Smiley, Raised Fist) with every hand at L1: about 2k per good hand against a 4800 Big, never an xMult in the shop I could afford (The Duo was $8 with $4 after the reroll).
Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 shop at $9: I rerolled ($5) hunting for a scorer and The Duo ($8) appeared at $4 cash. If I had kept $9 and re-checked, or skipped the pack gamble earlier, I could have bought The Duo. A reroll that leaves less than the price of the best likely offer (xMult $6-9) is a bad reroll; rerolling at $9 -> $4 only finds <=$4 jokers (Joker +4). Better: take Droll Joker ($4) or hold $9 for interest and the next shop.
2. Ante 1 Buffoon pack with both options being rentals: I took Popcorn. It worked for ante 1 but its rent + decay made me broke for ante 2-3 (net: 5 rents = $15). The alternative (Even Steven rental $1) is the same trap. Ante 1 with $6: the Celestial pack / saving was no better; this was a bad shop, not a bad pick.
3. Ante 3 Big (4800): had no consumables, no discards left after chasing; unavoidable with 2 jokers.
Reusable rules:
- IF you hold <$10 and the shop shows a $8 xMult (Duo/Trio) you cannot afford, THEN sell your cheapest flat joker only if the sale reaches the price; otherwise do NOT reroll before checking that a reroll leaves enough for the likely buy.
- IF a rental joker has perishable decay (Popcorn), THEN sell it the moment its mult drops to <=+8 and a permanent +15 joker is affordable; each extra round costs $3.
- IF you skip the Small blind at ante 2 with <$3 and 0 income, THEN do it: it saves rent and a decay tick (worked here: skipped 1000 Small, still cleared Big 1500 and Boss 2000).
- IF Gros Michel is your main mult source, THEN treat it as gone after 5-6 rounds and plan a replacement; it vanished at ante 3 Small (1/6 per round).
