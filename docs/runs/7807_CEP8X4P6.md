## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. Zero xMult all run; the score was flat +mult (Spare Trousers +18, Mad +10, Abstract +18,
Wrathful) on Two Pair L3, ~8-9k per Two Pair/FH at ante 4 vs 6k needed per hand at the Plant, so one non-Two-Pair hand
lost it. No r/g xMult was offered in 6 shop views (2 Chaos free rerolls included) or 2 Mega Buffoon packs.

Decisions I would change:
1. Plant 2nd discard: I overrode the solver (discard 8D 6S 222, keep AC + debuffed KH QD; p 0.77 n=100) as "noise" vs the
   rule line (keep 222; p 0.71 n=400). The same 5 cards (AS QH JC TH 9H) came either way: the solver line would have held
   Two Pair AAQQ (~9.2k) AND Straight A-K-Q-J-T, the rule line held only Trips. With Mad/Trousers/Abstract the hand TYPE is
   the score; debuffed cards still make the type. The solver knew that, I did not.
2. Ante 3 skipping BOTH Small and Big for Double+Buffoon: 2 Mega Buffoon packs gave Chaos + a perishable Abstract. It cost
   ~6 Two Pair plays of Spare Trousers growth (+12 mult permanent) and ~$9 (Big $5 + hands + interest). +12 mult on the
   Plant's FH (50 -> 62 mult) alone was worth ~2k, the margin I lost by. [INFERENCE]
3. Ante 4 Big shop: left with $17 unspent before a boss my SIM said no option clears (score ratio 0.77). Earth was in that
   shop ($3, FH L3 ~+900 on the FH I played) before I spent the free reroll; the floor/interest mattered less than the boss.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your mult comes mostly from hand-type jokers (Mad/Jolly/Spare Trousers/Abstract) AND the solver's discard line keeps
  debuffed cards that complete a pair/Two Pair, THEN follow it: debuffed cards make the type and the type jokers fire.
- IF you own a per-play growth joker for your main type (Spare Trousers, Supernova, Green), THEN do not skip both Small and
  Big at ante 3: each fought round is 2-3 permanent growth ticks; skip at most one blind for a pay-now tag.
- IF Riff-raff is offered as a $1 rental with 3+ free slots, THEN buy it, select the next blind (2 free Commons), and sell it
  in the next shop: 2 jokers for $1 + one $3 rent.
- IF the Small Blind tag is Handy (pays $1 per hand played this run, ~$5 at ante 2) AND you hold under $5, THEN skip: the
  post-Small shop buys nothing at $1-3.
- IF Blue Joker is a core chip source, THEN plan for it shrinking within a round (+2 per card left in the draw pile: +104
  at hand 1, +48 by the last hand); late-round hands score ~20% less than the first.
