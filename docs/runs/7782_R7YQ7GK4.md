# Run R7YQ7GK4 (port 7782) - LOSS at ante 1 boss (The Goad)

Started 2026-08-30. Black Deck, stake 2 (Red Stake, Small Blind reward disabled).

## Ante 1
Blinds: Small 300 (tag Rare), Big 450 (tag Polychrome), Boss The Goad 600 (debuffs Spades).

- Small Blind: dealt AH TC 9S 9H 8H 8D 6S 4H. `advise` chose to discard toward a flush (kept 4 hearts),
  drew into Two Pair A3 (played for 96, pacing) then a Straight (11-high) for 296. Cleared 392/300.
- Shop 1 ($5): shopadvise recommended Wrathful Joker ($5, +3 mult per Spade). OVERRIDDEN: deck composition
  showed only 4 Spades of 52 cards (~0.6 expected per 8-card hand), and the playbook explicitly excludes
  single-suit jokers from the ante 1-2 "scoring joker" priority list. Bought Buffoon Pack ($4) instead,
  gambling for an ordinary +mult/+chips joker.
  - Pack offered Fortune Teller (needs tarot uses, useless with 0 tarots) vs Troubadour (+2 hand size,
    **-1 hand per round** — not -1 discard as I initially assumed). Picked Troubadour as "scores soonest"
    since Fortune Teller needed a scaling chain I had no way to start. In hindsight this was a bad pick:
    the -1 hand penalty (Black Deck's 3 hands -> 2 every round for the rest of the run) is a severe,
    permanent tax with zero scoring benefit, effectively the opposite of what ante 1-2 needs (a scoring
    joker). Left with $1, shopadvise said LEAVE (nothing affordable) — ended shop.
- Big Blind (need 450, only 2 hands due to Troubadour): dealt a strong hand (K K Q Q J T T 9 7 4), advise
  discarded to keep a straight framework and draw around it, then played Straight (13-high) for 316,
  followed by another Straight (10-high) for 280. Cleared 596/450.
- Shop 2 ($5): shopadvise said LEAVE (Saturn planet for an undecided main hand, Throwback useless since we
  never skip, nothing else affordable). Ended shop.
- Boss The Goad (need 600, debuffs Spades, only 2 hands / 3 discards): discarded debuffed spades and
  off-suit cards twice chasing a Club flush, found 6 clubs, played Flush for 304 (est matched exactly).
  Final hand (0 discards left): best available was Full House 22277 (one 7 was a debuffed Spade scoring 0)
  for est 212, still 84 short of the remaining 296 need. No consumables were held (never bought a rescue
  tarot; none appeared). Forced to play it — scored exactly 212 (est matched actual), total 516/600. LOSS.

Post-mortem: binding constraint was the Troubadour pick. Losing 1 of 3 hands every round for the rest of
the run (a 33% cut to hand budget) with zero scoring benefit directly caused the boss loss: with the
standard 3 hands I would have had one more hand/discard cycle to find the missing 84 chips (I still had
cards left in a 22-card deck to draw from). The decision I would change: at ante 1 with $1 and a forced
pack pick between two non-scoring jokers, take Fortune Teller (does nothing now but at least doesn't
actively shrink hand economy) over any joker with a hands-per-round or discards-per-round penalty, even if
it "scores soonest" in a vague sense — a flat zero beats a negative multiplier on your only scarce
resource (hands/discards) at Black Deck stakes where hands are already tight.
Also worth flagging: the shopadvise ADVICE to buy Wrathful Joker at ante 1 with 4/52 Spades in the deck
looks like a gap in the shop advisor's suit-density awareness — it should discount single-suit jokers by
the deck's actual composition (available in `raw`/`deck_composition`) rather than treating "+X mult" as
uniformly good. I overrode it for a documented reason but a less careful agent might not have.
No `est` vs actual deviation over 20% occurred; every played hand's est matched the actual scored amount
exactly (96, 296, 316, 280, 304, 212).

Bridge issues: none. All commands worked as documented; no `ACTION FAILED` or retries needed this run.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Explicitly warn against picking any joker with a "-1 hand" or "-1 discard per round" clause during the
   forced ante-1 Buffoon Pack choice when neither option is a scoring joker — these permanently shrink the
   hand/discard budget for the entire run and are worse than a joker that merely does nothing yet.
2. `shopadvise`'s single-suit joker recommendation (e.g. Wrathful/Greedy/Lusty/Gluttonous Joker) should
   weight against the deck's actual per-suit card count (from `deck_composition`) rather than flagging it
   as a generic "scoring joker weighted up" pick when jokers are scarce — a 4-Spade deck makes a
   per-Spade mult joker nearly as weak as an economy joker.
3. Consider adding an explicit playbook note: at ante 1-2 with 0 scoring jokers, if forced to pick between
   two non-scoring pack jokers, prefer the one with no negative side effect on hands/discards, even over
   one that "helps indirectly" (bigger hand size) if it carries a hand-count tax.
