## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING (supply). Zero xMult offered in 12 shop views + 6 rerolls + 2 Buffoon packs (one all-eternal).
Every scorer I owned was a bridge (Popcorn, Ice Cream, Gros Michel, perishable Abstract) or a flat +mult (Summit, holo Odd Todd).
Mult capped at ~45 with no multiplier, so the best normal hand stayed ~4-7k against ante 5's 8-17k per hand.
The flat build reached ante 5 only through lucky draws (Broadway into Quads at the ante-4 Big, AAA33 at the House).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3-4 rerolls: I spent $15 on 3 rerolls that showed nothing. That money as interest plus a Celestial or Buffoon pack
   would have bought about the same odds of an xMult and left levels behind. Rule 7 (rerolls only with $20+ left after) holds.
2. Ante 4: with 1-2 free slots and no xMult, I should have bought an Arcana pack (Justice -> Glass on a main rank) over the
   reroll that only showed Green Joker and an eternal Zany. Flat jokers cannot reach ante-5 numbers.
3. Ante 5 Big skip: with $2 the shop could not help, so skipping for P(Fish) > P(Big)*P(Fish) was right, but by then it was
   already about a 15% shot. The real fix was earlier: keep $10-15 through ante 4 so the ante-5 shops could buy an xMult.
4. Mercury in the jumbo did not show up. Venus (Trips) was never played. Pluto would have been used more under
   Burglar+Splash, where high-card hands with 5 scoring cards come up.

What worked:
- Burglar + Mystic Summit: 0 discards makes Summit's +15 permanent, and 6 hands per round doubled throughput (Small 25k cleared).
- Splash + Odd Todd: every odd filler adds 31 + its own chips. Blind face-down plays under The Fish scored 3.5-5.4k each.
- The House: playing 5 face-down cards blind scored 3040 (est 2170) and cycled the hand; better than a blind discard.
- The Arm: playing High Card A (L1, no level to lose) first kept the made pair for a safe last hand.
- Skipping the ante-2 Big for the Uncommon Tag kept decaying bridges (Popcorn, Ice Cream) strong for the Hook.

## Reusable rules (if-then)
- IF you own Mystic Summit and Burglar is offered, THEN buy Burglar: 0 discards keeps Summit always on and 6 hands double
  the round total. Under Burglar, play junk as 5-card digs, since there are no discards.
- IF you own Odd Todd / Even Steven / a suit joker and Splash is offered ($3), THEN buy it (even Eternal): every filler of the
  right rank or suit then scores.
- IF the shop after the Big Blind cannot buy anything (under $5 and nothing to sell) AND the boss is beatable only by
  luck, THEN skip the Big: one coin-flip instead of two, no rent tick, no Gros Michel roll.
- IF the boss is The House, THEN play 5 face-down cards as the first hand. It scores like a random hand and keeps both
  discards; a blind discard scores nothing.
- IF the boss is The Arm and the made pair would clear only by a small margin, THEN first play a High Card (L1 can't drop) as a dig.
- IF you are at ante 4+ with 0 xMult after 2 empty rerolls, THEN stop rerolling and bank interest. Supply is the limit, and
  money in hand lets you buy the xMult when it does appear.
