## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. Zero xMult all run; joker views were few (mostly 2-slot shops, $3-13 wallets) and no
xMult-shaped joker appeared in any shop or in the 2 Buffoon packs (Erosion{E,R}/Greedy, Faceless/Seltzer). The engine
was card-phase flat: Even Steven (+4/even card) x Hanging Chad + Seltzer retriggers, Arrowhead (+50/spade), Blue
Joker{E}, Raised Fist. It scored 10-18k on an even Two Pair/Pair with a spade first, but only 2-7k on any hand with
faces/odd cards, so every round was a 3-hand dig for even cards. Ante 5 Big needed 12.5k/hand; I averaged 9.8k.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2: Blue Joker{ETERNAL} as the 3rd joker and Popcorn (decaying) as the 2nd. Both are fine for ante 2 but neither
   scales; with Popcorn gone by ante 3 I hit EMERGENCY 0.27-0.31 and spent ante 3-4 shops on bridges (Gluttonous{P},
   Chaos{P}). A permanent +mult (or holding $ for a Buffoon pack) at ante 2 would have kept a slot for an xMult.
2. Ante 2 Big shop: Celestial pack ($4) gave Venus (Trips, off-hand). Keeping $12 for ante 3 would have bought
   Raised Fist/Hanging Chad a shop earlier and kept interest.
3. Ante 5 Small shop: Jumbo Arcana for Justice was reasonable in theory (glass on an even card multiplies Even Steven's
   card-phase mult), but one Glass card in 44 is drawn in only ~1 of 3 rounds; it never showed up. Keeping $9 for a
   reroll/xMult in the Big shop was probably no worse.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your mult comes from card-trigger jokers (Even Steven, Odd Todd-type, suit +mult) THEN face/odd hands score ~25%
  of an even hand: at the Mouth, lock Pair only after discarding to an even pair (here: discarding KKQQ for 44 gave a
  14061 opener vs 2611 for QQ).
- IF you own Hanging Chad, THEN `move hand` the card with the most per-card joker effects (a spade with Arrowhead, an
  even card with Even Steven, a club with Gluttonous) to the first scoring position: +30-50% every time (FH 13596 vs
  8928; 2P 14770 vs 11448).
- IF you own Raised Fist and play fewer than 5 scoring cards, THEN use your LOWEST cards as fillers so the held
  minimum rises (2P 3322 + 4S filler: 18146 vs 14770 without).
- IF a Buffoon pack offers Seltzer (retrigger all cards, 10 hands) and your engine is card-trigger jokers, THEN it is a
  strong 3-round bridge even as a perishable rental (SIM P(next 3) 0.51 vs 0.09); it carried the Mouth.
- IF you reach ante 4 with 0 xMult and $<15, THEN expect to die at ante 5: the realistic fix is earlier (ante 2-3 money
  kept for Buffoon packs/xMult), not ante-5 tarots.
