## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling from ante 3 on, caused by having ZERO permanent scorers until ante 4. Every scorer I owned
before ante 4 was perishable (Even Steven{R}, Seltzer, holo Crafty) or utility (Midas Mask{E}); they carried ante 1-3
and all died at once. The only multiplier (Joker Stencil x4) came from a Judgement pick at ante 4. Max hand at ante 5
was a Crafty Flush L2 at ~14k vs 16.7k per hand needed; a skip-everything line can reach the boss but not beat it.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop: Even Steven{PERISH,RENTAL} $1 + Buffoon -> Seltzer{PERISH}. Both were great for 5 rounds and then gone;
   I spent $16 of rent on a bridge and still had no permanent scorer by ante 3. Droll{ETERNAL} (+10 mult on Flush) was
   the same SIM value, and my ante-1/2 hands and the final build were all Flushes: in hindsight the eternal Flush joker
   was the permanent one and Flush was my real main hand (Crafty + suit tarots).
2. Ante 3: I skipped Small+Big to keep the bridges alive for the Manacle (correct, it cleared), but it also removed two
   shops ($ and joker views) right when I needed permanent scorers. The skip saved the ante but starved ante 4-5.
3. Ante 4 D6 rerolls: 5 views found nothing; fine. The Arcana packs (Sun/Moon) for a 2-suit deck were reasonable.
4. Ante 5: the Spectral pick (Aura on one card) was too diluted; Ectoplasm on Stencil (x5 permanent) was arguably
   better despite -1 hand size. Neither reaches 50k.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your only scorers at ante 2 are perishable bridges with the same perish count, THEN they all die in the same round:
  buy a PERMANENT scorer in the very next shop even if the SIM says the next 3 blinds are saturated (the SIM saw 0.97
  with bridges alive and the SCORE CHECK without them was 0.07).
- IF you own Joker Stencil with <=3 jokers, THEN sell Riff-raff/any utility you can and buy nothing but xMults or
  planets: each joker costs x1 (Stencil x4 with 3 jokers made the Psychic clear).
- IF a perishable carrier has 1 round left and the boss is beatable with it, THEN skip Small+Big (it cleared The
  Manacle 7.2k/6.4k with Even Steven+Seltzer on their last round).
- IF The Psychic and your best hand is a 5-card Flush, THEN play it; for the finish keep a known High Card (est is
  deterministic without Misprint) instead of chasing.
- IF D6 Tag is active, THEN rerolls cost $0,$1,$2...; combine with The Hermit first if it shows (doubled $13 -> $26).

Advisor/CLI defects:
- Ante 2 shop SIM: "SIM SATURATED: next 3 blinds clear with or without any buy" while both carriers perished at the end
  of that ante; the SIM horizon ends exactly where the bridges die, so it said LEAVE with $7 and EMERGENCY 0.07.
- Ante 4 blind select advised `b blind` (play the 9000 Small for Riff-raff's commons) at a projected round of ~1-5k;
  the Riff-raff rule ignores whether the blind is winnable. With Joker Stencil it also ignored that commons cut Stencil.
- `Scorers still alive next ante: 2` at ante 2 Big-shop when both were perishable with 2 rounds left (counted them).
- Spectral pack advisor ranked Aura 70 over Ectoplasm 40 with Joker Stencil owned and every other joker already
  editioned (Ectoplasm could only hit Stencil: +x1 permanent).
Bridge issues: none (76 steps, no rejects).
