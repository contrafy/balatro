Post-mortem: binding constraint = boss (The Club) + no rescue tarot. Every hand of the boss round had at least
one debuffed club card breaking up what would have been a pair or bigger hand, and by the time discards ran out
the last dealt hand had zero pairable ranks (High Card only), so even Acrobat's x3 could not save it. We held
zero consumables all run (Judgement/Death/Strength/Justice never appeared as a shop card-row item at ante 3-4
prices we could afford after buying jokers) — the run never had a rescue tool for exactly this failure mode.
The one decision I would change: at the ante-4 shop (before The Club), buy a rescue tarot or a Luchador if either
had appeared, and/or keep $3 spare specifically earmarked for one, rather than spending to $6 on Campfire alone.
No such tarot/Luchador was ever offered in this run's shops, so the concrete fix is procedural: shopadvise never
surfaced a "no rescue tarot + Club boss next" warning even though the boss debuffs a whole suit for the whole ante.

Bridge issues: none. All commands returned expected state; two `est` vs actual hands differed >20%:
  - "Playing Two Pair with idx [1, 2, 3, 4, 8]... est 290" -> actual scored delta 406 (+40%), ante 2 Small Blind.
  - "Playing Two Pair with idx [3, 4, 5, 6, 8]... est 294" -> actual scored delta 378 (+29%), ante 2 Small Blind
    (both hands had Green Joker's per-hand-played mult growing mid-round, which likely explains est undershoot).

Playbook suggestions:
  1. `shopadvise` should add an explicit warning line when the upcoming boss is a suit-debuff boss (Club/Goad/
     Window/Head) and the player holds zero card-targeting tarots/Luchador, ranking a rescue tarot or Luchador
     above a second xMult joker for that shop.
  2. Under The Club (and other single-suit debuff bosses), `advise` should flag when a discard is likely to keep
     reintroducing debuffed cards from a suit-heavy remaining deck (3 of the 4 discarded/played cards this round
     were the debuffed suit each time) so the discard strategy can prioritize suit balance, not just rank pairs.
  3. Green Joker's per-hand mult growth (+1 mult per hand played, capped nowhere seen) makes `est` for Two Pair
     hands undershoot by 30-40% mid-round; est calc for it may need to account for the joker's growth from
     earlier hands in the SAME round, not just the round-start value.
