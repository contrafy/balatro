## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: money, which became scaling. Total cash seen across 6 shops: $5, $2, $4, $2, $4, $1. The only scorer offered at ante 1 was a rental (Shoot the Moon, -$3 per round-end) and on Gold the Small pays $0, so per ante the run earned ~$13 (Big $5 + boss $8) and paid $6-9 rent: one $4 common joker per ante, never a reroll, never a second pack, never a planet. The build (Shoot the Moon + Mad + Zany, all +mult, main hand Two Pair L1) made 2000-3000 per round when 2-3 Queens were held and ~1000 when none were; The Eye at 6400 (each type once) needed a Full House with Queens held (~4400) plus a Two Pair, and the deck gave Two Pair + a dead High Card + a FH that had to spend the Queens. 7 round-ends paid $21 rent in total, more than every purchase of the run combined ($12).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 first shop ($5): the Buffoon pack offered Shoot the Moon{RENTAL} vs Sly{ETERNAL,RENTAL}. Taking the rental was still right (0 scorers, 450 Big next), but I never planned its exit: the rule "sell a flat rental once 2 other scorers carry" never triggered because the two others (Mad, Zany) were hand-gated and weaker than it. With a held-card rental the exit plan must be "replace it with the first unconditional +mult joker", and at ante 2 ($4 shop) Mad Joker was that chance only for Two Pair.
2. Ante 3 first shop ($4, EMERGENCY): Zany (+12 on Trips/FH) fired once all ante (the losing FH). The Arcana pack ($4) with Empress (+4 mult x2 cards) / Hierophant / Wheel of Fortune was the higher-ranked rule pick and would have added mult to EVERY hand type, which is what The Eye punishes. Under The Eye, an any-hand bonus beats a hand-gated one even when the SIM (noisy at 0.70 vs 0.60) says otherwise.
3. The Eye hand 1 (KH QH QD JS JH TD 9S 9H after discards): Two Pair J9 was forced, but the Straight K-9 (1343, plays one Queen) + later Two Pair would have kept more types open; neither line clears 6400 without a FH. The real mistake was earlier: entering The Eye at a 0.40 score ratio with $1.
4. Ante 2: skipping the Small for the Voucher Tag was right for the rent reason only; the tag (an extra $10 voucher slot) was worthless at $0.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your only scorer is a RENTAL at ante 1 on Gold, THEN budget the run: income ~$13/ante (Big $5 + boss $8, Smalls $0), rent $6-9/ante; that is one $4 joker per ante and no rerolls/packs. Treat every $0-Small as a skip (no round-end = no rent) and buy the first UNCONDITIONAL +mult joker to replace the rental by the end of ante 2, or the economy never starts.
- IF Shoot the Moon is owned, THEN never play a Queen: keep all Queens held and build the hand from the other cards (QQQ held = +39 mult: Straight T-6 scored 3010, Pair 44 scored 738 at ante 3). Use a debuffed Queen (suit boss) as a filler, it adds nothing held.
- IF a Queen-held hand type is dead (Eye already used Two Pair), THEN the dig must keep BOTH the Queens and a made pair: a FH with Queens held (~4400 at L1) is the only one-hand clear for a 4000+ remainder; FH that spends the Queens (333QQ) scores 1794.
- IF The Eye is this ante's boss AND your mult is held-card/hand-gated, THEN in the last shop before it buy whatever adds mult to EVERY type (Empress/Hierophant cards, an unconditional +mult joker) over a second hand-gated joker; The Eye needs three different types to clear.
- IF a Holographic/Foil/Polychrome tag is on the ante-3 Small, THEN discount it: the free joker is Eternal/Perishable/Rental 30% of the time each (here Gluttonous{ETERNAL,RENTAL}, unusable under the hard rule). Skip for it only when the skip is also right for rent/money reasons.
- IF a boss blind is projected under 0.6x in the last shop before it and you hold < $4, THEN the run is already lost on average: the decisive shop was the previous ante's; spend ante 1-2 shops on permanent any-hand mult, not on packs with sticker odds.
