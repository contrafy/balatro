## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: money and no mult. Ended ante 1 with $1, a free-but-useless Mime and a Flush main hand; the $0 Small pay and an unused-hand-less cash-out meant the ante 2 Small shop was visited at $0 with Hologram ($7) and Smiley ($4, previous shop) both out of reach. Blackboard was the only scorer and it fired on 2 of 6 hands; the Big Blind (1500) needs ~500/hand with no x3 on 4 of the 6 hands I wanted to use.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop 1 ($5): bought the Buffoon pack at $4 (only junk, Mime). Alternative: keep $5, reroll later... but the shop had no scorer and the pack was the SIM/rules pick; the real loss was the reroll in shop 2 (-$5, left $1) which revealed Smiley $4 one dollar out of reach. With $6 I would have bought it by selling Mime ($3). If a reroll could reveal a $4 joker, keep enough money to afford it: reroll only when money after reroll + sellable jokers >= $4 (Mime $2 + $1 = $3 failed this by $1).
2. Ante 2 Big Blind: never took Blackboard's constraint into the hand decisions early enough. Discarding with the solver kept red face/diamond cards, and 6-7 red cards in an 8-card hand makes Blackboard (needs held all black) impossible. With Blackboard owned, discard RED cards first (keep black), then play all reds + the best blacks.
3. In the Small blind, playing Flush 276 with 9H held (est 276, no x3) instead of discarding 9H for a made flush est 828 (p~0.4) was the solver's pick at 0.64 vs 0.61 (noise); the solver does not model Blackboard's held-card draw well.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Blackboard, THEN you must play every red card in the 8-card hand (max 5) and hold only black: discard red cards first; if the hand has 6+ reds the x3 is impossible this hand.
- IF the shop reroll costs the money you would need for the $4 joker it may reveal, THEN count sellable junk jokers: reroll only if (money after reroll + sell values) >= 4.
- IF ante-2 Small blind pays $0 and you have no hands left, THEN you reach the next shop with $0: before the Small, plan so the Big/Boss shops are affordable (skip the Small only for a pay-now tag).

Advisor/CLI defects:
- shopadvise ante 1 said REROLL leaving $1 even though the cheapest joker it could reveal ($4) was unaffordable after the reroll: reroll leaves $1 +$2 Mime = $3 < $4. The advisor should require money_after + sellable >= typical joker price.
- Pack advice at ante 1 offered no scorer (Mime / Satellite{P,R}) and still recommended PICK; fine, but the SIM had no way to value Mime (0.13 vs 0.09) - noise.
- advise solver recommended discards keeping red cards (flush/pair draws) while Blackboard was the only scorer; the Blackboard condition (all held black) is applied to est but not to what the solver chooses to keep.
