## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. Whole run = Stencil bridge (x5-x6 on a 2-mult base) then ONE flat +mult joker (Even Steven) at ante 3. Stencil carried antes 1-2 with huge ests (Straight 5600), which hid how weak the real build was; the moment it perished the typical hand was ~660 vs a 3200 Small. I never held more than $6 after ante 1 (rent -$3 x 5 rounds, $0 Small payout), so no joker in ante 2's shops was affordable (Gros Michel $5 at $4, Duo eternal rental).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 second shop: bought Even Steven ($4 -> $0) making Stencil x5 -> still strong, fine; but I never replaced the perishing Stencil. In the ante 2 Big shop ($6) I left with $6 (nothing scoring offered); with Telescope/Arcana/Celestial packs only, a Standard/Arcana pack for Hologram/Vampire feeding was not on the table. A better line was probably to take Gros Michel ($5) in the first ante-2 shop by selling nothing... I had $4, one dollar short, because Temperance/other spending was impossible. Real miss: the ante-1 Big shop spent the last $4 instead of buying the $1 Raised Fist{P,R}? (SIM said no). Unclear.
2. Ante 3 shop: rerolled for $5 (money $10 -> $5) and Hologram ($7) then appeared; had I rerolled AFTER selling nothing and kept $10 I could not have bought it either way, but I should have first checked the shop with $7+ in hand: Vampire $7 was already shown, and a Hologram-or-Vampire needs card/enhancement feeding I could not provide. The honest conclusion: at $10 with 1 joker, nothing in that shop fixed a 0.11 ratio.
3. Skipping the ante-3 Small (Boss Tag) was possible but would only delay: the Big (4800) and boss (6400) were harder.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you take Joker Stencil as a bridge (x(empty slots)), THEN the next joker you add costs x1 but a +mult joker like Even Steven still multiplies it; use the Stencil rounds to bank money, not just to win blinds: its perish date (5 rounds, ~ante 2 end) is a hard deadline and the replacement must be affordable by then ($7+ saved).
- IF the SCORE CHECK reads Stencil-inflated numbers (3300 at ante 2) THEN recompute without it: the real ratio after perish was 0.11.
- IF an all-even Full House/Two Pair with Even Steven appears THEN it is the engine (FH 88866 = 1824, 6622 = 3240 under Stencil); keep even pairs when discarding.
- IF The Mark is the boss and you hold 3+ face-down cards you cannot use THEN play them as fillers next to a made hand: they re-draw 5 cards and 27% of the time form a Full House (it did here: 585 -> 1380).
- IF you are $1 short of a shop joker at ante 2 and own a perishable bridge, THEN selling/skip is not an option: plan the previous shop to arrive with $7+ (e.g. do not buy the $4 joker when a $3-4 planet/tarot is not needed and money is the constraint).
