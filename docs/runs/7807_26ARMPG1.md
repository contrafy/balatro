## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING, then MONEY. The only xMult all run was Acrobat (x3 on the final hand only); everything else was flat
(Mystic Summit +15, Supernova ~+28, Popcorn, holo Photograph ~+15, Square +52 chips) on Two Pair L4. That build made ~10k on a
normal hand and ~33k on the final hand at ante 6, i.e. ~50-60k per round, against 60k/90k/120k. Ante 5 Big and Boss were both
cleared only by drawing a Two Pair on the last draw (AAKK, 8866: 25272 vs 24023, 27816 vs 26802). Money: $60 at ante 3 (Investment
tag + Hermit) was spent on Grabber ($10) and ~11 rerolls over antes 3-5 (~$60) that showed exactly two xMult-ish jokers (Hologram,
Photograph); then two rentals (Acrobat + Popcorn, $6/round) took the wallet from $26 to -$1 in 4 rounds, so the ante-6 shop was dead.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 5: bought Hologram ($7, selling Jolly) and sold it unfed one shop later for Photograph. Net: -$4, -8 mult for the Big,
   no growth. Either commit to a feeding plan (Standard packs) or do not buy a growth xMult with $11 and no feeding source.
2. Ante 3 with $60: 3 rerolls + Drunkard + Grabber. Grabber (+1 hand) was good (4 hands every round, and Acrobat liked the extra
   spare hand). The rerolls found nothing; holding $25+ for interest through ante 4 and buying Celestials (Two Pair was only L3 at
   ante 4) would have given ~+2 levels (~+30%) for the same money.
3. Popcorn{PERISH,RENTAL} at ante 4: helped the Plant margin, but rent with Acrobat drained $3/round for 4 rounds while it decayed.
   With 2 rentals the money only goes down; sell the decaying one as soon as the blind it was bought for is cleared.
4. Ante 2 Buffoon: took Joker{PERISH} (+4) over Egg. Fine for ante 3, but Egg would have been ~$10 sell value by ante 5 when $8 bought
   Photograph and $4 more would have kept Hologram.

What worked:
- Mystic Summit (+15 at 0 discards) as the first scorer: burn both discards before the first play, every round; the discards also dig.
- The Mark (ante 2): kept the 3 face-downs and discarded the visible cards; 6 face-downs are all J/Q/K, so any 5 of them are Two Pair+
  (est 1040, actual 2250 one-shot).
- Acrobat sequencing: bank the best Two Pair for the final hand (x3) and spend earlier hands on 4-card spare plays; it cleared the
  ante-4 Big and Plant comfortably.
- Deck-math for last-two-hand decisions (raw deck `remaining` + quick enumeration): ante-4 Small High-Card dig 73% vs 62%; ante-5 Club
  5-card Two Pair (25%) vs 4-card (20%). Both lines hit.
- Skipping the ante-5 Small (25k for $0, coin flip) saved $6 rent + a Popcorn tick; skipping the ante-6 Small for a Mega Buffoon was the
  only route to an xMult with -$1 money (it had none).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Mystic Summit, THEN spend every discard before the first play of every round (discard junk around your made pairs); never
  play a hand with discards left unless it alone clears.
- IF Acrobat is your only xMult, THEN keep your best Two Pair in hand for the final hand and play 4-card junk/pairs before it; the
  final hand is worth ~3x a normal one, so a Two Pair drawn early is worth more banked than played.
- IF you own Square Joker, THEN play exactly 4 cards (Two Pair without filler, 4-card High Card spares); a 5th filler card stops its
  +4 chips growth. Fillers only when the extra draw is decisive.
- IF you own To Do List, THEN read the target from `raw` jokers[].ability.to_do_poker_hand (the `s` line shows a stale
  extra.poker_hand), and play that type when it still clears (+$4).
- IF you hold 2 rentals and money is under $15, THEN sell the flat/decaying rental right after the blind it was bought for; rent
  turned $26 into -$1 in 4 rounds here and killed the ante-6 shop.
- IF a growth xMult (Hologram) appears and no Standard pack/card source is in sight and money < $15, THEN skip it; unfed it is x1.
- IF your money is negative at ante 5+ and the Small is a coin flip for $0, THEN skip it for any tag (saves rent + perish ticks).
- IF the last 1-2 hands decide a blind, THEN enumerate the remaining deck (`raw` deck.cards remaining) for the keep/filler choice.
