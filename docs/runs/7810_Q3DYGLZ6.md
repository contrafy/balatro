## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no flat/chips or second always-on scaler. The whole engine was Shoot the Moon (held Queens) + Mystic Summit (+15 at 0 discards) + Odd Todd (perishable chips) times Hologram x2.0 and Obelisk x4.2. Money was $0-9 from ante 2 on (rental + $0 Small), so I never bought anything at ante 5. The ante-5 boss (The Flint halves base chips and mult) cut the base to ~10% and the Obelisk reset (every type tied at 3 plays) took the x4.2 away at the same moment. 50000 needs ~17k per hand; I reached ~3.5k.

Decisions I would change:
1. Obelisk play-count bookkeeping: by ante 5 I had played High Card, Pair, Straight, Flush, Trips and Two Pair 3 times each. Once every common type is tied, ANY play except Full House resets it, and the reset hand scores at x1. I rotated types evenly instead of spamming ONE sacrificial type (e.g. High Card) to keep the other six types 1-2 plays behind. Rule: with Obelisk, pick one type as the "max" type EARLY and play only that type when you need a reset; never let 4+ types tie at the max.
2. Ante 5 Small: I played it. With Orbital tag (High Card) and Flint at the boss I could have skipped Small and Big (no Throwback, so no) ... but at least should have saved the Obelisk growth for the boss by planning one FH (count 2) kept unplayed; FH was the only non-resetting type and I burned it on the Big (it did win the Big one-shot, 52008, so it was right, but the boss then had nothing).
3. Ante 5 shop: $7, 6th slot free after Odd Todd perished; I bought a Standard pack for Hologram (+0.25) instead of a joker. Nothing affordable/legal (rentals barred) so no real alternative. The real cost was ante 2-4 purchases: Mad Joker{E} (Two Pair only, +10 mult) and Mystic Summit{E} (+15 only at 0 discards) are weak eternal slots; a Photograph-style or flat chips joker would have survived the Flint better.
4. The Flint was visible at the ante-5 shop ($4 then $7); halved base chips/mult hits builds whose power is xMult on small base. Counter: Luchador/Chicot or a real flat chips joker bought at ante 3-4.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Obelisk THEN count plays per type from the start (`raw` run.hands_played) and keep ONE type strictly ahead as the reset sink; if 3+ common types are tied at the max, a reset is unavoidable next play: plan the reset on the cheapest hand, not on the boss.
- IF you own Shoot the Moon (+13 mult per Queen HELD) THEN play non-Queen hands and keep Queens in hand (est already models it); The Plant (faces debuffed) kills it, so keep a second +mult (Mystic Summit worked: +15 at 0 discards, burn both discards on junk first, Pair AA 22470 at 0 discards vs 2174 with discards left).
- IF Mystic Summit is owned THEN every round dig with both discards (junk dumps keep made hands), then play at 0 discards; with a made hand and 1 discard left, discard 3 junk cards for +15 mult.
- IF a rental is the only scorer at ante 1 and it is a +mult joker (Shoot the Moon) THEN buy it; I kept the single rental all run and it carried every blind to ante 5.
- IF broke (<$4 after rent) at ante 2-3 THEN skip the $0 Small for a tag that pays now (Standard = 2 wild cards fed Hologram; Handy = +$8 at ante 3 after 6 hands played).
- IF Hologram is owned THEN every Standard pack ($4, +1 card = +0.25 xMult) beats rerolling at low money; it reached x2.0.
- IF the boss is The Flint THEN halved base chips/mult makes per-hand value ~10%: an xMult build without flat +chips cannot clear 50000; hold Luchador/Chicot or flat chips from ante 3-4.
