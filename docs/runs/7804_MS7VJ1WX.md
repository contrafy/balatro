## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING. In 11 shop views and 3 Buffoon packs the run never saw a reliable/growth xMult it could use (the only one was
a foil Constellation{RENTAL} at ante 1, sold at x1.0). Ante 3 through ante 5 Small were cleared by stacking temporary/conditional
multipliers on the final hand: Acrobat{PERISHABLE} x3, Loyalty Card x4 every 6 hands, Mystic Summit +15. On ordinary hands the build made
~2-6k. When Acrobat perished, the best normal hand was an Ancient-suit flush at ~11k against 12.5k per hand at the ante-5 Big, and that
flush only exists on rounds where the Ancient suit cooperates.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 Small skip (Garbage, $8): it saved an Acrobat tick, and that tick won the ante-5 Small. But it cost a shop view at the point where
   I most needed an xMult sighting. With zero r/g xMults, a shop view is probably worth more than $8.
2. Ante 4 shops: $8 went to two Celestial packs (Uranus, then a fallback Mercury). With Acrobat due to perish, $30+ at ante 5 would have
   paid for 3-4 rerolls hunting a permanent xMult. Mercury was close to worthless.
3. Mixed build: Mad Joker{ETERNAL} plus Two Pair L4 on one side, Ancient Joker{ETERNAL} plus Jupiter on the other. Both eternals stay
   forever, and the levels were split. If I take Ancient, I should commit to Flush (Jupiter only, The Star/Sun-type tarots to fatten
   suits) or skip it.

## Reusable rules (if-then)
- IF you own Acrobat AND Loyalty Card, THEN read advise's "Loyalty Card x4 in N hand(s) (this one counts)" note and line the x4 up with
  the final hand (x12 together). Spend the earlier hands on 1-2-card throwaways while you hold the best hand. A 4532-est flush cleared
  25000 this way (126684).
- IF you own Ancient Joker, THEN at round start run `raw` and read run.ancient_card.suit, and chase that suit. Use `order hand` to put
  +mult card triggers (Fibonacci ranks, Mult cards) LEFT of the suited cards, so the later x1.5s multiply the +8s. Moving 5H 2H to the
  front took a flush from est 4532 to 10.5k before Acrobat/Loyalty.
- IF a perishable Acrobat is your only multiplier, THEN its last round is your deadline: from the shop where it shows <=3 rounds left,
  money goes to rerolls and xMults, not Celestial packs or skip tags.
- IF the boss is The Psychic (exactly 5 cards) with an 8-card hand, THEN you can hold at most 3 cards for the final hand. Hold
  "made pair + one kicker of a rank you want" and play the other 5. QQ+A held, 5 dumped, drew QQQ22: FH x3 23490.
- IF Ancient Joker is your only xMult at ante 5+, THEN treat it as NOT scaled. A matched-suit flush arrives in maybe a third of rounds;
  on the others the hand is ~3-5k.
