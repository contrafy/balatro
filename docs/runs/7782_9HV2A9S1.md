Post-mortem: Binding constraint was SCALING, compounded by a misplay. Entering the ante-2 boss (The Flint, halves base chips/mult) with only 2 flat +chips/+mult jokers and zero hand levels on the actually-played hand (Full House/Two Pair were played repeatedly but never leveled; only Flush was leveled via a Celestial pack, and Flush was never usable against The Flint's dealt cards) meant total available scoring power was far below 1600 even before variance. The one decision I would change: after the 2nd discard in the boss round produced a clean Full House KKKAA (est 304, already only 1296 short with 2 hands + Two Pair fallback in the deck), I followed `advise`'s DISCARD suggestion for a 3rd time chasing quads/a bigger full house instead of playing that made hand immediately. The redraw came back weaker (est 244) and burned the last discard, after which the remaining 2 hands (Two Pair 82, Two Pair 86) had no more escape valve. Per playbook section 4.4/8 ("Play a made hand that clears its share... never concede... Never play a hand whose est is far below need while you still have discards" - inverse case: with only 1 discard left and a made hand already in hand, converting it to cash via PLAY preserves the sure 304 rather than gambling it away). Playbook suggestions:
  1. Under The Flint (and other stat-halving bosses), `advise`'s discard-vs-play EV math should weight the made hand's certainty higher, since halved base stats shrink the total achievable pool and a late bad redraw is unrecoverable with 0 discards left.
  2. When a Celestial pack does not offer the actual main-played hand's planet (here: Full House/Two Pair were played far more than Flush, yet Jupiter/Flush got leveled), shopadvise's "main hand" detection should track which hand TYPE was actually played most in the run so far, not just the most recently leveled one, before recommending a pack purchase.
  3. Consider flagging explicitly in `advise`'s output when `need` for the remaining hands exceeds the sum of all realistic candidate ests (i.e., the round is already unwinnable) so the agent can stop optimizing hand quality and instead just log the loss cleanly.
