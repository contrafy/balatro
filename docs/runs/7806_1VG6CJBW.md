## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling (mult starvation). A face Pair was (10+20 cards +50 Sly +100 foils) x (2 +10 Smiley +4 Joker) = 2880: chips ~180 vs mult ~16. Four of six slots were chips/econ/conditional (Sly, Rocket, Idol, Arrowhead), three of them Eternal, so there was no room or money left for +mult or an xMult. The Idol (x2 on one rank+suit per round) never fired in 6 rounds. Non-face hands were 500-1400, so every blind from ante 3 on depended on drawing face pairs.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 Jumbo Buffoon: Sly{ETERNAL} (chips-only, SIM +0.14) over Supernova (+mult growth, +0.06). With Smiley as the only mult source, a second chips joker didn't scale. Supernova on the pair it would have grown to +8-10 by ante 3.
2. Ante 3 Big shop ($28, ratio 0.48): Arrowhead{ETERNAL} was a 4th eternal and another chips joker. I also left $13 unspent before the boss and lost by 470. That money should have gone to a Jumbo Arcana (Empress +4 mult on two Q/K, or Justice Glass on a face). MONEY DOES NOT SCORE: when TIGHT before a boss, spend it.
3. Fish, 2 hands left, 1 discard, need 3200, holding AS QH JS TH 66 33: I broke Two Pair 6633 (1428) to dig with the last discard for a face pair and drew blanks. Banking 1428 and keeping JS QH for a last hand was about as good. The real problem was that I had no hand above ~3000 without a face pair.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your scorers are chips-heavy (chips/mult > 8, e.g. ~180 chips vs ~16 mult), THEN rank any +mult joker above every +chips joker, even when a short-horizon SIM prefers the chips one by < 0.10.
- IF you already own 3 Eternal jokers and no reliable xMult, THEN never take another Eternal unless it is an xMult. Flexible slots are what later xMults and +mult replace.
- IF The Idol is your only "xMult", THEN treat the build as having 0 xMult. It fired 0 times in 6 rounds here.
- IF it's the last shop before a boss, ratio < 0.7, and you hold $10+ with nothing to buy, THEN buy the Arcana/Buffoon pack anyway. Interest doesn't clear a 6400 boss.
- IF The Fish has 1 hand left, THEN use the rank-sorted position of face-down cards: a face-down card left of your highest visible card is at least that rank (card 1 was an Ace here). Pick the play that includes the slot where your pair rank would sit.
- IF a purple seal gives Temperance, THEN use it before the round ends so the cash earns interest (+$15 -> +$3 interest here).
