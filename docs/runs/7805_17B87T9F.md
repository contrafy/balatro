## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING. Zero xMult of any kind was offered from ante 1 to ante 5 except Bloodstone{PERISH,RENTAL} (hearts,
non-fit) and Hit the Road at the last shop, across ~14 rerolls ($80) and 11 shop visits. The flat build (foil Mail-In + foil Walkie
= +100 chips, holo Zany, Gros Michel, Popcorn, Half Joker, Pair L4 / Trips L3) peaked at 12-16k per hand, which cleared every
ante-5 Small/Big but met The Wall (2x boss, 100000) at ~1/3 of the need. The economy was fine (Mail-In earned ~$40, Hermit +$20,
Temperance +$11; $40-53 in every ante-4/5 shop); there was nothing to buy with it.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4: skipping the Small for the Charm tag (Fool -> Mercury, Death) cost one shop visit while I owned 0 xMult; a shop view was
   worth more than a Pair level. With 0 xMult at ante 4, play the Small for the shop (it was a 9000 blind I could clear).
2. Ante 5 first shop: once The Wall was visible as the ante-5 boss with 0 xMult, the realistic outs were Mr. Bones (survives at 25%
   of the need: my 39780 would have been enough) and Luchador. I rerolled for "an xMult", but should have treated Mr. Bones as the
   priority target from the first ante-5 shop and kept a free slot for it.
3. Ante 2 shop 1: I took eternal foil Mail-In over a Buffoon pack. It paid well (~$40 + 50 chips), but Buffoon packs were then
   rarely offered; at ante 2 with 0 xMult a Buffoon pack's xMult chance may matter more than the money.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Mail-In Rebate THEN read `raw` run.mail_card at the start of every round and discard every held card of that rank
  before the clearing hand (it costs nothing when the made hand is kept): +$5 each, ~$40 over 4 antes here.
- IF the ante-5 boss is The Wall (100000) AND your typical hand is under 25k THEN the only realistic counters are Mr. Bones
  (needs 25% = 25000 total), Luchador (sell during the boss) or Director's Cut: put every reroll toward them, not toward a flat joker.
- IF you own Half Joker THEN Pair/Trips (2-3 cards) is the main hand: Venus is worth taking for Trips with Zany owned, and never pad
  the pair with fillers past 3 cards.
- IF you hold 0 xMult at ante 4 THEN do not skip the Small for a level/tarot tag: the shop view (xMult chance) is the scarce resource.
- Hit the Road is not an xMult plan: it needs Jacks in hand AND a discard to throw them (got x1.5 once at the Wall).
