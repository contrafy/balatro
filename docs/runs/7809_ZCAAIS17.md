## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling, caused by money. The only permanent scorers ever owned were Seeing Double (conditional x2) and
Wily (+100 chips on Trips only); everything else was a bridge (perishable Gros Michel, rental Supernova, rental Popcorn).
Gros Michel was destroyed after ante 2 Big, which put the run in EMERGENCY with $17 and no +mult; every later shop had $4-6.
The deck never got a level (0 planets bought) and never got a second flat +mult joker. At ante 3 the 75th-pct hand was
~2000 vs a 6400 boss.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 first shop ($14 -> Buffoon $4 -> $10): I then skipped the Small for an Ethereal tag. The Spectral pack offered
   Sigil/Hex (useless) and the skip cost a shop visit and ~$3 (interest + hand money) at the moment I most needed a second
   scorer. With 1 real scorer, an Ethereal/Orbital tag is not worth the shop.
2. Ante 2 boss shop ($17, EMERGENCY): two rerolls ($5+$6) found only Wily. A Jumbo Arcana ($6) or keeping $11 for the
   ante-3 shops (Mega Celestial $8 was on offer later, unaffordable at $6) might have been worth more than a Trips-only
   chips joker. Rerolling in EMERGENCY at $16 dropped me to $1 and then rent made it -$2.
3. Manacle: I followed the solver into a 2-card set draw (keep 9S 9C, discard 5) over keeping the Club flush (1600) as a
   floor. Possibly right (p 0.90 vs 0.73 claimed) but the line needed a third 9 in 15 draws and missed every time.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF Gros Michel is your only scorer, THEN treat it as gone after the next round (1/6 per round end) and buy a permanent
  +mult scorer in the very next shop, before packs.
- IF you own fewer than 2 permanent scorers at ante 2, THEN do not skip the Small Blind for an Ethereal or Orbital tag:
  the shop visit and ~$3 matter more than a random Spectral pick (here Sigil/Hex, both skipped).
- IF the Small Blind of ante 3 needs more than your 75th-pct hand x3 and its shop would have <$4 after rent, THEN skip it:
  it pays $0, skips a rent tick and a Popcorn decay (did it here, Big was then cleared with 1440 to spare).
- IF Seeing Double is your xMult, THEN play pairs/straights that include exactly one club plus another suit; an all-club
  Flush does NOT fire it (x2 lost on the Manacle flush option).
- IF you hold two rentals at ante 2-3 with <$6, THEN sell the weaker one (Supernova at +3 mult) before buying another.
