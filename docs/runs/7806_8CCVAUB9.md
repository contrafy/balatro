## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING (no lasting +mult joker). In 6 shops I was offered one scoring joker I could afford (Popcorn,
decaying) plus Photograph from a Buffoon pack. All mult came from Popcorn (20 -> 8 by the boss) and hand levels, so only a
Straight L3 (90x10 base) could reach 2100+ per hand. The Pillar 6400 needed two straights in three hands; I made one.
Money was second: I never held more than $11, so I never earned interest and never had a reroll budget.

Decisions I would change:
- Ante 3 first shop ($8): I paid $5 to reroll and lost the Mercury $3 that was on offer. Mercury + Arcana/Spectral ($7)
  is two scaling items. The reroll showed no joker, and I had to settle for Saturn. At $8 a single reroll is a coin flip
  that also destroys the items already offered.
- Ante 2 first shop ($11): the $5 reroll found nothing; Celestial gave Venus. Keeping $11 for Popcorn+Photograph shop
  would have left money for Mercury later. Two $5 rerolls in this run returned zero scorers.
- Ante 1: Ice Cream (+100 chips) from the first Buffoon pack scored about as much as a Pair; the real need was mult.

What worked:
- Selling Ice Cream ($2) to afford Popcorn (+20 mult) at ante 2: pair 190 -> 660, Trips 555 one-shot the 1500 Big.
- Skipping the ante-3 Small Blind with $0: its shop would have had ~$3; skipping kept Popcorn at 12 for the Big (no round end).
- The Fool picked from an Arcana pack recreated Saturn (last planet used): Straight L2 -> L3.
- Cycling with a made Two Pair instead of discarding (ante 3 Big): kept QJT, saw 5 new cards plus the hand, and hit the straight.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your only +mult is a decaying joker (Popcorn/Ice Cream) and no scorer is offered, THEN level the hand with the
  highest BASE MULT you make often (Straight: L3 = 90x10) - it was the only hand that scaled here.
- IF you own Photograph, THEN remember it fires during card scoring, BEFORE joker +mult: it doubles only the hand's base
  mult (Straight L2 7 -> 14, then Popcorn +12 = 26), not the joker mult. est 2834 vs my naive 4142. Photograph needs
  a high base mult (levels, Mult cards) to matter; it does little with only flat +mult jokers.
- IF the Small Blind tag pays after the Big Blind (Uncommon/Coupon) and your wallet is <= $3, THEN skip: the Small pays $0
  and its shop buys nothing, while the skip saves a decaying/perishable tick and one blind of risk.
- IF The Pillar is the boss, THEN straights keep most of their value: debuffed cards still count for the type and the L3
  base (90x10) dominates the score. Plan the boss around your leveled 5-card hand, not around pairs.
- IF the shop shows a planet for a hand you play and you have under $10, THEN buy it before rerolling: a reroll
  removes it and returned nothing twice in this run.
- IF the last hand needs a straight and you hold 3 connected cards with 2 hands left, THEN play the 5 other cards as a
  junk High Card to see 5 new cards (33% here) rather than a Two Pair that leaves an unreachable remainder.
