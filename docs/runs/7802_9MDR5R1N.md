## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: multiplier. I never saw an xMult in 7 shop views + 2 Buffoon packs (Misprint/Mr. Bones, Seance/Satellite),
so the build was flat (Zany +12 on Trips/FH, Ride the Bus, Misprint) at ~1500-2000 per normal hand vs ante-3 needs of ~2100-2700
per hand. The only hand that could reach the Mouth's 6400 was a Full House (L6 after the Orbital skip, ~8000), and The Mouth
turns that into one ~70% roll: if the 2 discards do not make the Full House, every play locks a type that cannot reach 6400.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 first shop: Zany (+12 only on Trips/FH) over the Buffoon pack. It worked at ante 1-2 because it pushed me into
   Trips/FH digs, but a Trips/FH engine has no repeatable hand, which is exactly what The Mouth/The Eye punish. A Pair-gated
   joker (Jolly/Sly) or the Buffoon pack fits a repeatable main hand.
2. Ante 3 Small shop: Paint Brush $10 took my last money. It only raised the FH find rate a few %; $11 kept would not have
   changed the Mouth either (Big shop was skipped), so the real alternative was 2 rerolls ($5+$6) for any +mult/xMult joker
   that makes a Pair/Two Pair lock viable. Neither clearly better; I note it as a coin flip.
3. Ante 2: Ride the Bus{ETERNAL} was a weak permanent slot (reset by every face card in a FH/Trips build: it reached 5 once).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the boss is The Mouth AND only one hand type of yours can reach the boss need in one play (e.g. a Full House), THEN
  the round is one draw: P(clear) = P(make that type using only the 2 discards). Before the boss, spend the shops on making
  a REPEATABLE type (Pair/Two Pair) reach need/3: +mult jokers or its planets, not the one-shot type.
- IF your scorers are Trips/FH-gated (Zany, Trio, Wily) and The Mouth or The Eye is this ante's boss, THEN treat the build
  as unscaled for that boss (no repeatable hand) and buy a Pair-gated/any-hand +mult joker first.
- IF an Orbital tag on the Big levels your main hand by +3 and the Big shop could buy nothing (<$4), THEN skipping the Big
  for it is right (FH L3 -> L6 doubled the hand); `s` still says "never skip the Big".
- IF after the Mouth's first discard misses and you hold three pairs, THEN keep the two non-face pairs (Ride the Bus kept)
  and draw 5: 4 outs x 5 draws ~45% vs 6 outs x 3 draws ~41%.
