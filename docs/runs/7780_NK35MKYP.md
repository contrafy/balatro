# Post-mortem
Result: LOSS, stake 2, ante 2 boss (The Water), scored 252/1600 needed.
Final jokers: Crazy Joker (+12 mult if Straight, sell$2), Walkie Talkie (+10 chips +4 mult per 10/4 rank, sell$2). Straight leveled to L2 (60x7). $5 cash at game over.
Money curve: start $4 -> skip Small Blind $9 -> after Big $9 -> shop end $6 -> after boss $6 (cashed at ante1 boss with $9 total incl. carryover, entered ante2 shop $14->9->5) -> after Small $8 -> Celestial pack $4 -> after Big $10->5 -> lost at boss with $5.

Binding constraint: LUCK, compounded by build fragility. Ante 1-2 hands 1-4 all cleared with huge margin (280-2300 vs need) by drawing straights (Crazy Joker synergy: Straight was the de facto main hand despite the playbook's pair-family preference, because the shop kept offering straight-friendly jokers/planets and the draws cooperated). The Water boss (no discards) removed the one lever (discarding to draw into a straight) that had carried every prior hand; with no pair/trips floor joker and no rescue tarot in bank, three forced hands of whatever was dealt (accumulating 60+76+116=252) could not approach 1600. Zero jokers scored on a bare pair (Crazy Joker needs Straight, Walkie Talkie needs rank 10/4 specifically), so there was no fallback hand type.

Decision I would change: at the ante-2 shop before The Water (upcoming boss was shown as "The Water" in shopadvise the entire ante 2), I should have prioritized a floor pair/two-pair joker or a card-targeting rescue tarot (Strength/Justice/Death, $3 in the card row) over rerolling/endshop at $5-10 — shopadvise never surfaced one as available/affordable, but I did not consider skipping straight-leveling to instead bank cash toward a rescue tarot appearing in a later reroll. More fundamentally: a build with only a Straight-conditional joker (Crazy Joker) and a rank-conditional joker (Walkie Talkie) has zero scoring floor on a no-discard boss; per playbook section 5 ante 1-2 guidance, the FIRST joker bought should be an unconditional or Pair-triggered floor joker, and Crazy Joker (single-hand-type, explicitly listed as NOT a scoring joker in the playbook) should have been treated as a placeholder only, with priority given to rerolling harder for Jolly/Sly/Joker+4/Mad/Clever even at the cost of the early pack pick.

Bridge/CLI issues: none. No `ACTION FAILED`, no retries needed, no connection issues. All `advise`/`shopadvise` outputs were clear and actionable throughout.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Add explicit guidance: "Before a no-discard boss (The Water, The Serpent-partial, The Needle), verify you hold a scoring joker that fires on a PLAIN hand type your draws can reliably make (Pair/Two Pair), not only a Straight/Flush-conditional one — a Crazy Joker/Ancient Joker-only build has a hard floor of 0 on forced dealt hands."
2. Add a rule: "If your only scoring joker(s) are single-hand-type (Crazy Joker, Ancient Joker, The Tribe, etc.) by ante 2 and a no-discard boss (The Water) is upcoming, spend the ante's shop on a floor pair/two-pair joker or a rescue tarot even at the cost of a pack/level, rather than banking cash or leveling the conditional hand further."
3. Consider flagging in `shopadvise`'s WARNING line specifically when the upcoming boss is The Water/The Serpent and no joker in inventory scores on a bare Pair — this run's shopadvise WARNING correctly noted "none of your jokers scores on a plain Pair" at ante 1-2 shops but that warning was not repeated/escalated once The Water was confirmed as the ante-2 boss.
