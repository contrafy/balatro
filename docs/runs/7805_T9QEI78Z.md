   paid interest and, combined with the $4 left at ante 4, bought a Buffoon pack at the ante-4 shop.

   die together. If bridges are needed, stagger them or replace one with a permanent scorer before the next ante.
Right calls: skipping ante-4 Small (Rare) + Big (Orbital Pair +3) saved $12 rent and kept 3 perishables alive for the
Plant (one-shot); skipping the ante-2 Small for Economy ($9 -> $18).
