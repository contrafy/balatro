## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: economy, caused by rent. The first shop's $1 Wrathful{PERISHABLE,RENTAL} plus the $4 Buffoon pack
left $0; rent -$3 x 5 rounds = $15 of a ~$30 ante 1-2 income, so I never held $5 for interest and every shop from the
Big of ante 1 on had $0-4. Only 4 jokers were seen for sale in 5 shops that I could afford (Clever{E} $4 at $3, Even
Steven at $2 after selling Banner). I entered ante 3 with ONE permanent joker (Even Steven, +4 mult per even card) and
no xMult: score check 0.11, Big 4800 vs median round ~1530.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 Small shop: Wrathful{PERISH,RENTAL} $1 + Buffoon $4. The SIM liked the rental (+0.26) but it charges $3 at
   every round end for a joker that dies after 5 rounds: it was effectively a $16 bridge. Green Joker $4 (permanent)
   or the Buffoon pack alone with $1 left would have kept the economy alive. A perishable rental is double-bad: you pay
   rent for its whole life and it dies anyway.
2. Ante 2 Small shop: Celestial pack $4 at $4 (TIGHT advice) gave Earth, a fallback level that did nothing until the
   lucky Pillar FH. Holding $4 would have bought Even Steven one shop earlier AND kept Banner (both affordable at $6).
3. Banner pick: fine for ante 1, but chips-only Banner + Wrathful made every round a spade hunt; Photograph{PERISH}
   would have perished at the same time as Wrathful. Nothing better was on offer.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the only affordable scorer at ante 1 is a PERISHABLE+RENTAL joker, THEN count its true cost as $1 + $3 x 5 = $16
  and prefer a permanent $4 joker (even Green Joker) or a Buffoon pack; never buy both the rental and a pack to $0.
- IF money after a buy would be < $5 at ante 1-2 while paying rent, THEN skip non-joker purchases (Celestial packs,
  planets): the next shop's $4 joker is worth more than a fallback level.
- IF you are broke (<$5) at ante 3 with 1 joker and the Small tag is Handy/Garbage (pays now), THEN skipping the Small is
  right: the $0 Small's shop could buy nothing (here +$12), but it does not save a run already at score check 0.11.
- IF The Pillar is the boss, THEN in the Big play the lowest cards of a flush (TS 9S 7S 6S 2S over QS) and dig with
  junk High Cards; the Pillar was then cleared with a dig-to-FH line (solver p 0.36 -> 0.70 -> FH 2697).

Advisor/CLI defects:
- shopadvise SIM ranked Wrathful{PERISHABLE,RENTAL} $1 as the best buy (+0.26) at ante 1 and then a Buffoon pack to $0
  (+0.21): the 3-blind SIM window does not price 5 rounds of rent against a joker that perishes; the combo killed the
  economy for two antes.
- ante 2 TIGHT advice bought a Celestial pack with the last $4 (score ratio 0.62 vs 0.59, noise) although a scorer
  was the binding need; the SIM's "a later shop sees $0.8" should have vetoed it.
- advise solver did not consider multi-card High Card dumps under The Pillar ("WITHOUT fillers") although dumping 4
  junk cards draws 4 (found the spade flush).
Bridge issues: none.
