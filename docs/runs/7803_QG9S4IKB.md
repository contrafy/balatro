# Run QG9S4IKB (port 7803, claude-opus-5-5) - Black Deck Gold Stake - LOSS at ante 2 blind The Water (1920/2000)

Ante 1 (300/450/Club 600; Small tag Rare, played per advice): Small: 2P AA66 108 + 2P QQ77 108 + K-high Straight 316 = 532 (no jokers). Shop $4: Mad Joker{RENTAL} $1. Big: 2P 3322 360 + 2P KKJJ 720. Shop $5: nothing (Jumbo Buffoon $6, Sock and Buskin second rental). Club: 2P AATT (debuffed AC) 612/600 in one hand.
Ante 2 (1000/1500/Water 2000): shop $9: Joker Stencil{ETERNAL,RENTAL} $1 (x5 with 2 jokers; advisor said AVOID), Celestial -> Uranus (2P L2). $4. Skipped Small (Voucher Tag) and Big (Ethereal Tag -> Familiar: +3 enhanced faces) to dodge $6/round rent. The Water (no discards): Pair QQ 300 (cycling fillers), club Flush with wild QS 1540, last hand dealt AS KD JH TD 7C 6H 5D 3C (no pair) -> High Card 80 vs 160 needed. 1920/2000. GAME OVER.

## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: no unconditional scorer. Mad Joker only fires on Two Pair, and Stencil x5 multiplies a bare base
(Pair L1 = 30 x 2 x 5 = 300, High Card A = 16 x 1 x 5 = 80). Under The Water (no discards) Two Pair never came in
3 hands, and the last hand had no pair at all. A plain Joker (+4 mult) would have made that High Card 16 x 5 x 4 = 320 and
the Pair QQ 720: the boss was lost for want of a $2-4 unconditional joker.

Decisions I would change:
1. Skipping the ante-2 Big Blind for the Ethereal Tag. Money-wise it was a wash (Big pays $4 + hands, rent took $6), but
   it removed the only shop visit before a known no-discard boss. With $4 that shop could have bought a Joker/Jolly/planet.
   The Spectral pack (Familiar) did nothing for the boss.
2. Ante 1 boss shop ($5): left with nothing. Should have rerolled? No ($5 -> $0). Fine. But ante 2 first shop: after
   Stencil ($8) I bought a Celestial pack instead of keeping $4+ for a Pair-scoring joker; Uranus only helps Two Pair,
   which The Water then denied. With a type-gated +mult only, the first $4 should go to an unconditional scorer.
3. Stencil itself was right (x5 for $1); its rent was what made the Small/Big skips tempting.

## Reusable rules (if-then)
- IF your only +mult/+chips joker is hand-gated (Mad/Clever/Trio...) AND the boss is The Water or The Needle THEN do not
  skip the Big Blind; spend its shop on any unconditional or Pair-triggered scorer (Joker, Jolly, Sly, Half, Scholar).
- IF you own Joker Stencil THEN each extra joker costs x1: buy only jokers that add >=25% per hand, prefer planets, but
  still own ONE unconditional +mult joker so High Card / Pair hands are not x5 of nothing.
- IF two rentals drain $6/round and the Small Blind pays $0 THEN skipping the Small is free money (+$6 vs a $0 shop);
  skipping the Big is NOT free when the next blind is a boss: it trades the last shop before the boss for a tag.
- IF The Water and the hand shows a wild/4-suited flush with a 1-pair fallback THEN take the flush only if the remaining
  need after it is coverable by High Card (the last 8 cards may hold no pair: ~25-30%).

## Advisor/CLI defects
- `shopadvise` ranked Joker Stencil{ETERNAL,RENTAL} -100 "AVOID(x1 with full slots)" with 1/6 jokers owned; it was x5
  (state ab x_mult 5). It should value Stencil at (empty slots + 1) with the current joker count.
- `shopadvise` "scoring jokers alive next ante: 1" ignored Stencil's x5.
- Pack `advise` in a Spectral pack (Familiar/Sigil): "No pack advice for this pack type".
- `s` at ante 2 Small: "Voucher Tag is not worth a shop visit" while two rentals made the Small shop $0 net; and it
  gave no warning against skipping the Big before a no-discard boss with only a Two Pair-gated joker.
Bridge issues: none.
