## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: MONEY -> no permanent multiplier. The run was in debt (Credit Card) from the first shop to the end:
never a cent of interest, so every scorer was a $0-3 sticker bridge (Jolly{PERISH}, Mystic Summit{PERISH,RENTAL}, Banner
{PERISH} from a tag, Seeing Double{PERISH}). In ~14 shop views (6 rerolls) not one reliable/growth xMult was offered; the
only multipliers were Seeing Double (conditional) and a polychrome edition. Every bridge died around ante 5, so I skipped
the ante-5 Small and Big to fight The Hook with them alive and fell 5930 short (best possible last hand 11664 vs 17594).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop 1: Grabber $10 on Credit Card with zero scorers (advisor's ADVICE). The 4th hand helped every round and
   paid ~$1/round back, but -$10 at ante 1 meant the whole run was spent at -$5..-$14 and every later purchase was a
   sticker bridge. Two $5 rerolls for a permanent +mult joker at ante 1 would have been better.
2. Ante 5 shop: Spectral $4 (Cryptid) + reroll $5. With 1 round of Mystic left, the $9 was better as a Celestial pack /
   Mercury hunt: one Pair level is ~+10% on the 3 pair hands at the Hook (~+3500 of the 5930 missing).
3. Ante 4 Celestial: Venus (Trips L2) got used once. Fine given the offer, but a pair engine should skip a Celestial pack
   without Mercury rather than take a side level when broke.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Mystic Summit (+15 mult at 0 discards), THEN spend both discards before the first play every round (junk or
  draw-toward), keeping the made hand; at Gold's 2 discards it turns ~2k pair hands into ~6k (verified 6 rounds).
- IF you own Seeing Double, THEN choose the pair that contains a club (KC KS over QH QD): it doubled 9 of 11 plays here.
- IF your key scorers are perishables with 1 round left at the Small Blind of an ante, THEN skip Small and Big (they do not
  tick) and fight the boss with them alive; a shop you cannot afford is worth less than the bridge (cleared 88% of the
  Hook this way; playing Small would have left the Hook at ~30k max).
- IF you own Credit Card at ante 1-2, THEN do not spend credit on vouchers: debt removes interest for the whole run and
  forces sticker bridges; use credit only for a permanent scorer/xMult.
- IF the Needle (1 hand, 9000 at ante 4) is the boss and you own Mystic Summit, THEN burn both discards while keeping the
  made hand; a better pair often arrives with the draws (Straight 9315 -> Pair 66 12312).
