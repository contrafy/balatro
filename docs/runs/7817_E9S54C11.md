## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. I never saw a reliable xMult until ante 4 (Obelisk{E,R}, the only xMult offered in 9 shops/3 packs) and
money was $0-9 at every shop, so the build stayed additive: Shoot the Moon (+13 per held Queen, red seal on QH), Half Joker,
holo Mad, Clever{P}, Misprint{P}, Mystic Summit. Typical hand at ante 5 was ~5-7k vs 12.5k/hand needed for the Big. SCORE CHECK
was 0.24-0.40 from ante 3 on and never recovered; ante 3 and 4 bosses were won only by last-hand luck (4th 8, a face-down Ace).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop $7 and ante 2 first shop: I left with money twice (passed Wily{E} $4 at SIM +0.11). The money then went into
   two Judgement buys (Juggler, Satellite: neither scored). At ante 1-2, $3 Judgement is a lottery; a known +chips joker in a
   free slot is worth more than a random one.
2. Ante 4: Spectral + Arcana ($8 total) gave a red seal and two planets. At 0 xMult a Buffoon pack or saving $8 for the next
   shop (Obelisk came the shop after, $1) would have left money for Mercury/rerolls at ante 5.
3. Selling Credit Card for the holo Mad Joker removed my only way to afford an xMult while broke; Obelisk was $1 anyway, but
   at ante 5 I had $9 and no reroll budget.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF a Wheel/Fish face-down card sits in a rank-sorted hand, THEN its rank is bounded by its neighbours: left of your Ace means
  it is an Ace (it made FH AAA88 here and cleared The Wheel). Use it as a known card.
- IF your Small shop would have < $5 (Gold Small pays $0) AND the Small's tag pays in the next shop (Coupon, Uncommon), THEN skip
  the Small: the shop you give up buys nothing anyway (Coupon gave a free Buffoon + Jumbo Celestial + Clever here).
- IF Shoot the Moon is your mult engine, THEN keep Queens held and never play them; red seal (Deja Vu) on a Queen doubles its
  +13 while held; with Half Joker play 2-card pairs and hold the Queens.
- IF Obelisk is owned and two types are tied for most played, THEN rotate Pair/Trips/High Card digs; the final hand of a lost
  round can reset it, but never reset it on a round you can still win.
- IF SCORE CHECK is < 0.4 at ante 4 with 0-1 xMult, THEN expect to die at ante 5; the only outs are an xMult in a pack/shop, so
  every dollar should go to Buffoon packs/rerolls, not Spectral/Arcana or planets for off-hand types.
