# Run 2US9SYGS (port 7807, claude-sonnet-5-5) - Black Deck Gold Stake - LOSS at ante 3 blind The House (2492/6400)

Ante 1: Small 300 (Polychrome tag, not skipped, 0 jokers): Trips 888 162 + Full House 44477 264. Shop $5: Buffoon -> Mad Joker (eternal; Ride the Bus other choice). Big 450: Two Pair TT88 672. Shop $7: Sly Joker $3 (perishable), Buffoon $4 (both junk, skipped). Boss Hook 600: Two Pair 88/55 1152.
Ante 2: Shop $7: Uranus $3 (Two Pair L2) + Misprint rental $1. Small 1000 Two Pair 3300 (est 2090). Big 1500 Flush 832 + Two Pair 3480. Celestial pack -> Earth (FH L2). Boss Mouth 2000: discarded to Full House 999JJ (est 3564, cleared).
Ante 3 (3200/4800/6400 The House): shop $4 Celestial -> Venus (Trips L2). Skipped Small for Investment tag ($0 wallet). Big 4800: Two Pair 1770 + 3248 = 5018 (money -$3 from rent). Sly perished, sold; Empress $3 -> Mult cards on the 2s. Boss House: whole opening hand face down, 2 discards spent digging, hands 1400 (pair 2s + unknowns, Misprint) + 1020 (Trips 666) + 72 (Pair 7s, last hand with no outs) = 2492/6400. Lost with Mad Joker + Misprint only, Two Pair/FH/Trips L2, $0.

Post-mortem:
- Binding constraint: scaling + money. At ante 3 boss (6400) I had two jokers (Mad = Two Pair only, Misprint random) and L2 hands: ~2-3k per good hand, and The House makes the first hand unreadable so Two Pair was never assembled. Money was $0-4 every shop (Small pays $0, Misprint rent -$3/round, Sly bought $3), so I could only afford one $3-4 item per shop and never a joker slot-filler beyond 3. Only 2 jokers were alive entering the ante 3 boss.
- Decisions I would change: (1) Ante 2 Big shop ($4): took a Celestial pack that gave Earth (off-hand) instead of keeping money; (2) ante 3 Small skipped for Investment tag: reasonable but left me with ZERO shop before the boss, with only Mad+Misprint; playing the Small at 3200 would have given a shop with $0 anyway but an extra rent tick; (3) Mad Joker eternal was a conditional joker occupying a core slot all run; with Two Pair-only mult my Pair hands scored ~200-600 and Trips ~1000. (4) At the House boss I discarded 8 face-down cards for 2 discards instead of playing the 3 facedown cards early to sample - hand 1 with unknowns scored 1400 only because of Misprint rolls.
- Reusable rules:
  * If The House is the boss and you have no main-hand level 4+ or xMult, expect to lose; use the Small/Big shops to buy a flat +mult joker that works on Pair/Trips (Jolly, Sly, Half Joker) not just Two Pair.
  * If a rental (Misprint) is your 2nd joker and money is <= $4, the rent (-$3/round) eats the whole income: sell it once a permanent scorer appears, buy it only if it is your only multiplier.
  * If you hold The Empress/Chariot-type targeting tarot before a face-down boss (House/Wheel/Mark), use it BEFORE the first hand on visible cards; the first hand is face down and gives nothing to enhance.
  * Two Pair-only jokers (Mad) give nothing on Pair/Trips hands; with fewer than 4 scorers prefer an unconditional one.
- Advisor/CLI defects:
  * `advise` under The House said "no hand found (all face-down?)" and suggested discarding non-faces; it does not recognise that a full face-down opening hand should be discarded 5 at a time to see fresh cards (I did this by hand) and does not say which hand type to chase with L2 Trips/FH.
  * `est` for a Pair of 2s with two Mult cards + 3 unknown face-down cards: est 224 vs actual 1400 (unknown cards form hands the est cannot see, plus Misprint).
  * `shopadvise` labeled Misprint [PLUS] +11 mult and ranked it 82 but at $4 wallet this cost me the whole ante-2 -> 3 rent budget ($9/ante); it printed no warning when the single rental already left me at $-1/-3 after a round.
  * `shopadvise` Celestial pack advice "take Uranus" printed even though the pack held no Uranus; fine, but the final pick advice "free level elsewhere beats skipping" gave Earth/Venus (off-hand) of little value.
Bridge issues: none (buy 2 after buying Uranus in the same command line failed only because I sent the original slot index; shop reindexes after a buy).
