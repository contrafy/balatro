## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: the boss fight itself, and I walked into it with $40 unspent. Base scoring was tiny (all hands L1,
+mult only Summit +15) so everything rode on three xMults whose conditions conflict: Blackboard x3 needs every held card
black, which in practice means "play all your red cards", which is mostly a High Card or Pair; that made High Card and
Pair the most played types, so Obelisk kept resetting (x2.4 -> 1 at ante 5, x2.0 -> 1 at the Water). Under The Water
(0 discards) I could not discard reds or dig for a non-max type: hand 1 had 5 reds and only High Card/Pair available.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 6 Big skip. The Small's Skip Tag turned $18 into $40, then I skipped the Big too and fought The Water with no
   shop in between. The SCORE CHECK said 25k (ratio 0.34, EMERGENCY). I trusted my own Blackboard math (~50-70k per
   hand), which only holds with discards. Playing the 90k Big was risky, but $40 in the post-Big shop could have bought
   a +mult joker, planets or a Mega Buffoon. Or I could have played the Small with discards available and kept the Big
   skip. MONEY DOES NOT SCORE: never skip into a boss holding $25+ at a ratio < 0.5.
2. Judgement sat unused (full slots) for 2 antes. When I sold Swashbuckler for Throwback a slot was free for one action;
   using Judgement first (random joker), or never taking the purple-seal tarot with full slots, was free value.
3. Taking Blackboard{ETERNAL} next to Obelisk: their conditions fight each other (see above). With Obelisk owned, a
   Blackboard needs a black deck (Moon/World/Hanged Man) to work so it does not force High Card plays. I started
   converting too late (2 Moons/Hanged Man at ante 5).
4. Ante 5: I let High Card and Pair both reach the max (4) while those were the types Blackboard pushed me into.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF a skip tag pays cash now (Skip/Handy/Garbage) at ante 5+, THEN play the next blind or keep a shop visit before the
  boss; never carry $25+ unspent into a boss while SCORE CHECK < 0.5.
- IF you own Blackboard, THEN at every play put all red cards into the played hand and hold only Spades/Clubs; give
  Moon/World targets to red cards and Hanged Man to red junk. Discard reds with Summit discards. It scored 36k-48k one-shots
  at antes 4-5 (House, Serpent) where the advisor's est said 3-4k.
- IF you own Obelisk AND Blackboard, THEN expect High Card (play all reds) to become the max type: make it the sacrificial
  type early (one reset at x1.2, not at x2.4) and rotate Pair/Two Pair/Trips/FH below it.
- IF the next boss is The Water AND your multiplier needs discards (Blackboard reds, Summit is fine), THEN get flat
  score (levels, +mult jokers) in the shops before it; do not skip those shops.
- IF a Double Tag is on the ante-2 Small and a Buffoon Tag is on the Big, THEN skipping both gives 2 Mega Buffoon packs
  (4 jokers): it took me from 1.5 scorers to 6 jokers incl. Obelisk at ante 2 and won The Club in one hand.
- IF you hold a joker-creating tarot (Judgement) with full slots, THEN sell it or use it at the first free slot.
