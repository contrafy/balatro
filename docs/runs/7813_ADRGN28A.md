## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no xMult at all through ante 4 (flat build: Shoot the Moon, Misprint, Scary Face, Even Steven, Ice Cream, Sly). The build lived on two accidents: held Queens (+13 mult each) and an Ouija that turned 8 hand cards into 4s (11 of 50 cards), giving Five/Quads at 7-10k. At The Water (0 discards) the 4s did not come and a no-xMult hand is ~1.5k High Card / ~3.4k Pair vs 18000: 8892.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 Ouija pick was great (Five of a Kind 10440 at ante 4), but I never fed it: Walkie Talkie perished at the ante-3 boss, and with 7-card hands and 0 discards a Water round can't find 4s. Before The Water I should have kept a discard-independent plan: with $5-12 in the ante-4 shop I bought Ice Cream/Temperance/Sly instead of looking harder for hand size or an xMult (2 rerolls max).
2. Ante 4 Small skip for the Rare tag: the tag produced Baseball Card (perishable rental, x1.5 per Uncommon only). A Rare tag is a gamble on which rare appears; the skip itself was costless (Small pays $0, $7 wallet) so I would do it again, but the rare has stickers too.
3. At ante 2-3 I never took Strength/Death/Hanged Man targets to build Queens: Shoot the Moon gave +26 mult from 2 held Queens; Death/Strength on Jacks into Queens would have scaled it at ante 3-4 (Strength pack at ante 2 was skipped because no Jack was in the displayed hand: the card only changes the cards shown).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Shoot the Moon, THEN High Card plays with 2-3 Queens held score 400 (a1) / 1100 (a2) / 4000 (a4): play ONE junk card and keep Queens held; fillers can't be Queens.
- IF a free Spectral pack (Ethereal tag) offers Ouija and you own rank-triggered jokers (Walkie Talkie 10/4, Even Steven, Fibonacci...), THEN take it: the 8 cards in hand all become one random rank; it was rank 4 here, 11/50 fours, Five of a Kind 10440 and Quads 7366 at ante 3-4 (also -1 hand size).
