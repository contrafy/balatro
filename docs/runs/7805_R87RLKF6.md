## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling + money. Zero xMult through ante 4 (none offered in 8 shop views; only The Idol at ante 4,
$2 short). The build was flat: Half Joker (+20), Smiley (+5/face), Pair L4, plus rental/perishable bridges (Popcorn,
Hanging Chad). The best hand was a face pair ~4275; ante 4 Big needs ~4500 every hand, and only face pairs got there.
Rent ($3/round on Popcorn, then Chad) and planets kept money at $0-7 from ante 3, so no ante-4 shop could buy a $6 joker.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 first shop ($17): Celestial $4 + reroll $5 + Mercury $3 brought Pair to L4 but left $5. Levels on a flat
   build add ~15% each; keeping $13-17 into ante 4 would have bought The Idol ($6) and kept interest. Gold policy rule 10
   (interest is the default sink) applies even at ratio 0.4-0.5 when the shop shows no joker.
2. Ante 3 Small-blind shop ($3): buying Hanging Chad{PERISHABLE,RENTAL} as a 2nd rental cleared the Big and the House,
   but drained $9 over 3 rounds. It was probably still right (SIM 0.79 vs 0.00) — without it the House was likely lost.
3. Ante 2: buying Popcorn{RENTAL} ($1, +20 decaying) was right for The Wall (4000 at ante 2), but the 2 rentals overlapped.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF The House deals all 8 face-down AND you `sort rank` first, THEN each draw is inserted in rank order: a face-down card
  sitting between two visible cards of ranks R1 >= R2 has a rank in [R2, R1]. A face-down between KC and QC is a K or Q,
  so playing it with both is a guaranteed face pair (4275 here). Discard the lowest-sorted face-downs first.
- IF a Pair is your main hand AND you own Half Joker, THEN prefer Venus over Uranus as the fallback planet: Trips is a
  3-card play that keeps the +20, Two Pair (4 cards) loses it.
- IF you hold two made hands whose est sum clears the blind (e.g. Pair 66 1591 + held Pair 22 1247 vs 2065), THEN play
  one and keep the other; do not discard for a draw even when the solver shows p >= 0.99.
- IF ante 3 starts with 0 xMult and the score check is 0.35-0.5, THEN do not spend below ~$10 on levels/rerolls: the
  ante-4 xMult (Idol $6 here) is the only exit, and rent from bridges eats ~$3/round.
