## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: MULTIPLICATION and a 3-joker board. At ante 3 I owned Photograph (x2 only when a face scores) plus two PERISHABLE flat jokers (Sly +50 chips, Wrathful +3/spade) and no lasting scorer. Typical hand was 1.5-2.5k; The Wall needs 12800 = 4300/hand. Only two things ever exceeded 3k: a Glass King (7106 FH at ante 2) and nothing else. Ante-3 shop with $18 made zero progress because two rerolls ($11) showed no scorer.
Decisions I would change:
- Ante 3 first shop ($18): rerolled twice with Blue Joker (+100 chips, $5) already on offer. The first reroll removed Blue Joker and I found nothing. If the shop has a $5 scorer and $13+ spare, BUY it before rerolling (reroll rerolls the joker row away).
- Ante 2 Mega Celestial took Earth+Uranus (FH/Two Pair levels). Good, but Pair was what I actually repeated; two different levelled hands split the benefit.
- Ante 1 Big/Small: Photograph with no face cards in hand is a dead joker; face-gated jokers need a deck of faces (Standard pack/Death/Hanging) to be a real engine.
- Used Justice on JH and Wrathful ordering correctly; the Fool->Justice pick was strong (held rescue Glass).
Reusable rules:
- If a Glass/enhanced face card is in the hand with Photograph, build the played hand around it (Pair/Two Pair/FH containing it): est jumps 4-5x. Fool copying a just-used Justice gives a second Glass.
- If a skip tag says "Uncommon" but the shop's only $0 joker is a Rental/Perishable like Midas Mask, the tag is worth nothing; at ante 3 skip only if you also expect the free item to be a real scorer (it is a random Uncommon with stickers at Gold).
- If you reach The Wall with no xMult beyond Photograph and a ~2k hand, the blind is lost: at the ante-3 Small shop spend ALL money on scorers/levels (not rerolls) and carry Justice/Death to make 2-3 Glass face cards.
- Two-pair/FH hands with 3 pairs in hand: the hand type with a face scoring first (Photograph) is worth 2-3x; check `hands` for which pair leads, not just the type.
- Mega/Jumbo Celestial at $6-8 with no jokers worked at ante 2 (Pair/FH/Two Pair levels roughly doubled est), but Gold ante 3 requires xMult, not levels alone.
