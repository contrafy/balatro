## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING at ante 1 - zero real scorers. One Buffoon pack in 2 shops; $5-6 per shop never reached a +mult joker
(none offered in either shop) and the one pack offered two Eternal picks.

Decisions I would change:
1. Buffoon pick: Hanging Chad over Scary Face. With L1 hands (mult 2-4) Chad adds only the first card's chips twice (~+20 chips,
   +40-80 score). Scary Face adds +30 chips per face. In the boss my scoring hands were Pair/Two Pair of Kings and QS flushes:
   Scary would have added ~+60 chips on the KK33 final (132 -> ~250, cleared 198) and +30 on the flush. Chad is a late-game
   multiplier-of-multipliers (with Photograph/Glass), not an ante-1 scorer.
2. Ante-1 shop 2 ($6): Drunkard (+1 discard, $4) was arguably worth it with no scorer available - the boss round was a pure dig
   round and a third discard raises clear odds noticeably at L1. Unproven; the advisor rated it 20 (UTIL).
3. The boss discards followed the solver (flush draws over a made QQ77 two pair); with flat L1 hands that was right on the
   numbers (two pair 128 x 3 hands cannot reach 600).

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF a forced ante-1/2 Buffoon pick is between a retrigger joker (Hanging Chad, Sock and Buskin, Hack) and a flat +chips/+mult
  joker (Scary Face, Sly, Jolly) and you own no other scorer, THEN take the flat one: retriggers multiply card chips that are
  only 2-11 at L1, worth ~+20 chips; a flat +30/+50 chips joker is worth 2-3x more until you have xMult/Glass.
- IF the ante-1 boss is a suit boss (The Club/Goad/Window/Head) and you own no scorer, THEN treat each debuffed card as a
  discard target and keep pairs in the other three suits; flush draws only in a non-debuffed suit.
- IF you reach the ante-1 boss with zero +chips/+mult jokers, THEN expect ~50% clear odds; the fix is in the shops before it
  (any scoring joker at $3-5 beats packs/levels).

Advisor/CLI defects:
- Pack advise rated Hanging Chad{ETERNAL} and Scary Face{ETERNAL} both 90 ("PLUS") and picked Chad on index order. At ante 1
  with no other joker Chad is worth ~+20 chips per hand vs Scary +30 per face; retrigger jokers should be scored by their actual
  est contribution on the current deck/levels, not as a generic PLUS.
- Shopadvise ranked Drunkard (+1 discard) 20 / UTIL at ante 1 with zero scorers; a discard is worth real clear odds on Black
  Deck Gold's 2 discards when no scorer is available (suggest valuing it at least like a weak scorer when no scorer is offered).
Bridge issues: none; 20 steps, no rejects; est exact on every hand.
