## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING, on two axes. (1) Only one real xMult all run (The Duo x2) plus Throwback (x2.25 by ante 6, fed by skipping every Small Blind from ante 2). No second lasting xMult was offered in 14 shop visits (the offers were Idol eternal+rental, Hanging Chad rental, Golden/Bootstraps perishable). (2) Pair never got a single level (no Mercury in 2 Celestial packs; I skipped the one shop Mercury for Grabber), so every hand ran on 10 base chips and 2 base mult. The Mouth then exposed it: with Pair locked the ceiling was ~28k x4 < 120k, with Two Pair locked every hand needs two pairs with no discards.

Decisions I would change:
- Ante 4 shop: Grabber ($10) over Mercury ($3) was right for that ante (Arm) but I never saw Mercury again. With a pair engine, buy the main-hand planet whenever it is offered even if the boss is The Arm; just don't play Pair at the Arm (or accept losing one level).
- Ante 6 boss: Blue Joker counts cards REMAINING IN THE DECK, not deck size: +88 on the first hand, ~+60 by the last. I sold Swashbuckler (+10 mult, x4.5 = +45 effective) for it. Roughly neutral; not the cause.
- The Mouth with 2 discards: the first hand should not be played before both discards are spent (done), but the lock choice should be planned in the shop before: a pair-only engine cannot beat a 120k Mouth at ~25k/pair. Buying Sly Joker{ETERNAL} ($3) in that shop instead of nothing would not have changed it either. The real fix is earlier: hand levels at ante 4-5.

What worked (reusable):
- Gold-stake skip loop with Throwback: every Small Blind from ante 2 skipped (Uncommon, Skip, Investment x2, Top-up). Each skip = +0.25 xMult, no rent tick ($3-6 saved), no perish tick, and Skip/Investment tags paid $10/$25/$25. Small pays $0 at Gold, so the shop visit was worth ~$1-5. Cleared every Big Blind with the stronger engine anyway.
- Ceremonial Dagger{ETERNAL} as a free tag joker: at Gold, perished jokers keep their sell value, so they are Dagger food (+2 x sell value). Eternal jokers cannot be eaten: an Eternal scorer (Scary Face) placed right of Dagger lets Dagger sit LEFT of the xMults safely.
- Death on a pair + kicker -> Full House 4992 one-shot the ante-3 Big; Moon converting the off-suit cards of a trips into a Flush that still contains a Pair doubled the hand (Duo fires on Flush containing a pair): 15453 -> 29295.
- The Mark: 4 face-down cards always contain a pair (only 3 face ranks); discard non-faces to reach 4 face-downs.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF you own Throwback (or skipping is otherwise free) at Gold THEN skip every Small Blind whose tag is not junk: +0.25 xMult, no rent/perish tick, and Small pays $0.
- IF Ceremonial Dagger is free THEN take it at Gold: feed it perished/rental jokers; keep an Eternal joker (or nothing) to its right between feedings, NEVER a live joker (it eats it at the next blind select, including after reorders in the shop).
- IF the next boss is The Mouth THEN compute (best per-hand est of your main type) x hands; if that is below the requirement, lock Two Pair (or Flush with a pair inside) only when you already hold the cards for the next hand too; otherwise spend that ante's shops on levels/xMult before arriving.
- IF Raised Fist is owned and a play must be 5 cards (Psychic) THEN choose fillers so the lowest HELD card is as high as possible (Pair 4 + Q J 3 fillers kept 8 7 6 held: est 432 vs advised 324).
- IF the main hand is still L1 at ante 4 THEN buy its planet whenever offered, before vouchers.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- `advise` at The Psychic picked fillers 6 7 8 (low cards) with Raised Fist owned, leaving 3 held as the lowest (est 324); fillers Q J 3 keep 6 held (432). Filler choice ignores Raised Fist.
- `advise` at The Mark said discard the 3 face-down cards ("keep the 3 highest") although 3 face-downs hold a pair 71% and 4 always do.
- `advise` at The Arm suggested Pair fillers that broke a second held pair (5S of 55): fillers should not include a card of another held pair when hands remain.
- `shopadvise` blocks on a perished joker (`ADVICE: SELL`) and refuses to evaluate the shop while it is owned; with Ceremonial Dagger the right move is to place it as Dagger food. It also rated Throwback "useless, we never skip" and the blind-select advice said "do not skip" at ante 5-6 while Throwback grew x0.25 per skip.
- `shopadvise` "SCORE CHECK" printed one-Pair estimates that did not include Mystic Summit (+15 when discards are spent), understating by ~40%.
Bridge issues: none; every command settled first try.
