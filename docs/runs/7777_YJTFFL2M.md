Post-mortem: The binding constraint was money/scaling, not a single misplay: entered ante 4 with 0 xMult jokers and a build that was flat-scaling only (Two Pair L6-L7 + Mad/Jolly/Blueprint(copy Jolly) = solid but no multiplicative source), and The Wall's x2 requirement (20000, effectively an ante-5-sized wall one ante early) was too far ahead of that scaling. The Order ($8, x3 mult if Straight) appeared in the ante-4 shop right before this boss and was passed over because it didn't fit the Two Pair build - in hindsight, given I already held Straight L2 as a leveled backup hand and the boss needed 2.5x more than my best single hand could produce even with perfect discards, taking The Order and being willing to pivot to a Straight for ONE hand against a wall boss might have been worth it; but the playbook's "commit to one main type" and "do not drift" guidance argues against pivoting reactively, so the deeper fix is upstream: reach ante 4 with at least one xMult source already banked, which requires prioritizing $ for jokers over pack/planet purchases in ante 3 even when scaling looks tempting. The one decision I would change: at the ante-3->4 shop transition, when down to $1-2 repeatedly after packs, bank money one shop earlier instead of spending to $0/$1 every visit, so an affordable xMult joker (even a conditional one) could be grabbed the moment it appeared rather than being priced out at $8 with only $5 in hand.
