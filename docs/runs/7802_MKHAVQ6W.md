## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: BOSS + SCALING SHAPE. The whole engine from ante 4 on was Card Sharp (x3 on hands 2-3 of the same type) over a Two Pair/Pair base of ~22k; The Eye forbids repeating a type, so the ceiling collapsed from ~220k per round (22k + 2 x 100k) to ~120k (three different types at 20-44k each) and the real deal gave 37k. I saw The Eye named at the ante-7 shop with $5 in hand, two rentals costing $6/round and nothing to sell: there was no money for Luchador/Mr Bones/Director's Cut even if they had appeared. Second constraint: MONEY after ante 5. Two rentals (Seeing Double eternal+rental, Gros Michel rental) cost $6 every round-end while Small Blinds pay $0 at Gold; from ante 5 the net income per round was about $0 and 9 shop visits with ~$3-12 found no third multiplier. The build never got a joker-main xMult that works on every hand (Seeing Double needs a club + another suit, Card Sharp needs a repeat, Vampire only reached x1.4).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 shop ($61 after two Hermits): I left with $61 instead of rerolling further at $9-10; ante 5 shops then burned $35 on 7 rerolls that showed nothing. The right spend was a Buffoon pack / rerolls while money was abundant, or saving for Director's Cut when a bad boss appeared. More important: by ante 6 I should have been shopping for a boss answer (Luchador $5, Mr Bones $5, Director's Cut $10) as a line item, since a Card Sharp build has three bosses that kill it outright (The Eye, The Mouth is fine, The Needle (1 hand) and Crimson Heart).
2. Taking Gros Michel as a SECOND rental at ante 4 (+15 mult, +68%) was right for scoring, but I never planned the rent: from ante 5 the two rentals ate the whole blind income. Selling Gros Michel at the ante-6 Big (keeping +15 only for the boss) would not have fixed The Eye either; the real fix was more money earlier (not buying Reroll Surplus $10 at ante 5, which paid back maybe $12).
3. Ante 5-6 shops: 9 rerolls at $3-6 found zero xMult; the Gold data (2 of 41) was right. The money would have been better as interest + a Director's Cut reserve.

What worked (keep doing): Riff-raff rental at ante 1 ($1) produced 6 sticker-free Commons over 3 blinds (holo Swashbuckler, Shoot the Moon, Scary Face...) and was sold once slots filled; The Hermit twice via The Fool (+$40 total) and Temperance twice (+$21); Economy Tag skip at ante 4 ($35 -> $70); Negative Tag skip at ante 6 (free slot-less Vampire); Card Sharp registration lines (cheap Pair first, keep the club pair for the x3 hand); Seeing Double fired on every hand I needed because I steered pairs to include one club.
