Post-mortem: Binding constraint was BOSS DESIGN (The Eye) colliding with a build that had only ONE truly strong hand type (Full House L8) and three much weaker backups (Pair/TwoPair/Trips all only L2). The Eye forces you to cycle through distinct types across 3 hands; once Pair and TwoPair were spent, the only material left in hand was another pair (no trips/straight/flush ever materialized despite discarding for one on all 3 hands), leaving no legal non-zero scoring type for the final hand. Zero xMult joker the entire run (flagged repeatedly by shopadvise, but no actual xMult was EVER offered in any ante-3-6 shop this run - pure bad luck) meant the flat/conditional-mult stack, while large, was capped well below what ante 6+ needs; the Full House L8 carried antes 1-5 comfortably but ante 6's boss punished type-narrowness rather than raw scoring power.
The one decision I would change: at ante 6 shop, instead of leveling Trips (Venus) as Eye-prep, I would have bought/kept a rescue tarot (Death was already spent at ante 6 Big Blind and never rebought) - a spare Justice/Strength in hand during The Eye's final forced hand could have upgraded the lone Pair into a Trips or Two-Pair-compatible shape (e.g. Strength ranks up a card to make a third of a kind), avoiding the zero-score trap entirely. Playbook section 7b explicitly says "RESCUE TAROTS... USE it before a forced final hand that does not clear" and "re-bought right after you use one" - I used Death at ante 6 Big Blind and did not re-buy one before The Eye, which was the actual gap.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Add an explicit rule for The Eye: "before entering The Eye, buy/hold a rescue tarot (Justice/Strength/Death) specifically so a forced final hand with only an already-used type can be upgraded to an unused type" - the existing rescue-tarot rule (7b) only mentions "a forced final hand that does not clear", not the narrower The-Eye-specific failure mode of a hand whose ONLY type is already excluded.
