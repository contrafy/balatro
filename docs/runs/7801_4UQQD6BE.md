## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING (zero xMult for the whole run), with MONEY as the reason it could not be fixed. 25 jokers seen (19 in shops incl. 4 rerolls, 2 in a Buffoon, 4 in a Mega Buffoon) contained exactly one xMult-shaped card (Baseball Card, conditional on Uncommons I did not own). Income at Gold was tiny: Small pays $0, Burglar rent $3/round, and I skipped 4 of the 9 Small/Big blinds for tags (Meteor, Buffoon, Handy, Coupon), so the only real cash was $4+$5 per ante plus unused hands; the $20 Credit Card paid for Mystic Summit, Venus, Abstract, three rerolls and two Arcanas, all flat. The holo Burglar engine (6 hands, +10 mult, Mystic Summit permanently on, 0 discards) cleared antes 1-3 with hands to spare and hid the problem: the moment it perished (ante 4 boss) the build was a 3-hand, ~3500-per-hand flat deck against a 6000-per-hand boss. Projected 0.44x, actual 0.41x.

Decisions I would change:
1. Justice target and the Glass model. I put Justice on the red-seal Mult 6C believing seal retrigger x Glass = x4 on the whole hand. Glass (like Photograph) is a card-trigger xMult: it multiplies the mult accumulated at card-scoring time, i.e. the base hand mult (2-6) plus earlier card effects, BEFORE my +mult jokers (Greedy holo +10, Summit +15, Abstract +15, Supernova ~9) are added. In this build Glass x2x2 on a Pair is (2x4)+49 = 57 vs 51 (+12%), not x4. The Stone card from The Tower (+50 chips on a 30-chip pair = +170% chips) was the strong pick and I only took it second. The playbook says this ("card-trigger xMults like Photograph do not count: they multiply before +mult jokers"); I did not apply it to Glass.
2. Ante-4 rerolls on credit: three rerolls ($18) across two shops found one $4 flat joker. The Gold policy's "at most 1-2 rerolls, $20+ left after" exists for this reason; on credit the cap should be one reroll per shop, and the rest of the credit kept for the next shop's fresh items (which cost $0 to view).
3. Ante-3 Big skip for the Coupon Tag: the free shop was Showman{PERISH}, Square Joker{RENTAL} and two Standard packs (QD, red-seal 6C). Playing the Big would have paid ~$5 net, shown one more joker pair and given Supernova 6 plays. The Handy Tag (+$10) and the ante-2 Meteor/Buffoon skips were worth it; the Coupon one was not. Rule of thumb: a tag whose payout depends on an unseen shop (Coupon, Negative, editions) is a coin flip; a tag that pays NOW (Handy, Buffoon pack, Mega Celestial) is not.
4. The ante-4 Coupon shop at $13: one reroll found Abstract; I left with $6 instead of rerolling again at $6 (the Small shop's first reroll was $5 anyway). Fine. But the ante-4 post-Small rerolls (2, 3) should have been one: the freed credit ($13) at the post-Big shop would have bought Zany + both Arcanas, i.e. +12 mult on Trips/FH as well. Still ~0.5x the Fish; the loss was decided by ante 3.
5. What could actually have saved it: an xMult sighting. The two Buffoon packs offered Mr. Bones/Midas Mask and Supernova/Popcorn/Obelisk/Credit Card. Nothing to do about that except more shop views, which needed money, which the Gold $0 Small and the skips denied.

What worked (reusable):
- Jokerless ante-1 play with the Pillar ahead: Flush 292 + a lone 5 (10) cleared the Small with 6 cards exposed; the boss then debuffed only 2 of 8 dealt cards per hand and a debuffed card used as a FILLER cycles it out at no cost.
- Holo Burglar at $1 (ante 1): +10 mult on every hand and 6 hands per round for 5 rounds; with it, Mystic Summit ($5) is a permanent +15 (0 discards always), and "discarding" is a High Card L2 play that still scores ~1000-1500. Burglar's +3 hands trigger at blind select, so it can be sold mid-round after its last blind select without losing the hands (I let it perish instead; selling before the Big's last hand would have saved $3).
- Mega Celestial (Meteor tag) at ante 2 when no pair planet was offered: Pluto (High Card L2) was the best 2nd pick for a 6-hand/0-discard round: every lone A/K became 550-1500 for 3 antes. Earth (FH L2) paid 3131 at the Wheel and 4000-5000 later.
- Credit Card from a Mega Buffoon: $20 of credit let a $2 wallet buy Mystic Summit + Venus at the ante-3 shop; the debt is still owed when it perishes (money stays negative, no new debt). Treat it as a one-ante loan for scorers, not for rerolls.
- The Fish: discards draw face-UP, only draws after a PLAY are face-down, so spend both discards before the first play (also turns Mystic Summit on). Face-down cards are rank-sorted into the hand, so a face-down card's position between visible neighbours bounds its rank; the last hand "KC + the face-down sorted above it + 3" was a hidden Pair K (3120 vs est 1150).
- Mr. Bones from a forced Buffoon pick: never needed, but it made the ante-1 Big (jokerless, 450) and the Pillar safe to play aggressively, and sold for $2 one round before perishing to fund Abstract.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF a holographic or polychrome joker is offered at ante 1-2 for <= $8, THEN it is a scoring joker regardless of its printed effect (holo = +10 unconditional mult, poly = x1.5); `s`/`shopadvise` do not count editions as scorers.
- IF your mult is mostly flat jokers (+40 or more on a base of 2-6), THEN card multipliers (Glass, Photograph, red seal on them) add only 10-30%: score = (hand chips + card chips) x ((base mult x card xMults) + joker +mult). Prefer +chips (Stone card +50, Square, Hiker, foil) or a JOKER xMult (applied after the +mults). Put Justice on a card only when the base mult is high (leveled main hand) or you own no +mult jokers.
- IF you own Burglar (0 discards), THEN Mystic Summit is +15 on every hand; buy it on sight. Burglar grants its +3 hands at blind select: sell it during its last round before the final hand to skip that round's rent.
- IF the Small Blind tag pays NOW (Handy = $1 per hand played so far, ~$10 by ante 3; Meteor/Buffoon = a pack now) and the Small shop would open under $3, THEN skip. IF the tag pays in a later shop (Coupon, Negative, Rare/Uncommon/editions), THEN play the blind unless 2+ scorers are owned and the current ante's boss is already covered.
- IF you are on Credit Card debt, THEN at most one reroll per shop; the next shop shows new items for free and the credit is needed for the item itself.
- IF The Fish is the boss, THEN use both discards before the first play, make hand 1 the big one, and read face-down ranks from their sorted position; include face-down cards next to a held pair as fillers (they can only upgrade the hand).
- IF The Pillar is the ante-1 boss, THEN clear the Small with a 5-card flush/straight plus a 1-card High Card rather than three pair hands: 6 cards exposed instead of 10-12, and the boss then rarely debuffs more than 2 cards per deal.
- IF ante 4 starts with 0 xMult and under $15, THEN the run is already lost in expectation (this run: 0.17-0.24 score ratio at every ante-4 shop); the fix is at ante 2-3: keep $8+ unspent per shop so that the ~10% xMult sighting is affordable, and skip only for tags that pay now.
