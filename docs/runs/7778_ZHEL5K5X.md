Post-mortem: binding constraint was JOKER SCARCITY, not misplay. By the boss blind I still had only 1 joker (Acrobat, a conditional xMult that only helps the literal last hand of a round) and zero flat +mult/+chips scoring joker. Every hand up to that point was carried entirely by lucky flush draws (no pair-family engine ever got going - suits kept aligning for clubs/spades flushes). The Goad debuffed spades, which had been my best suit all game, and cost 2 of my 3 discards just clearing junk before I could even look for a new flush. With no card left to draw into (used all 3 discards by hand 2) and no scoring joker to add a mult/chips floor, the final forced hand (bare high card) was 248 short of the 296 remaining need. shopadvise correctly flagged the gap ("WARNING: none of your jokers scores on a plain Pair") at shop 2 but had nothing affordable to fix it with ($7 vs Burnt Joker's $8), and its own ADVICE was LEAVE - the shop simply never offered an affordable scoring joker before the boss arrived.
  Decision I would change: at shop 1 (Buffoon Pack, $2 remaining after purchase), the two options were Acrobat (x3 final-hand-of-round) and Scholar (+20 chips/+1 mult per Ace played). Per the playbook, unconditional flat jokers are preferred at ante 1-2 over conditional ones; Scholar is narrow-condition (Ace-only) but at least fires immediately when an Ace is drawn, same as Acrobat only fires on the literal last hand. In hindsight neither was a good "Pair/Two Pair" joker (Jolly/Sly/Mad/Clever were not offered), so this pick was a wash either way - the real problem was money: starting with $4 (stake 2 Small Blind pays $0, but this was ante 1 with only $4 starting cash) left no room to reroll for a better pack at ante 1. I would have rerolled the ante-1 shop's Burglar/Mail-In-Rebate/voucher offer once (if $5 had been available) to hunt for a Jolly/Sly/Joker+4 before committing to the Buffoon Pack, but at $6 total the reroll was unaffordable, so this was likely not fixable at ante 1 with this exact draw.

Bridge issues: none. Every `est` matched the actual scored delta exactly across all six hands played (328, 308, 304, 244, 60, 48) - no CLI or estimation errors this run.

Playbook suggestions:
1. The ante-1-2 shop guidance ("Buffoon Pack fine at ante 1 if no joker in shop... pick the mult/chips joker") should explicitly note that a pack offering ONLY conditional jokers (Acrobat: final-hand-only, Scholar: Ace-only) with no Pair/Two-Pair/unconditional option is a coin-flip and the run should budget for needing a SECOND scoring joker within 1-2 shops, not treat the pack pick as "done, joker acquired."
2. Consider adding a rule: when a boss blind debuffs the suit that has carried every prior flush this run (as The Goad did here to Spades), and the player holds zero rescue tarot/consumable and zero flat-mult joker, log this combination as a known-loss pattern so future agents recognize it earlier (e.g. spend the ante-1 shop 2's $7 on ANYTHING that adds flat mult/chips even if `shopadvise` says LEAVE, since LEAVE assumes a future shop will have an affordable option before the boss - which did not happen here).
3. `advise`'s pacing check ("3 hands: pacing, keep discards for later hands") played the Big Blind flush across only 2 hands leaving 1 hand + 1 discard unused, which was fine there, but on the boss blind the same discard-conservation logic left zero discards for the final two hands when the debuffed suit consumed early discards - a rule that reserves at least 1 discard specifically for after a suit-debuff boss's initial cleanup would have helped, though it's unclear it would have found a clearing hand in this specific deck draw either way.
