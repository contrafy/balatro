Post-mortem: binding constraint was a misplay, not money or scaling -- money was never tight ($47 banked at the end,
well above every target). After registering Full House with Card Sharp on the first hand of the Big Blind, the next
two hands each formed a DIFFERENT, never-before-played type this round (Two Pair, then Pair) instead of re-forming
Full House or repeating the same Two Pair a second time, so Card Sharp's x3 never fired again for the rest of the
round. This is exactly the anti-pattern playbook section 7a warns against ("playing three different types wastes
[Card Sharp]"), and it happened despite following `advise` at every step -- `advise`'s discard heuristic ("keep the
best made hand and draw around it") does not currently weigh Card Sharp's already-registered type when choosing what
to keep, so it chased the locally-best hand each time rather than the type that would trigger the x3 multiplier.
The one decision I would change: on the second hand of that Big Blind (High Card, must discard), I would have
overridden `advise` and discarded toward re-forming a Full House (or at minimum toward the same pair rank as a
previous Two Pair) to keep Card Sharp's x3 alive, even at a lower raw est, rather than following the straight-chase
suggestion that led to a fresh, unbonused Two Pair.
Bridge issues: none. Every `b` command succeeded on the first try all run; no ACTION FAILED, no retries needed.
Estimator accuracy: matched exactly in every hand except one -- the second Two Pair play at ante 4 Small Blind
(est 9120, actual 11960, +31% over estimate), the run's only >20% est/actual deviation, in the estimator's favor.
Playbook suggestions:
  1. When Card Sharp (or any repeat-type xMult) is active and a discard is needed, `advise` should prefer keeping/
     rebuilding toward the ALREADY-REGISTERED hand type over a higher-raw-est but unregistered type, since the x3
     multiplier on a repeat almost always beats a marginally-bigger first-time hand. Currently `advise`'s discard
     step appears to ignore Card Sharp registration state entirely.
  2. Section 7a's Card Sharp guidance ("first hand registers, next two hands of that type get x3") should be
     restated as an explicit DISCARD rule too, not just a play-order rule: "while chasing Card Sharp's bonus and
     short on hands, discard toward the registered type even over a bigger first-time candidate."
