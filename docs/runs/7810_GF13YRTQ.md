## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: scaling. Entire run was flat +mult (Gros Michel +15, Gluttonous +3/club) on L1 hands, no xMult, no hand levels, money $0-11 the whole run (Small pays $0, rental -$3/round). Without Seltzer a good hand was ~1000-2400; ante 3 needs ~2100 per hand for the boss, ante 4 9000+.

Decisions I would change:
- Ante 1 shop 1: bought a Buffoon pack ($4) over Eternal Sly. It gave a 5-round perishable Seltzer, which then died at the ante 3 Small. Fine either way, but both were bridges and I never found a permanent scorer.
- Ante 2: never spent on a Celestial pack/level (kept $8 as advised). Pair stayed L1 through ante 3. With no xMult available a Jumbo Celestial ($6) for Pair levels might have added ~+25% per hand; unlikely to change the outcome.
- Goad: kept AC 7C 4C 3C for a club Flush (hit, 2380) but with 0 discards the last two hands were Pair 1000 and an all-spade debuffed hand. Burning discards earlier on a flush draw left no recovery. Nothing to fix cheaply: the build topped out ~2.4k per hand.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF Gluttonous/Greedy-type suit joker + Splash + a +15 mult joker are your only scorers, THEN a 5-card flush of that suit is your best hand (3.7k with Seltzer, 2.4k without): discard to 3-4 of the suit early in the round, and expect nothing above 2.5k per hand at ante 3.
- IF The Goad (spades debuffed) is the boss and you rely on non-spade suit synergy, THEN discard spades first at every chance: late-round draws were 6 of 8 spades and the final hand scored 0 from debuffed cards.
- IF Small pays $0 and you hold a Rental, THEN skipping Small for any tag that pays now (Handy gave +$3 after 3 played hands) is right: saves the $3 rent and a perish tick.
- IF a perishable's last round is the next blind and the Big needs ~3x your best hand, THEN skipping Big for Investment is worth it for survival (it removes one blind you cannot clear), but it also removed the only shop visit; do it only when the Big is out of reach anyway.
