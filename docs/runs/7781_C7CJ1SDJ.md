Post-mortem: binding constraint was SCALING SPEED, not misplay. Green Stake ante 2 Big Blind (1350) needed roughly
  2.7x what a Two Pair L1 + Card Sharp x3 + Banner could produce (peak hand was 360). The run never got a second
  scoring joker or any hand level before ante 2's Big Blind, and had zero consumables the entire game (no Arcana pack
  was ever bought -- every ante-1-2 shop had too little money after the Coupon-tag pack/reroll spending). The one
  decision I would change: at the ante-1 shop with the free Coupon-tag Buffoon pack and $8, after picking Mr. Bones I
  rerolled once for a scoring joker instead of buying the free Arcana Pack (B1, $0) first -- a free pack has zero
  downside and might have produced Justice/Chariot to start enhancing the Ace/King/Queen ranks that kept reappearing
  as pairs, which would have compounded through both Big Blinds. shopadvise's LEAVE call there technically had no
  cost-benefit rationale against a $0 pack; worth flagging.
Bridge issues: none. Every `play`/`disc` matched the CLI's own `est` exactly when non-debuffed (280/280, 96/96,
  280/280, 320/320, 64/64, 288/288, 360/360, 56/56); no `est` diverged from actual by more than 20%; no ACTION FAILED

Playbook suggestions:

     nothing else in the shop is affordable -- a $0 Arcana/Buffoon/Standard pack should never be advised against.
  2. Mr. Bones (survive at >=25% of blind chips, then self-destruct) is a strong ante-1 boss-blind pick when no
     scoring joker is available yet -- add it explicitly to the pack-pick guidance alongside "take the one that does
     nothing" as "take Mr. Bones if offered, it is strictly better than doing nothing."
  3. At Green Stake, a single Card Sharp + Banner + Two/Three-pair engine tops out around 360/hand by ante 2; the
     stake-3 ante-2 Big Blind (1350) needs ~3.75x that. Confirms 7781_8C3BV4GW/7777_8S1B92B4 data: Green Stake truly
     needs 2 scoring jokers AND a hand level (not just 1 joker + 1 xMult-conditional joker) in place before the
     ante-2 Big Blind, not just before ante 3.
