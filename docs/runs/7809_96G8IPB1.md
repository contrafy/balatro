## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING via CHIPS, caused by MONEY. Final build was Half Joker (+20) + Dagger (+12) + Supernova (+6) = ~40 mult on a Pair L1 that has 10 base chips: Pair K = 30 x 40 = 1200. All money went into mult (Dagger feeding, Half Joker rent, Buffoon packs) and the wallet sat at $1-3 from ante 1 on, so no planet or chips joker was ever affordable. With 40 mult, +50 chips (Sly) or one Mercury level would have been worth +60-170%, far more than another +mult.

Decisions I would change:
- Ante 2 Big shop ($8): Half Joker $1 was right, but the Jumbo Buffoon $6 (gave Supernova, ~+5 mult) was worse than the Jumbo Celestial $6 (any Mercury: Pair L2 = 45 x 41 = 1845 vs 1200) or Clever{PERISH} $4 + money. I let the advisor's "main hand Straight / take Saturn" label talk me out of the Celestial pack.
- Ceremonial Dagger as a mult engine: $6 + $1 + $4 of food for +12 mult, and it locked my money at $1-3 for two antes. A plain Joker/Jolly gives +4/+8 for $2-3. Dagger only pays when fed free jokers (Riff-Raff, $1 rentals); paying $4 for Crafty to feed it was poor.
- Ante 3 Small skip: neutral at best. The shop it unlocked was worthless at ~$1, but playing it would have added 3 Pair plays to Supernova (+3 mult, ~+8%). The Big was out of reach either way (needed 1600/hand, had 1200).

## Reusable rules (if-then)
- IF your summed +mult is 25+ and your main hand is still L1 (Pair = 10 chips), THEN the next buy is chips: the main-hand planet / a Celestial pack / Sly-Clever-style +chips, not another +mult. Check: score = (base chips + card chips + joker chips) x (mult); add to the SMALLER factor.
- IF you own Ceremonial Dagger, THEN keep it rightmost by default and only put a $1 rental (or a free tag joker) to its right; each $1 rental becomes +2 mult with no rent charged because it is eaten at blind start. Never buy a $4+ joker just to feed it.
- IF you own a rental and your money is under $5 at ante 2-3, THEN every Small Blind is net -$3 (pays $0, rent -$3): the rental must be your main scorer or it goes.
- IF Half Joker is your main mult, THEN your main hand is Pair (2-3 cards); Trips (3 cards) is the only bigger hand that keeps the +20, so Venus/Mercury both matter; Straight/Flush lose the +20.

## Advisor/CLI defects
- `shopadvise` main-hand detection stayed "Straight" through ante 3 after Half Joker (+20 on <=3 cards) and Supernova committed the deck to Pair (5 Pair plays vs 5 Straights). It labelled Jumbo Celestial "take Saturn" and Venus "planet for other hand"; SCORE CHECK used a Straight. Owned hand-gated jokers (Half Joker) should decide the main hand.
- Hand solver at ante 3 Big (hand 2, need 3630, KK held): it printed `SOLVER DISCARD 2 3 5 6 7 p_clear 0.19` (throw away the KK pair, keep AC TD 5D) twice, above every KK-keeping line (0.08-0.12), but the ADVICE line used the rule pick (0.08) instead. Either the 0.19 is a modelling bug or the ADVICE ignored it; both are wrong.
- Solver p_clear looked overconfident in easy spots (1.00 for several ante-2 discards) and not in hard ones; fine for ranking, misleading as a probability.
- Pack `advise` ranked Erosion 90 ("+4 mult per card below 52") when the deck was exactly 52: it adds 0 with no deck-thinning plan.
- `shopadvise` ranked Ride the Bus 95 at ante 1 with zero jokers; it starts at +0 and resets on any face.
Bridge issues: none; 54 steps, no rejects.
