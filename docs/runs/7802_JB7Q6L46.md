## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING. One xMult all run (The Duo x2 from ante 2). In 8 rerolls and ~12 shop/pack views after ante 2 I saw
zero reliable xMults; the build was flat +mult (Abstract 21, Swashbuckler 30) on Pair L5 x Duo, which tops out at ~40-48k
per hand at ante 6 (best: Trips 999 48024). The Ox needed 40k average over 3 hands with 7-card hands (Ectoplasm) and
2 discards; one hand with no pair after both discards ended it.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 5 shop: Ectoplasm (-1 hand size) for a 7th slot. The extra slot only held Gift Card/Ice Cream (+3 Abstract each),
   while the 7-card hand made the final Ox hand pairless. On a pair engine with 2 discards, hand size is worth more than a
   flat 7th joker.
2. Ante 3/5 rerolls ($28 total, nothing found). With 1 xMult and a flat build, that money in Celestial packs (Pair L6-L7)
   would have added ~15% per hand for sure; rerolls at 2-8% xMult odds per view did not pay.
3. Ox second discard: I followed the solver (keep AH 99, break 44, p0.90) and used my last discard on hand 1. Playing
   2P 9944 (41800) and keeping 1 discard for the later hands would have kept a pair-finding tool for the end. With 7 cards
   and 3 hands needed, never spend both discards before the first play.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the Big Blind tag is Boss Tag AND the boss is The Wall (or Violet Vessel), THEN skip the Big (and the Small if its tag
  pays): the reroll turned The Wall 36000 into The Eye 18000 here, which I cleared with 2 hands.
- IF the Small Blind tag is Coupon at ante 2-5, THEN skip it: two Coupon shops gave 3 free jokers and 4 free packs
  (Temperance +$11, Hermit +$20, Jumbo Celestial, Standard).
- IF an Arcana pack offers Temperance or Hermit at $10-22 / joker sell values >= $10, THEN take it over enhancement tarots
  on a flat build (Glass/Steel apply before +mult jokers).
- IF you own Gift Card, THEN keep cheap consumables (planets) as savings: Pluto/Mars bought nothing but sold for $4 each
  after 3 rounds; Swashbuckler gains +1 mult per other joker per round.
- IF a joker PERISHES, THEN it still counts for Abstract (+3) and Swashbuckler (its sell value, observed: selling the
  perished Even Steven dropped Swashbuckler 32 -> 27); sell it only when the slot buys something better.
- IF the next boss needs more than your 75th-pct hand x3 and the Big tag pays money (Skip/Garbage), THEN spend everything
  in the shop before (money cannot help during the boss), skip the Big and fight only the boss.
- IF facing The Ox, THEN prefer Trips/Two Pair over your most-played Pair when ests are within ~10%: it keeps your money
  (Trips 999 48024 kept $27 here).
