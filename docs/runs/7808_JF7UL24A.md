## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling at the ante-2 boss. My whole engine was Droll (+10 mult on Flush) + Lusty (+3 per Heart) = a heart-flush-or-nothing build: a 5-heart flush scored 1900-2100 and any non-heart flush only ~920. Two hearts-flushes cleared ante 1 Pillar and the ante-2 Big in one hand, but The Hook (2000) needed a heart flush again and with 3 hands/2 discards I whiffed (3 hearts then 4 hearts). Zero xMult, 2 scorers, $5-9 and a rental draining $3/round: I never had money for rerolls/Buffoon packs after the first one.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop: Buffoon pack gave only Lusty{ETERNAL}/Hallucination{ETERNAL}. Lusty (+3 per Heart, scores on ~1 card per hand) is a nearly dead eternal slot outside a hearts flush. Passing the pack and taking Droll alone, then keeping $4 for the next shop, may have been better; can't know.
2. Ante 2 Celestial packs (two, $8 total) gave Mercury twice and never Jupiter: the "take Jupiter" Celestial gamble wasted money on a flush main hand; Pair L3 only added ~30 to a Pair. With a heart/flush engine I should have bought the Arcana pack (Hermit/Strength/Empress) or kept $9 for the ante-3 Buffoon/xMult hunt.
3. The Hook dig: I played Two Pair 112 as a dig (solver p 0.59), then the solver showed 0.69 on a second dig; my own math said only a 5-heart flush clears; the first play burned a hand. Discarding first (discards are immune to the Hook) was the better line; the solver's 0.5-0.7 on Hook rounds were far too optimistic (actual outcome 1036/2000).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your only +mult is flush-gated (Droll) plus a suit joker, THEN the boss clears only when the suit flush lands: a suit-gated flush (5 hearts here) is ~0.35-0.5 across 2 discards; count that as a coin flip, not 0.9, and plan the shop money for a general scorer.
- IF the Hook (discards 2 random held cards per played hand) is the boss and you need a one-shot flush, THEN use both discards BEFORE the first play (discards are not subject to the Hook); do not play a dig hand first.
- IF a Celestial pack is the buy and your main hand is Flush, THEN expect Jupiter in only ~1/3 of packs; two packs gave Mercury twice. Prefer Arcana/economy at $9 unless you own the planet.
- IF the Small blind pays $0 and you hold $2 with nothing buyable, THEN skipping it for a tag (Garbage) is right: it saves $3 rent and a perish tick (it did, $2 -> $6).
