## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: misplay under The Hook (boss rule ignored by the draw plan), on a thin ante-1 build (Gluttonous + rental Crafty,
no +mult on pairs). The first hand held Two Pair AATT (124) plus a pair of 6s; all three pairs were discarded for a 3-card
spade flush draw. Under The Hook every played hand also discards 2 random HELD cards, so a draw kept in hand across a
"dig" play loses cards: my 4-spade draw lost the AS after the 14-point dig, and the last hand fell 16 short.

Decisions I would change:
1. Hook hand 1: keep the made Two Pair AATT (rule pick, p 0.80 at n=400) and discard around it, instead of the solver's
   flush chase (p 0.95 at only n=100). Made pairs cannot be destroyed by the Hook once played; held draws can.
2. Hook hand 3 (0 discards, 4 spades held): play the Pair 9s + fillers (56) instead of a 14-point dig; the dig only works
   if the held draw survives the Hook's 2 random discards (each held spade had ~2/5 chance to be hit).
3. Shop 1: with $5 and 0 jokers the Buffoon pack was right; Gluttonous (club-only) was the weaker shape for a pair-family
   ante-1 deck. Hanging Chad perishable would not have changed the Hook either.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the boss is The Hook, THEN do not plan a draw that must survive a played hand: discard toward the draw with your
  discards only, and play made hands (pair family) each hand; never play a small "dig" hand while holding a 4-card
  flush/straight draw (the Hook discards 2 random held cards after every play).

  instead of an off-suit duplicate rank, THEN `sel` both versions: here Straight with 6C was 490 vs 280 with 6S.

  made-hand line at ante 1 boss unless its n is 400 too.
