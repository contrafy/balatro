## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING, and the build was exposed to The Flint. I never owned a reliable xMult joker. The engine was Hanging Chad + Photograph (a face scoring first = x8 on the hand's base mult), Mult/Lucky enhancements on faces, and Blackboard x3 (rental, ante 6) that I made fire on almost every hand by playing red cards as fillers. The Flint halves base chips and base mult, which is exactly the part Chad+Photograph multiply. A x3 face hand fell from ~100k to 21-77k, so 5 hands could not reach 220k without early Lucky hits. Money was also thin: 2 rentals ($6/round) left $2-4 in every ante-7 shop.

Decisions I would change:
- Ante 7 first shop: I passed Luchador{RENTAL} $1 because the joker slots were full (Photograph/Jolly/Blue are Eternal). Small and Big then cleared with margin (166k/110k, 198k/165k), so Scary Face (~25% of chips) could have been sold for it. Selling Luchador at The Flint would have restored ~1.6x per hand (77k -> ~120k for the 2P QQTT hand), which was enough to clear. The upcoming boss counter mattered more than Scary Face.
- Ante 5: I sold Constellation (x1.1) for Blue Joker{ETERNAL}. Blue then filled an Eternal slot for the rest of the run (+~60-90 chips, shrinking as the deck is drawn), and that slot is the one that blocked Luchador. Never take a flat Eternal joker into the last free sellable slot after ante 5.
- Ante 3 Wheel: I copied the advisor's `play HIGH 3 8 6 2` without checking it. AD was a filler, ranks above KS, and became the scoring card (722 instead of 3250).
- Ante 6-7: 3+3+1 rerolls found nothing; that $35 would have been better spent on Arcana packs (Death, Hanged Man and Empress fed the engine every time).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Hanging Chad + Photograph, THEN put a face card FIRST in hand order (`move hand`). The first face scores 3x with x2 each time, so x8 on the base mult. Hand levels and Mult/Lucky cards on that first face are multiplied by up to 8. Move a Lucky/Mult face to the leftmost slot before every play.
- IF you own Blackboard, THEN pick the play so that every HELD card is a Spade/Club: put red cards in the play as fillers (up to 3), spend discards on red non-pair cards, use Hanged Man on red junk and Death to copy a black face onto a red card. Wild cards count as black. Example: FH QQQJJ played with the red JD instead of a black JC left JC 8C 3C held, giving 70728 instead of 23576. It made x3 on ~12 of 15 hands at ante 6-7.
- IF your engine multiplies base mult (Chad/Photograph/levels) and the ante's boss is The Flint, THEN buy Luchador (or Director's Cut) and sell your weakest flat joker for it. Flint cut this engine by ~45%.
- IF you play HIGH CARD, THEN every filler must be lower rank than the card you want to score. The game scores the highest card.
- IF Coupon Tag is offered at ante 1 (Big) or ante 4 (Small) and the shop it replaces would have <$10, THEN skip for it. Both Coupon shops gave 2-3 free items (Jolly, Mega Buffoon, Riff-raff, Jumbo Arcana/Death).
- IF money is under $5 at ante 5 and the Small pays $0, THEN skip the Small for any pack tag. The shop is unaffordable anyway, and the skip saves a rent tick.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- `advise` ADVICE `play HIGH 3 8 6 2` at The Wheel included AD as a filler while the target was lucky KS. The game scored AD: est 722, not 3250. HIGH CARD fillers must rank below the target.
- `advise` candidates and solver ignore Blackboard red-filler lines. Pair JJ + QH 7D 6H (38160) was not listed while the advice was a dig (p 0.52). FH QQQJJ with JD in the play (70728) was listed as "FH est 23576". The solver did value x3 when it picked the filler itself (Pair QQ + 5D 92664).
- `est` treats a held Wild card as not black for Blackboard: est 5256 vs actual 21120, est 9344 vs actual 35040 (x3 fired both times).
- shopadvise said SELL Blackboard (`2 rentals cost $6`) in 4 shops, and SCORE CHECK stayed at ~16-24k ("ratio 0.16-0.29 EMERGENCY") while x3 hands were scoring 70-150k. It treats Blackboard as rarely firing.
- The Flint solver line `disc 1 2 3 4` threw away KC, my only face, and kept 7C 6C 4H 2C. 14 of the 26 cards left in the deck were faces.
- The solver's p_clear (0.99 / >=0.99 with n=16-100) was overconfident on early ante-1/2 decisions. The Lucky-card EV is in the solver est but not in `hands` est, so the two numbers differ by 2-3x for the same play (Pair JS JC 18130 vs 7770).
