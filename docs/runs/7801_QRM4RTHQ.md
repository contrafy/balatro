## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: MONEY, turned into scaling. Ante 1 offered no clean joker; the Buffoon pack held only two perishable rentals, and the ante-2 shop's only scorer was Smiley Face{ETERNAL,RENTAL}. From the ante-1 Big on, every played round cost $6-9 of rent against rewards of $0 (Small) / $4 (Big) / $5 (Boss), so the wallet never rose above $5 and went to $-13 after The Tooth. I never bought a planet, never saw an affordable xMult, and arrived at ante 4 with two flat jokers (face Two Pair ~2400, face FH ~4230) against 9000/13500/18000. The scoring itself was fine for what it had: every blind through ante 3 was cleared, mostly by Full Houses drawn on the last hand or by KKQQ-type face Two Pairs.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 Buffoon pack: Clever{PERISHABLE,RENTAL} was correct for survival (it cleared the Big 528/450 and the Manacle 651/600 and carried antes 2-3), but it set up the rent spiral. The alternative, skipping the pack and keeping $5 for the next shop, would have faced the 450 Big jokerless. I would still take it; the real fix is below.
2. Ante 2 shop: Smiley Face{ETERNAL,RENTAL} $1. It won antes 2-3 (every clearing hand had 2-4 faces) but it is a permanent -$3/round that can never be stopped: over the run it cost $21 of rent and guaranteed negative money. With hindsight the alternative (reroll for $5 was impossible at $5... it was buy Smiley or die at 1000), so the decision stands, but it means the run's only path forward was skipping every Small/Big blind for rent, which starves scaling.
3. Ante 2 Arcana pack ($4 -> High Priestess -> Neptune/Jupiter): $2 of planets I could not use. Keeping the $4 would not have bought anything either (next shops had $0 purchasing power), so it was a free roll that missed; Judgement/Justice/Chariot were the targets.
4. Ante 3: buying Raised Fist (a third rental) was necessary to reach 6400, and it did (5600 hand). The alternative, selling Clever as the advisor said, would have made the Tooth unreachable.
5. The Tooth cost $10 of card play; with $0 it pushed money to $-19 and the ante-4 Scary Face ($4) out of reach. Playing the Ace pair with 2 fillers ($4) and a 5-card Two Pair ($5) were needed for the score; the only saving available was ~$2.

What worked (reusable):
- Keeping the trips/pair and discarding 5 at ante 1 with no joker: FH 55599 one-shot (292) at the Small, FH 555JJ (620) at the Manacle after two junk digs, FH KKKQQ (4930) at the ante-2 Big. Jokerless Black Deck Gold ante 1 is won by Full Houses, not by banking 135-point trips.
- Face-pair engine with Smiley + Clever + Raised Fist: KKQQ Two Pair = 5600 at ante 3 (est exact). Raised Fist's "hold three cards of 8+" rule (play the pair plus the lowest fillers, keep T/J/Q/K/A) added +16-20 mult on every hand.
- Skipping Small/Big blinds while owning rentals: at -$6..-$9 rent per round and $0/$4 rewards, each skipped round was +$6..+$9 and one more live round for the perishable scorer. The Boss tags rerolled The Hook -> The Water -> The Tooth; the double skip delivered Clever alive to the boss, which was the difference (704 + 5600 + 144 = 6448/6400).
- Under The Tooth: the final 96 points were covered by a lone Ace (1 card, $1) instead of a pair ($2).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own a rental and the Small Blind pays $0 (Red Stake+), THEN skip the Small unless its shop would buy a scorer with the money you actually have: the skip saves the rent tick, saves a perish tick, and removes the risk. With 2+ rentals (-$6/round) ALSO skip the Big: $4 reward - $6 rent is negative, and the shop after it has no money.
- IF your only affordable scorer is a perishable rental at ante 1-2, THEN buy it and plan the skips above so it stays alive for the boss blinds (it ticks only on played rounds).
- IF a joker is ETERNAL+RENTAL, THEN it is a permanent -$3/round: buy it only when the alternative is losing the next blind (as here), and expect to never buy anything again unless a money tag appears; from that point every shop decision is "sell planets/junk for $1 to afford the $1 rental scorer".
- IF you hold a made Pair/Trips at ante 1 with no joker and est < need, THEN discard the other 5 cards once and play the FH if it comes; a banked Trips (135) leaves two hands for 165 with the same cards.
- IF you own Raised Fist, THEN play the pair plus your LOWEST cards as fillers (5 cards) and keep three cards of rank 8+ (T/J/Q/K/A all count 10-11): +16..+22 mult on every hand; a 4-card Two Pair that holds a 4 gets only +8.
- IF The Tooth is the boss and a 1-card High Card covers the remaining need, THEN play the single card: each extra card is -$1.
- IF a Boss tag is on a Small/Big you were going to skip anyway, THEN the reroll is free; the boss it produces is random, so do not skip for the reroll alone.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- `shopadvise` ranked Clever{PERISHABLE,RENTAL} as the SELL target three shops in a row ("2 rentals cost $6 every round, more than the blinds pay") while it was the +80 chips that made every Two Pair 2-3x bigger and it had 1-3 rounds left; the rent logic should compare the rent to the scorer's contribution to the next blind, not to the blind payout.
- `advise` filler choice: at the ante-1 Big it proposed `play TWOPAIR 1 2 5 6 7` with 7 = 2S while 22 was a held pair (breaks the pair the next hand needs). Same defect as in 7801_AJAKNU2C.
- `advise` at the ante-1 Big proposed keeping 3H with the 22 pair (disc 1 2 3 4 5) instead of the Ace; the kept third card should be the highest rank (it pairs for more chips).
- Blind-select skip ADVICE ignores rent entirely ("do not skip: the Boss Tag tag is not worth a shop visit") at $0 with -$9 rent per round; at Gold with rentals the Small's shop visit is worth $0 and the round costs rent.
- Solver `p_clear` lines appeared only from the ante-1 boss on; at the ante-1 Small/Big the plain PACE line said "pacing" for Trips 135 vs 300 with no probability. Fine, but the solver was clearly better (it found the 15% FH dig that won the Manacle).
- `shopadvise` ante 2 listed Smiley Face{ETERNAL,RENTAL} at -2 ("NEEDED NOW") and advised LEAVE; with Two Pair ~276 vs a 1000 Small it was the only line to survive, and the ADVICE should have been the buy (its own EMERGENCY line said so).
- Bridge: no failures; 56 steps, every action settled first try, no rejects.
