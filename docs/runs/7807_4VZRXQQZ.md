## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: scaling AND money. The whole run was carried by two $1 RENTAL jokers (Banner foil, flat +4 Joker) and Flush/Straight hands; there was never an xMult, no planet, no permanent joker. Rent (-$3 x2 per round) plus Gold's $0 Small blind payout put me at negative money from ante 2 on, so the ante 2 -> 3 shops (Idol, Arrowhead, Riff-raff, Popcorn) were all unaffordable. The Banner (+110 chips) made a pair worth 744-840, which hid how weak the build was until the 3.2x jump to ante 3.
Decisions I would change:
- Ante 2 first shop ($8): I rerolled to $3 and bought a $1 rental Joker. That left $2 with two rentals charging $6/round. Better: sell Banner right after the Small blind scored (or never hold two rentals); the debt (-$9 at ante 2 boss) made every later shop dead. A rental at ante 2 with <$10 in the bank is a pure loan against the next shop.
- Ante 1 shop 2: bought Banner with $5 and kept only $4. Fine for survival, but I never planned the exit: by the ante 2 Small I should have had a plan for rent (sell one of them as soon as a flush cleared it in one hand).
- Ante 3 Small: skipped for Charm tag with money -$2; Emperor/Empress/Chariot/Temperance did nothing for a 4800 blind. If you are broke and below boss/3, no skip tag rescues the run, so the decision was irrelevant; the real mistake was upstream.
Reusable rules:
- If money is below $5 and you own a rental joker, rent will take it negative; negative money blocks ALL shop buys. Sell rentals before the round ends if cash would fall below $0 (a sale returns $1 and stops -$3/round).
- If Banner (+30 chips per remaining discard) is owned, do not spend discards unless needed: each discard removes 30 chips from every later hand; with 0 discards it is nearly worthless.
- Flush/Straight with a +chips/+mult flat board clears ante 1-2 (all five blinds cleared with Flush/Straight/Two Pair), but the ante-3 jump (3200/4800/6400) needs xMult or levels; nothing in a flat 2-joker board cleared 4800.
- Under The Fish: after a played hand the new cards are face-down; discard the face-down cards (keep the visible ones) when the visible hand is still enough, and check that the last hand only needs a single high card before spending resources.

It did not warn that rent would push money negative and block all shops (money -$2 shown but no NEGATIVE-MONEY warning; "LEAVE: nothing worth buying at this money level" at -$3 while Shoot the Moon/Idol were on offer).
