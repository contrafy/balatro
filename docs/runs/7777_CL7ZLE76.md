Post-mortem: binding constraint was BOSS + BUILD MISMATCH, compounded by bad luck. We bought a single-suit joker (Greedy Joker, Diamonds) at ante 1 before knowing the ante-1 boss, which is standard playbook advice (any scoring joker beats none) - but ante 1's boss (The Window) happened to debuff exactly that suit, and the deck kept dealing diamond-heavy hands all boss round, so both the joker's trigger AND a large fraction of raw chips were nullified hand after hand. No Luchador or suit-counter was available in either shop to sell/bypass the debuff. Unlike the stake-1 run's losses (which were scaling/economy problems many antes in), this was an early single-blind bad-luck/build-collision loss: a single-suit joker is a known risk for exactly this reason.
Decision I would change: none of the individual decisions (buy first scoring joker at ante 1, discard debuffed cards first, play the best available hand each time) deviated from the playbook; the outcome was driven by (a) an unlucky diamond-heavy deal against a diamond-debuffing boss, and (b) having no way to hedge against it (no Luchador/Director's Cut offered, no money to buy insurance even if it had appeared, since Greedy Joker + Crafty Joker consumed most of the ante-1-2 budget). If replaying this exact situation, buying a joker that isn't tied to a specific suit (even a less immediately powerful one) when facing an unknown ante-1 boss would remove this specific failure mode, at the cost of slightly weaker early scoring on average.
Bridge issues: none - all commands succeeded, no retries needed. Playbook suggestions:
1. Add an explicit exception to the ante 1-2 "buy the first scoring joker" rule: if the shop's only scoring-joker option is a single-suit joker (Greedy/Lusty/Wrathful/Gluttonous), check `shopadvise`'s upcoming-boss warning before committing - if it doesn't fire, fine, but be aware this is the one class of ante-1 joker that a same-ante boss can fully neutralize (both the trigger AND the raw chips of that suit), unlike generic +mult/+chips jokers which only lose a joker-value increment when debuffed.
2. Consider having `shopadvise`'s boss-debuff warning also fire pre-emptively at the VERY FIRST ante-1 shop (before any purchase), not just after a conflicting joker is already bought, since by ante 1 shop 1 the boss is already known from blind-select.
