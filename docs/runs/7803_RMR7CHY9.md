## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling, then a perishable engine. No permanent +mult joker was offered at ante 1-2 (only barred $1
rentals Jolly/Scholar), so the run lived on Straights (Devious +100, Saturn) and perishable bridges (Greedy, Mystic Summit,
Wrathful) until ante 5. From ante 5 a found combo carried it: Blueprint (free from a Rare Tag) copying Photograph, plus a $4
PERISHABLE Hanging Chad = x4 per trigger, three triggers = x64 on the FIRST scoring card when it is a face (Two Pair 25k,
Straights 104k-183k, Acrobat final hand 538k). Chad's 5 rounds were stretched over antes 5-7 by skipping 3 blinds
(Handy, Negative, ante-7 Small+Big), so its last round was The Hook. Ante 8 without Chad: x4 only, ~15-40k per hand vs
200k. No second retrigger (Sock and Buskin, Seltzer, Dusk, another Chad) or permanent xMult appeared in 4 rerolls/packs.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 Big shop: Celestial $4 (missed Saturn -> Venus) left $1 before The Tooth; the Tooth then put me at -$7 and the
   first ante-5 shop had $0. Keeping $5 would have bought Hanging Chad without selling Mystic Summit.
2. Ante 6-7: once Chad was known to carry everything, every spare $ should have gone to rerolls for a permanent retrigger
   (Sock and Buskin / Dusk / Seltzer) or Arcana for Mult/Glass faces; I spent ~$15 on Pluto, a Crafty pick and a Standard
   pack instead. Ante 8 needed a 5x jump the shops could not give by then.
3. Ante 7: skipping Small and Big gave up two shops (~$10-15) to keep Chad for The Hook. It cleared the Hook, but the
   ante-8 shop then had $8. Probably still right (Big 165k without Chad was ~10% to clear).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Photograph AND Hanging Chad (or Blueprint/Brainstorm copying Photograph), THEN `move hand` a face card to
  position 1 before every play: Chad retriggers the FIRST scoring card, Photograph doubles each trigger (x8, x64 with a
  copier). Everything that adds mult BEFORE that card (hand level, a suit +mult joker on that card) is multiplied too;
  +mult on later cards and joker-phase +mult (Mystic Summit, Even Steven on later cards) is not. Sell such flat jokers.
- IF The Mark (faces face-down) is the boss, THEN `sort suit` and read where each face-down card lands: it sorts by its
  real suit/rank, so a face-down card before your Ten of spades is a spade J/Q/K. A face-down card IS a face: lead with it.
  Play the uncertain face-downs first and keep the certain made hand for last (I banked 2240 then the Flush 47840).
- IF a Rare Tag is on the ante-3 Small and your shop money is under $8, THEN skipping it is fine: the free rare (Blueprint
  here) arrives in the shop before the boss.
- IF a Handy Tag is on the ante-5 Big while you are at $0 with 20+ hands played, THEN skipping it (+$21 here) beats the
  $5 Big reward plus an unusable $5 shop, and the perishables do not tick.
- IF your only big multiplier is a perishable joker (Hanging Chad x8-x64), THEN skip Small/Big blinds (tags pay now) so its
  last rounds are bosses, AND spend every shop on its permanent replacement before its final round; the run dies the
  ante after it perishes.
