## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: money, then scaling. Two rentals (Madness -$3, eternal Hanging Chad -$3) cost $6 per round against ~$4/round of income once the Small Blind pays $0 at Gold; from the ante-5 Small on I was at -$5..-$13 and could not buy ANY of the seven scoring items the shops offered (Acrobat $1, Mercury $3, Empress $3, Mega Celestial $8, Zany foil $6, Wee Joker{E} $8...). Scaling was then Madness alone (+0.5 per Small/Big: x3.5 at the ante-6 Big) on Pair L5 with three eternal flat jokers; face pairs made 24-28k, the two Bonus Kings (Hierophant via Chad x3) 38-49k, and 90000 needed three such hands in one round. The build was rescued at antes 3-5 by Riff-raff (free Summit/Photograph for The Flint), Madness being unable to eat eternals, and Hierophant+Chad; it died because nothing permanent was added after ante 4.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 Big shop ($23): Telescope $10 + Hanging Chad{E,R} $1. Chad carried the run (every big hand was Chad x3 on a Bonus K) but its -$3/round forever on top of Madness's rent is what bankrupted me. Buying Telescope and never affording a second Celestial was the real waste: $10 for one guaranteed Mercury ($4) = one Pair level. Keeping $10 would have bought Acrobat{P,R} $1 (x3 final hand) + Mercury $3 + Empress $3 at ante 5.
2. Ante 5 Small: with -$6/round rent and the Small paying $0, playing it netted -$5 and +0.5 Madness. Skipping Small AND Big at ante 5 was wrong too (Mouth at x1.5). The real fix is earlier: never hold two rentals when the Small pays $0 (2b rule, which I broke knowingly for SIM +0.20).
3. Ante 3 Rare shop: Riff-raff $6 was right (it made Summit + Photograph for The Flint). The Family{E} $0 was correctly passed.
4. Ante 6 Big: no line reached 90000 once the two discards found no K; with Madness the only xMult, ante 6 needed Pair L8+ or a second xMult, both unaffordable.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Madness and 3+ ETERNAL jokers, THEN Madness is a free growth xMult: it can only destroy non-eternal jokers (verified: Riff-raff, then nothing, x3.5 by the ante-6 Big) and keeps growing +0.5 at every Small/Big select even with nothing left to eat. Sell the remaining non-eternal scorers for cash the shop before they would be eaten.
- IF you own Hanging Chad and a Bonus/Glass/Mult face card, THEN `move hand` that card to the FIRST position of the play: KH(bonus) first = +120 chips +15 mult (31250 at x2.5 vs 20000 for a plain KK); est only shows it after the move.
- IF you own Raised Fist, THEN play low cards as fillers so the held cards are all T+ (Fist +20) or A/K (+22): KK+3 fillers 9375 vs KK alone 6500 at ante 4; a held 2 costs 16 mult. Count the fillers: two low cards of the same rank cannot both be fillers (the play becomes Two Pair).
- IF money is negative, THEN every shop is dead ($0 items included: the buy check is dollars >= cost); only tags (free packs) and Madness growth scale you. Two rentals at Gold (Small pays $0) = -$6/round vs ~$4/round income: NEVER hold two rentals past ante 4, even for SIM +0.20.
- IF The Mouth locks Pair and two hands remain, THEN plan the LAST hand's Fist: play the pair whose partner can still be drawn as a Bonus card (kept KC, drew KH = 31250) and dump the low cards as fillers of the second-to-last hand.
- IF the SCORE CHECK is < 0.5 at ante 4+ and the only xMult is Madness, THEN the next ante needs Pair levels every shop (Celestial $4 = +12%); buy them BEFORE any voucher (Telescope was -$10 for +1 level).
