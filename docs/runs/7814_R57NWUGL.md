## Post-mortem

**Binding constraint:** economy and scoring at ante 3. Two cheap planets and a Buffoon pack were bought, but only one permanent non-rental scorer (Zany/Crazy were Straight/Trips-gated) existed at The Mark. Money was $2 entering The Mark after the Big Blind, so no further shop purchases; the ratio to The Mark was 0.34-0.45 for the whole shop sequence.

**Decisions I would change:**
1. Ante 2 Shop 3 ($12) passed Jumbo Celestial $6 (Runner $5 and Death $3 also LEAVE). A planet there would have added score before the 4800 Big; the $12 held was never converted into score.
2. Shoot the Moon (rental, $1) perished with 2 rounds left at The Mark and cost about $3/round in rent while held. Its +13 mult per Queen rarely showed up on the Two Pair/Trips hands played, so it was a weak scorer.
3. Riff-raff sat in a 6/6 slot through ante 2 and produced nothing; it was sold for $3 at ante 3, after Shoot the Moon took the last slot. Selling it earlier would have freed money for the Jumbo Celestial.
4. Big Blind 4800: chose Two Pair 5678 (est 3036) over the solver line; the 704 High Card banking play at 3740 left only one hand (needed 1060, got 2222 FH). Correct move was to keep both hands and discard toward a Full House, which had 2222 est with the three queens in hand.

**Reusable if-then rules:**
- If the Gold ante-2 shop has $12 and a Jumbo Celestial $6 is offered with no permanent scorer, buy it before the Big Blind; holding money for a floor you cannot reach at ante 3 loses to the score ratio.
- If a Shoot the Moon-style rental perishes within 3 rounds of a boss, do not count it as a scorer for that boss.
- If The Mark is the ante-3 boss, face cards are face-down: plan for a Pair/Trips of unknown faces plus known number cards, and count only 4-of-a-kind-able hands (Full House) as the finisher.
- If a discard can draw toward Full House with 3 queens held and 2 discards left, prefer it over High Card banking when both show p ≥ 0.99.
