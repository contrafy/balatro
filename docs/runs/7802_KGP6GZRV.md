## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING and MONEY together. The whole run saw one xMult offer (Seeing Double from an ante-4 Buffoon pack, conditional on a club scoring) and zero reliable ones in 9 shops and 3 packs. Every scorer except Banner/Summit was temporary (Onyx rental, Mad perishable, Popcorn decay, Blue Joker perishable, Gros Michel rental), so each ante I was rebuying the same +15-20 mult instead of stacking. Money never exceeded $21 (once, after the Skip Tag) and sat at $0-9 otherwise because the Small Blind pays $0 at Gold and rent cost $3/round; the ante-5 shops had $4 and nothing affordable. At the ante-5 Big my Two Pair without a club was 4.4-4.9k against 37500 (12.5k/hand needed); even with the x2 it was 9-10k. The ante-4 -> 5 jump (18000 -> 37500 Big) needs ~x2 on top of a x2-every-hand engine; a build whose only xMult fires on 68% of hands and whose chips come from a perishable Blue Joker was never going to clear it.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 second shop: Golden Joker{ETERNAL} $6 (+$4/round, 16 rounds = $64) over Mystic Summit $5. Summit carried antes 2-4, but the run died of money: with Golden I would have had ~$30-40 by ante 4 to buy the xMults/planets the shops did offer later (The Family at ante 2 was the only joker-main xMult seen; a Jumbo Arcana/Celestial each ante). Summit also fights Banner (one wants 0 discards, the other 2). Unclear, but money was the deeper problem.
2. Ante 1: The Family{ETERNAL} foil $10 at the ante-2 first shop was passed for Mad ($4). Mad was right for the Mouth, but a foil (+50 chips always) eternal x4-on-quads joker would have been a permanent scorer; I never found another permanent +chips.
3. Ante 4 first shop: two bridges (Blue Joker $10 + Gros rental) spent the whole $13 and the rent pushed money negative. It cleared ante 4 (needed), but the ante-5 money hole was then guaranteed. The alternative (Gros only + hold $12) risked the 13500 Big. No clear fix: the ante-3 reroll spend ($10 for Uranus) is where $10 of future money went.
4. Hand play was fine: every blind through ante 4 was cleared by est-exact hands; the ante-5 Big deal (KK, two 5-card redraws with no third pair and no club in 18 cards seen) was the loss, but the build was already 3x short.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the Small Blind's tag pays now (Skip $10, Orbital, Economy, Garbage) AND you own 2+ scorers AND the shop after the Small would have under $5 (Small pays $0 at Gold, rent due) THEN skip it: every skip in this run saved $3 rent, a perish tick and a Popcorn decay step, and the shop it cost would have bought nothing. Garbage Tag pays $1 per unused discard this run (9 here): count them before deciding.
- IF you hold three pairs after the first discard (KK TT 77) THEN discard only the 2 spare cards: all three pairs stay, Summit turns on, and the round is two Two Pairs plus a guaranteed Pair floor (15950/13500 at ante 4).
- IF Mystic Summit is your main +mult THEN burn both discards before the first play even on a made Two Pair; the +15 is worth more than Banner's +60 chips once mult is above ~15 (Two Pair 55x27 vs 115x12).
- IF The Mouth is the boss and a made Two Pair scores >= 95% of the blind with Banner at 2 discards THEN lock it immediately: any later Two Pair (>= 480) finishes with 2 hands + 2 discards (~95%); discarding first costs Banner chips and drops the lock hand by 25%.
- IF Seeing Double is your xMult THEN keep club pairs over non-club pairs when choosing what to hold (QQQ with QC and 999 with 9C both fired x2 at the Psychic; KKQQ with no club at the Big did not), and treat its hand est as 0.68 x the x2 value when pacing.
- IF money is <= $4 at ante 5 with a rental owned THEN the run is lost on money regardless of hands: the only shops left have $0-5. Spend ante-3 money (the one $21 shop) on permanence (Golden Joker, a non-sticker scorer) rather than on a perishable bridge + rent.
- IF a Full House is leveled (Orbital tag) THEN every discard decision should keep trips draws alive: FH L4/L5 one-shot 568, 8360, and was the only hand that would have cleared ante 5 (FH L5 x2 = 21k).
