## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. Build was flat (Even Steven +4/even, Half Joker +20, Gros Michel +15, Sly +50 chips, Hanging Chad + Splash) with ZERO xMult and Pair L1: ~6-11k per hand at ante 5 vs 25k Small (needs 8.3k x 3 hands every hand). Never saw an xMult in ~8 shops/packs (3 Buffoon packs at ante 3-4 gave Half Joker, Sly/Smeared, Diet Cola/Business Card). Money stayed at $2-15 (Gold economy: Small pays $0, rerolls/packs ate cash).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 Hook: `play HIGH 2 1 3 7` with fillers scored KS (the highest rank among played cards, High Card scores only the best card) instead of 8D: lost ~900 chips of a 2000 blind. For High Card with fillers the SCORING card is the highest rank played, not the card I listed first; use `sel` and read `scoring [..]` before sending, or play the single card alone.
2. Sold Hiker (permanent +chips per scored card, retriggered 3x on the first card by Chad) for Gros Michel and then Ice Cream for Sly. Hiker-boosted cards (TH) scored 11k vs 5-6k for plain pairs (Pair 88+TH 11172 vs Pair 88+7H 6042); I never leveled or kept Hiker long enough to build more of them. With Chad+Splash Hiker was the only growth I had.
3. Spent ~$20 on 3 rerolls hunting xMult at ante 3-4 and nothing came; also bought no planets (Pair L1 at ante 5 is +0 growth). A Pair-level Celestial pack at $4 each shop for ante 3-4 would have given Pair L4-5 (+45-60 chips x +3-4 mult), probably +20-30% on every hand: not enough for a 25k Small but would have kept money-less runs alive.
4. Took Droll (flush-only) at ante 1 over Mad Joker on SIM advice; worked out (Flush hit) but it perished before ante 3 and left nothing permanent.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Even Steven + Hanging Chad (+ Splash), THEN the best play is often a 1-3 card hand led by an even card (Chad triples its ES +4 mult, and Half Joker +20 stays on at <=3 cards): a single even 10 scored 1000-6000 at ante 2-4. Put the best even card FIRST in hand order (`move hand`) and use `sel` to confirm the scoring list.
- IF High Card is played with fillers, THEN the highest-RANK card scores, not the card you intend; the filler list must contain only LOWER ranks than your scoring card.
- IF `advise` says REORDER for Chad, THEN do it (11130 vs 7950 with a Hiker card first); do NOT guess the move direction: `move hand FROM TO` takes the card at FROM and puts it at TO (I once moved TH from slot 1 to 5 by typing the wrong order and had to undo it).
- IF flat build with 0 xMult at ante 4 and round est < 2x the ante-5 Small (25000), THEN the run is already dead by est: the last ante-4 shops must find an xMult/Blueprint/Brainstorm or sell for Photograph-like multipliers, not +chips; buy Celestial packs for the main hand rather than Ice Cream-type flat chips.
- Strength from an Arcana pack is a permanent rank +1 on two deck cards (odd -> even for Even Steven); small value.
