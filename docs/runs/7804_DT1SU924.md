## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: MONEY at ante 1-2, which became SCALING at the first ante-2 boss. Shoot the Moon (+13 mult per
Queen held, rental -$3/round) was the only mult source. It is all-or-nothing: with 2 Queens held a Pair made
900-1400, with none a flush made ~330. At The Club (clubs debuffed, so QC was dead too) I drew 1 Queen in the whole
round. The economy never recovered: $4 start, $1 after ante-1 shop 1, rent ate $9 over 3 rounds, and I entered every
shop with $1-7. No chips joker, no planet, no level-up all run.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop 1: buying the Buffoon pack with $5 left me at $1 and the pack was junk (eternal Oops / rental
   Burglar). With a rental scorer already eating $3/round, the $4 should have stayed in the wallet (it became the
   ante-2 shop's reroll/joker money). If I did open it, Burglar (+3 hands, no discards) was worth more than skipping:
   6 hands at The Club with STM would likely have cleared it.
2. Ante 1 shop 2: I overrode the advisor's Ice Cream{RENTAL} ($1, +100 chips decaying 5/hand). Shoot the Moon is pure
   mult; a flat +chips joker multiplies against it. At The Club the final flush with Ice Cream (~+85 chips) would have
   been (82+85) x 17 ~ 2840 instead of 1564 - a clear. Two rentals for 2-3 rounds (~$9 extra) was the right price.
3. Ante 2 shop with D6: the first $0 reroll threw away a polychrome Ride the Bus{RENTAL} for $1 (x1.5 always via
   polychrome, plus +mult on faceless hands). With $3 a $1 polychrome joker beats any reroll.
4. Sock and Buskin ($6) added only face card chips (+20 per face pair). A $6 buy that adds ~+40% to one hand type
   at ante 2 with $7 total was weak; but nothing else in that shop scored.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF your only scorer is Shoot the Moon (or any pure +mult joker), THEN the next buy is a +chips joker (Ice Cream,
  Sly, Wee, Stuntman, Hiker...), even a $1 rental: chips x (13 per Queen) is multiplicative; one more Queen is luck.
- IF you already pay rent on one joker at ante 1 and hold <= $6, THEN do not buy a $4 pack; keep the money for the
  next shop's $3-5 scorer. Rent + $0 Small payouts leave no buffer for a junk pack.
- IF a shop shows a polychrome (x1.5) joker for $1 (rental), THEN buy it before any reroll: it is an xMult at ante 1-2.
- IF The Club / any suit boss debuffs the suit of a card your joker needs held (QC for Shoot the Moon), THEN count
  only the live copies when estimating your odds; plan for the worst case (0 Queens) and keep a chips source.
- IF you own Shoot the Moon, THEN at the boss do not play Queens, and dig with plays of 2-3 junk cards before using
  the last discard; it still needs 2 Queens held to make ~2000 on L1 hands - it cannot carry an ante-2 boss alone.

Advisor/CLI defects:

  (Buffoon $4) leaving $1.

  outcome is bimodal (Queens held or not) and The Club debuffs QC, so the projection overstated a ~50/50 round.
