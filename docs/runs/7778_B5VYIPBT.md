Post-mortem: Binding constraint was scaling, specifically that Card Sharp (my only xMult source) is structurally worthless under The Needle: its "x3 if this hand type already played this round" condition can never trigger when the boss allows exactly one hand for the entire blind. Without that x3, Two Pair sat at L2 (40x3) which was too low to clear 2000 even stacked with Clever Joker (+80 chips) and Even Steven. The decision I would change: at the ante-3 shops, prioritize a second, unconditional scaling source (a Two Pair level via Uranus, or a flat +mult/+chips joker) instead of a third Saturn/Straight level and a random Judgement pull, since Straight's L6 level was never usable this ante (never had 5 cards toward a straight when it mattered) while Two Pair carried every actual win.
