## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING, specifically the total absence of any xMult joker or joker edition for the entire run
(6 antes, dozens of reroll/Wheel-of-Fortune attempts, zero hits). The engine (Two Pair L3 / Full House L3 / Pair L4,
6 flat/conditional +mult+chips jokers) scaled additively and cleared antes 1-5 with 1.3x-3x margins, but The House's
40000 requirement (2.67x the Big Blind's 30000, itself already needing 2 discard-fueled hands to clear) was simply
out of reach for a hand that peaked around 11-12k per play with no multiplicative layer. The Justice-tarot rescue on
the Big Blind's final hand (924-point margin) was a preview of exactly this problem one blind earlier.
Decision I would change: at ante 5-6, with $22-26 in hand at various points and zero xMult after 40+ reroll/pack
opportunities logged across this run, I should have treated ANY xMult-shaped joker (even a conditional one like
Ride the Bus or Card Sharp, which I turned down twice for reliability reasons) as mandatory once one appeared,
rather than continuing to hold out for a "reliable" one that never showed. The playbook's own guidance ("take a
conditional xMult as a placeholder if none has appeared by ante 3") was not followed here because no xMult joker of
ANY kind (reliable or conditional) was ever offered in a shop slot -- only in the reroll-blocked-by-cash situations.
In hindsight, sustained rerolling past the "$15 left after" threshold (e.g. spending down to $2-3 hunting harder at
ante 4-5 when money was flush at $17-26) might have surfaced one; the additive-only engine was simply not going to
clear a 2.67x boss multiplier.
Bridge issues: `cash` returned "ACTION FAILED: Round eval blocking events still pending, retry shortly" 3 times this
run (ante 4 twice, ante 6 once) -- retried after `sleep 2` each time and always succeeded on the 2nd attempt, per
playbook's retry rule. Opening a joker Buffoon Pack while jokers are at 6/6 leaves ONLY `SKIP_PACK` as a legal action
(no `SELECT_PACK_CARD`), silently wasting the pack's cost -- this is not called out in the playbook and cost $4 in
ante 4. Buying a Planet/consumable-slot item (e.g. Uranus) while both consumable slots are full fails with
"ACTION FAILED: buy_from_shop returned false: no space or card not purchasable" -- fixed by `use`-ing or
`sellc`-ing a held consumable first to free a slot.
Playbook suggestions:
1. Add to section 6 (bosses): The House's face-down effect applies ONLY to the initial hand dealt at blind-select;
   discarding face-down cards draws normal face-up replacements immediately, so discard aggressively rather than
   trying to "play around" face-down cards.
2. Add a rule: before opening any Buffoon/joker-choice pack, check joker slot count first -- if 6/6, sell a joker
   BEFORE opening the pack, not after, since a full-slots pack only offers SKIP_PACK once opened (verified twice
   this run: once wasted $4, once required using a held consumable to free a slot before a direct planet purchase).
3. Consider hardening the "if 0 xMult owned and $15+ after reroll, REROLL" rule at ante 4+ into "if $15+ after reroll
   AND jokers not full, reroll; if jokers full, sell your weakest conditional-PLUS joker for room first" -- this run
   repeatedly had cash available ($17-26) but no free joker slot at the moment an xMult candidate might have
   appeared, and by the time a slot was freed the shop had moved on.
