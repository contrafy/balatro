## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. The additive base was tiny: after Gros Michel went extinct at ante 5, the only +mult was
Abstract (+18) and the only chips were Sly (+50, pair hands only). Obelisk (x3.4-4.2) x Ramen (x2) multiplied about 21
mult on 30-100 chips. Obelisk also made the common types unplayable: with Burglar (6 hands, 0 discards) every round
played 6 hands, and by ante 7 High Card, Pair and Two Pair were all tied at 7 plays. Any hand I could make reset it,
so the Small Blind came down to the 1-2 Flush/Full House/Quads hands I happened to draw.

Decisions I would change:
1. High Card digs (7 of them, antes 5-7) and two Pluto picks. I treated High Card as an unlimited Obelisk-safe filler and
   stopped counting. It reached the max tie at ante 7 and the last common safe type was gone. Those two planet buys
   ($8) and the levels should have gone to a +mult joker.
2. Ante 7 Campfire into a full 6/6 board. With no free slot and no consumables in the shop, it stayed x1 the whole
   round: a dead slot. Zany (+12 mult on Trips/FH, the types Obelisk still allowed) or keeping the money was better.
3. Ante 5-6: I spent rerolls hunting for xMults while my additive base shrank, and passed Raised Fist{E} and Ice Cream.
   With two xMults already owned, a flat +mult/+chips joker was worth more than a third xMult hunt.
4. Ante 2: Sly{E} (pair-only chips) later fought Obelisk, which punishes pair plays. It was the right buy at the time,
   but it was a permanent slot that did nothing once Obelisk arrived.

What worked: Popcorn and Mystic Summit as ante-1 bridges (burn both discards, then play the made hand). Investment tag
skip at ante 3 ($25), Hermit at $28, and Burglar + Ramen (0 discards = Ramen never decays). Free resets while Obelisk
was x1.0 built a Two Pair lead. Skipping the ante-6 Big so a 1-round Blueprint copied Obelisk at the Manacle cleared
120k (it would have scored about half without it).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Obelisk, THEN write down the play count of every type each round (the advisor's OBELISK WARNING lists
  ties). High Card digs COUNT. Never let 3 common types (High, Pair, Two Pair) tie at the max: when two are within 1
  of the max, reset while Obelisk is <= x1.4 and repeat that type while it sits at x1.0 (free resets) to build a 3+ lead.
- IF you own Obelisk AND Burglar (0 discards), THEN the safe types (Flush/Straight/FH/Trips) cannot be dug for, so
  Obelisk caps around x4 by ante 7. Do not treat it as the ante-8 engine: buy flat +mult/+chips that fire on any hand.
- IF your additive mult is a single joker at ante 5+, THEN the next buy is a flat +mult/+chips joker, not a growth
  xMult that needs feeding.
- IF you buy Campfire, THEN you need a free joker slot ($1 rentals) or 2 consumable slots plus shop tarots/planets to
  sell. A full board means Campfire stays x1.
- IF a perishable copy/xMult has exactly 1 round left before the boss AND the boss is the hardest blind, THEN skip the
  Big so its last round is the boss (worked: Blueprint x Obelisk at the Manacle).
- IF Mystic Summit is owned, THEN burn both discards on junk first, then play the made hand (cleared a2 Big, a3 Big
  and Hook in one hand each).
