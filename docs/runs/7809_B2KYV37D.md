## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. Zero xMult all run. 13 shop views at ante 3-5 plus 9 paid rerolls (~$55) and 2 free Chaos
rerolls showed no reliable/growth xMult at all (the only multiplier-ish offers were a foil Brainstorm{RENTAL} with nothing
to copy and Ancient Joker{PERISH} at $8 when I had $2). The build was flat: Clever{E} + Sly{E} foil (+180 chips on Two
Pair) and Swashbuckler{E} + Gros Michel + Mad Joker (~41 mult), Two Pair L2: ~10k per hand at ante 4-5 vs 16.7k needed
per hand at the ante-5 boss. Lucky cards (Magician on 9H 9C at ante 2) were the best "multiplier" of the run: they fired
3 times (684 -> 2964 at The Flint, 9000 -> 14000 at The Pillar, 4433 -> 7293 at The Psychic), each time +20 mult.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 Big shop ($53): 4 paid rerolls ($26) found nothing. GOLD_ANALYSIS rule 7 (rerolls rarely show xMults) held
   exactly. Two Arcana packs ($8) were on offer for 3 shops in a row; for a flat +mult build Magician/Empress (+20 lucky /
   +4 mult cards) and Hermit were worth more than the rerolls.
2. Ante 5: skipping Small AND Big without Throwback sent a 0.25-ratio build into a 50000 boss with zero shops. It did not
   lose a better position (Small 25000 was ~45% and the Big 37500 ~0%), but the real error was earlier: arriving at
   ante 5 with 0 xMult and $20-30 spent on rerolls instead of on cheaper multipliers.
3. Gift Card{ETERNAL,RENTAL}: the idea (+5 Swashbuckler mult per played round) is sound, but I bought it 1 round before
   skipping two blinds, so it ticked once. Growth jokers that tick per round conflict with a skip plan.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Swashbuckler, THEN every joker's sell value is +mult: a $1 rental joker adds +1, Matador/Golden add +3, and
  Gift Card adds +1 per joker per played round. Prefer high-sell jokers when slots are free. Swashbuckler bought at
  ante 2 next to one scorer doubled base mult (2 -> 4) and grew to +18; it was not "useless as the only joker".
- IF The Mark shows face-down cards and you hold a made pair, THEN play the face-downs as the fillers: any pair among them
  upgrades the hand to Two Pair/Full House for free (Pair 77 est 5427 -> Two Pair 7696, one-shot boss).
- IF you have 0 discards left and hold The Hanged Man, THEN destroying 2 junk cards works as an extra discard (the hand
  refills to 8 on the next play) and thins the deck.
- IF the build is flat chips/+mult with no xMult and the main mult is under ~40, THEN Magician (Lucky) on your main-pair
  ranks is a real multiplier (+20 mult = +50% when it fires, 1 in 5 per card); it fired 3 times in 8 plays here.
- IF you own Chaos the Clown, THEN use its free reroll before selling it, and only reroll after buying what you want
  from the current row.
- IF ante 5 starts with SCORE CHECK < 0.3 and no Throwback, THEN skipping Small+Big does not save the run (the boss is
  ~2x your 3-hand ceiling); the decision that matters is two antes earlier: buy packs/tarots that add mult to your cards
  instead of paid rerolls.
