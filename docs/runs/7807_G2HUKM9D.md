## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: boss (The Wall x2) on a build with no real xMult. Final engine: foil Smiley{RENTAL} + Sock and Buskin
(face retriggers) x Joker Stencil{ETERNAL} x3 x Throwback x3.0, Straight L5 / Two Pair L4. It made 166k at the Wall, which
would have cleared a normal ante-6 boss (120k) by 1.38x. I reached the boss with $50 that could buy nothing.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 5 shops ($47 and $29): I passed Director's Cut ($10) twice and spent on Celestials/rerolls. On a Throwback
   skip-to-boss line, skip-tag money (Skip $40, Economy, Garbage) lands at BLIND SELECT, which is exactly where
   `rerollboss` spends it. With Director's Cut, the $50 at the Wall rerolls it 1-5 times into a 120k boss: a likely win.
2. Ante 6: skipping Big for the Skip tag ($40) was correct for Throwback, but only because I had no use for a shop; with
   Director's Cut owned it would have been worth even more.
3. Joker Stencil{ETERNAL} at ante 1 carried antes 1-4 (x6 -> x3), but it makes every flat joker a net ~x1.3 at best,
   so every later buy competed against it. The SIM/advisor handled this well (AVOID flats under Stencil).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you are on the Throwback skip-to-boss line (or will hold $10+ at blind select) AND Director's Cut is offered at
  ante 4-6, THEN buy it before Celestials/rerolls: skip tags pay at blind select, and a Wall/Violet Vessel reroll
  is worth 2x the boss need. 166k lost to The Wall 240k here while $50 sat unused.
- IF The House/The Fish deals you face-down cards, THEN remember the hand is rank-sorted: discard the LOWEST positions
  (rightmost) to dig and keep the leftmost (highest, likely faces); positions between two known cards bound the rank
  (Fish here: fd between JS and TC is J or T -> a guaranteed pair).
- IF you hold Strength and the hand has Q-J-T-9 style gaps, THEN check rank-up combinations before playing: T->J,
  9->T made a 3-face L5 straight (115k) and T,T->J,J made FH JJJQQ (77k at the House). Spend the discard first,
  Strength second (a natural draw saves the tarot).
- IF you own Joker Stencil, THEN planets are slot-free scaling: prefer Celestials/planets and xMults over flat jokers;
  a card-phase xMult (Photograph) loses to the x1 it costs the Stencil.
