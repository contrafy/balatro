Binding constraint: no real mult. All run long I had +4 flat mult and chips jokers (Blue, Square foil) and a decaying Popcorn; hands base mult 2-8 made every hand ~1-2k. Acrobat x3 (perishable) gave one big hand per round but at ante 4 Small (9000) typical hand x3 was ~5k. Money was $0-10 all run (rent, $0 Small, bought $4-6 packs every shop) so I never saw an xMult I could afford: Loyalty Card $5 and Flower Pot $6 shown at ante 4 with $3.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop 2 ($5): bought Jupiter ($3) because Swashbuckler{E} was the only joker. Fine (won Window with Flush L2) but it left me flush-dependent; ante 2 hands were pair-family anyway.
2. Ante 3 Big: played all 3 hands to clear 4800 (spare High Cards) and ended the round with $-3: unused hands pay $1 each. Playing the final hand earlier was impossible (Acrobat needs final), but better to clear with 2 hands when possible. More important: I arrived at ante 4 with $3.
3. Ante 4 shop: sold Popcorn to buy another $4 Buffoon (Square Joker foil, Wily eternal): neither added mult. Should have kept the $4 floor idea: with $3 and Loyalty Card $5/Flower Pot $6 visible, selling Popcorn ($1) + Blue... Honestly the right buy was to sell Square/others for Loyalty Card? Loyalty x4 every 6 hands is unreliable. Real miss: no scaling by end of ante 2-3 (SCORE CHECK was 0.14-0.18 at the ante 3-4 shops).
4. Ante 3 Flint shop (after Big, $1): money gone because of Popcorn rent -$3/round + Buffoon packs at $0 each time.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF a Popcorn/decaying rental gives +20 mult, THEN it carries ante 3 Small/Big only; sell it immediately after the round it hits +8 (rent $3/round vs ~+8 mult), and have the replacement chosen BEFORE it fades.
- IF Acrobat (x3 final hand) is the only xMult, THEN spend hands 1-2 on 1-card High Cards (cheap, est ~1500-2100 with Blue Joker) and keep the best hand for the final: 8844 on Two Pair at ante 3 Big. It stops scaling at ante 4 (9000 Small needs ~3k typical hand).
- IF Blue Joker is owned, THEN a single High Card scores almost like a Pair (+2 chips x ~40 deck cards), so never break a pair/flush draw for it; but chips-only builds die when base mult is 8: buy +mult.
- IF Flint is the boss, THEN est already halves base; Acrobat final Flush still cleared 6400 (7236) after two spare High Cards.
- IF the shop shows money-neutral choices at $4 with 4 jokers and 0 xMult, THEN consider keeping $4-5 for the xMult-looking joker next shop rather than another Buffoon pack that offers only 2 options (Jumbo Buffoon with 4-5 choices was far better than normal Buffoon with 2).
