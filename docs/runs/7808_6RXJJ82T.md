## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scale/variance. Only 3 scorers by ante 2 (Misprint random mult, perishable Joker +4, Baron needing a King held) on Pair/Trips L1 at ~850 a hand; Window needed 2000 in 3 hands with 4+ diamonds debuffed in my hand. Every hand scored 20-75% under est (Misprint low rolls: 690 vs 1345, 882 vs 1165, 336 vs 444).

Decisions I would change:
1. Skipped the ante-2 Small (1000) for the Negative Tag. I had $6, so the skipped shop was cheap, but the tag only gave an Eternal 8 Ball (no scoring), cost me the Small's shop visit, and the Small was clearable. With 3 scorers and zero levels, I should have played it for the shop plus interest. I also had no consumable planet use planned.
2. Passed Mega Celestial $8 at ante 1 and the Jumbo Celestial/Earth later: ended ante 2 with every hand type L1; L1 Trips (est 1165) vs L2-L3 would have covered the 92-point shortfall. A cheap $3 planet for the type I played 3x (Trips) was a free +~15%.
3. Left $7 unspent at the end of ante 2 (Arcana $4 / Earth $3 both affordable): "money does not score". Earth was not a main hand but Mercury/Venus were not offered; an Arcana pack (Strength/Justice rescue) could have fixed the last hand.
4. Misprint is a variance joker: `est` uses the mean (11.5). Plan the round by the roll-0 floor when a boss needs a near-exact total: three plays all came in at 55-75% of est.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF a Negative Tag is the skip reward, THEN the "negative joker" in the next shop is a random shop joker with random stickers (mine: ETERNAL 8 Ball); it is not a free scorer, so skip the Small only when you actually need that visit skipped.
- IF the boss is The Window (or any suit boss) and 4+ cards of that suit are in hand after play 1, THEN discard the debuffed cards first (they only count as hand type), and plan the whole round with the debuffed suit removed from est.
- IF you own a Misprint-style variance joker, THEN judge "clears now" by the roll-0 estimate (it is ~35-45% of est) and prefer the line that leaves a spare hand.
- IF you have $7+ after the last ante-2 shop with no xMult stocked, THEN spend on a planet for the hand you played most (Trips/Pair) rather than keeping it: 92 points short with $7 unspent.
