Post-mortem: Binding constraint = a combination of (a) the ante4 Small-Blind skip, which forfeited a full scoring+shopping cycle right as blind sizes started their steep ante3->4 jump (4000->5000->7500), leaving no buffer shop between a weak final hand and a large Big Blind; and (b) pure card variance - the final hand of the Big Blind had zero paths to any hand better than a pair (no 3+ of a suit, no consecutive run), and with 0 discards and only one weak Tarot (Death) available, there was no way to reach the remaining 3559 chips needed. Money was never banked for interest; every shop dollar went into hand levels per shopadvise, which built strong hand levels (Full House L3, Straight L3) but left zero xMult and only mediocre flat-mult jokers, so one bad final draw was fatal at this blind size.
The one decision I would change: do NOT skip the ante-4 Small Blind for a Foil Tag. Skipping merged what should have been two scoring/shopping cycles into one, denying an extra shop's worth of money and joker options before the jump to a 7500 Big Blind, and the Foil Tag's benefit was never realized because the run ended before the next shop. Given blind sizes roughly double each ante and our joker scaling was still thin (no xMult, ~+20 mult from flats), preserving every scoring opportunity mattered more than a slot-filler tag at this point.
Bridge issues: none - `advise`/`hands`/`shopadvise` all responded normally all run, no ACTION FAILED or Connection refused errors encountered. One `sel`/`use` sequence for Death tarot (targeted, needs 2 highlighted cards via `sel i j` before `use N`) worked exactly as documented in playbook section 1.
Calibration (est vs actual, >20% diff): Ante1 boss The Window, first hand: Pair 7s est672 vs actual 360 (46% low) and second hand: Pair Kings est840 vs actual 450 (46% low). Root cause: The Window debuffs a suit (Diamonds that game); a held Queen of Diamonds was debuffed, and Shoot the Moon's "+13 mult per Queen held" apparently does not fire for a debuffed held card, but `advise`/`hands`'s est calculation counted it anyway both times. No other >20% miscalibrations were observed the rest of the run (Spare Trousers' dynamic per-play growth caused a ~10% est-vs-actual undercount later, not >20%).
Playbook suggestions:
1. `hands`/`advise` est calculations should not count a debuffed held card toward held-card-triggered joker bonuses (e.g. Shoot the Moon, Baron) under suit-debuff bosses (The Window/Club/Goad/Head) - this caused two >20% overestimates in one boss fight in this run.
2. Add explicit guidance for ante 4+: do not skip a Small Blind for a skip-tag (even a priority one like Foil/Holo/Poly) if the run's joker scaling has no xMult and money is below the $25 target - the lost shop/scoring cycle is worth more than the tag reward at that point. Consider gating the "take priority tags" advice in section 5 on money/xMult state, not just free joker slots.
