## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no xMult ever seen in 5 shops/3 packs, and money ($0-8 all run: Gold Small pays $0, one rental ate $3/round from ante 1). The build was Lusty (+3/Heart), Supernova (+~5), Misprint (+0-23) and a perishable Shoot the Moon; hands of ~2.5-3.2k at ante 4 vs 9000 Small (3 hands = ~8k). The run was held up by Shoot the Moon's +26 for two held Queens (Death made a 5th Queen) and Turtle Bean's 13-card hand, both of which expired.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop $5: bought Buffoon ($4) AFTER Shoot the Moon, leaving $0. Fine for survival, but I never had $ for the ante-2 shops ($3, $7): the whole run was priced out of jokers (Gluttonous $5 at ante 2 with $3, Cartomancer...). A ante-1 Hermit/Judgement gamble worked once (Supernova) but money stayed the binding resource.
2. Ante 3 shop ($3 with the Coupon tag): free Misprint was the only scorer; I had 2 empty joker slots and no way to fill them. Skipping the Small for Coupon was right (rent, perish tick, free shop) but a Coupon only helps if there are scorers on offer: it had 1.
3. Ante 4 Small: hand 1 Straight (2400, Misprint low roll), then 2 discards digging flush/straight both missed (p 0.45-0.53 line). A flat 8-9k at ante 4 with no xMult is the cap; nothing in the shops could change that ($8, no jokers shown in the shop at ante 4).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF Shoot the Moon is owned, THEN use Death on a Queen (copy onto the lower slot after `move hand`) and discard to keep Queens in hand: 2 held Queens = +26 mult; High Card 380-500 and Pair 620-1000 at ante 1-2, FH 2960 at the ante-2 Wall.
- IF a Wall/Fish (big boss) is next with a rental/perishable scorer, THEN Turtle Bean ($6, +5 hand size) on the shop before it cleared The Wall (13 cards: 4000 needed, Flush 1440 + FH 2960).
- IF The Fish flips cards face down after the first play, THEN discard all face-down cards (discard draws are face up): worked twice (solver p 0.97-0.98).
- IF Lovers + Sun are in hand under The Fish, THEN make the straight with the Lovers wild card (Straight Flush 100x8 = 3915 actual) and use the Sun on non-played cards for Lusty hearts.
- IF the Charm tag (Mega Arcana) or Coupon tag is on the Small while rent-broke, THEN skip: it saved $3 rent each time and the free packs gave Hermit/Death/Sun/Emperor.
- IF all hearts matter (Lusty), THEN the Sun is good; a 5-heart flush is +15 mult, and flush est 2762 actually scored 3230.
