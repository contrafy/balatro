# Run R2ZPE22S (port 7814, claude-haiku-5-5) - Black Deck Gold Stake - LOSS at ante 3 blind Small (1952/3200)

- Ante 1: Small 300 (Fullhouse 296 + Trips 162), Big 450 (Trips 306 + Straight 616), Boss The Club 600 (708). Bought Buffoon pack -> Seeing Double (x2 suit-gated, perishable) over advisor's To the Moon. Bought Odd Todd (the one rental). Bought nothing else.
- Ante 2: Small 1000 SKIPPED for Charm Tag (advisor ADVICE; Mega Arcana: Hierophant, Lovers). Big 1500 (Two Pair 1056 + 992). Shop: plain eternal Joker +4 mult for $2. Boss The Mouth 2000 (locks first hand type): Flush 1016 then club Flush 1080 cleared it with 0 discards left.
- Ante 3: Small 3200 LOST. Shop $9: Buffoon pack bought ($4) but both offers were rentals (Drunkard, Vampire), both skipped. Shoot the Moon ($5) barred by advisor sticker rule; left with $5. Small blind: discards 4 5 6 7 8, then 4 5 8; Two Pair KK 99 (960) played, Pair J (1200 actual vs 240 est), Pair A (1952 final, est 752). Ended 1248 short.

## Post-mortem

**Binding constraint:** scoring power at ante 2-3. The run had 3 jokers, only one permanent scorer (+4 mult Joker), Seeing Double perished at ante 3, and no xMult. Ante 3 Small 3200 needed ~2x what 2 Two Pair hands could do; the shop never offered a clean scorer at the money available.

**Decisions I would change:**
1. Ante 1 shop: I overrode the advisor (To the Moon ECON) for Seeing Double. Seeing Double perished at ante 3 with nothing permanent behind it. Prefer a permanent +mult/+chips scorer at ante 1-2 even if it is weaker.
2. Ante 2 Small skip for Charm Tag cost the economy and gave no scorer beyond two Arcana cards. Skipping at ante 2 needs the Small to be clearly out of reach; I accepted a TIGHT projection (0.99x).
3. Ante 3 Buffoon pack: I bought the pack ($4) before checking whether its offers were usable. Check `shopadvise` for the pack contents first; a pack whose stock is all rentals is a $4 loss.
4. Ante 3 shop: at $9 I could have taken Shoot the Moon ($5) and kept $4; the advisor barred it, and the sticker-rule bar on an eternal non-rental scorer looks like a defect (see below).

**If-then rules for weaker models:**
- If the shop offers only rentals (or eternal rentals) in a pack, skip the pack; do not buy a rental just to fill the slot when you already own one.
- If an ante-2 blind is TIGHT (projected <1.0x) and you have 0 permanent scorers, do not skip it for a tag; buy a permanent scorer first.
- If The Mouth is the boss: the first hand locks the type. Pick a type with >=2 hands of projected score; a single pair line (est 156) will not clear 2000.
- If you have 0 discards and 2 hands left and no line clears the blind, the run is lost; play it out and move on.
- If a perishable scorer has 1 round left at ante 3, buy a permanent scorer before leaving the shop even at $5.

**Advisor/CLI defects:**
- Shoot the Moon (eternal, non-rental +mult scorer) was barred by "eternal non-scorer" / hard sticker rule in shopadvise at $5, while the same advisor listed it as ADVICE BUY at $9. The bar appears to depend on money, not on the joker.
- Pair J est 240 scored 1200 actual; pair A est 752 scored 752. Estimates are unreliable when a pair is scored with a Two Pair/Flush held in the hand: check `advise` after every play.
- Odd Todd (rental) sell/keep recommendation was not revisited at ante 3, even though its +31 per odd card was weak in a 2-pair round.
- The ante-3 pace warnings (ratio 0.68 TIGHT, emergency) were correct; the shop advisor offered no affordable xMult at $5-9.
