## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING of the base, not of the multipliers. By ante 5 the build had x3 Stencil x x2.4-3.0 Obelisk
(~x8-9 total) but the base was Mystic Summit +15 mult, Smiley and L2-L3 hands: ~60-110 chips x ~20-35 mult. Joker
Stencil made every added flat joker cost a full x1 (x3 -> x2), so no flat scorer could be added, and Obelisk made my one
leveled hand (Two Pair L5) a reset hand once it tied Full House at 4 plays. The two multipliers fought the base: I had
13-24k per hand against a 50k boss that needed 3 x 16.7k.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 shops: I bought Uranus x2 (Two Pair L4) BEFORE seeing Obelisk, then Obelisk, then a third Uranus. With Obelisk
   owned, concentrated levels in one type are a trap: Two Pair hit the max count (tied with FH 4) after two more plays, and
   from then on my best hand (2P L5 ~12-17k) reset x1.8-2.6 to x1. I should have kept FH as the clear max and spread
   later levels over Pair/Straight/Trips (Black Hole was exactly right).
2. Ante 4 Jumbo Buffoon: Joker Stencil over Seltzer{RENTAL}. Stencil reached x4 cheaply, but it made the run unable to
   add scorers. In hindsight Stencil is a good xMult only when the remaining jokers are already strong (a +mult
   engine); with Summit + Smiley it multiplied a ~2k base.
3. Ante 2 last shop ($19) and ante 3 shop: I banked and then rerolled twice with nothing found. A cheap flat scorer
   bought in ante 2 (e.g. the Crafty/any +mult) is worse than nothing at Gold rent, but the $19 could have bought a
   Buffoon/Celestial earlier; low-impact.
4. Ante 5 last shop: Earth and Jupiter levels were near-worthless (+0.01/+0.02 SIM). Rerolling twice ($5+$6) for a
   strong flat +chips joker (Stencil x3->x2 but e.g. +100 chips doubles a 100-chip base) was the better gamble.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Mystic Summit, THEN burn both discards before the scoring hand every round (even with a made hand); it
  turned a 108 Two Pair into 918 at ante 1 and carried antes 1-3 alone.
- IF perishable scorers have exactly 2 rounds left at the ante-3 Small, THEN skip Small (and Big if its tag pays now):
  they stay alive for the boss (worked: Boss Tag rerolled The Manacle, Economy doubled $16 -> $32, House cleared).
- IF you own Obelisk, THEN never buy planets for the currently most-played type(s); level 2-3 types that are below the
  max (Pair, Straight, Flush) so every hand you play grows it; when two types tie at the max, reset ONLY with the one
  you will not play again.
- IF you own Joker Stencil at x3+, THEN a new joker must more than pay for losing x1 (x3 -> x2 = -33%): only an xMult or
  a +chips/+mult joker that roughly doubles your base qualifies; prefer card enhancements (Bonus/Mult cards via
  Hierophant/Empress) and planets, which Stencil multiplies for free.
- IF your last hand cannot clear but a joker adds nothing to it (Smiley on a faceless Pair AA), THEN sell it before the
  hand when you own Joker Stencil: +1 Stencil (x3 -> x4) applies immediately (verified 10602 -> 14136).
- IF The House deals all 8 face-down, THEN discard 5 first; hand sort places face-down cards by rank, so a face-down
  card between a 6 and a 4 is a 4-6 (it was the 5 that completed 8-7-6-5-4).
