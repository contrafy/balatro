# Run 1WEY5A1B (port 7781) - LOSS at ante 1 boss (The Psychic)

## Stake 1 (White)

Ante 1: Start $4, no jokers. Blinds: Small 300 (tag Charm), Big 450 (tag Uncommon), Boss The Psychic 600.
- Small Blind (300): discarded twice chasing a spade flush (advise-driven), played FLUSH S est 320, scored 320 (exact). Cash -> $9.
- Shop: bought Buffoon Pack ($4) per shopadvise, picked Gluttonous Joker (+3 mult per Club) over Certificate (Certificate has no direct scoring effect). Left rest per shopadvise LEAVE.
- Big Blind (450): discarded chasing a straight (K Q J T held), drew 9S completing 14-high straight, played STRAIGHT est 810, scored 810 (exact). Cash -> $12.
- Shop: bought Red Card ($5, +3 mult per pack skipped) per shopadvise. Jokers now 2/6: Gluttonous Joker, Red Card. Left rest per shopadvise LEAVE ($7 remaining).
- Boss The Psychic (600, exactly 5 cards per hand): no consumables held.
  - Hand 1: discarded twice building toward Two Pair, ended with three pairs (9/7/6); played TWO PAIR 97 est 260, scored 260 (exact).
  - Hand 2: discarded last discard chasing a straight (K J T 9 draw needing Q, EV 349 per advise) - drew a second Ten instead, straight failed. Forced to fall back to PAIR T with fillers, est 150, scored 150 (exact). Running total 410, need 190 more, 1 hand / 0 discards left.
  - Hand 3 (forced, no discards left): only candidate PAIR FACE K (KC KD) est 150 with fillers. No consumables available to boost. Played it: scored 150. Final total 560 vs need 600 - LOST by 40.

## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: bad luck (the last-discard straight chase whiffed, drawing a second Ten instead of the needed Queen) combined with the Two Pair engine (no jokers yet interact with pairs/two-pair) leaving no mult scaling for the boss's cumulative 600 requirement. Money and shop decisions were sound (two scoring jokers bought by end of ante 1, matching playbook guidance); the loss came down to the final-hand draw, not a misplay.

Decision I would change: on hand 2 of the boss round (410 scored, need 600, 2 hands + 1 discard left), the advisor's straight chase (EV 349) was mathematically reasonable but had high variance with no fallback if it whiffed - the safer line was to keep the discard in reserve for the guaranteed-forced final hand instead of spending it on a single-out draw, since after the whiff there was no way to recover (0 discards, est 150 vs 190 needed, no consumables). In practice both lines were tight given only 2 jokers and no mult scaling by ante-1 boss; the deeper fix is buying a joker that helps pairs specifically (Jolly Joker, Mad Joker) at ante 1 shop instead of Gluttonous Joker (suit-based, never triggered meaningfully) and Red Card (never fed since no packs were skipped) - neither purchased joker contributed any mult to the two hands that mattered.

Bridge issues: none. All `est` values matched actual scores exactly (320/320, 810/810, 260/260, 150/150, 150/150) across every hand played this run - no case of est differing from actual by more than 20%.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Section 5 (ante 1-2 shop) should more strongly prefer PAIR/TWO-PAIR-triggering jokers (Jolly, Mad, Zany, Clever) over suit-conditional jokers (Gluttonous) or self-referential econ-only jokers (Red Card) when the player's actual hands so far have been flush/straight/pair-based with no consistent suit - Gluttonous Joker never fired once in 5 scored hands this run (no club-heavy hands appeared) and contributed 0 net mult.
2. Red Card (+3 mult per pack skipped) should be flagged CONDITIONAL/AVOID by shopadvise unless the player has a concrete plan to skip packs - buying it at ante 1 costs a joker slot that never grows because Buffoon/Celestial packs are always worth opening.
3. Consider: when a boss requires a fixed hand count and the player is forced into a final hand with 0 discards and est < need, the advisor could flag it as "unwinnable, no options remain" earlier (during the prior hand's decision) so the log/agent understands the loss was already effectively locked in one hand before it happened, rather than only discovering it via the final `advise` call.
