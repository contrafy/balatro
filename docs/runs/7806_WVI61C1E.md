## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling, caused by money. One xMult (Acrobat, final hand only) on a flat Two Pair L5 build
(Jolly +8, Abstract +21, Mad +10, foil Ice Cream chips) made ~10k per non-final hand and ~37-42k on the final hand:
a round of ~50k at best, exactly the ante-5 boss. SCORE CHECK said 0.88-0.91 (TIGHT) in both ante-5 shops and was
right; I had $5-8 there and nothing to buy. The money never existed: Small pays $0, three Small skips plus one Big skip
forfeited ~$12 of income/interest, and the Ice Cream rental cost $3 x 7 rounds = $21 while decaying from 100 to 35 chips.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2: foil Ice Cream{RENTAL}. It carried ante 2-4 (+150 chips), but I never sold it: by ante 5 it gave 40-60 chips
   for $3 a round. Selling it at the ante-4 Big shop (Abstract/Jolly/Mad already carried) would have funded a Celestial
   pack or a reroll in each ante-5 shop.
2. Ante 1: Dusk{ETERNAL} $5 (SIM +0.15). It retriggers only card chips on the final hand: ~+5-8% at ante 5, and it
   blocks a slot forever. Keeping the $5 for the next shop's +mult joker was probably equal or better.
3. Ante 3: skipping BOTH Small and Big (Orbital +3 Two Pair levels) was good for score, but it left me with $7 at ante 4.
   With a rental draining $3/round, that line needs the Big's $5 + shop.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your only xMult is Acrobat AND your flat mult is <= ~45 at ante 5, THEN the round caps at ~3.5x one normal hand
  (~50k at Two Pair L5): treat SCORE CHECK < 1.0 as EMERGENCY and spend the ante-4 shops on a second xMult/levels, not
  on flat jokers.
- IF a decaying chips joker (Ice Cream, Popcorn) is a RENTAL, THEN sell it as soon as it is below ~60 chips or 2 other
  scorers carry: its value shrinks every hand while the rent stays $3.
- IF under The Tooth you hold trips of your main type's ranks and the main type is Two Pair, THEN keep the trips only if
  Full House is leveled; otherwise break it (solver did; the dig still missed). Two Pair L5 >> FH L1 with Mad Joker.
- IF you are rent-broke at an ante-5 Small (shop after it would hold <= $4), THEN skipping it is fine (no rent tick,
  tag), but plan to have $10+ before the boss shop or the TIGHT warning has nothing to buy.
