## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: BOSS (Crimson Heart) on a build whose whole score came from 3 single-point-of-failure multipliers (Photograph x2^4, Acrobat x3, Duo x2) plus Hanging Chad/Scary Face. With 7 jokers and one disabled per hand, 5 of 7 picks cripple a hand (Photograph out = -85%, Acrobat out = -67%, Chad out = -75%, Duo out = -50%). I needed ~133k per hand across 3 hands and had ~620k only if every joker was alive on the single final hand.
Decisions I would change:
1. I never looked at which joker Crimson Heart had disabled. `raw` shows `"debuff": true` on the joker; `s` and `hands` do not. The disabled joker for the NEXT hand is already chosen (it is re-rolled in Blind:drawn_to_hand after every PLAY, not after discards; verified in lovely dump blind.lua lines 530/635), so before each play I can see exactly what that play will lose. I banked a 31k pair into a halved 15k and a 4.7k pair into 1k, and left the 617k QQQ for the last hand with a random, unknowable joker pick. Correct play: before hand 1 read the debuff in `raw`; if the disabled joker is one the big hand needs, play a cheap hand to re-roll (the re-roll excludes the previous victim, so the pick after it can still hit Photograph/Acrobat), and only commit the big hand when raw shows an irrelevant joker disabled (Rebate, Half Joker, Scary). Hands 1-2 were NOT free throwaways: each one also needed to score.
2. With a 7-joker engine of which only 3 matter, 400k needs a SECOND independent scoring path or redundancy (a second xMult, Blueprint/Brainstorm, or a pair hand that scores 130k+ without Acrobat). Three junk jokers (Mail-In Rebate eternal, Hanging Chad, Half Joker) were not diluting the pick enough to matter.
3. Skipping the ante-8 Small/Big for tags was fine only if the boss was beatable; it removed two chances to see the disable pattern and to bank money. I had $9, so shops were not an option anyway: money starved after Acrobat rent ($3/round) and rerolls at ante 4. Keep ~$25 entering ante 7-8 for Director's Cut/rerollboss (Director's Cut was in the ante-8 shop at $10 and I could not afford it with $9; rerolling Crimson Heart would have been the real answer).
Reusable rules:
- IF the upcoming ante-8 boss is Crimson Heart (shown from blind select), THEN before every play run `b raw` and read `"debuff": true` on jokers; play a throwaway/cheap hand to re-roll when a key joker is the disabled one; never rely on Acrobat's final hand without checking.
- IF you own Acrobat/Photograph-style single-source multipliers, THEN buy Luchador/Director's Cut/Chicot-style boss insurance, or hold $10+ to buy Director's Cut when it shows.
- IF Hanging Chad (retrigger first card 2x) is owned, THEN `move hand` a Red Seal / Mult card to position 1: the first scoring card triggers 3-4 times and Photograph fires on each trigger (est went 336960 -> 617760).
- IF a Death tarot is held, THEN the LEFT card becomes a copy of the RIGHT card (move the good card to the right with `move hand`).
- IF Emperor/Priestess packs are bought late, THEN they are real multipliers: Emperor gave Death+Empress twice and carried the Pillar boss.
- IF skipping a Small Blind (stake 8 pays $0), THEN Meteor/Ethereal/Coupon/Negative tags were all worth it through ante 7; skipping also dodges rental ticks.
Advisor/CLI defects:
- `s`/`hands`/`advise`/`play` est ignore Crimson Heart entirely: est 31280 vs 15640, est 4680 vs 1080, est 617760 vs ~91k real. `s` never prints joker DEBUFF although raw has it; it should print which joker is disabled and recompute est without it.
- `advise` on the final hand said DISCARD (keep trips as floor) when est 336960 < need; that was correct, but it did not warn that Photograph was debuffed.
- `hands` est does not model Lucky cards (counts none; documented as floor) and does not model a Red Seal first card until the card is moved; it never suggests `move hand` to put the seal first.
- shopadvise pushes Celestial/Standard packs at $4-8 when slots are full and money is the binding constraint; it never suggested saving for Director's Cut ($10) at ante 7-8 when the boss list includes Crimson Heart/Verdant Leaf/Amber Acorn.
Bridge issues: none (one NOT LEGAL for a wrong pack slot index, no action was sent).
