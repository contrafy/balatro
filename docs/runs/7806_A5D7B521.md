## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING. The only multipliers were Photograph (x2 on first face), Acrobat (x3, final hand only) and three
Glass faces; every other slot was +mult. Pair was L3 at ante 5. The whole run hung on one 3-card face pair played as the last
hand under Acrobat, which topped out near 20-30k per round. Gold Stake ante 5 (25k/37.5k/50k) needs about 2x that. No clean xMult
showed up in ~9 rerolls plus 3 Buffoon packs (offers were eternal/rental junk: Banner, Cloud 9, Troubadour, Hiker, Showman).

Decisions I would change:
- Ante 3-4: I spent about $30 on rerolls and Standard/Spectral packs. That money should have gone into Celestial packs and Mercury,
  because Pair L3 at ante 5 was far too low (each level adds chips to every hand, including the Acrobat x3 hand).
- Ante 5 shop 1: I sold Splash (kicker scoring added about 25-40%) to open a Buffoon pack that whiffed, then spent the slot on
  Swashbuckler. Net loss. Rule: never sell a working scorer to open a pack blind. Sell only when the shop shows the replacement.
- I sold Golden Joker at ante 4 for a +14 mult holo Joker. That was correct for tempo, but it left no income: money ran $3-$15 in ante 5.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Acrobat (x3 on the final hand) THEN play your filler hands first (1-card hands under The Tooth) and hold the best
  3-card face pair for hand 3. This cleared 4 blinds that the made hand alone could not (6006/4800, 13440/12800, 19650/13500, 24000/18000).
- IF you own Photograph THEN a single face card alone as High Card (with Half Joker) scores about as much as a low pair: QD alone 990 vs Pair 7 528.
- IF a forced final hand cannot clear on est and you hold a Lucky card THEN pick the hand that contains it and clears WITH the +20
  proc by the biggest margin (KK77 with glass 7 clears by 50k vs KK alone by 33k). Both have the same 1/5 odds; take the safer margin.
- IF you own Riff-raff with free slots THEN play the Small Blind instead of skipping it. Riff-raff fires on blind select (2 free commons).
- IF it is ante 5+ on Gold Stake with under $5 THEN skip the Small Blind for any pack tag. The shop it unlocks buys nothing, and skipping
  avoids a 25k risk plus a $3 rental charge.
- IF the build tops out below the ante's boss by more than 30% at the ante-4 shop THEN put every dollar into main-hand levels
  (Celestial/Mercury). Rerolls for xMult rarely hit at Gold Stake, where about 1/3 of jokers are eternal/rental.

  actual 5040; filler est 935, actual 715).

  joker position is irrelevant; the advice is noise (and it never mentioned Acrobat should be rightmost).

  scoring kicker under Splash when the 3-card cap of Half Joker allows it.
- `advise` repeatedly proposed flush chases (Big 13500 ante 4) that would have broken Half Joker (+20 at <=3 cards). The 3-card face pair
  + Acrobat line was much stronger.

- Lucky card upside is not shown: KK77 est 18090 vs actual 50250. Showing a "with proc" number on forced final hands would help.
