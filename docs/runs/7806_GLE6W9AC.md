## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: misplay + variance on a jokerless 300 Small. Three hands of L1 Two Pair (~104 each) are the only
reliable way to 300 without jokers; I spent hand 1 on a 60-point Pair because the solver rated it 0.98 vs 0.93, and hand
3 on a 56-point Pair dig. Two made Two Pairs (QQ66 hand 1, KK99 hand 3 = 116) were thrown away for 60 and 56. Banked as
Two Pairs the round would have been 104 + (KK66 104 or better) + 116 = 324 >= 300 even with zero draws hitting.

Decisions I would change:
1. Hand 1: play Two Pair QQ66 + filler (104) instead of Pair QQ (60). At ante 1 with no jokers, 300 / 3 hands = 100 per
   hand, and a made Two Pair already IS that pace; a Pair play is a guaranteed 40-point deficit that later hands must
   recover with draws. The solver's 0.98 assumed the dig lands; it did not.
2. Hand 3 (2 hands left, 0 discards, need 240, holding KK 99 66): play Two Pair KK99 (116), keep 66 + draws for the last
   hand (solver 0.38 vs 0.47). Marginal by the solver, but it banks points instead of a 56 dig.
3. Hand 2 discards were per rule; fine.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own no jokers at ante 1 AND a Two Pair is made in your opening hand, THEN play it (with 1 filler) on hand 1;
  never play a lone Pair as a "dig" while a Two Pair is in hand: each Two Pair is ~100 = exactly the 300/3 pace.
- IF a solver PLAY line scores well below need/hands (a 60 Pair vs a 100/hand pace) AND a made hand at pace exists, THEN
  treat its p as optimistic (it needs later draws to land) and prefer the made hand unless the gap is > 0.10.
- IF both discards are spent and a dig play is needed for a 4-out draw (~0.44), THEN compare it with banking the best
  made hand now and digging on the final hand; neither is good, so avoid getting there: bank made Two Pairs early.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- SOLVER at ante 1 jokerless Small, n=100: "PLAY Pair QQ + 3 fillers p 0.98" over keeping/playing Two Pair QQ66; the round
  was lost 220/300. Same solver then rated breaking KK66 for a 3-card flush draw at 0.95 (n=400) over keeping Two Pair
  (0.87). A 0.98 that leads to a loss after only standard misses suggests the n=100 jokerless ante-1 lines are

- Rule advice in hand 1 was disc 3 6 7 8 (keep Two Pair, draw) rather than PLAY Two Pair; at 0 jokers a made Two Pair
  is pace for 300 and should be played (keeping both discards for hands 2-3).
