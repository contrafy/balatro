## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. Zero xMult for the whole run; the build was flat +chips (Stuntman +250) times flat +mult (two holo +10, Even Steven, Half Joker x2). At ~17k per hand, 3 hands cap at ~55k, so The Wall (100000, x2 boss at ante 5) was mathematically out of reach. Only an xMult or a Boss Tag (skip Big to reroll The Wall) could have fixed it; Big carried a Rare tag, not a Boss tag, and I had no Director's Cut/Luchador.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 shop (after Big): money sat at $12-13 and I took a Judgement gamble; no Buffoon packs were available. Fine, but I never held interest money; the run was at $0-$14 from ante 3 on, so xMult was never affordable when seen (none were seen: Showman/Egg/Half Joker/Credit Card/Luchador shops).
2. Ante 5 Small: Standard Tag was on the Small and Rare Tag on the Big. With ~13k/hand and the Wall at 100k, skipping Big for the Rare tag would have produced a free Rare joker, but the boss shop does not exist before the Wall, so tags cannot help; correct play there was only to maximize score. A better ante-4 plan would have been to bank money for Mega Buffoon packs instead of rental/Celestial packs.
3. Stuntman (-2 hand size) with hand size 6 made all hands 1-3 cards, which is an excellent pairing with Half Joker (+20 each). I found that synergy only at ante 5: Half Joker + Stuntman + holo +10 jokers turned 5k hands into 13-19k hands. If Half Joker had appeared at ante 4 the Big/Boss would have been much easier.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Stuntman (or any hand-size loss) and plays are 1-3 cards, THEN Half Joker{even perishable} is a +20-mult scorer; Showman lets you buy duplicates of it (each +20, stacks). Sell weak chips jokers for the slot.
- IF a Spectral pack offers Wraith and you own 0 xMult with a free slot and money you cannot use anyway, THEN take Wraith over Cryptid (it gave +250 chips Stuntman here, +9k single-card hands).
- IF the ante-5 boss is The Wall (x2 = 100000) and you own no xMult/Boss Tag/Luchador/Chicot, THEN the run is capped at ~3 x best hand: before the ante-5 Big shop, price a path to ~35k per hand or skip the Big for a Boss Tag if shown.
- IF Shoot the Moon is owned, THEN play 1-2 card hands and keep Queens held (+13 mult each): single high cards scored 9044 with one Queen held.
- Riff-raff with 5 free slots at ante 2 gave 4 usable commons (Clever, Walkie Talkie, 2 suit jokers) for $6; sell it once slots fill.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- Pack `advise` ranked Cryptid ("target 8H, your most common rank") over Wraith in a Jumbo Spectral pack with a free slot and 0 xMult; Wraith produced the run's best joker.
- SOLVER rule lines for ante 4 Pillar at est 9044 for a single High Card were right, but `advise` kept saying "Playing WITHOUT fillers and with the fewest cards" at a time when fewest cards was also the Half Joker/Stuntman optimum; no defect, just note the tool handled it.
- `shopadvise` at ante 5 Big shop listed Half Joker{PERISHABLE} as "PLUS(<=3 cards: not your main hand)" with score 70 while it was the best scorer in the build (main hand estimates ignore that my plays are all 1-3 cards because of Stuntman); Half Joker rule score should follow the actual play size.
- SCORE CHECK said "Pair builds with 0 xMult top out ~10k/hand at ante 5" while my build did ~13-19k: the 75th-pct check ignores 1-3 card plays that use Half Joker/Stuntman (check showed 8280 when real typical hands were 12-19k).
- Bridge issues: none (111 actions, no rejects).
