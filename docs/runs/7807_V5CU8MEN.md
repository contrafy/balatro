## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: misplay + a single conditional scorer. Shoot the Moon (+13 mult per Queen HELD) was the only joker,
and The Pillar debuffs every card played earlier in the ante; a debuffed Queen gives Shoot the Moon nothing. I finished
the Small Blind (8 chips needed) with a lone High Card QC, although TC (15) or even 3H (8) cleared it too. At the boss
QC came back debuffed on hand 2. With a clean QC the line was: Straight 172 + dig High (~210 with Q held) + Pair AA with
QC held (32 x 15 = 480) = ~860 >= 600. That single card choice lost the run.

Decisions I would change:
1. Small Blind finisher: play the lowest card that clears (3H = 8 for an 8-point need), never a Queen/King/Ace, and never
   a card of a rank a likely joker cares about. Under The Pillar this matters double: every played card is dead at the boss.
2. Big Blind: the solver's discard broke AA66 for a straight draw; the two straights then used 10 cards, and 9 of the
   boss deal's cards came back debuffed (9S 8H 5C 5D 9H 7H...). Under The Pillar prefer the rule pick that keeps the
   made pairs (2-card plays) over 5-card straights when both are close in p.
3. Shop 1: Shoot the Moon as the only scorer is fragile (needs an undebuffed Queen in the 8-card hand: ~50% per deal
   without discards). Defensible at $5 with SIM 0.93, but it must be paired with Queen-safe play (rule below).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF a blind needs only a few more chips, THEN finish with the LOWEST single card that clears (compute 5 + rank chips),
  never a face/Ace; any card you play may be wanted later (Pillar debuff, Shoot the Moon/Baron held-card jokers, pairs).
- IF The Pillar is this ante's boss AND you own (or may buy) a held-card joker (Shoot the Moon, Baron, Raised Fist),
  THEN never play that rank (Queens/Kings) in the Small/Big blind; keep them in hand or discard them only if the deck has
  others. Debuffed held Queens/Kings give nothing.
- IF your only scorer depends on a held rank, THEN at the boss count clean copies left in the deck before choosing a
  dig: here 3 clean Queens in 31 cards (~42% to see one in 5 draws).

Advisor/CLI defects:
- shopadvise SIM rated Shoot the Moon{PERISHABLE} p_next 0.93 for the next 3 blinds at ante 1 with The Pillar as the
  boss; it lost the second of those blinds. [INFERENCE] the SIM does not model Pillar debuffs on previously played
  Queens (or Queen scarcity: 4 per deck).
- advise does not warn when a minimal finisher would spend a card the build needs; it never suggested the cheapest
  clearing card (I chose QC myself without running advise, my error, but a "use your lowest card" note would help).
- Under The Pillar the Big-Blind solver pick (break AA66 for a straight, 5-card plays) conflicts with the printed rule
  "play WITHOUT fillers and with the fewest cards"; the solver p values (0.99 vs 0.95) do not price boss debuffs.
Bridge issues: none.
