## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no xMult and no real +mult source by ante 3. The jokers I owned in antes 1-3 were perishable bridges (Popcorn, Walkie) or chips-only (Clever). The only permanent scorer (Clever) adds +80 chips on a base mult of 2-4. Walkie Talkie on 4s/10s carried the Small and Big of ante 3 (4848, 5656 hands) but perished the same round; The Water (no discards, need 6400) was then unwinnable (solver p 0.02). Gold's $0 Small plus no income kept me at $0-8 every shop.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 first shop ($15): I rerolled away Hologram{ETERNAL}$7 for Walkie Talkie{PERISHABLE}$4. A perishable bridge bought with a 5-round clock ended exactly at The Water. With $15 and 1 dying scorer I should have kept the money for a permanent +mult joker (never found) or bought Hologram plus Standard packs to feed it (+0.25/card): unfed x1 was still a slot-holder for the later xMult plan.
2. Ante 2 Jumbo Spectral ($6 of $9): picked Incantation over Ectoplasm. Neither scored; the $6 would have bought Popcorn-class jokers in the next shop (Popcorn $5 appeared at ante 3 when I had $1).
3. Ante 3 first shop: rerolled twice ($15 -> $4) hunting a scorer and found only Clever (+80 chips on Two Pair): a chips-only joker on base mult. Better: one reroll and keep $9-10, then buy Popcorn $5 + Hologram {R} $1 when they appeared a shop later (the second-shop Popcorn was unaffordable at $1 because Small pays $0).
4. Ante 2/3 Small Blinds pay $0, so every Small-blind shop was visited with ~$1. I had no line to build money.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the Small blind is a Gold $0-payout round AND you have <$5 after it, THEN the Small's shop is a "look only" shop: do not rerolls-to-$0 in the shop BEFORE it (the shop after the Small cannot buy anything).
- IF Popcorn (+20 mult, -4/round) is offered at ante 1-3, THEN buy it: it one-shot Big/Small blinds (Pair QQ 660, Pair TT 1100) at ante 1-2 on a $4 pack, but plan the permanent +mult before its 5th round.
- IF Walkie Talkie is owned THEN play 4s/10s: each scoring 10 or 4 is +10 chips +4 mult; two Two-Pair/FH hands of 4s/10s scored 3.0k/4.8k/5.6k on ante 3.
- IF the upcoming boss is The Water (no discards) and your typical hand is <1500, THEN the run is lost unless a +20-mult joker or xMult is bought in the preceding shop; spend EVERYTHING there, never end the previous shop with unused money.
- IF a tag/shop item shows an unfed growth xMult (Hologram) at $7 eternal with no better scorer, THEN the xMult-by-ante-2 target is worth more than a perishable chips bridge.
