## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no multiplier beyond Ramen x2 (decaying) and a flat +mult stack (Droll flush +10, Abstract +15, Swashbuckler +8, perishable Half Joker +20). Every flat scorer was perishable/rental and died at the ante 3-4 boundary; the per-hand ceiling was ~5.2k (Flush L1) vs 9k/13.5k/18k at ante 4. Money was $0-6 every shop (rent + $0 Small + zero interest), so I never could afford a reroll or a second Buffoon pack; only 3 Buffoon packs were seen in 4 antes and gave Droll/Ramen/(junk rentals).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 shop: Swashbuckler{P} at $4 left me at $1 and rent pushed money to -$2; the Celestial/Arcana packs were skipped. A reroll for xMult was unaffordable. Money, not slots, was the constraint: with a rental already owned, every extra $4 spend was unrecoverable. Better: hold the $4+$5 for one Buffoon pack in the ante 4 shop.
2. I never levelled Flush (main hand with Droll). No Jupiter was ever offered; planets seen: Earth only. Droll as the only +mult source made my hand type Flush, which the playbook says dies by ante 3-4; it did.
3. Ante 4 Small skip for the Negative tag only pays after the Big; the Big (13500) was unclearable (needed 3 x 4.5k with a 5.2k flush max). The tag never got used. If a blind cannot be cleared anyway the skip does not matter, but the sound alternative at ante 3 was to keep the Half Joker perishable alive via skipping Small AND using the Double tag.
