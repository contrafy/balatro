## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling + money. No reliable xMult was ever offered in a shop (0 r/g xMult sightings in 9 shops);
the only multipliers were Blackboard{ETERNAL,RENTAL} (x3, bought at EMERGENCY 0.04) and Ramen (x2, a free Judgement
roll). Every hand topped out at ~40k from ante 6 on (Pair L1 + Supernova ~+20 + Abstract +18 + holo Sly, x3 x1.9).
Money never rose above $14: two rentals (Ice Cream ante 1, Blackboard ante 4) cost ~$30 of rent, and $0-5 shops from
ante 5 on could not buy a planet, a pack or a reroll. Pair stayed L1 the whole run (never once had $3 spare for Mercury
after the scorer buys).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 5 shop ($14): I spent $8 on a Mega Buffoon (got a perishable foil Crafty) and $6 on Seltzer, then sold Seltzer
   one shop later at a $3 loss for Ice Cream. A Jumbo Celestial/Mercury levels or simply holding $10+ for interest and the
   next shop would have kept the economy alive for ante 6-7 shops. Seltzer on a 2-card Pair engine is nearly worthless
   (it retriggers only scoring cards; without Splash that is 2 cards).
2. Ante 2: Greedy Joker{PERISH} $5 was a 5-round bridge; that $5 + interest was the start of the money hole.
3. Ante 7: once 3x the best hand < Big need with no money, nothing remains; the death was decided at ante 5-6 shops.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Blackboard, THEN play every red card you hold as a filler of your Pair (up to 3 fillers) so the held cards
  are all Spades/Clubs: `advise`/`sel` find these lines; it turned ~8k into ~25k at ante 4 and ~13k into ~40k at ante 6.
  Discard reds before blacks. Under The Club, debuffed clubs held still count as black for Blackboard (verified 33660).
- IF a held Wild card is debuffed (suit boss), THEN it counts only as its printed suit for Blackboard: play it as a filler
  rather than holding it (est treats it as black).
- IF Supernova is owned and Pair is your most played type (15+ plays), THEN a Pair beats Trips/Full House/Two Pair at L1
  (Pair 99 40919 vs Trips 999 ~32600 at ante 6): do not chase bigger types.
- IF Ice Cream is your main chip source and has 1 round left, THEN skip Small and Big to bring it to the boss (did it at
  ante 3: Manacle cleared 7105/6400; without it Pair scored ~700).
- IF a Double Tag is held, THEN skip a blind whose tag gives packs (Buffoon) to get two; it was my only joker access at
  -$ money.
- IF your engine is a 2-card Pair, THEN Seltzer is not a scorer: Ice Cream/Sly (+chips) give 3-5x more per slot.
- IF the next ante's Small needs more than 3x your best hand and you hold < $5, THEN the run is decided: spend the
  ante before on joker views, not on temporary bridges.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- `advise` ADVICE at The Club played Pair JJ with fillers that left a debuffed Wild AH held, est 33660 assuming
  Blackboard x3; in-game is_suit(flush_calc) treats a debuffed Wild as its base suit (Hearts), so the x3 would fail. The
  solver's alternative (play the AH) had the same est; est should drop x3 when a debuffed Wild is held.
- Pack `advise` after `skippack` of the first of two Mega Buffoon packs printed stale state (warning "last SKIP_PACK
  is not visible after 15s") and took 66s; shopadvise right after a pack printed pack advice until `s` was run.
- The solver at The Tooth recommended breaking Pair 66 + Pair 33 (DISCARD, p 0.84 n=64) over playing Pair 66 41322
  (p 0.74); I played the pair and won the blind 125538/120000 with 2 more pairs. Small-n discard lines look inflated.
- `s` skip advice at ante 5+ ("do not skip: the shop after the Small Blind is worth more than a tag") ignores money:
  at $1/-$5 the post-Small shop could buy nothing and the Buffoon tag was strictly better.
- Bridge issues: none (104 steps, no rejects).
