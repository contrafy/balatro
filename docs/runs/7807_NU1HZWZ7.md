## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: multiplier. The whole run was a flat stack (Banner chips + Half Joker +20 mult on 1-3 card plays + Odd Todd/Even Steven/Raised Fist) with 0 xMult; it scaled 100 -> 4.3k per hand by ante 3 but The Wall asked 12800 (4x the 3200 ante-3 Small, i.e. 2x the usual boss) and I saw 0 xMult in 6 shops/packs from ante 1 to 3 (no Buffoon pack was ever offered after ante 1).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 Small: skipped nothing, but Handy Tag (+$1 per hand played = ~$12) was on it. With a 4.3k/hand build vs a 12.8k Wall (0.9x 3 hands) money was the only lever left; $12 = a second Mercury (+15 chips, +1 mult = ~+350/hand) or a reroll hunt, enough for the 95 missing. I followed 'never skip' (rule says money tags only when one-shotting), which is wrong when the boss is a Wall and the Small is cleared by 2 hands anyway.
2. Ante 1 shop: bought a Buffoon pack with $4 of $5 and skipped both picks (Ride the Bus rental, Midas Mask eternal): the $4 was wasted. With 0 jokers, no non-rental scorer in the shop and a $5 bankroll, a $4 pack whose odds of a sticker-free scorer are ~50% is OK, but the picks were both useless; no fix except the Buffoon at ante 1 is a coin flip.
3. Ante 3 final shop (Wall): $5 left over with a $5 reroll available is not spendable (rent). Keep $8 for Even Steven rent? The rental (Even Steven +8 mult on even pairs, -$3 per round) added only ~+800 on even pairs; skipping it and rerolling the $1+3 rent for a Mercury would have been equal.
4. Wall hand 2: played 9H + 2S 3S fillers (est 4305) which kept the two lowest cards OUT of hand, but the 3 replacements drawn were 8C 4D 3H (ruined Raised Fist). Could instead have played KK again with the low cards (hold TT QQ J; lowest held 10) - not knowable; the real error is that Raised Fist makes variance high.
