## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no multiplier beyond Ramen x1.9. Three flat +mult/+chips jokers carried Pair (Half Joker +20, Mystic Summit +15, Shoot the Moon +13/Queen held) to 4-13k per hand, enough for antes 1-3 and Big 4, but a 3-hand no-discard boss at 18000 needs ~6000/hand on average and my variance (3910, 1806, 8313) missed. Money never exceeded $13: Gold Small pays $0, Ramen cost $3/round, and every shop was $4-9.

Decisions I would change:
1. Water blind: I played a 5-card Full House 77722 (3910) as hand 1 on the solver's 0.28 over Trips 4889 (3 cards, keeps Half). The solver's gain was draw variance; total need was 18000 with 3 hands, so the line needed ~6k per hand either way. Real alternative: nothing better existed with 0 discards, so the fix is earlier (a 4th hand: Grabber $10 was $1 out of reach at ante 4 - I should have kept the $1 from selling Delayed Gratification/Sly instead of buying DG for $4 first).
2. Ante 4 first shop: bought Delayed Gratification $4 (UTIL, perishable) then sold it the next shop for $2. A pure $2 loss; the plan to play with 0 discards conflicted with the Summit plan (which wants 0 discards LEFT, i.e. discard them away) - DG only pays when no discard is used and Summit needs them gone.
3. Skipped Small (Coupon) at ante 4: it worked (free Business Card/Mercury), but the Coupon shop had no xMult, so the skip brought no score for the Water.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Mystic Summit (+15 mult at 0 discards left) and no discard-hungry joker THEN burn both discards on 1 junk card each at the start of every round (Ramen loses only 0.01/card); advise itself recommends it. Pair JJ est went 3821 -> 8460 (x2.2).
- IF you own Shoot the Moon (+13 mult per Queen held) THEN never play your Queens: hold them; with Ramen a held Queen is worth ~+25 mult.
- IF Needle (1 hand) or Water (0 discards) is the boss and you own Summit THEN estimates already include Summit; check `hands` after burning discards, a drawn face pair is 13.5k.
- IF Top-up tag is on the Big and you have 1 free slot THEN sell a weak joker first so BOTH commons land (got Scary Face + Shoot the Moon, both permanent, no stickers).
- IF a perishable carrier has 1 round left THEN skipping Small AND Big preserves it for the boss (Sly carried the Needle at est ~5600 pair vs 3200).
- IF Business Card/Delayed Gratification-type economy jokers are the only offers THEN they do not fix a 0.38 SCORE CHECK; Water at ante 4 with typical hand 6.4k and no xMult is a ~30% blind: buy the 4th hand (Grabber) or xMult first.
