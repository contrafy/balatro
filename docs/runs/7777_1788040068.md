## Key Decision Points

### 1. Ancient Joker was a game-changer
Purchasing Ancient Joker for $8 at ante 2 was the best decision. It provided massive scaling multipliers:
- Ante 2 boss: 1000 → 1370 (370 multiplier from 4-card bonus)
- Ante 3 round 6: 1463 → 6908 (~4.7x multiplier from 6-diamond hand)
- Ante 3 round 8: 1425 → 6728 (~4.7x multiplier from 6-diamond hand)

### 2. Diamond flush strategy was optimal
Building diamond flushes worked perfectly with:
- Greedy Joker (+3 mult per Diamond)
- Ancient Joker (rotating suit multiplier)
- This combination provided exponential scaling until ante 3

### 3. The Pillar boss debuff effect was the breaking point
The Pillar debuffs cards played earlier in the ante, meaning:
- My diamonds (played in previous rounds) were debuffed
- Only new cards could score
- My best hands (diamond flushes) couldn't be formed fresh
- This forced me to build weaker hands (quads, pairs) without the diamond bonus
- The rotation of debuffed cards across hand types broke the synergy

### 4. Spending $8 on Ancient Joker vs $10 on levels
In hindsight, the Ancient Joker was more valuable than purchasing levels. Every level purchase would have cost $4 and only added ~5% scaling. The Ancient Joker added 300-400% scaling in certain scenarios.

## Why I Lost

**Binding Constraint**: Boss gimmick (The Pillar's debuff mechanic) broke the synergy.

The strategy of building diamond flushes worked because:
1. First hand draws new cards, builds diamond flush
2. Second hand redraws, builds another strong hand
3. Ancient Joker boosts both

But The Pillar forces debuffs on previously-scored cards, creating a mismatch:
- Round 9 hand was weak (only 4s and 9s available fresh)
- Previous diamonds were debuffed so couldn't rebuild flushes
- Total available scoring: ~2100 vs needed 4000

**What I'd change**: 
I should have skipped The Pillar boss, as it fundamentally breaks the diamond-flush-based scaling. The trade-off of $0 income but preserving $36 for ante 4 would have been better. At ante 4, with $36 cash, I could have purchased 2-3 more jokers or levels to rebuild scaling for ante 4+ bosses.
