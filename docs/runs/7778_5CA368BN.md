     retry shortly" three times this run (ante2 boss, ante3 boss). Retried after `sleep 2` each

     but not obviously retryable either (retrying without freeing a slot just fails again).

     card is silently consumed with zero effect - confirmed 2x (The World, Justice) before I

  4. Death tarot's actual direction: `sel i j` then `use N` makes the FIRST-selected card (i)
     become a copy of the SECOND-selected card (j) - i.e. left becomes a copy of right. I assumed
     the opposite (right copies left) and it cost us a scoring opportunity.
