## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING. No xMult was ever offered (13 shop views incl. 7 rerolls, 3 Buffoon packs: Abstract, Chaos, Drunkard, Jolly, Diet Cola). The build was all flat +mult (Abstract +18, Popcorn +16, Shoot the Moon +13, Gros Michel +15 until it perished) on an unleveled Pair/Two Pair: ~3000-5000 per hand at ante 4 against 4500/hand needed. The free Astronomer levels went to Trips/High Card/Full House because Mercury/Uranus never showed in a shop (and the Arm took the one Pair level).

Decisions I would change:
- Ante 4 first shop: I passed Even Steven $4 (to leave slot 6 for Riff-raff) and then spent $11 on 2 rerolls that found nothing. Even Steven would have added +12 mult to the final club Flush (6C 4C 2C) = ~4490 vs 4198 needed: the run was lost by the price of one reroll. A sure +mult joker now beats a reroll hunt when the next blind projects under 1.0x.
- Ante 4 second shop: 2 more rerolls ($21->$10) instead of selling Shoot the Moon for Misprint $4 (avg +11, always on; Shoot the Moon fired only when a Queen happened to stay in hand). Total $29 of rerolls at ante 3-4 bought one free Mercury, Earth and two Plutos.
- Ante 3: Justice $3 bought as "rescue" was worth +268 on the final hand (3417 -> 3685): Glass x2 multiplies only the base mult before the flat joker +mult is added. In a flat-mult build a Strength/Death or simply keeping $3 is better.
- Big Blind hand 2: discarding 9H 7C for a 9-out diamond flush draw with 2 cards was right on expectation, but the whole round hinged on draws because no single hand reached 4500.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF the next blind projects under 1.0x (shopadvise TIGHT/EMERGENCY) and the shop has an affordable unconditional +mult joker (Even Steven, Misprint, Joker), THEN buy it (sell the weakest conditional joker for room) BEFORE any reroll; reroll only with what is left.
- IF your build is flat +mult jokers on a low-level hand, THEN Justice/Glass is NOT a rescue: card xMult applies before joker +mult (x2 on a base mult of 2-4). Buy Strength/Death or a +mult joker instead.
- IF you own Astronomer, THEN every reroll also buys free planets; still level only pair-family types and treat the reroll cost as a level gamble (~1 useful planet per 2 rerolls in this run).
- IF a Coupon Tag is offered at ante 2 and the post-Small shop would be spent with $0-3, THEN skip the Small for it: free Jumbo Buffoon + Jumbo Arcana + 2 free jokers turned $3 into Abstract, Riff-raff, $22 and cleared the Wall.
- IF Riff-raff is free (Coupon) or cheap with 2+ empty slots before a hard boss, THEN take it even Eternal: it filled 2 slots at the Wall (+6 Abstract mult, Scary Face) and cleared 4028/4000.
