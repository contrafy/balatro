## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no +mult scorer. Ante 1 shops offered zero jokers (Magician/Saturn, then perishable chips-only Blue
Joker); the two $4 Buffoon packs gave Scary Face{PERISHABLE} (+30 chips per face) and Ride the Bus (+1 mult per
faceless hand), which anti-synergize: every Scary hand resets the Bus. So ante 2 started with ~200 per hand at base
mult 4 against 1000/1500, and the only multiplier I could afford was a rental Obelisk at x1.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 shop: rerolled $5 and bought Obelisk{RENTAL} $1. With 7 hands played over 6 types every type was tied at 1,
   so Obelisk reset on nearly every play: it reached x1.2 once and that x1.2 never touched a scoring hand. The $6 would
   have been better kept: with $9 + Skip Tag $5 the Big shop could have bought a real +mult joker.
2. Skipping the ante-2 Small for the Skip Tag sent a ~0.5x build into the 1500 Big with no extra shop. It was probably
   lost either way (0.75x vs the 1000 Small), but playing the Small gives two shots at a lucky draw and one more shop.
3. Ante 1 Buffoon pick 1: Juggler (permanent +1 hand size) vs a perishable chips-only Scary Face. The SIM preferred
   Scary (0.39 vs 0.16) for ante 1, but Scary dies at the ante 2->3 jump and adds no mult; with Ride the Bus later
   picked they conflicted.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF fewer than ~8 hands have been played this run (every type tied at 1-2 plays), THEN Obelisk is ~x1 for the rest of
  the ante: almost every play resets it. Do not spend your last $6 (reroll + rent) on it at ante 2; it needs a
  sacrificial most-played type first (pick one, e.g. Pair, and let it lead by 2).
- IF you own Scary Face/Smiley/Photograph (face jokers), THEN skip Ride the Bus in a pack (and vice versa): they cancel.
- IF ante 2 starts with only chips jokers and EMERGENCY < 0.2, THEN keep the cash for a +mult joker in the next shop
  rather than a reroll + a $1 rental that starts at x1; and do not skip the Small (one more draw + one more shop).
- IF the Big Blind needs more than 2x your best made hand with 2 hands left, THEN the solver's dig for a 2-out
  Straight Flush (17%) is the right call over banking a doomed Straight: it was the only line that could clear.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- shopadvise labelled Obelisk{RENTAL} 'BARRED: flat rental at ante 1-2' although it classed it XMULT(growth); the
  one-xMult-rental exception should apply (I overrode).
- shopadvise ADVICE at ante 2 was `buy Seance` ($6, spectral on Straight Flush, adds 0 score) as 'EMERGENCY best
  affordable item'; a 0-score utility should never be the EMERGENCY buy over a reroll for a scorer.
Bridge issues: none (30 steps, 0 aborts, 0 rejects).
