## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. The whole run was perishable joker bridges (Blue Joker, Abstract) plus a 1-card High Card + Card Sharp x3 trick; there was never a permanent xMult or a permanent +mult scorer, and the base hand was L1-L6 High Card. Ante 4 Small (9000) and Eye were cleared only by arming Mystic Summit and by Mr. Bones; ante 5 boss (The Flint halves base chips AND mult) collapsed the flat build (est 201 per 1-card hand before Summit).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3: skipping Small and Big to keep the perishable alive for the boss meant arriving at ante 4 with $21 and 6 slots of junk, and no shop visits at the moment I most needed to find a permanent scorer (2 skipped shops). I did it because Blue was the whole engine; the real fix was ante 1-2: picking the permanent Half Joker (or buying Hiker/more permanents with the $10-16 I held at ante 2) instead of two perishables (Blue + Abstract) with nothing permanent behind them.
2. Ante 4/5: I skipped Big at both antes to keep perishables/tags; at ante 5 the Skip tag paid $30 + Handy $14 = $50 but there was no shop before the boss, so the money was useless. When skipping both Small and Big, tag money is only worth anything if the NEXT stop is a shop, i.e. never when the boss is next.
3. Ante 5 shop: I spent $5 on a reroll and $4 on a Celestial pack and rental Bones instead of keeping the $14 for... nothing better existed; the shop had no scorer. The run was already lost at ante 4 (ratio 0.07).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF a perishable carrier (Blue Joker etc.) is your only engine at ante 1-2 THEN buy the best PERMANENT scorer you can afford in every shop of ante 2, even a weak one (Hiker/Half Joker): the ante-3 cliff comes when the 5-round clock ends and you need 3200 per blind.
- IF you own Blue Joker THEN 1-card plays (single High Card) score 1100-3000 at ante 1-3: play ONE card per hand, keep 2 hands for +$2, keep deck large (do not add cards via Grim/Familiar, they raise Blue; do not shrink the deck).
- IF you own Card Sharp (x3 on a repeated hand type) AND Mystic Summit (+15 mult at 0 discards) THEN burn both discards first, play a cheap card of a type, then the best card of the SAME type twice (High Card Q/K x3 = 4590 at L1 with Scary Face); one-card face High Card with Scary Face (+30 chips) is the flat engine.
- IF The Flint (halves base chips and mult) is the boss THEN estimate with `est` (it models Flint): a flat +chips/mult build at L1-L6 base only scores ~1200 per 1-card hand; the boss needs a real xMult. Plan Mr. Bones' 25% threshold (12500 at ante 5) from est, not hope.
- IF Small and Big are skipped THEN the tag money has no shop to be spent in before the boss; skip only for tags that help the boss (Orbital level-ups, free Spectral/Arcana packs) or keep playing Big for its shop visit.
- Rental Mr. Bones ($1, -$3/round) is a cheap one-blind safety net, but it only saves at >= 25% of the blind; count whether the build reaches it before buying.
