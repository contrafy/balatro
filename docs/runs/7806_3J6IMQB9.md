## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling + money. Three flat jokers and no levels made a face Pair ~1980 at best (75th pct ~1980) against a 4800 Big = 1600/hand needed every hand, and non-face pairs were 450-700. Money never exceeded $9: the $0 Small, Half Joker rent (-$3 x 4 rounds = $12) and a $5 reroll ate every shop, so no ante-2/3 shop could buy a second joker or a level.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 shop (EMERGENCY 0.33, $8): I followed LEAVE and died the next blind holding $8. Money does not score: a $5 reroll for a +mult joker (or an Arcana pack for Empress/Justice on a Queen) was the only way to add score, and then the skip took away the second shop. Either spend in that shop or play the Small to get one more shop - not both "leave" and "skip".
2. Ante 3 Small skip for the Boss Tag (Psychic -> Hook): it removed The Psychic, but with a hand ~0.4x of the Big's pace the run needed shop views more than a better boss. Playing the 3200 Small (3 face-pairs ~1980 or Trips) was plausible and would have opened a shop with ~$10 after Gold TD.
3. Ante 2: buying Scary Face{ETERNAL} at $4 to $0 was right (it made every face pair ~2k); the issue was having only one +mult source (Half Joker) - Scary is chips-only and needs a mult partner.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the shop is EMERGENCY (ratio < 0.35) AND no joker/multiplier is on offer AND you hold $8+, THEN reroll once (or buy the Arcana pack) instead of leaving: leaving with $8 at ratio 0.33 lost the very next blind.
- IF you skip a Small at ante 3 (e.g. Boss Tag to reroll a counter boss), THEN you lose a shop: only do it when the current shop already bought what it could AND your best hand reaches ~1/3 of the Big; otherwise play the Small.
- IF Half Joker is your only mult source, THEN The Psychic is a hard counter (5 cards); a Boss Tag on the Small is a legitimate reroll, but it does not fix 0.33x scaling.
- IF hand 2 of 3 must set up a one-card draw for the final hand (QQQ needs 1 of 2 Qs in 5 draws, ~28%), THEN it is already a ~30% round: the fix is upstream (score), not the dig.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- shopadvise ante 3 (EMERGENCY 0.33, $8): "Arcana Pack $4: would leave $4, below the $8 money floor" and LEAVE, contradicting its own EMERGENCY line ("through the money floor"). In EMERGENCY with no multiplier on offer it should suggest a reroll or the best pack.
- Blind-select advice on the ante-3 Small did not consider the Boss Tag vs a counter boss (The Psychic vs Half Joker); it just said "do not skip".
- Bridge issues: none (40 steps, no rejects).
