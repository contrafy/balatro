Post-mortem
- Binding constraint: SCALING (and money behind it). Antes 1-4 were cleared at 1.05-2x on Two Pair L3 + flat jokers +
  Mystic Summit + Ramen x2 + Photograph. Gold Stake ante 5 is 25000/37500/50000 (2.8x the ante-4 Small); the build made
  6-8k per hand with no glass draw, i.e. ~22k per round. It needed a second permanent xMult or Two Pair L6+ by ante 4.
- Money: I spent to $0-2 every shop from ante 4 (Mega Arcana $8 with $8, Spectral $6 with $8). With $0 at ante 5 the
  Small Blind shop was worthless, so ante 5 had no scaling purchase at all. The $25 Investment and two Hermits were good,
  but the rerolls at ante 2-4 (7 rerolls, ~$40) found only Ramen.
- Decisions I would change: (1) ante 3-4 rerolls: stop at 2 per ante and buy Saturn/Uranus/Celestial levels with the
  rest; (2) Lovers on a suit-boss card (wasted the flush and a tarot); (3) Wraith picked with 6/6 jokers forced selling
  Ice Cream (65 chips) for a random rare that was off-engine (The Tribe) - Familiar/Incantation would have been
  permanent deck value; (4) at ante 4 the Idol (perishable, $6) was a 5-round x2 bridge that would have covered ante 5.
