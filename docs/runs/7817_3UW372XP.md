## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling + boss. I never saw a reliable xMult (5 Buffoon packs: Riff-raff, Business Card{E,R}/Sock,
Credit Card{E}/8 Ball, Chaos/Superposition/To the Moon/Cartomancer; shop xMults: none but Photograph). The engine became
Photograph + Hanging Chad + one Glass QS: x64 on the base mult when QS scores first (Pair QQ 46640, QS alone 22040), but
it is a FACE engine resting on a single card, so The Plant (faces debuffed) left a flat ~8-9k/hand build vs 50000.
Red Card fed by bought-and-skipped packs reached +27 mult, which carried antes 2-4 but is flat.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4-5 shops: I spent $10 + $6 + $4 on packs/skips with The Plant visible as the ante-5 boss from the first ante-5
   shop. The face engine (Photograph + Glass Queen) was always going to be dead there; the only real counters (Luchador,
   Chicot, Director's Cut, a non-face xMult) need money and rerolls. With $14 + $10 at ante 4 I could have rerolled twice
   for a non-face multiplier instead of skipping Celestial/Standard packs for +3 mult each.
2. Ante 2: I sold Riff-raff when slots were full; I could instead have sold one weak joker so it made 1-2 more commons at
   the boss select (one more random scorer for free). Minor.
3. Ante 5 Small/Big: both were cleared by Glass QS; I should have recognised at the ante-5 first shop that the run was
   ~0% vs The Plant unless a non-face scaler appeared, and spent everything on rerolls (EMERGENCY) instead of a Jumbo
   Buffoon + Celestial. Expected value was low either way.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Red Card and a pack offers nothing that beats +3 mult on every hand, THEN skip it: a $4 pack bought and
  skipped is a permanent +3 mult (Red Card went 0 -> 27 here and carried antes 2-4). The pack-level SIM ("skip pack
  +0.00") ignores this; the shop-level SIM ("buy+skip ... Red Card +3") is right.
- IF Photograph + Hanging Chad are owned, THEN Justice (Glass) on a Queen/King that you can put first is the best tarot in
  the game for you: Glass x2 and Photograph x2 each fire 3 times (x64 on base mult): Pair QQ 46640 at ante 5 vs ~9k without.
- IF your multiplier is face-only (Photograph, Glass face, Smiley, Scary) AND The Plant is the upcoming boss, THEN the
  shops of that ante buy only a non-face multiplier or counter (Luchador, Chicot, Director's Cut) and reroll for one; a
  flat +mult build reaches ~9k/hand there (23644/50000 at ante 5).
- IF Riff-raff is owned and slots are full, THEN sell the weakest flat joker (not Riff-raff) before the next blind select
  while Riff-raff has rounds left: each select is up to 2 free commons.
