**Binding constraint:** Cash starvation plus zero xMult by ante 3. I had $1-$6 from ante 2 through the end, one rental (Mad, -$3/round) burning money until I sold it at the ante-2 shop, and no xMult was ever affordable (Campfire $9 eternal, Fibonacci $8 passed). The Straight build had only 2 scorers (Crazy +12 straight-only, Popcorn +20 perishable) and Straight was played only 2 times. At ante 3 the Big Blind (4800) needed about 2x what the pair/two-pair hands could draw into with 3 hands.

**Decisions I would change:**
1. If shop money is under $5 after the ante-1 Big Blind, do not buy a rental (Mad) at all; buy the cheap +mult scorer instead, or keep the $6 for an xMult the next shop.
2. If the ante-2 shop has no xMult affordable and money < $5, sell the rental immediately in that same shop; I delayed that by one shop (paid one extra $3 round).
3. Do not take a Straight main hand with 0 xMult by end of ante 2 (Gold check target 1); instead buy any xMult at the $9 Campfire even at the cost of the $8 floor, because ante 3 Big needs 4800 and two scorers cannot reach it.
4. Do not spend $4 on the Celestial pack at ante 3 when no Saturn/Jupiter is offered; keep the money for a scorer or xMult in the next shop.

**If-then rules:**
- If your main hand is Straight and you have fewer than 3 scorers at ante 3, expect the Big Blind (4800) to be lost; buy the cheapest xMult in the shop (even Campfire at $9 via sale of rental).
- If money is below $5 at a shop and a rental is alive, sell the rental before buying anything else.
- If the Big Blind need is 2x+ your pace after 2 hands, play the made hand (do not dig); the dig at p under 0.3 did not recover.
