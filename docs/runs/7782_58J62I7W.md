Post-mortem:
- Binding constraint: scaling. 3 flat/conditional jokers (Raised Fist, Lusty Joker, Misprint) with no xMult multiplier meant Small Blind ante 4 (5000) simply outgrew hand output even with L2 Full House/Flush/Two Pair. Money was never the issue ($8-13 throughout); the issue was 3 straight shops (ante 3 x2, ante 4 x1) failing to offer any xMult joker, and shopadvise's reroll-then-leave logic stopping rerolls once below the ante-appropriate money floor rather than pushing through.
- The one decision I would change: at ante 3 shop 2 (the $11 shop with Obelisk $8 available), even though shopadvise flagged Obelisk AVOID (penalizes main hand), NO alternative xMult ever appeared across 3 ante-3/ante-4 shops combined. In hindsight, buying ANY conditional/weak xMult early (even Obelisk, playing mostly Two Pair to keep the "most played" bonus) would have been better than banking on flat-mult jokers alone once ante 4-5 hit; a weak xMult beats zero xMult per playbook 7a. I followed shopadvise faithfully but should have overridden once it became clear (2+ consecutive shops with zero xMult offers) that "keep leaving/rerolling once" wasn't converging.
- The final hand's discard (chasing a gutshot/open straight draw with EV 879 against a need of 1179) was mathematically the best of bad options given no made hand and no consumables — not a misplay, just bad luck (drew a Q pair instead of the straight card).
