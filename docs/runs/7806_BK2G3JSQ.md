## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling, specifically no lasting +mult. Every +mult source was a perishable bridge (Half Joker a1-3,
Abstract a4-6, Scary chips a3-5, Stuntman chips a6-7) and each died on schedule, so every ante restarted from Duo x Trio
on a base hand mult of 3-9. The engine that carried antes 4-6 was The Duo + The Trio (x6 on Trips/Full House/Quads) with
a 10-card hand (Troubadour) and Earth/Venus levels: FH one-shots of 13.6k, 33k, 95k. At ante 7 the hand-size trade
(Stuntman -2, then selling Troubadour for Luchador) cut the hand to 6 cards and Trips/FH stopped coming.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 6 shop: Stuntman (+250 chips, -2 hand size) cost the 10-card hand that produced the x6 hands. Score check fell
   45.8k -> 19.8k right after the buy. It still won ante 6 on two FHs, but a hand-gated x6 engine needs hand size more
   than flat chips. Should have kept 10 cards and bought +mult instead.
2. Ante 7: I sold Troubadour for Luchador. Hand 6 + 3 hands made trips rare. Selling Stuntman instead (keep 8 cards,
   2 hands) or skipping Luchador was worth comparing with the SIM before picking.
3. Ante 2-3: The Trio ($8) bought before any lasting +mult. It paid off later, but ante 3 was survived only by skip-
   timing Half Joker's last rounds; a permanent +mult first (Jolly/Joker) would have been safer.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own The Duo AND The Trio, THEN your main hand is Trips/Full House (x6, Quads too): level Earth/Venus, buy
  hand size (Troubadour's -1 hand was +0.24 SIM here), never trade hand size for chips.
- IF a perishable is your only +mult and it has 2 rounds left, THEN skip the Small (if its tag pays) so it lives through
  Big AND boss; here Half Joker scored at The Water (+20 on 2-card pairs) thanks to the skip.
- IF The House deals all cards face down, THEN read the hand order after the first discard: the hand stays rank-sorted,
  so face-down cards between two visible ranks are bounded by them (3 face-downs between 3H and 2D = a Full House of
  3s/2s, played blind for 28956).
- IF Skip Tag is offered late (5+ skips this run), THEN it is $25-30 instantly: skipping a Big with it funded ante 6-7.
- IF Bootstraps is owned, THEN each reroll costs ~2 mult; stop rerolling once a target is found.
- IF Acrobat is the final-hand x3 and a made hand x3 still falls short of the need, THEN play it now and dig with the rest.
