## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no flat chips/+mult beyond Blue Joker, so every big score came from DIGGING with discards for a hand
of the Ancient Joker's suit (flush/straight x5-x57) or a Card Sharp repeat. The Water removed the discards; the deck gave
one straight and no second suited hand, and the final Pair QQ (x4 Triboulet, x3 Card Sharp) was 1828 short. Money was the
second constraint: the wallet sat at $0-7 the whole run (Blue $5, Devious $4, Photograph $5, 2 Celestials, Card Sharp $6
each took it to $0-2), so no shop could ever afford a reroll or a $6+ joker.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 last shop before The Water ($7 -> $3): I passed Sly Joker{ETERNAL,RENTAL} $1 (+50 chips on any pair) because of
   the -$39 eternal rent. The final Pair QQ ran at x48 mult (4 x2 x2 x3): +50 chips = +2400 = 8544 > 7972 needed. The
   whole run died for a $1 joker. At a TIGHT 0.17x boss with 0 discards, the rent is irrelevant next to the loss.
2. Ante 1-2: Blue Joker{ETERNAL} + Devious{PERISH} + Photograph{PERISH}: two perishables bought as bridges meant two
   slots refilled at ante 4, and no +mult joker was ever bought. A flat +mult joker would have scaled every later xMult.
3. Ante 4 Water hand 1: an alternative was to play a 5-card junk hand first and keep the 3 spades/clubs, but the straight
   banked 9468 and the solver agreed; I don't think the play order was the mistake.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the next boss is The Water (or The Needle) AND your scoring depends on discarding into suited/straight hands (Ancient
  Joker, Card Sharp repeats), THEN buy ANY any-hand flat scorer in the last shop before it, even an eternal rental at $1:
  a +50 chip / +mult joker on a x30-x50 mult pair is +2000-4000 points; rent is a later problem.
- IF you own Ancient Joker, THEN check `raw` run.ancient_card.suit each round and dig with discards for 4-5 cards of that
  suit: a 5-card suited flush is x7.6 (x57 with two Ancients); straights with 2-4 suited cards are x2.25-x5 and cleared the
  Wall, the ante-4 Small and Big one-shot here. Choose the suited copy of a rank when building a straight (7C vs 7S: 9468
  vs 4208).
- IF you hold Invisible Joker with 2 rounds done, THEN sell the dead weight (perished jokers) first and sell Invisible in a
  shop so the copy lands on an xMult (here it duplicated Ancient Joker).
- IF a Spectral pack costs $4 and you are broke and TIGHT, THEN it is worth it: Wraith/The Soul turned up twice in two
  Spectral packs (Ancient Joker, Triboulet).
- IF Card Sharp is your xMult and the made hand is a one-off type (Full House), THEN do not play it "to register"; play
  the repeatable type (Pair/Straight) or dig.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- SIM rated Sly Joker{ETERNAL,RENTAL} $1 at -0.20 before The Water (rent beyond the horizon), although its +50 chips on a
  x48 pair would have cleared the boss; the TIGHT/EMERGENCY rule should override the eternal-rental penalty for the next
  boss when no other scorer is affordable.
- Pack SIM said a planet LOWERS P(clear) (Mercury -0.02, Jupiter -0.05 vs skip): a level can never lower score; noise of
  +-0.05 is presented as a decision ("SKIP" was nearly advised).
- Pack SIM rated Invisible Joker -0.03 (cannot model the duplication); it copied Ancient Joker (x1.5 per suited card).
- SCORE CHECK at ante 4 printed ~711-1599 (75th pct, ratio 0.04-0.10) while actual blinds were cleared with 10-31k hands:
  the deal-only estimate ignores discards, so it overstates EMERGENCY for dig-based (Ancient/Card Sharp) builds.
- `advise` at the Wall suggested "PLAY (register for Card Sharp) FULLHOUSE ... later Full House hands score x3", which
  assumes a second Full House; the solver lines were not printed for that suggestion.
- shopadvise kept labelling the main hand "Straight"/"Flush" and printing the pair-family BUILD warning; with Ancient Joker
  the suited flush/straight was the correct engine.
