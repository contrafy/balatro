Post-mortem: Binding constraint was SCALING, not misplay -- by ante 5 we had 7 flat/conditional jokers (2x Mystic Summit, Scholar, Smiley Face, Raised Fist, Blackboard, Wily Joker) and zero reliable xMult. Blackboard (x3 if all held cards Spades/Clubs) never fired all game because our hands mixed suits by design (playing Straights/Flushes across all suits). Full House L3 and Straight L2 leveled but Big Blind at ante 5 (16500) needed ~2-3x more raw power than a Two-Pair/Pair floor could deliver once the good draws ran out.
Decision I would change: when Invisible Joker duplicated into a second Mystic Summit and pushed jokers to 7/6 (over the 6-slot cap), I should have sold ONE of the two Mystic Summits (redundant, both only fire on the last hand of a round) at the ante 5 shop to free a slot for an xMult joker or Square Joker, rather than leaving the roster overfull and unable to buy Square Joker when it appeared. Also: should have kept rebuying the Death/Strength/Justice rescue tarot every shop from ante 3 onward when affordable ($3) rather than skipping it once Wily Joker was preferred -- the final Big Blind loss came down to exactly the situation Death rescued at ante 4 (a non-clearing final hand with 0 discards), except this time no rescue tarot was in hand.
Bridge issues: `cash` failed once after the ante 4 boss with "ACTION FAILED: Round eval blocking events still pending, retry shortly None" -- retried after `sleep 2` and it succeeded on the second attempt. No `est` vs actual score discrepancies over 20% were observed; every played hand's actual score matched or exceeded its `est` (Mystic Summit and joker order effects were already priced into `est`).
Playbook suggestions:
1. When a joker duplication effect (Invisible Joker) pushes the roster over the slot cap ("Jokers (n/slots)" with n>slots), explicitly flag in `shopadvise` that a redundant duplicate (two copies of the same conditional joker) should be sold to restore room for an xMult joker, since two copies of a situational joker (Mystic Summit only fires on 0-discard hands) rarely double its value the way an xMult would use that slot.
2. Add an explicit reminder in section 5/7b: once a rescue tarot (Death/Strength/Justice) is CONSUMED to save a blind, treat re-buying one as equal priority to a first xMult joker at the very next shop that offers one in the card row, not merely "buy if $3 spare" -- this run had exactly one rescue-tarot save (ante 4) and then lost two shops later to the identical scenario (forced final hand, 0 discards, no tarot).
3. Consider flagging in `shopadvise` when a purchased xMult joker's condition (e.g. Blackboard's all-Spades/Clubs) is structurally incompatible with the player's actual leveled hand types (Straight/Full House/Two Pair spanning all suits), since it never fired all game and the $6 spent on it produced zero return.
