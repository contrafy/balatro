## Post-mortem

**Binding constraint:** no scaling joker by ante 2. The run had 3 jokers at the start of ante 2, one rental, and one perishable, so the score ceiling was about 500 per hand. The Wheel (2000) needed roughly 3-4x the score available. Money was $0 to $3 for the whole ante from the Ante 1 Pillar shop onward (Sly Joker at $5, then debt from Walkie rent). With no money, no xMult or planet could be bought in ante 2.

**Decisions I would change:**
1. Ante 1 second shop: buying Walkie Talkie (rental, $1) plus Sly Joker (perishable, $3) left $2 and started rent drain. Better: keep $5-6 and buy Shoot the Moon or nothing, or one scorer that is permanent.
2. Ante 2 shop at $4: Arcana pack ($4) became a planet Uranus (Two Pair) that was 0 use on the Wheel hands (Full House). The planet should have been Earth (Full House) or Mercury (Pair), since the Wheel rounds were pair and full-house draws. Read the planet list before buying the pack.
3. Ante 2 Small Blind: skipping the Small (1000) to get money was not tested; the skip tag would have given an economy tag. Money was the constraint, so the skip deserved a SIM check.
4. Walkie sell at $-2 was right (it stopped the $3/round charge), but it came too late: the rent had already driven money below zero.

**Concrete reusable rules:**
- If money is below $4 at the start of ante 2 and you have no xMult, do not buy the Arcana/Celestial pack: a random tarot or planet will not add score for the boss. Buy the cheapest permanent flat mult instead.
- If a rental costs $3 per round and money is under $5 after buying, sell the rental the same shop.
- If the next boss is more than 2x the projected round, a Full House or Flush plan (Earth/Jupiter levels) beats Two Pair plans; check the planet list before paying for a pack.
- On The Wheel (face-down cards): discards can draw face-down cards; the solver's p values (0.01-0.20) were low-confidence, so do not trust single-hand plans against this boss without 2+ joker scorers.
