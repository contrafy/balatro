## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: zero scoring jokers through the whole of ante 1. Every point came from L1 base hands (Flush/Straight/FH
~300 each); the Manacle's 7-card hand and 2 discards could not assemble two 300-point hands. The only scorer offered in
3 shop views was Dusk ($5, first shop), which I passed for a Buffoon pack that held no scorer.

Decisions I would change:
1. Shop 1 ($5): Dusk over the Buffoon pack. Dusk retriggers every played card on the final hand; on L1 Flush/Straight/FH
   hands that is +40-60 chips x4 mult = +150-250 on the last hand of each blind. At the Manacle my final-hand-equivalent
   Straight (324) would have been ~530, and Big/Small finished on the final-ish hand too. The advisor's SIM showed
   p_next 0.15 vs 0.01 baseline; the pack was a coin flip on a scorer and lost (Mail-In Rebate / Superposition).
2. Pack pick: Mail-In Rebate{ETERNAL} was fine money ($5 at the boss) but no scorer existed in that pack either way.
3. Shop 2 ($6): Celestial -> Venus (Trips L2) never got played. With ante-1 hands being Flush/Straight/FH, Jupiter
   (Flush L2, +15 chips +2 mult ~ +100 per flush) would have helped the Manacle more; I picked the long-term
   pair-family fallback for a run that had to survive the next blind first. [INFERENCE: Manacle draws had no flush either.]

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF ante 1 shop 1 with $5 and 0 jokers offers ANY joker that adds score to hands you make (even final-hand-only like
  Dusk, or a retrigger) AND the alternative is a Buffoon pack, THEN buy the joker: a certain scorer beats a pack that
  can contain zero scorers (this pack: Mail-In Rebate + Superposition).
- IF a Celestial pack is bought at ante 1 with 0 jokers to survive the NEXT blind, THEN take the planet of the hand
  type you actually scored with this ante (Flush/Straight/FH here), not the pair-family long-term fallback.
- IF Mail-In Rebate is owned, THEN read `run.mail_card` (raw) each round and prefer discarding that rank when the
  discard is otherwise neutral ($5 each; paid $5 at the Manacle).
