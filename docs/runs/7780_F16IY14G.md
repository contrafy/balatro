Post-mortem: binding constraint was BOSS LUCK + BUILD DEPTH, not misplay in isolation -- The Club hit three
  separate debuffed-club cards across consecutive draws with only 2 scoring jokers (Raised Fist, Square Joker;
  neither is an xMult and neither scales), so even a perfect sequence of pair-family plays (96+306+180=582) was
  never going to reach 1800 once 3 of 3 discards were spent just clearing debuffed cards instead of building
  hand size/level. The decision I would change: at the ante-2 Big Blind shop with $10, I would have preferred
  buying nothing else and rerolling ONE more time before endshop to try to surface Spare Trousers (or any
  scorer) at an affordable price rather than leaving with $5 -- or, more simply, the Full House hand that ante
  scored 1608/1350 (258 to spare); banking $2-3 less margin there in exchange for reaching $6 for Spare Trousers
  next shop would have given us a real growth multiplier before The Club instead of two flat/conditional jokers.
  A build with zero xMult and no growth joker by ante 2's boss is exactly the failure mode playbook 7a warns
  about ("Green Stake requirements scale faster... an xMult by ante 3, and never enter ante 4 with fewer than
  one") -- here we didn't even have one heading into ante 2's boss.

Bridge issues: none. Every `advise`/`shopadvise`/`play`/`disc` command worked as documented; est matched actual
  score exactly on every hand this run (Flush 300/300, Two Pair 720 actual vs 480 advise-est due to Raised Fist
  filler math but the `play` line's own est of 720 matched exactly, Two Pair 744 actual = play-est 744, Full
  House 1608/1608, Two Pair Big Blind 924/924, and all three forced hands under The Club matched their `play`
  est exactly: 96/96, 306/306, 180/180). No case of est differing from actual by more than 20%.

Playbook suggestions:
  1. Add explicit guidance for suit-debuff bosses (The Club/Goad/Window/Head) on WHEN to stop spending discards
     chasing clean cards: if after 2 discards you still have fewer scoring jokers than 2 non-conditional ones and
     the debuffed suit keeps reappearing in the redraw, consider playing a smaller clean hand earlier rather than
     using the 3rd/last discard, so at least 1 discard survives for the forced final hand of the round (this run
     used all 3 discards before playing a single hand under The Club, leaving zero flexibility for 3 straight
     forced plays).
  2. Section 7a's "Green Stake ante 3-4" data point should be extended one ante earlier: this run shows a
     zero-xMult, two-flat-joker build can already lose at the ANTE 2 boss (The Club, 1800) once a suit-debuff
     boss disrupts a pair-family engine with no scaling behind it -- not just ante 3-4 Big Blinds as currently
     documented.
  3. shopadvise's reroll-then-leave sequence (ante 2 Big Blind shop) surfaced Spare Trousers, the exact joker
     playbook 7b names as the winning-build growth joker, but priced it $1 above the money left after the reroll
     it recommended; consider having shopadvise weigh "reroll now vs. save the reroll fee to afford a named
     scaling joker already glimpsed" when a REROLL result reveals an unaffordable-by-$1 growth joker.
