# Run Q8A71DUR (port 7778) - LOSS at stake 1 ante 3 Big Blind (2978/3000)

Post-mortem: binding constraint was scaling, not money or bad luck on a single draw. Runner, Supernova and
Ride the Bus are all conditional/situational (Runner needs straights we never built; Ride the Bus is fragile
and resets on any face card, which is common; Supernova only grows with repeated Two Pair plays, which we
were not committed to - our main hand was Full House). None of the three is the "reliable xMult" the
playbook (7a) calls for (Duo/Trio, Card Sharp, Hologram/Constellation, Cavendish, Baron). By ante 3 Big
Blind the deficit was small (22/3000, under 1%) but structural: three flat/conditional jokers plus a leveled
Full House (L2) was not enough headroom, and the Big Blind chewed through most of the round before a decent
hand appeared. The one decision I would change: earlier in the run (ante 1-2 shops), sell or avoid Ride the
Bus in favor of holding out for any reliable xMult joker per section 7a/7b, even at the cost of a slower
early game - Ride the Bus's face-card fragility directly cost the hand that would have closed the gap (est
756 collapsed to 378 the moment a King scored).

Bridge issues: `hands`/`advise` estimator significantly overestimated a hand that was about to break Ride
the Bus's streak: est 756 vs actual 378 (50% miss) when playing TWOPAIR K7 (K is a face card, resetting Ride
the Bus's consecutive-non-face mult to 0). This is the second occurrence of an est/actual mismatch exceeding
the playbook's 20% threshold in this run (the first, ante 2 Three of a Kind, est 300 vs actual 240, ~20%,
also Ride-the-Bus-adjacent). No other CLI errors; no `ACTION FAILED` retries needed this session.

Playbook suggestions:
1. `advise`/`hands` est should explicitly discount for jokers like Ride the Bus that reset on the SPECIFIC
   hand about to be played (a face card in the candidate hand), not just show the joker's current stored
   mult - the two miscalibrations this run were both this exact case.
2. Add Ride the Bus to the "conditional, do not rely on as sole scaling" list in section 7a/8 alongside
   Loyalty Card/Ancient Joker/Seeing Double - its reset condition (any face card) triggers far more often
   in practice than the pair/suit conditions of the other listed jokers.
3. When a shop has no reliable xMult and money is tight (ante 1-2), consider this run's evidence that three
   conditional/flat jokers plus one leveled hand can still lose a mid-ante Big Blind by under 1%: the
   playbook's ante 3-5 reroll-for-xMult rule (section 5) should perhaps start one ante earlier (ante 2) when
   $15+ is available after reroll.
