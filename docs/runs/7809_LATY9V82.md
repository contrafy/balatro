Binding constraint: one-hand boss (The Needle, 25000) vs a build whose best hand is ~21-24k at x5: flat +20 mult x one growth xMult, chips from Banner/foil. Missed by 500 chips on a dealt Trips (24500) and then every discard costs 30 Banner chips so digging could not recover. Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 shop 1: rerolled BEFORE buying Mercury ($3): the reroll replaced the whole row and the planet vanished. Buy the main-hand planet first, then reroll.
2. Ante 3-5: I took 5 Celestial packs hoping for Mercury/Venus; 2 hit (Mercury L2, Venus L2), 3 whiffed (Pluto/Uranus/Neptune/Jupiter/Mars). Money at $0-5 every shop meant no Baseball Card/Rocket/rerolls. I should have held cash for the pre-boss shop of ante 5 (the Needle was known from the ante-4 post-boss shop): Baseball Card{E} $8 would have made 24500 -> ~55000.
3. Sold Ceremonial Dagger ($3) from Judgement; fine. But Temperance gave $7 and the pack Hermit would have been better at $13+.
4. Ante 4 Jumbo Buffoon/Celestial/Standard spending left $1 entering the Big; nothing scoring was affordable at the pre-boss shop anyway.
