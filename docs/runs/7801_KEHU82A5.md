## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no permanent scorer ever. In 9 shop views (2 rerolls) the only permanent any-hand +mult jokers offered
were Green Joker $4 (ante 1, passed for Mystic Summit) and Fibonacci $8 (revealed by a reroll that left me $4). Mystic
Summit{PERISHABLE} carried antes 1-2 alone (one-shots of 2200, 1710, 1539) and perished exactly at the ante 2 -> 3 jump
(1000 -> 3200), leaving 0 scorers and $9. Everything else offered was a rental (Photograph, Red Card, Clever, To the Moon,
Baseball) or eternal junk (Certificate, Egg, Splash).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 first shop ($15): Buffoon pack ($4, junk) AND a reroll ($5, junk) left $6. Keeping ~$11 would have carried
   ~$15 into the Small shop, where the reroll revealed Fibonacci $8 (bought = a permanent +8/+16 mult scorer for ante 3).
2. Ante 2 Small shop ($9, 0 permanent scorers): the reroll to $4 could only buy commons; it showed Fibonacci $8 and
   Popcorn $5, both out of reach. Selling the 2-round Summit ($2) for Popcorn ($5) was also possible and I did not do it;
   Popcorn (+20/16/12/8/4) would have covered the ante-3 Big where I scored 1070/4800.
3. Ante 1 shop 1: Summit (perishable) over Green Joker (permanent, weak) was right for antes 1-2 by SIM, but from that
   moment the plan had to be "permanent scorer by the Flint shop"; I spent the ante-2 money on gambles instead.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your only scorer is a perishable bought at ante 1, THEN it dies at the end of ante 2: from the first ante-2 shop,
  save so that every shop holds $8+ (an Uncommon like Fibonacci costs $6-8); spend on ONE gamble (pack or reroll) per
  shop at most, never both.
- IF you are $1-3 short of a permanent scorer AND own a perishable/bridge with <= 2 rounds left, THEN sell the bridge
  and buy the scorer in the same shop (Summit $2 + $4 bought Popcorn here; I missed it).
- IF the ante-3 Small offers a Top-up (or Buffoon) tag AND your projected round is under the Small's need, THEN skip it:
  the Small is lost anyway and the tag's 2 commons are free and sticker-free.
- IF a rental-free shop at ante 2 has nothing but planets/Celestials and you own 0 permanent scorers, THEN leave with the
  money (no planets at 0 scorers); the next shop's joker is worth more than any level.
