## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: MONEY, which then capped scaling. I never held more than $10. Ante 1-2 cash went to one Eternal
straight-only joker ($4) and two planet packs ($10 for Pair L3); the scorers that actually carried ante 2-4 were two
rentals (Blue Joker, Gros Michel) costing -$6 per round against ~$9-13 per ante of income (Small pays $0, no interest
below $5). From the ante-2 boss on I was at $1-2 or negative, so every later shop was dead: Photograph $5 and Clever $4
at ante 3 and Celestial/Arcana at ante 4 were all out of reach. With 0 xMult and Pair L3 the build made ~2700 per pair /
~4400 per straight against ante 4's 4500-per-hand Big.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 first shop ($10): Jumbo Celestial $6 for a Pair level (SIM 0.43). Holding $10 (=$2 interest) or rerolling once
   for a permanent scorer would have kept the economy alive; that $6 was exactly the Photograph money one ante later.
2. Ante 1 Big shop: Celestial $4 -> Mercury. Same issue: a level on a 1-joker build is worth ~+30% of a tiny number;
   $7 held would have bought the ante-2 shop's Runner/Photograph-tier joker without needing rentals.
3. The Tooth: Flush first cost $5. All-pairs/High-Card line would have cost ~$4 total and still cleared (KK 2622 + JJ
   2622 + AH 1440 = 6684 vs 6400); I had 2 discards to find pairs. $4 would not have saved the run, but it was waste.
4. With hindsight the second rental (Gros Michel) was forced: without it the ante-2 boss (2000) was ~600/hand. The real
   mistake was arriving there with $2 and only rentals as scorers.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own 0-1 scoring jokers at ante 1-2, THEN do not buy planets or Celestial packs (even Jumbo, even with SIM gain);
  hold the money for the next shop's joker. Planets multiply a base that is still ~10x2.
- IF rent paid per ante (3 x $3 per rental) is >= your expected income per ante (~$10-13 at ante 2-4 on Gold), THEN you
  are in a debt spiral: skip $0 Small Blinds (no rent tick, tag money now: Skip tag paid $10 here) and sell a flat rental
  the moment a non-rental scorer is affordable. Do not plan on interest; you will have none.
- IF The Tooth is the boss and pairs clear at ~1/3 each, THEN play 2-card pairs / 1-card High Card (Blue Joker + Gros
  made High Card ~1440 here) and use discards to find pairs; a 5-card Flush costs $5.
- IF the last hand cannot reach the need with any non-straight-flush hand, THEN the solver's UNWINNABLE is right; spend
  the last discard on the only out (royal/straight flush draw) rather than on the pair floor.
- Blue Joker counts cards left in the DECK: playing without fillers keeps it larger (+2 chips per undrawn card).
