## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: MONEY and a missing growth xMult. Ante 1-2 I spent every dollar on the first scorers (Even Steven, Scholar, Summit, Blueprint) and then held three rentals ($9/round) from ante 3 on: money sat at -$3 to $5 for the last 3 antes, so every shop (Abstract Joker $4, Buffoon $4, Arcana/Celestial $4, a free Rare tag pending) was unaffordable. The engine was flat chips (Blue Joker +104, Ice Cream +100) + Summit +15 (doubled by Blueprint) x poly Loyalty: ~15k per hand at ante 5, ~60k only on the Loyalty-fire hand, vs 37.5k Big needs ~12.5k x 3 hands with zero variance allowed.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 5 Big: I skipped the Small (Rare tag) to realign Loyalty, but that left 3 hands against 37500 with a ~15k hand. Fighting the 25000 Small first (2 hands, hands 2-3) would have kept the 3rd Small hand's slack and moved the Loyalty fire (x4 = 46k) INTO the Big (3rd Big hand): the Big would have been cleared with ~46k+15k. I counted Loyalty hands wrong (the state says 'x4 in N hand(s) (this one counts)': read it before planning skips, `s` blind-select line did not show it).
2. Ante 4 Needle: kept Blueprint/Summit/Ice Cream rentals ($9/round) instead of selling Ice Cream (decays -5/hand, 75 chips by ante 5). Net money never recovered; with $10 I could have bought Abstract Joker (+3 mult x6 jokers) or the 4th joker-slot xMult in the ante 4-5 shops.
3. Ante 5 Big hand 1: I held JJ99 and discarded to FH-draw; a 4-of-a-kind/flush route was not available. Trips (L2) vs FH (L1) were equal, so FH was no upgrade; I should have used the discards to chase an EVEN-card hand (Even Steven +4 x5 evens = +20, doubled by Blueprint) rather than odd 9s/Js.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Loyalty Card, THEN read `advise`/`hands` note 'Loyalty x4 in N hand(s) (this one counts)' and arrange for the fire hand to fall on the blind you cannot otherwise clear (boss or the hardest Big): it was 3.1-3.9x a normal hand (46320 vs 14976). Hands are counted across blinds; a skipped blind plays 0 hands.
- IF you own Mystic Summit (+15 mult at 0 discards) and Blueprint, THEN put Blueprint directly left of Summit and always spend ALL discards on the first hand of a blind (est jumps ~5-15x; est shown by advise before the last discard is spent is 5-10x too low, ignore it).
- IF Blue Joker is owned, Standard-pack cards add +2 chips each; fine as a free pick but not a plan.
- IF you hold 3 rentals ($9/round) and income per round is <$9, THEN you are at $0 forever and cannot buy any xMult: sell the weakest rental (decaying Ice Cream) the moment a scorer is covered. Every round end costs $9; skipping a blind (rent-free) at least preserves $9.
- IF a Blueprint is owned and the final hand is short by <10%, THEN test `move jokers` orders with `hands`: copying Even Steven gave 17080 vs 15633 when the hand had five even scoring cards (the advisor does not search Blueprint targets per hand).
- IF Needle/one-hand boss: bare Pair already clears 9000 when Summit is active; the solver p>=0.99 was right.
