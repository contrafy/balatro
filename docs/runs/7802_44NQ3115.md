## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling (multiplier), caused by slot lock. By ante 3 four of the six regular slots were ETERNAL
flat jokers (Even Steven, foil Raised Fist, Half Joker, Scary Face). The only multiplier I ever found was Joker Stencil
(x2 with exactly one empty slot), and with 4 eternals + Stencil every further joker, even an xMult, first cost Stencil
x1 (-50%). So the build was capped at roughly (45 chips-ish base + Scary) x ~45 mult x 2 = 10-15k per hand at ante 5,
i.e. ~37k a round against 50k. The engine itself worked: Raised Fist + Half Joker + Stencil on 1-card High Cards
(Pluto x3 -> High Card L5 45x5) with 2 low fillers per play to lift Raised Fist's lowest held card.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 shop: Scary Face{ETERNAL} as the 4th eternal flat joker (SIM 0.99 vs 0.69 for the next 3 blinds). It got
   me through ante 3-4 but left 2 free slots for the whole run; the checklist's 'never a 4th non-xMult eternal' was right.
2. Ante 2-3: Half Joker{E} as the 3rd eternal was also a slot lock; a non-eternal +mult would have kept options open.
3. Ante 5 post-Big shop ($31, TIGHT 0.69): 3 rerolls ($18) found nothing; with a Stencil build a joker could not have
   helped anyway unless x2.5+. Better: Hanged Man ($3, thin 2s/3s so Raised Fist reads +20 more often) + Empress and
   keep $20 for Celestials. Probably still a loss at 0.69x.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you already own 3 eternal non-xMult jokers, THEN do not buy a 4th one even at SIM +0.30: from then on every
  multiplier must displace a non-eternal slot, and a Stencil/xMult plan has nowhere to go.
- IF Joker Stencil is your only multiplier, THEN a new joker must be worth more than x2 (it costs Stencil x1); in
  Buffoon packs take a junk joker only to sell it at once for $2-3 (pick + sell keeps the empty slot). Hunt Negative
  jokers and planets instead of jokers.
- IF you own Raised Fist + Half Joker, THEN your main hand is a 1-card High Card: level it with Pluto (HC L5 45x5
  beat Pair L2 25x3), play the scoring card plus your 2 LOWEST cards as fillers (3 cards keep Half Joker), and discard
  low cards before the first play so the lowest held card is 9+ (+18-20 mult). Under The Mouth lock High Card.
- IF Raised Fist is owned and Hierophant/Empress targets are chosen, THEN never target a glass card (it overwrites
  the enhancement): put Bonus on faces you play as High Card.
- IF the Small Blind's tag is Buffoon at ante 3-4 and your shop money is <$12, THEN skip it: two Buffoon skips gave me
  Banner+Photograph and Joker Stencil when the shops offered nothing.

  ranked Stencil -0.01); selling Photograph + Banner and taking Stencil was the best move of the run (Pair TT 5040 -> ~8000).
- Arcana pack advice `pick 3 4 5` put Hierophant on 8S and the GLASS 8H (would replace Glass with Bonus).

- `s` at blind select said 'do not skip' for both Buffoon Tags (no free slot) though selling a flat joker inside the
  pack makes room; and 'ante 5+: never skip' for a Skip Tag worth $20 when the post-Small shop would have had $4.
