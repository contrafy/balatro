## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: MONEY -> only two jokers ever. Gold Small Blinds pay $0 and Spare Trousers' rent took $3 every round
(8 rounds = $24, i.e. 4-5 shop jokers). Wallet after each cash-out: $0, $2, $6, $10, $8, $-1, $1, $3. Every shop from
ante 3 on had $-1..$3, so I never added a third scorer, an xMult, or a second planet. A two-joker flat Two Pair engine
(Two Pair L3, +36 mult) makes ~3300-4000 per Two Pair, so ante 4 Small (9000) needed Two Pair on all 3 hands with
2 discards; one dead hand (no pair after both discards were spent on hand 1) lost the run. Variance, not a misplay,
decided the last blind, but the build was structurally lost going into ante 4 (Big 13500 / boss 18000 out of reach).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 Big shop ($10): I rerolled for $5 instead of buying Scholar ($4, +20 chips +4 mult per Ace) or Zany ($4, +12
   mult fires on Full House). The reroll showed nothing, so I left ante 2 with 2 jokers and $5. A third flat joker was
   worth ~+15-30% per hand for the rest of the run; at $10 with 2 jokers, buy the scorer, do not reroll.
2. Ante 2 Big Blind (Buffoon Tag): skipping the Big for a free Mega Buffoon pack (2 of 4 jokers) would have added two
   jokers when money could never buy them. I followed "never skip Big"; with a rental-drained wallet it may be the
   only way to get jokers 3-4. Not tested.
3. Ante 4 Small, hand 1: I spent both discards on hand 1 (no pair dealt) and then needed Death anyway. With Death held,
   using Death first (any pair + Death = Two Pair) and keeping the discards for hands 2-3 would have spread the pair-
   finding resources across the three hands that all had to be Two Pairs.

What worked: Mad Joker (eternal) + Spare Trousers on Two Pair carried ante 1-3 on $1 of joker spend (2P L1 840 at ante
1, ~3000 per hand at ante 3). Emperor from a Jumbo Arcana produced High Priestess (-> Uranus) + Death. Handy Tag skip at
ante 2 Small was +$8 net (tag $5 + no rent tick).

## Reusable rules (if-then)
- IF you hold a RENTAL scorer and Small Blinds pay $0 (Gold) THEN count income per ante as ~ Big $4 + Boss $5 + unused
  hands - $9 rent = ~$1-3: you will not afford shop jokers from ante 3 on. Buy jokers 3 and 4 at ante 2 when you have
  $8-10, even mediocre +mult ones; never reroll away the last affordable scorer.
- IF your only engine is one pair-family type (Two Pair) THEN a blind needs that type on every hand once the requirement
  is ~3x your per-hand est; plan for 1 dead hand in 3, i.e. per-hand est must reach requirement/2 to be safe.
- IF you hold Death and the dealt hand has exactly one pair plus a high single THEN use Death first (copy the single onto
  junk, `move hand` the good card to the right first) and keep the discards for the later hands.
- IF a Death copy target sits left of all junk cards THEN `move hand <good> 8` first: Death overwrites the LEFT card.
- IF a boss debuffs a suit THEN debuffed cards still form the pair: keep a debuffed card that completes Two Pair (the
  Head: 2P AT with debuffed TH still scored 3128 with Mad + Trousers).
