## Post-mortem

**Binding constraint.** The run never had a reliable xMult. Hand levels stayed low (Two Pair L3, others L1), and the joker row was all flat +mult and chips (Walkie, Even Steven, Abstract, Banner, Drunkard, Raised Fist). By the Window (ante 4) the ratio was already ~0.29 vs the next boss. Ante 5 Big 37500 needed ~3 hands of ~12.5k each; the max we produced was ~12.4k per hand, which wasn't enough because the jokers were all flat. The Window debuffed diamonds, which wasted most of the discards.

**Decisions I would change.**
- Ante 3 Buffoon: I took Photograph (perishable x2) over Wily Joker. Fine, but I did not hunt xMult. Buffoon packs should have been used for xMult (Sock/Baron-type) rather than flat +mult.
- Ante 4 shop: I opened Buffoon instead of the advised Arcana (SIM 0.74 vs 0.64). Buffoon gave Drunkard (eternal, +1 discard). Drunkard does not add score. The Arcana then gave a Hierophant (bonus cards), also not a multiplier. Next time: with $8 and no xMult, pick the pack most likely to contain xMult (Buffoon), but skip discards-only jokers.
- Ante 5 shop: Celestial pack (Uranus) over Mad Joker rental. Uranus gave +L3 Two Pair (60x4 vs 40x3). Acceptable, but score was still insufficient.
- Ante 4 Window: I discarded debuffed cards and drew into a straight. Nothing could have fixed the 18k need without an xMult.

**If-then rules.**
- If the ante-4 shop has money $8+ and no reliable xMult, buy an xMult before any planet or flat +mult. Two Pair L3 is not a substitute.
- If a boss debuffs a suit (e.g. The Window: diamonds), count the debuffed cards before planning: they score nothing, so do not discard for a flush or straight that needs them.
- If the next boss needs more than 3x your best single hand (e.g. 37500 vs ~12k per hand), the run is lost without an xMult. Spend all money on xMult at that shop.
- If the solver says "clears 0.99" but the blind need exceeds 3 hands of the best printed est, the solver p is wrong. Use est, not p.

**Advisor / CLI defects.**

- Play-line est (e.g. "PAIR TS TH est 7280") matched the actual score, but the candidate list est was lower (list "PAIR T est 5980"). Trust the play-line est for banking choices.
