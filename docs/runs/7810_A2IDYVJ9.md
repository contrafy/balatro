## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING + MONEY. Zero xMult all run, Pair only L2, money $0 or negative from ante 2 on (rentals, $0 small blind, Tooth). Score depended on one rental (+15 mult Gros Michel) and one perishable (Greedy); both died exactly between the Big and the boss of ante 3, taking ~60% of the per-hand score with them (two pair est 3100 -> ~1500).
Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 shop at $6 (after boss 1): I left with $6 rather than buying Jumbo Arcana / rerolling; later I never had >$8. Not clearly wrong, but the real issue was income: 4 jokers' worth of rent+perish meant no lasting build was ever funded.
2. Ante 3 shop 1 ($4): Celestial gave Uranus (Two Pair L2); fine. But with $-1 at shop 2 there was Death/Justice/Jumbo Buffoon on offer and no money: I should have SOLD Greedy (1 round left) + Zany (2 left) = $4 + GM... to buy the $6 Jumbo Buffoon Pack when GM was alive? Better: sell the perishables the moment they have <=1 round to live, in the shop BEFORE they die, to fund a permanent joker.
3. Gros Michel 1/6 destruction chance hit at the worst time. With rental GM and no second lasting mult source, the run was one coin-flip from losing its engine. A rental/perishable engine needs a replacement bought in the same ante.
Reusable rules:
- IF your only big mult sources are rental/perishable jokers, THEN at the ante's last shop sell any with <=1 round left and spend the cash on a permanent scorer; otherwise your per-hand score halves going into the boss.
- IF the Buffoon Tag is on the ante-2 Small, THEN skip it: Mega Buffoon (pick 2) gave +15 mult GM and carried Big and Manacle boss.
- IF Greedy Joker (+3 mult per Diamond) is owned, THEN prefer playing diamonds as scoring cards (pair with a diamond ~1400 vs ~300 without).
- IF the ante-3 boss is The Tooth and money is negative, THEN every card played costs $1 but that is irrelevant; the problem is 6400 needs ~2200/hand. Plan 2 Two Pair hands of 3000+ before ante 3.
- IF GM is rental, THEN account for 1/6 destroy per round end: expect it to last ~6 rounds.
