## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING. At the ante-3 Small I had five flat or dead jokers (Misprint, Walkie Talkie, Superposition, Wrathful,
Wily), no hand above L1, and no xMult. My real Pair/Two Pair hands were 150-700 against a 3200 Small Blind. Ante 1-2 cleared on
lucky draws (an A-high straight, a flush, two full houses), and that hid how weak the build was. The SCORE CHECK said so
(0.23 at the ante-2 shop, 0.18 at ante 3), and the scores I actually got matched it.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante-3 shop: I passed holo Scary Face {ETERNAL,RENTAL} for $1 because of the -$54 lifetime rent. At an EMERGENCY ratio of 0.18,
   +10 mult (holo) plus +30 chips per face would have roughly doubled every hand (base mult ~12-16 -> ~22-26). The rent only matters
   if I survive, and the $5 I spent on the reroll instead bought nothing. The advisor's "NEEDED NOW" was right.
2. Ante-3 shop: I kept $8 for the money floor instead of opening the $4 Spectral pack (Wraith/Aura). I died holding $8. Below a
   0.35 ratio the floor protects nothing.
3. Ante 2: skipping the Small Blind for Top-up gave two random Commons (Superposition = dead, Wrathful = +3 per spade). The shop
   visit I gave up was worth more to an under-scaled build than two Commons that were random by design.
4. Ante-1/2 shops: I never bought a planet. Wily + trips/full house had no level support. A Celestial pack at ante 2 with $13
   would have helped more than holding cash.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF SCORE CHECK < 0.35 (EMERGENCY) at ante 3 and the shop has a +mult joker with a holo/poly edition, even ETERNAL+RENTAL,
  THEN buy it: the rent is a cost you pay only if you survive.
- IF you are in EMERGENCY and still hold $8+ after the shop's best item, THEN spend it on the next-best score item (pack,
  planet). Dying with the floor money intact was the worst outcome in this run.
- IF ante 1-2 blinds are cleared only by Straight/Flush/Full House draws, THEN treat the build as unscaled: the SCORE CHECK
  ratio is the real signal, not the blinds you cleared.
- IF a Top-up Tag would fill slots with random Commons while you own no xMult and fewer than 2 good scorers, THEN take the shop
  visit instead (Gold policy rule 12 only lets you skip for a tag that pays now; Top-up pays junk now).
