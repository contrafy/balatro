## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING, compounded by boss luck. We never found or could afford a single xMult joker the entire run (Baron $8 appeared once at ante 3, unaffordable alongside the celestial-pack scaling buy that shop). All 6 joker slots were flat +mult/+chips/economy jokers riding hand-level scaling only (per playbook 7a's stated strategy), which worked fine through ante 3 but wasn't enough margin for The Head at ante 4: three hands in a row were dealt majority-Hearts (the debuffed suit), zeroing out most of each hand's card-chip contribution. The final score (9524) was 95% of the 10000 target - a small, boss-luck-driven shortfall, not a strategy failure per se.

One decision to change: with 0 xMult after 4 antes and money repeatedly bottoming out at $0-5, I should have deviated from "money floor $0, spend on scaling" and instead skipped a scaling buy in ante 3 or 4 to bank $10+ toward Baron the next time an xMult joker appeared, even at the cost of one level-up. The playbook's own ante-3+ rule ("if you own no xMult joker and have $15+ after reroll, REROLL until one appears") was never actionable here because money never reached $15 - the run needed a deliberate savings turn earlier to make that rule reachable.

Bridge issues: `buy N` on a Planet card in the shop puts it into a CONSUMABLE slot rather than applying it immediately (unlike Celestial-pack planets, which auto-apply). Required an explicit `use N` afterward each time (ante 3 buy 2 Uranus, ante 4 High Priestess->Uranus). No error text, just an easy step to miss - flagging as a playbook gap.

Est vs actual calibration: two hands differed from `est` by more than 20%:
  - Ante 1 Small Blind, TWOPAIR Q4: est 480 vs actual 528 (+10%, within tolerance)
  - Ante 2 Big Blind, TWOPAIR 75: est 1240 vs actual 1612 (+30%, over tolerance)
  - Ante 1 Big Blind, TWOPAIR Q4 (round 2): est 480 -> covered above
  All other hands this run were within ~10-15% of est. The +30% outlier suggests `est` may sometimes undercount a joker interaction (likely Even Steven's even-card bonus stacking with Spare Trousers/Clever Joker in combos with several even ranks) - worth double-checking the est formula for TWO PAIR hands with 2+ even-rank cards.

Playbook suggestions:
  1. Add an explicit note under section 5 / the CLI reference: buying a Planet directly from the shop (not from a pack) goes to a consumable slot and needs `use N` to apply - it does not auto-level like pack planets.
  2. Add a concrete money-management rule: if ante 3+ ends with 0 xMult jokers and money has stayed under $10 for 2+ consecutive shops, skip ONE scaling purchase (planet/pack) that shop to bank toward the reroll-for-xMult threshold, rather than always spending down to the floor.
  3. For suit-debuff bosses (The Head/Club/Goad/Window), consider having `advise`/`hands` weight the discard EV calculation to actively avoid drawing into the debuffed suit when a made pair of the non-debuffed suit is available, since debuffed-suit hands can silently lose most of their card-chip value even when the hand "completes".
