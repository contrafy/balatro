## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: multiplier / money. At ante 5 I owned exactly one real xMult (Acrobat x3, final hand only) plus a perishable Stencil (x4) and no scorer besides Jolly. A typical round (~36k) equalled the Big Blind (37500) with no slack, and Big needed a Trips on the final hand (Trips final ~32k) PLUS ~5.6k from the other two hands. I had $4 at the Big (the $27 from ante 4 went on 2 rerolls ($11), Mercury, Celestial, Arcana, then rent), so Half Joker foil ($7, +20 mult +50 chips, fires on my <=3 card plays) was unaffordable.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 5 shop 1 ($27): two rerolls ($11) for an xMult found nothing (To the Moon, Supernova{R}, Hologram) and left $16; then planets/packs left $5. Half Joker foil was in the NEXT shop at $7. Keeping $18+ would have bought it (+20 mult + 50 chips on every <=3-card Pair/Trips: about +60% on top of a ~19 mult hand). Rule: with a 3-card-play engine (Acrobat/no Fist) do not spend below $10 on rerolls/planets in the shop before a blind projected at ~1.0x; the next shop's scorer needs the cash.
2. Ante 4: skipped the Big for a Buffoon Tag that gave a junk Jumbo pack (Zany{R}, Scary Face{E}, To Do List, Flash Card) and skipped the shop before the Club boss; the tag is a gamble. The Big itself (13500 vs my ~19k) was a clear, and its $5 + a shop visit was worth more than a 25% chance at a good joker.
3. Ante 5 Big: I dug with BOTH discards holding two pairs for 4 trips outs (33% each) and failed twice; no alternative existed once est trips was the only path. The real error was arriving at a 37500 blind with no flat +mult beyond Jolly: Wrathful (sold for Stencil) and Raised Fist (perished) were both gone.
4. Perishable Raised Fist + rental Stencil were bridges I did not replace in time: both died/expired within 3 rounds of ante 5 with $4 in hand.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF Raised Fist is owned, THEN play the Pair with 3 low fillers so the HELD cards are high (A/K/J): est 864 -> 5040. IF the lowest held card is DEBUFFED (The Club/Goad/etc.), Raised Fist gives 0, so play that card first as junk (5040 -> 36630 on the final Trips).
- IF you own Acrobat and no Raised Fist, THEN fillers add nothing: play only the 2-3 scoring cards (Half Joker would fire) and use the extra draws only when digging.
- IF Joker Stencil is owned, THEN selling the other flat jokers raises it +1 per slot (x3 -> x4): worth it when the flat joker adds < 1/3 of the round. A Stencil + final-hand xMult (Acrobat) round reached ~36k at ante 5, but both perish/expire: have the next scorer funded before the Stencil's last round.
- IF the blind is projected at ~1.0x with a final-hand-only xMult, THEN the clear needs a Trips/FH on the final hand: hold $7+ for the next shop's flat +mult joker instead of rerolls.
- IF The Mouth locks Pair and you own Raised Fist, THEN lock Pair with low fillers: 928 / 754 / 1890 cleared 2000 comfortably.
- IF an Orbital Tag (level-up of a hand) is on the ante-2 Small, THEN take it: Trips L4 gave 2091 on a bare 3-card play and later 10-36k finals.
