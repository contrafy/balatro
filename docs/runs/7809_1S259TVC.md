## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling + money. I never owned a permanent reliable xMult. The run's multipliers were Baseball Card
(x1.5 per uncommon: Dusk, Burglar) and a perishable rental Duo; once Duo perished after the ante-5 boss the build made
~5k per hand against a 60000 Small. Three rentals at once (Baseball, Duo, Burglar = -$9/round) plus the ante-4 Madness
buy drove money to $-8, so ante 5-6 shops could buy nothing (no rerolls, no packs, no planet) for 3 consecutive shops.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 Madness $7 (SIM +0.40 with baseline 0.00): the SIM ignores that Madness destroys a random joker on every
   Small/Big select. With 5 key jokers its EV is below 1x; I sold it unused one blind later for $3, a net -$4 at the
   moment money was the constraint. Those $4 + interest would have bought a Celestial/Arcana at ante 5.
2. Burglar as a third rental: it was right for the ante-4 Big/boss (6 hands carried 13.5k and 18k), but its $3/round
   rent on top of Baseball + Duo meant no interest and negative money from ante 4 on. Without the Madness buy I would
   have entered ante 5 with ~$5 instead of $1, still too little; the real fix was decision 3.
3. Ante 3: The Duo perishable was the only xMult offered in 10 shop views; with it I should have saved for a
   permanent xMult replacement instead of spending on Celestials (Mercury/Uranus gave +1 mult each on ~33 flat mult).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your key xMult is perishable with 1 round left at the start of an ante, THEN skip Small and Big (no round end =
  no perish tick and no rent) and fight the boss with it alive. Here it turned an unwinnable ante 5 (Big 37500 at ~4k
  per hand without Duo) into a boss clear (50000 at ~9k per hand with Duo).
- IF you own Burglar (0 discards), THEN Mystic Summit is a flat +15 mult on every hand: buy it on sight.
- IF you own Baseball Card, THEN count uncommon jokers (Dusk, Burglar, Madness, Oops, Mystic Summit is common) as
  x1.5 each when ranking pack/shop picks; a utility uncommon is a real multiplier.
- IF you own Dusk and The Serpent/last hand needs a big score, THEN hold the trips/FH for the final hand: Dusk doubles
  the card chips (KKKK quads 23310 vs ~14k earlier). Under The Serpent play 2-card pairs so the hand grows (+1 card).
- IF Madness is offered and you own 4+ jokers you need, THEN do not buy it: it destroys one of them every Small/Big.
- IF you hold 3 rentals ($9/round) and money is < $10, THEN sell the weakest rental before the next round end; negative
  money blocks every shop until you win a blind.
