## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no real multiplier and a decaying build. The whole engine was 1-card High Card plays (Smiley +5/face, Photograph x2, Abstract +18, Card Sharp x3 on the repeat): every round was 1 normal hand + 2 x3 hands = ~5k + 15k + 15k = 35k at the very best, and The Wall asked 36000 at ante 4 (Gold boss is x2 on 18000). Ice Cream also lost 5 chips per hand (it was ~60 of my 165 chips). 6 jokers, all weak scorers, no permanent growth; Photograph/Abstract were perishable.

Decisions I would change:
1. Skipped the Big for the Buffoon Tag at ante 4 BEFORE the Wall. The pack was all barred/junk and the skip removed the only shop before a boss my own SCORE CHECK said I could not beat (est 10.9k/hand x 3 < 36k). I should have played Big and kept shopping with $38-45 (and rerolling) for a Mystic Summit-type +15 mult / any xMult. A skip for a *pack* tag is only good when you have a free slot and a plan for the pick; here slots were full and the second-rental rule barred the best card.
2. Declined Obelisk (Jumbo Buffoon, $6 pack) at ante 4: it was the only growth xMult seen in 4 antes. With slots full I judged it x1 start = a loss, but the build was decaying anyway (Ice Cream, 2 perishables). With 3 spare face pairs, Obelisk plus rotating types was the 9IIGB2FF line. Rule: at ante 3-4, with a decaying build and no xMult but Card Sharp, take the growth xMult and sell the decaying joker.
3. Passed Mystic Summit{ETERNAL} $5 at the ante-4 shop (+15 mult at 0 discards, trivially on with 2 discards spent) to reroll; the reroll also deleted it from the shop. If a +15 mult flat joker is on screen and you are SCORE CHECK 0.65, buy it first, then reroll.
4. Chose a High Card engine. The est on one card with Photograph/Smiley is fine up to ante 3 but caps at ~16k per x3 hand; a face Two Pair (4 faces ~24k x3 hand) is worth 50% more. Face pairs/Two Pair should have been the build target (Smiley, Photograph, Scary-type).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the boss is The Wall (x2 chips) and your repeated-type hand x3 is below boss/2, THEN your only outs are shops: never skip the Big/Small in front of it for a pack tag unless the free slot exists.
- IF you own Card Sharp, THEN the maximum round is (h1 + 3 x h2 + 3 x h3) with all three hands the SAME type; compute it before choosing the type: HC faces max 34.6k, face Pair ~36k, face Two Pair ~50k+. Pick the type by that sum, not the single hand est.
- IF a chip joker decays (Ice Cream -5/hand) THEN each hand of the round is ~3% weaker than `est` at the start; est already includes the current value but not the later decay (hand 3 was 6% below hand 1).
- IF Smiley Face + Photograph are both owned, THEN order Smiley LEFT of Photograph (per-card, left to right: (2+5)x2 beats 2x2+5).
- Economy Tag at $34: skipping the Big for $34 -> $68 is only good if there is something to buy; with full slots I rerolled 6 times for nothing. Money over $25 earns nothing: keep rerolling jokers only when a slot is free or a weak joker can be sold.
