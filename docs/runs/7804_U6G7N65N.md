## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. Zero xMult offered in 9 shop views + 3 rerolls (ante 1-4). The build was all flat:
Green Joker{E} (grew to +22 by never discarding), Banner (+60 chips), Bootstraps (+2-6), Abstract{PERISH} (+12).
That tops out at ~4-4.5k per hand at ante 4 (Pair/Two Pair), ~5.5k for a Flush/Straight; the Tooth needs 6k/hand.
SCORE CHECK said EMERGENCY (0.17-0.35) every shop from ante 2 on and was right.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 Buffoon pick: Green Joker{ETERNAL} vs Credit Card. Green was a fine early scorer and grew to +22, but it
   forced a no-discard style for the whole run, which also kept me off flushes/straights. Acceptable; not the loss cause.
2. Ante 2 first shop: $5 reroll at $13 (found nothing). Keeping it would have made Bootstraps/Banner affordable earlier
   and added interest. Same lesson as 7804_I9HDGAQA: at $9-13 a reroll rarely pays at ante 2.
3. Ante 4: two rerolls ($10 total) found nothing; skipping the Big removed one shop. With 0 xMult at ante 4 and a 0.3
   score ratio the run was ~dead either way; an earlier Arcana/Buffoon gamble at ante 3 (instead of Celestial for Mercury)
   might have had a better xMult chance.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Green Joker AND Banner, THEN never discard (each discard = -1 Green permanently and -30 chips this hand);
  play the best made hand + fillers to cycle cards. This gave clean clears through ante 3.
- IF you own Banner or Green Joker, THEN Mystic Summit is a trap: reaching 0 discards costs more than +15 mult
  (Pair L2: 105 chips x 34 mult -> 45 x 47).
- IF Bootstraps is owned AND the boss is The Tooth, THEN every card played lowers Bootstraps before scoring
  (5-card Flush est 5796, actual 5520 because $17 -> $12): count Bootstraps at money-after-cards.
- IF the flat build's 75th-pct hand is under boss/3 of THIS ante at the ante-4 shop with 0 xMult, THEN skipping the
  Big (Skip tag money, perishable ticks avoided) is no worse than fighting it: the boss is the death check either way.
