## Post-mortem
- Binding constraint: no xMult and no real scoring engine by ante 3. Jokers were Juggler (no score), Greedy (+3 mult per diamond, conditional), Jolly (+8 mult per pair, rental). Two Pair at L2 scored ~600-840 per hand; Gold Big 4800 needs ~1600/hand over 3 hands with no xMult. The ante 2 boss was cleared only by a last-hand Full House and facedown luck, so zero margin entering ante 3.
- Decisions I would change:
  1. Ante 2 shop with $3 and Fortune Teller advised as BUY: I left at $3 (second rental, correctly barred). Better: at ante 2, spend the $6 Buffoon pack earlier or keep Walkie as the 1 rental and buy a non-rental xMult/planet; never run at $1 for two shops in a row.
  2. Ante 2 Small skip at $1 after the Jolly swap saved $3 rent once, but it also skipped the only ante-2 income. Skipping a 1000 blind with no scorer besides Greedy/Jolly removed the only cash source.
  3. Ante 3 Big 4800 was entered with one Celestial planet and no xMult. Skip-Small at ante 3 gave a Foil Tag but no money; the $1 left made the Big unwinnable.
- Reusable if-then rules:
  - If boss is The Mark and facedown cards exist: count facedown as face cards; keep a made pair/face pair, do not discard it for pair draws.
  - If the advisor says BUY a rental and you already own one: do not buy it; sell the old rental in the same shop only if the new one is strictly better (Jolly +8 pair beat Walkie +4 conditional).
  - If Two Pair at L2 is the main hand and est < 1000/hand at ante 3 Big (4800): expect a loss unless xMult is bought in the same shop; prioritize xMult over a Celestial pack at $4-5.
  - If skipping a Small at ante 2-3 leaves $1 and no xMult: do not skip; play the blind for $0-$3 and buy xMult with the reward.
