# Run 5MLA5VY7 (port 7780) - stake 3 (Green Stake) - LOSS at ante 3 Small Blind

Ante 1: Skipped Small Blind (Charm Tag -> free Mega Arcana pack, per playbook stake-3 rule: $0 reward,
  no joker-giving tag). Picked High Priestess (banked Pluto + Mars planets) and The Hermit ($4 -> $8) from

Post-mortem: binding constraint was SCALING - by ante 3 the deck had zero xMult that actually fits its
  pair/full-house shape (Driver's License needs 16+ enhanced cards we never bought/created) and no planet
  had ever leveled our Full House (still L1 all game; Uranus for Two Pair was offered at $3 in the ante-3
  shop and was skipped in favor of the Spectral pack). The requirement curve outpaced a flat, unlevel engine:
  2600 for ante-3 Small Blind against a L1 Full House averaging ~400-650/hand with no discards left on the
  final two hands is not reliably clearable. Decision I would change: at the ante-3 shop, buy Uranus ($3,
  planet for the actual second-most-played hand) before gambling $6 on the Spectral pack, or skip the pack
  entirely and bank the $10 toward a real xMult/rescue tarot next shop - arriving at ante 3's boss-adjacent
  blinds with a L1 main hand and zero fitting multiplier is what actually lost the run, not any single bad
  hand of cards.

Bridge issues: `shopadvise` incorrectly reported "you own NO scoring joker" / "0 scoring joker(s)" on at
  least 3 separate shop visits (ante 1 shop 2, ante 2 shop 2, ante 2 shop 2 continued) while the player
  demonstrably owned Sly Joker, a Pair-triggered floor joker that the playbook itself classifies as scoring
  and that fired correctly in every subsequent hand (`ab={"t_chips":50}`, verified firing in the `est`/actual
  scores above). This pushed the advisor toward REROLL recommendations that would have dropped cash to $0-1
  even though a real scorer was already held; I overrode these three times (documented above) using the
  playbook's own "own >=1 scoring joker means don't breach the $4 reserve" logic. No other CLI/ACTION FAILED
  errors occurred all game; every `play`/`disc`/`pick`/`buy`/`cash`/`blind`/`skip` call succeeded on first try.

Playbook suggestions:

     Pair-triggered floor joker, +50 chips if Pair) as non-scoring in at least 3 shop visits this run, even
     though the same tool correctly recognized it later ("jokers 2/6" ... "main hand Full House" messages

     back on when the cards did not cooperate.
