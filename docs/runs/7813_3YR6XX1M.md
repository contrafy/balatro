## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. Entered ante 5 with ONE real xMult source (Ancient Joker, found at the last second by Wraith) plus a polychrome x1.5; ~10k per hand vs 50000 boss / 25000 Small. Credit Card debt (-$5 to -$13 for 3 antes) meant no real shops from ante 3: two rentals ate $6/round and I could never buy the joker views that find xMults.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 shop: I took Raised Fist{RENTAL,PERISHABLE} ($1) and, at ante 3, Abstract{RENTAL}. The rents (-$6/round) drained me into permanent debt; at ante 3-4 every shop was "broke, buys nothing". Sell Abstract/rentals the moment a second scorer lands, or do not hold two.
2. Ancient Joker came at the very last moment. The Arm boss was lost by 5016: had I kept Smiley (sold for the Wraith slot) and pre-planned the Club flush, a 5-club flush est ~70k. Quads of Aces (Grim Aces + AC lucky) scored only 22223: Ancient suit bonus appears much lower than x1.5^clubs in the actual score (est 19469 vs actual 22223), do not plan on it as x3.4.
3. At ante 5 Small I played 3 hands (26k total) when 2 would have cleared with a better draw plan; leaving 3 hands for the boss with a discard unused is better. Also I used both boss discards chasing clubs (found Quads instead of a flush).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the shop has no joker and you hold 3+ open slots AND a Credit Card (-$20), THEN a reroll or two ($5-6 each) is fine: it found Credit Card(foil)+Smiley at ante 2 and Abstract at ante 3. A foil/holo/polychrome edition on ANY joker, even an eternal junk one (foil Credit Card +50 chips, polychrome Droll x1.5), is a real scorer; the effect text does not matter for edition bonuses.
- IF a skipped Small/Big tag is Holographic/Polychrome/Voucher/Ethereal/Economy, THEN the tag pays at the next shop OR at once (Ethereal pays at once as a free Spectral pack: Wraith gave a rare joker). Skipping both Small and Big at ante 4 when your hands are < 0.6x their need is right.
- IF you hold Wraith's slot-free condition (a free slot), THEN Wraith is a strong take when poor: rare joker + resets debt to $0.
- IF Hex shows in a Spectral pack THEN it destroys all non-eternal jokers: pass it on any build with 3+ scorers.
- IF The Fish: the rank-sorted face-down cards expose a rank window (idx2 in J..K, idx4 in T..J): play visible J/T next to them to guarantee a pair+ (worked: Fish cleared at 18000).
- IF Ancient Joker is owned THEN the suit rotates each round and is shown in raw `run.ancient_card.suit`; `est` does NOT clearly count it. Dig discards for that suit with spare discards; Quads/Trips with 3 of the suit gave ~1.14x est.
- IF ante 4+ and best hand < 0.5x the Big, THEN skip both Small and Big for tags (free joker/voucher) rather than risking a 3-hand round.
