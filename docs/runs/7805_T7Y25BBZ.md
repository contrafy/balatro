## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: MONEY -> no xMult. Every shop had $2-9: Small Blinds pay $0, the Hanging Chad rental took $3 x 8
rounds = $24 (+$1 buy), and being rent-broke forced 3 Small skips (3 fewer shop views). I never saw an affordable xMult and
reached ante 4 with a flat engine (Chad retrigger x3 + Smiley on a face lead, Ice Cream decaying, Banner, Scholar{E}):
~5-7.7k max per hand, while The Needle wanted 9000 from one hand.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 Big shop: Hanging Chad{RENTAL} on top of Smiley. It made ante 1-3 easy (High K 560 at ante 1, 4810 at ante 3)
   but the rent consumed the whole economy; with Smiley alone the Head/Wheel were still clearable (Wheel 2000 needed a
   face lead anyway). Should have sold Chad at ante 3 once Banner + Scholar + Ice Cream carried (playbook rule: sell a
   flat rental once 2 other scorers carry) - I treated it as the engine and kept paying.
2. Ante 4 first shop ($9): Empress + Arcana for Mult cards added only ~+1000 per hand (needs the enhanced face to be the
   lead). Keeping $9 -> ~$13 next shop would have bought the Buffoon pack AND a Celestial/planet, or a joker.
3. Under The Needle, Banner (+30 chips per remaining discard) fights the dig: two discards cost ~60 chips x ~45 mult
   = ~2.7k, more than the gap. With Banner owned and a one-hand boss, a made hand within ~25% should be PLAYED, not dug
   (the first Pair KK was est 7700 with both discards kept; the dig ended at 6380).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own a rental at ante 2-4 AND your money is under $8 at two shops in a row, THEN sell the rental (unless it is an
  xMult): rent-broke means skipped Smalls, fewer shop views and no xMult money.
- IF Banner is owned AND the boss is The Needle (1 hand), THEN each discard costs 30 chips on your only hand: discard only
  when the draw's expected gain beats ~30 chips x your mult per discard.
- IF your engine is Hanging Chad + Smiley (face lead retriggered), THEN play High Card with the best face (enhanced first)
  plus fillers: it scored 4.8-5.5k at ante 3-4, more than most pairs; Mult/Glass enhancements belong on Kings/Queens.
- IF The Fool is in an Arcana pack and you hold a good targeting tarot (Empress), THEN use the held tarot first, then
  pick The Fool: it creates a copy of the tarot you just used.
