## Post-mortem

**Binding constraint:** the deck's per-hand score ceiling (~10-20k with Two Pair/Trips/Straight at L1-L3 and Acrobat x3 on the last hand only) was far below the ante 5 Big target of 37500. Only one xMult source (Acrobat, final hand only) and no scaling xMult joker. Each shop from ante 3 onward had $5-$20 idle; interest was the sink, and no xMult or planet-level growth was bought in time.

**Decisions I would change:**
1. Ante 4 shop ($13): I left the Arcana Pack and the Jumbo Standard Pack unbought. With the score ceiling at ~20k, a scaling xMult or a Two Pair/Full House planet would have been worth more than interest. Rule: if projected per-hand score < 0.5x the next Big target, spend idle cash on xMult or main-hand planets before endshop.
2. Ante 5 Big opening: I discarded toward a full house from a Two Pair / high-card hand (AA55 est 2432), which put no pressure on a 37500 target. Rule: if one hand cannot reach the blind even with 2 discards, play the strongest current pair/two-pair line early and do not discard for draws that need p>0.3.
3. Ante 4 Small: the Flush est dropped 1876 to 1106 across discards with no clear joker change; I kept discarding and only cleared on the Straight. Rule: if an est drops more than 30% after a discard, stop discarding and play the best made hand.

**Concrete reusable rules:**
- If the next Big or Boss target is > 2.5x the best single-hand est, buy an xMult or a main-hand planet before rerolling or ending the shop, even at the cost of the $8 floor.
- Acrobat (x3 final hand) only clears when the final hand already exceeds the target; do not plan a blind around it alone.
- A negative-edition joker (Shoot the Moon) does not take a slot; take it over rentals, but it is only useful if the hand holds Queens.
- Before the boss, check the shop for scorers; a Buffoon pack with no slot-free scorer is not a fix.

**Advisor/CLI defects:**
1. Debuff blinds: est ignores debuffs (The Window, ante 1).
2. Shopadvise suggested sell-then-buy of a permanent scorer (Gluttonous) for a perishable non-scorer (Sock and Buskin), ante 2.
3. SCORE CHECK said EMERGENCY while the shop line said LEAVE, ante 2 and ante 4.
4. The House: face-down cards are unknown; PLAY advice with facedowns is weak.
5. Flush est dropped sharply (1876 to 1456 to 1106) after discards removing non-diamonds, with no joker change to explain it.
6. Small 9000 advice said "skip" (Uncommon tag) with 6/6 slots full; skip is unusable when slots are full.
7. Solver `p >=0.99` labels appear on candidate plays whose est is far below the need (e.g. Flush 1106 vs 9000). Low-confidence labels (n=64/100) are common and look certain.
8. The PACE line ("discard toward a bigger hand") appeared only at the Big 37500, after both discards had already been spent; it should fire at the start of the blind when the best line is below 1/3 of the need.
9. UNWINNABLE warning was only raised on hand 2 of 3 at Big 37500; it should be raised when the blind opens with projected best < 1/3 of need.

**Top lesson:** At Gold Stake ante 5 the Big target (37500) is ~3x the per-hand score this build reached (~10-20k). Money idle at $13-$20 through ante 4-5 shops was the real loss; buy scaling xMult or planets earlier.
