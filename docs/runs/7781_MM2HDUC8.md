## Post-mortem
- Binding constraint: SCALING + BOSS LUCK. By ante 2 boss we had only 2 jokers (Flash Card - dormant mult-per-reroll
  with 0 rerolls done, and Constellation - dormant xMult with 0 planets fed) and NO joker that scores on a plain Pair
  or adds flat mult/chips unconditionally. Full House was leveled only to L1. Combined with The Fish forcing a
  face-down final hand (unscoreable, zero information), the deficit (1233 short after 2 hands) was unrecoverable.
- Decision I would change: at the ante-2 shop just before the boss ($5, 2 Buffoon Packs on offer), `shopadvise` said
  LEAVE, but the shop-advisor's own WARNING flagged that neither joker scores on a plain Pair. With The Fish (a
  "play as few hands, no info" boss) coming up and Full House only L1, spending $4 on a Buffoon Pack for a shot at a
  real scoring joker (even a Two Pair/Trips-gated one, per the WARNING's own fallback advice) was worth the override
  given the alternative was leaving with an idle unscoreable slot. I followed `shopadvise` literally per instructions,
  but this is the one shop I'd override in retrospect.
- Luck factor: The Fish dealt all 8 replacement cards face-down on the final (3rd) hand of the boss blind, leaving
  zero information and no discards to react with (0 discards remained after aggressively discarding for Full House
  earlier). This is close to unwinnable regardless of build once it happens with 0 discards banked.
- `est` vs actual: every hand this run tracked `est` closely except (a) hands scored while Flash Card's effect was
  "not in table" (explicitly flagged by the CLI's own `notes: Flash Card not modeled` on 3+ hands in ante 1/2 - no
  quantifiable deviation seen since Flash Card had 0 stacks all run, never rerolled), and (b) the final face-down
  HIGH-card plays where `advise` could give no estimate at all (by design, per the face-down rule).
- Bridge/CLI issues: none. `sel` before `use` for mid-round tarot rescue (Justice, The Moon) worked exactly as the
  playbook describes; USE_CONSUMABLE only appears in `legal` after `sel`-ing a target card, which is a genuinely
  useful discovery to add to the playbook for anyone assuming they can `use N` directly with 0 discards remaining.
  `play` without a TYPE word correctly accepted an all-face-down 5-card play at the very end.
- Playbook suggestions:
  1. Add to section 4.8 (LAST HAND CHECK): the mid-round `sel i (j) (k)` + `use N` combo works for card-targeting
     tarots/spectrals just as well as at pack-opening time -- it is not limited to pre-hand setup. The Moon/Star/Sun/
     World suit-conversion tarots are a strong rescue tool for completing a Flush around already-enhanced (Mult/Glass)
     cards specifically, worth calling out alongside Death/Strength/Justice.
  2. Add an explicit rule for The Fish: bank at least 1 discard into the boss's final hand whenever possible, since an
     all-face-down final hand with 0 discards is an automatic loss if the running total is not already very close to
     the requirement.
  3. `shopadvise`'s LEAVE recommendation at ante 2 with $4-5 and a WARNING about "no plain-Pair floor joker" should
     weight the WARNING more heavily when the upcoming boss is a "no information" or "few hands" boss (Fish, Wheel,
     House, Water, Needle) -- a marginal pack gamble may beat certain failure to score at all in that blind.
