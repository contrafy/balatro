Post-mortem:
Binding constraint: bad luck (The Wheel's face-down RNG hit repeatedly right when we needed suit information) COMBINED with a misplay -- going all-in on a flush chase against a boss whose entire gimmick is hiding suit/rank information is the wrong bet. Trips (our actual best-scaled hand, L4 90x9 with Wily+Zany, capable of ~4700-9000 per hand) or Two Pair (also live off 4 pairs already in hand) were safer, discard-light paths that did not depend on suit visibility. We should have played/leveraged the pairs-in-hand for Two Pair or fished specifically for a 3rd card of an existing pair (rank fishing, immune to the face-down-suit problem) rather than chasing a 5th suit card.
Decision I would change: on The Wheel, never chase a flush -- fish for pairs/trips (rank-based hands) since The Wheel obscures suit identity, not rank matching once a card is later revealed at showdown, but mid-hand rank is still visible even face down per the CLI ("Nonex2" rank grouping) -- actually rank was also hidden (shown as NoneN). Correct lesson: on The Wheel, minimize discarding entirely (each discard risks 1-2 more cards coming back unusable-face-down) and instead play the best hand available EARLY before the deck accumulates face-down cards, rather than repeatedly discarding into more risk.
