Post-mortem: binding constraint was JOKER SCARCITY / no rescue tarot, not misplay. By the ante 1 boss I
still had only 1 joker (an economy joker, not a scorer) and zero consumables, so The Club's suit-debuff
chewed through most of a 52-card deck (21 cards remained, 28 discarded) without ever finding a 5th
scoring type. The two Full Houses in Small/Big blind were pure card luck (no joker support at all) and
both beat their `est` by 49-84%, which masked how joker-starved the run was until the boss forced a fair
fight with no debuff workaround. The one decision I would change: at Shop 1, instead of the Buffoon Pack
gamble (which produced 2 non-scoring choices), it may have been better to just endshop and bank the full
$8, hoping Shop 2 offered a real scoring joker directly — but this is speculative since Shop 2 also had no
scoring joker; the deeper issue is the shop pool simply did not offer any qualifying joker in either of the
two visits before the boss, which no legal action could have fixed.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Consider adding an explicit tie-break rule for `shopadvise` vs the "Do NOT reroll under $10 in ante 1-2"
   rule: shopadvise currently recommends reroll even when it would leave less than $10, which directly
   contradicts section 5's hard rule. Either shopadvise should respect the $10 floor itself, or the
   playbook should clarify which one wins.
2. With only 2 shop visits before the ante 1 boss and both offering zero qualifying scoring jokers, the
   Ante-1-2 plan (2 scoring jokers by end of ante 2) is not always achievable by the boss itself — consider
   noting explicitly that the ante 1 boss can be lost purely on shop-pool bad luck even with correct play,
   so agents should not over-index on "misplay" in post-mortems when the shop simply never offered a
   scoring joker.
3. RESCUE TAROT guidance (section 4.8/5) says "from ante 3 on" hold a card-targeting tarot — but this run's
   ante-1 boss (The Club, a suit debuff) would have benefited from one much earlier. Consider noting that
   suit-debuff bosses at ANY ante (including ante 1) are a reason to grab a card-targeting tarot from an
   Arcana pack opportunistically even before ante 3, if one is cheaply available.
