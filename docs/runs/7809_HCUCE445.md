## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING (zero xMult in 4 antes). In 9 shop screens (7 shops + 3 rerolls) plus 2 Buffoon packs (one Mega) I
was never offered a single xMult joker, Luchador, Mr. Bones or Director's Cut. The flat build (Mad +10, Supernova ~+14,
Smiley, Wrathful, Chad, Odd Todd) topped out at ~10k per hand; The Wall at ante 4 (36000 = 12k/hand) was ~1.35x out of reach.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 first shop ($40): I spent $13 on Banner{P} + Green Joker (both sold within 2 shops for $4 total) and $6 on a Jumbo
   Celestial. That cash would have bought 2-3 more rerolls at ante 3-4, when the Wall was already the visible ante-4 boss
   only one shop later. Flat bridges that get replaced 1 shop later are a $9 loss each.
2. Ante 3: skipping the Big for the Buffoon Tag cost one ante-3 shop (and ~$7 Big money+interest). The Mega Buffoon gave only
   Wrathful (a flat +mult). With 0 xMult, a shop view + money is roughly equal to a Mega Buffoon; not clearly wrong.
3. Ante 4 Big skip for Juggle: the Big was a coin flip (0.98x) and its shop would have had ~$7; +3 hand size let me make
   10k/9.7k/7k hands. I still think it was right, but the run was lost in the ante-3 shops, not here.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the next ante's boss is The Wall (shown in the first shop of that ante: check the ante-4 boss when you enter ante 4 shops)
  AND you own 0 xMult, THEN the target is boss x2 / 3 per hand (here 12k at ante 4): flat +chips/+mult bridges cannot reach
  it; spend every ante-3/4 dollar on joker views (rerolls/Buffoon packs), not on perishable flat jokers or planets.
- IF a flat joker you buy will be replaced within 2 shops (perishable, Green Joker, Banner), THEN it is a ~$5-9 loss: prefer
  keeping the money for rerolls when you already own 4+ scorers and the score check is < 0.5.
- IF Hanging Chad is owned, THEN lead with a live face (Smiley) or spade (Wrathful)/odd (Odd Todd) card; a debuffed first card
  (The Club) wastes both retriggers. A lone High Card of a spade face with Chad+Smiley+Wrathful scored ~2470 at ante 3 and is a
  cheap closer.
- IF the Big Blind tag is Juggle and the boss is a high-requirement boss you cannot reach, THEN skip the Big: +3 hand size at
  the boss raised my per-hand ests from ~5-6k to ~10k.
