Post-mortem:
- Binding constraint: zero scoring jokers by ante 2. Three separate shop visits (ante 1 x2, ante 2 reroll) never offered a single scoring joker (Joker+4/Jolly/Sly/Mad/Zany/Clever); the only joker acquired all run was Loyalty Card (conditional xMult, fires 1-in-6 hands), which the playbook and shopadvise correctly do not count as "scoring". Without a flat +mult/+chips joker, a L1 Two Pair/Full House tops out around 100-300 per hand and cannot reach a 1200 need with only 3 hands/3 discards. This matches the playbook's own warning ("Ante 1-2 with fewer than two scoring jokers... a hand with no joker support cannot clear ante 2") almost exactly.
- Decision I would change: skipping the Small Blind at ante 2 for the Holographic Tag. Per the new stake-2 rule the Small Blind itself pays $0 so skipping loses no direct cash, but it ALSO skips the shop visit that follows beating it -- and that shop visit was exactly the extra joker-hunting chance we needed while holding 0 scoring jokers. With 0 scoring jokers already at ante 2, I should have played the Small Blind (it was very likely clearable off L1 Two Pair/Full House) purely to bank one more shop roll at a scoring joker, rather than take the tag. The tag never got used (game ended in the very next blind).
- Luck: three consecutive shop rolls (2 fresh shops + 1 reroll) with zero scoring jokers is unusual per playbook data (jokers offered ~20% shop rate); still, the fix above (play Small Blind for the extra shop) would have given a 4th roll before the fatal Big Blind.
- Bridge/CLI issues: none. Every `play`/`disc`/`advise`/`shopadvise` command worked as documented; `est` matched actual scored deltas exactly on every hand played this run (no >20% deviations to report).
- Playbook suggestions:
  1. The stake-2 "skip Small Blind, it's free" rule should carry an explicit exception: do NOT skip the Small Blind while holding fewer than 2 scoring jokers, because skipping also forfeits the shop visit that is the only way to find one, and losing the run costs far more than a missed skip tag ever saves.
  2. Consider tightening the ante 1-2 reroll floor: after one reroll leaves only $7 with 0 scoring jokers, `shopadvise` said LEAVE (to protect the $4 floor) rather than reroll again at $6 (leaving $1) -- but $1 held into a hopeless Big Blind was worth strictly less than one more $6 reroll shot at a scoring joker. A floor of $0 (spend it all rerolling) may be correct when 0 scoring jokers are held at ante 2 and the Big Blind is next.
