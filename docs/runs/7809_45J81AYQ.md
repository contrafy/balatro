## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING. I had no xMult until the last shop of ante 5 (The Duo, perishable, on the second reroll).
Antes 3-5 were carried by three perishable bridges bought at about the same time (Scary Face, and two foil Mystic
Summits: +30 mult +100 chips at 0 discards). All three expired in the same round. That turned the ante-5 boss
into a cliff: my best hand dropped from ~19-28k to ~15-18k per hand, against 16.7k needed per hand. Shoot the Moon
(+13 per Queen held) was the run's best permanent scorer. It needs Queens in hand, and the deck has only 4.
Money was not the limit: I had $21-31 in each ante-4/5 shop and spent it on rerolls/packs that found nothing until
the Duo.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 Invisible Joker: I sold it as soon as it was ready. The random copy was a 2/5 chance of a perishable, and
   it copied Mystic Summit. The copy died in the same round as the original. Holding Invisible until I owned a
   permanent joker worth copying (Abstract/Duo at ante 5) would have given a permanent slot. The cost was ~10k on
   the ante-3/4 hands, and those blinds were cleared with room to spare.
2. Ante 4 shop ($31): Jumbo Arcana $6 -> Strength TT->JJ added little. That $6 plus interest would have paid for
   a third reroll in the ante-5 shop, where the Duo showed up on reroll 2 and a second xMult or Mercury was
   needed.
3. Club boss hand 2: I banked Pair JJ (18000) and kept K Q 9 plus 1 discard. That was right by the numbers, but the
   final two hands needed two face pairs (non-face pairs ~14-15k), and they did not come. At ~1.08x projected, no
   line was safe. The real fix was earlier: a permanent second multiplier before the perishables ran out.

What worked: Shoot the Moon at ante 1 (High card plays of a single K/A with Queens held cleared ante 1-2 and the
ante-5 Big: High A with 4 Queens held = 13108). Mystic Summit armed by a 1-card second discard. Skipping Small
blinds to stretch perishables (ante 3, 4, 5): the Summits covered the Window, the Wall (36000) and the ante-5 Big.

## Reusable rules (if-then)
- IF you own Shoot the Moon, THEN never play Queens. Hold them, and score with 1-card High Card plays of K/A/J
  (with a Queen held this beats a low Pair at antes 1-2). When digging, play 1-4 junk cards and keep every Queen.
- IF you own Mystic Summit, THEN make the main discard first (3-5 cards), then spend the second discard on ONE junk
  card to arm +15 before playing. Two Summits make this the biggest mult source at antes 3-5.
- IF two or more perishable scorers share the same rounds-left count, THEN they die together. Buy one permanent
  scorer/xMult per ante before that round, and value a 3rd perishable bridge with the same timer at almost nothing.
- IF Invisible Joker is ready and 2+ of your other jokers are perishable, THEN wait. Sell it once the jokers it
  could copy are permanent (ideally an xMult). A perishable copy keeps the perish timer.
- IF a perishable bridge has exactly 1 round left at ante 4-5, THEN skipping the Small Blind moves that round to the
  harder Big Blind (worked here: 47108/37500), but you lose a shop. Do it only when you have $20+ to spend in the
  next shop.

## Advisor/CLI defects
- shopadvise STALE STATE after a sell: after `sell 3` (perished Summit), shopadvise printed `ADVICE: SELL -> b sell 3`
  (now Smiley Face), and after `sell 4` it printed `sell 4` (now Popcorn) for "Mystic Summit has perished". Running `s`

  Abstract Joker (+3).

  Pair, although Mad Joker (+10 TP only) and TP L3 made Two Pair the bigger hand.

- Solver: >=0.99 on most ante-3/4 discards (n=16-100). The Club's 0.80 discard missed, and 0.33 for the dig line
  could only clear with two face pairs (my estimate was well below 33%). Ante-4 Wall: the solver's n=16 top pick
  threw away AA.
- Bridge: two NOT LEGAL errors (my own fault): I sent `blind`/`use` in parallel with the previous action, so they ran
  before the prior state settled. Nothing was sent. No rejects, no offline time.
