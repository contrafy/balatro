## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING + MONEY. The whole build was flat +mult/+chips (Sly, Mad, Gros Michel, Ice Cream) with every hand at L1 and ZERO xMult / zero planets all run. Per-hand ceiling was ~5-6k (two pair) vs 18000 boss needing 6k x3 on hands that never improved. Money never exceeded $9 because Gold pays $0 for Small, rentals ate $3/round, and The Tooth cost $8.
Decisions I would change:
1. Ante-2/3 shops: I never bought any planet (no money). Hand levels stayed L1 so Two Pair stayed 20x2. At ante 4 the gap to 18000 was 2x; a Pair/Two Pair L4 (Mercury/Uranus) would have closed it. Take the $4 Celestial Pack over a marginal joker once you own 3+ scoring jokers.
2. Taking the free eternal Business Card (ante 3, 6th slot) cost me the open slot I needed at ante 4 to take a Buffoon pick; I sold the perishable Joker to compensate. A free eternal joker that does not score is not free.
3. Ante 4 Small: I chose to play it rather than skip; that was right (cleared 9000). But I should have saved the $2 and bought nothing at ante 3 shop... money was never the lever; xMult availability was. Rerolling was impossible at $0-8.
Reusable rules:
- IF you own Shoot the Moon (+13 mult per Queen held), THEN keep Queens held and play a throwaway/1-card hand: a lone Ace High with 2 Queens held scored 3132 at ante 2. Replace before it perishes (5 rounds) and note The Hook discards held cards before scoring (est 6102 -> 3164).
- IF a perishable/rental joker has N rounds left and the Small Blind pays $0, THEN skipping the Small keeps those rounds for Big/Boss and saves one rent tick.
- IF the boss is The Tooth, THEN expect ~-$8 (two 4-card hands); arrive with cash or accept negative money; rental jokers then cannot be afforded.
- IF the ante-4 boss needs 18000 and your best hand is Two Pair ~5k at L1, THEN the run is already lost at ante 3: spend ante 2-3 money on Celestial packs/main-hand planet and on an xMult, not on third/fourth flat jokers.
- IF three pairs are in hand with 2 discards vs 18000, THEN no line reaches the target; the decision point was earlier.
Advisor/CLI defects:
- `est` ignores The Hook's discards: est 6102 vs actual 3164 (Shoot the Moon lost its held Queen).
- `shopadvise` ranked Oops! All 6s ($0, UTIL) highest at ante 2 although it does nothing for this build.
- `advise` in the final Plant hand correctly listed only flush/pair; no defect there. `hands` offers a Flush with 3 debuffed cards counted (fine).
- The advisor never raised that no line could reach 18000 after the first Plant hand; harmless.
Bridge issues: none.
