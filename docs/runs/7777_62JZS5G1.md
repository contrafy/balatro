  Binding constraint: SCALING, not misplay. By ante 5 our only two xMult sources were The Idol (conditional, rank+suit
  changes every round, "not modeled" by the CLI and appears to have fired for ~0 extra mult on the final hands - the
  hand had QH QH 9H which are all hearts, so if Idol's shown card that round was NOT a heart it contributed nothing) and
  Card Sharp (needs the SAME type replayed - useless on a forced no-pair opening hand). Full House sat at L2 the whole
  run (only one Earth planet bought at ante 4) while Two Pair also sat at L2; neither hand was leveled past ante-2 power
  by ante 5, and money was too tight (never above $11) to buy both a level and a joker in the same shop.
  Decision I would change: at the ante-4 Big Blind shop ($9 available) I bought Earth (main-hand planet) then had only
  $4 left and had to LEAVE past a Ride the Bus / Trading Card shop; with hindsight, skipping ante 4's Small Blind tag
  reroll-hunting harder for a SECOND main-hand planet (or a real flat +mult joker) before ante 5's Big Blind (30000,
  2.3x the ante-4 Big Blind) would have mattered more than the free Judgement/Fool Mega Arcana pack, since Judgement's
  Card Sharp turned out to be dead weight on a hand with no repeat-type discipline (we played 3 different types across
  the Big Blind: Straight, Flush(chased), Two Pair - Card Sharp only paid off if we'd forced a Pair-only sequence).
  A pure pair-family discipline (never chase a flush at ante 5 with a leveled Two Pair sitting right there) would likely
  have kept the discard budget for a second Two Pair attempt instead of a flush chase that ate all 3 discards and then
  needed a rescue tarot to survive.
  CLI problems: none. Every `play`/`disc`/`buy`/`pick`/`sel`/`use` call succeeded on the first try, no ACTION FAILED or
  retry messages, no Connection refused. `est` matched `actual` on every single hand this run (largest recorded delta:
  0%; the closest thing to a surprise was The Idol contributing nothing extra on the final hand despite being flagged
  "not modeled" every time).
