## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING, caused by MONEY. My only multiplier was The Tribe (x2, Flush only). Everything else was flat:
Abstract +18, Gros Michel +15, Even Steven, Stuntman +250 chips. Flush with Dusk peaked at 36-48k; every other hand was
9-14k. Ante 6 needs 20-40k per hand. Money never went above $14 after ante 1: the ante-2 debt ($-15 via Credit Card)
plus two rentals (Abstract all run, Gros Michel from ante 4) ate every payout, so no shop from ante 3 on could buy a
second xMult.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 5 post-Big shop: I spent $11 on two rerolls hunting a suit tarot for the Needle (~5% per view). The second
   reroll showed Blackboard $6 (x3 when every held card is a Spade/Club). With a 6-card hand you play 5 and hold 1,
   so Blackboard is x3 about 50% of the time. Even at x1.5 average it beats rerolling. With $13 I should have kept $6+
   through the first reroll.
2. Ante 2: Credit Card + 2 rerolls + Tribe pushed me to $-15. It saved ante 2 (Tribe flush 3552 beat the Big, Coupon
   tags paid for the rest). But the debt carried into ante 4 and cost all interest. Tribe was right. The second reroll
   ($6) and Uranus ($3) were not.
3. Stuntman (-2 hand size) made flushes, my only multiplied hand, much rarer from ante 4 on. It still won ante 4-5
   (High Card went 1-card 5k -> 10k), but it locked the build out of 3-flush rounds at ante 6.
4. I should have sold Abstract{RENTAL} once Gros Michel was in. Two rentals = -$6 per round, more than any blind paid.

What worked:
- Stuntman on a build whose mult is all joker +mult (~33) and whose chips are tiny: a 1-card High Card scored 6-10k.
  Playing ONE card per hand at the Pillar ante kept the deck clean (only 3 cards played before the boss).
- Spare discards farm Mail-In Rebate: once a hand clears, discard all non-scoring cards. That made +$15 over 3 rounds.
- Coupon Tag twice (ante 2 and 3 Small): skipping Small while in debt turned two dead shops into free jokers and packs.
- Skipping Small when money < $5 and rent > payout (ante 4, 5, 6): saved $6 rent each time, and the tags (Ethereal,
  Charm) paid immediately.
- Big-blind solver lines (ante 5): it kept 3 hearts instead of a Pair, which led to the 47880 straight flush (p_clear 0.61 vs 0.28).

## Reusable rules (if-then)
- IF you play 5 of a 6-card hand (Stuntman) or 5 of 6 under any -hand-size effect, THEN Blackboard is ~x3 half the time:
  rank it as a real xMult and keep $6 for it instead of rerolling.
- IF you own Stuntman and your mult comes from joker +mult (>25), THEN play single even/high cards as High Card (1 card
  ~ Stuntman 250 x total mult). Pairs barely beat it; do not waste discards chasing pairs.
- IF you own Mail-In Rebate and a made hand already clears, THEN discard every non-scoring card with the spare discards
  before the clearing play (each card ~1/13 to pay $5).
- IF money is under $5 and rentals cost more than the blind pays, THEN skip the Small Blind for any tag that pays now
  (Coupon, Charm, Ethereal, Buffoon). This holds at ante 5+ too: a $0 shop is worth less than the tag.
- IF The Needle is the boss and you own a Flush xMult (Tribe), THEN arrive with a held suit tarot (Sun/Star/Moon/World)
  or Blackboard. Do not reroll for them below $12: each reroll shows a specific tarot ~5% of the time.
- IF you own Credit Card in an emergency, THEN spend only on the multiplier itself. Every $ of debt is lost interest
  for 2+ antes.

## Advisor/CLI defects
- shopadvise ignores Credit Card. With $-7 and $13 of credit left it said "LEAVE (nothing worth buying at this money
  level)", and items show as "costs $8, you have $-7".
- Coupon Tag shops with negative money: every $0 item was ranked 89 but listed under "Ranked higher but passed over:
  The Sun $0: costs $0, you have $-10". ADVICE was LEAVE. Free items must be bought at any money level.
- Main-hand detection stayed "Flush" while I played 1-card High Cards for 2 antes (Stuntman). The SCORE CHECK used a
  Flush (~22-36k) as the "ordinary hand", but my real ordinary hand was 9-10k. That hid how under-scaled I was at
  ante 5-6.
- Blind select ADVICE at ante 5 and 6: "do not skip: the shop after the Small Blind is worth more than a tag" at $1-14
  with -$6/round rent. The tags were Ethereal and Charm, which pay immediately.
- Pack advise in ante 4 said SKIP on the Jumbo Buffoon with Stuntman inside (ranked 50, "nothing beats your weakest
  sellable joker"). Stuntman doubled every hand: est went from ~2.4k to 6-10k.
- Mail-In Rebate's current rank is not shown in `s`/`raw` (grep for "mail" found nothing), so you discard blind.
- Bridge/CLI: right after `endshop`, `blind` was refused with "NOT LEGAL NOW: SELECT_BLIND is not available in SHOP"
  although the endshop output already showed BLIND_SELECT (stale state). A re-check of `s` fixed it. I also sent
  `order` and `endshop` in parallel by mistake; they ran out of order (endshop step 83, order step 84). Both were legal.
