## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scoring. I reached ante 3 with ONE scorer (Half Joker, +20 mult on <= 3 cards) plus Golden Joker
and an unfed Canio. Pair L1 with Half Joker is 30 chips x 22 mult = ~660; ante 3 Small needs ~1070 a hand. Six shop
views (incl. 3 rerolls) offered only one usable scorer that I passed (Zany) and one I passed for economy (Mad{P}).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 first shop: Golden Joker ($6) over Mad Joker{P} ($4). Golden paid $12 over 3 rounds, which then went to a
   reroll and a Celestial whiff. With one scorer, score now > economy. (Mad still conflicts with Half Joker, so the
   real fix is item 2.)
2. Ante 2 Small shop: passed Zany ($4, +12 mult on Trips). Trips is a 3-card hand, so it stacks with Half Joker
   (Trips K = (30+30) x 35 = 2100 vs 1380). I kept $5 for "the next shop"; that money bought nothing that scored.
3. Ante 2 Big shop ($14): reroll $5 + Celestial $4 = $9 for Saturn. Keeping $14 into ante 3 ($17+) would have
   paid for Bootstraps/Arcana plus a reroll at the ante 3 shop, still probably not enough.
4. With p 0.22 at the first Small decision there was no fix left; the run was lost in the ante 2 shops.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF Half Joker is your main scorer, THEN chips are the bottleneck (Pair 30 chips x 22 mult): +chips jokers (Sly,
  Banner, Blue Joker, Ice Cream, Scary Face), Pair/Trips planets and Hierophant Bonus cards are worth ~2x their usual
  value; +mult jokers ~1.3x; Glass/Steel (card-phase) ~nothing. Gate-matching jokers must be <= 3-card hands
  (Jolly, Sly, Zany, Wily, The Duo/Trio), never Mad/Clever/Two Pair jokers.

  Trips with Half Joker) before saving or rerolling: the ante 2 -> 3 step (1000 -> 3200) kills one-scorer builds.
- IF an economy joker (Golden) competes with a scorer at ante 1-2 while you own <= 1 scorer, THEN take the scorer.
- IF The Soul gives Canio and you have no face-destruction source (Hanged Man, Trading Card, Glass faces), THEN it is
  x1 dead weight short-term; at EMERGENCY consider its $10 sell value for a scorer/reroll.
