## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: zero scoring jokers in every view the seed offered (ante-1 Small shop, its Buffoon pack, ante-1 Big shop, ante-2 Small shop and its reroll = 5 views, 9 joker/pack slots: Marble, Mail-In Rebate, Square{R}, Superposition{E,R}, Throwback{P}, Diet Cola, Brainstorm{P,R}, Luchador). A jokerless Black Deck (3 hands, 2 discards) tops out at ~450-700 per round at L1; the ante-2 Small (1000) needs FH L2 (~650) + a straight/flush + a pair-family hand in the same 3 hands, which is a ~5% round. Ante 1 was survived only because Earth made FH L2 (684 vs 356 at L1 for the same AAA88) and Luchador switched The Pillar off.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante-2 Small shop, $10, no scorer: I followed the advisor's reroll to $5. Playbook 2b already says "reroll never below the $6-8 a revealed joker costs"; the reroll revealed two $6 jokers I could not buy (neither scored anyway). Better: keep $10 (the Big shop could then afford a $6-8 joker after the Small's $0 + $1-3), or spend $8 on the Mega Arcana (5 tarots, pick 2: ~67% to contain Empress/Hierophant/Justice/Judgement; Empress on the J/7 ranks would have turned the 2P JJ77 in hand 2 into ~540). Both beat a $5 reroll that cannot buy what it shows.
2. Ante-2 Small, hand 2 (JJ 77 66 TS 4H, need 780): I kept all three pairs and drew 2 (4 outs, 21%). Keeping JJ77 and drawing 4 (3 outs, 31%) had more FH equity; I chose the 3-pair keep for the leftover pair's trips draw on the last hand. Both are ~5-6%; the difference is noise, but the 4-card draw is the simpler rule.
3. Ante 1 Big shop: Earth $3 was right (it is the only reason the Pillar was cleared). Luchador over Brainstorm{P,R} was right. Nothing in ante 1 changes the ante-2 outcome: the seed sold no scorer.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you are jokerless at the ante-1 Big shop and no scorer is offered, THEN buy Earth ($3) over any pack or reroll: FH L2 (65x6) is the one jokerless hand that clears 600 (AAA88 = 684 at L2, 356 at L1) and FH L2 keeps paying at ante 2 (~650 of the 1000).
- IF you own Luchador and the boss is The Pillar, THEN discard the debuffed cards first and sell Luchador only when a debuffed card must score (here AH in the FH); selling it before the first play wastes the option when the draw comes clean.
- IF jokerless at an ante-2 shop with no scorer on offer, THEN never reroll below $6 (the price every revealed joker had): either leave with the money for the next shop or buy a Mega Arcana (Empress/Hierophant/Judgement give a jokerless deck real mult/a free joker); a $5 reroll from $10 buys nothing it reveals.
- IF jokerless at the ante-2 Small (1000) with FH L2, THEN the round is FH + (straight or flush) + any made hand: bank a made Straight/Flush when dealt (220/280), keep every made pair while drawing for the FH, and compute the exact last-hand need per FH variant (JJJ77 = 654 leaves 126 = trips 666 clears; 666JJ = 618 leaves 162 = 777 does not) before choosing which out to chase.
- IF three pairs are held with 2 hands and no discards and the need is > 2x a Full House, THEN play the two low pairs with a junk filler (draw 5) and keep the face pair plus an Ace: the last hand's only clearing shapes are face trips + T+ pair (690-702) or face quads (700); keeping a low pair cannot build 670+.
- IF the first 4 views of a seed contain no +mult/+chips joker, THEN the run is already ~95% dead at the ante-2 Small; record it as a seed loss, not a decision loss, and do not burn the last $5 chasing it.
