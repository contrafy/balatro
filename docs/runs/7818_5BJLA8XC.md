**Reusable rules (if-then):**
- If the boss blind is Flint and the best hand is High Card or a single Pair with no discards left, then the blind is lost; plan the last hand as a flush or straight, but expect a loss.
- If money is $0 at the end of ante 2, then ante 3 has no shop floor; buy only zero-cost xMult or planets, and do not spend below $0 on a joker.
- If a Buffoon pack offers Troubadour and the deck has 3 hands, then do not take it; take Smeared or any joker without a hand penalty.
