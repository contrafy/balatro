## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: scaling built on Card Sharp (needs the SAME type 3x per round) with Two Pair as the leveled type, and only one flat +mult joker (Green, +17-20). Two Pair x3 with a club hit 32-40k, but any round where 3 Two Pairs did not appear collapsed to ~10k per hand. The House ate both discards on the face-down first hand, so the round needed 3 natural Two Pairs from ~20 cards; it got one.

Decisions I would change:
- Ante 4-5: I put 5 Celestial packs (~$22) into Uranus while Pair was my repeat type, then switched main type to Two Pair because Uranus kept coming. Pair appears in almost every 8-card hand and Two Pair does not. The final hand held a pair of 5s that would have been a Pair x3 if Pair had been the leveled type. Card Sharp builds should level the type you can make three times per round.
- Same shops: spend reroll money on a second unconditional +mult joker (Jolly/Sly/Supernova/Spare Trousers) before buying the 3rd-5th planet. Abstract (perishable) and holo Golden Ticket (perishable) carried antes 3-4, and both died by ante 5 with no replacement.
- Ante 5 Small skip: neutral. It saved $3 rent and kept Golden Ticket alive for Big, but cost a round of Space Joker/Green growth. Not the reason I lost.
- Good calls: free Coupon shop (Invisible Joker -> sold to copy Golden Joker), Card Sharp bought at ante 2 by selling Lusty, Death held from ante 3 and used at the Tooth (TH -> copy of 9C to make QQ 99 with a club for x3 x2 = 32k).

Reusable rules:
- IF you own Card Sharp THEN make your leveled hand type the one you can make 3 times per round (Pair), not the one with the highest base (Two Pair). Register with the cheapest pair, then play two stronger pairs.
- IF the boss is The House (first hand face down) and your engine needs specific hands THEN plan to use both discards on the first draw, and arrive with a rescue tarot (Death/Strength). The House is effectively "no discards" for the rest of the round.
- IF the boss is The Fish THEN cards drawn after a DISCARD come face up (only draws after a PLAY are face down): play first, then discard the face-down cards to dig with visible draws.
- IF a perishable/rental joker is carrying your scoring THEN buy its permanent replacement before its last 2 rounds. Count perish ticks against the boss you will face.
- Seeing Double + Card Sharp: put a Glass (Justice) or Death copy on a CLUB card. Every pair that includes it then gets x2 (Seeing Double) x2 (Glass) x3 (repeat): 40k at ante 5 from Two Pair L6.
- Never send two state-changing commands in parallel. I sent `cash` and `sell` together and they ran concurrently (sell landed after cash, which was harmless only by luck).

Advisor/CLI defects:
- `est` ignores Obelisk resets and growth. The final Pair 55 had est 3359 vs actual 2400: est applied Obelisk x1.4, but Pair was the most-played hand, so Obelisk reset to x1. The register Two Pair had est 5772 vs actual 6926, and the Straight est 8229 vs actual 9601 (the Obelisk increment was not modeled).
- shopadvise called "main hand" Full House at antes 2-3 while every hand I played was a Pair (Card Sharp repeats). Later it printed "Uranus levels Two Pair (NOT your main hand Pair)" when Two Pair was L6 vs Pair L3 and was the type I repeated. Main-hand detection should follow the Card Sharp repeat pattern and the highest-leveled type.
- Advice at ante 4 to sell a Golden Joker for an open slot was reasonable. But shopadvise never flagged that the build had only one unconditional +mult joker after the perishables died ("Scorers still alive next ante: 1"), and it kept ranking Celestial packs (76-81) above rerolling for a scorer.
