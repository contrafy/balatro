## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: MONEY -> SCALING. I never held more than $12; every shop from ante 2 on was entered with $0-7, so the only
affordable scorers were $1 rentals and perishables (Clever, Smiley, Wily, Misprint all perishable; Smiley+Misprint rental).
Each bridge cleared the next blind (Dusk + Wily + Smiley won the Big and the House on the final hand) but burned the money and
slots that a permanent scorer needed. Not one non-sticker +mult joker was affordable in 7 shop views; no xMult except a
Wraith Campfire, which is ~x1 without sell fodder. At ante 4 the projected round was 0.29x of the Small.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 shop ($7 after selling Clever): Jumbo Spectral $6 -> Wraith gave Campfire (x1.25 once, from selling Wily). Rare
   jokers from Wraith are not mostly xMults; with $0 after it, Campfire had nothing to eat. Keeping $7 for the post-House shop
   (+$1 interest, $12+ there) would have afforded a real $5-6 scorer. [INFERENCE: the ante-4 shop had Chaos $4 / Burglar $6 only]
2. Ante 3 Small skip: saved $3 rent + a perish tick but cost one shop view; at $4 the view could still have shown a $1-4 joker.
   With zero growth and a sub-1x build I needed looks more than rent savings. Marginal.
3. Ante 1: nothing to change found; start $4 + $0 Small is the structural problem at Gold.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you hold an unused Double Tag AND the next blind's tag is Negative/Buffoon/Rare, THEN skipping that blind gets the tag
  twice (verified: 2 Negative tags). Plan skips around a held Double Tag.
- IF the projected round at blind select is under ~0.5x of the SMALL blind itself (e.g. 2600 vs 9000), THEN skip it for any
  tag: a lost Small ends the run, a skipped one costs only a $0 payout and a shop you cannot afford anyway.
- IF your only scorers are perishable/rental bridges at ante 2-3, THEN the next $5+ must go to a permanent (no-sticker)
  scorer before packs/spectrals; bridges keep you alive one ante, not two.
- IF Dusk is owned, THEN dig with junk on hands 1-2 and keep the best made cards for the final hand: twice here the final
  hand alone scored ~2x the rest (3608 of 4800, 8296 of 6400).
- IF you get Campfire with no money, THEN it is ~x1: count it as an xMult only when you can sell 3+ cheap cards per ante.
