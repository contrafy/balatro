## Post-mortem

Best result: stake 1 (White), ante 6, lost on Big Blind (21180/30000 needed).
Final jokers (6/6, all flat +mult/+chips, ZERO xMult all game): Shoot the Moon (+13 mult/Queen held), Square Joker (+4 chips/4-card hand), Half Joker (+20 mult if <=3 cards), Bootstraps (+2 mult/$5), Scary Face (+30 chips/scoring face), Drunkard (+1 discard).
Money curve: $4 start -> hovered $0-15 through antes 1-4 (spent down every shop on jokers/packs) -> dipped to -$4 under The Tooth (ante 5 boss) -> never exceeded $15 after ante 3, well under the $25 ante-4 target and far under what ante 6+ needs.
Binding constraint: SCALING, specifically the total absence of any xMult joker for the entire run (6 shops in a row offered none we could afford or fit; joker slots were full by ante 4 with only flat-add jokers). Main hand (Pair) reached L5 (70 chips x6 mult) but with zero xMult multiplier stacking, a 2-card Pair capped around 5000-10000 even with 3-4 enhanced (Glass/Mult/Steel/Bonus) cards from a lucky Familiar spectral pull. Ante 6 Big Blind (30000) needed roughly double what a leveled-but-unmultiplied Pair could produce.
Decision I would change: at ante 4-5, with 6/6 joker slots full of flat jokers and zero xMult, should have sold the weakest flat joker (Drunkard or Bootstraps, both marginal) proactively to make room for any xMult candidate the moment one appeared, rather than only reacting to shopadvise's per-shop recommendation which never surfaced a "sell for xMult" prompt because no xMult was ever offered in-shop to compare against. Also, several Arcana pack picks (Empress, Hierophant) buffed whichever Q/J/K pair happened to be highlighted in the moment rather than concentrating buffs on a single fixed rank, diluting the effect across many different King/Queen/Jack cards instead of stacking multiple enhancements onto the SAME one or two cards.
Bridge/CLI issues: none beyond the previously-noted USE_CONSUMABLE gating behind `sel` (documented above, consistent with playbook, not a bug). All `play TYPE` and `disc` commands behaved as expected; est vs actual matched within a few percent on every hand where compared (notably: Pair K Glass+Bonus est10080 vs actual matching cash-out delta; Pair J double-Mult est9000 matching).
Playbook suggestions:
1. Add explicit guidance: when joker slots are full (6/6) and zero xMult owned by ante 4+, proactively sell the weakest flat joker EVERY shop (not just when an xMult is present to buy) to keep a slot open, since xMult offers are rare (~10% of shops per section 7a data) and reacting only when one appears means the slot is often full at the critical moment.
2. When choosing targets for card-enhancing tarots (Empress, Hierophant, Justice, Chariot) in a flexible "any pair" build, prefer RE-ENHANCING a rank that already has an enhanced copy (stacking Glass+Mult+Bonus+Steel onto the same 1-2 physical cards) over spreading one enhancement per pack across many different ranks - concentration guarantees the enhanced cards show up together as a pair more often than scattering.
3. Confirm whether Familiar/Grim/Ectoplasm (random enhanced face cards) is worth prioritizing over Cryptid/Aura on Spectral packs even when the target rank for Cryptid isn't in the current hand - this run's Familiar pick paid off very well (multiple Mult/Bonus/Steel/Glass jacks and kings accumulated over the game) and deserves a documented callout in section 7a alongside the other spectral guidance.
