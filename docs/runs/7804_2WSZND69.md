## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no +mult/+chips joker in 6 shop views (ante 1: Blue Joker $7 at $6, Scholar{ETERNAL}, Wily{PERISH};
ante 2: Chaos{ETERNAL}, The Idol{PERISH}, Trading Card, To Do List/Sixth Sense rentals). All my score came from Aces (Scholar
+20c/+4m each) and FH/Trips (Wily +100). Every blind was cleared by one FH; The Mouth, which forbids switching types, exposed
that only Ace two-pairs (~1000) and FHs (3500+) reached the per-hand need.

Decisions I would change:
1. The Mouth first decision (AH AD JH JC + 4 junk, 4 hands, 2 discards): I followed the solver and spent both discards on
   the FH draw (4 outs: AS AC JS JD, ~35% per 4-card discard), then had to lock Two Pair with no discards left. Locking
   Two Pair FIRST would also have needed both remaining Aces, so this was close to a coin flip either way; but the solver's
   0.74 overstated it (two 4-card draws at 4 outs is ~0.58 for the FH, and the fallback was ~0.15).
2. Ante 2 Small shop: Grabber $10 vs a $5 reroll for a scorer. Grabber's 4th hand did save the Big (Straight dig + FH),
   but a pair-triggered +mult joker would have made Pair/Two Pair viable under The Mouth. With the Mouth upcoming and 0
   +mult jokers I would now reroll first and buy Grabber later.
3. Ante 1 Big shop: Scholar{ETERNAL} was the right survival buy, but it ties scoring to 4 cards. With $13 I could have
   rerolled twice first (keeping $4) and taken Scholar only if nothing better appeared; once rerolled, the Scholar was gone,
   so I took it first. Fine in hindsight; the real miss was supply.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF The Mouth is the boss and your only big hand is a Full House/Trips (from +chips on Trips, FH levels), THEN in that
  ante's shops a pair-triggered +mult joker (Jolly, Sly, Zany, Mad) outranks vouchers and levels: the Mouth needs a type
  you can repeat 3-4 times, and a lone FH lock almost never repeats.
- IF The Mouth deals you a made Two Pair and the rest of your score comes from a few specific cards (Scholar Aces), THEN
  count outs before discarding for the FH: 4 outs x two 4-card discards is ~58%, and after a miss the locked Two Pair
  needs the same specific cards again with no discards left.
- IF a hand type is locked by The Mouth and you cannot make it, THEN play 5-card junk hands (they score 0) to cycle
  toward the cards you need, keeping only the cards that complete the locked type.
- IF Temperance is in the shop and your jokers' total sell value exceeds $3, THEN `buyuse` it: free money (+$3 here).
- IF at ante 2 you have $10 and 0 +mult jokers, THEN Grabber (+1 hand) is a survival buy for Big Blinds (it saved this
  Big), but not a substitute for a scorer before a type-restricting boss (Mouth, Eye, Psychic).
