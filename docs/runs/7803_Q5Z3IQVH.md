## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: MONEY -> SCALING. The run never held more than $15 and never owned a multiplier. Ante-1 Buffoon gave
an Eternal Greedy Joker, so the whole engine was "+3 mult per Diamond" (Splash later made every played diamond count).
That cleared ante 1-2 with diamond flushes (~1400) but tops out ~1500 per hand, a third of ante 3's 4800 Big.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 shop 1 ($15): two rerolls took me to $4 and the second one showed Photograph $5 (the only clean xMult of the
   run) one dollar out of reach. Buying Hack ($6) or Jumbo Celestial ($6) and rerolling once (keeping $5+) would have
   kept Photograph affordable. Rule: never reroll below $5 left after it at ante 2+, even in EMERGENCY - the thing a
   reroll reveals costs $4-6.
2. Splash{ETERNAL}: helped clear The Flint (diamond fillers scored), but two eternal flat jokers (Greedy, Splash) left
   only 4 working slots for the rest of the run. Acceptable as a survival buy; it was the right call only because the
   Flint was otherwise ~40%.
3. Ante 3 Small skip: with $2 the post-Small shop would have bought nothing, so the skip only removed a blind; still
   lost at the Big. Neutral.

Reusable rules:
- IF you own Greedy/Lusty/Wrathful/Gluttonous (+3 mult per suit card) AND Splash, THEN the best play is a Pair plus 3
  fillers of that suit, not the Flush (the Pair keeps Sly/Jolly firing): 1498 (TT + QD 9D 8D) vs 1444 (diamond flush).
- IF the shop's reroll would leave you under $5, THEN do not reroll at ante 2+: buy the best cheap item or bank.
- IF your only scorer is a suit +mult joker at ante 2, THEN under The Flint (base halved) the flat +mult per suit card
  is the engine: keep suit cards over pairs on discards (Greedy +3 survives halving, Pair base 10x2 -> 5x1 does not).
