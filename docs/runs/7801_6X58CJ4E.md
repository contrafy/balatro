## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: MONEY under Gold rentals. Two rentals (Trio, Scary Face) cost $6 every played round; with Small Blind paying $0 and only
$4-5 Big/Boss payouts, cash never exceeded $9 and interest never started. Every shop from ante 2 on had $0-8, so no planet, no second real scorer,
and the ante-3 Big Blind (4800, already the stake-1 ante-4 number) was faced with Two Pair L1 at ~1000 per hand and a Trio x3 that only fires on
trips/FH. The skips of two Small Blinds (to dodge rent) were correct arithmetically but also removed two shop visits: by ante 3 the build was
exactly what it was after the first ante-2 shop.

Decisions I would change:
1. The Trio at $0 with RENTAL+PERISHABLE was the root mistake. x3 that only fires on trips/FH, for $3/round and dead in 5 rounds, with no way to
   level or support it, cost ~$15 and bought exactly one blind (The Wall). A $0 rental is NOT free at Gold: it is a $15 loan against a 5-round life.
2. Scary Face as a second rental doubled the bleed. One rental max, ever, and only if it is a permanent-feeling engine (xMult on the main hand).
3. Ante 3 Big: `advise` offered Flush H est 1414 as hand 1 while holding 99/44. I overrode to hunt a Full House (36% per discard). Both missed and the
   round died 488 short. Taking the flush first (1414) and THEN hunting with 2 discards would have ended 4800-1414=3386 short of... no: 1414+1012+2640
   = 5066 would have cleared IF the same cards came, but the draws would have differed. The real lesson is below (rule 3).
4. Loyalty Card at $5 with $5 in hand left $0 before The Wall; it never fired once in 5 hands and would have fired on the ante-3 BOSS hand 2.
   A $5 that cannot fire for 6 hands is not a "2nd xMult" at ante 2; a planet (Uranus for Two Pair) would have added ~+30% to every hand.

Reusable rules (if-then, for weaker models):
- IF a Gold-stake shop joker has RENTAL, THEN its true cost is price + $3 x (rounds you will play); with PERISHABLE as well cap that at 5 rounds
  (= $15). Buy at most ONE rental in a run, only if it fires on your MAIN hand every hand, and never a second while the first is alive.
- IF you own a rental and the Small Blind is on deck, THEN skipping it saves $3 per rental but costs a shop; skip only if the shop would have under
  $4 after rent (otherwise play it for the shop).
- IF your only xMult is type-gated (The Trio on trips/FH) and the need exceeds 3x your best made hand, THEN spend BOTH discards hunting the gated type
  from a made two pair (4 outs x 2 discards ~ 58%) BEFORE playing anything; if you must play first, play the biggest made hand, not the smallest.
- IF `hands` shows a made Flush/Straight whose est is >= 25% of need AND you also hold two pairs, THEN at Gold ante 3+ play the made hand first and
  hunt with the remaining discards; a two-pair base of ~1000 per hand cannot sum to 4800 in three hands, so banking 1414 up front is the only line
  where two ordinary hands finish.
- IF a Judgement tarot is in the shop at $3 and you have a free joker slot, THEN buy-use it: the created joker carries NO Gold stickers (verified:
  Mad Joker arrived clean).
- IF at ante 2+ you have under $10 and the shop offers Loyalty Card, THEN do not buy it: it needs 6 played hands before the first x4, which is two
  rounds away; prefer the main-hand planet or a flat scorer.
- Gold ante targets observed: a1 300/450/600, a2 1000/1500/4000(Wall x2 of 2000), a3 3200/4800/6400. These are ~1.6x the playbook section 3 table
  from ante 2 on. Plan ante-3 Big as if it were the stake-1 ante-4 Small.

Advisor / CLI defects:
- LOYALTY CARD TIMING IS OFF BY ONE in `hands`/`advise`/`play` notes. cli.py treats stored `loyalty_remaining == 1` as "fires on THIS hand". Game
  source (card.lua 4020-4036): at joker_main it recomputes remaining = (every-1-(hands_played-created)) % (every+1) and fires when the result ==
  every (5); hands_played increments AFTER scoring (state_events.lua 537). So stored 1 -> recomputes to 0 (no fire, just juice); stored 0 ->
  recomputes to 5 -> FIRES. Correct rule: it fires on the hand where the STORED value is 0, and "x4 in N more hands" should be N = stored value.
  Observed this run: bought at hands_played 6, notes said "x4 in 1 more hand" on the 4th hand after purchase; by source it fires on the 6th.
- `shopadvise` WARNING "none of your jokers scores on a plain Pair" ignored a HOLOGRAPHIC Egg (+10 mult every hand). Editions on utility jokers
  should count as unconditional scorers.
- `shopadvise` ranked a $0 RENTAL+PERISHABLE The Trio at score 100 with no sticker penalty; at Gold it should price rentals as cost + $3/round and
  perishables as 5-round rentals, and demote a type-gated xMult when the main hand is undecided/Two Pair.
- `shopadvise` ORDER advice asks to swap two xMult jokers (Trio, Loyalty) relative to each other; multiplication order is irrelevant, so the advice
  is noise.
- `s` at blind select advises "do not skip: tag not worth a shop visit" without accounting for rental drain making the shop visit worthless ($1).
- Bridge: no failures; every action settled on first try.
