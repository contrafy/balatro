## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING, specifically the absence of a permanent second multiplier. The engine was chips-heavy (Odd Todd +31/odd card, Blue Joker +88, Hanging Chad retrigger, foil red-seal Ace) on a mult of only ~27-45 (holo Bootstraps + holo Sixth Sense + Bootstraps' +2 per $5), multiplied by Campfire, which resets after every boss. From ante 4 on every shop was spent re-feeding Campfire to x2.5-3.25 by buying and selling $1-6 cards (about $2-3 per +0.25). That worked through ante 5 (Wheel 50505/50000) because money was high ($36-55, Bootstraps +14 to +22) but at ante 6 the feeds and Bootstraps fought over the same dollars: the ante-6 shops left $11 (Bootstraps +4, mult 27) and Campfire x2.75, i.e. ~25k per odd Two Pair against a 30k-per-hand Big Blind. The Big was lost by 2471 (one Campfire feed or $9 of Bootstraps) after a no-pair deal.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 5 Rare tag (Small 25000): I played the Small because the Big at Campfire x1.75 looked like a coin flip; the two ante-5 shops then produced zero joker worth keeping. A free Rare joker (Duo/Trio/Baron/Blueprint-on-Campfire ~40%) was the only realistic route to a permanent second xMult; with hindsight the Big at x1.75 (Two Pair ~14k + Pair A 16k + ...) was closer to 65% than 50%, and without that joker ante 6-7 were unwinnable anyway. Rule: at ante 5 with one growth xMult that resets (Campfire) and no permanent xMult, take a Rare tag if the Big is >=60% at current strength.
2. Ante-6 post-Small shop ($25): the Empress ($4, +4 mult on two 7s that never scored) and the Mega Spectral ($8) cost $12 = Bootstraps -4 mult (-13% on every hand of the Big, ~9k over the round). The Aura+Deja Vu Ace produced ~+15k on the straight, so the spectral paid; the Empress did not: a $4 Arcana with a 3-card choice at $25 and mult 31 was -2 mult for a lottery ticket. Rule: once Bootstraps is a main mult source, count every $5 spent as -2 mult (-5-7%) and only buy things that beat that THIS round.
3. Ante-6 first shop: Hanging Chad into the last joker slot was right (+30% on odd-led hands), but it ended joker churning (no slot to buy-and-sell $1 rentals or $4 jokers); from then on only the 2 consumable slots could feed Campfire, so rerolls (71% jokers) became nearly worthless. Keeping a slot free until the boss shop, or selling Sixth Sense (+10) only when a +12 or better joker appears, would have kept the feed engine alive.
4. The Big's first discard kept 9S 5D 3H (odd singles) and threw K J T 8 6; the draw gave 333 and the FH. Fine. The final-hand gap came from the Ace hand being played second: with AD TD 7H 5S 5D 4S 3D 2S the straight (41426) was correct and the Pair 7 (17151) was the only hand left; no line in that position reaches 19622.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Campfire, THEN every shop item is a potential feed: buy a $1 rental joker and sell it immediately ($0 net, +0.25x; no rent is charged before a round end), buy $3 tarots/planets and sell them for $1 (+0.25x for $2), sell every planet you will not use, and sell perished/rental jokers in the shop AFTER the boss (feeds count until the boss is beaten, then reset). Keep one joker slot empty so $4-6 jokers can be churned too; with full slots only 2 consumables per visit can be fed.
- IF a Holographic/Foil/Polychrome tag is held and the shop shows no plain joker (only tarots/planets, or a joker that already has an edition), THEN the tag is NOT lost: it waits for the next shop that generates a plain joker (here it paid two shops later: free holo Sixth Sense). Do not reroll to force it.
- IF a Holo tag Small Blind skip is offered at ante 2-4 with 3+ scorers, THEN skip: the free joker gets +10 mult (holo), and the skipped round end charges no rent and no perishable tick (Jolly lasted one blind longer twice).
- IF The Fool is in the shop ($3), THEN check the last used Tarot/Planet: it recreates it. After Temperance it is +$17-18 for $3; after High Priestess it is 2 more planets; after Uranus it is a free Two Pair level. Keep this in mind when choosing WHICH tarot to use last.
- IF you own Hanging Chad (retrigger first scoring card) and chips jokers, THEN lead every hand with your best odd/enhanced card: the hand is rank-sorted, so an Ace is always first; a foil+red-seal Ace scored 4 x (11+31+50) = 368 chips alone (High Card A ~34k, wheel straight 41k at mult 27 x2.75). Put Aura/Deja Vu/Justice on an Ace, not on a low card.
- IF the boss is The Needle (one hand), THEN a $1 Dusk rental (retrigger every card on the final hand) is a one-round x1.5 on chips; sell it in the next shop (+0.25 Campfire). Total cost $3.
- IF The House/The Wheel deals face-down cards, THEN `sort rank` first (the sort orders face-downs too, so the kept idx 1-3 are the highest ranks) and use face-downs as the fillers of a made pair: the floor is the pair, a hidden match upgrades it (Pair 3 est 4290 scored 7647).
- IF Bootstraps is a main mult source, THEN money boundaries are the budget: spending that crosses a multiple of $5 costs 2 mult (~5-7%); a Campfire feed is +0.25x (+8-10% at x2.5-3). Feeds are worth it only while they do not cross a boundary or while Campfire is below ~x2; above that, keep the money.
- IF ante 5 arrives with only a resetting xMult (Campfire) and no permanent one, THEN treat a Rare/Uncommon tag as mandatory and plan the preceding shop to make the Big safe at reset strength; the ante-6 Big (90000) needs ~30k per hand, which a chips build with mult ~30 and x2.75 does not reach without one more multiplier.
