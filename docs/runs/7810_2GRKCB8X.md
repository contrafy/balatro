## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: scaling + money. After ante 1 I never held more than $14 and I was at $0-7 in every shop; the only multiplier I ever owned was a PERISHABLE Duo (x2 for 5 rounds) bought by selling a growth joker, and two flat +mult jokers that died (Gros Michel destroyed, Misprint perished). By ante 4 the whole engine (Duo, Misprint) expired at the same time, so the 18000 boss needed ~6000 per hand against a ~3.5k ceiling. Hands were never above Pair L3 / Two Pair L3 / FH L2.

Decisions I would change:
- Ante 1 Big/Hook shops: I left with $7 and $1 two shops running while the shop had nothing; fine. But at ante 2 I burned $14 -> $3 on two rerolls and found nothing; then spent the last $4 on a Celestial pack that returned Earth. A Standard/Arcana pack or keeping $14 for the ante-3 shop (Duo $8 + Photograph $5 were both on sale there, I could afford only one after selling a joker) would have been better. Two rerolls with one scorer owned was the single worst spend.
- Ante 2 Buffoon pack: I took Spare Trousers + Misprint and sold Trousers one shop later. Taking Photograph instead of Misprint would have kept a permanent xMult-ish card (and Photograph was in the ante-3 shop again for $5). Misprint (perishable) + Duo (perishable) both died at the end of ante 4: two perishables together = nothing left at the boss. Rule: never hold two perishables that expire in the same ante without a permanent replacement bought before the last round.
- Ante 4 Small: with 9000 needed I burned discards and cleared at 9822 (lucky Misprint rolls); the real mistake was upstream (no economy, no growth).
- Skipping the ante 4 Big for a tag was the highest-EV line once the boss was unwinnable anyway, but 7158/18000 shows the build was ~2.5x short.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF you hold Mystic Summit (+15 mult at 0 discards) THEN discard on the first two plays (even junk, even a debuffed card) before any hand you want to score big: the printed `hands` est excludes the +15 until discards are 0 (FH 972 -> 3738).
- IF a Buffoon tag is on the Big Blind at ante 2 THEN skip the Big when you own 0-1 scorers: the pack opens immediately, you pick 2 jokers, and you avoid a 1500 blind (cleared the 2000 boss anyway).
- IF Photograph is offered with <2 other scorers THEN it is NOT a priority: it multiplies only base mult before +mult jokers (~+2 mult at Pair L1-L3); a Duo ($8, x2 on the final mult) is worth ~10x more at ante 3.
- IF you must choose between selling a growth +mult joker (Spare Trousers at +4) and missing a x2, THEN sell the growth joker; but expect to be broke the next shop (I was $1-2 for three shops).
- IF the boss is The Window / a suit boss and you hold a Duo THEN discard debuffed cards first, a Pair/Two Pair/FH of non-debuffed ranks scores fine (FH KKK44 with 2 debuffed diamonds still 3738).
- IF a Jumbo/Mega Celestial pack is the only purchase at $6-8 THEN it was good value here (Mercury Pair L2->L3, FH L2); but one level is only +50% on a hand that is ~2k, it does not replace a joker.
