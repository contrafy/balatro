## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: the only scoring engine was a Flush (Crafty +80 chips / Droll +10 mult / Ancient suit xMult). Every blind depended on drawing a 5-card flush with 2 discards + 1-2 dig plays; it hit 3 of 4 times (Manacle, Big, and not House), and the House's face-down first hand burned a draw event. No pair-family scorer existed, so the fallback hands scored 100-240. Ante 1 offered only suit-gated jokers in the 2 Buffoon packs (Crafty/Swashbuckler, Chad/Constellation).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 shop #1: Droll was the only affordable scorer, fine, but I took no scorer for the non-flush case. With $10 in the ante-2 post-Big shop I passed Stuntman ($7, +250 chips, any hand: 3 hands of Trips +250 chips would have scored ~1.5k) because of -2 hand size; with a flush-only engine that's debatable but Stuntman is a universal scorer and would have given Trips/Pair a fallback vs The House. Probably should have bought it and dug for pairs with 6 cards.
2. I skipped the ante-2 Small for the Rare tag with a flush-only engine that needed a lucky flush each blind. The skipped Small was 1000 against a ~2500 flush; keeping the shop visit changes nothing, but a second Small shop would have allowed a reroll for a non-flush scorer. Ancient (xMult) was only useful if a flush of the Ancient suit landed.
3. At The House I used the first play (Trips KKK + 2 face-down) as a dig; with 4 clubs + 4 draws p(flush) was ~70%, twice (~91% overall) but both missed. Nothing to fix except having a fallback.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your only scorers are flush-gated (Crafty, Droll, Ancient) AND the boss is The House (face-down first hand), THEN buy any any-hand scorer (Stuntman +250 chips) first; a face-down 8-card first deal wastes one draw event and p(flush by hand 3) drops to ~50%.
- IF a flush-gated engine is all you have, THEN keep exactly the suit with 3+ cards and never play a made 2-pair; digs (play junk cards, keep suited) count as draw events: 2 discards + 2 dig plays = ~4 draw events.
- IF Ancient Joker's suit equals your flush suit (check `hands` est on a single card of that suit), THEN the flush is x7.6 - prefer that suit when two are tied.
- IF an ante-1 Buffoon pack offers only suit-gated jokers, THEN take the one whose hand you can make most often (Crafty flush was made 2 of 4 times) but keep $ for a real +mult joker next shop.
