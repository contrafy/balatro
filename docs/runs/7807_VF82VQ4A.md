## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING, caused by riding a PERISHABLE bridge (Joker Stencil, copied by Blueprint) for 3 antes while
skipping 5 of 6 Small/Big blinds. Stencil+Blueprint made x25 with only 2 jokers and cleared everything through the ante-4
Big, but every skip forfeited a shop visit and every shop I did visit bought nothing permanent "to keep Stencil high".
When Stencil perished after the ante-4 Big I owned Blueprint + Acrobat + Photograph: ~2-3k per hand against The Wall
(x2 = 36000). The Wall at ante 4 was known from the start of ante 4 and made it unwinnable without 2 real xMults.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3: skipping BOTH Small and Big (Coupon + Rare tags). The free shop delivered The Tribe (flush-only) and two
   off-hand planets; two normal shops with ~$25 each would have bought 2-4 permanent scorers before Stencil died.
2. Ante 3 first shop ($22 after Blueprint): left with money "to keep Stencil x25". Stencil had 2 rounds left; the money
   should have gone into permanent +mult jokers immediately (rerolls at $22 were affordable).
3. Ante 4 Mega Buffoon: skipped the 2nd pick to protect Stencil x3 for one round. Credit Card (+$20 of buying power for
   the Wall shop) or Clever would have been worth more than one round of x3->x2.
4. Ante 4 Big: selling The Tribe was forced (else lose the Big), but it was forced because the build had no flat mult;
   the dig plays for a heart flush (solver 0.92 -> 0.76) missed twice.

What worked:
- Joker Stencil from an ante-1 Buffoon pack: x6 alone; Blueprint copying Stencil = x(empty+1)^2 (x25 with 2 jokers).
- Skipping Small AND Big at ante 2 with 2 rentals and $2: each played blind nets <= $0 after $6 rent, the skip keeps the
  perishables ticking slower, and Investment paid $25 after the boss.
- Selling a non-firing joker on the forced final hand to raise Stencil (x3 -> x4, Blueprint copy too) turned 9990 into 17760.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your main multiplier is perishable with <= 2 rounds left, THEN buy permanent scorers in EVERY shop now, even though
  each one lowers Stencil; never skip a blind just to stretch a perishable past its replacement window.
- IF you own Joker Stencil and Blueprint, THEN put Blueprint directly left of Stencil (x(empty+1)^2) and keep slots
  empty only while the perishable lives; start filling slots 2 rounds before it perishes.
- IF you hold 2 rentals and < $5 at ante 2, THEN skipping Small/Big is money-positive (Big pays $4 + hands vs -$6 rent)
  and also saves perish ticks; take the tag.
- IF the ante's boss is The Wall (x2), THEN at the ante's first shop require your best round projection >= 36000 at ante 4
  (2x the normal boss); below half of that, skip nothing and spend everything on xMult/+mult jokers in every shop.
- IF Joker Stencil is in play and the final hand is short, THEN selling a non-scoring joker raises Stencil by x1 (and
  Blueprint's copy) before the hand: check `sel` after the sale.

  3 of 6 slots free (Blueprint, Stencil, Tribe).

  5 6" with discards 0 and a different hand (6C 3S drawn); stale cache.

  needed: probabilities in easy-looking spots are overconfident (fine for ranking).
- `shopadvise` counted Joker Stencil (x5-x6 alone, x25 with Blueprint) as "0 scorers alive next ante" and kept pushing a
  second rental (Supernova/Odd Todd) that cuts Stencil by x1 per slot; it does not model Stencil's slot cost.
- Bridge: no rejects, no offline; est exact on every hand checked.
