## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no permanent scorer. Stencil x4 was a great ante 1-2 engine only because two perishables (Raised Fist +20 mult, Blue Joker +100 chips) fed it; without them Stencil multiplies base 5x1/10x2 (~300-600 a hand). The perishables died exactly at ante 3, the shops before it (ante 2 Arm shop, ante 3 reroll) offered no flat +mult, and the one buyable scorer (Greedy Joker, diamonds only) is +3 mult on ~25% of cards: Two Pair AA77 est 2160 vs a 6400 boss. Money was $6-18 the whole time (Small pays $0), never enough to both reroll and buy.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 blind select: I skipped the Small (good) and then SELECTED the Big in the same command chain. The `s` after the Small skip said "skip the Big too: Investment Tag $25 after boss, carriers don't tick, no shop before the boss". Playing the Big spent Fist's last round (perished) for a blind I would have won anyway. Skipping the Big would have kept Fist (+20 mult) and Blue alive for The Wheel (est ~8-9k per hand) AND paid +$25. Chain reads in separate commands: ALWAYS read the post-skip `s` before selecting the next blind.
2. Ante 2: I passed Mad Joker $4 (permanent, +10 mult on Two Pair) because Stencil's AVOID tag said each joker costs x1. Stencil's x4 was real, but the perishables were temporary; a permanent +mult joker would have kept Stencil x3-x4 alive on a 6400 boss. Rule below.
3. Ante 3 shop ($18): Greedy{E} was bought but I left with $9 instead of rerolling for a real scorer; it was too weak anyway. The Jumbo Arcana (Empress = Mult card, multiplied by Stencil in card phase) was skipped for the $8 floor: with Stencil, card-phase +mult is amplified x4-x5.
4. Ante 3 Wheel hand 2: kept AD QH JH TH on a 4-out straight draw (K) after playing Pair 66 with 4 fillers; two cards were then face-down again. A Two Pair/Trips draw keeping AS+JJ or the Pair 66 played as 2-card... would not have been better; the real failure was the lack of base scoring.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your only xMult is Joker Stencil (or any xMult) and your flat +mult is perishable, THEN buy a PERMANENT flat +mult joker (even conditional like Mad Joker) at the first shop before the perishables' last round; the xMult multiplies nothing without flat mult on base 2.
- IF a perishable carrier has <= 2 rounds left and the boss ratio is safe, THEN at blind select skip BOTH Small and Big (the `s` skip-plan says so) instead of playing the Big; read `s` after every skip before choosing blind/skip again.
- IF Joker Stencil is owned, THEN card-phase +mult (Empress/Mult cards, Glass) is multiplied by x(empty+1) as well; those tarots are better than a slot-filling joker.
- IF a boss shows face-down cards (The Wheel) and you hold only a diamonds-gated scorer, THEN count on 1/7 cards being unusable per deal; Greedy Joker did not clear 6400.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- shopadvise SCORE CHECK for The Wheel gave ratio 2.37 (round ~15140 vs 6400) after Greedy; the actual round with Blue + Greedy + Stencil x4 produced 4468 (Pair 2000 with a diamond, then 2308, 2160): the sim overestimated Greedy (assumes diamond-heavy plays) and does not model face-down cards well.
- Joker Stencil ranked "AVOID(-20..25% round est)" on every later joker, including the permanent Mad Joker: the AVOID framing ignores that the perishables that fed the round est die within 1-2 rounds, so the 'round est' base is inflated.
- At ante 3 the first `s` after the Small skip ADVICE said skip the Big too (perishable-carrier plan) but the CLI let me select the Big with no warning; fine, my fault, but the advice only appears at the Big select screen.
- CLI cli.py `wait_settled` timed out (read timeout 5s) twice on the very first `blind` command after start (machine under load, 17 agents); retry worked and did not duplicate the action.
