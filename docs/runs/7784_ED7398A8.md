Post-mortem: binding constraint was a misplay/misunderstanding of The Arm boss mechanic, not money or scaling -
we had 6/6 jokers, two leveled xMult-adjacent hands, and $5+ cash. The decision I would change: after realizing
Two Pair (L1) scored surprisingly well (960 actual vs 92 est) as the first sacrificial hand, I should have played
TWO MORE L1-floor hands (Two Pair or Three of a Kind, both un-leveled) for the remaining 2 hands instead of
switching to Pair (L2) on hand 2 - that switch cost us the Pair level demotion AND still only netted 780, when a
second Two Pair/Trips at L1-floor plus a third L1 hand would likely have summed higher while never touching our
leveled hands. With 0 discards left for the final hand we were locked into whatever was in hand.

Bridge/CLI issues: none - all commands executed as documented. One recurring surprise: `hands`' `est` numbers were
wildly unreliable in both directions this run (under by 10x on some big multi-joker hands, exactly matching on
others) - cannot be trusted as a hard threshold for "will this clear the blind", only as a rough ordering of
candidates.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Document The Arm precisely: it decreases the level of the poker hand TYPE played by 1 EVERY time that type is
   played during its boss round (not just the first hand of the round). Correct strategy: for the ENTIRE boss
   round, only ever play hand types that are already at their base level (L1) if you have any leveled hands you
   want to protect - never let a leveled hand type touch the table until The Arm's blind is cleared or you've
   already accepted losing that level.
2. The Hermit's actual effect is "double your current money" (not "gain half, max $20" as folk-summarized) -
   confirmed empirically: $12 -> $24. Use it right when entering a shop with your highest money of the ante.
3. `hands`' `est` field for hands involving 2+ conditional jokers (Photograph/Scary Face/Sly/Jolly stacking mult
   and chip triggers) is unreliable by an order of magnitude in either direction; do not use it as a strict
   go/no-go gate near a blind's `need` threshold - treat any est within 2x of `need` as "probably fine" and any
   est under half of `need` as "definitely discard first," but don't assume an est far above `need` guarantees a
   loss is impossible if forced to play early due to boss level-locking (like The Arm).
4. Note observed in this run: this ruleset's jokers keyed on "Pair" (Sly +50chips, Jolly +8mult) do NOT contribute
   their bonus to Two Pair, Full House, or Four of a Kind plays even though those hands "contain" pairs - Wily's
   Three-of-a-Kind trigger likewise does not fire on Full House. Plan hand-type choice with this in mind: a plain
   Pair can out-score a Two Pair or Full House built from the same cards when your joker suite is Pair-specific.
