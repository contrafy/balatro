We beat Small and Big Blind comfortably (1150/800, 1544/1200) by playing full 5-card Flush/Straight/Quads hands for the biggest single-hand score, exactly as `advise` recommended for those individual blinds — but this used ~23 distinct cards out of the 52-card deck before the boss, and The Pillar debuffs every card played earlier that ante. Minimizing total distinct cards played pre-boss should have been the higher-priority objective once The Pillar was identified as the ante's boss.
