## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no real multiplier and L1 hands. Four flat/suit-gated jokers (Mad +10 on 2P, Crafty/Droll flush-gated, Gluttonous clubs) plus a 50-chip Hiker made 1.4-2.5k hands at ante 3 vs 4800: the Big needed three average hands. Blackboard (x3 only if every held card is a spade/club) was the first xMult I could afford, bought at $1 left at the ante-3 shop, and it works only if the whole hand structure is engineered around it.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 Big, hand 1: I played TT44 + red filler (1944) without Blackboard. Pair 4 with all four reds played kept Blackboard on (est 1494) and kept KS TC 5C black, but it would also have been a weaker hand; the real issue was the first discard: I discarded 5 reds and kept TD, which left 6 reds/8 and needed a second discard. Better rule: with Blackboard, count REDS in hand before the first discard; keep as many black cards as possible and discard up to 5 reds, never keep a red just for pair potential.
2. Ante 3 last hand: FH-now (p 0.75) vs dig AD 8D (p 0.89 by my own Monte Carlo) was correctly decided; the 11% miss is just variance. Still, the real mistake is relying on a conditional xMult needing 3 held black cards on the final hand with no discards left.
3. Ante 2-3 spending: Gluttonous ($5, +3 per club) and the Crafty/Droll flush kit are suit/hand-gated and did not scale; $5 + $5 reroll could not both hit an xMult. A jokerless-at-ante-1 start with a flush kit (Droll+Crafty) cleared ante 2 with two flushes only because 4-diamond draws hit.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Blackboard, THEN the played 5 cards must contain EVERY non-black card in hand (reds <= 5) and the 3 held cards must be black: before every play count reds; hands with exactly 5 reds can only be played as whatever those 5 reds form (often High Card/Pair), so discard reds first (est in `hands` already includes the x3: a Pair K est 285 became 855 with held all black).
- IF the deck seen so far is biased (here 15 reds of 20 seen -> 21/32 remaining black), THEN compute P(next draws black) from the remaining 52-card count before choosing a Blackboard dig.
- IF 4 same-suit cards (diamonds) are held with Droll+Crafty (or any flush kit) and 2 discards, THEN the flush was ~70% per 4-card draw and est 2072 (>1500 Big) so chase it; it cleared the ante-2 Big at 4 diamonds after two discards.
- IF the Small Blind pays $0 and you hold $0-1 at ante 2-3, THEN skip it for a useful tag (Foil -> free foil Hiker in the next shop; Meteor -> free Mega Celestial): both paid well, but Meteor offered no Uranus/Jupiter/Earth so the two levels were on non-main hands (Pair, Trips).
- IF the Judgement tarot ($3) is in the shop with free slots at EMERGENCY, THEN it is a $3 random joker (gave Crafty here): cheaper than a $4-6 gamble pack.
