## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: economy + zero multiplier. Two ante-1 Buffoon packs offered no scorer (Pareidolia/Trading Card rental;
Mr. Bones rental/Crazy{ETERNAL,RENTAL}), so I entered ante 2 with 0 scorers and $4, and from then on every shop was spent
to $0-3 on survival bridges (Popcorn, Four Fingers, Sly rental, Seeing Double perishable, Jolly). Never earned a cent of
interest, Sly rent ate $3/round, and no reliable xMult was ever offered in an affordable shop (Constellation rental at
$1 was the only "growth" one, with no planet income to feed it). Final engine ~2000-3400 per hand vs 3000 needed at ante 4.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 Big: I played it (Uncommon Tag). Skipping it with Popcorn at +20 would have reached the Manacle at full Popcorn
   and given a free Uncommon joker in the post-boss shop; the Manacle was cleared with 2 of 3 hands anyway (2540/2000).
   [INFERENCE] the free Uncommon joker was worth more than the $4 + shop visit I got for playing it.
2. Ante 4 shop ($3): Mercury was fine, but I lost by 554; the realistic alternative (sell Four Fingers for a Jumbo
   Spectral) was a lottery too. The real fix was upstream: arrive at ante 3 with any money at all.
3. `sort rank` at The Fish was a wasted recorded action: the hand is already auto rank-sorted.

  Small pays $0 and a $0 shop buys nothing.
