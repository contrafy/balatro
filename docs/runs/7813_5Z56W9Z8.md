## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: one scorer that was worth +0 mult (Bootstraps at $0-4 adds nothing; needs $5 per +2) on ante 1 with jokerless 100-330 hands; the Hook (600) needs two ~300 hands and a 3-hand round with 2 discards makes that ~40%.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 first shop ($6): took the Jumbo Buffoon ($6, leaves $0) over Smiley Face{PERISHABLE} $4 + $2. The pack had no scorer (Marble, To the Moon, Juggler, Bootstraps). Smiley (+5 mult per scoring face) was the only offered scorer and the advisor SIM put it at +0.45 vs +0.27 for the Jumbo pack; I overrode on the FIRST-BUY rule (perishable + conditional) and it cost the run. The rule "a Buffoon pack is better than a non-qualifying joker" is wrong when the pack costs ALL your money and the shop joker is affordable: a jokerless run at ante 1 needs a scorer NOW.
2. Bootstraps was a bad pick: with Gold's $0 Small Blind and $4-6 shop money it is +0..+2 mult. It should only be picked at $10+ income. Juggler (+1 hand size, 9 cards) was the better dig tool for the Hook, or To the Moon for the economy. Take a hand-size/dig joker over Bootstraps when money is <$10.
3. Ante 1 Big: I chose the 0.77 line (keep AA pair) over the 0.79 line; both missed and I won only by 2 chips (324 vs 322 exact). With no joker the Big/Hook are coin flips: every dollar should buy a scorer before the Big.
4. Shop after the Big ($4): left with $4 and nothing. Earth ($3, FH planet; I had made FH once) or the Standard pack were possible; SIM showed Earth +0.12 score ratio. Holding $4 with Bootstraps gave +0 mult. With a jokerless-equivalent run at 0.69x TIGHT, I should have spent it (floor OFF per shopadvise) instead of holding for Bootstraps.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own 0 scoring jokers at ante 1 and the shop sells a $4 scoring joker (even perishable/conditional, e.g. Smiley, +5 per face) with the rest of the money covering nothing else useful, THEN buy it instead of a Buffoon pack that takes all your money: the pack can be four non-scorers (Marble/To the Moon/Juggler/Bootstraps).
- IF Bootstraps is offered at $0-9 money THEN it is a non-scorer (+2 mult per $5 held): do not count it as a scorer.
- IF the Hook boss (600) is next and you have no mult joker, THEN you need two ~300 hands: dig for flush/straight/FH on hand 1-2 and bank flush/FH, never two-pair plays.
- The Hook discards 2 random HELD cards after each play (not the played ones): keep draws in the played cards.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- shopadvise ranked Smiley Face (SIM +0.45) over the Buffoon pack and the first-buy rule said otherwise; SIM was right here (pack contained only non-scorers). The pack SIM (+0.27 Jumbo) overstates expected scorers at 0 jokers.
- Pack advise ranked To the Moon (economy 60) and Bootstraps (PLUS 55) above Juggler by rule but SIM chose Juggler; all SIM values were within noise (p<0.16).
- Bootstraps classified [PLUS] at $0 money with est 0 mult added; it should score 0 when money < $5.
