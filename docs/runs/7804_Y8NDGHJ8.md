## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: money, then scaling. Zero xMult all run. From ante 2 on I was never above $15, and from ante 3 on
I sat at $0-4 with -$3 (later -$6) rent per round, so the ante-5 shop's holo Abstract ($7, +28 mult) and every
Celestial/Buffoon pack were out of reach. The engine was flat: Walkie Talkie (10s/4s) + Clever + Sly chips, base mult
2-4, and Dusk retriggering the final hand (doubles every per-card +mult/+chips). Dusk carried antes 3-4 (finals 7280,
6968, 17300, 8658), but a flat build tops out around 20k per hand at ante 5, against 25k/37.5k/50k needs.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop 2: Clever{RENTAL} $1. It scored well (+80 chips on every Two Pair/FH), but it cost $3 x 11 rounds = $33,
   which was exactly the money missing for an xMult or Abstract later. With Walkie as the only scorer it was right at
   ante 1. It should have been sold at ante 3 once Sly + Dusk carried hands.
2. Ante 2: two Buffoon packs ($8) gave Greedy (fine) and Trading Card. The second pack plus the $0 Small skip left me
   broke for ante 3. Trading Card paid only $9 in total, because I needed my discards in most rounds.
3. Ante 4 shop: holo Crazy{RENTAL} was free (+10 mult), but it added a second rental, and $6/round rent at $0-4 made
   ante 5 shops dead. Mystic Summit holo (free, from the 2nd Holo tag) was the better of the two.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Dusk (retrigger final hand) and per-card jokers (Walkie Talkie, Greedy/suit jokers, Scary/Smiley), THEN
  use hands 1-2 as cheap digs (play junk, keep the made Two Pair/FH) and play the big hand LAST: final FH TTT44 = 17300
  vs ~6000 non-final. `sel` shows the Dusk-inclusive est only on the last hand; compute it by hand earlier.
- IF the boss is The Hook, THEN do not hold a hand for the Dusk final: the Hook discards 2 random held cards per play.
  Bank made hands and play 5 cards per hand, so the final hand is dealt mostly fresh.
- IF Mystic Summit is owned, THEN burn both discards before the first play (all 3 hands get +15).
- IF The House deals the first hand face down and the hand is auto-sorted by rank, THEN the face-down cards sit
  between their visible neighbours: AC KS ? ? TH means two cards from K..T, so playing all five made a straight/pair.
- IF a Double Tag is pending and the next Small offers an edition tag (Holo/Foil/Poly), THEN skip for it: two edition
  tags = two free edition jokers in the next shop (here two free holo jokers, +10 mult each).
- IF you hold 2 rentals and < $5 at ante 4+, THEN sell the weaker rental before ante 5. Being $6/round in debt made
  every ante-5 shop unbuyable.

Advisor/CLI defects:

  the Dusk engine (every T/4 scored twice). The SIM line itself evaluated "sell Greedy" (ratio 0.70). The sell target
  should be the one the SIM scored, not the lowest sell price.
