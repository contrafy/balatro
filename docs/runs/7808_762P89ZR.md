## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: boss ante 1, The Goad (spades debuffed) with a joker set that is spade/Ace-dependent and only two flat scorers; round projected ~459 vs 600 at the Big-blind shop (TIGHT 1.00x) and I bought a spade-mult joker for the spade-debuffing boss. Wrathful Joker was worth exactly 0 that round, and 4 of 8 cards in the last hand were spades (debuffed). Variance: two discards found nothing at the Goad (no Ace, no flush), a second FH-or-better never appeared.

Decisions I would change:
1. Shop 2 (ante 1, boss = The Goad): bought Wrathful (+3 per Spade) at $5 when the upcoming boss debuffs spades. The SIM-preferred perishable Crafty Joker ($4, +80 chips on Flush; flushes appeared 2 of 3 hands in the Small and Big) was ahead for the boss round (0.62 vs 0.52 for P(clear next 3 blinds)); the "permanent, adds mult" rule I followed ignored that the boss round decides survival first. Also left $1 so a reroll/pack was impossible.
2. Hand 1 of the Goad: kept TT + 4H and discarded 5; keeping the pair TT only and aces was not available, but a flush-chase in clubs (TC 8C 4C 2C, p 0.56) at the second discard was not taken to avoid breaking the made Two Pair (solver margin +0.06 < 0.10). It hit Full House 288 instead; the flush (with no Wrathful/Scholar help) would have been ~276 as well, so no real loss.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the upcoming ante-1 boss debuffs a suit (Goad = spades, Club = clubs, Window, Head), THEN do not buy that suit's mult joker (Wrathful/Greedy/Lusty/Gluttonous) as the first/second joker: it scores 0 in the boss round that ends the run.
- IF the round projection is ~1.0x of the Big blind at the ante-1 shop (TIGHT), THEN the boss (600) is out of reach with 2 flat jokers; pick the joker that adds score in the boss round itself (chips on any flush/pair) over a suit-gated one.
- IF Scholar is owned, THEN a single Ace is a 180-point hand (High Card A with 4 filler cards) and a flush with an Ace is 700+; keep Aces and dig for flushes containing one.

Advisor/CLI defects: none found beyond the SIM not modelling that The Goad debuffs Wrathful's suit (it ranked Wrathful 0.46 / P(a2 boss) 0.55 while the boss it faces first scores it as 0). shopadvise printed the Goad as upcoming but did not penalise a Spade joker against it.
