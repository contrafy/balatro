## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING / variance of a one-joker engine. The only scorer was Shoot the Moon (+13 mult per Queen held)
plus foil DNA (+50 chips). A hand scored ~900 per live Queen held and ~70 with none, so every round was a lottery on
drawing 2+ of 4-5 Queens from 52 cards with 2 discards. The Goad then debuffed both spade Queens (the gold QS and its DNA
copy), leaving 3 live Queens in 53 cards. No xMult, no chips joker, no levels by the ante-3 boss.

Decisions I would change:
1. DNA copies were left on the table. With Shoot the Moon, a LONE Queen on hand 1 scores the same as High Card Ace with
   that Queen held, because DNA's copy lands in hand before held-card jokers score (est 1755, actual 2600). At the Flint
   (QH QC held: lone QC = 65 x 27 = 1755 vs High A 1701) and ante-3 Small I played Ace/pair hands instead; each copy
   would have been +1 permanent Queen (7 by the Goad instead of 5).
2. Ante-3 Big shop with $16 and TIGHT 0.56 before the boss: one $5 reroll then LEAVE with $11, which died unspent. Clever
   (SIM 0.64 vs 0.55) or the Jumbo Celestial (Pair levels add chips, the scarce factor) was better than interest.
3. Devil gold on QS was fine for money, but the suit choice mattered: The Goad (spades) was already the known boss; gold
   a non-spade Queen when the boss debuffs spades.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own DNA AND a held-rank joker (Shoot the Moon, Baron, Raised Fist-type) AND hold 2+ of that rank, THEN play one
  of them ALONE as the round's first hand: the copy is held when scoring (no score loss) and the deck gains a permanent
  copy every round. Copy the enhanced one (gold/steel copies keep the enhancement).
- IF your only scorer is a held-rank joker (Shoot the Moon), THEN treat it as ~50% per round, not a build: buy any
  +chips joker or Pair level next (the engine has ~40 mult and ~70 chips, so chips double the hand), and spend the
  shop before a suit boss rather than banking interest.
- IF the boss debuffs a suit, THEN cards of that suit held in hand also stop triggering held-card jokers (Moon/Baron):
  count only off-suit copies of your key rank when judging the boss.
- IF a TIGHT boss (projected < 0.7x) is next and you leave a shop with $10+ unspent, THEN you are making the 7802_X5MG1LC2
  / 7805 mistake: the money is worth nothing after a loss.
