## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no xMult and no +mult joker. Build was all flat chips (Blue Joker, Ice Cream) on a mult of 2-16 supplied only by Walkie Talkie's 10s/4s: 1-card High Card ~900-1000, Pair T/4 ~2300-2700, FH/Trips ~3400-4000. 3 hands x ~2000 cannot beat a 0-discard 6400 boss. Money stayed $0-6 all run (Small pays $0; every item cost the whole wallet).

Decision I would change (the actual 1021-short miss): I owned Campfire (x +0.25 per card sold, resets only AFTER the boss) and on the final hand had est 4032 vs 5053 needed. Four cheap flips before the boss (buy a $1 rental / $3 planet in the shop, sell it at once; also sellc spare consumables) would have given x1.25-x2: 4032 x 1.5 = 6048 clears. I only thought of Campfire as the free-Splash flip at ante 2 (which reset at the Arm boss) and ignored it before the Water. The ante-3 shop offered Blackboard{PERISHABLE,RENTAL} $1 and Saturn $3: buy+sell each = +0.25 for ~$0-2.
Smaller: spent $5 on Ice Cream (decays 5/hand; contributed ~1400 on the last hand, so it was right) but kept no money for flips.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Campfire THEN in the shop before the boss (and before any blind you struggle with) buy and immediately sell every $0-3 joker/rental/planet/tarot: each sale is x+0.25 on the whole hand (x1.5 after two); the multiplier resets only after the boss is beaten, so sell BEFORE the boss, not at ante start. Count sells before the last hand: est 4032 x(1+0.25n) vs need.
- IF a Coupon/D6 shop is free THEN take every free joker/pack/planet even a rental (buy+sell = Campfire fuel and +$1).
- IF Walkie Talkie is your scorer THEN 10s and 4s are everything: a single 10 scores ~1000, Pair of 10s/4s 2300+, FH with three 4s 3400-4000 (with Blue Joker + Ice Cream chips). Keep 10/4s in discards, dig with junk High Cards when the pair would lose a level (The Arm).
- IF the boss is The Arm THEN a junk 1-card/4-card High Card dig costs nothing (level 1 cannot drop) and keeps leveled types intact; it found FH 444TT 3660.
- IF the boss is The Water with ~700/hand typical THEN the blind is lost unless an xMult/sale-multiplier exists: plan ante-3 shops for xMult, not for more flat chips.
- A 1000-point Small with $2 in hand: skipping it is free (pays $0, shop buys nothing) and the Big/boss need the same power either way.
