## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: MONEY -> SCALING. Money never exceeded $12 and sat at $0-5 from ante 2 on, so I never earned interest and
never bought a single planet: Two Pair (main) was still L1 at the ante-4 Big. I lost by 476 (3.5%). One Uranus is +20 chips on a
~106-chip Two Pair (~+19%) and one Earth is +25 chips/+2 mult on Full House; either one clears that blind.

Where the money went (ante 2-4): Hologram $7 + Standard pack $4 (Holo x1.25, never fed again), Sly rent $6, Square rent $6,
Gros Michel $5 (destroyed after one round), Mad $4. Skipped Smalls pay $0 at Gold, and one-shot clears give only $1-2 per round.

Decisions I would change:
- Ante 2 Big shop ($12): Hologram $7 + Standard pack $4 bought x1.25 total. Two Uranus (Mega Celestial $8, or two $3 shop
  planets) would have been +40 chips/+2 mult on every Two Pair (~+45%) and left money for interest. Hologram with no feeding engine
  (no Certificate/DNA/Marble/Riff-raff, no money for Standard packs) is a $7 x1.25.
- Ante 1: Green Joker{ETERNAL} next to Mystic Summit. Summit needs both discards spent every round and Green loses 1 per discard,
  so Green sat at +0..3 mult the whole run: a dead eternal slot. 8 Ball (sellable) was the better pick once Summit was the plan.
- Ante 3 Big shop: Gros Michel $5 at $5 -> $0. It probably carried The Flint, but it was destroyed one round later; with $0 I
  could not buy the Earth/Uranus at ante 4.
- Ante 4 Big: nothing to change in play; the solver gave 0.19 at the start of the round.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF your main hand is still L1 at ante 3 and a $3 main-hand planet (or Celestial pack) is in the shop THEN buy it before any
  growth joker you cannot feed. A level is permanent and costs $3. Hologram x1.25 cost $11 here.
- IF you own or will own Mystic Summit THEN never take Green Joker (worse when Eternal): Summit wants 2 discards per round,
  Green loses 1 mult per discard.
- IF you own Hologram and no card-adding joker THEN do not count it as a growth xMult: it stays x1-x1.25. Buy it only with a feeding source.
- IF Sly (rental, perishing) is your chips joker and a foil Square Joker{RENTAL} $0 is offered THEN swap them. Foil gives +50 chips on
  every hand, and Square grows +4 on every 4-card hand (Two Pair = 4 cards). It is the same rent and it never perishes.
- IF you play Two Pair with Square Joker THEN play exactly 4 cards (no filler), so Square grows.
- IF money is <$5 for two shops in a row at ante 2-3 THEN stop buying bridges (rentals, Gros Michel) and keep $3 for the main-hand
  planet: every shop showed a planet I could not afford at ante 4.
