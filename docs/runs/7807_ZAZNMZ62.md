## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: single scorer (Card Sharp, x3 only on a REPEATED type) facing The Hook at ante 1 with 600 needed and no flat +mult/chips jokers; the build only works when the same type is hit twice, and The Hook discards 2 random held cards after every play, so draws kept for later hands get destroyed.

Decisions I would change:
1. Hook hand 1: I played the Straight (308) first to register it, then needed a second Straight x3 (924). The solver p was 0.82 at that point but the actual draw (6543, 2/7 outs) was only 0.49 and the Hook then randomly discarded held cards anyway. Alternative: on hand 1 after the discard, with Two Pair L2 (240 base, x3 repeat 738) and both pair draws kept, register the type I could most surely repeat. Hard to say it was wrong; the margin was thin.
2. Shop 1: I used Credit Card + Card Sharp, spending all cash and going to -$2; the Buffoon pack ($4, advisor choice, SIM 0.23) would have given a possible flat +mult joker. With Card Sharp as the sole scorer the Big and Hook were a coin flip each; a flat +mult second scorer is needed beside Card Sharp (playbook says so; I accepted it because Card Sharp was the only xMult). No money was left for rerolls (-$5 debt means every later buy stacks debt).
3. With Credit Card debt I bought Uranus (-$3) rather than a Buffoon pack ($6, not possible on credit?). I did not test whether packs are purchasable on credit; PLAYBOOK says no packs/vouchers on credit.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF Card Sharp is your only scorer at ante 1 and the boss is The Hook (discards 2 held cards after each play), THEN do not rely on holding a draw into the last hand; play the strongest made type on hand 1 and hand 2 only if it repeats ~x3 stays above need/2.
- IF your first-hand type was a Straight/Flush (low repeat probability) under Card Sharp, THEN the later x3 only fires if you rebuild exactly that type: prefer registering Pair/Two Pair (60-70% to repeat) with a weak version.
- IF Credit Card is bought to afford an xMult, THEN the money after is negative and rerolls/planets just add debt: do it only when the alternative is zero scorers.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- advise at Hook hand 3 (2 hands left, 0 discards) proposed `play PAIR 1 2 3 4` with 'p 0.33' but did not model that a fresh 8-card draw needs >=240 from a non-repeat type; nothing available reached it. No defect, just a low-p line.
- The solver's p 0.82 at the Hook after the Straight (disc 1 2 3 4 keep 6543) was much higher than the realised 4-card straight draw (p 0.49 for the straight itself; the other 0.34 came from alternative hands it assumed I'd make); calibration looks optimistic when The Hook discards held cards.
- `buy Card` (a bare name) was rejected: needs a full-name match or slot number (`buy 1 --expect 'Card Sharp'`). Purely a usability note.
