## Post-mortem

**Best result:** Stake 1 (White), ante 3, Small Blind (need 2000, scored 1346). Beat ante 1 boss (The Hook) and ante 2 boss (The Goad) cleanly; lost the very next blind.

**Jokers at end:** Supernova, Ancient Joker (only xMult source, and purely conditional/rotating-suit), Droll Joker, Gift Card. No reliable xMult was ever acquired in 2.5 antes of shops (matches playbook 7a: xMult is rare) — Full House L3 / Flush L3 plus one conditional xMult was not enough to keep pace once the Small Blind target jumped from 1600 (ante 2 boss) to 2000 (ante 3 small).

**Money curve:** $4 (ante1 start) -> $9 -> $0 (spent on first 2 jokers) -> $6 -> $13 (ante2 shop, after boss) -> $0 (spent on Droll/Earth/Gift Card/Jupiter/Earth) -> $7 (ante3 shop) -> $3 (spent $4 on Arcana pack) -> lost with $3 unspent-idle at the very end (not a violation of the ante 6+ rule, but shows money was never built up as a cushion).

**The one decision I would change:** In the ante-3 Small Blind, I spent ALL 3 discards chasing a rank-based Quads (Pair 6 -> Trips 6 -> Quads 6, est 672) instead of stopping earlier once a decent hand was in hand, or steering toward my leveled main engines (Full House L3 / Flush L3, both far stronger per card than an unleveled Quads at L1). Chasing the quads burned every discard on ONE hand, leaving zero discards for the remaining two forced hands (Two Pair est 90, then Two Pair est 208) which had no chance to build toward Full House or Flush. In hindsight, `advise`'s own math should have projected forward: even a "successful" Quads (966 actual) still left ~1034 needed from 2 undiscardable hands drawn from a random 8 cards, which is a bad bet compared to preserving 1 discard to shape a Full House out of the leveled L3 engine on the final hand. Playbook rule 4.3 ("keep the cards that build toward the best target") was followed locally per-decision but the *sequence* (spend 2 of 3 discards escalating a single Trips->Quads chase) wasn't validated against total remaining-hands budget.

This is a systematic, predictable undercount, not noise.

**Bridge/CLI issues:** None. No `ACTION FAILED`, no retries needed, no connection problems. `advise`, `hands`, and `shopadvise` all responded correctly and quickly for the entire run.

**Playbook suggestions:**
1. `est` should either (a) apply an expected-value adjustment for Ancient Joker (e.g. assume ~1/4 of scoring cards match the hidden rotating suit, so apply a partial multiplier instead of 1.5x-per-matching-card-not-counted) or (b) explicitly flag in `advise`/`hands` output "actual score will likely be 30-100% higher than est" whenever Ancient Joker is held, so the agent can factor that upside into discard-vs-play decisions instead of always treating est as a hard floor.
2. Add a "discard budget" check to `advise`: before recommending a discard to chase an escalating hand (Pair -> Trips -> Quads), it should project the total remaining-hands score assuming ONLY forced/no-discard hands after the last discard, and compare that sum against `need`. If the projected total with the chase is lower than a more conservative line (e.g. discard once to shape toward the leveled main-hand engine instead of an unleveled off-type), prefer the conservative line. This run's loss was exactly the case the current advice does not catch: it correctly recommended each individual discard step but never validated the whole 3-discard sequence against the round's total need.
3. Confirm the "0 xMult by ante 3" warning shopadvise already surfaces (it did fire correctly here) should perhaps also suggest rerolling when money allows, even below the $15 floor in section 5, if the alternative is certain to under-scale — in this run there was only $3-7 available so rerolling wasn't realistic, but the warning could explicitly say "insufficient money to reroll for xMult; risk of loss next blind" to make the tradeoff explicit rather than implicit.
