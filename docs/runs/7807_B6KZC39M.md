## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING at ante 8 (plus Amber Acorn's shuffle). The build was Two Pair L5-L7 + Mystic Summit (+15)
+ Mad (+10) + Clever (+80 chips) with Hologram x3.25, Cavendish x3 and Ramen x1.65: ~110-130k for a normal Two Pair and
~225k with a stone filler or a steel card held. Ante 8 asks 200k/300k/400k, i.e. 133k per hand at the boss with no
bad luck, and the Acorn shuffle put both +mult jokers after the xMults on hand 1 (63785 instead of ~178k). With the right
order on all three hands it would have been ~178k + ~130k + ~130k = ~440k, so the order alone decided this boss. The deeper
cause: only one Uranus offer in ante 6-8 out of four Celestial packs, and no 4th multiplier (Brainstorm was the only one).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 8 boss hand 1: I played blind into the shuffled order. I should have used the first hand as a cheap probe (a
   High Card throwaway; Summit fires on it) to locate Summit, then reordered and played the big Two Pairs. Two big hands
   in the right order (~178k + ~130k) were still short, but the probe-first line keeps the best hand for the known order.
2. Ante 6-7: I spent ~$30 on Jumbo/Standard packs for Hologram (+0.25 each). That was right, but I also bought 4 Celestial
   packs that missed Uranus (Pluto x3, Venus); planets bought directly from the shop ($3) were the only reliable levels.
3. Ante 8 Small: Justice on AC gave only ~x1.2 (Glass doubles the mult before the flat +mult jokers). Holding the Justice
   for the boss would have changed nothing here, but Glass is weak on a flat-+mult build (confirms 7809_WWQ5PQJX).

What worked:
- Mystic Summit from the first shop carried ante 1-7: always burn both discards before the scoring hand. Burning 1-card
  discards kept Ramen decay at -0.01 to -0.02 per round.
- Judgement from a free Charm-tag Mega Arcana gave Hologram at ante 3; Standard packs fed it to x3.25 by ante 7 and also
  brought 2 Steel cards, a Stone Red Seal (+100 chips filler), a Lucky TH (won The Eye) and a Blue Seal (a free Uranus).
- Unstickered Cavendish x3 for $4 at ante 4 and Ramen from a Jumbo Buffoon at ante 6.
- The Eye: Two Pair first, then a Full House, then any unused type (High Card) for the rest.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the boss is Amber Acorn, THEN play a 1-4 card High Card throwaway first (Summit/Banner/flat +mult jokers still fire),
  solve for the joker order from its score (try every order of your known jokers until chips x mult matches), `order`
  the +mult jokers left of the xMults, and only then play your big hands. The order stays fixed for the whole blind.
  A score from a hand whose type triggers different jokers (Mad/Clever on Two Pair) also pins down the order.
- IF you own Mystic Summit and a made hand already clears, THEN still burn the discards first when the extra +15 mult
  turns a thin clear into a safe one; use 1-card discards when you own Ramen.
- IF you own Hologram, THEN a $4 Standard pack is a +0.25 xMult purchase: buy one per shop from ante 3 on, and prefer
  Steel/Stone/Lucky/Blue Seal cards from it.
- IF a Steel card is in hand, THEN never use it as a filler: play 4-card Two Pairs so it stays held (x1.5).
- IF you are at ante 7+ and the shop sells the main-hand planet for $3, THEN buy it before any Celestial pack (packs hit
  the main-hand planet ~38%; 0 of 4 hit in this run's ante 7-8).

  with discards burned Summit is +15 of 31 mult (~x1.9) vs Ice Cream +47% chips. It values Summit at 0 discards unused.
- Under Amber Acorn every joker shows `None (effect not in table)` and `hands`/`play` est collapse to 176-2176 (actual
  12797-131338); `advise` then recommends a flush chase. The advisor could keep the pre-blind joker list and model the
  unknown order (or suggest the probe line above).
- `advise` rule picks used a Steel card as a filler at the ante-6 Big (`play TWOPAIR 3 4 5 6 8` with 2H Steel at 8); the

  solver ranked the pair keep higher. Straight/Flush keeps should be scored with the build's hand-gated jokers (Mad/Clever).

  same result).
