Playbook suggestions:
1. Add a rule under section 4/5: tarots requiring a card target (anything that isn't Hanged Man/Death/Hermit/Fool/Temperance/Emperor/Judgement/High Priestess/Strength-note: Strength also needs a target and was NOT auto-applied, correcting an assumption made mid-run) show `NOUSE` until you run `sel i` then `use N` during SELECTING_HAND; don't let them rot in inventory across multiple shops -- either use them on a hand card at the next opportunity or `sellc` them immediately if they don't fit your build, since they block the 2 consumable slots that better auto-applying tarots (Hermit for money, Emperor for more tarots) could otherwise fill.
