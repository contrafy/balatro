## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING. Zero xMult was ever offered in 9 shop views and 3 Buffoon packs (Wrathful, Satellite, Devious,
Dagger, 8 Ball, Abstract; shops: Seltzer, Onyx, Greedy, Smiley, Rocket, Wee, Matador, Riff-raff, Odd Todd, Superposition).
Ante 2-4 were carried by two temporary jokers (Seltzer = retrigger all, 10 hands; Abstract perishable at +15-18 mult). Both
expired at the ante-4 Big Blind, so the best hand dropped from ~7-9k to ~3-5k right before the 18000 boss. Every
remaining joker was a per-card suit/parity +mult (Wrathful spades, Onyx clubs, Even Steven, Smiley, Odd Todd): the per-hand
score depended on which suits/parities came, never multiplied.

Decisions I would change:
- Ante 3 Small skip for Top-up (Even Steven + Delayed Grat) was fine, but the ante-3 Big shop sold Delayed Gratification for
  Smiley; Smiley added ~10% while keeping $4/round would have funded one more reroll/pack at ante 4. Neutral at best.
- Ante 4 first shop ($18): I bought Celestial + Arcana (the Fool->Uranus combo was good) but should have rerolled once ($5)
  for an xMult while still above $13; at that point Seltzer/Abstract expiry before the boss was already visible
  (`perishing soon` warning). The planned path had no answer for "two temporary jokers die before the boss".
- The Tooth: used both discards on the first hand (straight hit, 7742) and then had no discards for hands 2-3. Given 18000
  needed ~6000 x 3, the run was already decided in the shop; no hand line reached it.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF two of your scorers are temporary (Seltzer hands, perishable) and both expire within the current ante THEN compute B
  WITHOUT them before the boss shop; that is your real boss hand. Spend that ante's shops on a permanent scorer first.
- IF The Fool appears in an Arcana pack right after you used your main-hand planet THEN take it: it creates that planet
  again (Uranus -> Two Pair L3 here) for $0 extra.
- IF the ante-2 Small Blind tag is Handy and you have played ~8 hands THEN skip: it pays ~$6-8 immediately and avoids a $0
  Small Blind with a weak build (here: $0 -> $6 -> Big cleared -> $12 shop bought the Jumbo Buffoon).
- IF you own holo Onyx Agate / suit +mult jokers and The Moon/Star/World shows up THEN convert 3 cards to that suit: each is
  +7 mult forever (I did; small but permanent).
- IF Mr. Bones is offered as a $1 rental at ante 1 with no scorer THEN buy it for the ante and sell it at the ante-2 shop:
  it cost $4 total and covered both the Big Blind and the Pillar (neither needed it in the end).

Advisor/CLI defects:
- `shopadvise` ante 2 said LEAVE with $6 while flagging EMERGENCY (464 vs 1000); Seltzer ($6, retrigger all for 10 hands)
  was rated UTIL 20. It doubled Wrathful and card chips and carried 3 blinds (est 1564/1760/9016 one-shots). Seltzer should
  count as a scorer/multiplier while it has hands left.
- `shopadvise` ante 4 (Tooth ahead) said LEAVE with $7 to "keep $6 for The Tooth" while in EMERGENCY; The Tooth's money
  loss does not block plays, so keeping cash for it at the cost of a scoring buy is backwards when the run is under-scaled.
- Arcana packs print "No pack advice for this pack type"; The Fool (copies the last planet) and The Moon (suit change for a
  suit-scoring build) need valuing.
- Ante 1 skip ADVICE `b blind` for Double tag was right; ante 2 said "do not skip: Handy Tag is not worth a shop visit" with
  8 hands played (Handy paid $6 immediately).
