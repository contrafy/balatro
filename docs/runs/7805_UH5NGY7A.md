## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING + MONEY, both caused by the Madness line. Madness (taken at ante 1 from a Buffoon pack, SIM 0.56
vs 0.07) became a real growth xMult (x5.5 at ante 5) but it destroys a random non-eternal joker at every Small/Big select,
so from ante 2 on only ETERNAL jokers could be added: I passed Zany/Mad (pack), Gros Michel, Juggler, Drunkard, Blackboard,
Hanging Chad. The final row was Wrathful{E} + Raised Fist{E,RENTAL} + Photograph{E} + Madness with 2 slots empty for the
whole run. Flat mult never exceeded ~+15 (Wrathful on 5 spades) + ~+16 (Fist), so the engine was "spade flush or nothing":
spade flush ~21k, everything else 5-12k at ante 5. The eternal rental Raised Fist (-$3/round, 9 rounds = $27) plus Red
Stake's $0 Small Blind kept money at $0-10 all run: no Jupiter, no Arcana/Spectral rolls, no rescue tarot.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 Madness pick. Correct for the next 3 blinds, wrong for the run: it locks out 70% of all jokers. If taken, SELL it
   by the ante-3 shop at the latest once a non-eternal scorer appears (Gros Michel +15 / Mad / Zany were all on offer at
   ante 3 with $8+). At ante 3 Madness was x3.5; two flat +mult jokers plus their slot growth beat it from ante 4 on.
2. Ante 3 Raised Fist{ETERNAL,RENTAL}. SIM +0.44 over 3 blinds, but -$27 of rent by ante 5 was every planet I could not buy.
   With Madness sold, Gros Michel ($5, SIM 0.33) would have done the job without the permanent tax.
3. Ante 4 shops: kept $10 then $6 for "an eternal joker" that never came; Jupiter never appeared. Flush L2 at ante 5 was the
   main hand; one more Flush level (or a Celestial pack at ante 2-3 instead of Mercury/Uranus for non-main hands) adds ~25%.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Madness, THEN every non-eternal joker you add dies at the next Small/Big select (random among non-eternals): only
  buy ETERNAL jokers, or buy non-eternals only in the shop right BEFORE THE BOSS (Madness does not fire on boss select) and
  sell them before the next Small for their sell value.
- IF you own Madness at ante 3+ AND the shop offers 2+ flat scorers you can afford, THEN sell Madness and fill slots: an empty
  joker row tops out ~10-20k at ante 5 even at x5.5.
- IF Photograph and Wrathful (or any per-card +mult joker) are both owned, THEN put the suited non-face cards BEFORE the first
  face in hand order (`move hand`): the +3s get doubled. Here: straight flush 31141 -> ~36000, spade flush 18430 -> 21340.
- IF you own Raised Fist, THEN pad plays with your LOWEST cards as fillers and keep high cards held: Pair AA + 3D 5D 6C (held
  J 9 9) = 5076 vs 2538 bare; discarding two low held cards before a made flush raised it 4424 -> 6083.
- IF the round needs one specific suit card and 0 discards remain, THEN dig with a High Card/Pair play of the off-suit cards
  (solver lines: 0.92 vs 0.69 at ante 4 Small; 0.18 -> straight flush at ante 5 Big).
