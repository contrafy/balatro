Post-mortem: binding constraint = misplay (discard sequencing), not scaling or bad luck per se. All 3 discards
  of the Big Blind round were spent on the FIRST hand chasing a Straight (K-Q-J -> hoping for T, then a gap-9)
  that never completed, leaving ZERO discards for hands 2 and 3. Hand 2 held K-K-J-J-4-4 (three separate pairs)
  -- one more K or J would have made a Full House (L2, 65x6, likely >4000 alone) but there were no discards left
  to dig for it, so it was forced into a plain Two Pair. Hand 3 (A-Q-J-T-8-5-4-4) similarly had no discard
  available to consolidate into anything better than a Pair. The decision I would change: stop chasing a single
  speculative Straight across all available discards early in a round when the need is very large (7500);
  instead spend at most 1-2 discards per hand and preserve at least 1 discard for the final 1-2 hands so a
  made pair-heavy hand (very common with this deck) can be upgraded to Two Pair/Full House instead of being
  played raw. This is a repeat of a pattern that worked FINE in every earlier round of this run (where discards
  were spread across the round) but broke down here because discards were front-loaded onto one hand.

Playbook suggestions:
  1. When consumable (cons) slots are full of untargeted, card-targeting tarots (marked NOUSE, e.g. from
     Arcana packs opened in the SHOP phase where no hand is visible), avoid opening further Arcana/Mega-Arcana
     packs until those tarots are either used (in the next SELECTING_HAND phase) or sold -- otherwise packs
     that grant instant-effect cards (Emperor's 2 random tarots, High Priestess's 2 planets, The Fool's copy)
     silently do nothing when there's no free slot to receive them. This run wasted 2 pack picks this way.
  2. Add explicit guidance for discard budgeting within a single round: cap discards spent on any ONE hand to
     leave at least 1 discard available for a later hand, especially when `need` is large relative to made-hand
     ests, so a made pair/two-pair hand encountered later in the round (very common) can still be upgraded
     toward Full House/Trips instead of being played raw with 0 discards left.
  3. `advise`'s self-reported EV-projection text has a display bug (prints "X < need Y" for cases where X > Y,
     or vice versa) -- worth a fix so the printed reasoning can be trusted without independent sanity-checking
     the arithmetic each time.
