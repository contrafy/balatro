Post-mortem: binding constraint was SCALING, not money or a single misplay. Across 5 antes, only one
  xMult-class item (Astronomer's polychrome edition, x1.5) was ever found; the run never got 2+ xMult
  sources or Glass/high-value card enhancements (only 2 Empress +4-mult cards, 1 Steel, 1 Lucky, 2 Bonus
  +30-chip cards) stacked on top of levels. Two Pair L2 + Three of a Kind L2 + Full House L2 with flat
  jokers plateaued around 3000-17000 per hand depending on draw, well under the ante-5 boss's 22000,
  especially with 4 of 8 cards debuffed under The Head. The ONE decision I would change: at ante 3-4,
  when repeated rerolls turned up only AVOID-flagged or weak-conditional jokers, I should have leaned harder
  into buying MORE Arcana/Spectral packs for Glass (Justice) enhancements specifically, rather than spending
  reroll money hunting for a clean xMult joker that never appeared again after Astronomer; Glass x2 stacks
  fast on a pair-based main hand and does not depend on a lucky joker offer.
