## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no permanent xMult; the whole engine was flat Misprint (+0..23, huge variance) + 3 perishables (Mad, Seeing Double, Photograph, Stencil) bought as bridges. Ante 5 needed ~50k from 3 hands with 0 discards left after a failed Full House dig, and the last hand rode on a Misprint roll (needed >= ~5, rolled 3).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 5 Window hand 1: I discarded 4 cards (keep JJ55) chasing a Full House at p0.46 with The Window debuffing ALL diamonds (1/4 of my draws are dead). The solver's other line (discard 3 8, keep JJ99 55 + made Two Pair) or just playing Two Pair JJ55 + 1 discard kept would have left a discard for hand 2/3. Under a suit-debuff boss, count the debuffed suit's share of the draw pool before spending the last discard on a 2-4 card draw.
2. I skipped BOTH Small 25000 and Big 37500 at ante 5 for free packs (Earth/Venus, glass/mult cards: all small effects). One of those blinds (a made Two Pair 30k clears Small in one hand) would have given a shop visit with $9 + a Skip/other economy and a 3rd reroll chance for a growth xMult. The packs added almost nothing; I had 0 permanent xMult and needed the shop looks.
3. Ante 4 skip of both blinds then two Boss Tag rerolls got The Eye (cleared one-shot), fine; but I should have used the $22 at ante 4 on Paint Brush/rerolls earlier instead of holding it (no growth xMult was ever found).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the boss debuffs a suit (Window/Head/Club/Goad) THEN a discard-draw has ~25% dead cards: prefer playing the made Two Pair and keep the discard for the next hand; debuffed cards still make the hand TYPE (a wheel Straight with 2 debuffed cards scored 5400).
- IF your engine contains Misprint (+0..23, est uses 6) THEN est is ~25-35% below the mean, but a single 1-card hand is a ~25% gamble; keep 2 scoring hands in reserve, never plan a 1-card finisher.
- IF you own Joker Stencil and the last hand is a 1-card High Card THEN sell any non-firing non-negative joker first (Seeing Double needs 2 suits): Stencil x3 -> x4 (+33%). A negative joker (Mad) frees no slot, selling it gains nothing.
- IF a Negative Tag is on the ante-2 Small THEN skip it: the post-Big shop gave a free negative Mad Joker (extra slot + scorer).
- IF EMERGENCY at ante 3 with $12, THEN one reroll found foil Photograph $1 rental: foil +50 chips x (mult ~20) was the biggest single add (SIM +0.49); a $1 rental was right here.
- Boss Tag on Small/Big REROLLS the boss immediately (Water -> Mouth -> Eye): you cannot decline it; skip it only when you accept a random boss.
- Skip Tag pays $5 x (skips so far incl. this) at the moment of the skip ($15 at the 3rd skip).
