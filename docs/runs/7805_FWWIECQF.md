## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING, specifically the mult side. Mult came only from Shoot the Moon (+13 per held Queen): with 0
Queens held my mult was 3-12, so hands swung 10x on whether Queens were in hand (Pair 4 + stone 2.9k vs the same hand with
QQQ ~50k). The only real xMults were polychrome Acrobat (ante 5) and a perishable Throwback (ante 5, x3.5 by ante 8 through
11 skips). No permanent +mult joker and no Pair levels (Pair stayed L2 all run: one Mercury seen in 25+ shop views).
The skip-to-boss line (ante 5-8: 7 skips) worked: it kept a perishable Throwback alive to ante 8 and every boss was the
only fight. Ante 8 needed ~2x what the deck could make; 5 dig hands found the first Queen on hand 4.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 5 first shop ($50): 3 rerolls found Acrobat but I sold Misprint (+11.5 avg mult, my only flat mult besides StM)
   for it and later Ice Cream for Throwback. Keeping one flat +mult joker would have made Queen-less hands 3-4x bigger;
   selling Turtle Bean instead of Misprint was available (Turtle decayed to nothing by ante 7 anyway).
2. Ante 7: Mime (foil, retriggers held effects = StM x2 per Queen and steel x2.25) appeared at ante 8 when I could not fit
   it (Verdant needs a sellable sacrifice; Smiley/Sly eternal). Owning two eternal flat jokers (Sly, Smiley) left only
   3 movable slots at the end: an eternal blocks the boss-specific sacrifice.
3. Ante 3-4: Arcana money went to Devil/Hanged Man/Empress; Death/Strength (more Queens for StM) never showed. With StM
   as the main mult, Standard packs (Queens) might have been the better feed.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the ante-8 boss is Verdant Leaf, THEN buy Burglar (or any sellable joker) as the sacrifice: select the blind, Burglar's
  +3 hands are granted at selection (6 hands, 0 discards), THEN sell it to un-debuff the cards. The hands are not revoked
  (verified: 6 hands after the sale).
- IF you own Throwback and a perishable carrier, THEN skipping Small+Big every ante keeps the perish counter frozen: a
  5-round Throwback bought at ante 5 lived through 4 bosses (antes 5-8) and grew x1.75 -> x3.5.
- IF Seeing Double is your xMult, THEN The Lovers (wild) on one card of your final pair/trips makes it fire (wild counts as
  the Club; verified Flint Trips JJJ with wild JH, est 170887 cleared). A DEBUFFED club does NOT count (SMODS
  seeing_double_check uses is_suit without bypass): under The Club, Seeing Double is dead.
- IF Acrobat is owned, THEN with 3 hands play the two smaller made hands first and keep the biggest (plus the stone card) for
  the last; never play the Queens you hold for Shoot the Moon.
- IF you rely on a held-card joker (Shoot the Moon), THEN Tower stones (+50 chips, always score) are the ideal fillers and
  hand size (Turtle Bean, Paint Brush, Juggle tag) is score; at ante 6+ with 0 Queens held the build scores ~5% of normal.
- IF The Needle (1 hand) is the boss and you skip the Big, THEN take a Juggle tag: +3 hand size for that round (11 cards)
  found 2P + stone + QQ held = 154k one-shot.
