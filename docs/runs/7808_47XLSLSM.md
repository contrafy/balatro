Post-mortem:
- Binding constraint: scaling. At ante 3 I had ZERO xMult, main hands at L3, ~2200 per good hand vs 6400 needed in 3 hands. Every hand needed to be a good Two Pair/Flush; The Mark (face-down) removed the visibility needed to build them. Three of six joker slots were permanently locked by Eternal jokers (Lusty, Juggler, Golden): each was a defensible buy alone, but together they left 3 slots, two of them perishable stopgaps (Jolly, Wrathful) that die in ~4 rounds.
- Decisions I would change: (1) Wrathful (+3/spade) over skipping: it scored almost nothing because my kept cards were hearts/mixed; with Lusty I should have bought another hearts-agnostic scorer or kept the slot open for an xMult. (2) At the Mark's first hand I discarded my whole hand twice for Two Pair (hearts + pair) and ended with zero discards for hand 2-3 that were played blind; a Pair 55 (1050) + Straight 960 play first would not have cleared either, so the real problem was no multiplier, not the line. (3) Using the 4 face-down cards as a "guaranteed pair" produced only 1500 (est said 1426) and 1150 (est 2106). Two Pair via face-down cards is ~25%, not a plan. (4) I bought Juggler (+1 hand size, eternal) instead of keeping $7 for a scorer in the ante-2 shop; Golden Joker gave +$4/round but I never had spare money for xMult in the shops anyway (every shop had $0-13).
- Reusable rules:
  1. If you own Lusty/Greedy-type suit-mult jokers, a 5-card flush of that suit scores 4-5x a normal hand (hearts Flush est 1989 under The Flint, 2541 at ante 3 Small): keep 4-of-suit at the first discard when you have 2 discards (~80% to hit).
  2. The Flint (halve base chips and mult): a joker that adds flat mult per scoring card (Lusty/Wrathful/Greedy/Gluttonous) is almost unaffected: build the hand around hearts and expect ~2000 only with a 5-heart flush; plain pairs score ~250.
  3. Under The Mark, do not expect to cash in with face-down cards: 4 face-downs always contain a Pair but Two Pair only ~25%; est assumed Two Pair and over-promised 2106 vs 1150.
  4. Do not stack more than two Eternal jokers on Gold: three eternals (Lusty, Juggler, Golden) left no room for xMult and two perishables (Jolly, Wrathful) expiring.
  5. Immolate from a Spectral pack at $0-4 is worth taking ($20): it funded Mega Celestial + Juggler + Golden in one shop.
  6. Ice Cream is a one-ante bridge: +100 chips cleared Big 450 with a Pair, but sell it by ante 3 for a slot.
