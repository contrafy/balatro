## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: luck on a jokerless ante-1 Small at Gold. With L1 hands and no jokers every pair-family hand is
44-112, so 300 in 3 hands needs at least one Trips/Straight/Flush/Full House (Two Pair x3 = ~310 only with high
ranks). The deck never produced one in 2 discards + 3 hands (21 cards seen: 4 pairs, no trips, max 4 of a suit).

Decisions I would change (all marginal, each was the solver's top line):
1. Discard 2: breaking BOTH made pairs (JJ, 66) for a 3-card flush draw. The solver rated it 0.90 vs 0.80 for keeping
   JJ66 and drawing 4. It missed; keeping JJ66 + discarding K 9 8 5 would have kept a Two Pair floor (and a 2-of-a-pair
   FH draw with 2 pairs = 4 outs over two draws). With no jokers and no discards after it, I would keep the made Two Pair.
2. Hand 2: the 3-card High Card dig (16 points) instead of 2P 8877 (100). Both lines needed a premium final hand; the
   dig only works if the FH comes. Close call, followed the solver (0.58 vs 0.45).
3. Possibly skip the Small for the Coupon tag? No: a jokerless Big (450) is worse than the Small (300), and the Coupon
   pays only in the shop after the Big. Playing was right.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you are at the ante-1 Small with 0 jokers and hold a made Two Pair with 1 discard left, THEN keep the Two Pair and
  discard the rest; do not break two made pairs for a 3-card flush draw (only ~30% to complete, and the floor is gone).
- IF your solver p_clear drops by 0.1+ after every draw in a round, THEN the early estimate was optimistic: prefer lines
  that keep a guaranteed floor (made Two Pair) over high-variance draws on the first two decisions.
- IF ante 1 Small with no jokers and est of the best made hand x 3 < 300, THEN discard toward trips/full house with the
  pairs kept (FH 312+ at L1 alone clears most of the blind).

Advisor/CLI defects:
- The "calibrated" solver p_clear was 0.97 at the first discard of a jokerless ante-1 Small and the round was lost;
  then 0.90 -> 0.74 -> 0.58 -> 0.00. One loss is not proof, but jokerless ante-1 Small at 0.97 looks over-confident
  (stake-2 data in PLAYBOOK: 4 of ~8 jokerless 300 Smalls lost). Worth checking calibration for 0-joker states.

  only Buffoon/Top-up): inconsistent text, `s` advice followed.
