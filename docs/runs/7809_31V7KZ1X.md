## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. One xMult the whole run (Ramen x2 decaying to x1.61), and it was a rental. Every hand was flat
+mult (Fibonacci, Onyx Agate, Hack retriggers) times L3-L4 levels; typical hand 6-7k at ante 5 against a 12.5k-per-hand
boss. Only Full Houses (Wily +100 chips) cleared anything big, and they show up about once a round. A reliable/growth xMult
was never offered in a shop I could afford (Campfire twice, one of them eternal in a pack). 3 of the 6 slots were eternal
(Fibonacci, Drunkard, Onyx Agate), and that hard-capped how much the build could change by ante 5.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 Buffoon: took Drunkard{ETERNAL} (+1 discard). An eternal utility joker is a dead slot for the whole run. By ante 5
   I could not sell anything for a scorer without losing Hack/Wily/Ramen. Skipping the pack was better.
2. Ante 3: Onyx Agate{ETERNAL} in EMERGENCY was correct for ante 3. But together with 2 other eternals it froze the build.
   Before you take a third eternal, count how many slots stay sellable.
3. Ante 4: I spent $8 on Celestial/Arcana at score ratio 0.4. The Strength (K->A for Fibonacci) and Empress picks were fine.
   The $ never reached the $20+ that buys an xMult plus a reroll: two rentals ($6/round) bled every shop.
4. Ante 5 Club boss: I spent the last 2 hands digging for a 20k+ Full House. Two Pair now and then a FH would have been
   equal. The solver said both lines were ~0.20, so the boss was lost before it started (projected round 0.56x).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF a Buffoon pick is an ETERNAL non-scoring utility (Drunkard, Juggler, Credit Card) AND you already own 1+ eternal, THEN
  skip it. By ante 5 every slot must be sellable for the xMult you will need.
- IF you own Fibonacci, THEN use Strength on Kings (K->A) and Empress/Justice on 2/3/5/8 cards. With Hack, put enhancements
  on 2/3/5 so the retrigger doubles them. Fillers are your non-Fibonacci cards (Q, T, 9, 7); never use a 2/3/5/8/A as a filler.
- IF your best hands are Trips/Full House (Wily/Zany type jokers), THEN those jokers outvalue Popcorn-style flat +mult for a
  boss. Compare on the hand type that actually clears, not the most played type.
- IF the score check is < 0.2 at ante 5 and no xMult is on offer, THEN skipping the Small for a Buffoon/Charm tag is right
  (saves rent and adds a free xMult roll), but expect to lose the boss without a new multiplier.
- IF Temperance is in the shop and jokers' total sell value >= $8, THEN buy-use it (here +$11 net at $6).
