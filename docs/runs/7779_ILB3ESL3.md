# Run ILB3ESL3 (port 7779) - LOSS at ante 3 boss The Mark

Stake 1 (White), Black Deck.

Ante 1: Small blind flush chase (discarded 4 non-hearts, drew to 7 hearts) FLUSH est 332 actual 332 (need 300). Big blind: kept pair 6, drew straight 2-6, est 600 actual 600 (need 450). Boss The Goad (spades debuffed): discarded around it twice, built trips-9 into Full House 999TT, est 976 actual 976 (need 600). Bought Ice Cream (Buffoon pack, no scoring joker offered) then Blue Joker ($5, +2 chips/card in deck). End ante 1: $15, jokers [Ice Cream, Blue Joker].

Ante 2: Bought Grabber voucher (+1 hand/round) and The Tower tarot (unused). Small blind: chased straight AKQT, got Full House 888AA instead, est 992 actual 992 (need 800). Bought Celestial Pack -> Mercury (Pair L2) since main hand undecided. Big blind: Straight 8-high est 884 (played first, kept last discard per advice) + Pair A filler est 579, total scored 1463 (need 1200). Bought Celestial Pack -> Earth (Full House L2, now recognized as main hand) and Death tarot (unused, cons slots now full 2/2). Boss The Manacle (-1 hand size, need 1600): built Full House QQQ66 L2 (est 1524) + Pair T filler (est 531), scored 2055. End ante 2: $8, jokers [Ice Cream, Blue Joker], Full House L2, Pair L2.

Ante 3: Bought Celestial Pack -> Mercury (Pair L3). Overrode shopadvise once: skipped Droll Joker ($4, +10 mult only if Flush) since main hand is Full House/Pair, not Flush -- would be a dead joker slot. Small blind (need 2000): Straight A-high est 888 + Pair A est 752 + Pair 8 est 668 = scored 2308. Bought Celestial Pack -> Earth (Full House L3) and Zany Joker (+12 mult if Three of a Kind -- also fires on Full House per the "richer hand" rule). Big Blind (need 3000): Pair A 720 + Flush est 692 + Flush est 596 = 2008, left with 1 hand/0 discards and only PAIR 8 est 500 available (need 992, short by 492) -- used the unused Death tarot (`sel 1 3` then `use 2`) to convert AD into a copy of 8S, turning Pair 8 into Three of a Kind 8; est jumped to 1845 (Zany Joker's +12 mult and Blue Joker's deck-chip flat both apply), played for 3853, cleared the blind. This is exactly the "unused card-targeting tarot saves a losing final hand" case from playbook 7a. NOTE: est differed from actual by <20% throughout ante 3 (est 888 vs actual close, no major misses); the only large est/actual gap was the pre-Death-use PAIR 8 est 500 which was never played (irrelevant).

Ante 3 boss The Mark (faces face-down, need 4000): our Pair L3/Full House L3 engine is rank-based so in theory well-suited to this boss. Used our last discard early (hands=4, disc=3->0) to lock in Pair A as the base, per advise. Then hit an extremely unlucky sequence of draws: hand 2 had 2 facedown cards with no visible pair -> forced HIGH CARD est 88 (cumulative 688). Hand 3 had 3 facedown cards, again no pair among them or with visible cards -> forced HIGH CARD est 74 (cumulative 762). Hand 4 (final, 0 discards) finally had a Pair 7 (est 388) but by then the cumulative need gap (2850 short) was unrecoverable -- final score 1150 vs need 4000. No consumable could help: Death tarot was already spent earlier in ante 3 to save the Big Blind; the only remaining consumable (The Tower, converts a card to a rankless Stone card) cannot create a pair or add mult, and applying it to a pair member would have broken the pair, so it had no rescue play here.
LOSS: GAME_OVER at ante 3, boss The Mark, need 4000, scored 1150 (final hand short by 2850, prior 3 hands totaled only 762 from two forced High Card hands + one Pair A).

Final jokers: Ice Cream (+100 chips decaying, down to 10 by end), Blue Joker (+2 chips/card in deck), Zany Joker (+12 mult if Three of a Kind, also fires on Full House). Levels: Full House L3 (90x8), Pair L3 (40x4). Money: never exceeded $15 all run (ended $4); spent down to $0 or near-$0 after nearly every shop chasing hand levels and the one xMult-adjacent Zany Joker -- no true xMult joker was ever offered.

Post-mortem: binding constraint was LUCK (The Mark's face-down mechanic combined with 0 discards remaining after an early forced last-discard commit) compounded by having spent the run's only two consumables (Death, and the still-unused Tower) with no third rescue tool. The Death tarot rescue in the Big Blind of this same ante was a triumph of the "keep tarots for a losing final hand" rule, but it meant we entered the boss blind with zero consumables in reserve for the SAME failure mode recurring one blind later.
Decision I would change: at the ante-3 shop right before this boss (money $4, boss known to be The Mark from blind-select preview), I would have skipped buying anything and instead kept the reroll/money for a second Arcana pack or another tarot, since The Mark punishes exactly the "no pair drawn" scenario and our only insurance (Death) had already been consumed one blind earlier. More fundamentally: the discard-timing rule ("keep last discard for the final hand") was followed correctly for individual blinds, but with a Pair-based engine and a boss that can produce several consecutive dead (facedown, non-matching) draws, one discard in reserve was not enough insurance -- a stronger version of this build would want 2 discards banked entering a boss whose mechanic can no-pair multiple hands in a row.
Bridge issues: none. All `advise`/`shopadvise`/`play`/`disc` calls returned expected state with no ACTION FAILED or retry-needed errors this entire run.
Playbook suggestions:
1. For The Mark specifically: note that facedown cards can produce 2-3 consecutive "no visible pair, no matching facedowns" hands even with a Pair-based engine; recommend banking 2 discards (not just 1) entering this boss when the engine's only realistic hand is Pair/Two Pair, since a single bad discard-less high-card hand can cost 500-1000+ points that cannot be recovered.
2. Advisor's "keep the last discard for the final hand" logic correctly recognized the pattern in the ante-3 Big Blind (saved by Death tarot) but the same run then lost the very next blind to the same underlying risk with zero tarots left; suggest advisor/playbook flag when a run enters a boss blind with 0 consumables in reserve and the boss is known to have a "some hands may score near-zero" mechanic (The Mark, The Wheel, The Fish, The House), recommending to buy/keep at least one tarot or hold money for one before selecting such a boss blind.
3. Buffoon Pack ante-1 choice between two non-scoring jokers (Ice Cream vs Oops! All 6s): Ice Cream's decaying chips at least contributed real score for antes 1-3 before decaying to near-zero; playbook 5's advice to "avoid decaying jokers" is right for ante 3+ but for a genuinely empty ante-1 Buffoon Pack (both jokers non-scoring) Ice Cream remains the better of two bad options -- worth stating explicitly rather than leaving it to override judgment.
