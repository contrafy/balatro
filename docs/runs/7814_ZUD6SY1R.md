## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: the run had one scorer engine (Two Pair / Pair, Obelisk resets, no reliable xMult beyond it). Ante 2 bought Obelisk with $8 (leaving $0) and had only 5 jokers, so every ante-3 blind (3200, 4800, 6400) was won or lost on the draw. Boss The Wheel hides cards; a bad opening draw (no pair after 2 discards) cannot be recovered with 3 hands.

Decisions I would change:
- Obelisk growth resets to x1 when the hand played becomes your most played hand. Two Pair was my most played hand by the Head boss (played 3 times), so the Boss Head clear reset Obelisk from x1.4 to x1 and it grew only to x2.4 by the Wheel. Do not keep feeding the most played hand to a growth xMult; alternate hand types.
- Ante 2 SIM preferred Half Joker ($5, +20 mult on 3 or fewer cards). I bought Obelisk by the Gold rule. Both were arguable; leaving $0 with 6 jokers meant no money for ante 3's Wheel shop.
- In ante 3 I skipped no blinds but the Big Blind advisor said "b skip" (Perishable-carrier skip plan) while the playbook says never skip Big. I followed the playbook. Log this conflict.
