## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no multiplier before ante 4 and no money ever (Gold $0 Small + rent: wallet was $0-7 for the whole run). The build was one flat +10 mult, +250 chips and a growing Obelisk x2; peak ~6-10k per hand vs 25000 Small at ante 5 (needs ~8.3k per hand over 3 hands with no misses, hand size 6 from Stuntman made Two Pair draws unreliable). Ante 5 was always going to be a coin flip at ~0.9x.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop: Baron (x1.5 per King HELD) was a rental whose value depended on holding a King; it paid $3/round and was sold at ante 4. A permanent +mult would have left more money. It was the only scorer offered (Zany E+R barred), so not a real error.
2. Ante 4: sold Walkie/Baron for Stuntman (-2 hand size). It carried the Big/Eye (+2700 per hand) but the 6-card hand made digs for Two Pair far weaker at ante 5; a Pair engine needs hand size.
3. Ante 4 Big: I played two ~4000 hands then needed a miracle (royal flush 0.5%). Better sequencing: the Big needed 4500 per hand but my average hand was 3600-4500; there was no fix at $0.
4. Ante 5 Small final hand: kept discards through the 2nd hand to protect Banner (+60 chips at 2 discards) then burned both discards on the last hand; Banner perished at that round end anyway, so its +chips were worth less than an earlier dig. When a perishable chips joker ends this round, treat its discard bonus as already spent and dig on hand 1.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF Superposition is holo (or any holo joker) and nothing else scores, THEN it is +10 mult on every hand: buy it even though the joker text is "util"; put holo jokers LEFT of xMults.
- IF Superposition is owned THEN playing a Straight containing an Ace makes a Tarot (I got Justice twice): include an Ace-high/low straight when it is free.
- IF a Rare tag is skipped for at ante 4, THEN the free rare in the next shop can be eternal (Obelisk{E}) - still take xMult growth jokers free.
- IF Banner is owned THEN each discard left is +30 chips; with ~12-20 mult that is +360-600 per discard per hand, so never discard unless the dig is worth more.
- IF Obelisk is owned with Pair as the most played type THEN play High Card / Two Pair / Straight (+0.2 each); est models the reset at Pair.
- IF the final hand needs only a sliver more than a made Flush, THEN throwing away non-royal cards to chase a royal/straight flush (100x8) beats playing the flush that cannot clear (it happened: Straight Flush 7218 vs Flush 4998 vs need 5316).
