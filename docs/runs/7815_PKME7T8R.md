## Post-mortem

**Binding constraint:** Scoring power was too low for the ante-3 blinds. Hand scores were Two Pair ~464-1430 and Pair ~870 at L2; no permanent xMult was owned at any point. Money was $2 entering the Big Blind after buying Arrowhead, so the shop had no xMult option (Card Sharp $6, Sock and Buskin $6, Jumbo Celestial $6 all unaffordable). The Water 6400 boss and ante-4 blinds (9000/13500) were far out of reach at this scoring level.

**Decisions I would change:**
1. Ante 2 Mercury purchase ($3) and Sly Joker ($3, ETERNAL, Pair-conditional) gave chips-only or conditional scoring; with the $6 Card Sharp xMult in view, saving toward it (skipping Sly) might have helped. Rule: if an xMult is shop-visible at $6, do not spend $3 on chips-only jokers the shop before.
2. Square Joker buy in ante 1 (chips-only, "adds ~0 now") was wasted money; sold at a loss later. Rule: do not buy chips-only jokers with descriptions showing "~0 now".
3. Ante 2 Small Blind skip (Foil Tag) was declined on SCORE CHECK EMERGENCY. Unclear whether skipping would have helped; the Foil Tag was not a scorer.
4. Ante 3 Arrowhead (+50 chips per Spade, $7) spent the last money on a chips-only joker with a 3-deck Spade distribution. Rule: at $7 with a 6400 boss ahead, prefer saving for xMult ($6 Card Sharp) over Arrowhead.

**Concrete reusable rules:**
- If a shop offers an xMult joker at or under your money, buy it before any chips-only joker, even if the chips joker has a higher immediate score estimate.
- If the next blind's required score is more than 3x your best made-hand estimate, do not spend money on chips-only jokers; save for xMult.
- If the last two hands of a blind are both made hands with est < need/2, the blind is lost; accept the loss and stop spending draws on the advisor's "draw" lines.
- Keep at least $4 at the end of each shop from ante 3 so a $6 xMult is reachable after the next blind.
