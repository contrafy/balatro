## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING from a JOKER SUPPLY that never offered a permanent scorer, made worse by my money choices.
Every scorer I owned was a bridge: Misprint{PERISHABLE} (ante 1-3), Shoot the Moon{PERISHABLE} (+13 per Queen HELD), Banner{RENTAL}
(+30 chips per discard left). Shop jokers seen in 6 shops + 1 reroll + 1 Buffoon pack: Crazy{R}, Misprint{P}, Madness, Golden{R},
Splash, Moon{P}, Cartomancer, Banner{R}, Swashbuckler, Business Card{E}, Rocket{E}, Diet Cola{R}: no +mult scorer without a
sticker and no xMult at all. At the Manacle my real hands without a held Queen were 390-426 (0 mult jokers active), so the
"~1700 per hand" SCORE CHECK was a Queen-held 75th percentile, not my typical hand.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 Big skip for Economy + Double Tag ($6 -> $24): it removed a 4800 coin flip but also the last shop before the boss,
   and I died holding $28. The money could only have helped if spent before the boss. With a build this weak the right line
   was to spend the $5-6 in the ante-3 Small shop on an Arcana pack (Strength/Death -> more Queens for Shoot the Moon, or
   a rescue) and play the Big for its shop; or, if skipping, accept that the money is for ante 4 and that the boss must be
   cleared with the current build (it could not: ~400 per hand without Queens).
2. Ante 1 Big shop: Mercury $3 with one perishable scorer (checklist: no planets with 0-1 scorers). It left $4 instead of $7,
   so the ante-2 shop needed a $5 reroll and the Moon buy left $1; with $14 I could have afforded joker + reroll.
3. Ante 2 Big skip for Coupon while broke ($2): the free items were a Business Card{ETERNAL}, a Standard and a Celestial pack,
   none a scorer. The Big would have paid ~$6 and shown a shop. "When broke at antes 2-4 never skip the Big" held here.
4. Ante 2 Small shop: Banner{RENTAL} $1 over Swashbuckler $4 (I had $3: unaffordable, so not a real choice), fine.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your scorers are all perishable/rental AND the next ante's boss is a hand-size boss (The Manacle), THEN evaluate held-card
  jokers (Shoot the Moon, Baron, Raised Fist) at their no-holder value: with 7 cards and 4 Queens in 54, most hands hold none.
- IF you skip a Big for money (Economy/Double Tag) at ante 2-4, THEN check first that the boss is clearable with the CURRENT
  build: the skipped Big was also the last shop, and money never scores.
- IF the shop has Diet Cola{RENTAL} $1, THEN buy-and-sell it in the same shop (net $0, no rent) for a Double Tag; it pays
  when the next skippable blind has an Economy/Coupon/Rare/Buffoon tag (here Economy x2 = +$18).
- IF you own 0-1 permanent scorers at ante 1, THEN keep the $3 instead of a planet: the ante-2 shop needs $8-10 to buy a
  scorer and still reroll once.
