## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling, then a money-hoarding misplay. Every scorer until ante 4 was a bridge (Wrathful and Half Joker
perishable, Ice Cream decaying 5 chips per hand, Gros Michel rental) and the only xMult (free foil The Duo from the Rare tag)
arrived in the last shop before the ante-4 boss. The run died 18 points short with $22 in the bank and an unused The Star.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 shop before Small: SELL 8 Ball and BUY Wrathful Joker{PERISHABLE} $5 (SIM said +0.04, I kept $21 for interest). The
   final hand was a Broadway straight with AS and TS: +6 mult would have made it 171 x 44 = 7524 > 6516. The interest it
   protected ($1) never got spent. Any +mult joker on any hand beats interest in the last shop before a no-discard boss.
2. Ante 4 Big-blind shop ($27): spent $5 on a reroll and left $22. With The Water next (0 discards, 3 hands, est ~1.0-1.3x),
   that money should have gone into score: Jumbo Arcana ($6) or Venus/Devil were weak, but a second reroll looking for any
   flat +mult was better than banking. Dying with $22 is the "deaths holding $8-12" rule in a worse form.
3. Taking the free 8 Ball (Coupon shop) was harmless only because it was sellable; it never produced anything.
4. The Water hand 2: I played Pair 55 + 3 fillers keeping K Q J (needed any K/Q/J pair, ~75%) instead of Straight A-T first
   (6688, then any pair of 6+ for ~5548 on the last hand, solver 0.73). Both were coin flips; the real error was arriving
   with ~1.0x the boss.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the next blind is The Water (0 discards) and your 3-hand round is under ~1.3x its need, THEN treat the shop as TIGHT:
  sell a do-nothing joker (8 Ball, utility) for ANY +mult/+chips scorer on offer, even a perishable one with a tiny SIM gain;
  money left after the boss is worthless if you lose by 18.
- IF your scorers are all bridges (perishable / Ice Cream / Gros Michel), THEN the ante's last shop must ADD a scorer, not
  bank: Ice Cream loses ~500 points per hand on a 3-hand round by ante 4.
- IF a Rare tag is on the ante-3/4 Small and a perishable carrier has 1 round left, THEN skipping the Small is right: the
  carrier plays the Big and the free Rare (here foil The Duo) arrives before the boss.
- IF Hermit is in the shop with $10-20, THEN buy-and-use it first (here $14 -> $28), then shop.
- IF you must dig on the final hands of The Water, THEN keep only the cards whose completions clear (here K Q J for a face
  pair) and draw 5; a 4-card straight draw that scores just under the need is a dead keep.
