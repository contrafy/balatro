## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: supply + economy at ante 1-2. In 7 shop views across antes 1-2 (incl. 3 rerolls) no PERMANENT
non-rental +mult joker was offered at all; the only joker scorers I could get were Greedy{P} (Buffoon pack) and
Jolly{P} ($3), bought one round apart, so both bridges expired at the ante 2->3 boundary exactly when the 3.2x jump hits.
Ante 3 began with $5, 0 scorers alive next ante, and base mult 3 on Two Pair L2. Every later dollar went to chips
(Blue Joker) or rerolls that showed no +mult. Final: 3 hands of Two Pair ~1300-1600 vs 1600/hand needed at the Big.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 Big shop ($13): I rerolled BEFORE buying Banner{P} $5, assuming it would stay; a reroll replaces the joker
   slots. Then Banner never came back affordable. Buy the affordable bridge first, then reroll with what is left.
2. Ante 2 first shop ($9): rerolled to $4 at ante 2 with 2 (perishable) scorers. The reroll showed Banner at $5, one
   dollar out of reach. Rerolling below $20 whiffs (playbook says so); holding $9 -> ~$15 after Small would have
   bought the next $5-6 joker AND kept interest.
3. Ante 2 Small: the Handy Tag was worth $7 immediately and perishables do not tick on a skip. With $4 and both
   scorers perishable, the skip (Greedy+Jolly alive one round longer, $11 for the post-Big shop) was probably better
   than the $0 Small shop that offered nothing.
4. Hard-rule cost: Clever{RENTAL} $1 (+80 chips on Two Pair, my main) was barred at ante 2. I followed the rule; noting
   that this seed's ante 1-2 never offered a single permanent +mult joker, so the rule left perishables as the only path.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you want an affordable joker in the shop AND also want to reroll, THEN buy it first: `reroll` replaces both
  joker slots (Banner $5 lost at ante 2).
- IF all your scorers are perishables bought within 1-2 rounds of each other, THEN they die in the same round; the
  first ante-3 shop must buy a permanent +mult, so carry $8+ into it instead of rerolling at ante 2 under $15.
- IF you are broke (<= $0 or on credit) at the ante-3+ Small AND a perishable scorer has 1 round left, THEN skip the
  Small: the $0 Small shop buys nothing and the skip keeps the scorer alive for the Big (Coupon then makes the next
  shop free). It did not save this run but it was the only line where the Big was ~50% at the start.
- IF your only perishable scorers expire before the next ante AND a Handy/Economy tag pays now on the Small, THEN skip
  the Small: perishables do not tick on a skip and the cash reaches the post-Big shop.
- IF Credit Card is bought in EMERGENCY, THEN spend the credit on the joker you need before rerolls, and stop at
  -$12 or so: two rerolls ($5+$6) at Gold ante 3 showed 0 +mult jokers here.
