## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: one flat joker (Shoot the Moon: +13 mult only per Queen HELD, i.e. nothing on most hands) and a suit boss. The Club debuffed about half my hand every deal (4-5 of 8 cards), so hands scored at base chips with no card chips and almost no mult, 240/140/84 per hand against 600. The Small/Big were carried by luck (a Broadway draw, a Queen held with Two Pair AA66); the Club had neither.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop 2 ($7): left with $7 unspent while only Shoot the Moon scored. The upcoming boss was The Club (debuffs clubs) and my projected round was 1.00x of 450 / 459 vs 600 ("TIGHT", floor OFF). I should have spent on score: Arcana pack ($4) or Jumbo Standard ($6), or both planets (Venus/Earth only +10-15 chips levels, weak). Honest read: nothing in that shop would have fixed a 600 boss, but a Pair/Two Pair planet or the Arcana pack (Empress/Justice style) had some chance. The first shop's choice (Shoot the Moon over the Buffoon pack) was the real gamble: Shoot the Moon is a conditional +mult that fires on ~half of hands; the pack gave 2 jokers to choose from with an any-hand option.
2. Boss hand 1: Straight with a debuffed card played as a 5-card hand scored only 240. After I banked 240 and 140, the 2 discards were spent on a 3-card draw for a 220 last hand. A better line would have been: hand 1 discard the 4 debuffed clubs first (keep 9H 8S 7D 6D + draw), so that the 2 played hands were rebuilt on non-club cards. I am not sure it wins, but the Flush of 5 debuffed clubs (140 for nothing) used a hand slot for the lowest possible value; the solver said 0.99 for it and was clearly wrong here (it counted exp 608 and the next draw gave 84 at the end).
3. Shoot the Moon est in `hands` counts a Queen held in debuffed state (QC debuffed was held): unclear whether it scores. Never counted on it.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the ante-1 boss is The Club/Goad/Window/Head and you own 0-1 flat scorers with a conditional trigger (Shoot the Moon, Raised Fist), THEN treat the Big shop as emergency: buy the Arcana/Buffoon pack with the money instead of leaving with $7; ante 1 boss 600 needs ~200 per hand through debuffs.
- IF a suit boss debuffs 4+ cards of your 8-card hand, THEN discard the debuffed cards FIRST on hand 1 and play non-debuffed pairs; never spend a hand on a made type whose scoring cards are debuffed (Flush of debuffed clubs = base 140, no card chips).
- IF shopadvise offers only a conditional joker (+X per Queen held, Zany eternal) at ante 1, THEN the Buffoon pack ($4, 2+ picks, any-hand jokers possible) is worth comparing even though SIM prefers the joker; SIM 0.89 for Shoot the Moon assumed Queens are held far more often than they are.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- SIM rated Shoot the Moon at P(clear next 3 blinds) 0.89 (+0.62): it overrates a held-Queen joker; est printed High Card AS 224 only because a Queen happened to be held, and the same hand shape without a Queen scored 15.
- Solver at The Club: "SOLVER PLAY Flush AC KC 6C 5C 3C est 140 round p 0.99" - a 5-debuffed-card flush for base score only; the 0.99 treated the remaining later hand as certain to clear 220 more with 2 discards, which it did not (actual 84).
- advise "p 0.69/0.67" for the final-hand draw was a roughly 1-in-3 failure; accepted, it failed.
