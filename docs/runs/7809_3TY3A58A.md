## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING (no multiplier). Two jokers: Scary Face (chips only) and Lusty (+3 mult per heart). Every non-heart hand scored at base mult 2-4, so the only hand that reached ante-2 numbers was a heart flush; under The Water (no discards) a single flush (1463) plus 2 scraps could not make 2000.

Decisions I would change:
- Ante 2 first shop ($7): buy Mr. Bones{PERISHABLE} $5. I was below boss/3 of the next ante (EMERGENCY) and facing The Water; 1688 is far above 25% of 2000, so Bones would have saved this exact loss and left $2.
- Skipping the ante-2 Small for Investment: it cost a shop visit (the shop after the Small is where a +mult joker could have been bought) for money that only pays after the boss I then lost. The advisor said play it; with 1 real mult source it was right.
- Ante 2 Big shop: the reroll ($14 -> $9) was fine, but with The Water ahead I should have counted Walkie Talkie{ETERNAL} (+4 mult on 10/4) or an Arcana pack as worse than holding money only if I had a plan for the boss; I had none.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF your only mult source is a suit joker (Lusty/Greedy/Wrathful/Gluttonous) and the upcoming boss is The Water, THEN buy any +mult joker or Mr. Bones in the shops before it: without discards you cannot assemble the suit flush more than once.
- IF Mr. Bones is in the shop at ante 1-2, you are in EMERGENCY (best hand < boss/3 of the next ante) and you own no other safety net, THEN buy it even perishable: 5 rounds covers the rest of the ante, which is where EMERGENCY runs die.
- IF you own fewer than 2 MULT jokers (chips jokers like Scary/Sly do not count), THEN do not skip the ante-2 Small Blind for a tag that pays after the boss (Investment): the shop visit is worth more than money you only receive if you survive.
- IF The Water and your best repeatable hand x 3 is far below the need, THEN dig with full 5-card plays toward the one high-mult hand (suit flush with a suit joker) instead of banking a small Two Pair; here dumping 5 non-hearts gave the flush on the next draw.
