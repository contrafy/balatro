## Post-mortem
**Binding constraint:** economy plus scoring. By ante 3 I had 3 jokers, $3 cash, and no xMult. The ante-2 shops left me at $0 after pack buys, and the rent on Gros Michel (-$3/round, $1 cash) drove me into debt at the Fish boss. Only Pair/Straight-level scoring was ever built: Wrathful (+3 mult/Spade), Ride the Bus (+1 mult, reset by any face card), Swashbuckler (+mult = sell value, ~+5). Nothing reached xMult, so the 4800 and 6400 blinds were not reachable.

**Decisions I would change:**
- At the ante-2 Buffoon pack, I took Swashbuckler over Banner. Both are ETERNAL non-xMult, so neither adds to the Gold check. Had I taken neither, I would have kept $0 anyway. The pack was a poor use of the last $6.
- I bought Gros Michel as the rental at $5 with $1 left. It was a mult boost that perished before it paid off, and its rent ($3/round) pushed me to $-3 at the Fish. Do not buy a rental when money after the buy is under $5.
- Ride the Bus was a poor fit: any face card in the Big's dig reset it to 0 (the Big's final dig resets it). Do not build around a conditional growth joker that resets on common cards.
- At the Big 4800 I dug toward a flush with 1 discard left, instead of playing for the made Straight. The spade flush dig was 1875-2400, but the blind was 4800, so the dig did not change the outcome. Preferred line: at ante 3 with no xMult, the blind is lost regardless; bank the money instead.

**Concrete if-then rules:**
- If money is under $5 and the next blind is 3x the current hand, then do not buy any rental; save for a scorer.
- If a rental perishes and leaves you below $0 money, then the next shop is nothing; rent is paid before any buy.
- If your jokers are all chips/mult with no xMult at ante 3 (4800 next), then you will lose the Big; plan a shop buy of any xMult (Erosion/Hieroglyph-class scorers) at $6+ or reroll until one appears.
- If a joker's growth resets on any face card (Ride the Bus), then do not play face-card hands while it is scoring.
- If the solver prints UNWINNABLE, then do not spend discards chasing; play the highest-expected hand.
