## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling, caused by money. Every scorer I could afford was perishable and/or rental (Gluttonous{P,R},
Misprint{P}, Shoot the Moon{P,R}, Even Steven{P,R}); the only lasting pieces were Flash Card (+2 mult per reroll) and
Joker Stencil (x3-x4). Money never exceeded $26, so I saw ~9 shops' worth of jokers in 5 antes and never found a second
multiplier. Stencil + Flash peaked around 9-14k per hand, enough for ante 4 but ~1/3 short of ante 5 (12.5k/hand Big, 16.7k/hand Manacle).
Skipping 5 Small/Big blinds kept me alive while rent-broke (Skip tag x2 = $25, Economy +$8) but each skip cost a shop view.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 Mega Buffoon second pick: Shoot the Moon{P,R} (SIM +0.46) over permanent Zany (+0.21). It carried The Fish but
   cost $9 rent and was sold one ante later; with Stencil coming, a permanent joker matters more than a 5-round bridge.
2. Ante 3 shop: the second reroll ($6 -> $0) revealed Constellation at $6 that I could not afford. With Flash Card the reroll
   was defensible (+2 mult), but stopping at $6 after Stencil would have kept a buy for the next shop's xMult.
3. Ante 5: three rerolls ($18) bought Flash +6 mult (~+15%) and no xMult; Jumbo Arcana/ keeping $26 for interest would not
   have changed the outcome either. The run was lost at ante 3-4 by having no second multiplier.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Joker Stencil (k = empty slots + 1), THEN a +m mult joker is worth adding only if (B+m)*(k-1) > B*k, i.e.
  m > B/(k-1) where B is your pre-Stencil mult; a perishing +mult joker is nearly free to lose (Stencil gains x1 back).
  Misprint (avg +11) on B~30 at x3 was worth the same as selling it for x4.
- IF you own Flash Card, THEN each reroll is a permanent +2 mult (count it as scaling, ~+5-10% per reroll on B 20-40);
  reroll when a shop has nothing, but keep the $6-8 a revealed xMult costs.
- IF every scorer you own is perishable/rental at ante 2, THEN take the permanent pack joker over a slightly better
  stickered one: bridges expiring at ante 3-4 force you to re-buy scorers instead of multipliers.
- IF rent-broke at ante 3-4 (shop after the blind opens < $5), THEN skip the Small (and the Big if its tag pays now:
  Skip tag = $5 x skips) - it worked here twice (+$8, +$20) - but every skip is a lost joker view; it only buys time.
- IF Misprint is your main +mult on a last hand, THEN the hand needs to clear at roll ~6 (est), not at the mean: it rolled 0
  on a key hand at the ante-5 Big.
