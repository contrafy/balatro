## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING + no lasting generic scorer. The engine was two perishable bridges (Abstract, Mad: both dead by ante 3)
then conditional pieces: Onyx Agate (+7 per scored Club), Sly rental (+50 chips), Shoot the Moon (Queens held), Loyalty Card (x4 every
6th hand). Base mult on a non-face hand stayed 2-4 with nothing adding to it. The Plant took away the only non-trivial +mult (Moon), and
the run had 4 jokers at ante 4, not 6. Ante-4 shops never offered a generic +mult/xMult joker (Egg, Drunkard, Runner, Bloodstone eternal+rental).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 shop ($10): Onyx Agate $7 (suit-gated) over Buffoon $4 + keeping $6. A suit-conditional joker as the first permanent scorer
   gave ~+7 mult per hand all run. With 1 perishable +mult joker, the next joker should fire on every hand type.
2. Sly Joker rental at ante 2: ~$15 of rent over 5 rounds for +50 chips on hands with mult 3-10, i.e. ~+400 per hand. That $15 was the
   reroll budget I lacked at ante 3. Skip a +chips rental when you have no mult to multiply it.
3. Ante 4 Big shop ($19): once the Plant showed my face engine dies, nothing in reach could make 6000/hand. The run was already lost at
   the ante 3 shops, where the money went to a Buffoon pack (Loyalty) + Moon + Strength instead of rerolls for an unconditional joker.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF Loyalty Card is owned, THEN count its fire hand (advise prints "fires on THIS hand") and use the discards BEFORE that hand to build
  the biggest hand: 555 + 2 discards -> Quads 5 x4 = 14040, a one-shot of the 13500 Big Blind with a weak build.
- IF Shoot the Moon is owned, THEN never play your Queens while another pair exists: Pair QC QC with QH held 2320 vs both Queens played 1280.
  Strength on a held Jack makes a Queen (+13 mult every hand it stays held).
- IF the Small Blind tag is Handy Tag (pays $1 per hand played this run) at ante 3+, THEN skip: it paid $12 at ante 4 ($2 wallet).
- IF the Small Blind pays $0, your wallet is <=$3 and you own perishable or rental jokers, THEN skip it for any money tag (Economy,
  Handy, Skip): no rent tick, no perish tick, and the lost shop would have had $1-2.
- IF your +mult comes from face cards (Shoot the Moon, Smiley, Photograph...) and the ante's boss is The Plant, THEN the Small/Big shops
  must buy a non-face +mult or xMult joker; without one, a ~1500-per-hand build meets 18000.
- IF the build at ante 3 is all conditional jokers (suit-, Queen-, every-6th-hand-gated), THEN reroll for an unconditional +mult/xMult
  before buying more conditional pieces.
