## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING + a single-point engine. The whole run's multiplier was Photograph{ETERNAL} + Hanging Chad{ETERNAL,RENTAL}:
a face scoring FIRST gets Photograph three times = x8 on the base mult (11704, 12264, 12480 one-shots). It owned zero xMult
jokers, and The Plant (ante-4 boss) debuffs every face, so the best non-face hand was ~3000 against 18000. Money never
recovered: Chad's rent (-$3/round, eternal) plus $0 Small blinds left $0-7 in every shop from ante 2 on, so Director's Cut
($10 + $10 reroll) and every xMult were out of reach.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 Small: I used Justice to Glass the KS and then played it on a blind I could clear without it (12480 vs 3200);
   the 1-in-4 shatter hit on the first hand. Glass on the engine card should be played only when the hand needs it.
2. Ante 2: Hanging Chad{ETERNAL,RENTAL} was right for survival (0.12 EMERGENCY), but with it every later shop was broke.
   A face-only engine at ante 3-4 must buy a Plant/face-boss counter early or keep $20 for Director's Cut; I had neither.
3. Ante 4 Small shop: the gold AS paid nothing because it was not in hand at round end; I should have kept $ for interest.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Photograph + Hanging Chad, THEN put a face FIRST in hand order (Aces sort before Kings: never let an Ace lead
  the played cards). KQJT9 with K first = 11704/12264 at L2 vs A-high Broadway 2982 with the same cards.
- IF your only multiplier is face-gated (Photograph, Chad on faces, Smiley, Scary) AND the upcoming boss is The Plant,
  THEN this ante's money goes to Luchador / Chicot / Mr. Bones / Director's Cut before any level; with none affordable,
  skip the Big blind for a Buffoon tag (pack before the boss) rather than spend it on planets.
- IF you make a Glass card on your engine card, THEN do not play it on hands that clear without it (25% shatter each play).
- IF The House deals all cards face-down, THEN discard 5 blind and use held tarots (The Star) to complete a flush from
  the visible cards: Star on 3 off-suit cards incl. a face turned KC JH 7H into a KD-first diamond flush (8064 one-shot).
- IF a Gold card is held, THEN keep it in hand through the final play (it only pays when held at round end).
