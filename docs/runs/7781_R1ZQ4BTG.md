## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: pure luck / misplay risk in the `advise` engine's discard-vs-play choice on the final hand,
compounded by an unusually card-poor ante-1 deal (three separate discard cycles, none completed a straight or
flush). No jokers existed yet to cushion variance, which is expected at ante 1 per playbook section 7
("Big Blind 450 with no jokers needs a flush/straight/full house") but this run lost the *Small* Blind (300)
before even reaching the Big Blind — an outcome the current playbook data does not call out as common.

The one decision I would change: on the final hand (Pair K est 60, need 116, last discard available), decline
`advise`'s DISCARD-for-flush recommendation and instead just play the Pair immediately when there is no way to
reach `need` even with a made flush this ante (a completed flush would only have scored ~130-150 at L1 with no
jokers per section 7 numbers, still short of 116 combined with zero cards left to fill the hand afterward) —
actually the flush chase was rational in isolation (flush EV > pair's 60), but the deeper issue is that after two
consecutive missed straight draws in the same round, the hand was already unrecoverable: `need` (116) exceeded
what almost any single 5-card hand without jokers could deliver except a completed flush/straight, and both were
long shots from a 4-card draw. In hindsight, playing the Pair K on hand 2 instead of chasing had already been the
correct call (scored 184, needed only 116 more) -- the actual misplay was earlier: hand 1's discard chased a
straight from AS QD JC TC (need K or 9, drawing 4 cards) when the safer Two Pair AJ path was not yet visible;
that part worked out by luck. No single override is clearly better in expectation than what `advise` recommended;
this reads as an unlucky ante-1 deal rather than a process error.

Bridge issues: none. All commands (`start`, `blind`, `advise`, `hands`, `disc`, `play`, `s`, `raw`) worked as
documented; every `play`/`disc` printed the expected state transition; est matched actual score exactly on all
three played hands (124/124, 60/60, 60/60) - 0% deviation, well within the 20% threshold.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Section 4 could add: when `need` at the last hand exceeds what a completed flush/straight would realistically
   score at L1 with zero jokers (~130-280 per section 7), note that the round is already very likely lost and
   flag it explicitly rather than only printing "X short" - so the agent can decide whether to preserve cards for
   the log/analysis vs. it not really changing outcome either way (advise already does the correct EV math here;
   this is a documentation/expectation-setting suggestion, not a functional change).
2. Consider logging a section 7 stat for "ante-1 Small Blind losses with zero jokers" separately from Big Blind
   losses, since this run shows it is possible to lose before ever reaching a shop - worth tracking frequency
   across runs to see if the deal-luck variance at ante 1 is being underestimated in the current strategy notes.
