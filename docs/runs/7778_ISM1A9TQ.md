# Run ISM1A9TQ (port 7778) - LOSS at ante 1 Big Blind

## Ante 1

**Small Blind (300):** Won with Flush C (AC QC JC 8C 5C) = 316
- Starting hand: AC AD TD 9C 7S 4S 4D 3C
- Discarded 3x, built toward flush
- Scored 316 > 300 needed
- Money after: $9

**Shop:** Bought Space Joker for $5, Buffoon Pack for $4, got Saturn consumable
- Ending money: $1
- Jokers: Space Joker (1/4 level up played hand)

**Big Blind (450):** LOST with score 285
- Round 1: Two Pair (AC AD KS KH 9C) = 210 (est 122)
- Round 2: Pair Q (QS QH JC TC 5D + fillers) = 80 (est 60)
- Round 3: High Card (TC) = 15 (est 15)
- Total: 210 + 80 + 15 = 305 scored, needed 450
- **Result: RUN ENDED**

## Post-mortem

**Binding constraint:** Poor hand sequencing and risky discard strategy in Big Blind.

**What went wrong:**
1. After winning Small Blind, I had full house (372) available but spent all 3 discards trying to optimize
2. Third discard was catastrophic - lost the 4-5 clubs I'd built and got worse hand (two pair 96)
3. Should have played the full house (372) early and hoped to beat 450 with remaining hands
4. Wasted 2 discards on marginal gains, lost the good hand on the 3rd attempt

**Decision to change:** After the first discard gave full house (372), should have evaluated more carefully whether another discard was worth the risk. The 372 → 320 (flush) → 100 (two pair) sequence showed diminishing returns. Playing the 372 full house would have given 210 + 120 + X from remaining two hands, which was at least more realistic than the path I took.

**Bridge issues:** None - all commands executed correctly.

**Playbook suggestions:**
1. Clarify guidance on discard risk in ante 1 when you already have a decent hand but miss target by ~80
2. Mention that Space Joker's "level up" doesn't help current hand - it's a cumulative bonus, don't expect instant scaling
3. Emphasize more strongly that with limited discards, securing one viable hand is better than chasing optimization
