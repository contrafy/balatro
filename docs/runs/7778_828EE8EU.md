Post-mortem:
- Binding constraint: SCALING, not misplay or bad luck alone. By ante 3 boss (need 4000) our best possible single hand was Four of a Kind at ~714 (with Full House L2 and Two Pair L2 flat levels only). Per playbook 7a, "a L5 main hand with +30 mult of flat jokers beats a L1 hand with one x2" -- but at L2 levels and only 4 jokers (Castle: growing flat chips; Shoot the Moon: conditional/inconsistent mult per Queens; Gift Card: pure economy; Square Joker: growing flat chips gated on exactly-4-card hands we rarely played) there was no reliable xMult and hand levels were still too low. The Wheel's two face-down draws hurt but even a clean hand would not have reached 4000.
- Decision I would change: skipping the ante-3 Big Blind (3000) for the Polychrome tag cost us ~3000 in scoring reps/cash and, crucially, a full shop visit's worth of planet/joker buys right before a boss that needed 4000. In hindsight, given our hand levels were only L2 and we had zero xMult, that shop and blind income were needed more than the tag. I would play the Big Blind instead of skipping it at this stake of scaling.
- Also: Square Joker was a mediocre pick since our filler-draw strategy (playbook rule 5, fill hands to 5 cards) works against its "exactly 4 cards" condition -- it barely grew. Should have picked a joker whose trigger matches our actual play pattern.
- Bridge issues: `buy` action twice returned `ACTION FAILED: buy_from_shop returned false: no space or card not purchasable None` when both consumable (tarot) slots were full -- had to `use`/`sellc` an existing tarot first, then retry the buy. No true errors otherwise; all `ACTION FAILED` cases were consumable-slot-full, not transient failures.
- Calibration (est vs actual differing >20%):
  - Ante 1 boss: PAIR A play est 1435, actual 980 (-31.7%).
  - Ante 2 boss: PAIR 5 play est 840, actual 450 (-46.4%); next hand PAIR T est 1260, actual 675 (-46.4%).
  These all involved Shoot the Moon (+13 mult per Queen "held"): `advise` picked plays that left Queens unplayed-but-in-hand expecting the bonus to apply, but the realized scores came in ~32-46% below estimate every time this joker's held-queen logic was the deciding factor. This looks like a real bug in how `advise`/`hands` estimates Shoot the Moon's bonus for cards that are held-but-not-scored versus the game's actual runtime behavior.
- Playbook suggestions:
  1. Investigate/fix the `est` calculation for "held" (not scored) conditional jokers like Shoot the Moon -- it consistently overestimates by ~30-46% in this run whenever the advised play relies on keeping trigger cards unplayed-in-hand.
  2. Add an explicit money/scaling check before recommending a Big/Small Blind skip for a tag: only skip if current hand levels and joker portfolio can already clear the ante's boss requirement comfortably, since skipping trades a guaranteed ~2x-blind's worth of income/reps for a tag whose value is speculative.
  3. Warn when picking a joker whose trigger condition (e.g. "exactly 4 cards played") conflicts with the standing filler-to-5-cards advice in section 4 rule 5 -- Square Joker and similar conditional-count jokers are close to dead weight under that strategy.
