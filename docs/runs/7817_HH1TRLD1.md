## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: luck on a jokerless ante-1 Small (two draws at p 0.78 / 0.67 missed: no K/Q/2 on 4 cards, no club
in 4 cards from 26), compounded by one off-solver decision.

Decisions I would change:
1. Hand 1, the first decision: ADVICE kept the rule pick (disc 1 4 5 6, p 0.91) over the solver's PLAY Pair QQ + 3
   fillers (p 0.96). With no jokers on a 300 Small, banking 60 and drawing 5 fresh cards while keeping 22+AS was the
   calibrated better line; the +0.10 "do not break a made Two Pair" bar is meant for draws, not for playing half of it.
2. Hand 2 (after the missed FH draw): I played Two Pair QQ22 + 1 filler (88) from the checklist rule, a line the solver
   did not list; its best was a 3-card flush keep at p 0.91 and the ADVICE was disc 1 4 5 6 at p 0.81. After my play the
   solver said 0.78. When the solver has printed calibrated p for the lines, do not substitute a heuristic line it did
   not evaluate.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you are jokerless at an ante-1 blind AND the solver prints calibrated p values, THEN pick from the solver's printed
  lines (best p, or within 0.03 the one banking more chips); never play an unlisted line from a checklist heuristic.
- IF the solver's best line is PLAY one pair of a made Two Pair with fillers (keeping the other pair in hand) and it
  leads the "keep Two Pair and discard" rule pick by 0.05+, THEN play it: it banks chips and still holds a pair.
- IF the ante-1 Small Blind deal is weak (best hand < 100) with no jokers, THEN expect p < 0.95 for the round; a 300
  Small is not a free blind on Black Deck (3 hands, 2 discards).

Advisor/CLI defects:
- advise (ante 1 Small, first deal): the "breaking your made Two Pair needs +0.10" bar overrode a solver PLAY line
  (Pair QQ + fillers, keeps 22 in hand, p 0.96 vs 0.91). That bar should apply to discards that break the pair, not to
  a PLAY of one pair that banks chips and keeps the other.
- PLAYBOOK 2b "Jokerless ante 1: ... after one missed draw bank the made hand and keep a discard" conflicts with the
  solver (it cost ~0.13 p here); 2b should say "follow the solver's best p".
Bridge issues: none (6 steps, no rejects).
