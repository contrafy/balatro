## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: BOSS x BURGLAR (proximate), MONEY (underlying). With Burglar the round has 0 discards, so under
The Mouth the first dealt 8 cards decide the only legal hand type. This deal had no pair, so the round was locked to
High Card before any choice existed. Selling Burglar before selecting the blind gives back 3 hands + 2 discards, so the
type can be dug for before the first play. Even then 6400 was unlikely: the build (Acrobat, Photograph, Sly, Wrathful,
all hands L1) made ~300-1300 per non-final hand. The real end state was -$20 at ante 3 with three $3/round rentals and
no buying power, so the ante-3 shops (Death $3, Jumbo Celestial) were out of reach.

Decisions I would change:
1. Before The Mouth (and any boss that punishes an unlucky deal: The Mouth, The Psychic, The Eye with few types),
   sell Burglar at blind select. Its 6 hands are worth less than 2 discards when the first play locks the type.
2. Ante 2 shop: Credit Card + reroll into debt + Burglar rental + Ice Cream rental. It cleared The Flint, but by ante 3
   rent was $9/round and every shop after that was spent from -$10..-$20. The $5 reroll into debt found only Burglar.
   I would have bought Ice Cream only and kept the $3 for an ante-3 planet/Death.
3. Ante 1: Buffoon pack -> perishable Odd Todd was right (only scorer). Fine.

Things that worked (Acrobat x3 final hand):
- Holding a made Full House for the final hand and spending the other hands as 2-4 card junk plays to dig won
  ante 2 Big (2040 final), ante 3 Small (4776 final) and ante 3 Big (3792 final).
- Burglar + Acrobat: 6 hands with no discards works like 5 dig plays + one x3 final. Junk plays of 4 cards draw 4.

## Reusable rules (if-then)
- IF you own Burglar (0 discards) AND the boss is The Mouth THEN sell Burglar before selecting the blind: the first play
  locks the hand type, and with no discards a no-pair deal locks you to High Card (this run: 212/6400).
- IF you own Acrobat THEN, before every play, compare (hold made hand for final x3 + junk plays) against (bank it now
  + dig for another final). Hold only if 3x the hand covers the remaining need minus ~50 per junk play; otherwise bank
  it (ante 3 Big: holding 999QQ projected 4170 vs 4529 needed; banking it and digging AAKJ found JJJAA x3 = 3792).
- IF Acrobat's final needs a face FH THEN keep the pair/trips plus a spare face, and play 4 junk cards per hand to draw 4.
- IF Credit Card is your only way to buy THEN do not take rentals with it: 3 rentals = -$9/round and the -$20 floor is
  hit within one ante, after which nothing can be bought (Death $3 sat in the ante-3 Big shop unaffordable).
- IF a +chips joker (Sly, Ice Cream, Odd Todd) sits right of an xMult joker THEN do not reorder it: only +mult needs to
  be left of xMult. Per-card jokers (Wrathful, Photograph) interact by position with each other, not with Acrobat.
