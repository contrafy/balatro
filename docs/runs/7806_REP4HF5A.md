## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING with no money. No xMult was offered in 6 shop views; the only +mult scorer (Gros Michel) died
to its 1-in-6 at the ante-2 boss round end, leaving ante 3 with zero scorers and $13. Every dollar after that went to
an emergency holo Raised Fist ($8) and the rent of a perishable rental Scary Face. The run lost the ante-3 boss by 1.9%.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 last shop before The Fish ($11 after Temperance): I spent $5 on a reroll (nothing) and $4 on a Celestial pack
   (Earth, unused). Red Card $5 + buying a $4 pack and SKIPPING it would have been +3 mult on every Fish hand right away
   (mult ~28-30 -> ~31-33, ~+10%: 3300/784/2196 -> ~+600 total, a clear instead of -120). The advisor rated Red Card
   "adds ~0 now"; it does not model that you can feed it in the same shop.
2. Ante 2 first shop: passed Wrathful Joker{ETERNAL} $5 (+3 mult per scoring spade, SIM +0.13). It is a permanent +mult
   source that would have survived Gros Michel's death; with Gold rent pressure I judged $1 left too thin. In hindsight a
   permanent mult joker at ante 2 with only Gros Michel (1/6 death per round) as mult was worth the eternal slot.
3. Ante 2 shops 2-3: I kept $5-8 and left twice while holding Gros Michel as the ONLY mult source. Gros Michel must be
   treated as gone next round (2b gotcha): those shops should have looked for its replacement (Celestial/ reroll at $8).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Red Card AND the shop sells any pack you can afford after it, THEN buy the pack and skip it: +3 mult now
  (Red Card + $4 pack = $9 for a permanent +3 mult before the boss).
- IF Gros Michel is your only +mult joker, THEN every shop is "no scorer alive next round" for planning: buy the next
  permanent +mult joker even if eternal/conditional (Wrathful, Lusty...), do not hold the floor.
- IF The Fish has flipped your hand after the first play, THEN discards still draw FACE UP (verified: 8D 4D 3C 2S came
  up visible): discard the low face-downs and keep the high-bounded ones.
- IF a face-down card under The Fish is bounded to a single rank by the rank-sorted hand (e.g. "1 in A..A" next to a
  visible AD), THEN treat it as known: play it with its visible partner as a guaranteed pair.
- IF you own Raised Fist, THEN play your lowest cards as fillers and keep high cards held: Pair KK went 1440 -> 2340 ->
  2700 just from which cards were held (est models it correctly).
