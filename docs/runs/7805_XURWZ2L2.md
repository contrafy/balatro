## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no scoring joker at the ante-1 Big Blind. A jokerless deck makes ~60-110 per pair-family hand; 450 in 3 hands
needs a Full House/Straight/Flush hit, which is a ~35% coin flip per discard.

Decisions I would change:
1. Shop 1 ($6): buy Gluttonous Joker $5 (+3 mult per scored Club) instead of the $4 Buffoon pack. Replaying the actual Big Blind hands
   with it: Two Pair T6 104 (no clubs), Pair QQ with QC 30x5=150, Two Pair JJ77 with JC 7C 54x8=432 -> 686/450, a clear win. A
   guaranteed suit-gated +mult joker in hand beats a Gold-stake Buffoon pack, where ~half the jokers carry Rental/Perishable/Eternal
   and the pick can be (as here) two rentals worth skipping. The pack only wins when the shop has no +mult joker at all.
2. The Big Blind line (two discards for FH on TT66) was reasonable given zero jokers; alternative lines (Two Pair 104 x3 = 312) could
   not reach 450 either. The loss was decided in the shop.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF ante 1, zero jokers, and the shop offers a +mult joker you can afford (even suit-gated: Gluttonous/Greedy/Lusty/Wrathful +3 per
  suit card) THEN buy it before any Buffoon pack: at Gold the pack can contain only rental/perishable junk, and $2 left buys nothing.
- IF you hold QJT9 (open-ended) vs AQJT (gutshot) THEN keep the open-ended four: 8 outs vs 4, the Ace's +1 chip is irrelevant.
- IF you reach the ante-1 Big Blind with zero jokers THEN expect to lose ~2/3 of the time: every shop decision before it must
  convert money into a scoring joker with certainty, not a gamble.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- `shopadvise` ante 1, $6, 0 jokers: ranked Buffoon Pack 78 over Gluttonous Joker 26 ("PLUS(one suit)"). With zero scorers and only
  $6, a certain +3/club joker should outrank a sticker-lottery pack (the pack then offered Marble{RENTAL} and Droll{PERISHABLE,RENTAL};
  pack `advise` itself said SKIP). Suggest: with 0 scorers at ante 1, a suit-gated +mult joker scores >= the Buffoon pack.
- `advise` Small Blind discard: kept AQJT gutshot (4 outs, "EV 107") over QJT9 open-ended (8 outs); straight-draw selection should
  count outs (open-ended > gutshot).
- Bridge: no failures; est exact on all 4 scored hands (324, 104, 60, 108).
