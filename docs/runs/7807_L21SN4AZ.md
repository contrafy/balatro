## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: MONEY -> SCALING. The Trio{ETERNAL,RENTAL} picked at ante 1 with $1 left charged $3 every round, so every shop
after it opened with $0-4: I never bought a single planet (all hands L1 at the ante-3 boss), Constellation was never fed, and Midas
Mask never got to pay. The build was x3 only on Trips/Full House; with L1 hands that is ~2000 (Trips) / ~3000-3500 (FH) per hand
vs 2133 per hand needed at the ante-3 boss, so it hinged on making two Full Houses in one round. It made one, then the 51% dig missed.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 Buffoon ($4 -> $0) taking Constellation: a growth xMult with $0 and $3/round rent can never be fed; it was sold at x1.0.
   With no money, a pack that cannot score now is worse than keeping $4 (it would have bought Venus $3 = Trips L2 next shop).
2. Ante 3 Big skip: it removed one ~35% blind but also the only shop of the ante (~$6-7 = Venus) and left the boss at L1 levels.
   With a rental draining money, skipping every Small AND Big means income is only the boss reward minus rent: zero scaling.
   Skipping the Small (pays $0, costs $3 rent) was right; skipping the Big at ante 3 was the error in hindsight.
3. Ante 1 pick: The Trio eternal+rental was the only scorer on offer and it did carry ante 1 Big (Trips 513) and the ante-2 House (FH
   3312). I would take it again, but then play every Big blind for income and spend only on Trips levels (Venus).

What worked:
- Holding two pairs and playing junk/low pairs as dig hands (no discards left) to hunt a Full House: hit at The House (FH 3312 from 15+220
  dig plays), 51% line missed at The Tooth.
- The House: discard all face-downs first; the redraws come face-up.
- Selling an unfed growth joker (Constellation x1.0) mid-round to feed Campfire (+x0.25) and get $3 under The Tooth.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the only joker in an ante-1 pack is ETERNAL+RENTAL, THEN take it only if it is an xMult, and from then on play every Big blind
  (its $4+ pays the rent) and spend shops only on that joker's hand planet; never buy a growth joker you cannot feed.
- IF you own a rental and the Small Blind pays $0 at stake 2+, THEN skipping the Small is free money (+$3 rent saved); but IF the next
  shop would be your only one before the boss, THEN play the Big for its reward even at ~35% risk only when your hand levels are
  below the ante number; otherwise skip.
- IF your engine is The Trio (x3 on Trips/FH) with no discards left and two pairs in hand, THEN play the 3-4 junk cards as a High Card to
  draw while holding both pairs (Full House outs = 4-5); a Two Pair play scores ~1/5 of a Full House.
- IF Campfire is owned and a joker is x1.0/dead (unfed Constellation, perished joker), THEN sell it before the big hand of a must-clear
  round, not after.
