## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: LUCK (variance of a jokerless ante-1 Small Blind on Black Deck Gold: 3 hands, 2 discards, $0 payout).
The opening deal had no pair; the only route to 300 was a flush/straight. I reached a 4-card club flush draw with 3 hands
and 0 discards and missed it twice (P(hit) 0.55 then 0.60: a 1-in-5.5 double miss, 6 live clubs in 35 then 31 cards).
By my own arithmetic the dig line was ~0.82 to clear vs ~0.65-0.70 for banking Two Pair T7 first, so the decision was
right by EV and lost its 18% branch. This is now the 5th Gold run (of 126) to die at the ante-1 Small Blind and the 16th
to die in ante 1 (12%); every one was jokerless, which is the structural cost of Black Deck (3 hands) at Gold (2 discards).

Decisions I would change:
1. Deal 2 (AS JS TH 9D 7H 7D 6S 5H, 1 discard left): I broke the made Pair 7 for a 3-card heart draw because the solver
   printed p0.98 for it vs 0.96 for keeping the pair + straight draw. The 0.02 margin is noise and both numbers are
   fantasy for a jokerless deck; a line that keeps the only made pair and still draws cards is the robust one. With the
   actual deck order (next cards JC TC 7C 6C 5D, then AH KS 8H 2H, KD JH 8D 6H) the keep-pair line would have produced
   Two Pair JT 120 + Trips 777 153 + Pair K 60 = 333: a win. That is hindsight on the card order, but the principle
   (never discard a made pair at ante 1 for a 3-card flush draw) does not depend on it.
2. Hand 1 (three pairs TT 77 55 + 4 clubs): the dig was correct by the numbers (0.82 vs ~0.68), and I did it the right
   way (played the pair that was not part of the draw for 40 instead of a High Card for 16). No change.
3. Blind select: skipping for the Juggle Tag (+3 hand size at the 450 Big) is not supported by the data (jokerless
   skips to the Big lost 284/404/436 of 450). No change.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF ante 1 jokerless and the deal has no pair, THEN three pair-family hands cannot reach 300 (Two Pair ~105, Pair
  ~50): discard toward the best 4-card straight/flush draw as `advise` says, but keep any made pair you are dealt later.
- IF ante 1 jokerless with 1 discard left and the hand holds a made pair plus only a 3-card flush draw, THEN keep the
  pair (and any straight/flush cards around it) and discard the rest; never throw the pair for a 3-card suit draw
  (needs 2 of 5 draws, p~0.37, and the 4-card dig after it is still only ~0.55 per hand).
- IF you hold a 4-card flush draw, a spare pair and 0 discards, THEN dig by PLAYING the spare pair (+ the other
  non-flush cards) instead of a High Card: same draw, 40-50 points instead of 15, and the flush then needs less.
- IF digging for a flush, THEN check the minimum completion clears the remainder: (35 + held chips + 2) x 4 at L1
  (here (35+33+2)x4 = 280 vs 260 needed: OK; a 2-9 club flush is 250-270, not 300).
- IF the solver prints p >= 0.95 for every option on a jokerless ante-1 deal, THEN its ranking carries no information;
  break ties toward the line that keeps made pairs (QT4Q8D21 and this run both died following 0.97-0.98 flush digs).
