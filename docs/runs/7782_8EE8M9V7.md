Post-mortem: binding constraint = The Eye boss combined with a Pair/Full-House/Trips-only engine. The Eye forces 3 distinct poker-hand TYPES per round; our whole build (6 jokers, 3 planet-leveled hands) only pays off on Pair/Two Pair/Three of a Kind/Full House - once those are burned on hand 1 and 2, hand 3 is forced into whatever weak leftover type remains (here, an unleveled Straight at L1 with no straight-supporting jokers), and the run had zero xMult beyond one Photograph (x2, conditional on a face card) to compensate for the L1 level. Zero jokers or held consumables could add flat mult/chips to a Straight. The single decision I would change: at the ante 5 shop, spend money leveling a SECOND, structurally different hand type (Straight/Flush) as an Eye/Eye-like insurance policy once ante 5's boss was known, or buy Luchador if it had appeared, rather than pouring every planet into the Pair/Trips/Full-House family exclusively.
