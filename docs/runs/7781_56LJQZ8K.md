# Run 56LJQZ8K (port 7781) - stake 3 (Green Stake) - LOSS at ante 1 boss (The Pillar)

Ante 1: skip Small (ADVICE: b skip, Voucher Tag, no jokers yet).
  Big Blind (need 450): Flush D est 296/actual 296, Two Pair 32 est 60/actual 60, Flush C est 296/actual 296
  = scored 652/450, cleared with 1 discard unused (+$1). Cashed out at $8.
  Shop: bought Buffoon Pack ($4, shopadvise ADVICE), picked Abstract Joker (+3 mult per joker owned) over
  Turtle Bean (no scoring value, decaying hand size) - our only joker. Endshop at $4 (shopadvise: LEAVE, nothing
  affordable/worth it).
  Boss The Pillar (need 600): every diamond/club card played during the Big Blind (from the two flushes and the
  two-pair) came back DEBUFFED when redrawn during the boss round - this bled through all three hands (Two Pair
  260/260, Two Pair 135/135 with 2 debuffed cards scoring 0, then forced to discard away a Two Pair J7 (debuffed
  J and 7) on the last discard hoping to draw a pair). Final forced hand (0 discards, 0 hands after) was 4 hearts
  with no pair and no straight (8H 7H 5H 2H + off-suit T/Q/J/4) - HIGH CARD JS, est 60, actual 20 (CLI printed
  "est 60 vs actual 20: logged for calibration" - a 3x overestimate, flag for calibration).
  LOSS: 415/600 (185 short). No consumables held (0/2 used all game) - never had a rescue tarot available since
  no shop before the boss offered a card-row tarot at this stake/ante.

Post-mortem: binding constraint was a combination of (a) The Pillar's card-identity debuff punishing the exact
  diamond/club ranks used to clear the Big Blind, which happened to be the SAME ranks (5s, T-J-Q-K faces) the
  redrawn hand kept reoffering, and (b) having only ONE joker (Abstract Joker, a flat-mult joker with no floor
  Pair trigger like Jolly/Sly) so every hand's chip total depended entirely on undebuffed card face value, and
  (c) zero held consumables for a last-hand rescue (Strength/Death were never available to buy - ante 1 shop's
  card row was empty this run).
  Decision I would change: at the ante-1 shop, with $4 left after the Buffoon pack, I still had $4 available
  before endshop but shopadvise said LEAVE (no scorer-relevant item at that price). In hindsight, given The
  Pillar was already flagged as this ante's boss during blind select, the discard sequencing during the boss
  round should have prioritized keeping NON-diamond/club pair candidates alive longer rather than following
  strict est-maximization discards that happened to strip the only intact (undebuffed) pair pairs one hand too
  early - by the final hand, both remaining pair ranks (J and 7) were half-debuffed already. A more conservative
  discard on the second-to-last hand (keep the debuffed-but-type-valid Two Pair J7 rather than discarding it
  away hunting a flush that the deck could not produce with only 4 hearts) would have guaranteed at least ~90-100
  points instead of gambling into a 20-point High Card - though both outcomes were short of 205 needed, so the
  loss was likely unavoidable once two hands landed on heavily-debuffed cards. This was a single unlucky boss
  draw compounding with a one-joker economy at ante 1; not a systematic misplay.

Bridge issues: `play HIGH 2 7 1 3` printed "est 60 vs actual 20: logged for calibration" - the HIGH CARD estimator
  overestimated actual score by 3x (est used base 5x1 + presumably filler/face-value assumptions that did not
  match; actual scoring card was just JS by itself, worth roughly 5 + 10(J) = 15 x1 mult = ~15-20, matching
  actual). No other CLI errors encountered this run; every `disc`/`play`/`pick`/`pack`/`shopadvise`/`advise` call
  worked as documented.

Playbook suggestions:
  1. The HIGH CARD `est` formula appears to overestimate significantly (60 vs actual 20, a 3x miss) - worth
     checking the estimator's chip math for HIGH CARD hands specifically, since it is exactly the hand type
     played when all else has failed and its accuracy matters most for judging "is this truly hopeless."
  2. Under The Pillar, `advise`'s discard recommendations during the round correctly flagged debuffed cards but
     did not appear to weigh "will discarding this pair-forming card strip my LAST viable pair before the final
     forced hand" against "chase a flush the deck cannot support" - worth adding an explicit check (playbook 4.8
     already covers held consumables, but there is no held-consumable case here; the gap is in discard sequencing
     itself when zero consumables exist).
  3. Ante 1 with The Pillar as boss and only one flat-mult joker is a fragile combination worth flagging in
     shopadvise's boss-preview line specifically (it already prints "Upcoming boss this ante" in the shop, but
     the ante-1 SELECTING_HAND phase for the boss round itself did not surface a similar reminder to preserve
     un-debuffed pair ranks specifically).
