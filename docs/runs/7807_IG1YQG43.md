## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING. The whole engine was Blackboard x3 (needs every held card black) + flat +mult from bridge jokers that decayed or perished (Gros Michel destroyed, Mystic Summit perished, Popcorn decayed to 0, Raised Fist perished). A max Two Pair hand was ~11-15k, so ante 5 Big (37500 = 3 hands of 12.5k) needed three perfect hands. Money was $0-8 all run (two rentals at -$3/round each plus packs), so I could never buy a second xMult or reroll for one.
Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1: taking rental Gros Michel with $2 left meant -$3/round forever; I should have sold it right after it stopped being my only scorer (it was destroyed anyway). More importantly I spent every shop to $0-$2 on packs (Hierophant, Moon, High Priestess, Celestials) instead of saving for jokers; xMult/mult jokers never appeared affordable.
2. Ante 4: I skipped the Small blind (9000) to preserve Mystic for the Big. It worked, but the shop I skipped would have given a chance at a permanent scorer; the Goad boss then cleared by exactly 0 chips. I should have kept a few $ for a rescue.
3. Ante 5: arrived with only 3 jokers, 1 real xMult, Two Pair L5, $2. Nothing to fix at that point; the miss was ante 3-4 shops spending on one-shot planets (Uranus x4) instead of a second xMult/Glass (Justice never showed).
Reusable rules:
- If Blackboard is your xMult: play 5 cards (put RED or debuffed cards among the played ones as fillers) and hold only spades/clubs; discard red cards first; est models it only when held cards are black (check `sel` with the exact filler).
- If the boss debuffs a suit that Blackboard needs (Goad = spades, Club = clubs), debuffed held cards count as NOT black: only the other black suit counts. Plan to hold only that suit or expect no x3.
- If Mystic Summit is owned: spend BOTH discards before the scoring hand (+15 mult at 0 discards); `advise` says so.
- If a perishable/decaying joker is the only thing clearing a blind, skip the preceding Small (it pays $0 at Gold) so the bridge is not spent on a blind you can skip; it saved Mystic for the 13500 Big.
- Orbital Tag with a hand type you never play still levels it x3 (Quads L1->L4 = 150x16): note it, but do not count on it.
- Perishable/Rental joker gets destroyed/decays: never plan an ante's scoring on Gros Michel (1/6 per round) or Popcorn.
- A Pair is useless at ante 4+ (Pair L1 = ~1k with +mult jokers); only Two Pair/Trips/FH with levels carry. Level Pair too, or keep every hand a Two Pair.
Advisor/CLI defects:
- `shopadvise` repeatedly said SELL Gros Michel (AVOID, rental) when it was my only scorer and the only reason I cleared ante 1 (Flush est 1444 vs 600). It should not recommend selling the sole scorer when no replacement is affordable.
- Pack advise ranked Misprint(PERISHABLE+RENTAL) 70 over Gros Michel(RENTAL) 24; Gros Michel's fixed +15 and no perish was better for the next 5 rounds (it did die at round 5, so both were bridges).
- `hands` est does not model Blackboard unless held cards happen to be all black; it never suggests which filler to play to make held cards black. `sel` with explicit fillers shows the real est (matched exactly every time: 10998, 15180, 11340, 14940, 10920, 11424).
- `advise` suggested discard patterns that break Blackboard (keep red cards) and chased Pair A when two pair was attainable.
- Raised Fist est correctly uses lowest held card; `advise` doesn't use it to choose which cards to hold (hold 10+ ranks for +20 mult).
Bridge issues: none (one NOT LEGAL on buy slot 2 after the shop shifted, nothing sent).
