## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no scoring joker for the ante-1 Big Blind. The only shop ($5 after a $0 Small) had no joker except a
Drunkard rental and the one Buffoon pack offered Trading Card / Delayed Gratification (eternal rental), so the Big Blind
was played on L1 base hands. 450 with 3 hands and no joker needs a flush/straight/FH plus another ~150 hand; I got a FH
(312) but the last hand was a lone pair.

Decisions I would change:
- Big Blind first discard: I spent one of only two discards on a lone 2D to trigger Trading Card (+$3, 8 outs drawing
  one card). With no scorer, a 4-5 card dig (QJT8 kept, 4 draws for a 9, ~33%, plus more pair chances) twice would have
  been better than $3 that can only be spent after the blind I needed to win.
- Small Blind skip for Charm Tag (Mega Arcana) was correctly declined; the Small paid $0 but the $5 shop at least gave
  a pack. Nothing else in the shop was usable.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF you own Trading Card and no scoring joker on a Big/Boss blind THEN never spend a discard as a 1-card discard for
  the $3; use every discard as a full dig toward a straight/flush/FH. Money is worthless if the blind is lost.
- IF ante-1 Big Blind (450) with zero jokers THEN plan two hands: one 5-card hand (flush/straight/FH ~300) AND a
  ~150 hand (trips/two pair high). With 0 discards and no made hand, play all off-suit/unconnected cards as one High
  Card to cycle 4-5 cards (here it turned a dead hand into FH 88877).
- IF the ante-1 Small Blind can be one-shot by a 4-to-a-flush dig (A K Q x hearts) THEN discard the non-suited cards
  even while holding two pair: two pair L1 (~124) x3 does not reach 300.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- `advise` on the jokerless Big Blind said "keep the 3 highest cards" (QD JC TH) and discard 5, ignoring the open
  straight draw QJT-8 / 8-7-6-4 with 8 outs; with no joker the advisor should value 4-card straight/flush draws over
  high cards, since high-card ranks barely matter at L1.
- `advise` on the Small Blind recommended playing AAKK two pair (est 124, pacing) with 2 discards left while 124 x 3 < 300;
  PACE did not trigger because the best hand was not compared against 3-hand total correctly (124*3=372 > 300 on paper,
  but repeating two pair three times is unrealistic). The 4-heart flush draw (A K Q 5 hearts, flush est 296) was better.
Bridge issues: none.
