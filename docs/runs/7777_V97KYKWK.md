Post-mortem:
- Binding constraint: boss mechanic (The Eye, no-repeat hand types) combined with an under-diversified joker/level portfolio. Full House (L3) was never actually playable during the boss round because no trips were drawn across all 3 hands' worth of discards -- discards were spent entirely on maximizing the Flush instead of leaving room to set up a Full House or Straight as the 3rd distinct type.
- Decision I would change: on entering The Eye, plan discards around GUARANTEEING three distinct playable types (even weak ones) rather than optimizing the first hand's score in isolation. Once the Flush was banked at ~4700, the 2nd and 3rd hands had no discards left to hunt for anything beyond whatever was dealt -- the Two Pair (11424 cumulative) then Trips (13404 cumulative) were pure luck-of-the-draw with zero agency. A better line: use the very first discard(s) explicitly to test for straight/full-house potential (we had Q/J/T/9/7 of diamonds, one card off a straight flush) before committing all discards to the flush; or discard less aggressively on hand 1 (play the flush with just 5 cards, no over-optimizing) to preserve at least 1 discard for hand 2 or 3.
- Secondary constraint: never acquired a single xMult joker in 5 antes (0/6 slots ever xMult) despite playbook data saying it's rare (1 in 46 logged shops) and the strategy is designed around that. Scaling was entirely hand-level (Two Pair reached L6, Full House L3, Flush L2) plus flat mult (Jolly Joker, Droll Joker, Abstract Joker, Mystic Summit) plus one conditional xMult (Flower Pot, x3 if all 4 suits -- fired reliably in practice, arguably our de facto xMult carry). In hindsight Flower Pot performed above its "unreliable" playbook classification for a pair/two-pair-heavy build that naturally spreads across suits; worth revisiting that classification for non-flush decks.
- No `est` vs actual mismatch over 20% was observed except one early case (ante 2 Big Blind: est 1716 vs actual 2332, ~36% low) which was noted in the ante 2 section and did not recur later in the run -- likely a low-money/early-hand estimation edge case in the advisor rather than a systemic issue.
