Post-mortem: binding constraint was the boss mechanic (The Fish) combined with a bad-luck face-down draw, not
money or scaling -- our jokers (Two Pair based: Abstract, Zany, Wrathful) and Flush L2 were reasonably on track
for ante 2. The literal cause of the loss was that BOTH remaining hands under The Fish had to be played fully
blind (face-down cards), and the `advise` tool's "guaranteed pair" estimate for face-down cards was wildly
optimistic both times (est ~510 vs actual ~195, est ~810 vs actual ~240) -- actual scores came in at 40-70% of
estimate for face-down plays specifically, while every other estimate this run (visible-card hands) was accurate
within a few percent (e.g. straight 715 est vs 650 that actual, high card 292 vs 292 flush, etc.).

One decision I would change: after the FIRST face-down hand scored dramatically under its ~510 estimate (actual
~195, a ~62% miss), I should have treated that as evidence the "always contains a pair" claim for The Fish is not
reliable (unlike The Mark, where it genuinely is: faces are guaranteed J/Q/K), and NOT relied on the same claim
again for the final, must-clear hand. With only 1 discard and 1 hand left and a razor-thin need (805) vs. a
demonstrated-unreliable estimate (810), I should have at least considered that the estimator was systematically
overconfident here and there was no safe path forward -- but there was also no better alternative available (all
cards in hand were face-down or already known singles), so this may have been an unavoidable loss once the boss
forced two facedown-only hands with only Two-Pair-style jokers and no way to see or select specific cards.

Bridge issues (exact text): the `advise` tool's face-down-hand note reads "any 4 of them always contain a pair (3
same-suit face-downs are K,Q,J). A face pair here would score about X" -- this phrasing is lifted verbatim from
The Mark's mechanic (faces face-down, guaranteed J/Q/K) but was shown under The Fish's "draw face down" boss,
where cards are NOT restricted to faces/ranks. The "always contain a pair" guarantee did not hold in practice:
both face-down hands scored far below (40-70% of) the tool's own estimate. This looks like a genuine bug --
the advisor is reusing The Mark's boilerplate reasoning/estimate formula for a different boss (The Fish) where the
underlying guarantee does not apply. No literal ACTION FAILED / retry errors this run; all commands succeeded on
first try.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Section 4 boss notes / `advise` tool: distinguish The Fish's "draw face down" (any card, no rank guarantee)
   from The Mark's "faces face-down" (guaranteed J/Q/K, guaranteed pair among 4). The current advisor text and
   score estimate for face-down plays appears to apply The Mark's guarantee to The Fish, and should be fixed or
   the playbook should warn explicitly: "under The Fish, face-down card estimates are unreliable -- do not trust
   `advise`'s face-down pair estimate; treat it as high-variance, not a guarantee."
2. Add an explicit >20%-deviation flag rule of thumb to section 7: when a face-down/blind estimate misses by a
   wide margin on hand N of a blind, do not reuse the same category of estimate uncritically on hand N+1 if it is
   the last hand and the margin over `need` is thin -- log/flag it as high risk explicitly before playing.
3. Section 5 (shop, ante 1-2): confirm the "spend to $0 every shop" behavior (as literally instructed by
   interest-floor $0 guidance) is intentional even at ante 2 -- this run never had banked money to fall back on,
   though in this case the loss was boss-mechanic-driven rather than money-driven, so this is a secondary note,
   not the root cause.
