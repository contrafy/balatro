Post-mortem: Binding constraint was the boss (The Window's suit debuff) combined with a run of unlucky flush/pair draws that kept handing us diamond-suited scoring cards during the one boss round it mattered. The decision I would change: after the second-hand TWOPAIR est-vs-actual miss (1245 -> 415, discovering how badly a single debuffed card guts a hand's score), I should have treated every remaining hands/discards estimate for this boss as suspect and pushed harder for a hand with ZERO debuffed cards in its scoring set rather than accepting a made hand with one debuffed card (as I did on hand 1 and hand 2, both of which included the debuffed 5D). The final-hand discard call (dropping the debuffed straight for a clean redraw) was correct in principle but the round was already too far behind by then — the fix needed to happen two hands earlier. A secondary factor: no xMult joker until Photograph in ante 3, and the shop that immediately preceded The Window boss had nothing worth buying at $6 (no Luchador/Director's Cut to neutralize the boss, no scaling); with hindsight, banking money instead of spending small amounts on off-target items (Empress $3, Shoot the Moon $5) earlier might have let a Luchador or similar suit-boss counter be affordable later, though none appeared in this run's shops regardless.

Bridge issues: none. Every command behaved as documented; no `ACTION FAILED` or connection errors encountered this run.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Section 3/`est` calibration: multiple hands this run showed `est` deviating from actual by 26-67% when the scoring set included a debuffed card (The Window/Hook rounds); est appears to compute card chips assuming full value and only partially discounts debuffed cards. Suggest the playbook explicitly warn: "when a candidate hand's scoring cards include a `(DEBUFF)` card, treat `est` as an upper bound only — prefer an alternative hand with zero debuffed scoring cards even if its raw est is lower."
2. Add explicit guidance for suit-debuff bosses (Window/Club/Goad/Head) beyond "buy Luchador/Director's Cut": if neither is available and a discard remains, actively discard AWAY debuffed-suit cards from the intended scoring hand before playing, even if it means abandoning an otherwise-complete made hand (this cost us the round here because the fix came one hand too late).
3. Misprint (random 0-23 mult) produced est-vs-actual swings of +/-30-60% independent of debuffs across this run (e.g. 1440->2232, 3081->2449, 1520->2432) — worth noting in section 3 that Misprint alone can swing a boss-round outcome and shouldn't be the sole source of variance late in a run; prioritize replacing it with a deterministic mult joker once a stable pair/flush engine exists.
