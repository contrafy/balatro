Post-mortem: binding constraint was SCALING, not misplay or bad luck on any single hand -- we ran from ante 1
through ante 4 with ZERO xMult jokers (4 straight shop visits + repeated rerolls found nothing but flat +mult/chips
and unaffordable/junk tarots), relying entirely on flat-scaling jokers (Even Steven, Wee Joker) and hand levels
(FullHouse L3, FourOfAKind L3). That carried us further than expected (won ante 3 and 4 boss blinds with 30-220%
margins over est) but by ante 5 the boss blind (22000, x1 chip multiplier boss) simply outscaled flat growth. Our
only xMult (Acrobat, bought at ante 5 for $6, leaving $0) fires on exactly 1 of 3 hands per round -- not enough
leverage to fully close a 2x+ ante-to-ante target jump. The ONE decision I would change: at ante 3 or 4, when
shopadvise repeatedly returned LEAVE/REROLL with no xMult found, I should have bought ANY conditional xMult
(Blackboard, even Acrobat) the moment it appeared rather than waiting for a "reliable" one per section 7a -- the
playbook's own numbers (7a: "reliable xMult beats conditional") are right in principle, but zero xMult for 4 antes
proved worse than an imperfect one. Money was never the binding constraint (we always had something to spend by
ante 5, just not enough to buy two overlapping xMult sources before the ante-5 boss).

Playbook suggestions:
