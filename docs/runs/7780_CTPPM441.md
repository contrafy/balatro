Post-mortem: Binding constraint was a combination of boss luck (The Club debuffed Clubs, which happened to be exactly the suit carrying our secondary pair-forming cards that round) and lack of any scaling insurance - no xMult joker appeared in 12 shop visits across the whole run (consistent with playbook 7a's "1 in 46" finding), money never reached the $25 ante-4 target (ended the run at $2), and our only "second hand" (Two Pair) had no dedicated joker support, just relying on the same flat jokers as the Flush. When the debuff stripped our pair options on the final hand, there was no fallback scaling (no economy joker, no backup consumable) to close a 666-point gap.
One decision I would change: earlier in ante 3-4, when shopadvise kept suggesting "The Trio" ($8, x3 mult on Three of a Kind) as our only xMult sighting all run, I correctly declined it since it doesn't fire on Flush/Two Pair - but in hindsight I should have bought it anyway as the playbook's "conditional xMult as a placeholder" rule suggests, and started occasionally building/keeping Three-of-a-Kind hands as a real tertiary type (we saw JJJ and QQQ multiple times this run), rather than always discarding trips down into Two Pair. A x3 mult on those hands could have supplied the missing scaling margin that cost us the boss.
Bridge issues: Picking a card-targeting tarot (Death, then The Lovers) from an Arcana Pack bought during the SHOP phase (before a hand exists) silently wastes it - the consumable never appears in the Cons list and produces no visible effect, effectively burning the pack's value with no warning or error. This differs from tarots obtained via SHOP_BUY directly or via The Emperor's create-2-tarots effect, both of which correctly sit in Cons as NOUSE until used later with sel+use during SELECTING_HAND. No other CLI errors this run; all `ACTION FAILED` messages were absent (none encountered).
