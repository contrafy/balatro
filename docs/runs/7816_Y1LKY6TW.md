## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling + money. One scoring joker (The Duo, x2 on Pair, est Two Pair ~220, FH 720) against a 1500 Big Blind at ante 2 (needs ~500/hand every hand). Money was $0-7 the entire run: Small Blind pays $0, ante-1 income was ~$2-4 per blind, and rent on two skipped/bought rentals plus a wasted $4 Buffoon pack (both options were Eternal/Rental/Perishable junk) ate the budget. Entered ante 2 with a single flat xMult and no +mult/+chips partner, so every hand was Duo x2 on base mult 2 (~120-240).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 first shop: bought the $4 Buffoon pack (advisor SIM +0.22) with $6 and got two unusable stickered jokers (Eternal-rental Sock and Buskin, perishable-rental Egg). Alternative: Green Joker{R} $1 / Faceless would not have helped either, but keeping the $4 + $3 would have bought Ride the Bus ($6) in shop 2 together with Sly ($1) = two scorers. The pack was a coin flip that cost $4 and the Ride the Bus buy.
2. Ante 2 shop: sold Sly to afford The Duo ($7 -> $8) and went to $0 with one joker. Duo x2 on mult 2 equals Sly +50 chips (both ~220 on Two Pair), so the swap added ~0 score now and only helped later scaling that I could not reach. Better: buy Sly-class chips AND keep Duo for the next shop (Duo was gone anyway), or reroll/skip nothing. The real fix was a second scorer; selling the only chips joker to buy an xMult that needs a mult base is a lateral move at $0.
3. Never reached a state where a Big Blind of 1500 was clearable: p was 0.27 from the first deal. Skipping the Big for the Boss Tag would not have helped either.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you have 1 scoring joker and $0-3 entering ante 2, THEN the 1500 Big is ~25-30% (solver 0.27 at deal 1); the real decision point is the ante-1 shops: two cheap scorers (Ride the Bus/Sly/Jolly) beat one pack roll plus one xMult.
- IF a Buffoon pack at ante 1 shows only Eternal/Rental/Perishable jokers, THEN the $4 is gone for nothing; with $6 and no jokers in the shop prefer holding $6 for the next shop's $6 Ride the Bus-class joker rather than a pack when 2+ of the 3 shop slots at Gold are sticker-heavy.
- IF an xMult like The Duo (x2 on Pair) is your only joker, THEN base mult 2 gives only +100 per hand over bare hands: do not sell a chips/mult scorer to pay for it, buy it only in addition.
- IF the Small Blind pays $0 and you are at $0, THEN every ante-2 shop offers nothing (Blackboard $6, tarots $3, packs $4 all unbuyable): ante-1 money must be preserved (do not buy the pack gamble).
