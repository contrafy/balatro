## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: ZERO jokers that score on a plain Pair/Two Pair. Five shop views (two rerolls) offered exactly one
scorer, Wrathful (+3 mult per Spade), plus eternal rentals (Blue Joker, The Tribe), Bootstraps{ETERNAL}, Crafty{RENTAL},
Swashbuckler{RENTAL}, Vagabond{RENTAL}; the one Buffoon pack held Credit Card / Oops! All 6s{ETERNAL}. So every ante-1/2
round was "spade Flush or nothing": 1425 when it hit (Head), 250 when it missed (ante-2 Small, 1000).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante-2 shop with Credit Card: I rerolled once and left at $-2 with $18 of credit unused. SIM baseline was 0.09 (91%
   dead by the boss); credit left at GAME OVER is worth $0. I should have rerolled 3 times ($5+$6+$7 = $18) hunting a
   Pair scorer (Jolly/Sly/Joker/Half/Abstract), or at least taken Crafty{RENTAL} $1 (SIM +0.20) for the Big/Boss.
2. Ante-2 Small first discard: kept QQ + 3S (fine), but my second discard threw QQ for a 2-spade "flush draw"
   (solver p 0.90). With 2 hands of digging left, a 4-5 card spade draw from 9 spades in 34 was really ~0.5-0.6.
3. Not the Small skip: Foil tag pays after the Big, and the Big (1500) needs the same spade Flush; playing was right.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Credit Card AND shopadvise shows EMERGENCY with 0 Pair scorers, THEN treat the -$20 as cash for rerolls and
  scorers this shop: unused credit at a lost blind is worth nothing, and a Pair scorer is worth the whole run.
- IF your only scorer is suit-gated (Wrathful/Lusty/Greedy/Gluttonous) at ante 2, THEN the ante-2 Small (1000) needs a
  5-card flush of that suit from 3 hands + 2 discards (~50-60%, not the solver's 0.9): buy ANY unconditional scorer,
  even a flat rental, before that blind.
- IF a Buffoon pack at ante 1 offers no scorer, THEN Credit Card beats an eternal utility joker: it bought Wrathful
  on credit one step later.
