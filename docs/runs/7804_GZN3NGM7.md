## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: ZERO scoring jokers through ante 1 and into ante 2. The run saw exactly one scorer for sale in
three shops (Scholar{PERISHABLE,RENTAL} $1, Ace-only) and one Buffoon pack (Mr. Bones / Wily{ETERNAL}). A jokerless
Black Deck makes 60-370 per L1 hand; the ante-2 Small (1000) needs ~333 per hand, i.e. three FH/flush-level hands.

Decisions I would change:
1. Buffoon pick: Mr. Bones over Wily Joker{ETERNAL} (+100 chips on Trips/FH/Quads). Bones did save the Manacle (224/600),
   but it only bought one more blind; with no scorer the very next blind (1000) was a certain loss. Wily would have added
   +100 chips to the Big's FH AAAJJ (372 -> ~770) and to any Trips/FH at the boss, but the Manacle needed 600 and my
   boss hands were 2P/Pair (Wily dead on them), so Wily alone probably also loses the boss. Both lines lose; the real
   fix is upstream (point 2).
2. Shop 1 ($5): Scholar{PERISHABLE,RENTAL} $1 + Buffoon $4 together ($0 left) instead of Buffoon only. Scholar adds
   +20 chips +4 mult per scoring Ace; I scored Aces in 4 of my 9 scoring hands (AAAJJ, AAKK, AA, the flushes). Rent
   would have been $3/round, but a $1 joker that fires is the only thing that moves a jokerless ante-1 deck.
3. Ante-2 shop ($6): the reroll to $1 was the right gamble in principle (only $1 rentals were reachable), but it found
   Popcorn $5 / Juggler $4. Nothing in that shop scored, and nothing was saved by holding $6 either.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own 0 scoring jokers at ante 1 and the shop offers ANY scoring joker at $1 (rental), THEN buy it in addition to
  a Buffoon pack; ante-2 Small (1000) cannot be cleared jokerless and rent ($3/round) is cheaper than a dead run.
- IF a forced ante-1 Buffoon pick is Mr. Bones vs an Eternal flat scorer and you own 0 scorers, THEN Mr. Bones only
  saves ONE blind; prefer it only if you can afford a scorer in the very next shop ($5+). With $1 left after the pack,
  the scorer is worth more because every remaining blind needs it.
- IF your best remaining line is a single high-value hand (royal flush/quads) and no normal sequence reaches the need,
  THEN dig with 3-card junk plays keeping the 4-card draw; it's the only nonzero line (solver agreed: 0.10-0.13 vs 0.00).
- IF Big Blind last two hands need ~120 with AA + K J T in hand and no discards, THEN play 3 junk cards as a dig: 15 outs
  (Q for Broadway, K/A/J/T for AAxx two pair 124) in 3 draws is ~86%, vs ~50% for Pair AA now (it hit FH AAAJJ).
