## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING (zero +mult). Both jokers were +chips (Clever +80 on Two Pair, Blue +2/deck card ~+60-88), so every
hand ran at base mult: Two Pair L2 x3, Pair x2, Trips x3. Two Pair maxed ~470-670, and the 1500 Big Blind needed 500 per hand
from three hands with 2 discards, i.e. a made two pair EVERY hand. One pairless deal (Small: straight 296) or one missed
dig (Big: last hand Pair K 168) loses the round. Money was never above $10, so there was no rescue budget.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 first shop ($10): I rerolled $5 and found nothing playable. The Celestial Pack ($4) was a ~30% Uranus gamble,
   but a direct $5 reroll gave nothing either. Better: keep the $10 (+$2 interest), then buy the Jumbo Buffoon AND have $6
   left for a planet in the next shop. Rerolling at $10 with one scorer bought nothing and cost the interest.
2. Jumbo Buffoon pick: Blue Joker vs Banner vs Scholar were all +chips. A +chips joker on top of a +chips joker barely
   helps a x3 hand (+80 chips = +240). Skipping the pack and buying Mercury/levels would not have been better either; the
   real fix was earlier: a +mult joker (Jolly/Mad/Joker) must be the first or second buy, not a second chips joker.
3. Big Blind hand 2 (need 436, KK held, 0 discards, 2 hands): I dumped 4 junk as High Card 77 to dig 4 cards for a pair
   next to KK (~65-70% estimated). It missed. The alternative (Pair K now 184, then two pair from scratch) was worse
   on paper, so the line was right; the loss was set up by hand 1/discard choices not producing two scoring hands.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your only jokers add CHIPS (Clever, Sly, Blue, Banner, Scholar, Odd Todd), THEN your per-hand score is ~3x(chips) and
  ante-2 Big (1500) needs a two pair EVERY hand; the next joker you buy or pick MUST add mult (Jolly, Mad, Joker, Half,
  Abstract, Greedy-type suit mult) even if a chips joker scores a bit more on paper.
- IF a Buffoon pack offers only +chips jokers and you already own one +chips joker, THEN still take the unconditional one
  (Blue > Banner > Scholar), but plan the next shop's money for a +mult joker or main-hand planet, not a reroll.
- IF you hold quads plus a pair with 2 hands left and need < 45% of quads' score more, THEN play the quads and keep the pair;
  on the next hand dump junk (not the pair) to dig for two pair; a bare pair (~170-200 at ante 2) never covers the rest.
- IF a made trips appears with 1+ discard and the trips est is below need/3, THEN discard 4 keeping trips + one kicker:
  K outs + 4th card + any drawn pair gave Quads+pair here (one discard, ~55%).

Advisor/CLI defects:
- `shopadvise` at the ante-1 Big shop called Two Pair "main hand undecided" and Uranus "planet for other hand" (score 15)
  while my only joker was Clever (Two Pair-gated); it ranked a PERISHABLE+RENTAL Odd Todd at 72 (-$15 rent). Owning a
  type-gated joker should set the main hand.
- `advise` at The Head (QQ held, need 201, 2 hands) proposed `disc 4 3 6 7 8`: discard QD (breaking the only made pair)
  to chase a straight (EV 104). Keeping QQ+A+T for two pair outs was better; the next draw happened to give the straight.
- `advise` on the ante-2 Small ("keep Pair, discard 1 4 5 6 7") kept a lone 2C kicker over AC/QD high kickers; two pair
  with higher kickers scores more and the out count is equal.
- est was exact all run (Quads 1064, High Card 77, Pair 168 vs actual sums).
- Bridge: no failures, every action settled on first try.
