## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING. The main hand (Full House/pair-based) was never leveled past Level 1 across all of ante 1-3 (no Celestial pack was ever purchased - none affordable/prioritized in ante 1-2, and the one available Celestial Pack in ante 3 shop was skipped because shopadvise's $10 interest floor said LEAVE at $8 money). Level-1 Two Pair/Full House tops out around 200-950 per hand even with 4 jokers, but the ante 3 Small Blind alone needs 2000 - a jump of +67% from ante 2's Big Blind (1200) that a Level-1 hand simply cannot reach even across 3 hands (max realized ~900).

Money curve: $4 (ante1 start) -> $8 (after Small Blind 1) -> $1 (bought Buffoon Pack) -> $1 (after picking free joker) -> $8 (after Big Blind 1, Campfire purchase dropped it to $1 same shop)... money hovered in the $1-$8 range the entire run and never approached the $25 ante-4 target. Two xMult-growth jokers (Campfire: grows on card sale, Hologram: grows on card added to deck) were bought early but barely fed (Campfire never fed at all - no cards were ever sold; Hologram fed once to x1.25) so effectively contributed near-zero xMult the whole game, despite occupying 2 of 6 joker slots.

Decision I would change: at the ante 3 shop, override shopadvise's LEAVE and buy the Celestial Pack ($4, Earth planet) to level the Full House/main hand, even though it dropped money from $8 to $4 (below the $10 floor). A single hand level compounds on every subsequent blind for the rest of the run, while the floor's interest income ($1-$3/round) is comparatively trivial. Not doing this left the main hand at Level 1 through the exact ante (ante 3) where blind requirements jump sharply, and that is what lost the run.

Also, in retrospect, buying Campfire (ante 1, $9, leaving $1) and Hologram (ante 2, $7, leaving $1) without ever feeding either one meaningfully (no card sales for Campfire; only one standard-pack pick for Hologram) meant two of four joker slots produced almost no xMult all game - the playbook's own warning ("a growth joker you never feed is a dead slot") applied here and was not acted on: no plan was made to actually sell jokers/consumables for Campfire or buy more Standard Packs for Hologram.

Est vs actual scoring: Ante 2 Boss (The Flint) Four of a Kind TTTT - est 956, actual 7650 (~8x, far beyond the 20% threshold). Likely cause: the TC Lucky Card (Lucky enhancement + Purple Seal) scored as part of the Four of a Kind and triggered its mult/money jackpot chance, which then multiplied through the xMult jokers (Hologram x1.25, Juggler polychrome x1.5) already in play - `advise`'s est calculation apparently does not model Lucky Card's probabilistic bonus at all.

Bridge/CLI issues: none. Every `play`/`disc`/`buy`/`pack`/`pick`/`shop` command succeeded on the first try; no `ACTION FAILED ... retry` or connection errors were encountered during this run.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Growth-type xMult jokers (Campfire, Hologram, and similar "must be fed" jokers) should carry an explicit warning in shopadvise's own output ("UNFED - contributing 0 xMult" or similar) whenever their current x_mult is still at its base/near-base value several shops after purchase, since it is easy to buy one and then never structure sells/pack-buys to actually feed it.
2. The $10 (ante 3) / floor-based interest heuristic in shopadvise should not block a Celestial Pack purchase for the player's already-committed main hand when that hand is still Level 1 and the upcoming blind's need has jumped by more than ~50% from the previous blind - hand leveling should outrank the interest floor in that specific situation, since Level 1 hands cannot scale fast enough otherwise.
3. `advise`'s `est` field should either model Lucky Card's probabilistic mult/money trigger (even as an expected value) or explicitly flag "hand contains untriggered Lucky Card(s)" so the agent knows actual score may deviate substantially (this run saw an 8x deviation, est 956 vs actual 7650).
