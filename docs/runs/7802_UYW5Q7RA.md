## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING via MONEY. From the ante-1 Small Blind ($0 at Gold) I never held more than $8; every scorer I found carried a sticker (Wily perishable, Banner perishable, Lusty rental, Mad eternal, Shoot the Moon eternal+rental). Two perishables carried ante 1-2 (Banner alone was +110 chips, i.e. 2/3 of my chips), and when Banner died after the ante-3 Big the Two Pair fell from ~3500 to ~1500 per hand while the Window asked 2133 per hand. The Credit Card let me spend $20 I did not have (Mercury x2, Mad, Supernova, Shoot the Moon, a Spectral pack that returned Wraith = +$12 and a negative joker) and that is the only reason ante 2 was cleared; by the Window the debt cap (-$20) was hit, so the last two shops could not take Golden Joker/Vampire/Death. No xMult was offered at a price I could pay (Vampire $7 at -$13 with 0 enhanced cards). 15 shop views, 6 rerolls: scorers without stickers seen = Supernova, Vampire, Golden Joker only.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante-3 Big shop (-$3): I rerolled twice (-$11 total) and bought Mercury + a Standard pack; Death ($3) from the first reroll was the better buy and I let the second reroll replace it. On the final Window hand I held QD (debuffed) next to a 2S: Death on 2S->QH would not have helped (debuffed Q), but Death a round earlier (hand 2, AH AD KH QH + junk) copying a junk card into QH would have given 2 held Queens for hands 2 and 3 (+13 each): 1326 -> 1989 and 1624 -> 2296, total 6760 > 6400. Rule: when the engine is "cards held in hand" (Shoot the Moon, Baron), Death is a permanent engine piece, not just a rescue.
2. Ante-2 House shop: Mad Joker{ETERNAL} was right, but the ante-2 Small shop's Jumbo Spectral was a $6 gamble that happened to pay (Wraith). Reasonable in EMERGENCY, but the Driver's License it produced was sold for $6 a shop later; net it was a $6 loan for +$12. Fine.
3. The Window itself: hand 1 played Pair 9 + stone with 1 Q held (2475) was right; hand 2's discard kept AA KH QH and drew no Q (31%). The alternative (play Pair A 1326 immediately, keep the discard) was worse. The blind was lost in the shop, not at the table: 5425 vs 6400 is one Banner (+110 chips x ~25 mult = 2750) short.
4. Ante-1 Big shop: foil Banner $7 (perishable) over keeping $8 for the ante-2 shop. Banner won three blinds (Hook, Small 2, Big 3) and was the correct buy; its expiry was planned but the replacement never appeared at an affordable price.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF Credit Card ($1) is offered at ante 1-2 with 0-1 scorers, THEN buy it: a $20 loan at Gold (Small pays $0) is worth two shops of rerolls and jokers. But treat -$20 as a hard wall: after -$12 only buy what clears the NEXT blind, and sell every non-scorer (Driver's License $6) before rerolling.
- IF you own a held-card engine (Shoot the Moon +13/Queen held, Baron x1.5/King held), THEN (a) never play that rank, (b) keep ALL copies in hand, (c) buy Death/Cryptid/Standard packs to add copies of that rank: each extra copy held is +13 mult on every hand, and (d) under a suit boss (Window/Club/Goad/Head) a DEBUFFED held copy gives nothing (QD under The Window counted for 0).
- IF a perishable supplies more than half of your chips (Banner +110 of ~165), THEN before its last round buy chips, not mult: Sly/Blue/Scary/Stone cards/planets. Mult jokers on 55 chips cannot replace it.
- IF the Small Blind is on deck at ante 3+ and (shop money < $3 AND rent >= $3/round AND a perishable scorer has exactly 1 round left), THEN skip it: no round end means no rent and no perish tick, so the joker scores on the Big instead of the Small, and a money tag (Garbage/Handy) pays a shop's worth. This saved 4800 here.
- IF The House (first hand face down) and you hold a visible made Pair, THEN use the face-down cards as the fillers: the floor is the same Pair, a hidden match upgrades it, and the visible cards stay in hand.
- IF a Jumbo Spectral pack is affordable while in debt, THEN Wraith (money set to $0) is +debt in cash and a free Rare, and Immolate is +$20: at -$10 or worse the pack's EV beats a $5 reroll.
- IF Stone cards with seals are offered in a Standard pack, THEN take them over plain/polychrome number cards: a Stone card scores +50 chips as a filler on every hand type and a Gold seal pays $3 per play.
