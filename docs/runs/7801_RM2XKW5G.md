## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING, caused by MONEY. The holo Smeared Joker made Flush a pair-frequency hand (two 26-card "suits": a flush in ~73% of 8-card deals, 2-3 per round with 2 discards) and it carried antes 2-4 easily (every blind cleared with 1-2 hands to spare, The Mouth 36690/18000). But every multiplier I found was a Gold-sticker rental (Photograph eternal+rental, Blueprint perishable+rental, Wrathful eternal+rental), so from the ante-3 boss on the wallet was $0 to $-4 with -$6..-$9 of rent per played round, and the ante-5 shop could not buy the $5 Banner (+60 chips) that would have added ~6000 per flush. The Serpent then capped the round at three flushes from shrinking hands; the maximum black flush with my chips (4 faces ~24k) could not reach the 50000/3 = 16.7k average when the deck gave a faceless third flush (5880: Photograph needs a face, so a faceless flush loses x4 of mult).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 first shop ($22): two rerolls ($11) found Scary Face. Fine. But at the ante-3 boss shop I rerolled to $4 and bought Photograph{ETERNAL,RENTAL}; it won The Eye (needed) but its rent plus Blueprint's and Wrathful's made every later shop unaffordable. With hindsight the alternative was not available (the shop had nothing else), so the real fix is earlier: keep $8-10 untouched from ante 2 so one of the later shops can buy a CLEAN scorer at $5-6 (Banner at ante 5).
2. Ante 4 Big played instead of skipped: it paid $4 - $6 rent and ticked Blueprint for a shop with $-1. With 2+ rentals and negative money, skipping the Big is strictly better (no round-end, a tag). I did this at ante 5 (+$33 from Garbage + Speed tags) one ante too late: that $33 at the ante-4 boss shop would have bought Banner/Green Joker/a Celestial.
3. The Serpent sequencing: the third flush had no face. After hand 2 I held AS 9D 6H 6D 4C; nothing could fix it, but at hand 1 (12 cards) the black flush took QS and JC, leaving the reds QH TD 9H 9D and the black faces gone. Keeping one black face back is not possible (a flush needs 5 of a colour), so the only real fix was more chips/mult per flush.

What worked (reusable):
- Jokerless ante 1 on Black Deck Gold: dealt trips twice; discarding 4-5 around 888 drew the FH (336 one-shot) and the solver lines (Trips+fillers, then a 4-flush draw) cleared the 450 Big and The Window 600 at 0.3-0.4 p_clear per step. The solver found every line.
- The Hermit HELD from the ante-1 shop ($3 at $7) and used at the ante-2 shop at $12 -> $24: +$12 instead of +$4 if used on the spot. Holding a Hermit through a boss costs nothing.
- Holo Smeared Joker at ante 2 ($10): +10 mult on every hand and 2-colour flushes; Flush L4 with Photograph/Blueprint/Wrathful reached 21k per black flush at ante 4.
- Luchador rental ($1) for The Head: under Smeared the boss debuffs BOTH red suits (is_suit), confirmed by DEBUFF on TD/8D; selling Luchador in-round lifted it and stopped the rent.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF a holographic/polychrome Smeared Joker (or Smeared + any +mult) is offered at ante 1-2, THEN buy it over a perishable/rental pair joker: with two 26-card colours a Flush is dealt in ~73% of hands, so the Flush-build data (0 of 6 reached ante 4) does not apply; level Flush (Jupiter) every Celestial and keep faces for Scary/Photograph.
- IF you own Smeared and the boss is a suit boss (Head/Club/Goad/Window), THEN both suits of that colour are debuffed: buy Luchador or plan black-only / red-only flushes.
- IF you hold The Hermit and own fewer than $20, THEN hold it through the boss and use it at the start of the next shop (the boss pays $5 + interest first).
- IF you own 2+ rentals and your money is <= $5 at Gold, THEN skip the Small AND the Big: the Big pays $4 against $6-9 of rent, its shop cannot buy anything, and each skip is a tag (Garbage/Speed/Investment pay cash; Uncommon/Rare/Buffoon/Charm/Meteor give free items). Play only the boss.
- IF you own Blueprint, THEN `move jokers` it directly LEFT of your xMult (it copies the joker to its right); a copied Photograph is a second x2 on the first face.
- IF the boss is The Serpent and your engine needs 5-card hands, THEN discard ONE card at a time (each discard draws 3) to grow the hand to 10-12 before the first play, and expect the third hand to come from 8 cards.
- IF your flush engine depends on Photograph, THEN a flush with no face scores ~4x less; when choosing which 5 of 6+ same-colour cards to play, always include a face, and when two flushes are available play the one that leaves a face for the next hand.
- IF the ante-N boss needs X and the round can hold only k hands of your main type (Serpent k=3 from shrinking hands, Eye k=1, Needle k=1), THEN the shop before it must raise the single-hand max to X/k; a build that clears Big blinds with hands to spare can still be 25% short at the boss.
