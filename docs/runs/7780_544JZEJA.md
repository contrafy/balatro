Post-mortem: binding constraint was SCALING, not luck or misplay. By ante 2 Big Blind we had only 2 jokers, and
  neither fires on a plain Pair (Baron = xMult per King held, Castle = permanent chips per discarded suit card) -
  violating the playbook's "first joker must be a floor Pair-joker" rule, but through no available choice: the
  only Buffoon pack option was Baron/Acrobat (no floor joker offered), and the only ante-2 reroll options were
  Castle/Clever (Clever is Two-Pair-gated, not a plain-Pair floor joker either). `est` matched actual score exactly
  on every single hand this run (no >20% misses to report) - the CLI's estimator was fully reliable; the loss was
  a genuine build/scaling shortfall, not an estimation error.
  Decision I would change: at the ante-2 reroll ($7, 2 items), buy Clever Joker ($4, leaves $3) instead of
  Castle ($6, leaves $1). Clever fires reliably on Two Pair/Full House/Quads (our actual hand shapes turned up
  Two Pair and Full House repeatedly in ante 1), while Castle's growth needs many discards of one suit to matter
  and contributed only +3 flat chips this entire run. Buying Clever would have left $3 (still nothing else
  affordable) but given a joker that scores on the hand types we were actually making, instead of one that scores
  on suit-discard volume we never built around.

Playbook suggestions:
