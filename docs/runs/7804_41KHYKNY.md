## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: supply + money. 8 shop views and 1 Buffoon pack offered zero xMult jokers (seen: To Do List{E},
Reserved Parking{R}, Misprint{R}, Oops{P,R}, Mail-In{E}, Sly{R}, holo To Do List, Delayed Gratification{P}, Ice Cream{P},
Banner{E}, Devious, Business Card{R}, Onyx Agate{R}). My only multipliers were two polychrome EDITIONS from Polychrome tags
(x1.5 each), one of them on a perishable. The three rentals (Misprint, Sly, Business Card) cost ~$51 of rent over the run, so
I never held more than $8, never earned $1 of interest, and the ante-5 shop could only buy a $6 pack gamble.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 shop: holo To Do List $7 took me to $1 with $6/round of rent. It carried ante 3-4 (+10 mult), but left 0 money for
   ante 4-5. With hindsight, holding $8 for the ante-4/5 shops would not have found an xMult either (none was offered), so
   this was probably right; the real problem is that a Gold run cannot afford 2 rentals from ante 2 on.
2. Ante 5 shop: Jumbo Spectral $6 (Cryptid on a 5) changed nothing. The Arcana $4 (Judgement/Wheel of Fortune chance) plus
   $4 in hand was about as weak; no option there could add the needed x1.6.
3. The Mark: discard 1 kept the 3 face-downs (they held a pair, 10530). Fine. Lost by 7334, mostly to Misprint rolls (FH
   at max roll would have been ~20k; nothing could reach the 26726 last-hand need).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you are rent-broke (2+ rentals and money under one round of rent), THEN skip every $0 Small Blind at any ante: the
  shop after it would open at ~$0, while the skip saves the rent and perish ticks and banks a tag. This run skipped 6 blinds
  (a2 Small+Big, a3 Small, a4 Small, a5 Small+Big) and each tag paid more than the forgone shop (Coupon: 4 free items;
  Polychrome x2: free x1.5 jokers; Boss Tag: The Wall -> The Fish; Meteor: 2 levels).
- IF the ante-2 boss is The Wall (4000) and the Big Blind's tag is the Boss Tag, AND your hand is ~800, THEN skip Small and
  Big: the reroll made it a 2000 boss that one FH cleared.
- IF a Polychrome Tag is pending, THEN the next shop's first joker gets polychrome (x1.5) whatever it is: take it even as a
  rental/perishable economy joker (Delayed Gratification, Business Card) when you own no xMult; place it rightmost.
- IF the boss is The Fish, THEN spend discards before the first play: discard draws are face-up, play draws are face-down.
- IF a polychrome/perishable joker is your only multiplier and has 1 round left at the start of an ante, THEN plan to fight
  only the boss (skip Small and Big) so it is alive for the blind that matters.
- IF Misprint is a main scorer, THEN `est` (roll 6) is ~30% low on average (real mean 11.5), but rolls of 0-3 happen: keep a
  hand or a discard in reserve rather than banking on the mean.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- SOLVER printed `p >=0.99 (calibrated)` for nearly every option while Misprint was owned, e.g. ante-4 Big 13500 with Trips
  est 5443 and 3 hands, and ante-3 Big 4800 after a 1989 first hand; the round was cleared only because Misprint rolled 23.
  Misprint's roll distribution is not being sampled (or the calibration is off for random jokers).
- To Do List shows `ab.poker_hand "High Card"`; playing a High Card at the Flint (only hand that round) paid $0 (state $0 ->
  -$6 after rent, cash-out +$10 = boss $5 + hand $1 + Delayed Gratification $4). The displayed hand is probably the static
  config, not the live `ability.to_do_poker_hand` [INFERENCE].
- `s` at blind select (ante 4 and 5) said "do not skip ... take the shop" while I held $2-4 with $9/round rent; the shop after
  a $0 Small would have opened at about -$5. The rent-broke skip rule from PLAYBOOK 2a is applied only at low antes.
- Mercury/Earth picks were labelled "NOT your main hand Two Pair" although Pair L2-L3 + Sly outscored Two Pair L1 in every
  `advise` listing; main-hand detection seems to ignore levels.
- Tags display as `Polychrome Tag([poker hand])` / `Coupon Tag([poker hand])`: the bracket text is a leftover placeholder.
- Pack advise for the Spectral pack ranked Cryptid 45 with target "most common rank" (5H) without considering The Mark (faces
  hidden) or the foil Mult 8S in the deck; harmless here.
