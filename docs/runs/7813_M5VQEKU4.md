## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. The build had only one multiplier (Baseball Card x2.25, an eternal rental) with flat +mult (Trousers, Popcorn, Gros Michel) and no growth xMult; ante 5 Small needs 25000 and a 3-hand round of ~14800 (~5-7k per hand) cannot reach it. Money was $0-12 every shop (Gold $0 Small, rent $3/round from Baseball Card), so I could never reroll for xMults or buy planets for the hands I actually played.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 Small skip for the Rare tag: it paid Invisible Joker, which needs 2 round ENDS to work; a skipped blind has no round end, so I needed one more round (the ante-5 Small, 25000) to activate it and died first. Rule: a Rare-tag Invisible Joker only pays if you survive 2 played rounds after it.
2. Ante 3 shop 1: reroll 2x for $11 found Baseball Card; no better line existed, but the whole run then ran on x2.25 that depended on counting uncommons (Trousers, Astronomer). Perished Four Fingers did NOT count for Baseball (est 4020 matched x1.5 only), so Four Fingers was dead weight from ante 3.
3. Early planets: levelled Two Pair/Straight/FH only at L2-L3; my actual plays were Flush/Straight/FH/Two Pair mixed, so no hand got a real level. A single main hand (Two Pair via Trousers) levelled from ante 2 would have scored more at ante 5.
4. Did not keep the money: ending ante 3 and 4 at $1-5 meant every shop was a free-item shop only.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF Baseball Card is owned, THEN count only NON-perished, non-debuffed uncommon jokers (Spare Trousers, Four Fingers while alive, Astronomer, Hologram...); sell a perished uncommon and its x1.5 is gone only if it was counted; verify with the `est` change.
- IF you own Astronomer, THEN every shop's planet and Celestial pack is $0: open them all, pick the hand you play most.
- IF you own Odd Todd, THEN each odd scoring card (A,9,7,5,3) is worth about +950 at ante 5 (High Card 3S 1550 vs 596 for a non-odd card): keep odd cards, play odd flushes/pairs.
- IF Four Fingers has perished, THEN `advise`/`hands` may still list 4-card straights/flushes (they are fake: `sel` shows High Card). Use `sel` before trusting them.
- IF the Rare tag gives Invisible Joker, THEN it needs 2 played round-ends after purchase; do not plan on it before a must-win blind.
- IF Spare Trousers is owned, THEN play Two Pair every round it clears (+2 mult each time: +14 by ante 5) but do not rely on it: it was +9% at ante 5.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- `advise`/`hands` list a 4-card Straight/Flush after Four Fingers perished (Straight 13-high KQJT est 4690); `sel` shows it is a High Card.
- shopadvise tells me to SELL the perished Four Fingers while Baseball Card's xMult may or may not count it (est 4020 suggested not); no warning about interaction.
- Invisible Joker's description in `s` does not show the rounds counter (extra:2 never changed), so I could not tell when it was ready.
- Solver p on hands where a 5-card minimum applies (Psychic) matched outcomes (0.89 -> won); the p 0.27/0.05 at the ante-5 Small matched the loss.
