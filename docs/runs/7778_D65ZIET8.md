## Post-mortem

Best result: stake 1 (White), ante 4, Big Blind (7500), lost by 182 (scored 7318). Antes 1-3 boss blinds and
all Small/Big blinds ante 1-4 were cleared; every `est` matched the actual scored amount exactly across all
~20 plays this run (0% deviation every time, including the two boss-debuff plays and the two face-down-boss
plays) - no case exceeded (or came close to) the 20% threshold the log is supposed to flag.

Final jokers (6/6, in scoring order): Jolly Joker (+8 mult/Pair), Greedy Joker (+3 mult/Diamond), Abstract
Joker (+3 mult/joker owned, so +18), Scholar (+20 chips +4 mult/Ace), Lusty Joker (+3 mult/Heart), Hologram
(xMult +0.25/card added to deck, rightmost - never verified to have grown beyond ~x1 since no card-adding
event happened all run). Consumables used: Strength tarot (rank+1 upgrade, used once in ante 4 Small Blind
to convert a losing Pair into a winning Two Pair). Hand levels reached: Flush L3 (65x8), Two Pair L2 (40x3).

Money curve: ante1 $4->9->8->3 (after Goad boss); ante2 bottomed at $1 twice, peaked $13; ante3 bottomed $1
(after Lusty buy), peaked $22 (after House boss); ante4 opened $22, spent to $18 on Uranus level, ended run
at $18 (never cashed a final blind). Money never reached the playbook's $25-by-ante-4 target and a reroll
hunt for xMult at ante 4 found nothing, leaving the run with only Hologram (a very weak, rare-trigger xMult)
as its sole scaling multiplier for all 4 antes.

Binding constraint: SCALING, not luck or a single misplay. Six joker slots were filled with six different
flat-PLUS jokers (pair-mult, per-diamond, per-joker-count, per-ace, per-heart) instead of a focused engine
with 2+ real xMult sources by ante 4 as the playbook prescribes (section 5, ante 3-5). The hand type actually
played was inconsistent ante-to-ante (Full House, Two Pair, Flush, Straight, Trips all appeared as the
"best" hand across different rounds) because no single joker rewarded a specific hand type strongly enough
to make discarding toward it always correct - this diluted the value of the two hand levels bought (Flush
L3, Two Pair L2) since roughly half of all plays used neither leveled hand.

The one decision I would change: in the ante-4 shop, after 1 reroll found no xMult, I should have SOLD a
weak flat joker (Greedy Joker, sell$2, worth ~+3-9 mult depending on diamonds held - among the least
reliably-triggering of the six) to open a slot and kept rerolling past the point shopadvise said LEAVE,
since ante 4+ needs 2+ xMult sources per playbook section 5 and this run never got even one working xMult.
Selling to make room during a reroll hunt is explicitly recommended by shopadvise's own advice text
("sell your weakest flat joker to make room when it appears") but I never exercised it because the hunt
never turned up a candidate to buy in the first place - the fix is to reroll more aggressively (past one
shopadvise LEAVE) specifically when zero xMult jokers are owned at ante 4+, even if it means dropping below
the interest floor, since a run with zero xMult by ante 4 is structurally capped regardless of income.

Bridge issues: one retryable `ACTION FAILED: Round eval blocking events still pending, retry shortly None`
on a `cash` call after the ante-3 boss (The House) - resolved cleanly on a single `sleep 2` + retry per
playbook section 0. No other unexpected errors; every `disc`/`play`/`buy`/`pack`/`pick`/`sel`/`use`/`order`/
`endshop`/`blind`/`reroll` call succeeded on the first try otherwise.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Section 4: add explicit guidance to check for an unused rank/suit-upgrade tarot (Strength, etc.) as a
   last-resort save whenever the best available `est` on your FINAL hand of a blind falls short of `need` by
   a small margin (this run: 10 points) - `sel i` then `use N` can retroactively convert a losing hand into a
   winning one (Pair->Two Pair here) and should be checked before accepting a loss.
2. Section 5 (ante 3-5): strengthen "REROLL until [xMult] appears" to explicitly say "continue rerolling past
   a shopadvise LEAVE, and sell a flat +mult joker on the first reroll if slots are full, whenever zero xMult
   jokers are owned at ante 4+" - this run stopped after one reroll+LEAVE and entered ante 4's decisive blind
   with no xMult, which was the direct cause of the loss (six diluted flat-mult jokers could not out-scale
   a x2 requirement every ante).
3. Confirms this run: `est` was exact in every one of ~20 plays this run, including two boss-debuff plays
   (The Goad spade-debuff, ante1) and two face-down-hand plays (The House, ante3) and the post-tarot-upgrade
   play (ante4). No changes needed to the est engine based on this run's evidence.
