## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no xMult at all (Banner + Trousers x2 via Blueprint + Onyx Agate = pure additive). A flat build of ~5k/hand against The Mouth 18000 locks one hand type: 3 hands x ~5k = 15k max. Money was $0-7 all run (Gold: $0 Small, $3 rent), so every shop was "buy the one $1-$7 thing".

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 shop: after skipping the Small for the Rare tag, my first action in the shop was a reroll. The Rare tag joker (Campfire, $0 rental) was in slot 1 and the reroll replaced it. Campfire (+0.25x per card sold) was not great, but I never got to evaluate buying it. IF a tag joker is shown in the shop THEN buy-or-pass BEFORE any reroll; reroll deletes it.
2. Ante 4 shop: Celestial pack gave Mercury/Earth/Saturn (no Uranus). I took Saturn (Straight L2), which paid once (Broadway 7866 on the Big) but not at the Mouth where only Two Pair counts. Earth (Full House, contains Two Pair so Trousers still grows) would not have helped the Mouth either. No real alternative at $7.
3. Entering the Mouth with ~5k/hand: Two Pair lock is 16k max with Trousers growth. The only line was to enter with 6k+/hand (a second +mult joker: Even Steven $4 and Strength $3 were in the last shop when I had $0). Rent ($3/round) + Gold $0 Small drained $9/ante; Blueprint was still worth it (+12 mult).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Spare Trousers (+2 mult per Two Pair, and a Full House/Two Pair-containing hand counts) THEN every Two Pair/Full House play grows it; Blueprint directly LEFT of Trousers copies its CURRENT mult (copy does not grow, but doubles the +mult): Trousers 8 -> 24 over ante 3-4, est 3.8k -> 5.7k per hand.
- IF you own Banner (+30 chips per discard left) THEN with 2 discards it is +60 chips on a ~116-chip hand (52% of the score): never discard unless it makes a clearly bigger hand; play hands with fillers instead of discarding (fillers draw cards with no Banner loss). I cleared 14 blinds this way.
- IF the boss is The Mouth THEN lock Two Pair with Trousers-type growth only if (typical hand x 3) > need; otherwise lock the type with the biggest hand (Straight L2 Broadway was 7866 here vs Two Pair 4.9k).
- IF a Rare/Negative tag joker appears free in the shop THEN decide before rerolling (reroll destroys it).
- IF broke ($0-3) with a rental, THEN Big pays only ~$5 and rent -$3: plan the ante-4 shop purchases one shop earlier (I had $7 at ante 4 shop 1, bought a Celestial pack for Saturn instead of Photograph/saving).
