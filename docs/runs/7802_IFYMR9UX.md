## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: multiplier. I had 0 xMult until ante 4 (EMERGENCY 0.12-0.20) and survived antes 2-3 on a perishable
Blackboard. The run was won by one combo found at ante 4: Acrobat{E} (x3 on the final hand) placed LEFTMOST, Brainstorm
(copies the leftmost joker) and Blueprint placed directly LEFT of Brainstorm (copies Brainstorm, which copies Acrobat):
x3 x3 x3 = x27 on the last hand of every round (verified 10 times: 98496, 49572, 104760, 75168, 158112, 298188, 202176,
339120, 453600 = exactly 27x the pre-mult value). Card-phase jokers (Even Steven +4 per even card, Scary Face +30 chips per
face, Photograph x2 on the first face) built the pre-mult value; their joker order is irrelevant, only the CARD order matters.
From then on every round was: hands 1-2 are digs (or banking under The Hook), hand 3 is the hand; Full House became the main
type and Earth x4 took it to L5. Skipping blinds whose tags paid now (Economy x2, Investment x2, Negative) or when shops were
useless (ante 7 Big, ante 8 Small+Big) cut the number of one-shot final-hand draws from 24 to 16.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4: I sold Wrathful (my 2nd flat scorer) for Blueprint before I owned anything to copy; it worked out only because
   Acrobat appeared two rerolls later. Fine in hindsight; keep the copy jokers when they are clean or cheap.
2. Ante 7 Ox: I entered with $28 knowing High Card was the most played type and two junk digs were unavoidable; the Ox
   set money to $0 and the ante-8 shop had $2. I should have spent the $28 in the ante-7 Small shop (Earth/rerolls).
3. Ante 8 shop: the Celestial gamble (sold Star) missed; keeping Star for a Flush House was about equal. Minor.

  own Blueprint, THEN put Blueprint directly LEFT of Brainstorm (it copies Brainstorm's copy) = x27. Buy Blueprint/Brainstorm
