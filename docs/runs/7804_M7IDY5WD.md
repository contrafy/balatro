GAME OVER.

## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no scoring joker at the Pillar plus a deck I had played half of. I played 25 cards across Small and Big, about half
the deck, and 9 of them were throwaway "dig" cards the solver suggested (KH 3C 7S 8S at the Small, 6H 4D 2D at the Big, plus fillers).
Every one of them was debuffed at the boss, and the boss draws came up with 4-5 debuffed cards per hand. The only joker was Mad Joker
(Two Pair), and on a 24-card deck that was half dead I never drew a second clean pair.

Decisions I would change:
1. Shop 1: I overrode the advisor (Scholar 130 vs Buffoon 78). The final hand was AA: with Scholar, Pair AA scores
   (10+22+40)x(2+8) = 720 against the 277 I needed. Mad Joker did clear the Big (JJ77 648). A Buffoon pick at $5 also left $1, so the
   rental pushed me to $-2 and the second shop (Buffoon $4, Arcana $4) was unaffordable. Scholar would have kept $1 with no rent.
   Lesson: at ante 1 with $5, a non-rental $4 scorer beats a Buffoon pack that may hand you a rental.
2. Pillar ante, Small/Big: I took the solver's multi-card High-Card dig plays when a discard or a smaller play was close in p_clear
   (Small 0.98 vs pair 0.81 is a real gap; Big 0.94 vs 0.26 too). The solver models only the current round, so it does not charge for
   cards that will be debuffed at the boss. I should have weighed that by hand.
3. Pillar boss, first discard: the solver threw away the debuffed KK. A debuffed pair still counts toward the hand type, so KK plus
   any clean pair was a Two Pair with Mad's +10 mult (~12 mult), which clears with almost any chips. I should have kept KK and
   discarded around it (the rule pick did keep it: 0.93 vs 0.95, which is noise).

## Reusable rules (if-then)
- IF the ante's boss is The Pillar, THEN in the Small and Big take a discard over a throwaway play whenever the solver's gap is
  under ~5 points, and never play more than one throwaway hand per blind. Each played card is dead at the boss.
- IF The Pillar debuffs a pair in your hand AND you own a Two Pair / Pair-gated +mult joker (Mad, Jolly, Clever...), THEN keep the
  debuffed pair. It still makes the hand type, so the joker fires on it.
- IF ante 1 with $5 and the shop has a $4 non-rental scorer (even a conditional one like Scholar), THEN buy it over a Buffoon pack.
  The pack can hand you a rental, and $3 rent at $1 cash puts you below the next shop's $4 pack.
- IF you hold a rental with under $3 cash, THEN expect the next shop to be unaffordable and plan the next blind with only the
  jokers you have.

  cards played before the boss.

  It probably samples the remaining deck without marking cards played earlier this ante as debuffed. It also discarded a debuffed

- The pack advisor called Mad Joker{RENTAL} "NEEDED NOW" at $1 cash without noting that the rent would take money negative and
