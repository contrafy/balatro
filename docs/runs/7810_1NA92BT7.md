## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling after the single flat joker died. Gros Michel (+15 mult) carried antes 1-2 (Pair 510, Straight 1400, FH 2900) but broke 1-in-6 right after the Mouth; from then on Banner (chips only) + Green Joker (+1 mult/hand) topped at ~1.3k a hand vs 3200/4800. The ante-3 shop offered no xMult/+mult (Wrathful perishable, Runner, Green Joker) and I held only $14.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 Big shop ($2): Half Joker{ETERNAL,RENTAL} $1 (+20 mult on <=3 cards, the engine of WINNING LINE 2) passed because the hard rule bars eternal rentals while I owned Banner (a chips-only rental). I obeyed the rule; the result was a 622/600 Pillar scrape and a chips-only bridge. Candidate rule tweak: allow eternal+rental when the only owned scorer is a chips-only rental that gets SOLD in the same shop and the new joker adds +20 mult.
2. Ante 2 shop $11: bought Banner{PERISHABLE} $5 (scores big next to Gros Michel) instead of Fibonacci $8 (permanent). When the only mult joker is a 1/6-break Gros Michel, the chips joker has no mult to multiply once it dies. A permanent +mult joker was worth more than a perishable chips joker; keep Gros's death in the plan (I should have treated it as gone by the next shop).
3. Ante 3: skipped Small for Charm tag with 2 weak scorers; tarot lottery gave 3 mult cards (+12 mult) for one hand only. Fine as a gamble, but nothing fixes ratio 0.07.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the only mult source is Gros Michel THEN assume it is gone after the next boss; buy a permanent +mult joker (Fibonacci/Mad/Jolly) in the same shop even over a perishable chips joker.
- IF Banner (+30 chips/discard left) is next to +15 mult THEN every hand is ~1000+ at ante 2; Banner is a strong ante 1-2 bridge, but it is chips-only and dies with the mult.
- IF Banner and Green Joker are both owned THEN never discard (discards cost both).
- IF a 5-card Full House is dealt under The Mouth and one hand clears, THEN play it (2907 vs 2000 here).
- The Fool after a tarot picked from the same pack copies that tarot (The Empress): the held copy lets you choose targets after seeing the next hand.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- `shopadvise` at ante 2 ranked Gros Michel BUY with "+750% on your Pair", but a bridge with 1/6 break and no second scorer needs a same-shop permanent follow-up; SIM did not model the 1/6 death before the Mouth (it died at the first round end).
- Pack advice for Banner said +133% and rental -$69 forever; correct but Zany{E,R} barred leaving only a chips-only choice.
- Ante 3 `advise` correctly says UNWINNABLE; nothing to fix.
