## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. I never saw a joker-row xMult in 6 shops (+3 free picks). The whole build was one hand type (Straight L5, ~10-20k when it hit) times a perishable polychrome Jolly (x1.5) and Photograph (x2 on base mult only); every round was "find the straight or die". Money was $1-10 the whole run (Small pays $0, rent on Jolly), so rerolls (one per shop at best) and Mega packs were unaffordable.

What worked (reusable): an Orbital tag at ante 2 (+3 levels) turned a 1-joker jokerless-ish run into a Straight L4 build that one-shot ante 2 (2565 vs 1500) and cleared the Mark/Water bosses (2320, 7605). Skipping Small+Big at ante 4 to fight only The Needle (Juggle +3 hand size on the boss) worked. Coupon tag shop gave a free Photograph.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4: I skipped Small AND Big (no income, no shop) and arrived at ante 5 with $4-9 and the same build. It cleared the Needle (9555 of 9000, lucky: 17% fail on Misprint 0-3) but the ante-5 gap (25k/37.5k/50k vs ~10-20k/hand) was then unfixable. A Small attempt first would have given a shop with money ($7 + Jolly rent) - probably also not enough. The real miss: ante 2 Small/Big never added a second durable scorer; I took Straight as main hand (policy says pair-family) because the tag made it 10x stronger than anything else.
2. Ante 4 shop: did not buy the Celestial pack ($4 for 25% Saturn) and kept $7 for rent; irrelevant in hindsight.
3. Ante 5 Big: discarded 3 cards at a time keeping 5 to a straight needing one rank (J, 4 outs) twice; 27% per try. A 2-card discard keeping a made Pair/Two Pair + face singles would have scored ~6k twice (still lost). The blind needed 2 face-straights.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the Small tag is Orbital and names a hand you can make (Straight/Flush here), THEN skip it at ante 1-2 with 0-1 jokers: +3 levels is x4-x10 base and clears the next two blinds in one hand.
- IF Photograph is owned, THEN play face-containing hands: it doubles the BASE mult before flat +mult (K-high Straight L4: 7605 vs ~3500 for a 9-high); a 5-9 straight has no face and loses the x2.
- IF you own polychrome Jolly (x1.5), THEN `order` it rightmost so the x1.5 also multiplies Supernova/Misprint/holo +mult.
- IF Misprint is your only large mult and the hand's est is within 25% of need, THEN treat est as having a 17% failure (rolls 0-3); est uses 11.5.
- IF a Needle is the boss and you own a straight-heavy build, THEN skipping Small+Big for Juggle (+3 hand size on the boss round) is positive: 11 cards found a made 5-9 straight on the deal.
- IF the ante-5 SCORE CHECK is <0.2 and no xMult is in the shop, THEN rerolls with $9 are noise; hold Handy/Skip tag money for Director's Cut only if a shop is reachable before the boss (a skip chain to the boss reaches no shop).
