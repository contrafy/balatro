## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no permanent scorer. Ante 1-2 shops offered only stickered jokers (Shoot the Moon{P,R}, Ice Cream{P,R},
Castle{R}, Ancient{E}, Wrathful{E,R} in a pack), so my two ante-1 scorers were both perishables that died at the end of the
ante-3 Big. Photograph (from Judgement) was the only permanent piece and it is a card-phase x2 that needs a face in the
scoring hand. Money stayed $1-9 the whole run (rent $3 x 4 rounds = $12 out of ~$30 earned), so no shop could buy a $6-8 joker.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 first shop ($9, EMERGENCY 0.07, no joker offered): I bought an Arcana pack ($4, Hanged Man = deck thinning, no
   score) and kept $5, then skipped the Small and died holding $5. A $5 reroll (2 joker views, $4 left for a $4 joker) was
   the only buy that could add a scorer. 
2. Ante 1 shop 1: Shoot the Moon rental + Buffoon to $1 left me unable to buy anything in the next 3 shops. Probably still
   right (0 scorers), but a rental perishable at ante 1 dies exactly at the ante-2/3 boundary when the curve jumps x3.2.
3. Ante 3 Big, 2nd discard: kept QQ held (+26 via STM) over the solver's 3-card dig (p 0.97 vs 0.89, n=64); both missed
   the 7. Minor; the round was ~50/50 at best.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF it is the last shop before an under-scaled blind (EMERGENCY, no joker offered) AND you have $9+, THEN reroll for a
  joker view ($5) before any Arcana/Standard pack: tarots rarely add score, a $4 common +mult joker does.
- IF your only scorers are perishables bought at ante 1, THEN they die at the end of the ante-3 Big at the latest (even
  with the ante-2 Small skipped): the ante-2 shops must buy a permanent +mult joker over planets/packs/interest.
- IF you own Shoot the Moon, THEN Queens score more HELD (+13 each) than played: play High Card/other pairs holding QQ.
- IF you own Raised Fist, THEN play your lowest cards as fillers so the held minimum stays high (8+).
- IF a Buffoon tag is on the ante-3 Small AND your perishable scorers are on their last round, THEN the skip is right
  (2 jokers now, one more round of the perishables), but it also removes the last shop before the Big: spend money first.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- Arcana pack `advise` for The Hanged Man targeted KS KD (my only face pair, KS a Mult card) instead of junk (5H 3D).
- Hand solver p is far too high on under-scaled rounds with n=64: Big 4800 opening read "round p 0.97/0.98" while the
  shop SIM just before said P(clear next 3 blinds) 0.00-0.06 and the round was lost; at the ante-2 Arm every line read
  p >=0.99 (n=64). Low-n solver lines should not print >=0.99.
- shopadvise ante-3: the Arcana pack ranked first in EMERGENCY with no joker in the shop; a reroll for a joker view was
  not offered because of the reroll floor, although the floor is OFF in EMERGENCY.
