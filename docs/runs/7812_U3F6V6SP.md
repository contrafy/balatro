## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: boss The Flint (halves base chips AND base mult) against a build whose whole mult was Stencil x5 on tiny base mult (Flush 35x4 -> ~17x2). Flint hands made 270-870 instead of the 1860 the SCORE CHECK implied (~1006/hand). I had no +mult or chips joker to survive the halving (only Hanging Chad, which retriggers 1 card), and no flat-mult source; hand 3 had no discards and a junk draw.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 shop before the Flint: I had $12 and 2 jokers and spent only $5 on a reroll. In hindsight Four Fingers{ETERNAL,RENTAL,foil} ($0) would have turned the final hand (JS 6S 4S 3S = 4 spades) into a Flush ~870 and cleared (1540+870). The hard rule forbids it, and eternal rental -$3/round for 19 rounds is a real cost, so I kept the rule; but note this exact situation: a boss that needs one more 5-card hand.
2. I skipped the ante-2 Small for a Foil tag (kept perishables alive, -1 shop at $5). That was fine, but it also removed a chance to draw the planets / pack for a +mult joker.
3. I never leveled any hand. Flint (base halved) punishes unlevelled L1 hands; the $3 Mercury/Mars/Earth at ante 1-2 planets were passed under the "no planets at <2 scorers" rule while I only ever made Flush/Straight/FH/Pair hands. With a Stencil bridge a Celestial pack would have helped more than leaving $6-7 unspent.
4. Plan for The Flint at the first shop of the ante: it showed in the shop ("Upcoming boss: The Flint") and the advisor's BOSS PREP said it counters the build, but I did not translate that into a purchase (Flush-level planet, Paint Brush for +1 hand size, or a flat mult joker).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the ante-2 boss is The Flint THEN divide your est by ~2.5 (base chips and mult both halved): a x5 Stencil on L1 hands only reaches ~270-870 per hand; you need 2000/3 = 667 per hand minimum, so buy a flat +mult joker or planet levels in the Big-blind shop, not Stencil alone.
- IF you hold a Joker Stencil THEN every extra joker costs x1, so the only buys are planets/packs/tarots or an xMult; with $12 and Stencil + 4 empty slots, buy the main-hand planet pack rather than leaving $7 unspent before a boss that halves base.
- IF the last hand of a boss round has 0 discards and need > 3x your best High Card/Pair est, the run is lost on the earlier hands: with hand 2 a made Flush (870) the remaining need was 470; keeping a discard for hand 3 (e.g. by playing Flush on hand 1, not the FH) was not possible here, but plan so the final hand still has a discard.
- A 4-card flush draw with a Four Fingers option on the table is worth ~+0.15 of clearing a boss with 3 hands; weigh -$3/round against dying at ante 2 when the SCORE CHECK for the boss is < 0.7.
