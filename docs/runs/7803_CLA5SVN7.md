## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: Obelisk play counts plus hand size. At the ante-6 Big and The Plant I scored with seven 1-card
High Card plays (cheapest growth, no discards needed). That pushed High Card to 7 = Pair 7, and the ante-7 Small 2P made
Two Pair 7 as well. From then on EVERY hand type I could make with a 6-card hand (Stuntman{ETERNAL} -2) reset Obelisk
x3.2 to x1, and Trips/FH/Flush (the free types) needed a 7-8 card hand. The Ox round was dead before the first play.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 6 Big/Plant: spreading growth plays across High Card until it tied Pair. With Obelisk, keep ONE type clearly ahead
   (Pair 7) and count the "free plays" left: High Card 7-c, Two Pair 7-c. I spent all of them in 7 hands and left no
   budget for ante 7-8. Better: take the planned reset early while Obelisk is small (x1.2-1.6) on a big Pair hand to
   create a leader with margin, or play 2P/High Card alternately and stop at leader-1.
2. Stuntman{ETERNAL,RENTAL} (hand size 8 -> 6) with Obelisk: the SIM gain (+0.68) was real for 3 blinds, but the -2 hand
   size permanently removed Trips/FH/Flush/Straight, i.e. the only Obelisk-safe types once the cheap types tied. The winning
   run paired Stuntman with Paint Brush + Palette (hand 8).
3. Ante 7 shop: Ramen (x1.78, always on) -> The Trio{ETERNAL} (x3 only on Trips/FH). With a 6-card hand Trips never came;
   keeping Ramen would not have saved The Ox (Obelisk reset), but the Trio bet needed hand size I did not have.
4. Ante 4-5: three rerolls ($15) never showed an xMult; that money would have bought the ante-7 Celestials/Grabber earlier.

What worked: Riff-raff at ante 2 ($6) filled 4 slots for free (Gros Michel, Square, Misprint, Hanging Chad) and carried
ante 2-3; Empress + Death made 3 Mult Jacks; Mystic Summit with single-card discards (two 1-card discards = Summit on for
-0.02 Ramen) roughly doubled ante 4-5 hands; skipping the ante-7 Big avoided a 165k fight.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Obelisk, THEN after every play write down the per-type counts; IF two or more types you can make with your
  hand size are tied at the max, THEN the next play of any of them resets Obelisk: plan the reset NOW (on a cheap round,
  while Obelisk < x2) instead of letting the tie happen at x3 before a boss.
- IF Obelisk is your main xMult AND your hand size is 6 or less, THEN never let High Card/Pair/Two Pair all reach the max
  count: keep at least 2 free plays of a 1-2 card type (High Card or Pair) for each future round.
- IF a joker reduces hand size (Stuntman, Merry Andy) AND your growth depends on hand-type variety (Obelisk, Trio,
  Supernova rotation), THEN do not buy it unless you also own +hand size (Paint Brush, Palette, Juggler).
- IF you play High Card under The Plant with a debuffed face card as filler, THEN that face card is the highest card and
  becomes the scoring card (scores 0 chips): use only non-face fillers lower than your scoring card (cost me 1146 here).
- IF The Ox is the boss, THEN check which hand it targets before playing (it took my $8 on High Card, not Pair, although
  Pair was the run's most played hand by tie-break).
- IF you own Mystic Summit, THEN spend the 2 discards as two 1-card discards of junk (Summit on for all hands at the
  smallest Ramen/deck cost).
- IF Riff-raff is offered at ante 1-2 with 3+ empty slots, THEN buy it: 2 free Commons per blind (no stickers).
