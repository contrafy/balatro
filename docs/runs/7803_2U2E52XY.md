## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING via CHIPS and MONEY. Every hand was ~30-100 chips x ~40-50 mult: all my mult came from flat
jokers (Half Joker +20, holo +10, Even Steven, Bus), the only xMult was Constellation x1.2, and no hand was leveled except
Trips L3. Money never exceeded $8 the whole run (ante 1 $5 pack, then Credit Card debt to -$9), so every shop from ante 2 on
could afford at most one $4-6 item. Ante 4 Big was cleared at 1.1x; The Arm then cost one level of my only leveled hand
and capped the round at ~13k max vs 18000.

Decisions I would change:
1. Photograph{ETERNAL} at ante 4 ($5, sold Greedy). With a joker-mult build (Half Joker, holo, Even Steven) Photograph
   doubles only the BASE hand mult (7 for Trips L3, 2 for Pair) because card-trigger xMults fire before joker +mult.
   QQQ gained ~+7 mult of ~52 (+13%). Two Celestial packs / a Mercury would have done more, and the eternal slot is
   now dead weight. The PLAYBOOK already says this ("card-trigger xMults ... multiply before +mult jokers") - I misread
   it as "still an xMult".
2. Chaos the Clown{ETERNAL} over skipping the pack: the free reroll found nothing usable in 3 shops (every hit was
   unaffordable at $0-4). Free rerolls are worth nothing when you cannot buy what they show.
3. The ante-1 debt reroll ($5 into Credit Card debt) showed an eternal Splash. Wasted; that $5 was a Celestial pack later.
4. What worked: Riff-raff{RENTAL} for $1 as a joker generator (2 commons at Small select: Ice Cream + Greedy, 1 at Big:
   Half Joker), then selling it mid-round AFTER the blind select so the rent never charged. Half Joker carried ante 2-3.

## Reusable rules (if-then)
- IF your mult comes from flat +mult jokers (Half Joker, holo, Jolly...) THEN Photograph/Triboulet/Glass-style card
  xMults are worth ~+10-15%, not x2: do not buy them eternal as "the xMult"; buy planets for the main hand instead.
- IF you own Riff-raff and have free slots THEN select the blind, let it fire, and sell it during the round before the
  round ends (rent is charged at round end; selling mid-round avoids it).
- IF you own Credit Card{PERISHABLE} and it has 1 round left THEN spend its debt room in this shop on scorers/xMults:
  once it perishes you keep the debt but lose the room.
- IF The Arm is the ante-4 boss and only ONE hand type is leveled THEN expect the round to score ~70% of your
  normal 3-hand total (the leveled hand drops one level before scoring each time); require ~1.5x margin on the Big.
- IF Garbage Tag offers $8+ while you are in debt at ante 3 THEN skip the Small: a shop visit in debt buys nothing.
