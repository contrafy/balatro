## Post-mortem

Best result: stake 3 (Green), ante 4, Big Blind (12000), scored 3956. Beat: ante 1 boss The Manacle, ante 2 boss The House, ante 3 boss The Fish. Died on the ante 4 Big Blind (not even the boss).

Jokers at death (5/6 slots): Four Fingers (4-card flush/straight), Jolly Joker (+8 mult if Pair), Smiley Face (+5 mult/face), Scary Face (+30 chips/face), Hanging Chad (retrigger first scored card 2x). Main hand Two Pair L2. Zero xMult joker the entire run - none was ever offered at an affordable price; every shop that had one (The Idol at ante 3) came up when money was $1-3.

Money curve: essentially $0-10 the whole run. Small Blind pays $0 at this stake (no_blind_reward), and no economy joker (Golden Joker, To the Moon, etc.) was ever bought - shopadvise never ranked one above a scoring/leveling buy, which was probably correct given jokers 5/6 was mostly floor-safe, but it meant every shop after ante 2 opened at $1-3, unable to afford anything but the cheapest single item, so scaling stalled (Two Pair stuck at L2 from ante 3 onward, no second xMult source, no Glass/Steel beyond the two ante-1 tarot picks).

Binding constraint: misplay/variance combination, not money or a boss mechanic. The engine (pair-family + face jokers) was strong when it hit face pairs (12000-16000 single hands) but had zero fallback for a hand with no pair, no trips, and no 4-card suit/straight. The decisive mistake was upstream: I spent all 3 discards on the Big Blind chasing/confirming Two Pair hands (TTT99 Full House, then 42 Two Pair) instead of holding one discard in reserve for the final forced hand, so when the last natural draw was a spread of unrelated high cards, there was no way to reshape it.

One decision I would change: on the second-to-last hand of that Big Blind (Two Pair 42, est 660, still 10044 short with only 1 hand left), the discard budget was already spent by that point in the round - the actual fix is further upstream, on the very first hand of the round (Full House TTT99, needed only 12000 total, cleared 1296 of it): instead of immediately playing every made hand, I should have spent a discard there reshaping toward guaranteed pair coverage (e.g. discarding the non-paired K/J singles for more pair-candidate draws) so a discard was still banked for the final hand, per playbook section 4 rule 8 / section 6 "always keep ONE discard for the final hand of a round when your engine depends on finding a specific card." This run's engine depends entirely on pairs, so that rule should have been treated as non-negotiable every round, not just under debuff bosses.

Bridge/CLI issues:
- `hands`/`advise` never surface a Four-Fingers-enabled 4-card flush as a candidate hand, even though `play FLUSH i j k l` (4 indices) is accepted and scored correctly by the game. Observed at least 3 times (ante 1 Boss, ante 2 Big Blind x2) where I had to manually test `play FLUSH` with 4 cards after `advise` recommended a much weaker Pair/discard line. No error text - it's a silent omission from the candidate list, not a crash.
- `shopadvise`'s "main hand" inference lagged behind the actual joker composition once: after several opportunistic Four-Fingers flushes it reported "main hand Flush" and recommended a Celestial pack for Jupiter, even though all 5 jokers owned at the time were pair/face-triggered (Jolly, Smiley, Scary Face) with no flush support. Opening the pack myself showed it didn't even contain Jupiter (Uranus/Venus/Pluto were offered); took Uranus (Two Pair) instead. No error text - a soft mis-classification, not a hard bug.
- No `ACTION FAILED`/retry or connection errors occurred this whole run; every command succeeded on the first try.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Make "hold back the last discard whenever the hand shape has no fallback for a no-pair, no-suit-run draw" an explicit per-round rule for pair-family builds, not just a boss-debuff-triggered one - this run lost specifically because all 3 discards were spent confirming made hands early, leaving zero flexibility for a genuinely bad final draw.
2. Note in section 3/CLI reference that `hands`/`advise` do not enumerate Four-Fingers 4-card flush/straight candidates; players holding Four Fingers should manually check `suits` counts for a 4-of-a-suit or 4-run and test `play FLUSH`/`play STRAIGHT` with 4 indices before trusting `advise`'s recommended discard.
3. Add a rule that `shopadvise`'s "main hand" label should be cross-checked against owned joker types (pair/face-triggered jokers vs flush/straight-triggered jokers) rather than trusted blindly when it conflicts with the actual scoring engine, since it can lag behind opportunistic off-type plays.
