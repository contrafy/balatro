## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: ante 4-5 scaling (best hand ~7-9k vs 25k Small), solved by (a) additive-mult stack Half Joker + Blueprint at ante 1-2 (1-card hands scored 600-6000), (b) skipping Small+Big from ante 3 on, which kept perishables alive for the boss and paid Skip/Economy tags ($20 + doubled $41 -> $81 + $35), and (c) spending that cash on 4 rerolls at ante 5 that found Throwback. Final engine: Throwback x4.25 copied by Blueprint (x18), polychrome Reserved Parking x1.5, holo Brainstorm + Stuntman (+250 chips, copied) and Grabber; hands were mostly 1-card High Cards (90-150k) because chips/mult are all joker-supplied.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 shop: I kept Blue Joker over a better chip copy for 1 shop; selling Blue for Stuntman at ante 6 was +40% and could have been done the moment Stuntman showed up (ante 6 shop 1 reroll).
2. Ante 5: Hierophant/Wheel/Hanged Man picks were near-worthless; nothing to buy with $80 once slots were full (2 eternals + 4 core). Eternal Golden Joker (pack pick, ante 2) occupied a slot all run for only +$4/round; it was only 'free' because money ended unspent ($102).
3. Ante 1 Big: Two Pair first (banked 120) then discards worked; the solver's play-with-filler line was right.
