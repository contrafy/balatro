## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: bad luck combined with two consecutive discardless hands under The Wheel (1/7 face-down draws)
right when the round needed exact clearance; the CLI itself flagged two `est` vs `actual` mismatches on the final two
hands (est 7072 vs actual 2720; est 4770 vs actual 1590) that meant the run's own scoring tool underestimated how far
short we actually were until it was too late (0 discards, 1 hand left) to course-correct.

Decision I would change: after the Big Blind of ante 5 (cleared 23428/16500, comfortable margin) I should have used
The Sun tarot immediately in that round (sel a card, use N) to convert a scoring card to Diamond suit and start
building toward a Crafty-Joker-synergy Flush contingency, or spent more of the $17 pre-boss money on a second xMult
source instead of leaving Zany/Photograph/Crafty as the only conditional multipliers — with the run under-scaled all
game (only 1 real xMult, Photograph, per the shopadvise flag every single shop), the margin against The Wheel (22000,
double the small/big of the same ante) was too thin. Losing by only 20 points also means slightly more disciplined
filler/discard sequencing in the final hand (e.g. verifying `hands` after each discard rather than trusting an `est`
that had just proven itself wrong two hands earlier) might have found a few more points.

Bridge/CLI issues: none — no `ACTION FAILED`, no connection issues, no Python errors during the whole run. The CLI's
own "(est X vs actual Y: logged for calibration)" messages appeared automatically twice near the end and were useful.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Explicitly warn that Arcana/Jumbo Arcana packs bought and opened FROM THE SHOP never show a `Hand:` line and have
   no `sel` action available — ANY card-targeting tarot (Empress, Hierophant, Justice, Chariot, Magician, Death, Moon,
   Star, Sun when picked from a pack) picked there is wasted with zero effect. Only non-targeting Arcana cards (Fool,
   Emperor, Temperance, Wheel of Fortune, The World, High Priestess, Judgement, Hermit) have any value from a
   shop-opened pack; prefer those, or better yet buy Celestial packs / direct planets in the shop instead of Arcana
   packs unless the only goal is fishing for a non-targeting card.
2. Note that buying a card-targeting tarot DIRECTLY from the shop (not from a pack) is different and safe: it lands
   in a consumable slot (like a bought Planet does) and can be `sel`+`use`d later during SELECTING_HAND with a real
   hand to target - this is the correct way to acquire Justice/Chariot/Empress/etc. for card enhancements, not via
   packs opened in the shop.
3. Add an explicit rule for boss The Wheel / The Fish (face-down draws): once `est` and `actual` disagree by more
   than ~50% on a hand played under one of these bosses, treat all subsequent `est` values that round as unreliable
   and prefer re-running `hands` immediately after every discard/draw rather than trusting the pre-computed advice text,
   since face-down-card noise appears to corrupt the estimator's chip/mult math more than documented.
