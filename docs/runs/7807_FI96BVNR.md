## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING from zero jokers. The first four shops offered exactly one scorer I could afford (Lusty, ante 2)
plus Popcorn $5 at $4. Two Buffoon packs gave Hallucination/8 Ball and Droll{E}/Bootstraps{P}. With $4-6 per shop
(Red Stake $0 Smalls, Blue Joker rent) I never saw an xMult. The build ended as Lusty + Droll + Blue: a heart flush made
~4200 and anything else 400-1800, so the ante-3 boss (6400) needed two heart flushes in one round.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 Big shop: Blue Joker{RENTAL} was the right buy (free, Negative, about +100 chips) but its $3/round rent then ate
   every interest dollar. Next time, sell it in the ante-3 Big shop: I had $15 there and +$1 sell value; without the rent I
   would have reached the boss with ~$10.
2. Ante 3 Big shop ($15): Celestial + Arcana ($8) bought Pair L3 and a Wheel of Fortune that missed. Neither came close to
   the 6400 boss. Two rerolls ($5+$6) for any +mult joker or Buffoon pack were the only buys that could add ~+30-50%
   to a flush-only build. The SIM ranked everything 0.58-0.64, so nothing in that shop moved it.
3. Ante 1 Big shop: Mercury left $1. Keeping $4 + $5 boss pay would have made the ante-2 first shop $10, enough for Lusty +
   a Buffoon pack together.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your only scorers are suit-gated (Lusty/Droll/flush jokers) at ante 3, THEN your round needs N flushes of that suit; count
  them against the boss: 6400 with ~4200 per heart flush needs 2 heart flushes in 3 hands + 2 discards, about 10%. Spend the
  ante-3 shops on any unconditional +mult/xMult (reroll if needed), not levels for a fallback hand.
- IF the Small Blind tag is Skip Tag AND your money is under $5 (the Small's shop could buy nothing), THEN skip it: +$5 per
  skip so far, no rent tick, and no risky blind.
- IF you hold The Lovers and a 4-card flush of your Lusty/Droll suit, THEN make the highest-chip off-suit card Wild: it counts
  as the suit for Lusty too (heart Flush 4205 here).
- IF The Wheel/The Fish is the boss and you need suited cards, THEN all discards come BEFORE the first play; on The Wheel
  face-down cards (1/7) cannot be planned around, so discard them with the junk.
