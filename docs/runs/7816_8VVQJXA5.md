## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no real scorer at ante 2. Two jokers were Bootstraps (+2 mult at $8, worth ~+2 mult total) and Lusty (+3 per Heart, hearts rarely in the scoring cards): the "typical hand" was ~490-500 vs a 1500 Big, i.e. needed 3 near-perfect hands. Fully 2 of 3 hands in the Big scored 224/208.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 first Buffoon pack (only $2 left after it): Bootstraps at <$10 is +2 mult; a pack that offers only 2 jokers is a coin flip. Fine, but then I should have kept $5 (reroll) rather than spending $5 on a conditional suit joker: Lusty scored hearts in 0 of 4 later hands (TH in 1 hand, +3 mult). The rule pick Scary Face (+30 chips per face, perishable) would have added +60-90 chips/hand on the K/Q/J-heavy hands I drew; SIM said Lusty +0.22 vs Scary +0.03 and was wrong in practice.
2. Ante 2 Big: with Pair AA est 224 and 588 still needed I chose the 3-filler play. Solver p for both lines was ~0.4-0.55; the real problem was already baked in at the shop. Selling Lusty to push Bootstraps over $10 only reached 312 vs 364: money should have been $15 (Bootstraps +6) if I had skipped the Lusty buy and kept the $7 -> Bootstraps +2-4 mult each hand. Bootstraps is a $-holding joker: buy only if you can sit on $15+.
3. Ante 2 shop with $8 I left with $8: a Celestial pack ($4) for Full House/Two Pair level would have given +1 level (~+10% per hand) at no real cost; "nothing worth buying" cost me ~10% on a loss by 10%.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your only scorers are a suit-gated +mult (Lusty/Gluttonous/etc.) and a money-scaled joker (Bootstraps), THEN at ante 2 you have ~0.23 of the needed score: before ante 2 Big, spend ALL money on planets/scoring buys, do not hold $8 for a floor.
- IF the Big Blind at ante 2 (1500) is the next fight and your best hand is ~500, THEN each play must be a made Full House/Trips (900+): solver p 0.68 here was optimistic (calibration n=100). Treat solver p < 0.75 at ante 2 as a coin flip and prefer playing every made 2P/trips immediately only if the 3 hands sum > need.
- IF a skipped blind's tag (Uncommon) pays only in the shop after the NEXT played blind, THEN make sure that blind is winnable; I skipped the $0 Small to reach the Big, but with 1 live scorer the Big (1500) was already the run-ender.
- IF a Buffoon pack offers only an Eternal junk joker and a perishable money joker, THEN consider skipping and keeping the $4 for the next shop's joker row instead.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- SIM scored Lusty Joker +0.22 (P 0.34 clear next 3 blinds) although it needs Hearts in the SCORING cards: in 3 of 4 plays no heart scored (suits are spread; 25% per card). The Gold-check SIM overrates suit-gated +mult jokers on a pair-family deck.
- shopadvise EMERGENCY said "LEAVE -> endshop" with $8 and no scorer in the shop; a $4 Celestial pack for main-hand levels was not even listed as a SIM alternative that could help the 0.23 ratio.
- Solver p at ante 2 Big (0.68 on a 1500 need, exp ~1578) overstated the clear chance: the realized 3-hand sum was 1344.
