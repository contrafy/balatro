## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING, with money as the cause. My only multipliers were Hologram (rental, grown to x2.25 by
5 Standard-pack cards) and The Order (x3 on straights only). A Pair made ~33k from ante 6 on and a straight ~55-70k.
Ante 7 needs 37k/55k/73k per hand. I never saw a second clean xMult I could afford. Two of six slots were eternal
flat jokers (holo Banner, Swashbuckler) and a third was flat (Abstract). Money was $2-8 from the ante-6 Big on:
Hologram rent ($3 x 9 rounds = $27) plus five Standard packs ($26) ate every dollar of interest.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 7 Big, hand 2 (need 94k in 2 hands, 11 cards: AD KS QS 777 66 55 3): I followed the solver's Full House line
   (p 0.16). The real numbers said it could not win: Blue Joker shrinks as the deck empties, and Banner loses 30 chips
   per discard, so no plain straight could make the remaining 72k. One hand later the solver printed 0.00 for every
   option. A better line was to discard 5 for a 4 (3-4-5-6-7) or for 8/9 while keeping 7765 3 plus AKQ, then hope for
   two straights. The odds were still low, but that line had real outs.
2. Ante 3, Swashbuckler{ETERNAL}: it was right for ante 3-5 survival (+15 mult, about +45%). But two eternal flat
   slots plus Abstract capped the build at ante 6-7. With hindsight I would buy only ONE eternal flat joker and keep a
   sellable slot for a late xMult.
3. Ante 6 shop 1: the $5 reroll ($15 -> $10) found nothing. That $5 plus interest would have paid for Shortcut ($7) at
   ante 7. Shortcut makes gapped straights count, so The Order's x3 fires far more often, and straights were by then
   my only hand that reached the ante-7 targets.

What worked: Mystic Summit (ante 1-2 bridge, +15 mult); Economy tag skip at ante 2 ($12 -> $24); Orbital tag skip at
ante 4 (Pair L3 -> L6); Hermit ($20 -> $40); feeding Hologram with Standard packs; a High Card dig instead of a discard
(it kept Banner's +30 chips); skipping the ante-7 Small for Juggle, which turned into a 70k straight on the first hand.

## Reusable rules (if-then)
- IF you own Banner (+30 chips per discard left), THEN bank made hands before you discard, and dig with a played
  throwaway (same number of new cards) rather than a discard. Each discard costs 30 chips on every later hand.
- IF you need 2 more cards and you hold a pair, THEN play the pair (2 cards) instead of discarding 2. You draw the
  same cards and also bank ~30k (Manacle: Pair 9 32962, then still drew to the straight).
- IF you own Blue Joker, THEN the last hand of a round scores ~10-15% less than the first, because the deck has
  shrunk. Plan the big hand early and do not count on a late straight to cover a large remainder.
- IF you own The Order (x3 on straights) at ante 5+, THEN straights are your biggest hand: keep open-ended 4-straights
  over low pairs while discards remain, and buy Saturn/Shortcut when offered.
- IF you choose between a Glass card and a Bonus card in a straight, THEN compare with `sel`. Glass x2 hits only the
  base + card mult before joker +mult (Bonus AC 64496 vs Glass AH 63483 in the same straight). Score Glass LAST in
  hand order (`move hand`).
- IF you hold a perished joker AND own Abstract Joker, THEN keep it until you need the slot: it still adds +3 mult.
- IF the ante-7 Small Blind shop would have less than $10 to spend, THEN skip the Small Blind for Juggle or another
  hand-shaping tag. The skip saves a rent tick and avoids risking the run on a blind that pays $0.
