Post-mortem: binding constraint = money + missing main-hand scaling. Two Pair (the de facto main hand played most often) was NEVER leveled past L1 the entire run - Uranus was offered exactly once (ante 3, $3) and we had only $2 at that moment because the prior shop had been spent to $0 on a Buffoon pack pick (Pareidolia, a long-term-only joker) instead of holding back $3-4 for the planet. The single xMult (The Trio, on Three of a Kind) carried both blinds it applied to (Small Blind ante 3: 4860 est) but Two Pair - the hand we were actually forced to close rounds with - stayed at base L1 (20x2) the whole game and could not produce enough chips x mult to clear a 3000 Big Blind on a broken final hand (no straight, no flush, no trips available with only 2 discards spent early).
The one decision I would change: at the ante 3 first shop ($8), after buying the Arcana pack for Chariot ($4, leaving $4), I should have skipped the Buffoon pack (Pareidolia, a joker with zero immediate scoring value) and kept the $4 in reserve — it would have covered Uranus ($3) two shops later when it actually appeared, leveling Two Pair before the Big Blind. Pareidolia's payoff (a future Photograph combo) never had a chance to matter because the run ended two blinds later.
Bridge issues: none. All `advise`/`shopadvise`/`play`/`disc` calls returned clean state with no ACTION FAILED or retry needed.
Playbook suggestions:
1. Section 5 (ante 3-5): explicitly warn that a Buffoon-pack pick offering only a long-term/setup joker (Pareidolia, Hallucination, economy) at ante 3 should be skipped in favor of banking $3-4 for the main-hand planet if one has not yet appeared - a setup joker with no near-term scoring value is worse than an unleveled main hand hitting ante 3-4 targets.
2. Add an explicit rule: "if your main hand is still L1 by the ante-3 boss, prioritize buying its planet over ANY joker or pack, even mid-scaling ones like Pareidolia" - two ests here (4860 est for Trips, but the played main hand Two Pair/Pair never scaled) show the model's xMult was doing all the work while the hand level lagged badly.
3. When a Buffoon pack's only options are a scoring-neutral setup joker vs nothing, `shopadvise`/`advise` could flag "hold $X for the planet seen in a future shop" isn't knowable in advance, but a static rule of "reserve $3 minimum after any ante-3 pack purchase, before spending the rest" would have prevented this exact loss.
