## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling at ante 5. The whole engine was flat chips/+mult (Clever, Jolly, Misprint, Supernova, Blue Joker) plus ONE always-on xMult (Ramen x1.8-2) and Loyalty Card (x4 only once per 6 hands). Hand levels were never bought (Two Pair L1 until ante 5). A normal Two Pair was ~10k at ante 5 against 25k/37.5k/50k; only the Loyalty hand (50k) cleared anything, so the blind AFTER the Loyalty fire (Big) was mathematically out of reach (max 3 x ~13k = 39k with perfect Misprint rolls, and cards were far below that).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 Small skip for the Buffoon Tag with 7/7 slots: the advisor said "no free joker slot"; I overrode it hoping to sell Mail-In inside the pack, then every pick was junk. If a skip tag opens a pack, free a slot BEFORE skipping or do not skip.
2. Ante 3-4 shops: never bought a Celestial/Mercury/Uranus (Two Pair stayed L1; Uranus at ante 5 gave +10%). With 3-4 flat jokers and $15-25 idle, 2-3 Uranus levels were cheap multipliers. The advisor kept saying LEAVE / noise.
3. Ante 4 shop: skipped Smiley Face{E}, StM{E}, Wee Joker{E} packs and Obelisk (ante 3 pack, chose rental Misprint). Obelisk would have been a growing xMult for the antes 4-5 rotation (Full House/Flush/Two Pair/Pair mix) and was the only growth xMult seen. I took Misprint for +11.5 flat.
4. Sold Mail-In Rebate (the only economy, ~$5-10/round) for Supernova; money was then $2 at the decisive Big with no way to reroll.
5. Loyalty Card timing: I planned the x4 hand correctly in rounds (ante 3 boss, ante 5 Small) but never planned the 6-hand cycle against the 3-blind ante; the x4 hand lands in the Small, then the Big has none. Consider selling/keeping Loyalty vs a permanent xMult.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Loyalty Card, THEN the x4 hand repeats every 6 hands = every 2 rounds; count hands (`hands` notes say "x4 in N hand(s) (this one counts)") and put your best Two Pair on that hand; skip-play 1-card High Cards on hands you need to burn.
- IF Misprint sits LEFT of Loyalty/Ramen it is multiplied too (x4 x1.9): worth ~+60% on the x4 hand, but variance is huge (roll 0 vs 23 = 2x).
- IF Mail-In Rebate names a rank, discard that rank for $5 per card (made ~$20 over 4 rounds); keep it while you have no better economy.
- IF a skip tag will open a pack (Buffoon Tag), THEN have a free joker slot first (sell a junk joker) or skip the tag.
- IF Blue Joker is perishable (5 rounds), it was ~45% of my chips for 3 antes (+2 per deck card); budget its replacement (another chips source) the moment it has 2 rounds left.
- IF you hit ante 5 with only one always-on xMult and Two Pair L1-L2, the Small may fall to one Loyalty/xMult hand but the Big will not: get the 2nd xMult or levels at ante 3-4.
