## Playbook suggestions
1. **Conditional joker trap**: Conditional multipliers (+12 if Straight, +10 if Two Pair) are weaker than broad xMult jokers (x2, x3) because they only apply to specific hands. By ante 2, you need permanent multipliers that scale across all hand types, not single-type boosters.
2. **Scaling priority by ante**: Ante 1 = build economy ($25 interest). Ante 2 = broad xMult engine (x2-x3). Ante 3+ = add second scaling engine (Photograph level, Celestial pack, or second xMult). Conditional boosters are ante 3+ fillers after primary scaling is in place.
