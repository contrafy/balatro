Post-mortem: binding constraint was MONEY, not scaling or luck. Every ante-1-2 shop was $0-8 (heavy reliance on
  free Coupon-tag items early), and by ante 4 the deck was still zero-xMult-jokers (Bloodstone conditional
  1/2-chance-x1.5 was the only multiplier; Yorick's x1-per-23-discards had likely not reached even x1 yet) with a
  main hand that peaked around 2000-2600 per Two Pair play and 6000-8000 per Full House play against a 12000 Big
  Blind. Two consecutive $3-4 shops right before the Big Blind had nothing affordable worth buying (per shopadvise),
  so the run arrived at ante 4's Big Blind with no scaling upgrade since the free Black Hole pick. The straight
  chase on the Big Blind (following `advise`'s EV line) burned all 3 discards without ever reaching a hand that
  projected to clear 12000 -- in hindsight, given the deck had zero real xMult and a main hand capped near 2600,
  no sequence of discards was going to find a single hand worth 12000; the loss was already effectively locked in
  once the shop money dried up, not a misplay in the final round. The ONE decision I would change: at ante 3-4,
  when `shopadvise` said LEAVE for lack of affordable items, I should have considered rerolling (even down toward
  $0 per the playbook's ante-1-2 zero-scoring-joker rule extended to "zero xMult" ante 3+) rather than accepting
  LEAVE at $3-4, since arriving at a 12000 blind with a flat, unleveled-enough build was the actual failure mode.
  Also: Bloodstone (our only multiplier) is suit-conditional (Hearts) but the build never specifically enhanced or
  favored Hearts cards, so its 1/2 chance frequently didn't apply to enough cards in the scoring hand.

Bridge issues: none. Every command behaved as documented; no ACTION FAILED / retry messages, no connection issues.
  `est` vs actual differed by >20% three times, always because Bloodstone's 50% x1.5 fired and the CLI explicitly
  flagged it in the `notes` field each time ("Bloodstone 50% x1.5 not counted"): ante 1 boss TwoPair FaceJ8 (784 est
  -> 1120 actual, +43%), ante 2 Small Blind Straight 14-high (1620 est -> 2389 actual, +47%), ante 3 Big Blind
  TwoPair FaceAJ (2244 est -> 3009 actual, +34%). This is expected/documented CLI behavior (conditional joker not
  counted in the conservative estimate), not a bug.

Playbook suggestions:
  1. When a shop item's listed price is genuinely $0 (verify via `raw`'s `cost` field, e.g. Coupon Tag rewards),
     `shopadvise`'s money-floor logic should not say LEAVE for it -- a free pack/joker/planet has zero opportunity
     cost and should always rank above LEAVE. This came up 3 times in ante 1 alone.
  2. Add explicit guidance for ante 3-4 when `shopadvise` says LEAVE at $3-5 with zero real (non-conditional)
     xMult joker: consider a reroll even if it drops money near $0, mirroring the existing ante-1-2
     zero-scoring-joker reroll rule, since a flat build with no multiplier caps out well below ante-4-5 blind
     sizes (verified here: best single-hand est ~2600-2800 vs a 12000 Big Blind).
  3. `advise`'s straight/flush chase EV logic used all 3 discards on the Big Blind without ever surfacing a
     projection that the chase could realistically clear the blind (12000) -- consider having `advise` compare the
     chase EV against the ACTUAL blind requirement (not just against the current best made hand) before recommending
     a full-discard chase, and warn explicitly when even a successful chase would still fall short.
