## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no real xMult by end of ante 3. Final build was Jolly (+8 mult) + Wily (+100 chips on Trips) + Splash + Smiley (+5 mult/face) + Yorick (x1 per 23 discards, never got past x1) + Perkeo. Hands maxed ~2-5k (a Full House of faces 5346) vs 9000/13500/18000 at ante 4. Two Souls (rare!) gave Yorick and Perkeo, both useless for score: a legendary from The Soul is random, so don't count on it as the plan. Both eternal jokers (Jolly, Wily) are conditional and plugged 2 of 6 slots.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 Small shop (after the Mark): spent $5 on a reroll then $4 on Wily{ETERNAL}. Wily only fires on Trips (~25% of hands). A Celestial/Pair-level or keeping interest would not have saved the run either; the real miss was not buying a Buffoon pack at ante 2 with the Mega Buffoon money earlier (I took Smiley + Business Card; Green Joker would have been an xMult-less filler too).
2. Ouija (ante 4): I took it as a high-variance gamble; it turned 11 deck cards into 2s (a random rank, 3/13 chance of a face). Quads of 2s scored 2655 only. Never pick Ouija unless a rank is already your engine. Deja Vu/Trance were also weak, so the correct play was probably skipping the spectral pack and rerolling.
3. Skipping Big at ante 3 (Handy tag) was right (kept Sly alive for the Fish, full house cleared 7326), but then I arrived at ante 4 with $20 and a 0.09 score check: 1550 vs 16666 needed. Gold ante 4 needs an xMult by ante 3; I had Yorick x1 only.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Splash AND Smiley Face (or any per-face +mult) THEN play 5 cards incl. every face you hold (faces add +5 mult AND +10 chips each: KKK+J = 3720 at ante 2). Fillers are not free chips, they are the scoring.
- IF The Soul appears in a pack THEN pick it only with a free slot (sell a perishable first: the pack stays open and `sell` works); the legendary is random (Yorick, Perkeo here), so keep the plan for scoring elsewhere.
- IF Yorick is owned THEN it needs ~2 full rounds of 5-card discards per +x1; it never reached x2 by ante 4. Do not count it as an xMult before ante 6.
- IF The Fish is the boss THEN discard 5 first (face-up draws), play the made hand (FH 5346), then discard the face-down cards before the next play.
- IF a Charm tag (free Mega Arcana) is on the Small at ante 3-4 THEN skipping it paid twice here (Soul x2); the tag is a joker-roll plus Death/Empress.
- IF Ouija is offered THEN skip unless a specific rank is already your engine; its rank is random (rank 2 here) and it costs a hand size.
