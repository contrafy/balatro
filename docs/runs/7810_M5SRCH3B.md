## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING at ante 5 (no growth/xMult besides Photograph, only Pair L3, and both perishables died right before the boss). The score engine was Hanging Chad (foil rental) + Photograph = x8 on a face first card plus Smiley; that is ~14k for a face Pair, 23k for a FH/Straight. A 50000 boss needs ~17k per hand with zero misses; hands 2-3 of the Mark had no face pair and no discard left.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 5 Big: spent both Death/Fool uses (and a hand-reorder) on making Trips; fine for clearing, but there was no Death left for the Mark boss. If the boss is the next blind and the Big clears with one more normal hand, hold Death/Fool for the boss (FH with Death-made trips was 23k).
2. Money: carried two rentals (Banner + Hanging Chad = -$6/round) while Small pays $0; money was $0-6 for all of ante 5 so no reroll or planet could be bought at the boss shop. A perishable (Half Joker, Banner) bought at ante 4 with 4-5 rounds left dies exactly before the ante 5 boss; plan the replacement slot/money one shop earlier.
3. I never took a growth planet-level strategy for Pair (only Pair L3); Straight/FH levels from Mega Celestial were wasted but helpful as backups.
4. Discards: I discarded my last discards at the Mark keeping 4S4D "FH shape" (4 cards kept) instead of drawing 5; five facedowns happened to make a FH, so luck.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF you own Hanging Chad (retrigger first card x2) AND Photograph, THEN lead every hand with a face card as the FIRST scoring card (leftmost): the face triggers 3x and Photograph doubles each trigger (x8). A Pair of Qs/Ks went from ~4k to ~20k. Keep Smiley LEFT of Photograph so the +5 per trigger is doubled too.
- IF you own Banner (+30 chips per discard left) at Gold chip levels (~45 chips), THEN play the best hand BEFORE discarding: 3 discards = +90 chips = x3 on a Pair (1260 -> 3645). Each discard you use shrinks it.
- IF Riff-raff shows up non-sticker (holo $9) THEN it is worth buying but only generates jokers into FREE slots: sell junk before each blind select so it makes 2 Commons, then sell the junk again; Riff-raff itself is just +10 mult once slots are full.
- IF you hold Death and a Fool, THEN Fool re-makes Death: copy your best face onto a junk card to the RIGHT of the junk (Death overwrites the LEFT card). Use `move hand` to put junk left of the face first.
- IF The Mark is the boss, THEN 5 face-down cards always contain a pair of J/Q/K; playing all 5 face-down as a Two Pair/Full House is a valid ~15-24k hand with a face-first engine (mine made a FH).
- IF a perishable joker has N rounds left, THEN it dies N blinds from now: count whether the boss of the next ante is covered before you plan around it.
