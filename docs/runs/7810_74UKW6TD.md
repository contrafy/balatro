## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. At ante 3 I owned 0 xMult: Trousers (+16 mult), Matador holo (+10), Green (+8), Raised Fist (~+8) on L1 Two Pair = ~2.0-2.6k typical hand vs a 6400 boss (SCORE CHECK was 0.34-0.43 for two shops: EMERGENCY). Money never exceeded $20 (rental rent + Small pays $0), so the two ante-3 shops bought one $5 joker and nothing else. The Window (diamond debuff) with a hand that dealt no pair after 2 discards was a coin flip I lost.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 shop 1 ($14, EMERGENCY): rerolled once ($5) and bought Raised Fist at $5, leaving $4. A Celestial/Arcana pack or a second reroll was not affordable afterwards, and Raised Fist only added ~+8 mult (~12%). Hunting for ANY xMult was impossible at $14; the real mistake was earlier: Matador's rent (-$3/round x 4 rounds = -$12) plus the ante-2 Hermit/rerolls kept money at $4-14, so no Buffoon pack was ever opened. Selling Matador once Raised Fist arrived would have saved $3/round (Matador +10 mult vs rent was probably still worth it, but a Buffoon pack at ante 2 shop 2 (instead of reroll + Green Joker) would have been a better xMult sighting).
2. Ante 2 shop 3 ($13): used a $3 Hermit (+$7) then rerolled to $15 and bought Green Joker. I did not buy a Buffoon pack because none was in the shop; fine, but the Arcana pack ($4) was passed.
3. Window boss (ante 3 end): after the first discard (kept AH TC 9H 8S for a straight draw) missed, nothing could be done. Possible improvement: keep a pair-draw hand (e.g. AH KS) rather than a 4-card straight draw; the solver said 0.73 -> 0.49 -> 0.03 sequence, i.e. the first discard was already a 0.73 decision at TIGHT 1.22x.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the next boss debuffs a suit (Window/Goad/Club/Head) AND your projected round is < 1.3x, THEN treat the shop before it as TIGHT and spend on score even at the cost of rent; here 1.22x lost.
- IF Raised Fist is owned, THEN play junk low cards as fillers (est jumps 1564 -> 1840) and keep Ks/Qs/Js held; it makes even a 1-card High Card ~500 at ante 3 with flat +mult.
- IF an Investment/Skip/Holo tag on the Small is the only reward and your hand is ~0.4x the Small, THEN skip it (done at ante 2 Small 1000: fine).
- IF a Royal/Straight Flush is dealt and a smaller hand already clears, THEN play the smaller hand (the round ends anyway) - no gain from overkill.
- IF a flat +mult build (no xMult) reaches ante 3 with $4-14, THEN the run is a coin flip at the first suit-debuff boss; the xMult hunt has to start at ante 1-2 via Buffoon packs, not rerolls.
