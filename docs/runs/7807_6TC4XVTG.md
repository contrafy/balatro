## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING / ECONOMY at ante 1-2. The run reached ante 2 with $0-5 and one flat joker. The first shop
($5) had only Zany{PERISHABLE} (trips-gated) and a Buffoon pack that offered a rental Hallucination or a rental-perishable
Scholar; the second shop had Gluttonous Joker ($5) when I held $3. Rent from Scholar ($6 over two rounds) is exactly the
money that would have bought Gluttonous ($3 + $3 = $6 vs price $5). Blue Joker alone makes ~200-640 per hand at ante 2,
against 500 per hand needed for the 1500 Big Blind.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop 1 ($5): the Buffoon pack gave only rental jokers. I took Scholar (rental, perishable). Its rent ($3 x 2
   rounds) cost me Gluttonous Joker in shop 2 ($3 held vs $5 price). Scholar did win the Head boss (AAA66 1296), but a
   pair of plain FH/flush hands also had real odds there. Better: take Scholar, but SELL it before the ante-1 Big Blind
   ends only if no Ace hand is planned; or skip the pack when every option is a rental and the shop has a $4 joker
   (Zany{PERISH} would have cost $4 once, no rent).
2. Ante 2 Small Blind skip for Coupon Tag (my override of `ADVICE: b blind`). Reasoning was that the $0-paying Small
   leaves ~$2-3 for its shop, so the skip removes one risky 1000 blind and the post-Big shop would be free. The Big
   Blind at 1500 with one flat chips joker was the real wall (solver p ~0.5 at the start, 0.37 after two discards).
   Playing the Small would have meant a 1000 blind with the same joker (probably ~0.6) and a $2-3 shop: still a bad
   line. I think the skip was not the cause, but it is recorded as an override that lost.
3. Big Blind hand 3 options were all dead (solver 0.03); the decisive moment was the 2 discards: I kept QQ (solver
   top) instead of 4 hearts + QQ (two pair floor + flush draw). Neither line reached 1500 often.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF a Buffoon pack offers only RENTAL jokers at ante 1 and you hold <= $5, THEN count the rent against the next shop:
  a rental takes $3 per round, which is a whole $4-5 joker by the second shop. Prefer a $4 unrented joker in the shop
  (even a perishable one) over a Buffoon pack when money is that low.
- IF you enter ante 2 with one flat joker (Blue Joker, Scholar...) and $0, THEN the Big Blind (1500 = ~500 per hand)
  needs Flush/Full House every hand; plan the discards for a Full House/Flush from the first draw, and expect to lose
  about half the time. The real fix is earlier: two +mult jokers by the end of ante 1.
- IF Scholar is your scorer, THEN keep Aces and dig with High-Card-Ace plays (High AS 180 at ante 1 = more than a Pair).
