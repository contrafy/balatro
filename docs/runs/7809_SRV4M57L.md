## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. Two flat jokers (Shoot the Moon conditional on Queens held, Mystic Summit +15 only at 0 discards) and Pair L1 gave ~1500-2000 per hand at ante 3 against a 4800 Big; zero xMult, zero planets, $0-9 every shop. The ante-1 Popcorn bridge was the only real mult source and expired at the ante-3 Small. Money was the cause upstream: Small Blind pays $0, I spent to $0-1 at every shop (Popcorn, Buffoon pack skipped, Shoot the Moon, Summit) so the ante-3 post-Small shop with $2 could not buy a planet, a pack or a reroll.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop 2: opened a Buffoon pack with $7 and left with both jokers unbuyable (Banner rental / Swashbuckler eternal), then skipped: it burned $4 and ended on $3. Alternative was Crafty{PERISHABLE} $4 (Flush +80) or keeping the money for ante 2's reroll. Rule: with $7 and one scorer, a Buffoon pack is a 35% gamble that cost the next shop's reroll.
2. Ante 3 Big: discarded 4D/5D/KC at hand 1 with the solver's top (0.69) rather than keeping a KQJ+A shape; the deal had no pair and Summit needs 0 discards, so every discard that does not find a pair is wasted. With Death in hand I used it only after burning one discard; using it at hand 1 on the Ace/King (pair of Kings with Q held) would have kept both discards.
3. Hand 2 of the ante-3 Big: dug with KD JD 9D (keeping a 4-heart flush draw, solver p 0.67 vs 0.51 for playing the safe line); the draw missed. A 1-in-3 outcome, not a misplay, but the build had no margin: 4800 needed ~2.4k per hand and my best made hands were 2106/748.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Mystic Summit, THEN every discard is spent before the hand you intend to score; hand 1 is a dig with discards, hands 2-3 are the scoring hands. Do not play hand 1 for score while discards remain.
- IF you own Shoot the Moon, THEN keep Queens in hand and play pairs/trips of other ranks; two held Queens = +26 mult (about +60% on a 40-mult hand). Death/Strength-copies of Queens add to deck density.
- IF Death is held with a junk card LEFT of a pair-making rank, THEN use it at hand 1 of a no-pair deal, before discarding (the copy keeps the seal: 8H gold seal copied gave +$6 on one Trips).
- IF a rental Buffoon pack offers only Banner/Campfire at ante 1-2, THEN take nothing; but do not open the pack with $7 when a $4 perishable scorer (Crafty) is in the shop at ante 1: price the pack at $4 for ~35% to find a permanent scorer.
- IF the Small Blind pays $0 and you own 2 non-xMult scorers at ante 3, THEN the pre-Big shop is the last cheap chance for a planet: arrive with $6+ (skip a Buffoon pack at ante 2 shop 2 if it leaves < $4).
