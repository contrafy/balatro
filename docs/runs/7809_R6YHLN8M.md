## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. The only real multipliers were Acrobat (x3 final hand) and Card Sharp (x3 on a repeated type),
both conditional on SEQUENCE: the round's score was ~75% one final hand (x9) and the rest of the round barely counted.
Flat mult was Abstract (+18) and Lusty (+0-6); no growing xMult ever showed (12 shop views, 5 rerolls). At ante 7 a
perfect round topped out near 100k vs 110k. Economy was the second constraint: two eternal flat jokers (Lusty, Scary)
and two perishable rentals kept the wallet at $2-18 except one Temperance+Hermit spike ($34), which bought Card Sharp.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 6 Mega Buffoon: I took Mr. Bones over Blue Joker{ETERNAL}. Bones did save the Fish, but the run then died at the
   very next blind; Blue Joker (+~80 chips every hand, +37% on face Two Pair, more on low pairs) might have made the
   Fish AND given a chance at ante 7. With ratio <0.5 Bones only buys one blind.
2. Ante 1-2: eternal Lusty Joker (+3 per heart) for $5 blocked a slot for 6 antes and was worth ~+3 mult; a $4 Buffoon
   pack at ante 1 shop 2 would have been a better gamble.
3. Ante 5 shop: kept $18 instead of rerolling twice; with only 2 conditional xMults at ante 5 the money had no better use
   (never got interest above $3/round anyway).

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Acrobat AND Card Sharp, THEN plan every round as: hand 1 = cheapest version of the type (registers), hand 2 =
  same type x3, hand 3 = your BEST version x9. Hold the best pair/two-pair cards from the start; never spend them early.
  A face Pair x9 made ~37k at ante 4-5, a face Two Pair L4 x9 made 79k.
- IF Mystic Summit + Acrobat, THEN burn both discards before the final hand even when the final hand is already made:
  +15 mult on an x9 hand is worth ~+60%.
- IF Card Sharp registered Two Pair and the final hand could become a Full House (3rd King face-down), THEN play only the
  4 Two Pair cards: a Full House is a different type and loses the x3.
- IF The Fish: discards draw face-UP (only cards drawn after a PLAYED hand are face-down); use discards to rebuild.
  Face-down cards are rank-sorted in hand, so one sitting between two visible Kings is a King.
- IF Temperance and Hermit are in the same Arcana/Mega pack, THEN take Temperance first (sell value of jokers, here +$11),
  then Hermit doubles the sum (+$17): $6 -> $34.
- IF the next blind is a near coin-flip (round est < 1.1x need) at ante 5+ with no Throwback, AND the tag pays now
  (Buffoon/Meteor/Charm), THEN skipping still helps: fewer fights, no rent/perish tick, and perishables live one more
  blind. It did NOT fix the underlying scaling: by ante 7 every blind was out of reach.
