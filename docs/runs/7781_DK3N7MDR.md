Post-mortem: binding constraint was NOT money, scaling, or the boss (The Club never got a chance to matter) - it was
a bad final-hand draw combined with never holding a rescue tarot. The run played 9 rounds (ante1 Big/Boss, ante2
Small/Big/Boss, ante3 Big/Boss, ante4 Small/Big) and NEVER bought a Death/Strength/Justice tarot from a shop's card
row despite `shopadvise` explicitly flagging "no rescue tarot held" from ante 3 onward in every single shop line. In
each case the ADVICE line ranked a pack/joker/econ item above the $3 rescue tarot, so it was never bought following
advice literally, and no override was applied. The ante-4 Big Blind forced final hand (0 discards, 1 hand left, no
pair-family cards) is exactly the scenario playbook section 4.8 and 7a warn about ("unused card-targeting tarots can
turn a losing final hand into a winning one") - a held Strength tarot could have ranked up 2 of the singleton high
cards (Q+J, or J+T) into a pair worth several thousand with Card Sharp/Scholar/Smiley Face support, easily clearing
the needed 3312.
The decision I would change: at the ante-3 shop where `shopadvise` first flagged "no rescue tarot held" (after buying
Scholar), spend the leftover $4 differently or hold back $3 from an earlier purchase specifically to buy a shop-row
tarot instead of always following the ranked ADVICE item, since the tool's ranking undervalues the tarot's insurance
value against a hand-shape risk (no pairs on the final card) that its per-item scoring model does not represent.

Bridge issues: none. Every `play`/`disc`/`pick`/`buy` call succeeded on the first try; every `est` matched or was
close to the actual score except: Big Blind ante3 Full House 999KK (actual 5406 vs est 4872, +11%, Steel-card
held-in-hand bonus under-modeled) and ante4 Small Blind Straight 13-high (actual 8784 vs est 6162, +43%, largest
est/actual gap this run - also likely a Steel-card or edition bonus under-modeled by `advise`/`hands`).

Playbook suggestions:
1. Add an explicit rule: from ante 3 on, if 3+ shops in a row have passed on a rescue tarot because a higher-ranked
   item was available, force the tarot buy at the NEXT shop regardless of ranking once $3+ is affordable - the
   ADVICE line's per-visit ranking never surfaces the tarot on its own once other items look better, so the run can
   go an entire game without ever holding one.
2. `est` under-modeling of held Steel/enhanced cards: two hands this run scored 11-43% above `est` when a Steel or
   otherwise-enhanced card was in the scoring or held group. Worth checking whether `hands`/`advise` account for
   Steel's "while held in hand" bonus on cards NOT in the scoring group of a hand (they still contribute if held).
3. Card Sharp (and similar repeat-type xMult) registration hands: consider having `advise` warn when playing the
   cheap "registration" hand will leave the round's final hand with a high risk of no pair-family cards at all
   (based on remaining deck composition), so the registration hand can be built to preserve a pair-family card
   for the fallback instead of purely optimizing this hand's score.
