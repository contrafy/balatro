## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. Three jokers (Summit, Misprint, Greedy) and Ramen carried me to ante 4; the first real multiplier set (Baseball Card + Blueprint) only appeared at the ante-5 shop, too late: ~16-22k per hand vs a 50k boss that also halves hand size (Manacle). Misprint/Seltzer perished exactly as the boss arrived. After the Blueprint/Baseball/Bootstraps purchase the est said round 145k but actual hands scored 0.74x, 0.61x and 0.38x of est (16384/22004, 9779/16136, 2514/6601): the advisor's est for the Blueprint(copy Ramen) + Baseball order is clearly optimistic (and the last est even undercut its own "roll 0" floor of 4629), so the "145k round" was not real.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 shop: I spent $5 on Greedy (common +3/diamond) and went to $8; the money was better kept ($24 -> Bootstraps scales with money, and Baseball Card at ante 5 would have been affordable anyway). A common flat joker never mattered.
2. Ante 5 after Baseball Card: instead of Blueprint left of Ramen I should have verified the copy with a probe hand (est vs actual) before the boss; I trusted ORDER FIRST. Alternative: Blueprint left of Baseball (copy x1.5/Uncommon) kept est 125k and is the order whose chain I could reason about.
3. Ante 3 Polychrome tag skip: tag landed on a rental junk joker, wasted. Do not skip the Small for a tag that pays "next shop" while a rental carrier needs shops.
4. Manacle: hand 2 filler trick (draw 5 new cards) did not help; with ~16k/hand average vs 50k I needed ~3 hands of 17k and Misprint rolled low twice.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Mystic Summit, THEN each round discard exactly 1 card per discard action when your made hand already clears (keeps Ramen); discard more only to dig toward a Straight/Full House. Every Summit hand then scores ~2x the est without it.
- IF the Mouth (lock one hand type) is the boss and your Juggle tag is available, THEN skip the Big to keep +3 hand size for the boss: 12 cards held 12 distinct ranks = two disjoint straights (K-high 15613 + 8-high ~10k) for 18000.
- IF The Arm is the boss, THEN play unleveled types (L1 FH/Pair) and do not buy that ante's planet before the boss; a played type loses a level (verified: only Straight L2 mattered).
- IF Baseball Card{E} is offered at ante 4-5 with 2+ Uncommon jokers owned (Summit/Ramen/Seltzer/Bootstraps), THEN buy it even eternal: with Summit+Ramen it was worth ~x2 at once and Bootstraps (Uncommon) adds x1.5 more. Put commons left, Uncommons after +mult jokers.
- IF the Skip Tag (blind skipped: +$1 per skipped blind so far) is on the Small at ante 5 with 5 prior skips, THEN skip it: it paid $25 ($9 -> $34).
- IF `est` after adding Blueprint/Baseball changes by >20% in one buy, THEN play a cheap probe hand and compare est/actual before relying on it for a 50k boss.
