# Run K6K8Z7P9 (port 7813, claude-sonnet-5-5) - Black Deck Gold Stake - LOSS at ante 1 blind Small Blind (159/300)

Ante 1 (Skip Tag/Holo Tag/The Manacle 600, $4): jokerless Small, need 300. Deal AD TD 7H 6C 6D 4H 4C 2H (two pair 66/44, est 80). Disc 1 2 3 8 (kept 66 44, full house draw) -> AKQ, no FH. Disc 1 2 3 8 again -> JS TH + 66 55 44 (three pair, 0 discards left). Hand 1: PAIR 55 dig (JS TH 5H 5D) 40 keeping 66 44 for FH p0.39 -> missed (drew AC JH 9H 3H). Hand 2: HIGH JH 9H 3H dig 15 (p0.49) -> missed. Hand 3: TWOPAIR QQ66 104. Total 159/300. LOSS on the first blind of the run.

## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no joker and base Two Pair = ~80-104 per hand, so 3 hands cap at ~250-300 even when every hand is a Two Pair; clearing 300 required a Full House (or Flush/Straight) that never came (3 FH draws at p~0.35-0.39 each, all missed; 2 discards + 2 dig hands spent). Banking two pair early would also have lost (84+80+104 = 268 < 300), so the only real alternative was skipping the Small Blind (Skip Tag) to reach a shop/Big with a joker - the advisor said "b blind" and I followed it.

Decisions I would change:
1. Skipped nothing at ante 1 with $4 and 0 jokers: the PLAYBOOK section 5 stake-2+ note says "At ANTE 1 with no jokers, SKIP the Small Blind for any tag except the Boss tag" (3 pair-shaped hands vs 300 lost 4 of ~8 attempts), but section 2a/`s` ADVICE say the opposite (skip only for a tag that gives a joker now). I followed `s`. A Skip Tag (+$1 per skipped blind) buys nothing, so honestly both plans are weak; the contradiction should be resolved with data.
2. On hand 2 with 66 55 44 JT and 0 discards I played the PAIR 55 dig (40) and kept 66/44. The three-pair hand gave 3 outs-pairs for a FH draw only via a single new card of the 4 held ranks, and the draw missed twice. Equal EV either way (solver said 0.76 vs 0.66), so this is the ~1-in-4 tail, not a clear mistake.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF ante 1 Small, 0 jokers, and the deal is Two Pair only (no trips/flush/straight draw), THEN the blind needs a Full House/Flush/Straight (base Two Pair est ~80-104 x 3 hands < 300): spend both discards keeping the 5-card draw with the most outs and do NOT dig with hands (a dig hand scores ~15-40 and costs one of only 3 chances).
- IF the Small Blind pays $0 and you hold $4 with 0 jokers, THEN consider the Small the riskiest blind of the ante: about 1 run in 4 dies here (recorded: this run, 224/300, 256/300, 292/300 losses).

Advisor/CLI defects:
- After the first FH miss the solver still advised discarding to keep 66/44 (p 0.36) when the hand also contained A-K-Q (a straight draw with 2 more cards is not available, fine); no defect found there, but the ADVICE on the 3-pair hand (play a Pair 55 dig) scored 0.76 vs 0.66 - both are far below the 0.94+ of the first hand; the advisor shows the discard steps as p 0.94 -> 0.89 -> 0.76 -> 0.49 -> 0 and never warns that the opening "p 0.94/0.97" was optimistic for a jokerless Two Pair deal.
- `s` at ante 1 Small, jokerless, printed BOSS WARNING "round reaches ~459 vs 600" but ADVICE `b blind`; no estimate that the Small itself (300 vs ~80 per hand) is ~75% clear with Two Pair only.
