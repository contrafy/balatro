## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no xMult at all and a flat engine capped at ~4-7k per hand. Mystic Summit (+15, doubled by Blueprint) with chips from Sly/Scary was fine through ante 3 (hands 4.6k-7.1k), but ante 4 Big needs 13500 = three 4.5k hands in a row with ZERO discards (Summit forces burning both discards before the first play), so no digging at all. Money never exceeded $17 and every shop was a bridge buy (Popcorn, Sly, Scary, Photograph) instead of an xMult hunt.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 Big: Summit forces 0 discards, so hands 2-3 are pure draws. After hand 1 (quads 5550) I played TP AA99 4290 with a 7S filler and kept 22 + K + Q + (face singles) hoping for a face pair (est 86%); it missed. A safer line was to play the bigger pair-family hands first and keep the best 3660 line... in truth nothing was safe: the build had no margin. The real fix was earlier: do not rely on a 0-discard joker as the only scorer past ante 3.
2. Ante 3 shops: after the Small skip I spent $0 on Cartomancer (useless slot) and reroll $5 for nothing; keep money for Buffoon packs. Ante 4 shop: Photograph (x2 on BASE mult only, here 2-4) added ~0; the $5 should have gone to the 2nd Celestial pack or a reroll.
3. Passing Director's Cut was right but irrelevant: the Flint/ante-4 curve (9k/13.5k/18k) needed ~2x more score than I had.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Mystic Summit, THEN each round burn both discards on junk while keeping a made pair-family hand, and play the made hand: it was worth +15 mult (x2 with Blueprint to its left) and carried antes 1-3 (Pair 510, 2P 850, FH 7130). But it caps you at the cards you hold: hands 2-3 are draw-only.
- IF Blueprint is offered with Summit/any flat +mult joker owned, THEN put it directly left of that joker and take it even as a perishable: copy of +15 mult is ~+45% score.
- IF the round needs 3 equal hands (Big at ante 4 = 13500 vs ~4.5k per hand) and you hold a made quads/FH on hand 1, THEN expect the last hand to need a pair of faces (Scary Face) or a two pair with Aces: plan which hand to play first by what the leftover cards can make, not just the highest est.
- IF a filler would let you draw more but keeps the same hand type (7S with TP), take it: fine, but it cannot save a build with 0 xMult.
