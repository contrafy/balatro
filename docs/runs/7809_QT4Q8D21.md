## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: LUCK on a thin jokerless line, with a structural flaw in the line. Every decision followed the
solver's top p_clear option (two discards, then two High Card digs) toward one 4-card club flush (KC 9C 8C 2C). The
flush draw hit only on the third try and the card was the 3C: a flush at L1 is (35 + card chips) x 4, and with
K+9+8+2 = 29 held the fifth club had to be >= 4 to reach the 270 still needed (only the 3C of the live clubs fell
short). The two High Card dig plays scored 15 each, so they added almost nothing toward the total.

Decisions I would change:
1. After the first discard I held KC KD 9S 9C 8H 8C 6H 2C (Two Pair K9 est 116, two more pairs on the board). Playing
   Two Pair K9 (116) then Two Pair 98 / Pair hands with 1 discard left needs ~184 from 2 hands; solver said 0.88. I took
   the 0.97 discard. In hindsight both are fine numbers; the solver's ante-1 jokerless probabilities are known to be
   overconfident (earlier post-mortems: 0.93-0.98 on lines that failed), so a made 116 in hand was worth more than the
   +9 points of reported p_clear.
2. Before any flush dig with a LOW 4-card flush (held card sum < 35 at L1), compute the minimum flush:
   (35 + held sum + 2) x 4. Here it was (35+29+2)x4 = 264 < 270 needed after two 15-point digs, so not every hit cleared.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you are jokerless at ante 1 and hold a made Two Pair (est >= 100) plus a 4-card flush draw, THEN play the Two Pair
  first (it banks a third of the blind and draws 4 cards that can still complete the flush) unless the solver margin is
  over ~15 points; ante-1 jokerless solver numbers are overconfident.
- IF your plan is High Card digs toward a single flush, THEN check that the WORST flush completion clears what remains:
  (35 + held card chips + 2) x 4 at L1. Low flushes (2-9 clubs) score ~250-270, not the ~280 the playbook quotes.
- IF a dig play is needed and the dig cards contain a pair you are not keeping, THEN play that pair (+ the other dig
  cards as fillers) instead of a High Card: same draw, ~50 points instead of 15 (here no dig set contained one).
