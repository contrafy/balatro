Post-mortem:
  Binding constraint: scaling, not luck or misplay. This build never found or bought a single xMult
  joker in 5 antes (Photograph is the closest thing to one, but it only doubles mult on ONE face card,
  not a growing multiplier) -- every other joker was flat +mult/+chips (Abstract, Greedy, Sly, Jolly)
  or a chip-decay joker (Ice Cream). Playbook section 3's guidance ("score per hand must roughly double
  every ante") was met by hand-leveling (Full House L2, Straight L3) plus flat-mult stacking through
  ante 4, but by ante 5 (need 11000/16500/22000) flat mult stopped scaling fast enough: our best single
  hand all game (Full House 888JJ) capped out around 8-9k, well under the 16500 Big Blind target, and
  no combination of remaining draws in the Big Blind round could close a ~7400-point gap on the last,
  discard-less hand.
  One decision I would change: in ante 3-5 shops, `shopadvise` never surfaced an xMult joker to buy
  because none appeared in the offered slots or packs across roughly a dozen shop visits -- this looks
  like bad luck on shop rolls rather than a missed pick (every ADVICE was followed, and no xMult option
  was ever declined). If I could redo one thing, I would rerolls more aggressively in ante 3-5 shops
  specifically to search for an xMult joker once money was comfortably above the $10-15 interest floor
  (we had $41-48 in ante 5 with nothing to spend it on) rather than just ending shop when nothing in the
  current offer was buyable -- the playbook's reroll guidance is conservative for antes 1-3 but does not
  give an explicit "reroll to hunt xMult" rule for ante 4-5 once money is abundant and jokers are full.
  Bridge/CLI issues: none. Every command succeeded except one expected `buy` failure ("ACTION FAILED:
  buy_from_shop returned false: no space or card not purchasable None") when consumable slots were both
  full of NOUSE tarots -- resolved by `sellc` on the unusable tarot first, not a bridge bug. The
  mid-run bridge restart (documented in docs/runs/7777_VLB551SW.md) cost one full ante-1 boss attempt
  but was an orchestrator-directed deploy, not a bridge fault.
  Playbook suggestions:
  1. Add an explicit ante 4-5 rule: once money is above ~2x the interest floor and jokers are 6/6 full
     with no xMult among them, reroll shops (even at $5-8 each) specifically hunting for an xMult joker,
     selling the weakest flat-mult joker to make room, rather than ending shop when nothing on offer is
     worth buying outright.
  2. Flag when `est` deviates from actual by >20% for follow-up: this run had two such cases --
     Three of a Kind under The Arm (ante 4, est 6825 / actual 5425, -20.5%) and Full House in ante 5
     Small Blind (est 8756 / actual 8159, -6.8%, not flagged but noting the pattern skews high). The
     advisor's est seems to run optimistic under boss-modified rounds; worth auditing the est model's
     handling of Ice Cream's per-hand chip decay (which compounds every hand played in a round) against
     boss-round hand counts.
  3. `advise`'s "keep the pair, discard the rest" heuristic does not evaluate open-ended or gutshot
     straight/flush draws when a made pair also exists in hand -- it correctly recognized a 4-flush once
     cards were sorted differently, but twice suggested abandoning a live, already-4/5-complete straight
     draw for a weaker/unleveled flush chase. Consider having `advise` compare draw EV against the
     hand's actual leveled multiplier, not just card count, when both a pair-based hand and a
     straight/flush draw are live simultaneously.
