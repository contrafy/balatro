## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: economy, then scaling. Two ETERNAL RENTAL jokers (Arrowhead at ante 2, Even Steven at ante 3)
charged $6 per fought round from ante 3 on; money was $-7..$8 for the rest of the run and every shop from ante 4 was
unaffordable (DNA $0 tag joker, Abstract, Jumbo Arcana, Celestial). With 0 xMult the engine was a single trick: a
spade Flush L2 played as the Dusk final hand (Chad x3 on the first card, Arrowhead +50/spade, Wrathful +3/spade).
It made 45k-71k per round from ante 4 to 6 and nothing else in the round mattered; 120k at ante 6 needs ~2x that.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 shop: Even Steven{ETERNAL,RENTAL} for $1 (SIM 0.87 vs 0.13). It did carry ante 3-4, but the second
   eternal rent made every later shop worthless. A Buffoon pack + keeping $3 would have kept ~$12 by ante 5,
   enough for a Jumbo Arcana (Justice/Chariot on spades). [INFERENCE] the SIM's 3-blind horizon cannot see the
   ante-5/6 cost of a permanent -$3/round.
2. Ante 2 Arrowhead{ETERNAL,RENTAL}: probably still right (0 scorers, p 0.55 vs 0.00), but it should have been the
   last rental: from then on, money for an xMult/Arcana pack outranks a second +mult rental.
3. Ante 4 shop: DNA ($0 from the Rare tag) was passed to keep Popcorn (+8) for the Manacle; the Manacle was won by
   30k, so Popcorn was not needed. DNA copying 1-card spade plays each round would have grown the spade engine.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you already pay rent on one ETERNAL RENTAL joker, THEN never buy a second one before ante 6, whatever the SIM
  says: two eternal rents (-$6/round) exceed fought-round income ($0-5 + hands) and every later shop is unaffordable.
- IF your money is negative or < $4 after rent (rent-broke) at ante 3+, THEN skip Small (and Big when its shop would
  also be unaffordable): skipped rounds charge no rent and no perish tick, tags still pay (Buffoon tag = free Mega
  Buffoon pack immediately; Rare tag joker needs $0+ and a free slot to take).
- IF you own Dusk (retrigger all played cards on the final hand), THEN hold the round's best hand for the final hand
  and spend the earlier hands as digs (High Card throws keep the made hand intact); the solver found this every time
  and the Dusk final was 1.4-2.5x the same hand played earlier (FH 1064 -> 1528, Flush 23358 -> ~66k).
- IF Hanging Chad + per-card suit jokers (Arrowhead/Wrathful/Smiley), THEN put the best suited card first in hand
  order: a single spade High Card scored 4888 (Chad x3 Arrowhead) - ideal for The Tooth ($1 per card played).
- IF the boss is The Ox and money is negative, THEN play your most-played hand: it sets money to $0 (a gain).
