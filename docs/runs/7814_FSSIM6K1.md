- Ante 1: Small 300 won (Pair QQ dig, then high-card dig, then diamond flush est 284 on the last hand). Shop: Buffoon pack Raised Fist ($4 -> $0). Big 450 won (Pair 88 est 572). Shop: Greedy Joker ($5). Boss The Psychic 600 won (Two Pair est 748-1100).
- Ante 2: Small 1000 won (Pair TT est 1760). Shop: Jumbo Buffoon, Sly Joker; Arcana pick Temperance; Mr. Bones ($5). Big 1500 won (Full House est 1677, $0 → $6). Shop: Ice Cream ($5, $1 left). Boss The Wall 4000 won (Pair 33 est 3652 + Two Pair 1656 on the last hand).
- Ante 3: Small 3200 won (Two Pair 1424, Straight 1620, Two Pair 860; two discards used on the flush/straight draw). Shop: Loyalty Card ($5), Spectral pack → Wraith → Blueprint (moved left of Loyalty). Big 4800 LOST on score: 516 + 687 + 1872 = 3075; Mr. Bones consumed at 25% (1200) and the run continued. Boss The Mark 6400: 288 / 412 / 528 on 3 face-down hands, GAME_OVER.

## Post-mortem

**Binding constraint:** economy starvation. Money sat at $0-$8 from ante 1 to ante 3 (end-of-shop balances: $0, $1, $0, $4, $1), so every shop bought one cheap perishable (Raised Fist, Greedy, Sly, Ice Cream) instead of a permanent scorer or xMult. Four of five jokers perished within 2-5 rounds. The only lasting scorers were Blueprint (a copy of Ice Cream) and Loyalty Card (x4 every 6 hands, hit once in ante 3). By ante 3 the Pair build projected ~1000-1300 per round against 3200-6400 blinds (ratio 0.07-0.21). Shop advisor said EMERGENCY from ante 2 onward.

**Decisions I would change:**
1. Ante 1 Buffoon pack: Raised Fist (perishable, 5 rounds) over a permanent option. Under a Gold economy a perishable scorer is a 2-3 blind rental, not an investment. Prefer the permanent pick (Jolly or any non-perishable +mult) whenever it scores within ~0.2 of the perishable pick.
2. Ante 2 shop: I overrode the advisor's Crazy Joker (Straight-gated) for an Arcana pack. The override was fine, but I then spent $5 on Mr. Bones (survive-once) instead of banking. Mr. Bones did save the Big 4800 round, which I count as a useful buy.
3. Ante 3 Spectral pack: I took Wraith (a random rare, sets money to $0 — I had $2) and it gave Blueprint, which had no joker on its right until I reordered. Taking Wraith at $2 cost $2 and gave a dead-slot joker. Check the joker to the right of Blueprint before buying it.
4. Ante 3 Small 3200: I spent the last discard on a straight draw (est 1620 landed). Correct at the time, but the run had no path to 4800 after it.
5. The biggest structural decision was ante 2: a Full House (est 1677) cleared Big 1500 with $0 in hand. Every cleared blind was won on a single lucky 4-5 card hand, with no xMult. The gap was never closed.

**Concrete reusable rules (if-then):**
- If money < $5 after ante 1 and the best pick is a perishable scorer, buy the permanent scorer instead, even at a lower est.
- If a perishable joker's est advantage over a permanent alternative is < 0.2x, take the permanent one.
- If you buy Blueprint, check the joker to its right; if none, REORDER before ending the shop.
- If shopadvise reports EMERGENCY at ante 2 with ratio < 0.25 (as here, ratio 0.27 at ante 2 and 0.07 at ante 3), then no single shop buy closes it; do not spend the last $ on a survive-once item (Mr. Bones) in hope of the next blind — it only buys one round.
- If the boss is The Mark (face cards face-down) and the next blind is ≥ 2x your typical hand, expect loss; prioritize a non-face Pair/Flush build before the boss.
- If Mr. Bones is held and the round score exceeds 25% of the blind but not the full blind, play the remaining hands for score: Mr. Bones consumes itself at the end, so do not discard away the score you already have.
- If the solver says UNWINNABLE, consider the final hands only for the 25% threshold (Mr. Bones) rather than the full score.
