### Key Decision Points

**Decision 1 - Boss Blind Engagement**: Should I have skipped the boss blind?
- Playbk says "Never skip INTO a boss you cannot beat" but also warns ante 1 boss is risky without 2 jokers
- I had only 1 joker (Wrathful) at ante 1, which was too little
- The shops in rounds 1 and 2 didn't offer good +mult/+chips jokers to buy a second one

**Decision 2 - Ante 1 Shop Strategy**: Should I have bought a booster pack for a second joker?
- Round 1 shop: Had Wrathful Joker $5 (bought), but no second joker available
- Round 2 shop: Had no jokers, only planets and tarots
- Following the playbook rule "never buy planets before owning 2 jokers", I ended the shop

**Binding Constraint**: Insufficient jokers - needed 2 by ante 1 boss, only had 1
- The Wrathful Joker was excellent for Spade hands (+3 mult/Spade gave 1387 on the big blind flush)
- But the boss blind required a different suit (Hearts were debuffed) and paired poorly with available hands
- Without a second +mult joker, the boss blind was mathematically unbeatable

### What Would I Change

**Single biggest decision change**: Buy Buffoon Pack ($4) in round 2 shop instead of ending early
- Buffoon Pack would have given a 50% chance of finding a second +mult joker in the booster cards
- Cost: $4 out of $10, leaving $6, enough to hold above interest cap
- The risk: even Buffoon wasn't guaranteed to have a joker, but it was better than ending with just 1
