## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: scaling/multiplier. Whole run was flat chips (Clever +80, Stuntman +250) on base mult 2-4 at L1 hands; no xMult, no +mult, no hand levels, money stayed $0-7 because the Eternal+Rental Clever drained $3 every round (and could not be sold), and Small Blind pays $0. Peak hand ~1300-1700; ante 3 Big needs 1600 per hand on all three.
Decisions I would change:
- Ante 1 shop: Buffoon pack at $4 left $1. The pack's only scorer was Eternal+Rental Clever. Alternatives (Sixth Sense) were worse, but I paid $4 + ~$3 per round for +80 chips on Two Pair only. Taking a forced Eternal Rental on an ante-1 pack is basically a -$9/ante tax that makes every later shop unaffordable.
- Never visited a shop with more than $7; no mult source ever appeared, and with $0-3 the advisor could only say LEAVE.
Reusable rules:
- If a Gold shop has a free ($0) Negative joker, buy it: extra slot costs nothing and sells for cash (Bootstraps negative sold for $6).
- If the Small Blind pays $0 (Gold) and the shop would be unaffordable anyway, skip it: saves a rental charge and a perishable tick.
- If you own flat +chips only (Stuntman, Clever) at ante 2-3, mult is the bottleneck: spend the first money on any +mult joker, not on more chips.
- If a flat +250 chips joker (Stuntman) is your only real scorer, expect hands ~560 (Pair) / 1300 (Flush) / 1700 (FH): 4800 at ante 3 Big is out of reach; need an xMult by ante 2.
