## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. Zero xMult in the whole run. xMult-shaped offers seen: Throwback{E} in the first ante-1
Buffoon pack (passed for Wrathful, correctly for survival), nothing else in 10 shops + 3 Buffoon packs. The build was
flat: Wrathful{E}, Odd Todd{E}, Abstract{E}, holo Drunkard, Ice Cream, Raised Fist; Two Pair L2. Money never exceeded
$14 (rent on Blue Joker and Drunkard took $15; the $0 Small Blinds and a $6 Jumbo Buffoon kept me at $0-6), so no
shop could hunt. Ante 5 Small needed ~8.3k per hand; my hands were 6-9k.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 Coupon shop: Grabber $10 (+1 hand every round) was passed to keep $10; that money went into Odd Todd and a
   whiffed reroll at ante 3. A 4th hand is +33% per round for the whole run and +$1 per round when unused; with a
   flat build and no xMult in sight it would have cleared the 648 gap here (and it cannot be bought later when broke).
2. Ante 2 Flint: the blue-seal card made Pluto because my LAST hand was a 1-card High Card finisher. Ending with a
   Two Pair (I had none) or playing the 1984 straight last would have given Uranus/Saturn.
3. Ante 4 Big shop: selling Popcorn (+8) and Pluto for a Jumbo Buffoon ($6) returned only Golden Joker, which I sold
   one shop later for Raised Fist. Net: -Popcorn, -$6. Keeping the $ for interest/next shop was probably equal.
4. Ante 5 Small: following the solver to break the made Q-Q-3-3 (n=64 lead) and then two more digs gave nothing; the
   rule's "keep Two Pair, draw 3" line was 0.44 vs 0.63 claimed. Small samples (n=64) overstate leads.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you hold a BLUE-SEAL card at round end, THEN make sure the round's LAST played hand is your main type: the planet
  is for the last hand played, not your most played (a High Card finisher gave Pluto).
- IF a Buffoon Tag sits on the Big Blind AND you are broke (<$5) AND a perishable scorer has 1 round left, THEN skip
  the Big: the Mega Buffoon pack opens immediately (2 jokers), the perishable plays the boss, and you save rent. Here
  it gave Popcorn + Ice Cream and the Tooth was one-shot 6700/6400 with a 2-card pair.
- IF you own Raised Fist, THEN hold your HIGHEST cards and play the low ones: Ace counts 14, so holding A-K-K gave
  +26 mult (2P 4433 est 8964) while holding a 5 gave +10.
- IF the shop offers a free (Coupon) Celestial + Standard + tarots, THEN take all of them; a glass blue-seal card from a
  Standard pack is a free recurring planet engine.
- IF you own no xMult at ante 2 and Grabber/Paint Brush is affordable, THEN buy the voucher over keeping $10: flat
  builds die by a few hundred chips (this run: 648), and an extra hand is the cheapest permanent +33%.
