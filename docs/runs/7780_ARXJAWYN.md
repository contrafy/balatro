LOST. No `est` vs actual deviation >20% occurred anywhere this run - the advisor's estimates were accurate throughout.

Post-mortem: Binding constraint was scaling, not misplay or bad luck on the hand math (all plays matched `est` closely). Two Pair leveled to only L3 by ante 2 boss, both jokers were flat +mult (Lusty +3/Heart, Wrathful +3/Spade, no xMult at all), and money was spent down to $0-3 every shop per shopadvise (never had a reroll-worthy pile at ante 1-2 per playbook rule anyway). Even without the suit-debuff disaster, three L3 Two Pair hands cap out around 900-1050, well under the 1600 ante-2 boss requirement - this build was not going to clear that boss regardless. The Goad specifically debuffing Spades was an added blow because half our jokers (Wrathful) and a large fraction of drawn cards were spades that round, gutting card chips further (final hands scored barely above their base joker-mult contribution).
The one decision I would change: taking Wrathful Joker (+3 mult/Spade) as our second joker in the ante-1 shop. With Lusty Joker (+3/Heart) already owned, adding a second suit-conditional joker meant two of our four suits (Hearts, Spades) were each a single boss-debuff away from being worthless, and a hand-type joker (Jolly/Mad/Zany/etc., unaffected by which suit shows up) would have been safer and still scored well with our Two Pair/Full House plays. No xMult joker was ever seen in either shop visit, so this loss cannot be blamed on skipping a reroll-for-xMult opportunity - none was offered at money levels the playbook allows spending down to in ante 1-2.
Bridge issues: one `ACTION FAILED: buy_from_shop returned false: no space or card not purchasable None` when buying the Uranus planet with both consumable slots full (2/2 tarots held) - not a bug, resolved immediately by `sellc 1` to free a slot then buying successfully. No other odd behavior; state printouts and `est` predictions were reliable all run.
Playbook suggestions:
1. Flag that stacking two suit-conditional +mult jokers (e.g. Lusty + Wrathful) is fragile: any suit-debuff boss (Club/Goad/Window/Head) neutralizes one joker's suit outright, and if that round's hand happens to be suit-heavy toward the debuffed suit, card chips collapse too. Recommend at most one suit-conditional joker unless a hand-type joker (Jolly/Mad/Zany/etc.) is also already owned as a suit-independent backup.
2. Add an explicit sanity check to section 3/7: at ante 2, a Two Pair/Full House capped at level 3 with only flat +mult jokers and 3 hands per round tops out near 900-1050 total, under the 1600 boss requirement - flag this arithmetic gap earlier (e.g. in shopadvise's "need" framing) so an agent knows by ante-2 shop that it is on pace to lose the boss, not just find out during the hand.
3. shopadvise could downweight buying a second suit-conditional joker when a suit-debuff boss is already visible for the CURRENT ante's "Upcoming boss" line, since it demonstrates such bosses are in play and a repeat is possible soon after.
