## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. One joker (Bootstraps, +2 mult per $5 held, worth only +2..+4 mult at $5-11) and no level on any hand I actually played; a normal hand was 200-460 and Big 1500 needed 500 a hand. Ante 1 was won on lucky flush deals (3 flushes in 6 hands), which hid how weak the engine was. Money was $1-11 all ante 1-2 (Small pays $0), so every shop was a choice between Bootstraps' mult and buying anything.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 shop ($11): I bought nothing that scored. Bloodstone ($7, conditional) and Gift Card were junk, but I never rerolled ($5) for a real joker: Bootstraps at $11 = +4 mult, at $6 = +2, so I protected a +2 mult difference instead of hunting a joker/xMult. With a single weak scorer and SCORE CHECK 0.27 (EMERGENCY), the advisor said REROLL and I overrode it for a Celestial (Earth, a level on a hand I did not even play). Probably should have rerolled once, or taken Saturn/Jupiter-type level for the hand I really played (Flush).
2. Skipping the ante-2 Small for the Rare tag: I removed a 1000 fight (est 1096 round) but then had to beat the 1500 Big with the same 1 joker and without the Small's shop; the Rare joker only arrives after the Big, so the skip bought nothing for the fight that killed me. With 1 scorer, PLAYBOOK says do not skip; I should have followed it (2 shop visits instead of 1).
3. Hand 1 of the Big: kept AA44 and discarded twice for a FH (p 0.33 then 0.28), both missed. The board also offered Flush draws later. A made Two Pair (200) banked first would not have cleared either; 1500 with this engine was ~35% from the start, the loss was decided in the shop.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own exactly one scorer and it is a conditional/money joker (Bootstraps, Bull, Gift) at ante 2 with SCORE CHECK < 0.35, THEN spend the money on rerolls for a second real +mult joker, not on planets for a hand you are not playing.
- IF Bootstraps is your only joker, THEN treat it as +2 mult per $5 and decide purchases by (mult lost) vs (score gained): a $4 pack that drops $11 -> $7 costs 2 mult on every hand (~25%).
- IF the Small Blind tag pays in the NEXT shop (Rare, Polychrome...) and you own 1 scorer, THEN do not skip: the Big between you and the tag is where runs die.
- IF a hand's est exactly equals the remaining need, THEN discard for margin first when you still have discards (est was exact here, but one miss = no second chance).
