## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling, from an economy that never left $0-9. Five scoring jokers (Misprint{E}, Supernova, Photograph,
Raised Fist, Abstract{PERISH,RENTAL}, Lusty) but zero reliable xMult in 8 shop views (none was offered; the only
xMult-shaped offer was Bloodstone{RENTAL}). Gold's $0 Small Blinds plus two skips meant I never had $10+ after a buy, so
I never earned interest and never rerolled. The 75th-pct hand was ~3k against an 18k boss (ratio 0.18); the Window round
reached 17.3k only because Misprint rolled high and Trips QQQ landed.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 pack: Smeared Joker (SIM 0.27) over Space Joker (0.19). Smeared did nothing for a pair build and was sold for $3
   at ante 2; Space Joker over ~25 hands played would have added ~6 levels to the hands I actually played. The SIM's
   3-blind horizon undervalues permanent leveling.
2. Ante 3 first shop: $12 on two Jumbo Celestials hoping for Uranus gave Trips L3, but I played Two Pair 9 times vs Trips 3.
   The Uranus that later showed up in a $3 shop slot was the better buy; one pack + keep $8 would have started interest.
3. Ante 2 skip of the Big Blind for Top-up was fine (Photograph + Raised Fist carried ante 3), but it cost $5 + a shop at
   the moment economy mattered most. Both together (skip + Celestial gambles) left me rent-broke at ante 4.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Raised Fist, THEN pick fillers so the lowest card you HOLD is as high as possible and NOT debuffed: play your
  lowest/debuffed cards as fillers. A debuffed lowest held card makes Raised Fist score 0 (The Club: 506 vs ~1000 est).
- IF you own Smeared Joker and the boss debuffs a suit (Club/Goad/Window/Head), THEN sell it before the boss (confirmed
  again: it spreads the debuff to the paired suit).
- IF a Buffoon pack offers Space Joker vs a utility joker (Smeared, Four Fingers, Showman) at ante 1-2 on a pair build,
  THEN take Space Joker: permanent levels on the hand you play beat a flush enabler.
- IF you are rent-broke at ante 4 ($1 with a rental) and your hand is under boss/3, THEN skipping Small+Big to fight only
  the boss with the Top-up joker was the max-probability line (lost by 4%); the real fix is not spending to $0-2 at ante 3.
- IF The Pillar is the boss, THEN clear Small/Big with your LOWEST cards (low red flush here) so high cards stay clean.
