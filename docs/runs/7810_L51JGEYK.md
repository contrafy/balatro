## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING (zero r/g xMult all run) compounded by MONEY (rental + perishable bridges kept cash at $1-7 for 3 antes, so I never could afford the $6-9 xMults/Buffoon packs that showed up). Ante 3 -> 4 is a 2.8x step in requirement; my best hand was ~2.5-3.4k and ante-4 Small needed 9000 (SCORE CHECK ratio was 0.09 entering the shop).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1-2 relied on three decaying/rented jokers (Green, Odd Todd rental, Gros Michel perishable). Each bridged one ante and left nothing permanent. At the ante-2 Big shop I had $4 and skipped a Celestial pack; at the ante-3 shop I had $7 and bought Constellation with no planets to feed it, then sold it 1 shop later for $3 (net -$3 and a wasted turn). If I had bought the $4 Celestial packs (or Uranus $3) in ante 2-3, Constellation would have grown. Buying a growth xMult I cannot feed is a waste: either feed it the same shop (planet pack) or take the flat +mult joker.
2. Green Joker: its -1 mult per discard wiped the stored mult twice on a 2-discard blind (mult 2 -> 0, 7 -> 5 -> ...). With 2 discards/round at Gold it nets ~+1/round. It was a bad $4 buy next to the Buffoon pack that same shop.
3. I cleared ante 1-3 mostly with Straights/Flushes/Full Houses (Odd Todd + Gros Michel + Summit boost them), not Pair builds, so the advisor's "Pair-family only" warning applied late: I had no planets leveling anything (all hands L1 all run) and no xMult.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF a boss forces 0 discards (The Water) THEN Mystic Summit's +15 mult is ALWAYS live that round (the advisor's note that The Water "disables Summit" is wrong; it scored Full House 3362 with Summit on); buy Summit before The Water.
- IF you skip a blind THEN rent and perish timers do not tick for that round: skipping Small froze Odd Todd/Gros Michel for a round and also avoided $3 rent; it is a legit move when your jokers perish soon and the skipped blind is not the one you can clear.
- IF you own a growth xMult that grows per planet (Constellation) and have <$10 for planets THEN do not buy it that shop; buy the planet/Celestial pack for your hand and the flat mult first.
- IF the advisor shows a ratio < 0.3 (best hand vs boss/3 next ante) with 0 xMult at ante 3, THEN the run is almost certainly dead at ante 4: spend ante 3 money on the best chance of an xMult (Buffoon packs) not on another flat bridge.
- IF you carry a perishable +mult joker (Gros Michel) THEN plan to replace it in the shop that follows the round it perishes, and keep $6+ ready for that shop (I arrived with $4-7 and a 1-round warning).
