## Post-mortem
Binding constraint: SCALING, specifically a single-joker engine (Shoot the Moon) whose multiplier lives in the HAND, not on the table. Every round was a dig for a Queen first and a hand second: 4 Queens in 52 cards means ~50% of deals have none, and two discards only raise that to ~65-75%. Antes 1-3 were cleared mostly because the Q showed up (and once by 20 chips under The Mouth). At ante 4 the Q never came and the whole build was worth 475 per hand. Behind it: 0 xMult, $0-3 at every shop from ante 2 on (income ~$10/ante with Small paying $0), only ONE xMult-shaped offer in 9 shop visits (Hologram never appeared; Space Joker was the closest) and Gold stickers on most jokers (Scary Face eternal+rental, Zany/Clever/Droll/Superposition/Gluttonous perishable).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 shop ($13): Judgement + reroll ($8 spent) bought Crafty (flush-only) and a 5-round Clever. Buying Space Joker ($5) in the next shop or keeping $8 for interest/a real joker would have been better than a perishable. More importantly, I never found a second unconditional engine; after ante 2 every purchase should have been planets for Two Pair/Pair (the hands I actually made) rather than Flush/HIGH/Trips levels spread over four types.
2. The Chariot on the Queen itself was a mistake in VALUE: a Steel card's x1.5 resolves before the same card's Shoot-the-Moon +13 (actual 2812 vs est 3774). Steel belongs on a NON-Queen card that sits to the RIGHT of the Queens in hand order (or just not on this build). Est should model it.
3. Ante 4 shop ($11): Abstract (+12 mult) + Jupiter was flat scaling on a build with no multiplier; a reroll hunting for any xMult was the only thing that could have saved ante 4-5. With $11 even one reroll ($6 left) was affordable.
4. Playing hands around a 50%-conditional joker: I should have treated Shoot the Moon as a bonus and built Two Pair levels (Uranus was offered twice: ante 2 shop at $3 when I had $2, ante 4 Jumbo Celestial where I took Jupiter). Two Pair L3 + Clever would have been a reliable 2500-3000 per hand with no Q.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF your only +mult source is "per card HELD in hand" (Shoot the Moon, Raised Fist, Baron...), THEN it is a 50% joker: count every round as "no multiplier" when planning shops, buy an unconditional scorer (+mult/+chips on your hand type) before anything else, and level the hand you can make WITHOUT the held card.
- IF you own Shoot the Moon, THEN a HIGH card + 3-4 junk fillers with a Queen held is a real play at ante 1-2 (HIGH L1 with QQ held = 405, Pair = 840): spend early hands on fillers to draw 4-5 cards while keeping the Queens.
- IF a Steel card is a card that also triggers a held-card joker (Steel Queen + Shoot the Moon), THEN its x1.5 fires BEFORE that joker's +mult for the same card; put plain Queens LEFT of the steel one in hand order (`move hand`), and prefer Steel on a non-Queen card placed rightmost.
- IF the Gold Small Blind tag is Negative/Holographic/Foil/Polychrome and your wallet is under $4, THEN skip it: the Small shop buys nothing, the free edition joker arrives after the Big, and perishable tickers skip a round. (Worked twice here: Negative Smiley Face, kept Clever alive for the ante-3 boss.)
- IF The Mouth is the boss and your best made hand is within ~5% of the requirement with a Queen held, THEN still lock Two Pair with it: any Two Pair (even 250 without the Queen) finishes the blind on the next hand.
- IF ante 4 starts with 0 xMult and under $12, THEN reroll once for an xMult before buying flat jokers/packs; a flat +12 and a planet (what I bought) raised per-hand output ~40%, the Big Blind needed ~300%.
