## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no real scorer (no +mult, no xMult) by ante 2 - the whole run was chips-only jokers (Scary Face, Ice Cream) on a base mult of 2-4 and $0 income (Small pays $0, rent ate the Big payout). Typical hand ~250-650 vs Big 1500. The only forward chance was a joker shop that never offered a +mult joker (shops: Reserved Parking{P}, Earth, Baron $8 unaffordable at $1, Pluto, Ice Cream).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 Buffoon pack pick: Abstract Joker{ETERNAL,RENTAL} (+3 mult per joker, i.e. the only +mult option) vs Scary Face{RENTAL} (+30 chips on face scorers). Advisor barred Abstract; Scary Face scored on faces only and the rent left me at $1 after the Big, unable to buy Baron $8. Hindsight: a flat +mult (even rental-eternal) was the only thing that could scale; but I still think barring an unsellable -$3/round at $0 was reasonable. Marginal.
2. Ante 1 shop: 291/300 flush on the Small left no money (Small pays $0). Unavoidable.
3. Ante 2 Small: I skipped it for the Uncommon tag, which only pays after the Big; I had to clear 1500 with Ice Cream first. Sim-wise one fewer blind to clear, but I never reached the Uncommon shop. Playing the 1000 Small would have faced the same 1500 Big anyway; the loss was driven by the build, not the skip.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the ante-1 shop leaves you at $0-$4 with 0 scorers AND the Buffoon pack offers a rental +mult joker (Abstract) and a rental chips-on-faces joker (Scary Face), THEN prefer the +mult one only if it is not Eternal; with Eternal rental both are bad - take the sellable one and sell it as soon as rent threatens the next shop.
- IF rent would drop you below $4 at the cash-out and a better joker costs $8 next shop, THEN sell the rental before the end of the blind round that gets you there (rent is charged at round end, before cash-out).
- IF you own only chips jokers at ante 2 (est 250-650 per hand), THEN the Big 1500 needs 2-3 flush-level hands; discard hard for a flush on the first hand and keep a pair floor.
- A debuffed card (The Head) still completes a Straight/Pair: a Broadway straight with one debuffed Ten scored 644 vs est 644 (the est is exact).
