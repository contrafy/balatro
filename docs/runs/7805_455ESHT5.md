## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: no permanent +mult scorer ever offered. In 7 shop views (+1 reroll, 1 Buffoon pack, 3 free Celestials) the
joker offers were Gros Michel{P,RENTAL}, Luchador{P}, Astronomer{E}/Crafty{P} (pack), Reserved Parking{E}, Pareidolia{P},
Scholar, Blackboard{P,RENTAL}, Trading Card{P}, To the Moon, Superposition{P}, DNA{E}. The only any-hand mult was Blackboard
(x3, conditional, perishable rental) on a base of foil +50 chips and Scholar (Aces only). Every hand was "play the red cards, hold
black ones", so the hand type was dictated by colour, not by rank; at ante 3 that capped hands at ~1.4-2.3k (need 1.6k/hand).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 Buffoon pick: Astronomer (eternal utility; +50 chips foil) gave 3 free Celestials (Two Pair L1->L4) but no mult.
   The alternative (Crafty{P}) was no better; the real mistake is structural: with $5 at the first shop, the pack was a
   1-in-2 gamble. Nothing better was available, so I would repeat it.
2. Ante 3 Small skip: I overrode `s` ("do not skip") because the Small (3200 vs ~2.9k round) was itself risky and the skip
   kept Blackboard alive. In hindsight the Big (4800) was the harder fight; playing the Small would have given one more
   shop ($6 + interest) but Blackboard would have perished before the Mouth. Both lines were likely losing; the skip kept the
   only xMult for one more blind and that blind was lost by 600.
3. Ante 2 Window: I chose High AS x3 (1290) over the advisor's Two Pair (1062) for margin. Correct, but it shows the advisor
   prefers "the hand you are leveling" even when a different type clears by more.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF Blackboard is your only multiplier, THEN choose every play by colour: play all red cards as fillers (up to 5 cards),
  keep only Spades/Clubs in hand; discard red cards first. A single scoring Ace with Scholar + 2-4 red fillers made 1290
  at ante 2, more than any Two Pair L2.
- IF you own Astronomer, THEN every Celestial pack and planet is free: open each one and stack your main hand (here Two Pair
  L1->L4 by ante 3 for $0). It is a level engine, not a scorer: still buy +mult jokers first.
- IF SCORE CHECK < 0.2 at the ante-3 shop with no +mult joker on offer and < $10, THEN the run is likely lost at ante 3;
  the only levers are the Small-skip tag (Uncommon/Rare joker) and keeping the perishable xMult alive for the boss.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- advise prefers "the hand you are leveling" over a bigger clearing hand (Window: Two Pair 1062 vs High AS 1290 with
  Blackboard x3; Small: Pair 480 vs High AD 1290). Harmless when both clear, but it banks less.
- My own error: I issued `endshop` and `blind` as parallel calls once; the CLI refused `blind` (NOT LEGAL in SHOP, nothing
  sent), so no junk action was recorded.
