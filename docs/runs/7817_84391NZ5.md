## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling, caused by money. I never held more than $10, never saw an xMult I could afford (none was
offered at all in 6 shops + 4 packs except The Tribe at ante 1), and the build's real multiplier was Seltzer, a
10-hand temporary retrigger. With Seltzer, face Two Pairs made 3.8-5.2k at ante 3; without it the same build made
~2k (2P 8855 1976 included Seltzer's last charge; JJJ55 FH without it 5920). The ante-4 Big (13500) needed ~4.5k per
hand from a 0-xMult, L1-hands build: flat Smiley/Clever/Gluttonous/Zany/Supernova tops ~6k on a face Full House.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 3 shop ($10): Mega Buffoon $8 gave Gluttonous{E} (+3 per club, ~1 club per hand) and a perishable Zany. Sly{E}
   $3 + keeping $7 for the next shop (then $13+ with interest) would have kept an xMult affordable. A Mega Buffoon at
   $8 that leaves $2 is only worth it when it can still buy you a scorer you lack; I already had 3 scorers.
2. Seltzer accounting: I counted Seltzer as a scorer through ante 3 although it had 5 hands left; I should have planned
   ante 4 as if it did not exist (it is a perishable with a hand counter) and bought permanent mult in ante 3.
3. Ante 4 skips: skipping the ante-4 Small (broke, Charm Mega Arcana) cost a shop view; the Arcana gave Justice on a card
   that was never drawn and Emperor -> Lovers/Hermit. Judgement after selling the 1-hand Seltzer (a permanent joker for a
   joker that was about to expire) was the better second pick.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF a joker is temporary by hands (Seltzer 10 hands, Ice Cream, Popcorn), THEN count its remaining hands like a perish
  tally: with <= 3 hands left it is NOT a scorer for the next ante; buy its permanent replacement while it still carries.
- IF you own Seltzer + Smiley, THEN play faces: High Card K/Q (275 at ante 1) and face Two Pair (5k at ante 3) are the
  engine; Seltzer doubles card-phase +mult (Smiley/Gluttonous) as well as chips.
- IF a pack offers Judgement and your slots are full only because of an expiring joker (1-2 hands/rounds left), THEN sell
  the expiring joker and take Judgement: a permanent random joker beats the last charge.
- IF The Wheel shows a face-down card and the rank sort bounds it (e.g. between QD and JC), THEN it is a J or Q: keep it
  as part of a guaranteed face pair; play it as the 5th card of a Two Pair (it can only upgrade the hand).
- IF you are at ante 3 with 0 xMult and <= $10, THEN keep the money floor over a third/fourth flat joker: the next
  xMult costs $6-9 and you need it by ante 4.
