## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING, caused by MONEY. Total cash earned in the run was ~$30 ($0 Small Blinds, I skipped all three Smalls anyway, $4-5 per Big/Boss, -$3/round Half Joker rent, interest never reached $5). Every shop had $4-10, so the build at ante 4 was a foil Raised Fist (+20 mult only when the 3 held cards are all T+), a perished Odd Todd, Devious (+100 straight) and a fresh Clever (+80 two pair), with Two Pair/Trips at L2 and zero xMult. That is a ~2000-4000 per-hand engine against 13500. Ante 3 was cleared by 57 chips only because of Raised Fist hand-management; ante 4 Big needed three ~4500 hands and I had draws for one.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 Buffoon pick: Half Joker (rental+perishable) did carry antes 1-3 (every blind cleared), but it cost $1 + $9 rent and was dead at the ante-3 boss. With $6 and no alternative scorer that was still right; the mistake was downstream: not converting its window into a permanent engine. The ante-2 Big shop ($6, Raised Fist free) and the ante-3 Small shop ($10) were the only shops with money, and I spent $6 of the $10 on a Jumbo Arcana (Emperor -> Judgement -> Devious Joker, a dud). A $3 Uranus + keeping $7 for the next shop (Clever/Banner would have been affordable one shop earlier) was the better scaling line; Arcana gambles with $10 total are too thin at Gold.
2. Ante 3 boss shop: Jumbo Arcana again ($6 of $6) -> Justice on a Queen. Glass x2 fires at CARD-SCORING time, BEFORE +mult jokers are added, so with a +mult build (Raised Fist +20) Glass only doubles the hand's base mult (3 -> 6), not 23 -> 46. est showed it: Pair Q with Glass 1600 vs 1440 without. Glass is worth ~x1.1 on a +mult build; it is only x2 on a build whose mult is already in the base/levels. I should have left with $6 toward the ante-4 shop (Jumbo Buffoon $6 was offered there and unaffordable at $5).
3. Skipping all three Small Blinds. Each skip was locally right (rent, perish ticks, Meteor/Foil tags, $0 payout) and the Meteor skip delivered 2 levels; but three skipped shops meant the first ante-4 blind was the 13500 Big with the same jokers I had at ante 3. At Gold the Small-skip math should include "one fewer shop visit to hit the 2% xMult offer".

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Raised Fist, THEN every play must be 5 cards so exactly 3 cards stay in hand, and those 3 must all be rank T or higher (T/J/Q/K/A -> +20 mult, A alone lowest -> +22). A single low held card kills it (held 2 = +4). Pick fillers from the LOW cards; never keep a low card "for later". Verified all run: est 2698 (held QD JD 8S) vs 426 (held 5H debuffed) for the same Two Pair.
- IF a suit-debuff boss (The Head/Club/Window/Goad) is up and you own Raised Fist, THEN a DEBUFFED card that is your lowest held card gives +0 (game source: Raised Fist returns "Debuffed" for a debuffed raised card). Play debuffed cards as fillers so the lowest held card is a clean one.
- IF your mult comes from +mult jokers (Raised Fist, Jolly, Mad, Half Joker...), THEN Glass (Justice) is worth only ~x1.1-1.3 because its x2 applies to the base mult before jokers add theirs; buy Glass only when your mult is mostly hand levels or when you already have xMult jokers (which multiply after it). Prefer the planet.
- IF Odd Todd is owned, THEN with a +20-ish mult a single odd High Card (A/9/7/5/3 + 4 junk fillers) is worth ~2000 (chips 5+rank+31+50 x 21). Under 6400 that is a legitimate hand; `hands` only lists it when no pair exists, so check `sel <odd card> + fillers` yourself.
- IF a Lucky card (Magician) is in a scoring slot on a hand you must play anyway, THEN est is the floor: 1 in 5 gives +20 mult, which on a 21-mult hand nearly doubles it (1953 -> 3813 here). Use Magician on the odd/main-rank cards you score most, not on holders.
- IF Gold ante 4 is next and you have 0 xMult and main hand at L2, THEN the ante-3 shops must buy LEVELS or a permanent +mult joker with the money you have, not $6 Arcana packs; a tarot pack at $10 total cash left me with Devious Joker and a Glass Queen for $12.
- IF the previous blind's shop had a $6 Jumbo Buffoon and you leave with $5, THEN you lost it to the $1 overspend somewhere earlier; keep $6 through ante 3-4 boss shops when the next shop may show a Buffoon pack (the cheapest sticker-discounted joker source at Gold).

Advisor/CLI defects:
- `advise`/`hands` est for Raised Fist uses the CURRENT held set, so a 2-card Pair shows est 612 (holding 6 cards, lowest 2) while the same pair + 3 low fillers (holding 3 faces) is 2244. `advise` never proposes the filler version and told me to discard to a straight instead ("disc 1 6 7 8") at The Head when the two-pair-plus-filler line was already made. It should search filler sets for Raised Fist.
- `advise` at The Head told me to keep 2S (lowest card) as one of the 3 kept cards ("keep the 3 highest cards" kept QD 9C 2S); with Raised Fist the kept set should be the highest non-debuffed cards, and debuffed hearts must be dumped first (it did say that part).
- Pack `advise` for Mega Celestial said "pick 1" (Pluto, High Card) when Uranus/Venus (Two Pair/Trips, my actual 5-card plays) were in the pack; main hand "Pair" was inferred from 2-card plays forced by Half Joker.
- `shopadvise` ranked a $6 Jumbo Arcana at 62 with $6-10 total cash and 0 xMult at ante 3 twice; both were duds. At Gold with under $12 a Jumbo Arcana should rank below the main-hand planet and below keeping $6 for a Buffoon pack.
- `s` at ante-4 blind select advised playing the Small ("D6 Tag not worth a shop visit") with $1 cash and Odd Todd at 1 round left; the shop was worthless and the skip kept the perishable alive for the Big. Skip advice should count "perishable survives one more blind" and "shop has < $4".
- Bridge: no failures; every action settled on first try. est was exact on every non-Lucky hand (328, 440, 352, 660, 484, 3588, 2496, 2256, 2425, 1872, 1995, 2698, 1764, 3894, 1760).
