Post-mortem: binding constraint = misplay/build risk, not money or boss. The Wily Joker + The Trio combo only scores when a Three-of-a-Kind-or-better forms; on a hand with only two-pair draws (no trips) they contribute nothing, so this ante-2 Big Blind had effectively ZERO joker support once no trips appeared (only base Two Pair L1 chips/mult, no Lusty hearts either). The one decision I would change: at the ante-2 shop, with only 1 scoring-conditional joker family (all Trips-gated) and $4, I should have prioritized keeping a discard-safety margin or accepted the Droll/other alternatives more carefully - but more fundamentally, the deck lacked ANY unconditional +mult/+chips joker (Joker+4, Scholar, Smiley, etc.) by ante 2, so a pure-pair hand with no trips draw had no scoring backup. In hindsight, buying an unconditional flat joker at ante 1 (if one had appeared) instead of leaning entirely on Wily+Trio (both Trips-gated) would have given a chip/mult floor for hands that never form Trips.

Bridge issues: none. All `advise`/`shopadvise`/`play`/`disc` calls returned cleanly; no ACTION FAILED or retry needed the entire run. `pick` printed a useful synergy/mismatch warning when picking Earth over the CLI's own "main hand" label (Two Pair) - this is good UX, not a bug.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Section 5 (Ante 1-2 buying order) should flag when BOTH scoring jokers held are Three-of-a-Kind-gated (Wily, The Trio, Mad, Zany, Clever) with no unconditional flat +mult/+chips joker in the mix: such a build scores nothing on a plain Pair/Two-Pair hand, which is common when trips do not materialize before discards run out. Recommend keeping at least one unconditional joker (Joker+4, Scholar, Smiley, Half Joker) as a floor even after acquiring a conditional xMult/PLUS joker.
2. `shopadvise`'s top pick occasionally recommends a joker (Droll, Flush-gated) that does not fit the already-declared "main hand" for the run (Full House/Two Pair here) while its own printed ranking list shows a pack scored higher (76 vs 30) - worth checking why ADVICE didn't follow the printed top rank; document the override criterion (pack score vs joker score) so future agents know when it is safe to override.
3. Consider surfacing "0 unconditional scoring jokers" as an explicit warning in `s`/`advise` output at ante 2, similar to the "debuffed cards" and "main hand" warnings already present, since a Trips-only or Flush-only joker set can silently strand a Pair/Two-Pair-only hand.
