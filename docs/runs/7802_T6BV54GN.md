## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING, caused by never owning a permanent multiplier and an engine built from bridges. Of 9 jokers owned,
only Clever was permanent; Lusty (rental), Smeared, Even Steven, Popcorn, Bootstraps, Stuntman, Photograph were perishable or
rental. Mult was the bottleneck all run: Stuntman made chips ~400 per hand, but mult was 2 (base) + Popcorn (20 -> 4, decaying)
+ Bootstraps (2 per $5 held). In 11 shop views I saw exactly two xMult-shaped jokers: Blackboard{PERISH,RENTAL} and Baron{ETERNAL}
(no kings held with a 6-card hand). Ante 5's 75th-pct hand was ~3.4k against a 50000 boss, so the run was decided before the
Serpent. The Small blinds paid $0, and I skipped 4 of 5 of them, which saved rent and perish ticks but also cost shop views.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 Lusty{RENTAL}: -$9 of rent over 3 rounds when I had $0-6 led to the rent-broke Small skip at ante 2 and an empty ante-2
   economy. Lusty was the advised buy (SIM 0.74), but my first permanent scorer arrived only at ante 3 anyway.
2. Ante 4 Big shop ($22): Grabber $10 got me through the Arm (4 hands, cleared by about 1.9k) but left nothing for ante 5. With
   Bootstraps, holding money was mult. Still probably right: 3 hands x ~5k could not have cleared 18000.
3. Ante 5: a misclick (`buy 1` after a reroll bought The Empress, not the Credit Card I meant) cost $3, and Photograph for $5
   took Bootstraps from +4 to +0. Net about +0 mult, money wasted. With Bootstraps owned, a buy that drops you below a $5
   step costs 2 mult per step.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Bootstraps, THEN money is mult (+2 per $5): a buy is only worth it if it adds more mult than the $5 steps it
  crosses. Spend only the excess above the last multiple of 5 ($19 -> $15 is free; $15 -> $14 costs 2 mult).
- IF your mult is mostly flat jokers that will perish within the ante (Popcorn, Bootstraps, Stuntman), THEN by ante 4 you are in
  EMERGENCY even when you clear blinds: list what stays alive at the next boss and buy for that.
- IF Handy Tag is on the Small Blind at ante 3+, THEN skip it: it pays $1 per hand played this run immediately (13 hands = $13
  here), more than the Small Blind's $0 payout plus its shop.
- IF the boss is The Arm and your hands are all L1, THEN the Arm has no effect: play your best type every time. Do not buy the
  main-hand planet in the shop just before it.
- IF you own Smeared Joker and the boss debuffs a suit (Window/Head/Club/Goad), THEN sell Smeared before the boss: Smeared makes
  the paired suit count as the debuffed one too.
- IF you rerolled the shop, THEN run `s` and re-read the slot numbers before `buy N`: slot 1 is a different card now.
- IF at ante 5 your hand is under boss/3 and there is no xMult, THEN skipping Small+Big to fight only the boss with every
  bridge alive (Juggle +3 hand size, no perish/decay ticks) is the max-probability line, but it is still about 0: the fix is
  earlier (ante 2-3 Buffoon packs for a permanent scorer).

Advisor/CLI defects:
- After a shop reroll, `buy 1` sent slot 1 of the NEW shop (The Empress) with no warning. The pack `pick` path has a
  name-echo/remap guard; `buy`/`pack` should echo the item name too.
- shopadvise ranked Uranus (main-hand planet, 98) as the top buy with The Arm as the next blind; the Arm takes the level back
  the first time you play the hand. Its own SIM ranked Uranus below buying nothing (0.45 vs 0.54).
- shopadvise does not value Grabber/vouchers in its SIM; with the Arm at 0.87x projected, +1 hand was the deciding buy.
- The SIM did see Bootstraps' money-as-mult (baseline beat every purchase at ante 4), but the rule ADVICE still said BUY.
- Pack advisor SIM chose Blackboard{PERISH,RENTAL} (0.07) over a permanent Clever (0.00) at ante 3 because both were below
  0.1 on a 3-blind horizon. When every option is near 0, it should fall back to rule score or score ratio.
- `s` at the ante-3/4 blind select said "do not skip" for an Uncommon/Handy tag even with $0 money (its shop could buy
  nothing) and the Handy Tag worth $13 immediately.
- 1 bridge glitch: `blind` right after `endshop` returned "NOT LEGAL NOW: SELECT_BLIND is not available in SHOP" although the
  printed state was BLIND_SELECT; it worked after `s`.
