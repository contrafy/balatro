## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling from ante 6 on (75th pct hand ~25-30k vs 60k Small). Solved by NOT playing Small/Big blinds at
ante 5-8: 9 skips total made Throwback x3.75, skip tags paid $20 (Skip) + $36 (Garbage x2) + Boss Tag (rerolled The Wall
100k into The Psychic 50k) + Coupon/Buffoon packs, and every skipped blind was one fewer 2-3-big-hand fight. From ante 5
I only fought bosses. Final build: Clever{E} + Stuntman{E} (+250 chips) + Abstract{RENTAL} (+18 mult) + Even Steven{E} +
foil Obelisk (x3.6) + Throwback (x3.75), Palette + Paint Brush + Grabber (8 cards, 4 hands). Flat chips/mult x two growth
xMults made the hand TYPE almost irrelevant, which is exactly what Obelisk needs.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 + ante 6: two Chariots put Steel on junk cards. Steel x1.5 multiplies only the base+card mult accumulated BEFORE
   the +mult jokers (KKJJ: 2 x 1.5 = 3, then Abstract +18), so in a flat +mult build it adds ~2-10%. The Emperor/Hermit would
   have been worth more.
2. Ante 6 shop: 2 rerolls ($11) found nothing; with the skip plan that money was better kept for the ante-8 Grabber/Director's Cut.
3. I only learned the exact Obelisk rule at ante 5 (see rules); a High Card at the Psychic was close to resetting it.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Throwback AND your best hand clears the boss of this ante with margin at the current x, THEN skip Small AND Big
  from ante 5 on: each skip is a permanent +x0.25, the tags pay now, and you face one blind per ante instead of three.
  Big-blind tags matter: Boss Tag = reroll a Wall/Violet Vessel; Garbage/Skip/Economy = money for the post-boss shop.
- IF you own Obelisk, THEN a play RESETS it only when that type's play count (after +1) is strictly greater than every
  other type's count; ties keep it growing. Track counts (raw `run.hands_played_total`, `run.most_played_poker_hand`) and
  pick types below the max: Straight/Two Pair/FH/Flush over Pair/High Card. Never take a "1-card throwaway" when High
  Card is your most played type.
- IF Crimson Heart disables a joker that the current best hand does not use (Clever on a Straight, Even Steven on KKJJ),
  THEN play the big hand NOW; spend discards first (they do not re-roll the disabled joker).
- IF The Mouth locked a type you cannot currently make AND 2 hands remain, THEN play a junk hand (scores 0) to cycle
  cards for the last hand instead of giving up; it found the 5th club here.
- Abstract Joker counts debuffed/disabled jokers (verified 3 times: 110880, 83002, 41817); `est` under Crimson Heart is
  ~15% low because it drops the disabled joker from Abstract's count.
- Steel cards x1.5 apply before joker +mult: low value with Abstract/Even Steven-type flat mult builds.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- shopadvise ranked Raised Fist{ETERNAL,RENTAL} BUY at ante 1 ("NEEDED NOW") although it is unsellable rent for 22 rounds
  (-$66); an eternal rental should never be a BUY when you own 1 scorer.
- shopadvise marked foil Obelisk (free) AVOID ("penalizes playing your main hand"); with flat chips/+mult it was the
  run-winning xMult (x3.6). It should evaluate Obelisk by how many non-max types the build can play.
- advise flush card choice ignores Even Steven: picked AD QD 8D 7D 6D (68425) / JD TD 9D 7D 4D (105456) over the
  3-even versions (76520 / 117117) that `sel` showed.
- advise said "Obelisk resets: this is your most played hand" for Pair/Straight when High Card was the strict max (the
  ests themselves assumed growth); and the Crimson Heart throwaway advice (`play HIGH 9`) would have reset Obelisk 3.2 -> 1
  and played the steel card.
- The Mouth: `advise` printed "NO VALID PLAY ... blind is likely lost" with 2 hands left; a 0-score junk play to cycle
  cards was the winning line.
- Under Crimson Heart `est` excludes the disabled joker from Abstract's count (FH est 36590, actual 41817).
- At ante 5+ `s` says "do not skip" even with Throwback owned; skip value should include Throwback's +0.25.
