## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: ZERO scoring jokers. Four Buffoon-pack/shop buys across two antes produced only Splash, Drunkard (both non-scorers) and a Celestial; the only damage came from flat chips x base mult (Flush 320, Straight 728 with L2) against a 1000 Small Blind at ante 2. Luck decided the pack contents (Bootstraps rental, Splash, Drunkard eternal, DelayedGratification perishable) but the key choice was the ante 2 shop: spending $6 on a Celestial pack with $2 left instead of finding a scorer.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 shop ($8): bought Jumbo Celestial (Saturn, L2 straight) with no scoring joker. Alternatives: Devious Joker $4 (+100 chips on Straight; with Saturn that is (60+44+100)x7 = ~1400, would have cleared the 1000 Small on the straight I did hit!) plus keeping $4. I passed it because "perishable, Straight-only" and the pack came first; I only sold nothing. Rule below.
2. Ante 1: could have taken the Rare tag by skipping Small (a Rare joker in the next shop). I played the Small as the playbook says (no skip with 0 jokers) and won it with a lucky flush, but I arrived at ante 2 with no scorer anyway.
3. Ante 2 Small: with the straight draw I discarded 3 times (keeping 2 pair first, then a gutshot straight). The solver said ~0.5-0.6; the real answer was the blind was already unreachable without a joker.

Reusable if-then rules:
- IF you own no mult/xMult scorer at ante 2 AND a joker that doubles a hand type you can actually hit is in the shop (Devious +100 chips on Straight, Crafty/Droll flush, Sly/Jolly pair), THEN buy that joker before any Celestial/Arcana pack; a pack's one planet cannot replace a joker ((60+44)x7=728 vs ~1400 with Devious).
- IF you are at ante 1 with 2 or fewer non-scorers after two Buffoon packs, THEN the next shop's first $4 must be a scorer, not a planet.
- IF Splash is your only joker and you play flush/straight (all 5 cards already score), THEN Splash adds nothing; do not pick it over a scorer, and prefer Delayed Gratification/Drunkard only if no scorer.
- IF a hand needs 1000 and you have no mult joker, a made Flush L1 (320) / Straight L2 (728) cannot clear in 3 hands; skip the Small (tag D6 etc) if it saves the fight, or the shop after it still would not help at $2.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- `shopadvise` at ante 2 ($8) listed Devious Joker 35 pts as "adds ~0 now: grows only with its condition" and ranked REROLL first; after Saturn L2 the estimated straight with Devious would have been ~1400. It does not model buying a joker AND combining with a planet pick; SIM did say Devious was better (0.63 vs 0.44) and was overridden by rules. I overrode toward the Celestial pack; the advisor itself recommended reroll (would have left $3), also bad.
- `advise` solver p values at n=100 (0.5-0.63) were far above reality for the Small 1000 with no jokers (it lost); the exp column (~900-1000) mixes sims that include lucky straights.
- Pack advisor ranked Bootstraps (+2 mult per $5) as 30 "NEEDED NOW" though with $2 it adds +0 and rent takes $3/round.
