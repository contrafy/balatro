# LOSS at ante 5 boss (The Wall, 44000 needed, scored 9224)

Post-mortem: Binding constraint was a combination of an unusually large boss requirement (The Wall's x2 chips doubled the ante-5 boss target to 44000, roughly double what the playbook table implies) and running out of discards too early in that same round. I spent all 3 discards chasing a bigger Full House on the FIRST hand of the boss round (going from JJJQQ est 3216 -> 3144 -> QQQJJJ est 6912, a modest gain of ~3700 for the cost of both remaining discards), which left hands 2 and 3 with zero discards and no way to recover from a cold draw (no pair, no flush, no straight on the final hand). The decision I would change: once a Full House was already made on the boss round's first hand with 2 hands still to come, I should have registered it immediately (accepting est 3216 for the Card Sharp x3 setup) rather than spending both remaining discards trying to upgrade it further - that would have left at least 1 discard in reserve to rescue hand 2 or hand 3 from a dead draw, and the Card Sharp x3 bonus (3x on the SECOND full house) would have applied one hand earlier for a larger total.
Bridge issues: One `cash` command returned "ACTION FAILED: Round eval blocking events still pending, retry shortly" after the Big Blind round of ante 5; retried after `sleep 2` per playbook and it succeeded. No other CLI issues encountered this run.
Playbook suggestions:
1. The Wall boss entry in section 6 should note it can double the blind's own chip REQUIREMENT (not just an in-hand chip multiplier) - this run's ante-5 boss needed 44000, double the table's 22000. Worth flagging so agents budget discards/hands more conservatively going into it.
2. Add explicit guidance: when a made hand (e.g. Full House) is already strong enough to register for Card Sharp/xMult AND 2+ hands remain in the round, prefer registering it immediately over spending multiple discards chasing a marginal upgrade to the SAME hand type - the x3 multiplier on the next occurrence is usually worth far more than a same-round card upgrade, and preserves discards as a safety net for later cold draws.
