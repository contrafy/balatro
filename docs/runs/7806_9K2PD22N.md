## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. The whole engine was two xMults that only fire on some hands (Acrobat x3 on the final hand,
Card Sharp x3 on a repeated type) plus a perishable rental Baseball Card (x1.5 per uncommon = x3.4) that carried
antes 5-7. Flat mult was Mystic Summit +15, foil Joker +4/+50 chips and Supernova. No always-on xMult was offered in
~20 shop views from ante 4 to 7 (13 paid rerolls). The ceiling per round was ~4 Two Pairs = x1 + x3 + x3 + x9 = 16 units of
~11k = ~180k; ante 7 Big needed 165k (4 Two Pairs in one round) and the Ox 220k was out of reach.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 shop 2: I rerolled with Gros Michel (+15 mult, $5) on the shelf; the reroll removed it. Buy the scorer first,
   reroll after.
2. Ante 3-4: I spent ~$40 on rerolls/Celestials over three shops looking for xMults and kept $15-20; Card Sharp came
   from one of those rerolls, so that part was right, but Mega Celestial planets went to Two Pair/Trips when High Card
   ended up the most reliable Card Sharp type. Level the type you will REPEAT (High Card / Pair), not the "main hand".
3. Ante 6: skipping Small AND Big for the House (one fight, 2x Investment = $50, Baseball kept alive) was right and
   won the House 126k/120k; but at ante 7 the $55 had nothing to buy. With Card Sharp + Acrobat, Director's Cut or more
   hand size (Juggler, Paint Brush) would have added a Card Sharp x3 hand or more pair draws.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Card Sharp AND Acrobat, THEN the round is x1 + x3 + x3 + x9 on one repeated type: with Mystic Summit burn
  both discards first, then play three or four 1-card HIGH CARDS (always available, deterministic). It cleared ante 5
  Small/Big/Pillar (39k, 58k, 68k) and plays only 3 cards, which is ideal before The Pillar.
- IF two Two Pairs are reachable (one now, one held for the end), THEN Two Pair x1, High x1, High x3, Two Pair x9 beats
  the pure High Card line by ~40% (126k vs 89k at ante 6-7). The final hand must be the strongest repeated type.
- IF you own Baseball Card, THEN order flat +mult jokers LEFT of the uncommon ones (its x1.5 fires right after each
  uncommon joker), and value any uncommon joker as x1.5 while it lives.
- IF The House (face-down first hand), THEN `sort rank` and discard the 5 lowest face-downs: the auto-sorted hand then
  bounds each remaining face-down card between its visible neighbours (here K..Q = a guaranteed pair partner).
- IF a perishable xMult carrier has 2-3 rounds left and your round cannot beat a blind, THEN skip that Small/Big so the
  carrier lives for the fight you can win (ante 6: skipped both, cleared the House with Baseball still alive).
- IF your best possible round (every hand perfect) is below the boss, THEN do NOT skip the Big for a "perishable carrier"
  plan: play the blinds you can still win and use the shops.
