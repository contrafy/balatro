Post-mortem: binding constraint was pacing/misplay, not money or scaling. The two mid-round "pacing" plays on Big Blind (TWOPAIR est1033 and est1278, both far under the per-hand share of 3000/3≈1000) burned all 3 hands and 3 discards on hands whose estimates never added up to 3000 (1033+1278+414=2725, i.e. the sum of achievable hands this blind was mathematically short of 3000 well before the final hand). The advise tool's own "sum of realistic ests" check (playbook section 4 step 4) should have flagged this ante 3 Big Blind as unwinnable-as-played once the second hand's est (1278) was locked in with only a Flush (est ~400-650) as the last realistic option - at that point discarding harder for the Flush's suit (5 diamonds were visible early: QD,JD,8D,7D/6D,5D) rather than playing the second Two Pair for banked-but-insufficient chips would have been worth exploring. The one decision I would change: on the second-to-last hand (TWOPAIR K7 est1278), consider discarding instead to chase a bigger Flush/Straight-based hand once the running total (1033 already banked) made it clear Two Pair alone could not reach 3000 in 3 hands - advise's pacing heuristic ("keep discards for later hands") does not appear to account for a per-hand ceiling that is provably below the remaining need.
Bridge issues: none. Every `advise`/`hands` est matched the actual score exactly on every hand this run (0% error), including the losing final Flush (est414/actual414).
Playbook suggestions:
1. Section 4's "sum of realistic ests" check (step 4, currently only invoked "out of discards") should also run proactively after each hand during pacing, and downgrade a "PLAY (pacing)" recommendation to a "DISCARD to reshape" recommendation if the candidate hand types available for the remaining hands cannot sum to `need` even in the best case.
2. When a Two Pair-based main hand's own level ceiling (est) provably cannot clear a blind in the hands remaining, and a Flush/Straight draw is live (4+ cards of one suit or 3-4 consecutive already in hand), `advise` should surface that alternative explicitly rather than only listing the currently-best-made hand.
3. Consider holding a second card-targeting tarot in reserve (2 consumable slots) rather than using both promptly - this run had 0 consumables left going into the forced final hand of the losing blind; the playbook's "rescue tarot" section 4.8 wasn't actionable because the only rescue tarot obtained (via Fool) had already been spent one hand earlier on a King instead of banked for the final-hand emergency.
