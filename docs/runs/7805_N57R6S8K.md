## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scoring jokers. I reached ante 3 with ONE permanent scorer (Shoot the Moon) and no multiplier: ante 1-2 shops
offered only one Buffoon pack and a handful of jokers, and the only scorers were perishable Mystic Summit and an eternal
conditional joker. SCORE CHECK was EMERGENCY (0.07-0.14) from the first ante-2 shop and never got better. Money never exceeded
$15 and every reroll ($5-7 each, 5 of them) found no scorer (Popcorn{R}, Gluttonous $5 unaffordable, Swashbuckler, Juggler, Matador...).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 Big/Pillar shops: left with $7 (no spend) and then ante 2 shop 1 rerolled $15 -> $4 with nothing found. If I had bought the
   $4 Arcana/Celestial or kept the interest it would not have changed the result; the real gap was no scorer supply, not a misplay.
2. Ante 2 Small: Rare Tag (free rare joker in the next shop) was declined per advisor (1 scorer). In hindsight, a Rare joker at the
   Big shop would have been the only realistic source of an xMult/strong scorer; with a single perishable bridge alive the
   downside (Big 1500 with Summit alone) was the same as the Small round I played anyway. Rule to test: with 1 scorer and 0 xMult,
   skip an ante-2 Small for a Rare/Uncommon tag when the Big is clearable by the existing scorer.
3. Ante 3 Small 3200: played it blind with $0; skipping for the Meteor tag (Mega Celestial) would not have fixed 3200/4800/6400
   either. The run was already dead at the ante-2 -> 3 jump.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Mystic Summit, THEN burn both discards on junk every round (keeping the best pair/queens); +15 mult at 0 discards turned
  Pair 52 into 476 and a made Straight/Flush into 1235-1273 at ante 1-2 (4 of 4 rounds cleared that way).

  held scored 400-640, Straights 3330, Trips (3 cards, Qs held) 1584. Never play the Queens; digs with 1-card plays keep them.
