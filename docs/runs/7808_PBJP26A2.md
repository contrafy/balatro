## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling/no real +mult. After the perishable Jolly died at ante 3 I had chips-only Faceless and a conditional xMult (Flower Pot) with a flat base mult of 2-4; typical non-FP hand ~400-1300 vs a 6400 boss that also forces 5-card plays. Flower Pot needs a club that my 8-card draws did not contain for 3 consecutive deals (~13 clubs in 36 cards, but discards/plays kept the suits I already held).

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 2 Small skip for the Negative tag: tag paid only in the shop after the Big and was a random junk rental. I lost a shop view and Jolly's perish clock burned anyway. Rule from playbook ("tags that pay next shop don't help the Big") was right; I overrode it.
2. Ante 3: bought Flower Pot (eternal conditional) as the 'xMult'. With 2 scorers and a flat build it fired only on 1 of 6 plays. Maybe correct as emergency placeholder, but I should have kept discards/World to set up suits (The World/Devil arrived only after the shop).
3. Ante 3 Coupon shop produced zero jokers; skipping Small for Coupon meant Jolly's last round was spent on the Big (worked) but the free shop was planets. A Coupon tag is a gamble on joker slots, not a joker.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF you own Flower Pot (x3, 4 suits) THEN check each draw for a missing suit and play a 4-suit Two Pair/FH/Straight; with a suit missing from the whole 8-card hand, a 4-card Two Pair cannot fire it (no 5th scoring card). Wild cards/Star/Moon/World-type suit changers fix this.
- IF The Psychic (5 cards) is the boss and the build relies on a 4-card Two Pair scoring set, THEN the 5th filler does not score: Flower Pot-style gating on scoring cards does not get help from fillers.
- IF a perishable +mult joker is your only mult at ante 3, THEN the SCORE CHECK drops to ~0.1 on the shop after it dies; buy its replacement in the shop before its last round (I had $13 and nothing to buy).
- Black Hole / Celestial packs at Coupon: take Black Hole (all hands +1) over a single planet when no main hand is certain.
