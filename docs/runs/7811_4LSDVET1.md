## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling, no real xMult (only conditional Seeing Double x2 and a perishable polychrome x1.5); the build was flat +mult that depended on Queens HELD (Shoot the Moon) and a Club in the scoring hand (Seeing Double) - both turned off or unreliable at The Plant (all faces debuffed). Lost by 38 points (0.2%), so any one of the small leaks below would have saved it.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 shop before The Plant ($10): bought Jumbo Arcana and took Empress (+4 mult on TT, never played). The Moon (3 cards to Clubs) or Lovers would have made Seeing Double fire on the last hand (x2 = +~3800 there). With a suit-gated xMult, spend Arcana picks on the tarot that fixes the suit.
2. The Plant boss hand 1: played KK(debuffed)+55 for 3900 plus a debuffed filler. Debuffed K scored 0 and the pair was odd (no Even Steven) with no club (no Seeing Double): a poor hand that only fed Supernova. A hand with TC/8C even clubs would have been ~2x. Under The Plant, read est per candidate and prefer even + club cards over face pairs.
3. Last-hand discard: kept 8D 7S 7D 6H 5S for a straight draw; the straight hit but had no Club. Keeping a club in the straight draw (or any hand with a Club + other suit) would have been +x2. Before a forced last hand, check which joker conditions (club, even, queen) the draw cards satisfy, not just the hand type.
4. Blue Joker counts cards REMAINING in the draw pile (27 on the last hand = +54 chips, not +104). SIM/shopadvise priced it at a full deck (+0.31); real value late in a round is about half.
5. Ante 1 Buffoon pack at $5: both options junk (skipped, fine). Ante 3 skipped Small for Polychrome tag: it gave a free polychrome Reserved Parking (x1.5) - good, keep doing it when a polychrome tag pays in the very next shop.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF the next boss is The Plant THEN list which of your jokers need faces/Queens (Shoot the Moon, Scary Face, Smiley, Photograph) before the shop and buy a non-face replacement or hold hands of non-face even/suit cards; est drops ~50-75% under it.
- IF you own a suit-gated xMult (Seeing Double: Club + another suit scoring) THEN prefer plays/draws that contain that suit; each Moon/Lovers/Star tarot is worth more than a +4 mult Empress.
- IF the final hand misses by <2% THEN nothing can fix it at that point: spend the earlier discards (2 of 3 here) on the hand that satisfies ALL joker conditions, and on hand 2 prefer the play that also leaves a Club/even for hand 3.
- IF a Polychrome tag is offered on the ante-3 Small and you hold no xMult THEN skip it: the next shop's free polychrome joker (x1.5) is cheap, and skipping avoids rent and Pillar-debuffed cards.
- IF holding Shoot the Moon THEN keep 2 Queens in hand and play 1-card/2-card hands of other ranks: a single card scored 1500-2500 at ante 3-4 versus Two Pair ~4000-15000.
- The House: discard the face-down cards first (5 then the remaining), redraws are face up; costs 2 of 3 discards but all 8 cards become readable.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- Blue Joker priced at full-deck chips in shopadvise SIM (+0.31); actual chips fall as the draw pile shrinks (est on the last hand showed 54).
- shopadvise said Plant 'disables Reserved Parking' but gave no counter for Shoot the Moon (held Queens debuffed under The Plant); the warning only named Reserved Parking.
- advise offered no club-aware line: with Seeing Double owned it did not mention that the 9-5 straight lacked a Club (est 3802 vs need 3840), and the discard choice (keep 8D 7S 7D 6H 5S) ignored joker conditions.
- Pack advice ranked Empress (55) over The Moon/Lovers although Seeing Double was owned and the boss debuffed faces.
