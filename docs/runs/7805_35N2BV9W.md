## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling. Zero xMult in 14 shop views + 1 Buffoon pack (both eternal rentals) + 1 Spectral. The build
was flat: Blue Joker (~90 chips), Abstract (+18), Raised Fist (+16-24), Wily (+100 on trips), Burglar (7 hands with
Grabber). That is ~3-6k per hand at ante 5 = ~26-30k per 7-hand round, which clears the ante-5 Small (25k) only by
using every hand and misses the Big (37.5k). Burglar's extra hands were the only reason it got this far: per-hand need
at ante 5 Small with 7 hands is 3.6k vs 8.3k with 3 hands.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 5 shop ($28): Wraith ($19 + Misprint) gave Invisible Joker, which needs 2 rounds before it does anything. Rare
   pool is about half real multipliers; the expected value was OK but the downside ($0 for the Big shop, Misprint lost)
   cost ~10% of Big score. With $28, Mad{E}/Arcana/rerolls across the two ante-5 shops plus keeping Misprint was safer.
2. Death at the Big: I typed the advisor's `use 1 5 2` without checking the direction. The game overwrites the LEFT
   (lower index) card, so AD became QH and the AAAKK Full House (solver p 0.73) became KKQQ (p 0.26). Always give Death
   targets as `<junk lower index> <keeper higher index>` and `move hand` first if the keeper is left of the junk.
3. Ante 4 Grabber $10 was fine; the ante-3/4 money ($9-19 left at three shops) bought no xMult because none appeared;
   Buffoon packs were never offered after ante 2 except the eternal-rental one.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF Burglar is offered at ante 2-3 on a flat build THEN it is a strong buy (6-7 hands halves the per-hand need), but
  plan hands as digs: play 5 cards (low junk as fillers) every non-scoring hand to cycle the deck for trips/quads.
- IF you own Raised Fist THEN play your LOW cards as fillers and keep 2-3 high cards held (+20-24 mult vs +4-8).
- IF using Death THEN the LEFT (lower index) card becomes the copy: the targets must be `junk keeper` with junk at the
  lower index. Check `s` positions; ignore the advisor's argument order if it lists the keeper first.
- IF you are at ante 5 with 0 xMult and a Spectral pack shows Wraith THEN only take it with a free slot AND when the
  current round already clears the next blind by 1.2x: Wraith's $0 kills the next shop.
- IF Chaos the Clown is offered at ante 2 THEN it is a good $4 buy while slots are free (free reroll every shop);
  sell it last among non-scorers.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- `advise` SOLVER USE Death printed "QH becomes a copy of AD (QH must be left of AD)" and ADVICE `b use 1 5 2`, but QH
  was at idx 5 (right of AD at idx 2): the game overwrote AD with QH. The advice should emit targets in game order
  (lower index = overwritten) or `move hand` first; the CLI should refuse/warn when the named copy direction is reversed.
- Pack `advise` rated Wraith 5 / SKIP with 0 xMult at ante 5 (ratio 0.47) with no SIM line; no estimate of the rare pool.
- SCORE CHECK swung wildly with Misprint (4.22 -> 1.59 -> 0.57 within ante 3-4) because it uses Misprint's mean.
Bridge issues: none (119 steps, no rejects).
