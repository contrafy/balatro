Post-mortem:
Binding constraint: a combination of discard sequencing and insufficient xMult scaling. We spent all 3 discards
across hands 1-2 (2 to build the Full House, 1 to chase the second flush) and had ZERO discards left for hand 3,
which drew a hand with no scoring path beyond a bare pair (270). Playbook section 6 explicitly says "Always keep
ONE discard for the final hand of a round" - we violated this. Structurally, our joker portfolio (Hologram,
Onyx Agate, Blue Joker, Lusty Joker, Walkie Talkie) is all flat +chips/+mult or slow-growing xMult (Hologram
+0.25/card added, and we never bought any cards to add), with no strong repeatable xMult stacking (no
Photograph/Blackboard/Card Sharp/Baron-tier joker all run) - by ante4 our best single hand (Full House 2465)
was well under half the 5000 needed, meaning even a perfect final hand likely would not have closed the gap
alone; the discard-sequencing mistake turned a hard blind into an unwinnable one.
Decision I would change: reserve the 3rd discard for hand 3 rather than using it on hand 2's flush chase -
after Full House (2465) and a marginal flush (976) we were already at 3441/5000 with one hand left; a discard
on that final hand (even a modest one, e.g. building a second pair from AD/KC/QC/JS/6H/3S/2S/2H) had a much
better chance of contributing meaningfully than the flush chase did, since the flush was already speculative
with 0 clear synergy from our jokers (none of our 5 jokers boost Flush specifically).

below) -> $9 (end ante1 boss) -> $16 (ante2 small) -> $9 (ante2 shop) -> $7 (ante2 big) -> $2 (ante2 shop2) ->

   advisor's FLUSH/STRAIGHT-only candidate list in that situation."
2. Strengthen section 6's "keep ONE discard for the final hand" rule to a hard MUST, with an explicit trigger:
   "if you have already locked in a hand that clears >0 but <100% of the remaining need with hands left to
   play, and only 1 discard remains, save it for your LAST hand of the blind, not an intermediate one - an
   intermediate flush/pair chase with no joker synergy is lower-value than insurance for the final, otherwise

   different hand is offered, unless you also hold a joker that specifically rewards that other hand type.
