## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: scaling / luck. A single xMult joker (Constellation, worth +0.1 xMult only when a
planet is actually used - none were bought or played all run) never contributed anything; effectively
the run was played with ZERO active jokers all game. Two enormous early hands (304, 700) banked plenty
of cash but the shop never offered a second scoring joker or a Planet for the straight/flush, and
`shopadvise` correctly said LEAVE both times since nothing available added real scoring power at $3-9.
The Window boss demands 600 cumulative with no jokers to lean on; a straight (264) plus two forced weak
pairs (56, 64) fell 216 short. Every `est` matched the actual score exactly (0% error) in all 5 hands
played - the estimator is accurate.

Decision I would change: at Shop 1, `shopadvise` chose Constellation (a scaling joker that needs
Planets to matter) over a raw scoring joker, but no scoring joker was even offered in either shop
this run, so there was no better option available at the time - the shops themselves were the
binding constraint, not the choice. Nothing to override in hindsight; the CLI/advisors behaved
correctly given the offered inventory.

Bridge issues: none. Every command returned expected state; no `ACTION FAILED` or retries needed.

Playbook suggestions:
1. Constellation is a trap pick at ante 1 with 0 planets owned and none in the shop - it does nothing
   until a Planet card is used. Consider flagging it AVOID (like Popcorn) when no Planet is visible
   anywhere in the current shop/pack inventory, or explicitly note in section 5 that ante-1 buys should
   prioritize a joker that scores THIS ante over one that scales a resource you don't have yet.
2. Section 3's "need N more this blind" note should explicitly state (as observed here) that need is
   CUMULATIVE across all hand-plays in the round, not per-hand - this was not obvious from the state
   line alone (`hands 3` at round start could be misread as "3 independent attempts").
3. When a boss requires 600+ and the run holds zero net-positive jokers after ante 1, `shopadvise`
   could add an explicit warning like "0 jokers with active effect - scoring will not scale, expect to
   lose the next high-need blind" to prompt earlier discard/skip-tag reconsideration (not skipping the
   blind itself, but flagging the risk clearly before it's spent).

`est` vs actual: no discrepancy >20% (all 5 played hands matched `est` exactly: 304/304, 700/700,
264/264, 56/56, 64/64).
