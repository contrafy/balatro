## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: NO +MULT SOURCE and no money. Five shops offered exactly two jokers I could afford (Photograph, Odd Todd);
every scoring hand was a lucky flush on base mult 4-6. The Water removed discards, so the flush engine (which needed 1-2
discards per round to find) disappeared exactly when the requirement went to 2000. Money: $4 start, Small pays $0,
Photograph $5, then Odd Todd's rent ate $6 of the ~$14 I earned in ante 1-2.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 1 shop 1 ($6): Photograph{ETERNAL} over Buffoon Pack $4. Photograph is x2 of a base mult of 2-4 when the hand has
   a face: it doubles ~30-60 points at ante 1-2 and does nothing on faceless hands. A Buffoon pack (4 joker choices, $2 left)
   had a good chance of a +mult joker. The SIM never evaluated the pack, so "SIM 0.57 vs 0.01" compared Photograph with
   buying nothing, not with the pack.
2. Ante 1 shop 2: Odd Todd{PERISH,RENTAL}. It cost $1 + $6 rent and contributed ~0 on the losing boss (KKK, JJ: no odd
   cards). The $6 rent was the difference between $1 and $7 entering the ante-2 Big shop (enough for an Arcana pack or a
   $5-6 joker on a reroll).
3. Ante 2 shop ($10): reroll + Saturn. Saturn levels a non-core hand I never played. Better: Arcana Pack $4 (Empress = +4
   mult cards, Hermit = money) and keep $6 for interest, or keep $10 entirely.

Reusable rules (if-then):
- IF ante 1 shop 1 offers a $5 xMult-on-condition joker (Photograph) and a $4 Buffoon pack and you own NO +mult joker, THEN
  the pack is at least as good: card-trigger xMults multiply only the base hand mult (2-4) and need a +mult joker first.
- IF a rental's ability does not fire on your pair hands (Odd Todd on faces/evens), THEN do not buy it as an ante-1 bridge:
  $3/round is a whole joker by ante 2 when the Small pays $0.
- IF your only scoring engine is flushes and the boss is The Water (0 discards), THEN treat the ratio vs boss as half of
  what the advisor projects: without discards flushes appear ~1 in 6 deals.
- IF dealt no discards and need > 4x your made hand with 2 hands left, THEN the solver's open-ended straight dig is right
  in expectation (62% to hit + other outs), but plan the money/joker purchase BEFORE such a boss; one dig is a coin flip.

Advisor/CLI defects:
- shopadvise ante 2 ($10, EMERGENCY 0.23, zero +mult jokers, no joker on offer): ADVICE was LEAVE; playbook says reroll
  with $4+ after when no scorer is offered. EMERGENCY should name the reroll or a pack, not LEAVE.
- SIM compared single items with "buy nothing" only; packs (Buffoon $4 at ante 1, Arcana) were never simulated, so
  Photograph's "0.57 vs 0.01" looked decisive against the pack.
- SIM recommended Saturn (Straight planet, "p_next 0.39 vs 0.08") against the pair-family policy; the next blind was a
  Small I then skipped (rent-broke), so the p_next it optimized was for a blind never played.
- Meteor pack advise: second pick Earth (Full House) over Mercury (Pair) "best pair-family fallback"; Pair is dealt in
  almost every hand, Full House rarely. Overrode.
- advise early in ante 1 printed p >=0.99 for flush draws (4/5 suited, 2 draws) that were ~0.6-0.7 to complete; they
  happened to hit, but the calibration at the top end looks optimistic.
Bridge issues: none; 30 steps, no rejects.
