# Run I7MH1E3L (port 7810, claude-sonnet-5-5) - Black Deck Gold Stake - LOSS at ante 3 blind The Pillar (5168/6400)
Ante 1: Small 300 via 4-heart flush draw (340). Shop $6: Buffoon pack (Splash ETERNAL vs Square Joker -> Square), rental Crazy Joker $1 (+12 mult Straight). Big 450: flush 304 + pair 68 + Broadway straight 1360. Boss Window 600 (diamonds debuffed): discarded twice for A234 wheel, got a 5, straight 880. Money -2..$2 all ante because of rent.
Ante 2 (1000/1500/2000): bought perishable Misprint $4. Skipped Small for Ethereal Tag (Grim: 2 enhanced Aces). Big 1500: club flush est 800, actual 2080. Celestial pack -> Saturn (Straight L2). Boss The Arm 2000: straight 1239 (Arm dropped it to L1 and the level was lost) + Broadway 1870 est.
Ante 3 (3200/4800/6400): skipped Small (Polychrome Tag, never produced a joker because the next shop had none). Incantation from a spectral pack. Big 4800: Broadway 1530 + straight 3034 + two pair 780 = 5344 (cleared with 236 to spare). Boss The Pillar 6400: straights 1679 + 1887 + 1602 = 5168. Lucky 9S paid $20 mid-fight, useless because shops are closed during a round.
Final: jokers Square Joker (+8 chips), Crazy Joker (rental), Misprint (perishable). Straight L1, no xMult, $21 unspent at death.

Post-mortem
- Binding constraint: scaling. The build was one hand type (Straight) on Crazy +12 mult + Misprint, with straight levels lost to The Arm. Ceiling was ~2000 per hand; ante 3 needs 6400 in 3 hands. Money was $0-6 all run (rental rent -$3/round plus Small Blind paying $0), so I could never buy a second real scorer, an xMult, or the main-hand planet. Total spend: 1 Buffoon pack, Crazy $1, Misprint $4, Celestial $4, Spectral $4.
- Decisions I would change:
  1. Buying a rental Crazy Joker at ante 1 for $1. It carried the run (3 straights = 3 clears) but its $3/round rent kept money negative at every round end, so I could not buy anything useful in 4 shops. I should have sold it once I had a second scorer, or used it only for the ante-1 boss.
  2. Saturn went into Straight, and The Arm at the ante 2 boss took that level back. When the upcoming boss is The Arm, do not buy a level for the hand you will play; buy it after the Arm.
  3. At ante 3 I skipped the Small Blind for a Polychrome Tag that could not be used (the next shop after Big had no joker, and I had $0). A tag that pays only in the next shop is worth it only if you will have money in that shop.
  4. At the Pillar (final hand, 5168/6400) the Broadway used 5 fresh cards but the Misprint rolled low: est at mean 11 would have been ~2600 and cleared (need 2834 on the last hand). Misprint variance decided the run.
- Reusable if-then rules:
  * If your only scoring joker is straight-gated (Crazy), then hold 4-card straight draws including A-2-3-4 and Q-J-T-9 and discard everything else; wheel (A2345) + Broadway came up in 4 of 8 straight attempts.
  * If a rental joker is not a core scorer, sell it the round after it has done its job: each $3 rent at $0 income blocks the whole shop.
  * If the boss is The Arm, play unleveled hand types first and never bank a planet level for the hand you will repeat.
  * If the boss is The Pillar and you already played ~15 distinct cards that ante, expect 3-4 debuffed cards in the 8-card hand and plan straights from the clean cards only; debuffed cards still count for the straight but score 0 chips.
  * If you hold a Lucky/Gold card, a Lucky-card $20 payout arrives mid-round and cannot be spent, so keep money-making cards for shops-before-boss, not for the boss.

Misprint est uses 6 so realistic mean ~11 would have shown straight est ~2600 vs the printed 1958; calibration-wise Misprint hands landed above est in 4 of 6 cases.
