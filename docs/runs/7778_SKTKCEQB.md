## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING. The engine (Pair-spam with Jolly/Trousers/Wily/Even Steven, one conditional xMult from ante 4 on) scored consistently in the 2500-4500 range per single Pair hand even at Pair L4, which is far too low against ante 5's ~11000-22000 targets. Full House (when it appeared) scored 2-3x better (6500-10700) confirming the joker set actually favors Full House/Quads far more than Pair, but the player kept defaulting to single-Pair plays (following `advise`'s per-hand est optimization) instead of actively holding/building toward Full House every hand. No reliable (unconditional) xMult was ever found in the entire run; only Ride the Bus (conditional, resets on any scoring face card) was secured, and it kept getting reset by face-card pairs (Q, K) that scored in nearly every winning hand played from ante 3 onward - defeating its own purpose.

One decision I would change: at ante 3, when jokers clearly synergized best with Full House/Quads (Jolly+Wily both fire on Full House, all three fire on Quads), I should have discarded more aggressively to hunt Full House/Quads every hand instead of accepting single-Pair plays that "cleared this hand's need" via advise's greedy per-hand logic - the cumulative margin kept shrinking each ante (532/300 huge margin at ante1 -> 50/11000 margin at ante5) because Pair scaling is linear in level while Full House scaling compounds with the same jokers. Also: given Ride the Bus explicitly conflicts with a Pair/face-heavy build (many of our best pairs were face cards Q/K/J), it was a poor "fits your build" placeholder in hindsight - a flat +mult/+chips joker (Scary Face, Sly Joker) would have been more reliable than a self-defeating conditional xMult.
