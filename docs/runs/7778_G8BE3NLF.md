  x1.5/uncommon-joker) was found the entire run despite rerolling and buying packs every shop from ante 3 on; the deck

  into the low-to-mid 20000s at best, nowhere near the 30000 Big Blind at ante 6. The decision I would change: at ante 6

  flagged as "mostly wasted" once Two Pair became the main hand.

Playbook suggestions:
  1. When a Celestial pack repeatedly fails to offer the planet for the CURRENT main hand (as identified by shopadvise),
     the playbook should say to skip picking any planet from that pack rather than defaulting to the secondary hand's
     planet -- "mostly wasted" levels still cost a pick that could have gone toward money banked for the next shop's
     Uranus/Earth search, or toward selling a joker for slot space.
  2. Add an explicit rule: at ante 6+ with 6/6 joker slots and fewer than 2 xMult sources, sell the WEAKEST flat joker
     (even without a specific xMult replacement in hand yet) to free a slot and reroll aggressively, rather than waiting
     for shopadvise to name a specific xMult to sell into -- being full with no room to react cost this run a shop-visit
     cycle when Sly Joker and other flat options kept appearing instead of anything that could close the ante 6 gap.
  3. Immolate (destroy 5 random deck cards for $20) is a fine one-time cash boost when broke, but note it shrinks the
     deck permanently -- worth flagging that it slightly reduces flush/straight draw odds for the rest of the run.
