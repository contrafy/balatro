Binding constraint: no growth xMult and no money. The whole build was Joker Stencil (x4-x6 while slots stay empty) + Raised Fist + The Duo; every economy source was drained by $3 Stencil rent each round, the $0 Gold Small Blind and the two rerolls/packs at ante 4. From ante 4 the per-hand ceiling (~10-20k) was 0.1-0.4x the blind, so the run was a luck chain that ended at the first 37500 Big.

Decisions I would change:
1. Ante 4 shop: spent $26 (Mega Celestial $8, 3 rerolls $19, Arcana $6) with 0 new scorer found; the planets were fine but 3 rerolls at $6-8 found nothing. Rule: reroll only to $20+.
