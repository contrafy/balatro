## Post-mortem

Binding constraint: SCALING with MONEY as the cause. The engine was Shoot the Moon (+13 per Queen held) + The Duo (x2) + foil Credit Card (+50 chips) on Two Pair L4: 6-13k per hand WHEN 1-2 Queens were in hand and a pair-family hand was dealt, 1-4k otherwise. Ante 4's boss needed 6k per hand for three hands; two of my three hands had no pair at all. Even had the Window fallen, ante 5 (25000/37500/50000) needed ~17k per hand with $-14 in the wallet, no permanent third scorer (Odd Todd perishing in 2 rounds) and 4 eternal jokers (Shoot the Moon, Joker +4, Duo, Credit Card) leaving two flexible slots, one of them wasted on a dead Baseball Card. From the ante-2 shop on, every shop printed EMERGENCY/TIGHT (ratios 0.18-0.63) and no shop ever offered a real xMult; the only one (Duo) came from a $4 Buffoon pack.

Decisions I would change:
1. Baseball Card from the Rare Tag: I took it believing Shoot the Moon was Uncommon. It is COMMON (as are Joker, Hanging Chad, Credit Card; The Duo is Rare), so Baseball Card was x1 and the SIM's "+0.00" was right. Cost: the 6th slot for two antes and $4 of unsold value. A Rare Tag with 5 non-Uncommon jokers should have been judged by what the Rare pool actually fires on.

The foil Credit Card{ETERNAL,RENTAL} at ante 3: +50 chips doubled my hands (SIM 0.65 -> 0.98 for the ante-3 blinds) and I cleared ante 3 and the ante-4 Small/Big in 1-2 hands, but the permanent -$3/round plus the Chad rental put income below rent for the rest of the run: $8 at ante 3 -> $5 -> $-14. A Gold run with negative cash never buys the second xMult. If I buy an eternal rental scorer, I must sell every other rental the same shop (I kept Chad 3 more rounds = $9) and stop buying packs on credit.
