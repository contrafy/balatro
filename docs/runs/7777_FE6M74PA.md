Post-mortem: binding constraint was SCALING, not misplay. Full House L3 + Two Pair L4 + doubled xMult (Blueprint copying The Duo, x4 effective) was producing ~12-18k per hand at ante 5, but The Wall's doubled requirement (44000) needed ~15k/hand x3 with no discards wasted, and our hand distribution across the round (Full House, then Two Pair, then a bare Pair on the forced final hand) meant the last hand landed on our weakest leveled type. The single xMult source (however doubled) was not enough once the boss doubled the target; playbook's own guidance (7a: "Wall boss DOUBLES the blind requirement... treat it like a boss one ante later") was correct in retrospect, we were under-prepared for an ante-6-equivalent requirement while only having ante-5 economy ($2 at blind start).
Decision I would change: at the ante 4 and ante 5 shops, we should have taken Golden Joker ($6, +$4/round economy) or otherwise built a money reserve so that going into The Wall we had $15-20+ to buy a second xMult or another level immediately if the first hand of the boss undershot pace; instead we entered the boss at $2 with no buffer and no way to react mid-round. Playbook suggestions:
1. The `est` calculation should account for Blueprint (or any joker) copying an xMult joker's ability - every calibration miss this run was exactly 2x because Blueprint copying The Duo's x2 mult was not modeled, only the original x2. This under-estimates real scores by half whenever a Blueprint+xMult combo is in play, which could lead to false pessimism (declining an actually-winning hand) in other runs.

3. shopadvise's ORDER advice can suggest an ordering that breaks a Blueprint's copy target (inserting another joker between Blueprint and the joker it should copy) - it should special-case Blueprint by always keeping it immediately adjacent-left of whatever xMult joker it should copy when proposing reorders.
